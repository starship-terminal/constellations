# Patterns

Public and signed-out pages only. The signed-in board is not in this package. The board drawings on the marketing pages are illustrations.

## Marketing home

A white page. A top bar holds the mark, four dropdown labels (AI platform, Solutions, Resources, Enterprise), then Pricing, Log in, a ghost Contact sales pill and a purple Get Started pill. The hero is a two-column split: a Poppins display heading and a department chip row on the left, an illustrated board on the right. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

Hovering the first nav link did not open a menu. The Enterprise chip showed a selected fill. See [components/crops/nav-hover.png](components/crops/nav-hover.png).

## Pricing

The same top bar, then a centred heading, a seat select, a yearly/monthly switch, and five plan cards. Pro is marked Most Popular. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png). The billing switch was not clicked.

## Login

A split page. The left half is a centred form: heading, email field, blue Next button, a divider, Google and Microsoft buttons, and two text links. The right half is an illustration. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

At 390px the form is replaced by a note to come back on desktop or download the app. See [screenshots/mobile-login-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-login-light-default.png).

## Signup

The same split. The form leads with Continue with Google, then an email field and a blue Continue button, then the terms line. See [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png). Continue was not pressed.

## Contact sales

A split page: a short pitch on the left, a long form on the right. The form includes a phone field. The flow stopped there. See [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).
