Public pages only. The product is a native iOS and macOS design tool. The web surfaces captured here are the marketing site.

The desktop home is a black page with an announcement bar, a centered nav, a 64px headline, and a green "Get Started for Free" button. The phone home replaces the nav with a menu icon and stacks an App Store button over a white macOS button. Pricing is four columns (Basic, Starter, Pro, Enterprise) with an annual toggle. Download is two cards, Mac and iPhone and iPad.
