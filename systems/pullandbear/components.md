# Components

Public controls on the United Kingdom storefront, 2026-11-22. Measurements are CSS pixels.

## Header

White bar. Menu icon at the left, 34px. Wordmark centred, linking home. Search is a button that looks like an underlined field: 128 by 20, 0.5px black bottom rule, Neue Haas about 13px, tracking 0.7px. Account and basket are 24 by 24. Basket shows 0. Neither was opened.

## Promo bar

36px, `#71c5fa`, black type, centred. Copy: "MIX & MATCH: 5% off with 2 items, 10% off with 3 or more | See selection". A close control sits at the right. Present on category and product. Absent on the homepage split.

## Menu

Full-width white panel. Department labels WOMAN, MAN, STWD are bold. Column headings (HIGHLIGHTS, COLLECTION, SHOES | BAGS, BEAUTY, SPECIAL EDITION) are small caps and disabled as buttons. Links are black. Promotions is blue, matching named token `#2d64e3`. A small "New" tag sits beside some labels. Cards on the right repeat Party, Promotions, and Perfumes | Makeup.

## Product grid

Four columns. Photograph, name, price. Price is black. A heart sits at the right of the name row. A sale chip is a red rectangle, sampled `#cb2c3b`, with white "-44%" and a struck price beside it.

## Product buy panel

Title, price in bold, a short description, composition chips (Viscose, Animal Print, Midi), a size select ("Select a size"), Apple Pay, and Add. Add is a black rectangle with white type. It was not clicked. Composition and care, and Shipping and returns, are collapsed rows.

## Brand history type

Kicker 22px weight 400. Display 46px weight 600, line-height 70px. Body 12px, line-height 26px, `#5a5a5a`, 500px wide.

## Footer

Three columns: Help, Company, Social. A legal row under a hairline: Cookie preferences, Privacy policy, Purchase conditions, Cookies policy, Use of AI, Gender Pay Gap Report, United Kingdom, English. Href values were not readable from the DOM.

## Gaps

Log in, the basket, search results, size selection, Add, and checkout were not opened. Phone width was not captured. Dark theme is not present. MAN's menu column was not opened.
