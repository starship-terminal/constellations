# Patterns

How the public pages are put together. Each row points at the screenshot that shows it.

## Homepage split

Two full-height photographs. WOMAN on the left, MAN on the right. The wordmark sits on the join, in white. Labels sit low on each half, white, large. The header is transparent over the photographs.

[screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Menu over the homepage

Opening Menu replaces the photographs with a white panel. Departments stay left. Category columns fill the middle. Editorial cards sit at the right edge, partly cut off. Close is an X where the menu icon was.

[screenshots/menu-light-1440.png](screenshots/menu-light-1440.png)

## Category grid

Promo bar, header, a centred title ("New"), then View and Filter at the right. Four product columns. Names and prices sit under the photographs. A short SEO paragraph sits below the grid, not photographed in the first viewport.

[screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png)

## Product

Thumbnails in a vertical strip. The main photograph is centred. The buy column is on the right: title, price, description, chips, size, Apple Pay, Add. Reference and care rows sit under the thumbnails.

[screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png)

## Brand history

A two-column page. Text on the left, a full-bleed photograph on the right. The header stays white. CONCEPT is a small kicker. The heading is two lines, 46px.

[screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png)

## Footer

A related-product row, then three link columns, then a legal row with the region and the language. The header stays pinned.
