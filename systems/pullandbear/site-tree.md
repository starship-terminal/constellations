# Pull&Bear public site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-22, signed out. The start URL was https://www.pullandbear.com. The browser landed on https://www.pullandbear.com/sg/, a Singapore closure notice, not a shop. The notice said stores close on 22 February and named a returns desk and a customer-service email. That page has one link, to a QR page. It was not opened.

https://www.pullandbear.com/ww/ bounced to a country picker at https://www.pullandbear.com/?select-store=true. The picker lists Spain, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy, The Netherlands, Greece, Turkey, USA, Poland, Mexico, and Portugal. One region was opened: United Kingdom, https://www.pullandbear.com/gb/. The title was "PULL&BEAR United Kingdom | New Collection 2026 | Pull and Bear". No second regional slug was created.

A location card asked "You're browsing from United Kingdom. Do you want to save your location?" No was clicked. The card was not saved. No email was typed. No password was typed. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. Add was not clicked. The basket was not opened. Log in was not opened.

The shop paints the header menu and the footer in a canvas the DOM does not expose as links. The menu URLs below come from the accessibility tree while the menu was open. The footer labels come from the photograph. Their hrefs were not readable.
