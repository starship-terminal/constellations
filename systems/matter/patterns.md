# Matter patterns

How the measured components combine on Matter's public surface, read from the screenshots and [components.md](components.md). The marketing site paints one near-black theme. The article and the sign-in card paint light surfaces of their own.

## Marketing chrome

Every marketing page opens on a 62px header ([header](components/crops/header-default.png), 1440 x 62 on `#0D0D0D`): the wordmark ([logo](components/crops/logo-default.png)), gray nav links ([nav-link](components/crops/nav-link-default.png), `#969698`), a Sign In text link ([nav-signin](components/crops/nav-signin-default.png)), and the blurple Get Matter pill ([nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png), 95 x 32, `#6D7EEC`, 100px radius). Sign In and Get Matter were measured, never followed into an account or a store checkout.

## Home hero

The home hero ([home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)) centres a Camphor headline ([hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png), 850 x 80, "Save. Read. Grow.") over a gray lede ([hero-lede](components/crops/hero-lede-default.png)) and a larger Get Matter pill ([hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png), 134 x 48). Below that, press logos and phone mockups sit on the same near-black field. A secondary text link ([secondary-link](components/crops/secondary-link-default.png)) sits under some sections.

## Footer

The footer is a full-viewport panel at the end of the home page, not a short bar. It holds the quote ([quote](components/crops/quote-default.png)), another Get Matter pill ([footer-cta](components/crops/footer-cta-default.png)), and a row of text links ([footer-link](components/crops/footer-link-default.png)): Terms, Privacy, X, Say hello. Those links were measured, never followed.

## Article

A published article loads signed out ([article-dark-default](screenshots/article-dark-default.png)) on a cream Substack page: the headline ([article-h1](components/crops/article-h1-default.png)), a byline link ([article-byline](components/crops/article-byline-default.png)), and body paragraphs ([article-body](components/crops/article-body-default.png)). The publication home ([words-dark-default](screenshots/words-dark-default.png)) is a subscribe gate: title ([words-h1](components/crops/words-h1-default.png)), Subscribe ([words-subscribe](components/crops/words-subscribe-default.png)), and No thanks ([words-nav](components/crops/words-nav-default.png)). Subscribe was never pressed, so the form was not submitted.

## Docs and sign-in

Docs ([docs-light-default](screenshots/docs-light-default.png)) is a public Mintlify home: heading ([docs-h1](components/crops/docs-h1-default.png)), a search button ([docs-search](components/crops/docs-search-default.png), never submitted), and an API link ([docs-card](components/crops/docs-card-default.png), never followed). A theme control switches that page to light ([docs-light-theme](screenshots/docs-light-theme.png)).

The reader sign-in card ([signin-light-default](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)) is a white page: heading ([signin-h1](components/crops/signin-h1-default.png)), Continue with Google ([google-button](components/crops/google-button-default.png), 340 x 48), Continue with Apple ([apple-button](components/crops/apple-button-default.png), 340 x 44), an email field ([email-field](components/crops/email-field-default.png), focused, never filled), and a 32px arrow ([email-continue](components/crops/email-continue-default.png), never pressed). That card is the sign-in boundary. The reader behind it is uncaptured.
