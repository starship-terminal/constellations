# Bershka

A signed-out capture of the public Bershka shop at [https://www.bershka.com](https://www.bershka.com) on 2026-11-22. The storefront is Spain, Spanish. Desktop 1440 by 900, `devicePixelRatio` 2. The page is `#ffffff`. Nav, titles, prices, and footer links are Whyte, ink `#000000`. Promociones is `#d72b2b`. Add to bag is a square black bar, white type, 40px, radius 0px.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

## Type

Whyte on the body, the header, the menu, cards, and the product panel. Header links are 12px, weight 700, uppercase. Menu links are 14px. Category and about headings are 28px, weight 700, uppercase. Card names and prices are 12px, weight 700. The product price is 14px, weight 700. About body is 14px, line-height 20px. Faces are ABCWhyte Light, Regular, Medium, and Bold. Font files are not in this package.
