# Patterns

How the public Spain shop combines the controls in [components.md](components.md).

## Gate

A centered wordmark sits above two image tiles, Mujer and Hombre. After Continuar, a location toast offers Guardar. That button was not clicked. [screenshots/gate-light-1440.png](screenshots/gate-light-1440.png)

## Home

A 40px white header sits over a full-bleed slide. The slide label is a white box on the photograph. Four category tiles follow in a row. A yellow Combo wins strip sits over the lower edge of the grid. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Menu

The Mujer menu is a white panel under the header, about 575px wide. Featured links sit above a hairline, then the collection list. The page behind the panel stays visible and dimmed. [screenshots/menu-mujer-light-1440.png](screenshots/menu-mujer-light-1440.png)

## Category

The heading is centered. A chip row (Ver todo, Cortos, Largos, Party) sits under it. Filtrar is left. Vista is right. Cards are four across, with the name and price under the photograph. [screenshots/dresses-light-1440.png](screenshots/dresses-light-1440.png)

## Product

Photographs fill the page. A gray panel sits on the right: New, name, price, colour, sizes, then a black Add to bag bar. The bar was not clicked. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png)

## About

The shop header stays. Sobre Bershka is a 28px heading. Body copy is 14px on white, beside store photographs. [screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png)

## Footer

A newsletter line sits above three columns: help, brand, and category links. Social icons sit on the right. A legal row closes the page with España | Español and the year. [screenshots/footer-light-1440.png](screenshots/footer-light-1440.png)
