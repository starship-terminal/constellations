# Reclaim AI

Public marketing site and the signed-out login for Reclaim, the AI calendar. Captured 2026-11-19 from [reclaim.ai](https://reclaim.ai/), [reclaim.ai/pricing](https://reclaim.ai/pricing), [reclaim.ai/contact](https://reclaim.ai/contact) and [app.reclaim.ai/login](https://app.reclaim.ai/login). Signed out the whole time. Marketing pages are light. The login page paints a dark field (`#171d26`) even though the document reports `color-scheme: light`. Viewport was the preview tab at 1169 by 731, device pixel ratio 2. No 1440 or 390 capture.

The signed-in app was not opened. Login offers Google, Microsoft, Dropbox and SSO, and no email field, so the email-only signup rule did not apply. Nothing was submitted.

## How the values were read

Custom properties and computed styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on a live T3 preview tab (`reuseExistingTab: false`). Marketing tokens come from Webflow custom properties on `:root` (`--_brand-colors-2026---*`, `--_apps---*`) and from `getComputedStyle` on the nav, hero buttons, pricing cards and billing tabs. Login tokens come from `--rai-*` and the computed styles of the provider buttons, the title and the links. The Osano dialog was dismissed with Reject Non-Essential before the marketing controls were measured. The contact form was read inside its HubSpot iframe.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are missing. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on the homepage and again on the contact page, so layout claims rest on DOM rectangles, not images.

The viewport is 1169 wide, not 1440 or 390. Phone layout was not measured.

`--_apps---colors--primary` is `#5562eb`. The marketing CTA that was on screen computes to `rgb(0, 97, 254)`. Both are recorded. The blurple is the declared brand primary and the colour of the cookie and HubSpot submit buttons. The blue is the colour of Get Started.

Dark semantic tokens are declared on the marketing sheet and resolved on the login page. They are not a captured signed-in theme. `--rai-radius-*` from small through xxlarge all resolve to `4px`, while the login buttons compute to `26px` and `40px`.

The contact form uses HubSpot's own Helvetica field styles. Those are not Reclaim marketing tokens.

Dropbox Digits variables (`--dig-*`, `--color__*`) load with the login shell. They are a vendor system. Reclaim's own app tokens are the `--rai-*` set.

## Fonts

Sharp Grotesk DB Book 20 and Book 23 are commercial display faces on the marketing headings, with Georgia as the fallback the computed style names. Poppins is the marketing body and the login body, loaded as a webfont (`wf-poppins-n4-active` on the marketing html class). Atlas Grotesk Web is the marketing button and pricing-label face, with Arial as fallback. None of these files are in the package. The tokens name the families the browser resolved, plus the fallbacks it reported.

## Do and don't

- **Use the blue CTA for the marketing primary action.** Get Started is `rgb(0, 97, 254)`, 10px radius, Atlas Grotesk Web at weight 300. Evidence is the computed style of `a.nav_start_button` in [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).
- **Keep marketing display type in Sharp Grotesk.** The homepage h1 is 72px on 90px, weight 500, letter-spacing -2px. Pricing prices are 50px in the same family.
- **Treat login as a dark OAuth sheet.** The field is `#171d26`. Provider buttons are 370 by 44 pills. Links are `#ee9ab2`. There is no email field to style.
- **Do not invent a screenshot.** The frames never arrived. Describe the measured rectangles, or recapture when the preview browser can write a PNG.
