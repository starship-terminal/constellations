# Patterns

How the public site combines the measured components. Screenshots are 256-colour copies. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Marketing shell

A white bar, Dropbox Reclaim wordmark, Product / Solutions / Resources menus, Enterprise, Pricing, Log In, a quiet Book a Demo, and a blue Get Started. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

The menus are hover panels, not pages. Product lists twelve features. See [home-light-product-menu](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png).

## Hero

A 90px Sharp Grotesk headline, a one-line subhead, a filled Get started free and an outlined Book a demo. Below that, a public calendar demo (May 2026 in this capture) and a chat demo. Those demos are marketing, not the signed-in app.

## Pricing

Yearly and Monthly are a local toggle. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [pricing-light-monthly](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png). See all features expands the comparison: [pricing-light-features](screenshots/pricing-light-features.png). Plan Get Started links stop at the signup wall.

## Phone

At 390 the header collapses to the wordmark, a Get started pill, and a close control once the menu is open. Product, Solutions, and Resources are disclosure rows. See [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Auth wall

Log In opens https://app.reclaim.ai/login. The wall offers Google, Microsoft, Dropbox, and SSO. None were clicked. See [login-light-wall](screenshots/login-light-wall.png). Signup is the same kind of wall: [signup-light-wall](screenshots/signup-light-wall.png).

## Tools

The pomodoro timer and meeting-cost calculator are public widgets with their own chrome, not the marketing header. See [pomodoro-light-default](screenshots/pomodoro-light-default.png).
