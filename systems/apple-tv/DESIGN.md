## Type

SF Pro is the face named in the computed body stack: `-apple-system`, `SF Pro`, `Helvetica Neue`, Helvetica, Arial. Body copy is 13px. The selected nav label is 16px, weight 600. The published `--headline` token is 48px, weight 600.

## Radius and size

Sign In is 28px tall with a 6px radius. The search field is 28px tall with a 4px radius and a 1px light border. Poster links are 14px radius. The header is 52px tall.
