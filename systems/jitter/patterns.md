# Patterns

How the public pages combine. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

A fixed header on `#f2f1f3`: wordmark, Product, Customers, Templates, Pricing, Log in, and a black Sign up pill. Product and Customers open hover menus on the same page. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-product-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png) and [home-light-customers-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-customers-menu.png).

At 390 the nav collapses to Log in and a round menu button. The drawer lists Product, Customers, Templates, Pricing, Contact sales, and Sign up. See [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png).

## Pricing

The page loads with Pay annually selected. A blue Free pill and a yellow Pro pill sit under the toggle. An accordion answers common questions. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [pricing-light-faq.png](screenshots/pricing-light-faq.png). Get started was not clicked. Checkout was not entered.

## Templates

A search field and category links over a grid of public previews. See [templates-light-default.png](screenshots/templates-light-default.png) and the focused field [templates-light-search-focus.png](screenshots/templates-light-search-focus.png). Search was not typed.

## Sign-in boundary

`/login/` and `/join/` render the same card: Continue with Google, an empty work-email field, and Continue with email. See [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png). Google was not clicked. The email was not typed. The form was not submitted.

## Contact sales boundary

A form with name, email, phone and a message, plus Send message. See [contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png). The form was not filled and not submitted. The page ships sample placeholders. The email and phone placeholders were covered in the page shot.
