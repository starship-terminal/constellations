# Family design reference

A token set and component reference for Family's public product surface — for building crypto-wallet marketing pages, docs/support surfaces and the signed-out account login in the style of the real thing. It was read from **family.co** ("Your favorite crypto wallet." — an iOS Ethereum self-custody wallet) and **app.family.co** (which now presents the login as **Aave Account** — "Family Accounts is now Aave Account") on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /blog, a blog post, /changelog, /support, a support article, /faqs, /legal, /docs, /docs/connectkit, /docs/family-account, the Aave Account login, and 390px passes on home, support and the login.

Family is worth a capture because both surfaces ship real token layers to the browser. The marketing site's stylesheets declare a compact role layer — `--body`/`--body-muted`/`--heading` text roles, `--blue`/`--orange`/`--beige`/`--gray-light`/`--white`, a full `--graphic-*` illustration palette (`--graphic-blue`, `--graphic-gold`, `--graphic-purple`, `--graphic-stone`…), `--color-*` surface roles, a `--focus` ring and layout vars (`--h-pad`, `--nh-pad`, `--gap`). The app's stylesheets declare a product layer: a `--family-*`/`--primary-*` violet scale (`--family`/`--primary` = `#9896ff`), 9-step `--danger-*`/`--valid-*`/`--warning-*` status ramps, 21 named `--wallet-*` colours, `--overlay-*`, `--input-*`, `--body-*`, `--icon-*`, `--selection-*`, `--hyperlink-*`, `--logo-*`, `--focus-*`, `--duration-*`/`--ease-*` curves and `--txn-*`/`--otp-*`/`--qr-*` component values — including a real `[data-theme="dark"]` layer (20 names switch, 3 dark-only overlay names) that never paints while signed out. Three voices: the proprietary "Family" face on every heading, Inter on marketing UI, variable Inter on the app. The surface is a white field with `#343433` headings, `#4a443f`/`#848281` body and muted text, `#f2f0ed` hairlines and near-black `#171717` CTA pills. **Boundary:** everything behind an account was never reached — the Log In / Get Started / Download on iOS / Watch actions were measured, never followed; the login field and the support search were typed into and cleared, never submitted; the iOS wallet itself is a native app outside this capture.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export family` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layers carry the design.
2. Do spend near-black on the actions that commit.
3. Do paint light.
4. Do pair the voices.
5. Do use hairlines, not borders, on cards.
6. Do keep pills pill-shaped.
7. Don't style the signed-in wallet or Aave Account from this package.
8. Don't follow the outbound links.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layers carry the design.** Both surfaces ship their tokens: use the marketing `--body`/`--heading`/role colours, the `--graphic-*` illustration palette and the app's `--family-*`/`--primary-*`/`--wallet-*`/`--danger-*`/`--valid-*`/`--warning-*` scales rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [nav-dropdown](components/crops/nav-dropdown-default.png).
2. **Do spend near-black on the actions that commit.** `#171717` fills the Download on iOS and Get Started pills; the secondary actions are beige `#f6f4ef` pills or plain text. See [download-cta](components/crops/download-cta-default.png) and [getstarted-btn](components/crops/getstarted-btn-default.png).
3. **Do paint light.** Every public surface paints the light theme; the app's dark layer is declared (20 switching names, 3 dark-only) but never paints signed out — treat dark values as declared evidence, not a verified surface. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do pair the voices.** The proprietary "Family" face owns every page title, hero and section heading; Inter owns controls and body copy; the app runs variable Inter. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png) and [feature-body](components/crops/feature-body-default.png).
5. **Do use hairlines, not borders, on cards.** Post cards, FAQ items and list rows sit on 1px `#f2f0ed` bottom hairlines over the white field; raised cards (the testimonial panel) go white with a 10px radius and an inset ring. See [blog-card](components/crops/blog-card-default.png) and [tweet-card](components/crops/tweet-card-default.png).
6. **Do keep pills pill-shaped.** CTAs are 48px-tall 32px-radius pills; the FAQ toggle is a 14px mark; the app login field is a transparent borderless input inside a 14px-radius card. See [faq-toggle](components/crops/faq-toggle-default.png) and [login-field](components/crops/login-field-focused.png).
7. **Don't style the signed-in wallet or Aave Account from this package.** The login card was measured as a card — the form was typed and cleared, never submitted; every post-login surface and the iOS app are documented boundaries. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
8. **Don't follow the outbound links.** Log In, Get Started, Download on iOS, Watch the Video and every social/testimonial link were measured, never followed — the capture stays on the public surface. See [watch-video](components/crops/watch-video-default.png) and [tweet-card](components/crops/tweet-card-default.png).
