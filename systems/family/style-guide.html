Family, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the marketing layer declared on family.co (the --body*/--heading inks, the --blue/--orange/--beige brand fills, the --color-* surface roles, all in Display-P3), and the product token layer the app.family.co login declares (the --family-*/--primary-*/--danger-*/--valid-*/--warning-* scales, the --wallet-* palette, the --overlay-*/--input-* roles and the --duration-*/--ease-* motion tokens).
