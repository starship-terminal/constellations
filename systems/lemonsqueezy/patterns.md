# Patterns

How the public pages combine the components. The signed-in app is not in this package.

## Shutdown shell

Every `www.lemonsqueezy.com` page opens with the yellow banner, then the purple nav, then the page, then a purple footer with Privacy, Terms, DPA and the Stripe wordmark. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) shows the shell. Phone width replaces Docs, Help and Sign in with a hamburger. [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png) shows it open.

## Migration homepage

The homepage is a migration notice, not a product tour. One white pill leaves for Stripe registration. Body links leave for Stripe docs or the Lemon Squeezy docs host. There is no pricing table on this page.

## Pricing

[pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) is a purple hero, then two columns: a green fee card (5% + 50¢) and a pink email card with a slider and an annual switch. [pricing-light-faq.png](screenshots/pricing-light-faq.png) is the accordion under that.

## Help

[help-light-default.png](screenshots/help-light-default.png) is three cards (docs, API, guides) plus Message us. [help-light-dialog.png](screenshots/help-light-dialog.png) is the contact overlay. The form was not submitted.

## Account entry

Sign in, Sign up, password reset, and My Orders are four separate walls. [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) is the merchant sign-in. [register-light-default.png](screenshots/register-light-default.png) asks for a business before any account exists. [reset-light-default.png](screenshots/reset-light-default.png) asks for an email and shows a reCAPTCHA. [my-orders-login-light-default.png](screenshots/my-orders-login-light-default.png) is the buyer email-link wall. None were submitted.

## Docs

[docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png) keeps the same banner and nav, then a sidebar index and an Open search control. A query was not typed.
