# Lemon Squeezy design reference

A token set and component reference for Lemon Squeezy's public marketing site and its account-entry walls, for building a payments-marketing page or a sign-in screen in that style. It was read from lemonsqueezy.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 wide and 390 wide. The product is winding down: a yellow banner on every page says new sign-ups are closed and the service shuts down on March 31, 2027. The look is a purple page (`#5423e7`), a yellow shutdown strip (`#ffc233`), white pill buttons, and Circular Pro Book headlines at 80px.

The merchant app and the buyer dashboard sit behind sign-in and were not entered. Nothing was signed into, bought, or submitted. Google and Twitter were not clicked. Checkout on the creators guide was not opened.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page purple and the banner yellow.
2. Do keep the pill at 40px radius.
3. Do set headlines in Circular Pro Book.
4. Don't invent a dark theme.
5. Don't treat the Webflow default blue as the brand.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were resolved on `:root` at https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/ with `getComputedStyle` and written to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The marketing CSS declares 86 names (`--purple-600`, `--ls-color-yellow`, the blue, green, pink, orange, red and yellow ramps). Component boxes were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements and written to [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json). `tools/build_tokens.py` maps those two files into [tokens.json](tokens.json).

The meta `theme-color` is `#5423E7`, the same hex as `--purple-600` and `--ls-purple`.

Login styles come from computed styles on https://auth.lemonsqueezy.com/login. That host's stylesheets are cross-origin, so its custom properties were not readable. The login colours are computed styles only.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- `--black` resolved to the keyword `black`. The token records `#000000`. The painted ink on the marketing site is `--black-2` (`#121217`), not pure black.
- The focus token is a declared ring colour. Keyboard focus on the primary pill did not change its computed outline.
- Status colours are palette ramps. A success, warning, or error component was not shown.
- The help dialog's radius, shadow, and field sizes were not measured. The dialog paints, and the crop shows it, but its node sits outside the page style root.
- Hover on the primary pill did not change the computed background. A stylesheet rule `.button:hover` sets a blue that this pill does not use.
- Reduced motion was not tested.
- Category URLs redirect to the homepage. They are not separate designs.

## Fonts

Inter is loaded from the site's Webflow CDN in weights 400, 500, 600 and 700. Circular Pro Book and Circular XX are loaded as opentype files from the same CDN. JetBrains Mono is loaded and was not seen on a measured text node. The tokens name the families and fall back to sans-serif or ui-monospace. The font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the page purple and the banner yellow.** The hero and nav are `#5423e7`. The shutdown strip is `#ffc233`. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do keep the pill at 40px radius.** The marketing primary button is a 40px-radius white pill, 40px tall. [button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Do set headlines in Circular Pro Book.** The 80px hero is that family at weight 400. Body copy is Inter. [hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site has one theme. A dark set would be a guess.
- **Don't treat the Webflow default blue as the brand.** `.button:hover` in the stylesheet is `rgb(3, 83, 233)`. The pill on the page does not use it. The brand purple is `#5423e7`.
