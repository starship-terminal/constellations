# Patterns

How the public pages combine. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots).

## App shell

A white header with the org-chart mark, Platform and Enrich menus, Pricing, Log in, and a blue Sign up pill. The page ground is off-white. There is no sidebar and no dark theme. Phone width hides the text links and opens a full-height sheet from a 32px header button. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero and chart

The home hero pairs a condensed uppercase headline with a black pill and a white pill, and an org-chart illustration of person cards. Company cards below repeat a logo, a name, a follower count, and industry chips.

## Pricing

Four plan cards in a row. A Yearly / Monthly control sits above them. Get started links to signup. Contact sales opens a form that was not captured. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png).

## Directory

Explore is three indexes: countries, industries, employee ranges. Each index has a search field. Company and people directories are A–Z letter pages. Person profile URLs are a stopped branch.

## Org chart

A company page shows a chart, Teams, and Offices. Follow stays on the page and opens no dialog. Offices on the representative company had no email, phone, or street match, so that state was captured. See [org-five-blocks-light-default.png](screenshots/org-five-blocks-light-default.png).

## Not on the public surface

Empty, loading, and error states of the signed-in app, a destructive confirmation, onboarding, and feedback after an account action. Those sit behind login.
