# The Org design reference

A token set and component reference for The Org's public site. It was read from **theorg.com** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844.

The Org publishes org charts. The public site is a light marketing and directory surface: an off-white page, a navy ink, a blue Sign up pill, and a black hero pill. Styled-components declare a real custom-property layer (`--primary-*`, `--neutral-*`, `--fg-*`, `--bg-*`, `--fs-*`, `--sp-*`). The signed-in product was not entered.

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium.

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) read the stylesheets on the home page, re-parsed them, and resolved each custom property on a probe. Colour, type, spacing, radius, and breakpoint tokens in `core` are those resolutions.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) read computed styles for default, hover, focus-visible, and a CDP-forced pressed state. Button accent colours in `light` come from those readings.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) took viewport and full-page shots. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) wrote 256-colour copies into `screenshots/`.

Nothing was submitted. No email was typed. Google, Apple, and Microsoft were not clicked. Password was not submitted.

## What to distrust

- **One painted theme.** The public site has no dark control. A dark-class probe resolved the same custom properties. There is no `dark` set.
- **Sign up blue is measured, not `--primary-100`.** The declared primary is `#0f4be5`. The Sign up pill computed to `rgb(23, 84, 207)`. `light.accent` records the computed colour.
- **Pressed states are CDP `:active`.** No pointer was held down, so a link could not navigate.
- **Info and focus are Not captured.** Info custom properties resolve empty. Measured controls did not paint a distinct focus outline.
- **Quantised screenshots.** Read colours from tokens.json, not from the PNGs.
- **Footer link colour.** The About link computed to `rgb(204, 204, 204)` because the measurement included a wide footer region. Do not treat that as the footer text colour.

## Fonts

`--font-primary` is Inter. `--font-secondary` is PP Right Grotesk, with Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, and sans-serif. DMSans is also declared as a `@font-face` and is the computed body family on the home page. The webfont files are not committed. Licences were not read. Not captured.

## Do and don't

- **One blue pill.** Sign up is the only blue control in the header. Other primary actions are black pills. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Menus are quiet dialogs.** Platform and Enrich open a white dialog of text links, not a mega-menu of cards. See [home-light-platform-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-platform-menu.png).
- **Phone navigation is a sheet.** The text nav collapses. A header button opens a white sheet with Sign up at the bottom. See [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** The public site paints light only.
- **Do not screenshot a page that shows an email, a phone, or a street address.** Contact, terms, privacy, and the letter index were left out for that reason.
