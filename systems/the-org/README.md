# The Org

Public marketing and signed-out auth for [theorg.com](https://theorg.com/), captured on 2026-11-19. The Org publishes org charts. This package covers the homepage, pricing, about, contact, a public company page, the 404, and the login and signup forms as they appear before anything is submitted. No account was created. Themes: light only. Widths: 1440 and 390 CSS pixels.

### Principles

1. One blue, and it is the Sign up pill.
2. Display type is uppercase and tight.
3. Cards are white, 16px radius, hairline border.
4. The footer is a near-black band.
5. Auth stays a single column of 350px controls.

## How the values were read

Computed styles and the CSSOM were read in the T3 preview tab on the live pages, signed out, at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. Custom properties on `:root` and `html` were walked from the stylesheets. Component values are `getComputedStyle` on the element named in each token. Screenshots and crops were taken with headless Playwright after the same public URLs had already loaded in the preview tab without a Cloudflare challenge. The preview tab itself returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so those PNGs are the Playwright copies, not preview frames.

## What to distrust

The per-element readings were not saved. [raw/components.json](raw/components.json) holds only the viewport and a note, so 90 tokens read from computed styles (the named colours, type, weights, component sizes, shadows and motion) are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time. The 124 values declared as custom properties in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) stay Measured.

The painted Sign up blue, rgb(23, 84, 207), is not `--primary-100` (`#0F4BE5`). The hover blue, rgb(15, 73, 189), is not `--primary-100-hover` (`#0515d4`). Body type computes as DMSans, not the declared `--font-primary` Inter. Inter was measured only on the auth inputs. `--card-box-shadow` is a four-layer stack. The trending cards computed a smaller shadow. The homepage footer computed 322px tall. `--height-footer` is 205px. Sonner declares a dark toast theme. It was not on screen, so there is no dark token set. Screenshots are full colour. Do not sample tokens from them.

## Fonts

DMSans is the computed UI face, a variable font from weight 100 to 1000, loaded on the page. PP Right Grotesk is the display face on the homepage h1, weight 900 loaded, with a fallback family registered. Inter is declared as `--font-primary` and computed on the auth inputs. PP Right Grotesk is proprietary and is not shipped here. DMSans is recorded by the name the page used. The tokens fall back to the system sans where a face cannot be loaded.

## Do and don't

- **One blue, and it is the Sign up pill.** Secondary actions are a dark pill or a white pill with a hairline. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Display type is uppercase and tight.** The homepage headline is PP Right Grotesk at 72px with a 68.4px line height. Page titles elsewhere are DMSans at 66px and are not uppercase. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Cards are white, 16px radius, hairline border.** The chart card is the exception at 12px with a ring shadow. [org-google-light-default.png](screenshots/org-google-light-default.png)
- **The footer is a near-black band.** Links are rgb(204, 204, 204) on rgb(23, 25, 28). [missing-light-default.png](screenshots/missing-light-default.png)
- **Auth stays a single column of 350px controls.** Identity buttons match the field width and were not followed. [components/crops/text-field-default.png](components/crops/text-field-default.png)
