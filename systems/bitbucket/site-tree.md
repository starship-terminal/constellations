Nothing was typed. No email, password, code, phone, or address was entered. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. No account was created. No repository was created. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

Query strings that carry a campaign were stripped from the recorded URL.
