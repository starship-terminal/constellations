# Starbucks

Public signed-out marketing surfaces of Starbucks Coffee Company, captured on 2026-11-19 from https://www.starbucks.com/ with no account. Desktop captures are a 1440 by 900 CSS viewport at device pixel ratio 2. Phone captures are a 390 by 844 CSS viewport at device pixel ratio 2. The site paints one theme. `color-scheme` is `normal`, and a dark `prefers-color-scheme` left the page light.

The T3 preview tab loaded the home page and supplied the custom properties and the first computed styles. It then stopped producing screenshot frames, so the screenshots and the rest of the computed styles come from headless Chromium on the same public URLs, which returned 200 with no Cloudflare challenge.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on the home page in the T3 preview tab at 1440 by 900, after the page settled. Computed component styles were read the same way, then again in headless Chromium on the same URLs: home, menu, a menu category, rewards, gift, the cafe locator, contact, terms, privacy, careers (which redirects to careers.starbucks.com), a missing route, and the sign-in URL. Hover was a real pointer hover. Focus was keyboard Tab until the control was the active element. The root font size on the marketing site is 10px, so a `rem` there is 10px. Careers uses a 16px root.

Nothing was typed. No account was created. No card, phone number, or payment was entered. The cookie banner's Agree button was pressed so later shots were not covered. Sign-in and Join now were not followed into a form.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from the tokens. The preview tab and headless Chromium agreed on the home custom properties and on Sign in, Join now, and the hero band. Later pages were measured only in headless Chromium.

`--radius-lg` is `0.5rem`. At the marketing root that is 5px. The careers search field measured 8px, and that measured value is the `sm` radius token. `--radius-4xl` is `2rem` (20px at the marketing root). The rewards and gift cards measured 32px and 40px, and those measured values are the card tokens.

The sign-in URL returned the marketing footer and no form. The screenshot named `signin-light-shell` is that shell, not a sign-in form. Careers is a separate site with a serif display face. Its values are labelled as careers, not as the marketing system.

`ease` control points are the CSS specification values for the keyword. The stylesheet declared the keyword, not the bezier.

## Fonts

SoDoSans is the marketing face, weights 400, 600 and 700, files `SoDoSans-Regular.woff`, `SoDoSans-SemiBold.woff` and `SoDoSans-Bold.woff` on the site. The computed stack is `SoDoSans, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Specimens fall back to Helvetica Neue.

Careers also loads `SoDo Sans` (a spaced family name) and `Starbucks Serif` (`StarbucksSerif-Regular.woff2` and a bold and italic). LanderTall is declared on the marketing site and was not used by a measured element. None of these files are committed.

## Do and don't

**Use the pill for every action.** Sign in, Join now, and the feature buttons are 50px radius, 7px by 16px padding, SoDoSans 600. See [components/crops/button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png).

**Put black behind the account action and green behind the product action.** Join now in the header is black on white text. The rewards Join now is `#00754a`. See [screenshots/rewards-light-default.png](screenshots/rewards-light-default.png).

**Set nav labels in caps with wide tracking.** MENU, REWARDS and GIFT CARDS are 14px, weight 700, 1.4px tracking. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

**Paint bands, not cards, on the home page.** Each feature is a full-bleed colour band (green `#006242`, cream `#f5f1eb`, holiday red `#bf0d3f`) with a photograph and one pill. See [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

**Do not invent a dark theme.** The public site does not ship one.

**Do not treat careers type as the marketing system.** The careers h1 is Starbucks Serif at 49px. The marketing h1s are SoDoSans.
