# Starbucks design reference

A token set and component reference for the public Starbucks consumer site (www.starbucks.com), for building marketing, menu, and rewards surfaces in that style. Captured signed out on 2026-11-21 in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. One light theme. Sign-in and sign-up were opened only far enough to record the empty walls at auth.starbucks.com. No email, password, card number, location, or form was typed or submitted.

The public site is green (#006242) and white, with a second green (#00754a) on links and the order button, black pills for Join now, and SoDo Sans. Corners on chrome buttons are pills. The order button carries a soft shadow. There is no dark theme on the pages visited.

## How the values were read

Custom properties on `:root` were counted and came back empty. Colours, type, radii, padding, and the order-button shadow were read with `getComputedStyle` on the home page, the Caffè Latte product page, and the Auth0 sign-in wall. The readings are in [raw/measured.json](raw/measured.json), [raw/measured2.json](raw/measured2.json), and [raw/measured3.json](raw/measured3.json). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) turns [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json) into tokens.json.

The cookie banner was hidden by aborting TrustArc and setting `display: none`. Agree was never clicked.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- The sign-in email field was focused, so its border is the focused border, not the resting one. The password field is the resting reading.
- `light.focus.ring` is the accent colour. A keyboard focus ring was not isolated.
- SoDo Sans is named from computed `fontFamily`. The file is not in this package.
- Hover was captured for Join now and Add to Order. A colour change was not measured on those hovers, so no hover token was added.
- The product hero green and the Customize fill were both read as rgb(30, 57, 50) on the button. The hero fill was not separately sampled.
- Contact, the health notice, and the 404 were loaded and then left out of screenshots because they show published emails, phone numbers, or a request id.

## Fonts

SoDo Sans (also computed as "SoDo Sans" on the Auth0 host) is proprietary. The style guide falls back to Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.

## Do and don't

- **Do set chrome on white and bands on green.** The header is white and the order strip is #006242. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do pill the account actions.** Sign in is an outline pill and Join now is a black pill. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do use the second green for links and the order button.** #00754a, not the band green. [product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png)
- **Do keep nav in uppercase with wide tracking.** Menu, Rewards, Gift cards. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Don't fill a sign-in field to capture it.** The wall is empty on purpose. [signin-light-wall.png](screenshots/signin-light-wall.png)
- **Don't screenshot the store locator.** It lists street addresses. Mapped in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/starbucks
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

Capture, from the repo root, with Playwright:

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node systems/starbucks/tools/capture/public/public.mjs
python3 systems/starbucks/tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/starbucks/raw-shots
```
