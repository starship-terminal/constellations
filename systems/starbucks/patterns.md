# Patterns

Public signed-out pages only. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/).

## Home

A transparent header, then a green band (`light.background.hero`) with "It's a great day for coffee" and a white-bordered "Start an order" pill. Below that, full-bleed feature bands alternate cream, holiday red, and green. Each band is a photograph plus one heading at 24px / 600 and one pill. The footer is five columns (About Us, Careers, Social Impact, For Business Partners, Ways to Shop) at 19px / 400, then a muted copyright line.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png), [screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png).

## Menu

An h1 "Menu" at 28px / 700, then "The Latest" and category groups (Trending, Holiday Classics). A category page such as Hot Coffee and Espresso is a grid of product links, about 247px wide, photograph plus name, no card chrome. "Start an order" and product links were not followed into an order.

[screenshots/menu-light-default.png](screenshots/menu-light-default.png), [screenshots/menu-category-light-default.png](screenshots/menu-category-light-default.png).

## Rewards

A cream hero, an eyebrow "STARBUCKS REWARDS" in kraft brown, a 50px / 700 headline, and a green Join now. Further down, star tabs and partner cards at 32px radius. Join now was not followed.

[screenshots/rewards-light-default.png](screenshots/rewards-light-default.png), [screenshots/rewards-light-full.png](screenshots/rewards-light-full.png).

## Gift

A mint hero, eyebrow "STARBUCKS GIFT CARDS" at 14px / 700, headline "Gift the perfect cup" at 50px / 700, then gift art and utility cards. Shop and checkout were not entered.

[screenshots/gift-light-default.png](screenshots/gift-light-default.png).

## Cafe locator

A split page: a search field and a Pickup / Delivery segment on the left, a map on the right. The empty state reads "Zoomed out too far". No location was typed and no store was opened.

[screenshots/store-locator-light-default.png](screenshots/store-locator-light-default.png).

## Contact, terms, privacy

A single column. Contact h1 is 36px / 600. Terms and privacy are the same type on a long page. Full-page shots of terms and privacy were taken and not kept in the package because the pages run past 28,000px. The viewport shots show the type.

[screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png), [screenshots/terms-light-default.png](screenshots/terms-light-default.png), [screenshots/privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).

## 404

The marketing header and footer with one sentence. [screenshots/not-found-light-default.png](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png).

## Careers

`/careers` redirects to careers.starbucks.com. That site uses Starbucks Serif for the h1 ("Your future, freshly brewed.", 49px / 400, green) and pill buttons at 999px radius. It is a public page and a different system. [screenshots/careers-light-default.png](screenshots/careers-light-default.png).
