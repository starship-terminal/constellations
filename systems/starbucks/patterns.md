# Starbucks patterns

Public consumer site, signed out, captured 2026-11-21 at desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844. One light theme. The map of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## App shell

The marketing header is a white bar, 100px tall, with the siren, uppercase nav (Menu, Rewards, Gift cards, Starbucks Shop), Find a café, a Sign in outline pill, and a black Join now pill. Phone width folds that nav into the Open menu drawer: [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Home

A green strip ("It's a great day for coffee") sits under the header, then alternating photo and cream or green panels. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), full page [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Menu to product

The menu is a category index with circular photos: [menu-light-default.png](screenshots/menu-light-default.png). A category is a product grid: [menu-category-light-default.png](screenshots/menu-category-light-default.png). A product opens on a deep green hero, size radios, selects, Customize, and Add to Order: [product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png). Add to Order was not submitted.

## Rewards and gift

Rewards is a long marketing page with star-tier buttons: [rewards-light-default.png](screenshots/rewards-light-default.png). Gift cards are a carousel plus a Check balance drawer with empty card-number and security-code fields: [gift-light-balance-dialog.png](screenshots/gift-light-balance-dialog.png). Those fields were not filled.

## Login and signup walls

Sign in leaves www.starbucks.com for auth.starbucks.com and shows an empty email and password form on a green ground: [signin-light-wall.png](screenshots/signin-light-wall.png). Join now is the same ground with an empty create-account form: [signup-light-wall.png](screenshots/signup-light-wall.png). Nothing was typed or submitted.

## Empty and error

Favorites and Previous are empty signed-out states: [menu-favorites-light-empty.png](screenshots/menu-favorites-light-empty.png). The styled 404 was reached and then left out of screenshots because it prints a request id.

## Not captured

Store locator (street addresses). Signed-in account, order history, checkout, and payment. Dark theme (not present). Forms were not submitted, so validation errors are not captured.
