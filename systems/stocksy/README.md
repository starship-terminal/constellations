# Stocksy

Stocksy is an artist-owned stock photo, video and illustration library at [stocksy.com](https://www.stocksy.com/). This package records the public signed-out site on 2026-11-19. No account was created. Nothing was purchased.

The capture used the T3 preview browser. Computed styles were read from the live page at 1440 by 900, once at the fill width 1169 by 731, and on the home at 390 by 844. `devicePixelRatio` was 2. The preview browser did not produce screenshot frames. Headless Playwright was stopped by Cloudflare, so this package has no screenshots.

## Health

## How the values were read

Custom properties on `:root` are Bootstrap `--bs-*` and the cookie dialog's `--cc-*`. Stocksy's own colours are literal `rgb()` in `primary-site.*.css`, with a few `var(--color-border-default, #292929)` fallbacks. Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live page. Stylesheet rules were read from `document.styleSheets` for hover colours, motion and `@font-face`.

Pages: home, `/pricing`, `/search/photo?text=ocean`, one photo page, `/category/healthcare`, `/service/about/`, `/business`, `/resources`, a made-up 404, and `/user/login` as a gate. Cookie Accept All was clicked after the first home read so the dialog would not cover later measures. No other control that changes account state was pressed.

## What to distrust

There are no screenshots. Do not treat the style guide as a picture of the site.

Hover colours (`#28d080` on the green button, and the danger, warning and orange button fills) come from stylesheet rules. They were not forced on a live control.

The live search focus showed no outline and no shadow. The stylesheet still has Bootstrap's blue focus ring. Both are recorded. The live one is what the search field did.

`.text05` line-height was not sampled on a live heading. The token uses 32px from the 1.5rem body rhythm. That part is a gap.

The first home read was at 1169 by 731, the fill viewport, before the resize to 1440. Do not call that pass a 1440 capture.

## Fonts

Poppins, weights 100 to 900, normal and italic, self-hosted as woff2 and woff at `/dist/fonts/poppins-latin-*`. The family is on the SIL Open Font License. The files are not in this package. The tokens fall back to `system-ui, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial, Roboto, sans-serif`. The style guide loads Poppins from Google Fonts so the specimens can render. That hosted file is not the site's woff2.

## Do and don't

- **Use the black page and the green pill.** Home, search, pricing and the photo page are `body.dark-mode` with `#0ae47c` on Add to Cart and the search submit.
- **Keep inputs white.** Search, login and the business form use a white field on the dark page, with a `#cecece` border.
- **Use Poppins, including the light hero.** Desktop headlines are weight 300, not a bold display face.
- **Don't invent a screenshot.** This package has none. The style guide is a specimen, not evidence of layout.
- **Don't follow Add to Cart, Log in, or the business request.** Those leave the public browse. The business form asks for a phone number.
