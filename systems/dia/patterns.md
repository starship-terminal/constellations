# Dia patterns

The public site is a cream page with a centered white nav pill. The homepage is the exception: a yellow hero, a serif headline in white, a product illustration, and a black download pill on a white curve.

Inner pages keep the same pill and put the headline in black Exposure on cream. Apps adds a row of featured cards. Security uses a left link list and a long article. Download is a short centered page: icon, headline, one black button, one line of platform text.

The phone drawer replaces the pill links with a full-height white sheet. It does not add account links.

There is no signed-in shell in this package. Login and signup are not public pages.
