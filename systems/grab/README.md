# Grab design reference

A token set and component reference for Grab, Grab Holdings' Southeast Asian super app for rides, food delivery, payments and offers, captured from its iOS app. Use it to build a native iOS service app that borrows its proportions and structure: a white page under a coloured service header, one green for every primary action, pale mint behind secondary controls, dark-teal selected chips, capsule buttons and a tab bar with a raised scan button in the middle.

It holds 24 captures from the owner's signed-in account, made through iPhone Mirroring on 2026-10-23: Home, the ride flow up to the Book button (not pressed), a Dine Out merchant page, Activity, Messages with a permission pre-prompt, Moments, the account page, dietary preferences, search, GrabFood (home, flash deals sheet, empty cart, Offer Zone, a pickup promo and the Pickup map list). Grab has no dark UI: it stays light with the phone in dark mode, so every capture and token is light. The owner's account name stays. On 2026-11-21, for the public repository, other people's names and profile photos (two posters on Moments, a reviewer on the merchant page) were painted out of `08` and `13` with [tools/redact_images.py](../../tools/redact_images.py) and [its rules](../../tools/redact-rules/grab.json); merchants, restaurants and post photos stay. The owner's home address and postcode, other saved addresses with postcodes, the map centred on the owner's home block and its Home pin label, trip titles that start with the home address and the payment card's last digits are painted over in every screenshot and crop ([tools/masks.json](tools/masks.json)).

This package contains no Grab code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Grab's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the 3D service icons, illustrations, logo or brand green.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do give every full-width primary action Grab green.
2. Do mark the selected option in dark teal, not green.
3. Do give each service its own header colour over a white page.
4. Do say status in coloured text, not a filled badge.
5. Do show focus with a darker green border and a soft halo.
6. Don't leave a disabled primary button green.
7. Don't copy white text on Grab green into an accessible design.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Grab is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB), 812 x 1780 px with the 390 x 844 pt screen at 2 px per pt. `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 106 named readings. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens and names the reading behind each.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (a gradient, a thin line, a small mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height, and the settings row height |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | Both font families |

Masking came after reading. The sRGB copies outside the repo had already had a first pass of masking painted on them before this package was built (some regions pixelated, some filled); no reading box sits inside any masked region, and the unmasked Display P3 originals (`.raw.png`) are kept beside them. `tools/capture/quantize.py` then paints every box in [tools/masks.json](tools/masks.json) with a flat colour before quantising, so the committed screenshots and every crop cut from them are masked. A whole map counts as one span: the map behind the pickup sheet (06) and the Pickup map strip (24) are flat grey with the floating controls kept. Text spans are filled with the surface colour around them, smallest span only, with no substitute text.

Scale steps (spacing, radii, the 40 pt field) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Scrims are Derived as black at the alpha that turns the undimmed reading into the dimmed one. The flash-deals sheet's backdrop is a navy veil that no black alpha reproduces, so it is described in `core` `$extensions.notTokenised` instead of being a token.

## What to distrust

- **The fonts.** Not determinable from pixels. Body text, rows, labels and buttons use a neo-grotesque close to Inter; titles (Messages, Activity, For more value, sheet and empty-state titles) use a rounded geometric display face that is Grab's own and is not identified here. Both `fontFamily` tokens are `system` and marked Not captured.
- **Brand green fails contrast with white text.** The primary button is `#02B150` with a white label, about 2.8:1, below even the 3:1 minimum for large text, so a bigger or bolder label does not fix it. The token keeps the product's value; when reusing the pairing in an accessible design, change one of the colours (white on `color.brand.forest` `#05804B` is about 5.0:1).
- **Gradients.** The Home header runs from `#034C35` to `#00322A`, and the Pickup header from green to teal; only the Home ends were read (`bg.header`, `bg.headerEnd`, Derived). Promo cards on Home are tinted gradients too.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: tooltip 8.5, search field 12, cards 15.4, sheets 24, capsules on buttons and chips.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em, which fits neither family exactly. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Two greens and a teal do different jobs.** Grab green `#02B150` fills full-width buttons; forest `#05804B` is the tab underline and the compact Buy button; deep `#025339` is the floating button and the tooltip; teal `#174440` is the selected chip and the scan button. They are four readings, not one ramp.
- **The neutral ramp is a list.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the app uses, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published ramp.
- **Masked text styles.** The masked strings are list titles, meta lines and card values whose styles also appear unmasked elsewhere in the same captures (for example the School of Computing rows in 04 and 17), so no text style is known only from a masked string.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs, no switches, no loading.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; the account page would not scroll to Settings, and every screen loaded before a capture could run. The toast after answering Later had gone before its capture.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs** with masked regions painted flat. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do give every full-width primary action Grab green.** Yes, Book Standard, Choose this pickup and Let's go are full-width green capsules with white labels; the second action is a pale mint capsule, never a second green one. See [11-notification-preprompt-sheet-yes-later](screenshots/light/11-notification-preprompt-sheet-yes-later.png), [07-ride-options-book-button-not-pressed](screenshots/light/07-ride-options-book-button-not-pressed.png) and [23-pickup-promo-interstitial](screenshots/light/23-pickup-promo-interstitial.png).
2. **Do mark the selected option in dark teal, not green.** The selected filter chip and service chip are dark teal with a white label; the others are mint with a teal label. See [17-search-focused-chips-recent-pills](screenshots/light/17-search-focused-chips-recent-pills.png) and [20-food-home-chips-categories-tabbar](screenshots/light/20-food-home-chips-categories-tabbar.png).
3. **Do give each service its own header colour over a white page.** Home is deep green, GrabFood blue, Transport and Offer Zone peach, Pickup green to teal; everything under the header stays white. See [01-home-tiles-search](screenshots/light/01-home-tiles-search.png), [20-food-home-chips-categories-tabbar](screenshots/light/20-food-home-chips-categories-tabbar.png) and [22-offer-zone-grid](screenshots/light/22-offer-zone-grid.png).
4. **Do say status in coloured text, not a filled badge.** Cancelled is red and Bill paid green, above the trip title; Open is green beside the closing time. See [10-activity-list-status-labels](screenshots/light/10-activity-list-status-labels.png) and [08-merchant-page-tabs-voucher-buy](screenshots/light/08-merchant-page-tabs-voucher-buy.png).
5. **Do show focus with a darker green border and a soft halo.** The focused field gets both; the unfocused field above keeps its grey border. See [04-destination-focused-recent-tabs](screenshots/light/04-destination-focused-recent-tabs.png) and [crop](components/crops/text-field-focused.png).
6. **Don't leave a disabled primary button green.** Save turns light grey with a grey label until a choice is made. See [16-checkboxes-disabled-save](screenshots/light/16-checkboxes-disabled-save.png).
7. **Don't copy white text on Grab green into an accessible design.** The product's buttons pair them at about 2.8:1, below the 3:1 minimum even for large text; keep the captured colours as a record and darken the green (or change the label colour) when you reuse it. See [button-primary-default](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` and `quantize.py` need the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/grab/raw-shots/light/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/grab/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/grab/raw-shots` (masks, then quantises) and `tools/crops.py` both need Pillow, and rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. After a re-quantise, run `python3 tools/redact_images.py apply systems/grab --rules tools/redact-rules/grab.json` from the repository root (it keeps each image's palette and every pixel outside the boxes), so the names stay masked. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

One colour asset per semantic key maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system tab bar and sheets with these colours rather than redrawing them, and keep the scan button as a raised circle in the bar's centre. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light theme, the only one.
