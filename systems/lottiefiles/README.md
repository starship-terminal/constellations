# LottieFiles design reference

A token set and component reference for the public LottieFiles marketing site. Read from **lottiefiles.com** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

LottieFiles publishes lightweight animations. The public site is a Next.js app on Tailwind v4 and a shadcn token set. It paints one light theme. The stylesheet also ships a `.dark` block. No public control switched to it, so the dark set is that declaration resolved by adding `html.dark`, not a live paint. Body type is Inter LF. The primary control is a teal pill. Pricing plan buttons use a second blue and a second teal that the marketing token set does not name.

The editor, the dashboard and checkout sit behind sign-in. They were not opened.

### Principles

1. Use the teal for the one primary action.
2. Keep Log in quiet.
3. Do not paint pricing plans with the marketing teal.
4. Do not invent a live dark theme.
5. Do not follow Sign up, Log in or a plan button from this package.
6. Keep the page white and the type near-black.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Foundation colours are the custom properties on `:root` and `.dark`, harvested by [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) from the live stylesheets and resolved in the browser. Light is the theme the public site painted. Dark is the same names after `html.dark` was added so the stylesheet block would resolve. Component fills come from [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs). Screenshots come from [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs). The cookie banner and a Google one-tap sheet were hidden. Consent was never clicked. Log in, Sign up, Download, checkout and app.lottiefiles.com were never followed.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens, not from the images.
- The committed readings and screenshots were taken with `navigator.webdriver` hidden by the capture scripts (`public/lib.mjs`, `public/public.mjs`, `vars.mjs`). The mask has been removed from them (2026-11 audit); a bot check is now a boundary, not something to get past.
- The font weight of each `core.typography` role was typed in by hand at capture time, as the Tailwind step the role appears to use. No element's weight was read for it, so those tokens are labelled Reconstruction.
- The Download tab entry in [components.md](components.md) was written by hand from [animation-light-download-tab.png](screenshots/animation-light-download-tab.png). No values were read for it.
- The dark set was not painted by the public site. It is the stylesheet's `.dark` block.
- Pricing plan buttons (blue `#0077bb`, teal `#017a71`, ink) are measured component colours. They are not the teal `--primary` token.
- Hover on the teal control computes `oklab(... / 0.9)`. That alpha form is in the component readings. The token keeps the resting `--primary`.
- Pressed states are `CSS.forcePseudoState`. No pointer followed a link.
- Full-page shots taller than 8 tiles are truncated. These pages fit in that cap: desktop home is 12588 px and pricing is 11918 px. Phone home is 30848 px and phone pricing is 43542 px, both stitched, not cut.
- The animation page sets a min-width that grew the phone layout to 459 px while the visual viewport stayed 390. The capture pinned `html, body` to 390 px. The sidecar still records document width 459.
- A Google one-tap sheet appeared on the first phone home shot. It was hidden and the phone pages were recaptured. It was not used.
- The Download tab was already selected. Clicking it did not open a new panel. The format buttons are in [animation-light-download-tab.png](screenshots/animation-light-download-tab.png).

## Fonts

Inter LF, DM Sans LF, Noto Sans JP LF and Noto Sans KR LF are named in the stylesheets. They are proprietary faces served by lottiefiles.com. The tokens name them with generic fallbacks. The font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

1. **Use the teal for the one primary action.** Sign up and Get started for free share `--primary`. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Keep Log in quiet.** It is a white control with ink text, next to the teal Sign up. See [components/crops/nav-login-default.png](components/crops/nav-login-default.png).
3. **Do not paint pricing plans with the marketing teal.** Individual is blue, Team is a second teal, Contact Sales is ink. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
4. **Do not invent a live dark theme.** The public site stayed light. The dark tokens are the stylesheet block only.
5. **Do not follow Sign up, Log in or a plan button from this package.** Those open the editor, the dashboard or checkout, which were not captured.
6. **Keep the page white and the type near-black.** The hero is a large ink heading on the page, with the illustration beside it. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
