# Womp

Womp is browser-based 3D modeling software at [womp.com](https://www.womp.com/). This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out chrome around the editor, captured on 2026-11-19. The marketing pages paint a light KUI theme (`data-accent-color="gray"`). The footer is the same theme with a `dark` class. Desktop shots are 1440 CSS px. Phone shots are 390 CSS px. No account was created.

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export womp` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use the near-black pill for the primary action.
2. Set display type in Inter Tight at 64px / 70px, weight 600, tracking -1.6px.
3. Keep the header white, 64px, with a 1px black 9% hairline.
4. Put a dark band only where the site puts one.
5. Leave the wordmark as artwork.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on `https://womp.com/`, `https://womp.com/pricing/`, `https://beta.womp.com/auth` and a public print preview. The same marketing pages were opened again in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844 after they loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. Custom properties were read from `.kui-theme` and `.kui-theme.dark`. Display-p3 greys were converted to sRGB hex and checked against the rgb() values Chromium reports (`0.125` reads as `#202020`, `0.067` reads as `#111111`, `0.849` reads as `rgb(217, 217, 217)`).

The preview browser refused screenshots with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". The PNGs are the headless captures of pages that had already loaded. Viewport, device pixel ratio and visual viewport are in [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

## What to distrust

The accent on the marketing site is gray, so the solid button is near-black on white, not a brand hue. The wordmark is the only strong colour in the chrome, and it is artwork, not a token. Auth and the print preview use Radix (`rt-*`) with a blue accent on a help icon. That blue was measured on the auth root (`--accent-9` is `color(display-p3 0.247 0.556 0.969)`) and is not the marketing accent.

Chip colours for "Save 25%" and "Most popular" were read as display-p3 and oklab. They are named in the gaps rather than converted into tokens. Hover, pressed, disabled and error states were not forced. A focus ring was read by focusing buttons in the preview tab.

## Fonts

Inter is the UI face, Inter Tight is the display face, and JetBrains Mono is the eyebrow and price face. The site loads them itself. The tokens keep the fallback stacks that computed style returned. The style guide loads the same three families from Google Fonts so the specimens can render. Those files are not vendored here.

## Do and don't

- **Use the near-black pill for the primary action.** The solid button is `#202020` on `#FCFCFC`, 40px tall, 20px radius, weight 500. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Set display type in Inter Tight at 64px / 70px, weight 600, tracking -1.6px.** Headings on the home and pricing pages share that step. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Keep the header white, 64px, with a 1px black 9% hairline.** Links are 16px Inter. The outline button sits between Log in and the solid button.
- **Put a dark band only where the site puts one.** The footer is `#111111` with `#EEEEEE` text. The rest of the marketing page stays white.
- **Leave the wordmark as artwork.** Do not sample a brand blue from it and apply that blue to buttons.
