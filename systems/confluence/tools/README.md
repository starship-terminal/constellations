# How this package was captured

2026-11-22. Public pages only.

Headless Chromium (Playwright at `~/.design-systems/pw`) opened https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence and the header and footer destinations. Viewport 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, device pixel ratio 2. Screenshots were quantised to 256 colours.

Sign in was photographed at https://id.atlassian.com/login with an empty email field. Get it free was photographed at the empty Confluence signup wall. Continue, Sign up, Passkey, Google, Microsoft, Apple, and Slack were not clicked. No form was submitted. No site was created. Start free trial and Contact sales were not clicked. The cookie banner was hidden with CSS and not accepted.

Impressum printed a street address and a phone number. That screenshot was discarded.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so `make design-md` and `make brief` were not run for this package.
