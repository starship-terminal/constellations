Palette

The core set is the site's own variable layer read off :root and the darkscope rules: the Kernel-Black / Blank-Canvas-White axis, the Grey ramp, the Neon-Loop action pink with its hover, the Syntax-Glow tint, the Startup-Lime / Seedling-Spark / Vision-Violet / Founder-Forest accents and the three named gradients. globalColor is the declared --e-global-color-* kit palette. Light roles alias the palette; the dark set is the island re-binding, not a user toggle.

Spacing and radius

spacePrimitive is the measured pixel ladder (the 8px grid runs 8-160); spaceScale names the section padding bands small through large. Radius runs fields at 4px, controls and plan cards at 8px, cards at 16px and the floating header, mega-menu and pills at 24px.

Shadows and motion

One measured shadow: the floating header's 5% black 15px blur with a 3px spread. Motion is short - 0.2s ease on button fills, 0.3s on links and toggles, 0.5s on panels.

Type

Roobert everywhere the surface is styled: 88/96.8 600 display with -0.88px tracking on the hero, 72/79.2 on the FAQ page, 24/31.2 section titles, 18px/500 buttons with -0.36px, 16/22.4 nav and fields, 14px lists and footer. Roboto serves the editor-side chrome. Samples fall back to system sans.
