# GOAT components

What was visible on the signed-out shop on 2026-11-22, and what was not measured.

## Header

50px bar. Homepage: black, white labels, hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`. Interior: white, black labels, hairline `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`. Search is a text button with a rotating suggestion beside it. Center nav: Home, Shop, Editorial, Timeline. Account is 12px, weight 300, 46 by 38. No logo image.

## Shop menu

Opens on hover under Shop. 564 by 311, black on the homepage, padding 20px 24px, no shadow. Column headings are 9.5px. Links are 11.5px, weight 300, 25px row with 8px bottom padding. Three columns: Categories, Collections, Featured Brands. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Homepage rail

Section title is 11.5px, weight 300, shown in capitals (JUST DROPPED). The count link sits at the right (206 Items). Product thumbs in the rail are about 90 by 82. The page background is black.

## Category

Sneakers title is 11.5px, weight 700, uppercase. The blurb beside it is 11.5px, weight 400, 418px wide, and its product names are underlined links. A sort list and filter labels sit at the left: Popular, Brand, Category, Gender, Size, and the rest. Product cells show a date, a name, and a price (S$237 on the first cell). Cells have a 1px divider.

## Product

The photograph is a large shoe on white, with a right-arrow control. Under it: year, title (11.5px, weight 300), a breadcrumb, and a size row. Each size is a number over a price or the word Offer. Buy New and Make Offer are 12px, weight 300, transparent background. They were not clicked. A Facts panel can open over the image. It was closed before the photograph.

## About

A left list of policy links, 176px wide. The title ABOUT US is 11.5px, weight 600, uppercase. Body copy is 11.5px, weight 400, line-height 14px, in a column about 418px wide. The parent-company link is underlined.

## Footer

Padding 64px 32px 24px. Column labels About, Buy, Sell, Support are 12.5px, weight 300. The bottom row is Terms, Privacy, Cookies Settings, Location Preferences, Accessibility, Sitemap, then the copyright line. The email wrapper is 358 by 49, 1px `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)` border, radius 1px. Sign Up is 77 by 32, black fill, white 11px label, radius 1px. Not clicked.

## Gaps

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured. Phone width was not captured. No error state was shown. Account, search submit, Buy New, Make Offer, and Sign Up were not opened.
