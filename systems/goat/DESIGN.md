## Geometry

The header is 50px, inner padding 12px 32px 0px. Nav links are 38px tall. The shop menu is 564 by 311, padding 20px 24px. Sign Up is 77 by 32, padding 7px 18px, radius 1px. The email wrapper is 358 by 49, radius 1px.

## Components

Home, Shop, Editorial, and Timeline sit in the center of the header. Search sits at the left. Account sits at the right. The homepage is a black field of product images under tracked section titles. Interior pages are white. A product page puts the shoe on white, with a size row and Buy New under it. Buy New was not clicked.
