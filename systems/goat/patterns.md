# GOAT patterns

Signed-out pages photographed on 2026-11-22.

## Header

The header is a thin bar, 50px, with a hairline under it. Search is at the left, the four section links are centered, and Account is at the right. The homepage paints this bar black. Sneakers, the product, and About paint it white. The same labels change color with the bar.

Shop opens a three-column menu. On the homepage that menu is black, sitting on the black page, with a light border. Column headings are smaller than the links (9.5px against 11.5px).

## Homepage

The homepage is a black catalog. A section title and an item count share one row. Under that, shoes sit in a tight grid with no card chrome and no prices in the first screen. The next section, NEW IN: SUPREME FW26, starts the same way, then switches to named goods with a year.

## Category

Sneakers is a white page. The title, a short underlined blurb, and "10,000+ Items" share the top band. Filters are a quiet right-aligned list at the left edge. The grid is four columns of product cells. Each cell leads with the date, the name, and the price, then the shoe.

## Product

The product page is mostly the shoe. The title, breadcrumb, and size prices sit in one row under the image. Buy New and Make Offer sit under that row. A size that has no ask says Offer. Selecting a size, Buy New, and Make Offer were not clicked.

## About

About is a policy page. The link list is on the left. The article is a narrow column near the center. The footer, including the empty email field, is in the same photograph. Sign Up was not clicked.
