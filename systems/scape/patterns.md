# Patterns

Public pages only. Desktop shots are 1440x900. Phone shots are 390x844 at 2x.

## Marketing home

Pink announcement bar, then a 72px header over a full-bleed photo. The wordmark is pink. Nav is cream. Find a room is a cream pill. Display type is Grotzec Condensed, uppercase, cream, over the photo. Body copy below is black Visuelt on `#fef5f0`.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

Phone width drops the nav into a hamburger. The header is 56px. The hero headline steps down to 40px.

[mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png)

## City page

London repeats the photo hero and the pink wordmark, then coloured sections (light purple `#e5dcfa`, light yellow `#f2ed8f`) rather than a second theme.

## Room search
