## Components

Filled button: background `#1b2121`, text `#ffffff`, radius 0, height 64px, padding 20px 40px.

Outline button: transparent, text `#000000`, 2px solid `#000000`, radius 0, height 48px on the homepage.

## Do and don't

Use the ink bar with lilac announcement type. Keep buttons square. Set nav and buttons in uppercase 13px weight 500. Fill the product add button. Leave hero and card buttons as outlines.
