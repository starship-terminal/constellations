# Patterns

Public pages only. Login was measured and not submitted, and it has no screenshot.

## Marketing shell

White nav, 96px at 1440. Wordmark left, four dropdown labels, Log in outline and Sign up fill on the right. At 390 the same wordmark and a hamburger. The open sheet is white, with Login outline and Try for free fill pinned to the bottom.

## Hero

Blush band `#F8DBD5`, padding 190px 64px 119px at 1440. Display heading 48 / 60, body Inter 18 / 27, one indigo button. The phone hero centres the same heading and button.

## Pricing

Grey page `#F4F4F4`. The index offers Meetings and Embedded as two illustrated columns. Meetings pricing is three white cards (Free, Pro, Business) under a 240 by 48 billing pill. Prices are display 56px. Plan names are indigo 24 / 500.

## Product choice

`/information/select-product` is the destination of Sign up and Get started. Two white cards, a Back link, no email field. The email form is on `/user/login`, which is a separate app shell (Inter var, Whereby Condensed, grey page).

## FAQ

A question row is 72px with 16px vertical padding. Opening one on the pricing index revealed an Answer block 277px tall. The answer text is in [raw/faq-open.json](raw/faq-open.json).
