# Whereby

Whereby is a video calling product (whereby.com). This package is a refresh of the public marketing site and the empty login and signup walls, read on 2026-11-21. The painted theme is light. No dark theme was on these pages. Widths are 1440 and 390.

origin/main already had `systems/whereby/`. The slug was kept. catalog.json was left alone. login-extracted stays not-started.

Nothing was typed. Google and Apple were not clicked. No form was submitted. No captcha was solved. No room was joined. No signed-in app was opened.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, device pixel ratio 1. Component colours, sizes, radii, and fonts were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Framer custom properties (`--token-*`, `--framer-*`) were read from the same-origin stylesheets. The login app's stylesheets are cross-origin, so its values are computed styles only.

The cookie card was hidden with CSS. Accept all cookies was not clicked.

Screenshots were quantised to 256 colours after the values were read.

## What to distrust

The Log in control's named `blue-outline` node computed a transparent background and no border. The indigo is the label colour, `rgb(43, 24, 155)`.

A keyboard focus ring was not isolated. `light.focus.ring` is derived from the Log in label colour.

`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.56)` on the disabled Continue label is stored as the declared `#0000008F`.

The 256-colour screenshots band photographs. Read colour from the tokens.

The homepage hero in the first reading was the carousel heading "Telehealth" at 80px, because that node is the document `h1`. The visible hero "Secure video calls. Zero hassle" is 48px.

Lato and Manrope are not in the computed stacks from this pass. Inter, Inter-Medium, Inter var, Whereby Roslindale Display Condensed Regular, and Whereby Condensed were.

## Do and don't

- Primary actions are the indigo fill. Sign up, Get started, Try for free, and the login submit use `#2B189B`, white 16 / 500 type, 48px height, 12px radius. [components/crops/button-signup.png](components/crops/button-signup.png)
- Secondary actions are an indigo label. Log in uses `#2B189B` type on a transparent 48px control. [components/crops/button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png)
- Display type stays at weight 400. Headings and prices use Whereby Roslindale Display Condensed Regular. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- The home hero is blush, and pricing is grey. The hero band is `#F8DBD5`. Pricing sits on `#F4F4F4`. [screenshots/meetings-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/meetings-pricing-light-default.png)
- These pages have no dark theme. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
