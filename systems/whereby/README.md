# Whereby

Whereby is a video calling product (whereby.com). This package covers the public marketing site and the signed-out login, read on 19 November 2026. The painted theme is light. No dark theme was on these pages. Widths are 1440 and 390. The login email was focused and not submitted. Google and Apple were not clicked. No card, phone, or payment field was entered.

2. Secondary actions are an indigo outline.
3. Display type stays at weight 400.

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on https://whereby.com/ and on https://whereby.com/user/login, after a freeform resize to 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2. The cookie button "Accept all cookies" was clicked so it would not cover the nav. The login email field was focused. It was not submitted.

Framer custom properties (`--token-*`, `--framer-*`) were read from the same-origin stylesheets on the marketing pages. The login app's stylesheets are on cloudfront and could not be read from the page (`cssRules` is blocked). Login values are computed styles only.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on snapshot. Screenshots were taken with headless Playwright on pages that had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge: the home page, the pricing index, meetings pricing, product select, and the 390 home page. The login URL was not opened in Playwright.

## What to distrust

The Log in outline has computed `border: none` and `box-shadow: none`. A 1px stroke of `rgb(43, 24, 155)` was sampled from the 2x screenshot. That sample is the token.

`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.56)` is stored as the declared `#0000008F`. The computed form is in the token description.

The 256-colour screenshots band photographs. Read colour from the tokens.

Marketing keyboard focus on the logo link was the browser outline `rgb(0, 95, 204) auto 1px`. That is not the product's field focus colour.

Lato and Manrope are loaded as `@font-face` on the marketing page. No measured heading or control used them.

## Fonts

Whereby Roslindale Display Condensed Regular is the marketing display face, computed on the hero and the prices. Whereby Roslindale Text Regular is the 28px subhead. The login page computes Whereby Condensed (with Georgia) on the h1 and Whereby Text (with Georgia) on footer headings. The woff2 files are preloaded from `d2qulvgqu65efe.cloudfront.net` and are not in this package.

Inter is the marketing body and nav face. The login app computes Inter var. The variable woff2 is preloaded and is not committed. The style guide falls back to Arial and Georgia.

Fragment Mono is declared as `--framer-code-font-family`. No code specimen was measured.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are the indigo fill.** Sign up, Get started, and the login submit use `#2B189B`, white 16 / 500 type, 48px height, 12px radius. [components/crops/button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png)
- **Secondary actions are an indigo outline.** Log in is a 1px `#2B189B` stroke with an indigo label, same height and radius. [components/crops/button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png)
- **Display type stays at weight 400.** Headings and prices use Whereby Roslindale Display Condensed Regular. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **The home hero is blush, and pricing is grey.** The hero band is `#F8DBD5`. Pricing and login sit on `#F4F4F4`. [screenshots/meetings-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/meetings-pricing-light-default.png)
- **These pages have no dark theme.** Declared indigo-deep and green tokens are in `core.color` and were not painted as a theme. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
