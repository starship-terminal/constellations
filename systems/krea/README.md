# Krea design reference

A token set and component reference for Krea's public signed-out website, for building a dark generative-media app shell, a pricing page and a login card in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.krea.ai** on **2026-11-18** and refreshed on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). Captured: the home app shell, /pricing, /enterprise, /blog, /plugins, /mcp, /features/ai-image-generator, /features/ai-video-generator, /models, /apps, /feed, /image, /video, /docs, /terms, /privacy, /login and a 404 at /about, plus 390px passes on the home, the gallery and login.

Krea paints one public theme, `html.dark`. The Tailwind v4 `@theme` layer on `:root,:host` declares a primary grey ramp (`--color-primary-0` through `--color-primary-1000`, including 150, 250, 850 and 975), action colours (`--color-action` `#006eff`, hover `#0064fe`, green `#65f223`, purple `#af00ff`), radius steps, a 4px spacing base, and Suisse Intl. Semantic roles (`--background`, `--foreground`, `--card`, `--sidebar`, `--destructive`, `--warning`, `--success`) live on `.dark`. The body field is `--color-primary-950` `#101010`. Those painted roles are the token file's `dark` set; there is no `light` set. There is no public light switch. Docs uses a separate sheet set and is not folded into the app tokens.

**Boundary:** the 2026-11-21 refresh is public only. Login (`/login`) and the phone login page were photographed empty and the branch stopped. No email, password, code, phone or address was typed. Continue with Google, Apple, SSO and Email were not clicked. Sign up, Claim offer, Get Pro, Book a call and Generate were not followed. Files redirects to `/login?redirect=%2Ffiles` and that redirect was not followed. A limited-time offer dialog's veil was hidden, never answered. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

### Principles

1. Do paint the app on #101010.
2. Do keep chrome neutral and spend blue on the action.
3. Do set UI in Suisse Intl.
4. Do use a 10px radius on sidebar controls.
5. Do keep the sidebar at 255px on desktop.
6. Don't invent a light app shell.
7. Don't style the signed-in product from these shots.
8. Don't follow Generate, Get Pro or Book a call.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **The token file is dark-only.** `html.dark` is the only painted theme, so the semantic roles and component values live in the `dark` set and there is no `light` set. Do not treat the white marketing heroes as a light product theme.
- **`--background` and the body field differ.** `--background` resolves to `#0a0a0a`. The body uses `dark:bg-primary-950`, which is `#101010`. The page you see is `#101010`.
- **Computed colours were read in oklch and oklab.** Chromium reports Tailwind v4 colours that way (Sign in's fill is `oklch(0.985 0 0)`). tokens.json converts them to sRGB hex or rgba and keeps the form Chromium reported under `$extensions.computed`.
- **The focus ring is a shadow.** Sign in's keyboard focus draws a 2px box-shadow ring of black at 10% (`dark.component.focus.ring`, `ring-width`) and leaves `outline` at none. The `#007aff` outline colour the page also reports is the browser default and is not painted, so it is no longer a token. `ent-cta` and `plug-link` draw a `1px auto` outline: the browser's own ring, whose colour is not the recorded value.
- **Marketing heroes can be light while html is dark.** /plugins and /features paint a white hero. That is a marketing surface, not a second theme.
- **A few selectors missed their node.** `price-card`, `faq-item`, `composer-send`, `image-generate`, `m-sidebar` and `m-signin` are recorded as missing. Get Pro, the composer field, Sign in and the phone menu were measured.
- **Quantised screenshots band gradients.** Read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw JSON.
- **The offer strip is a campaign.** The copy captured on 2026-11-18 ("Unlimited Nano Banana for 7 days") is what this session was served.

## Fonts

**Suisse Intl** is the UI face (`core.font.family.sans`), with ui-sans-serif, system-ui and sans-serif behind it. **Suisse Intl Mono** is the mono face (`core.font.family.mono`). Both are served by the site and are not redistributed. Weights include book at 450. The style guide falls back to system fonts.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the app on #101010.** The body uses primary-950, not the slightly darker `--background`. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do keep chrome neutral and spend blue on the action.** Sign in and plan buttons are white. Continue with Email and the 40% off badge use `#006eff`. See [nav-signin](components/crops/nav-signin-default.png) and [price-badge](components/crops/price-badge-default.png).
3. **Do set UI in Suisse Intl.** One family carries sidebar rows, headings and the composer. Book weight is 450. See [price-h1](components/crops/price-h1-default.png).
4. **Do use a 10px radius on sidebar controls.** Sign in and nav rows share `--radius` at 10px. Plan CTAs are pills. See [nav-signin](components/crops/nav-signin-default.png) and [price-cta](components/crops/price-cta-default.png).
5. **Do keep the sidebar at 255px on desktop.** Folding it is a local toggle, not a second layout. See [sidebar](components/crops/sidebar-default.png) and [home-dark-sidebar-folded](screenshots/home-dark-sidebar-folded.png).
6. **Don't invent a light app shell.** /plugins paints a white marketing hero. The app shell, login and pricing stay dark. See [plugins-dark-default](screenshots/plugins-dark-default.png) and [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from these shots.** The 2026-11-21 refresh stopped at the empty login wall. Settings, the account menu, billing and generation were not opened. See [login-dark-default](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
8. **Don't follow Generate, Get Pro or Book a call.** Those controls were measured. Submitting them is a gated action. See [price-cta](components/crops/price-cta-default.png) and [ent-cta](components/crops/ent-cta-default.png).
