# Frame.io design reference

A token set and component reference for Frame.io's public surfaces — for building video-collaboration marketing pages, feature pages, pricing grids, resource indexes and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **frame.io** on **2026-11-15**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /features/workflow-management, /features/review-and-approval, /features/c2c, /pricing, /enterprise, /enterprise/media-and-entertainment, /customers, /case-studies/ford, /integrations, /resources, /whats-new, /contact, /contact/form, /security, /ai, /drive and /transfer, plus the header's Features, Enterprise and Resources mega-menus, the 390px nav drawer, 390px passes on home, pricing and the contact form, and the sign-in surface at accounts.frame.io/welcome.

Frame.io is worth a capture because the whole public site is painted in one dark theme and it ships a real token layer: the marketing Next.js + CSS-modules app declares a 19-step `--color-grey-00..100` neutral ramp (00 the near-black field, 100 the #fcfcfc ink), five brand hues (`--color-cobalt`, `--color-iris`, `--color-coral`, `--color-electric`, `--color-turquoise-triumph`, each with light/dark steps) and per-hue alpha tints (`--color-cobalt-20` is rgba(91,83,255,0.2)), the `--color-alpha-black/dark/light-*` ladders, `--color-accent`, `--overlay-*` scrims, three font roles (`--font-main` FrameGothic, `--font-secondary` NeueMachinaInktrap, `--font-tertiary` Inter — each a self-hosted variable face with an Arial-metric fallback), a `--spacer-*` scale, the `--grid-*`/`--global-max-width` frame (12 columns, 1080px content), `--nav-height` geometry, `--duration-*`/`--ease-*` motion steps, `--card-hover-*` and a `--z-index-*` layer map. **Boundary:** the product — projects, the review player, Camera to Cloud uploads, Drive, Transfer deliveries — sits behind an account and was never reached. The sign-in page was measured as a form, never submitted; the contact form was typed into then cleared; every link and CTA was measured, never followed; no consent layer appeared for the en-US signed-out visitor and the cookie-preferences control was never pressed.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export frame` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layer carry the design.
2. Do keep ink near-white on near-black.
3. Do make the CTA a white pill.
4. Do pair the three voices.
5. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
6. Don't invent a light theme.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **The signed-in product.** Projects, the review-and-approval player, Camera to Cloud uploads, Drive and Transfer workspaces need an account this capture does not have. accounts.frame.io/welcome is the sign-in surface — a different styled-components stack that shares none of the marketing `--*` layer; its measured values sit under `component.productShell.*`, honestly labelled.
- **"Dark" naming.** frame.io paints one dark surface and ships no `[data-theme]` scope or toggle; `dark` is the package's only theme set because it is the only painted theme. accounts.frame.io/welcome is the lone light surface — kept as measured component tokens, not a second set.
- **Animated surfaces.** Heroes and product demos hold live canvas/video content; a screenshot freezes one frame. Sparse-looking panels are a capture artifact, not the design.
- **The forms.** The contact form and the sign-in email field were typed into then cleared — validation, error, loading and success states are unverified because nothing was submitted.
- **Detail pages.** Customers, integrations, resources and what's-new were captured at index level (plus one case study, Ford); individual stories, articles and integration pages are the same templates, unmeasured.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw values, never the pixels.

## Fonts

- **FrameGothic** — a 400-600 variable face (`--font-main`): every display heading and button label, with an Arial-metric fallback.
- **NeueMachinaInktrap** (`--font-secondary`) — labels, kickers and the mono-flavoured UI type.
- **Inter** — a 100-900 variable face (`--font-tertiary`): body copy and UI text.
- None is bundled here; the style guide falls back to system faces. Declared in the stylesheet's `@font-face` rules (harvested in `raw/css-vars.json`).

## Coverage

- 19 desktop routes and 3 mobile routes captured signed out, plus the three header mega-menus, the mobile nav drawer and the light sign-in page at both widths; every frame.io page painted the one dark theme.
- 52 components measured in 131 states, including the opened mega-menu and menu items, the expanded feature-comparison and FAQ accordions, the opened mobile overlay, forced-pressed controls and typed-then-cleared form fields; motion probed on five pages under emulated `prefers-reduced-motion` (verdict: partial — most motion keeps running under reduce, recorded not tidied).
- Missing surfaces (declared in the catalog's `capture.gaps`): the app and every post-login surface, case-study/article/integration detail pages beyond one case study, the Calendly scheduler behind Schedule a Meeting, the cookie-consent manager, the locale list, and the annual-billing price state (the toggle was measured at rest, never switched).
- Icons: layout gap — no standalone SVG set lifted; the Frame.io mark and glyphs are visible in crops/screenshots.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layer carry the design.** Frame.io ships its tokens: use the `color.grey.*` ramp, the brand hues and the `dark.theme.*` roles rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do keep ink near-white on near-black.** Every public page paints the same dark theme — `--color-grey-00` field, `--color-grey-100` ink, hierarchy through the alpha-light ladder. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
3. **Do make the CTA a white pill.** The primary action is a 39.75px-high full-radius pill (#f5f5f8 fill, near-black ink) — the inverse of the field, never a brand colour at rest. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
4. **Do pair the three voices.** FrameGothic carries display and control labels, NeueMachinaInktrap the kickers, Inter the body — a three-voice system, not one family at different weights. See [section-h2](components/crops/section-h2-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** The app was never reached; `component.productShell.*` marks the sign-in surface's measurements on a different stack with a different palette. See [welcome-light-default](screenshots/welcome-light-default.png).
6. **Don't invent a light theme.** The site ships no theme switch; `dark` is the only painted set and the welcome page's light scheme belongs to the accounts stack, not to frame.io pages. See [footer](components/crops/footer-default.png).
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** The contact form and sign-in field were typed into then cleared — validation, error and success states are unverified. See [form-email-filled](components/crops/form-email-filled.png).
8. **Don't copy the brand.** The Frame.io waveform mark, integration logos and demo imagery in captures are Frame.io's published artwork; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).
