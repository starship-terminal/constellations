# Yelp

Public signed-out pages of [yelp.com](https://www.yelp.com/), captured 2026-11-19. The homepage, a coffee search in San Francisco, one business page, the about page, the login form (not submitted) and a missing-page 404. One theme: light. The preview viewport was 1169 by 731 at device pixel ratio 2. The about and 404 screenshots are 1440 by 900 from headless Chromium.

No account was created. The signup form asks for a password, a name and a ZIP, so the email-only signup rule stopped there.

### Principles

1. Use the brand red for the primary pill.
2. Keep the search field as a shadowed 8px bar.
3. Use the rating ramp for stars, not the brand red.
4. Do not invent a dark theme.
5. Do not redraw the burst or the wordmark.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on the about page in the preview browser, after the same properties had been read on the homepage. Yelp stores colours as `R,G,B` triplets (`--ref-color-*`, `--sem-color-*`). 317 names were saved to [raw/css-vars.tsv](raw/css-vars.tsv). Component sizes were read from the elements on the homepage, the search page, the business page, the about page and the login page. The notes are in [raw/components.json](raw/components.json).

The preview pane returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so no preview screenshot was saved. Headless Chromium then loaded the about page and the 404. The homepage, the search page and the business page returned a block page in that browser. Those screenshots were deleted. Their computed styles, read earlier in the preview tab, are kept.

## What to distrust

The login page uses a different red (`#D32323`) and a 3px radius. That is the older login skin, measured, and it is not aliased to the semantic brand token.

Sign Up on the homepage reported `font-family: Arial` and `font-size: 13.333px` while the about heading and the loaded font set are Poppins. The button crop shows the marketing face. The token uses the measured Poppins stack for display and Open Sans for UI. The Arial reading is left in the raw notes.

Header category links on the photo header reported `font-family: Times` and `color: rgb(0, 0, 238)` on one link, and white on the category row. The screenshot shows dark text on the about header. Times and the default link blue are unread inherited values, not the painted type.

There is no dark token set. `prefers-color-scheme: dark` was true in the preview browser and the page stayed light.

## Fonts

Poppins (500, 700) and Open Sans (400, 600, 700) loaded from the page. Both are proprietary to their owners as shipped by Yelp. The tokens name the stacks. No font files are in this package. The style guide falls back to Helvetica Neue, Helvetica and Arial, which are already in the measured stacks.

## Do and don't

- **Use the brand red for the primary pill.** Sign Up, the search button and Write a review are `#D71616` with white text and a 20px radius on the marketing header. The about screenshot shows it.
- **Keep the search field as a shadowed 8px bar.** The field is 48px tall with `rgba(0,0,0,0.16) 0 0 18px`. The red search control sits at the right end.
- **Use the rating ramp for stars, not the brand red.** A filled star reads `--sem-color-bg-rating-*`, from `#FFCC4B` at one star to `#FB433C` at five.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** These pages did not switch when the browser asked for dark.
- **Do not redraw the burst or the wordmark.** The logo crop is evidence. It is not an icon to reuse.
