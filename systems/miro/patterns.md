# Patterns

Public signed-out pages, measured 2026-11-18. No screenshots: the preview pane produced no frames.

## Marketing home

[https://miro.com/](https://miro.com/) paints a Framer page on `#fbfaf7`. The title "Human collaboration at the speed of AI" is Apris Regular, 56px / 61.6px, black. The lede is Roobert PRO Regular, 20px / 30px, `#666666`. Section headings are Roobert PRO Regular, 36px / 41.4px, `#1c1c1e`.

The nav sits on the same page. Login is a ghost Noto Sans button, 14px / 500, 8px radius. Get started free is a near-black button, `#1c1c1e` on white text, 14px / 600, 36px tall. Below the title, a work-email field (white, 1px `#e9eaef`, 8px radius, 48px) sits next to a transparent "Get Started Free" button.

A yellow promo bar announces the Intelligent Canvas. An OneTrust banner ("Cookies, anyone?") sits at the bottom. It was not accepted.

## Product and pricing pages

[https://miro.com/pricing/](https://miro.com/pricing/), [https://miro.com/products/ai-workflows/](https://miro.com/products/ai-workflows/) and [https://miro.com/ai-trust/](https://miro.com/ai-trust/) share a page colour `#fafafc`, text `#1c1c1e`, and Roobert at 48px / 500 for the title. Body copy on the product page is Noto Sans, 18px / 24.3px, `#555a6a`.

Pricing lays four plan cards in a row: Free, Starter, Business, Enterprise. Business is the featured card, outlined in `#3859ff`. The billing control is a gray pill with Yearly selected. Primary actions on the featured plan are the blue button. Other plans use the outline button.

A wrong product URL, [https://miro.com/ai/ai-workflows/](https://miro.com/ai/ai-workflows/), is a 404 titled "Hmm, that didn’t work" in the same 48px title style. The nav still lists the real path, `/products/ai-workflows/`.

## Auth

[https://miro.com/login/](https://miro.com/login/) and [https://miro.com/signup/](https://miro.com/signup/) share a warm page `#fbf7ef` and a white card with a charcoal border. The title is 44px Roobert PRO. Providers (Google, Microsoft, Slack, Office365, SSO) are white buttons with a `#444749` border. The submit is `#2e4de6`.

Signup copy says a sign-in link is sent and no password is required. Submitting the capture inbox was rejected in the card: red field border, rose error text. No mail arrived. No board was created.

[https://miro.com/app/](https://miro.com/app/) redirects to signup when signed out.

## Templates

[https://miro.com/templates/](https://miro.com/templates/) is Miroverse. Page `#f4f4f1`. Title "Make. Share. Inspire." is 40px / 600. Search is a 66px white field. Category chips are white, 1px `#c7cad5`, 8px radius, 16px / 600.
