# Patterns

How the public Spain storefront puts the controls together. Signed out, 2026-11-22.

## Market gate

https://www.oysho.com/ opens a picker over a full-bleed photograph. The wordmark sits at the top. A white select, a remember checkbox, and a dark Go button sit in the middle. The picker defaulted to Singapore (English). Spain (English) was the storefront photographed after that. [screenshots/gate-light-1440.png](screenshots/gate-light-1440.png)

## Homepage

A black promo bar, a white header, then a full-bleed photograph with a white title and an underlined link. Further down, a two-column photo grid. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Menu
