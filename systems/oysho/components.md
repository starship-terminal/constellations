# Components

Public controls measured on the Spain storefront, https://www.oysho.com/es/en/, on 2026-11-22. Signed out. Add to basket was not clicked.

## Header

A black promo bar, then a white bar. The wordmark is an icon font, `oysho-icon-font`, beside a 32px menu button. Sign in is 14px, weight 400, `#3d3d3d`. Help and Basket sit to its right. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Search

An underlined field. The placeholder cycles product words. The field was not typed and the search was not submitted. Token `--input-font-size-desktop` is 18px.

## Menu

A full-width panel. Column headings are 14px, weight 500. Links are 14px, weight 400, ink `#000000`. A NEW badge is 12px, weight 500. [screenshots/menu-light-1440.png](screenshots/menu-light-1440.png)

## Product card

A photograph, colour chips, a name, and a price. The photographed grid says MULTIPLE LENGTHS under the image. [screenshots/leggings-light-1440.png](screenshots/leggings-light-1440.png)

## Product title and price

The title is 18px, weight 500, line-height 22px, `#000000`. The price is 16px, weight 400, `#000000`. The sample price was 35,99 €. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png)

56 by 116. Radius 0px. Border 1px `#e5e5e5`. Fill `#fefefe`. Type 14px, weight 400. Regular is the selected length and uses a darker border.

## Add to basket

56px tall, 424px wide. Radius 0px. Fill `#000000`. Type 14px, weight 400, `#f5f4f1`. Not clicked.

## Footer link

12px, weight 400, `#000000`. [screenshots/footer-light-1440.png](screenshots/footer-light-1440.png)

## Join button

A black rectangle beside the email field. The field was not typed. Join was not submitted.

## Gaps

Sign in, the basket, checkout, the newsletter, and account forms were not opened. No hover, focus, disabled, loading, or error state was forced. No phone width was captured. The shop has no dark theme on the public pages.
