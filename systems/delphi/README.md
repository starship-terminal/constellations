# Delphi design reference

A token set and component reference for Delphi's public product surface — for building expert-Q&A marketing pages, result cards, profile surfaces and chat composers in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.delphi.co** (the catalog's `delphi.ai` redirects here) on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /discover/productivity, a public /search/<question> results page, the public /karpathy expert profile, the public /karpathy/chat surface, the Sign-In dialog (via /auth) and the same dialog mounted by /onboarding, plus 390px passes on home and chat.

Delphi is worth a capture because it ships its real design system to the browser: every stylesheet declares a full token layer outright — Radix-style 12-step palette scales (`--sand-*`, `--tangerine-*`, `--amber-*`, …), a shadcn-style semantic layer (`--background`, `--card`, `--primary`, `--ring`, `--input` and their `-foreground` twins) and Delphi's own roles (`--fg-*` text tiers, `--surface-*`, `--brand-accent`, `--bubble-accent`, the `--mind-*` expert-tier colours, `--avatar-shadow`, `--sidebar-*`, `--chart-*`), on top of a Tailwind utility layer (`--text-*`, `--leading-*`, `--tracking-*`, `--radius-*`, `--container-*`, `--animate-*`, `--ease-*`, `--blur-*`, `--z-*`). 425 variables resolve per theme; 276 of them differ between light and dark — although only the light theme is ever painted signed out. The light surface is a white field with `#21201c` ink, `#63635e`/`#82827c` mid and low text, `#f7f3f0` warm cards, translucent ink hairlines and a `#fd5400` brand orange reserved for the actions that create. Three served voices: Delphi Inter for UI, Delphi Pythia for display headings/quotes/badges, Delphi Geist Mono declared. **Boundary:** everything behind an account was never reached — the Sign-In and onboarding dialogs were measured as dialogs, never submitted; no question was ever asked and no chat message was ever sent (the send button was measured with text in the field, then the text was cleared).

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layer carry the design.
2. Do spend the orange on creating.
3. Do paint light.
4. Do pair the two voices.
5. Do let the question be the chrome.
6. Do use ink translucency for borders.
7. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
8. Don't fabricate a conversation.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layer carry the design.** The product ships its token layer: use the `color.<family>.<step>` scales, the `fg.*`/`surface.*`/`primary.*` semantics and the `--mind-*` expert tiers rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. **Do spend the orange on creating.** `#fd5400` fills only Create Your Delphi, Expand, Send and the focus ring; surfaces and secondary actions stay neutral or near-black. See [create-cta](components/crops/create-cta-default.png) and [signin-btn](components/crops/signin-btn-focus-visible.png).
3. **Do paint light.** Every public surface paints the light theme; the dark set is declared but never painted signed out — treat dark values as declared evidence, not a verified surface. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do pair the two voices.** Delphi Pythia owns display headings, quote text and badges; Delphi Inter owns every control, label and paragraph. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png) and [srch-quote](components/crops/srch-quote-default.png).
5. **Do let the question be the chrome.** Big centered ask fields open every surface and /search/<question> keeps the question live in the field — there is no separate results header. See [hero-search](components/crops/hero-search-default.png) and [search-light-default](screenshots/search-light-default.png).
6. **Do use ink translucency for borders.** Hairlines are `rgba(33,32,28,…)` ink at ~9-20%, chips and badges ~6-10% ink fills — not grays. See [disc-card](components/crops/disc-card-default.png).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** The Sign-In dialog, /onboarding and every post-login surface are a documented boundary; the public home, discover, search, profile and chat are the evidence.
8. **Don't fabricate a conversation.** No question was asked and no chat message was sent; Save, Personalize, Share and the send button were measured, never pressed. See [chat-send](components/crops/chat-send-default.png).
