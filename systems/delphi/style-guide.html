Palette

The declared layer carries the Radix-style 12-step palette scales (--sand-*, --tangerine-*, the accent ramps) per theme under color.*; the shadcn semantic names (--background, --card, --primary, --ring) and Delphi's --fg-*/--surface-*/--mind-*/--brand-accent roles resolve per theme in the light and dark sets. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing and radius

Buttons and the dialog are 26px pills, cards 24px (topic/gallery) and 20px (mobile/experience), chips 18px, badges 6px; nav pills and round buttons are full pills (calc(infinity * 1px)). Borders are translucent ink hairlines (~9-20% ink), with #dad9d6 declared on inputs.
