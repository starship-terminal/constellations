# X

Public signed-out surfaces of X (x.com, formerly Twitter). First measured on 19 November 2026. Refreshed on 21 November 2026 in a signed-out T3 preview incognito tab, plus headless Chromium for the legal pages. No account was created, no email or phone was typed, and nothing was posted. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`. catalog.json was not edited.

Themes seen: dark on x.com, dark and light on about.x.com, light on the legal pages. Preview shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844 at device pixel ratio 2. The 1440 legal screenshots are 1x.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export x` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use the white pill on black for the primary action.
2. Keep the legal pages light and huge.
3. Use #1d9bf0 for the brand link.
4. Do not invent a hover or an error colour.
5. Do not ship TwitterChirp.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles came from `preview_evaluate` on a T3 preview tab opened at https://x.com/ with `reuseExistingTab` false. The tab title was "X. It’s what’s happening / X". `preview_snapshot` failed twice with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so those measurements have no preview screenshot.

The terms page was opened by clicking the footer link, then remeasured in headless Playwright (Playwright 1.55, Chromium) at 1440x900 and 390x844, device scale 2. Privacy used the same template. about.x.com loaded in the preview tab and was measured there. Headless Chromium got a Cloudflare "Just a moment..." page on about.x.com, and HTTP 403 with a blank body on x.com/ and the missing route, so those screenshots were discarded.

Custom properties on the login page were mostly a toast library (`--gray1` through `--gray12`, `--error-bg`). They do not paint the form. The product tokens that did resolve are `--color-brand: #1d9bf0` and the classes `text-primary`, `text-secondary`, `bg-primary`, `bg-btn-primary`, `bg-modal` and `border-normal`.

## What to distrust

The login field border was already #0ea5e9 on load. That is the painted state, not a hover measurement. The Continue control is a div with a stone label. It was not pressed, so its filled state is a gap.

The Google button is Google Identity Services chrome (Google Sans, 20px radius). It is recorded as third-party and is not an X token.

The date band colour is a screenshot sample, rgb(20, 23, 26), because the text node itself is transparent. The legal body computed at 15.96px and is stored as 16px. The QR card radius computed at 21.6px and is stored as 22px. The Show more radius computed at 14.4px and is stored as 14px.

The 19 November pass at 1169px hid the about.x.com desktop header. The 21 November pass at 1440px shows that header. Sign In was not followed. The Light theme control was pressed so the light marketing page could be photographed.

TwitterChirp, Helvetica Neue LT and xVF are not in this package. The style guide uses the system sans.

## Fonts

TwitterChirp is the product face on x.com, weights 300, 400 and 500, served from abs.twimg.com. Helvetica Neue LT is the legal-page face. xVF and xVFDisplay are the about.x.com faces, a variable 200 to 800 woff2. All three are proprietary and are not shipped. Tokens name them, then fall back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **Use the white pill on black for the primary action.** The painted Continue with phone control is #f8fafc on #000000, fully rounded. See [screenshots/home-login-dark-1440.png](screenshots/home-login-dark-1440.png).
- **Keep the legal pages light and huge.** The terms headline is 144px Helvetica Neue LT at 1440, black on white. See [screenshots/tos-light-1440.png](screenshots/tos-light-1440.png).
- **Use #1d9bf0 for the brand link.** Show more and `--color-brand` both resolve to it. See [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).
- **Do not invent a hover or an error colour.** Neither was on the page.
- **Do not ship TwitterChirp.** Name it, then fall back.
