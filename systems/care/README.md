# Care.com design reference

A token set and component reference for Care.com's public signed-out surface — for building marketing, category, listing and pricing pages in the style of the real thing. It was read from **care.com** on **2026-11-12**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, the five category landing pages (child care, senior care, pet care, housekeeping, tutoring), the safety and resource-centre pages, the plans-and-pricing page, the public /babysitters caregiver listing, the login and get-started shells as boundary cards, and a 390px mobile pass.

Care.com is worth a capture because the "find care" marketplace paints a warm, high-trust system on a strict two-colour brand pair: a deep `#025747` green against a signature lime ramp — `#d0f5a3` fills on the capsule CTAs, `#e4ffb8` on hover, a bright `#8ae718` on press, and lime text `#e4ffb8` on every deep-green button. Type runs three served faces — **NaN Druid Sans** at 550 for the 64px display headlines, **Oceanic Grotesk** at 400/500 for all UI text, **Oceanic Text Semibold** at 450 for the testimonial-name serif accent — over a 27-32px card-radius, capsule-button geometry, with the MUI 150-300ms micro-motion throughout. A legacy login layer in Proxima Nova survives under `/vis/auth`, and `/babysitters` is a genuinely public marketplace surface: real caregivers' first-name-plus-initial listings with photos, rates and tags. **Boundary:** the member product — dashboard, messages, bookings, payments, applications — sits behind the login and join shells, which were measured as cards and never used; the password step, the search and checkout flows were never reached or inferred.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do pair deep green with lime.
2. Do run CTAs as capsules.
3. Do flash the lime on press.
4. Do headline in NaN Druid.
5. Do keep cards white and round.
6. Do go dark-green for the inverse surface.
7. Do let the header ride the hero.
8. Don't style the member product from this package.
9. Don't invent a dark mode.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body (the Next.js CSS chunks from network responses plus the inline `<style>` blocks re-parsed with `CSSStyleSheet`) and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). care.com declares almost nothing — its MUI layer compiles values into `mui-*` classes — so every token is a measured computed value (plus the @font-face declarations the harvest did find). [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in one context per viewport class (the DataDome clearance cookie carries across routes, so navigations are paced) and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the Find-care mega-menu trigger, the mobile menu button and a listing FAQ question. The `Join Care.com` signup-gate modal that mounts over the listing on scroll is hidden after measuring, never answered. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, joined, searched, booked or paid for: the login and get-started shells were captured as boundary cards, never filled or submitted; every Join-now, Search, Find-care, City/State, Filter and Get-started action was measured, never followed; the mega menu, mobile drawer and one FAQ row were toggled as local UI state; the OneTrust consent banner and the signup-gate modal were hidden, never answered — no consent or gate button was ever clicked. Mid-capture the edge rate-limited the client into a DataDome reCAPTCHA interstitial; the capture paused rather than answering, and resumed after the flag cleared — a route that stays challenged is logged BLOCKED and keeps earlier readings, never retried into a harder block.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **This is the public shell, not the member product.** The dashboard, inbox and messaging, bookings, payments, job applications and full caregiver profiles sit behind auth and are uncaptured. Treat the member design system as unknown.
- **Two layers, two systems.** The marketing/listing surface is MUI (`mui-*`, Oceanic Grotesk/NaN Druid); `/vis/auth/login` is a legacy Proxima Nova form. Tokens name the layer each value came from.
- **The gate is frequency-capped.** The `Join Care.com` modal mounts over the listing on scroll but not on every load; a run where it stayed absent records `gate-dialog` missing.
- **Marketplace data is real.** Provider rows name real public caregivers (first name + initial, photo, rate); that is the product's published content kept under the repo's private-library policy — no contact details appear on the public page or in the package.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **Five components kept an earlier run's readings.** The 2026-11-12 recapture did not find `nav-dropdown`, `menu-item`, `menu-link`, `hero-title` or `section-h2` (`recaptureMissed` in `raw/components-computed.json`). Every token built from them (the section-heading type, the menu-link type and colour, the menu-row height and radius, the white canvas) says "stale" in its source, and components.md marks the five entries. They need a recapture.
- **The ZIP field's filled state typed the email default.** The spec passed its sample ZIP where the shooter does not read it, so `zip-field` `filled` was measured with `name@example.com` typed (then deleted). The script now passes the ZIP correctly (edited, not run).

## Do and don't

1. **Do pair deep green with lime.** The system is the `#025747` / `#d0f5a3` pair — green fills for inverse actions, lime fills for the bright CTAs, green text on lime, lime text on green. See [join-cta](components/crops/join-cta-default.png) and [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png).
2. **Do run CTAs as capsules.** Every action is a pill: 20px radius on the 40px buttons, 28px on the 56px Search, 32px on the plan CTAs, 50px on the legacy Continue. See [search-cta](components/crops/search-cta-default.png).
3. **Do flash the lime on press.** The pills go `#e4ffb8` on hover and a bright `#8ae718` on press — the signature accent flash. See [join-cta pressed](components/crops/join-cta-pressed.png).
4. **Do headline in NaN Druid.** Display titles run 64px at weight 550 with -1px tracking — white over photos, ink on white sections. See [cat-h1](components/crops/cat-h1-default.png) and [section-h2](components/crops/section-h2-default.png).
5. **Do keep cards white and round.** Category, service, review, plan and join cards are white fills at 24-32px radius with no border and no shadow. See [plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png) and [service-card](components/crops/service-card-default.png).
6. **Do go dark-green for the inverse surface.** The footer is a `#025747` panel with light links — the only dark surface on the page. See [footer](components/crops/footer-default.png).
7. **Do let the header ride the hero.** The top bar is transparent with white text over the hero photo and turns white on scroll — don't paint it white at rest. See [header](components/crops/header-default.png).
8. **Don't style the member product from this package.** Dashboard, messaging, bookings, payments and checkout are uncaptured; the login/join shells are boundary cards, not the app's design system.
9. **Don't invent a dark mode.** The only dark surface is the green footer panel and inverse buttons; there is no toggle.


## Fonts

Four served faces do everything. **NaN Druid Sans** (550) runs the 64px display headlines on the hero, category and listing titles. **Oceanic Grotesk** (400/500) is the UI family — nav, buttons, fields, cards and body. **Oceanic Text Semibold** (450) is the serif accent on testimonial names. **Proxima Nova** survives on the legacy login form. All are served from care.com's own `/css/cms/font-face/` files; none is distributed with the package — the style guide falls back to system fonts.
