# Clay public surface

A token set for the public pages that clay.earth sends you to. On 2026-11-19, `https://clay.earth/` returned 301 to `https://me.sh/`. The product on that domain is Mesh, the personal CRM that used to be Clay. This package is the `clay` slug. [systems/clay-earth](../clay-earth) is a different capture of the same redirect and was not edited.

Surfaces measured, signed out, in headless Chromium: the marketing home, pricing, integrations, teams, about, and contact on me.sh, plus the sign-in card at app.me.sh/login as a boundary. Desktop was 1440x900 at devicePixelRatio 1. Phone was 390x844 at devicePixelRatio 2. The T3 preview browser stayed on another site and then returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so the measurements come from the headless session. That session loaded the public pages with no Cloudflare challenge.

Nothing was typed. Apple, Google, SSO, Create account, and Sign in with email were not clicked. No phone, card, or CRM import was entered. AgentMail was not used.

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the page with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements, then written to [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) and [raw/readings-pass2.json](raw/readings-pass2.json). The hero CTA fill sits on an inner div, so that layer was read separately into [raw/hero-cta-layers.json](raw/hero-cta-layers.json). Stylesheets declared nine `@font-face` faces and no colour custom properties on the home page (`raw/css-vars-home.json`, `declared: 0`). Hover was a real pointer hover. Focus on the email field was a real focus, with nothing typed. The nav menu and the phone menu were opened with their own toggles and closed. Screenshots are full colour.

## What to distrust

The nav pill's own background computed as transparent. Its sheen is a `::before` gradient, recorded in the pass-2 reading, and it is not a flat surface token. Nav-link hover and programmatic focus did not change the computed colour. The email field's focus outline stayed `0px none`. The sign-in email button's hover did not change its fill. Verlag, VerlagCondensed, Chronicle, and SF Pro Text are named from computed styles and are not shipped, so the style guide falls back. The preview browser could not produce frames, so there is no preview-browser screenshot.

## Fonts

Verlag and VerlagCondensed are the marketing faces. ChronicleTextG1Roman and ChronicleCondensed are declared in `@font-face` and were not measured on a visible text node in this pass. The sign-in card uses SFProText / SF Pro Text. All four are proprietary and are not in this package. Tokens name them and fall back to system sans or Georgia.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint marketing routes black.** `html` is `#000` and `body` is transparent. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Do keep pricing on the cream ground.** `#f9f8f6` is a route, not a toggle. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Do set display type in VerlagCondensed.** The home headline is 64px, weight 700, `#fefef7`. [hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png)
- **Do ring the inverted hero pill.** The inner is `#fdfcfa` at 204x54. The ring is the measured cyan, violet, and amber gradient. [hero-cta-default.png](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png)
- **Do keep the sign-in card on SF Pro.** Its orange is `#ff6821`, not the marketing `#fb7232`. [signin-email-btn-default.png](components/crops/signin-email-btn-default.png)
- **Don't follow a CTA in a reconstruction of the signed-out page.** Plan and sign-in actions were measured and not submitted. [plan-cta-default.png](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png)
