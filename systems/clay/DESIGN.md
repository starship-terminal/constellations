## Type

Roobertvf for UI and display. Roobert mono is the declared secondary family. The home h1 is 88px, weight 575, line-height 88px, tracking -3.52px. Body is 16px. The font files are licensed and are not in this package.

## Radius

Nav and hero buttons paint 12px. The declared scale is 0.5rem, 0.75rem, 1rem, 1.25rem, 1.5rem, 2rem, 3rem, and a 999rem pill.

## What this file does not claim

Hover, focus, pressed, disabled, and dark theme were not measured. The auth wall is a separate app. Do not style it from the marketing buttons.
