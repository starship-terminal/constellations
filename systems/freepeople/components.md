# Components

What was on the public pages on 2026-11-22. States that were not shown are marked missing.

## Promo bar

44px strip, `#EDE6E0`, 12px Karla 700. It rotates. Pause, previous, and next are icon buttons. The text-signup link was not opened.

## Wordmark

200 by 20 SVG, fill `#2D2D2D`. The file is [icons/wordmark.svg](icons/wordmark.svg).

## Primary nav

12px uppercase Karla, 60px row, ink `#252423`. GIFTS and SALE are `#D52975`. Each item is a link plus a menu toggle. Clothes opens a white panel, about 1320 by 359, with three headings and a photo tile. Other menus were read from the DOM, not photographed.

## Search

150 by 26 field, placeholder SEARCH, radius 0, 12px Karla. Not typed. Not submitted.

## Utility icons

Country chip "SG ($)", Stores, Hearts, Cart, Account. Hearts and Account go to account routes and were not opened. Cart was not opened. Stores was not opened.

## Product tile

Homepage tray and the clothes grid. Name is a link. Price sits under the name on the grid. Heart badge on the product photo was not clicked.

## Add to Bag

45px bar, `#D52975`, white 12px uppercase label, letter-spacing 1.2px, radius 0. Not clicked. Size chips (XS through XL) were not selected.

## Size chip

Square outline, radius 0, label in Karla. Default only. Selected, disabled, and error were not shown.

## Colour swatch

Round chips under "Color: Red Scooter". A few carry a NEW badge. Not clicked.

## Help sidebar

On Our Story. Group headings (Quick Links, Order Info, Payment Methods, Connect With Us, Legal) and 14px links. Our Story is the current link. Contact, stores, and mailing list were not opened.

## Missing

Hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading, error, empty, toast, and dialog were not captured. The email field and the search field were left empty. No element crops were taken.
