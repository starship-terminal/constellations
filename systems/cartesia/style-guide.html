Palette

The declared layer carries the named --cartesia-* palette (greens, blues, oranges and the neutral ramps) under a shadcn-style semantic layer; light and dark roles are the same declared variables, re-bound by the .dark scope.

Spacing and radius

Spacing multiplies a 4px unit; the --radius scale runs 2-24px plus full, and the components paint inside it — 8px buttons and fields, 12px sign-in controls, 6px menu links, full-circle orbs and chips. Borders are quiet: #e4e3db border, #ededed boundary hairlines.

Motion

The --motion-* ramp runs 100-200ms over two declared eases; the marketing accordion and scroll reveal take their own --site-accordion (350ms) and --site-reveal (700ms, 60ms stagger) vars.

Type

Four declared faces: ABC Diatype on body and UI, PP Kyoto on display headlines, IBM Plex Mono on code and figures and Homemade Apple on handwriting accents; the docs app adds Inter. Sizes are the declared --text-* steps plus the measured 40px hero and 26px section display; samples fall back to system sans.
