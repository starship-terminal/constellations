Dub, measured

dub.co is three surfaces signed out: a Next.js/Tailwind marketing site, the app.dub.co auth shell on /login + /register, and the Mintlify docs/help surface. The declared token layer is Dub's semantic set — --bg-default/muted/subtle/emphasis/inverted and the status hues, --border-muted/subtle/default/emphasis, --content-inverted through --content-emphasis — plus a shadcn layer (--background/--foreground/--card/--primary/--ring) and Tailwind's --text-*/--container-*/--ease-* theme vars. What the atomic CSS never names was measured: the neutral ramp the marketing pages paint (#0a0a0a ink to #fafafa field), the black button fill with its 4px #e5e5e5 hover halo, 8px buttons / 6px fields / 12px cards, Satoshi 48/55.2 w500 display, and the docs surface's oklab color-mix washes. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The measured Tailwind-neutral ramp the marketing and auth surfaces paint, the Mintlify docs --gray ramp, the declared brand hues and the oklab color-mix washes; the light and dark roles are the declared semantic/shadcn variables resolved on each scope.

Spacing and radius

A 4px spacing unit and 320-1152px container steps are declared; the components paint 6-12px radii plus 9999px pills — 8px buttons, 6px fields, 12px cards and docs-nav items.

Type

Five stacks: Satoshi on marketing and docs headings (48/55.2 at 500 on the hero, 36/40 at 600 on docs), Inter on body and nav, Paper Mono on docs code, GeistMono on marketing code, and the plain system stack on the app.dub.co auth shell. Sizes are the declared --text-* steps plus measured display steps; samples fall back to system sans.
