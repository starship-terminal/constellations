# Patterns

## App shell

A 66px header stays fixed. On the homepage it sits on the hero. On a collection, a product, and About it is a white bar. The footer is a gray band with a newsletter column and link columns. The footer was read from the DOM and not photographed.

## Collection

A Georgia title, then a row of editorial tiles, then a product grid. Kith Winter 2026 is the photographed collection: [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Product

Breadcrumb, a thumbnail column, a large image, and a black buy column. The photographed product is Disney | Kith Kids Woody Jacket: [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png). Select a size was not clicked.

## Editorial page

About is a centered title, four paragraphs, and two photographs: [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Stops

Account, cart, search submit, newsletter, SMS, loyalty signup, contact, locations, and hospitality pages were not opened. The reasons are in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
