# Components

What was on the public pages. States that were not shown are marked not captured.

## Header

66px. Transparent over the homepage hero, white on interior pages. Logo centered, 54 by 23. Department buttons on the left, Hospitality and the three icon buttons on the right. See [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png) and [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured.

## Drawer

375px white panel under the header. Mens was opened. Rows are uppercase, with a caret when the row has a nested list. A feature image sits under the list. Nested rows were not opened. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Hero

Full-bleed image, 720px in the homepage layout, with a 28px Georgia title, a short paragraph, and two outline buttons: Shop now and Learn more. Both are white, 1px border, 41px tall, radius 0.

## Product card

Homepage rail cards are square images with a small category label, a title, and a price, all 12px Proxima Nova. The Winter 2026 page uses editorial tiles: a photograph, an 18px Georgia title, and a short paragraph.

## Product buy box

Black column. Collection link, 18px Georgia title, 12px price, color and fit lines, square size chips, and a gray Select a size bar. Select a size was not clicked, so Add to cart was not shown. See [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Size chip

79 by 41, 1px `#d9d9d9` border, radius 0. Default only. Selected, hover, and disabled were not measured.

## About

Centered 28px Georgia title, 12px body, then a two-column photo. See [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Not captured

Account drawer, search drawer, cart drawer, newsletter submit, text field focus, hover, error, loading, toast, and phone width.
