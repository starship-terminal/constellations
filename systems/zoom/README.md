# Zoom design reference

A token set for Zoom's public marketing site, for a dark homepage or a light pricing page. It was read from **zoom.com** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Playwright at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. The homepage hero is navy. Pricing, products, About, Trust, and Privacy are light. There is no public theme toggle.

origin/main had no `systems/zoom/`. This is a first capture. The slug is `zoom`. catalog.json was left alone.

Login and signup were photographed empty and not entered. Nothing was typed. No identity provider was clicked. No form was submitted. No meeting was joined. No captcha was solved. Contact Sales stopped on a Cloudflare check, and that shot was discarded.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages. Component colours, sizes, radii, and fonts were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. The marketing stylesheets on `st1.zoom.us` are cross-origin, so custom properties were not parsed from them. The tokens use the sRGB channels the computed styles returned (`rgb(11, 92, 255)` for Sign Up Free, `rgb(0, 5, 61)` for the footer). Screenshots were quantised to 256 colours after the values were read.

## What to distrust

- `muted` is Not captured. A secondary text colour was not isolated.
- The border role is Derived from the hairline under the header, not from a named custom property.
- Sign Up Free reported an 8px radius in one reading and 12px in the next. The 12px reading is the one on the visible header button.
- Sign-in Next is `rgb(13, 107, 222)`. Sign Up Free and Buy now are `rgb(11, 92, 255)`. They are two blues.
- Almaden Sans and Internacional are computed family names. The files are not in this package.
- The homepage was served with Singapore Dollars. Prices in the pricing shot are that currency.
- The sign-in field showed a blue focus ring because the field was focused when the page opened.
- Crops are 1x.

## Do and don't

- **Use one blue for Zoom's own sign-up.** Sign Up Free and Buy now are `rgb(11, 92, 255)`. The sign-in Next is a second blue, `rgb(13, 107, 222)`. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Keep Contact Sales pale.** It is a light blue pill beside the solid Sign Up Free. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Paint the homepage navy and the pricing page light.** The home has no theme toggle. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Set marketing headlines in Newsreader and controls in Inter.** Pricing and the auth walls use Almaden Sans. [about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png)
- **Leave Continue disabled until an email is present.** The signup wall shows that state with the field empty. [signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png)
