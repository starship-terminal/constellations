# Patterns

Public Zoom pages. Screenshots are 1440 x 900 or 390 x 844, at 1x, after a 256-colour quantize.

## Marketing home

Dark navy hero, white Newsreader headline, two pills (Explore products, Find your plan), then a product carousel. The header floats on the hero and turns white once the page is scrolled to the footer. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)

The footer is navy, four columns, with language and currency selects. [home-dark-footer.png](screenshots/home-dark-footer.png)

## Menus

Products, AI, and Solutions open the same white mega menu, with a left rail and a link pane. [home-dark-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-dark-products-menu.png) [home-dark-ai-menu.png](screenshots/home-dark-ai-menu.png) [home-dark-solutions-menu.png](screenshots/home-dark-solutions-menu.png)

Meet is a small white menu. [home-dark-meet-menu.png](screenshots/home-dark-meet-menu.png)

The grid button opens a right drawer of product shortcuts. [home-dark-more-drawer.png](screenshots/home-dark-more-drawer.png)

Search drops a field under the header. It was not typed. [home-dark-search.png](screenshots/home-dark-search.png)

## Pricing

Light canvas, a left list of products, and plan cards. Pro carries a blue "Most popular" cap. The annual switch was already on. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
