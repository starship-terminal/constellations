Aboard HR design tokens

Measured from aboardhr.com's public marketing pages in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. aboardhr.com is a Framer site: its design tokens are hashed --token-<uuid> custom properties resolved live and recorded with their provenance in the token file; component values are computed styles read off the running pages. Several brand fills paint in Display P3; the token file records the sRGB conversion. One theme; the book-a-demo form was never submitted and the app sign-in was never opened.

The HR product itself sits behind a sign-in at app.aboardhr.com and is not part of this package. Screenshots and per-component readings are in components.md.

Palette

A flat, quiet palette: a #fafafa ground, #262626 ink text at three opacities, pastel card tints (blue, mint, pink, lavender) and a single bright blue accent (#009cff) that every primary CTA carries.

Type

One face does everything: the OS-ui stack (San Francisco on Apple devices, Segoe/Roboto elsewhere). Display headlines run 64px semibold at 1.1 tracked -0.6px (48px at phone width); card titles 44px; buttons 18px semibold; body copy 15-20px medium. Inter appears only inside the book-a-demo form.

The stack resolves to the visitor's OS font; this page previews it directly.

Space, radius & structure

Every CTA is a full 99px pill on a 48px height with 24px side padding; product cards round to 24px (the partner card 32px); form controls to 10px. The fixed header is 90px on desktop, 64px on the phone; the content column centres at 1024px.

Motion

aboardhr.com animates in JavaScript (Framer springs): computed transitions on the components read 0s everywhere except the form's clear control, which fades colour over 150ms. There is no declared easing token.

duration-fast 150ms (click)

Navigation

The fixed header measured 1440 × 90: the wordmark, Product / Price / Resources links in 15px medium, and on the right a Sign-in link plus the light-blue "Book a demo" pill. A hovered link washes grey at 8%; keyboard focus draws the browser's own #005fcc ring, the site declares none.

Buttons

Every button is a 48px full pill in 18px semibold. Primary is bright blue with white text ("Book a demo", "Try for free" on the product pages); secondary is the light-blue tint with deep-blue text (nav CTA, plan CTA); the ghost "Learn more" frosts white at 60% over a lavender fill.

Forms

The one public form is book-a-demo: Inter labels (#888, 12px) over 40px controls filled ink-at-5% with 10px radius, and a submit that draws a 2px ink-at-10% ring instead of a fill. Measured at rest and at focus; never submitted.
