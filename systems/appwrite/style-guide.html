Palette

--brand-cta (#fd366e) is the action pink; the surface runs on the background / card / muted / accent neutrals and the foreground / muted-foreground text pair. The --color-* ramps under core feed the utilities, charts and status roles.

Type

Aeonik Pro carries marketing (its own --font-aeonik-pro); Inter is the product face. The Tailwind --text-* scale runs xs through 4xl with paired line-heights.

Radius and geometry

--radius is 10px; the measured controls wear 8px corners and the plan/sign-in cards 14px. Containers step xs to 7xl; spacing is a 4px base.
