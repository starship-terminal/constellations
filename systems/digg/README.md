# Digg design reference

A token set and component reference for Digg's public signed-out web app ([https://digg.com/](https://digg.com/)). Digg is a news reader: topic feeds, story pages, and public profiles, with login and signup as walls. It was captured signed out, in headless Chromium, on 2026-11-21. Desktop shots are 1440 x 900 at 1x. Phone shots are 390 x 844 CSS pixels at 2x (PNG width 780). Themes are light and dark. The theme control cycles Light, Dark, and System on `<html class>`.

The signed-in product was not entered. Login asks for email and password, or Google or X. No password was typed, no code was sent, and Google and X were not clicked. Follow on a public profile redirects to the login wall and that branch stops there.

This package contains no Digg code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. Screenshots and the visual system they show belong to Digg Inc. ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo or brand blue.

## How the values were read

Playwright loaded public URLs in headless Chromium. `tools/capture/public/read.mjs` walked every stylesheet (none were blocked) and resolved custom properties on `<html>` in light and in dark. `tools/capture/public/colors.mjs` painted each semantic role onto a probe and sampled sRGB with a canvas, because several roles compute as `lab()` even when the authored value is hex. `tools/capture/public/measure-live.mjs` read `getComputedStyle` on the controls named in [components.md](components.md).

The cookie notice was hidden with CSS. Its Accept, Only necessary, and Save preferences buttons were not clicked. Cookie preferences was opened from the footer so the dialog could be photographed, then left unsaved.

Declared hex (the blue, red, yellow, and gray ramps, and the roles Digg writes as `#fdfdff` and `#161617`) is Measured from the stylesheet. Roles that only exist as computed `lab()` or `color-mix` are Measured from the canvas sample. Radius steps that are `calc(var(--radius) * n)` are Derived from `--radius: .625rem`. A value that was not read is Not captured, in the coverage table and in `tokens.json` `$extensions.notTokenised`.

## What to distrust

- **Hover colours.** Sign up hover computed as `oklab(0.549935 -0.0324798 -0.210823 / 0.8)`. That is not stored as a hex.
- **Green ramp.** Only `--green-500` was sampled (`#6aa15a`). The other green steps are `lab()` in the stylesheet and were not converted.
- **Secondary in dark.** The stylesheet declares `--secondary: #242424` on `.dark`. The canvas sample of the computed value was `#252525`. The token keeps the declared hex.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Login password field.** A probe of `/login` listed email and password. The photographed login paint showed email and Continue, and not the password field. Signup shows both fields. Do not treat the login screenshot as proof the password field is absent.
- **Story display size.** The home title link measured 16px / 600. The story page headline size was not measured.
- **Pressed, disabled, loading, error.** Not shown, except the class `active:translate-y-px` on buttons, which was not held.

## Fonts

Roobert is the UI face: `--font-sans` is `"roobert", "roobert Fallback", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`, and `@font-face` registers `roobert` at weight 100–900. `--font-mono` aliases the same face, so it is not a monospace stack. IBM Plex Sans and VT323 are registered as `@font-face` families. Where those two are applied on the captured pages was not measured. The font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep one blue for the product's own actions.** Sign up, Follow, Continue, and the selected phone topic use `#1c66eb`. Secondary actions are the quiet `#f0f0f5` pill. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do set the UI in Roobert.** Titles, nav, and buttons share `--font-sans`. [screenshots/story-light-default.png](screenshots/story-light-default.png)
- **Do use pills for sidebar and feed controls.** Sidebar rows and the Trending control are fully rounded. Story cards stay at 18px. [screenshots/home-light-feed-view-open.png](screenshots/home-light-feed-view-open.png)
- **Do keep cards flat, with a hairline or a quiet fill.** The right rail cards sit on white with a light border. The feed rows do not float on a shadow. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Don't treat the login wall as the product.** The feed, story, about, and profile are public. Follow, and anything past Continue, is a wall. [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png)

## Rebuild

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node systems/digg/tools/capture/public/read.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node systems/digg/tools/capture/public/colors.mjs
python3 systems/digg/tools/build_tokens.py
python3 systems/digg/tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/digg/raw-shots
```

Readings stay in `~/.design-systems/digg/`. The package does not contain account text, an email address, or a client address. Contact screenshots hide the published address links before the shot, so those strings are not in the PNGs. A sample inbox line on the World and Business feed is painted out. Public handles stay.
