Spacing and radius

Sidebar items, primary CTAs and the feed vote pill are full pills (calc(infinity * 1px); the story digg-it is a 999px rounded-full); controls are 26px, story cards and rail panels 18px, auth fields 8px and source links 2px. Hairlines are rgba(0,0,0,0.08), fields #e5e5e5, and the focus ring #ababbf.
