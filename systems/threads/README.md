# Threads

Public signed-out chrome of [Threads](https://www.threads.com/) (threads.com), captured on 2026-11-19. No account was created, Instagram was not used to log in, and no phone number was entered. The live signed-out theme is dark (`html` class `__fb-dark-mode`). Light values come from forcing `__fb-light-mode` on the same page, which remaps the published custom properties. That is a class-forced reconstruction of the published remap, not a theme toggle the capture clicked.

Surfaces: the signed-out home feed with the login card and the "Say more with Threads" dialog, `/login`, the same two pages at 390 CSS pixels, and the public Threads Terms and privacy supplemental on help.instagram.com. Desktop shots are 1440 by 900. The T3 preview tab that supplied the computed styles was 1169 by 731 at device pixel ratio 2. Preview screenshots failed ("The browser is not producing screenshot frames"), so the PNGs are headless Chromium of pages that had already loaded in that tab without a Cloudflare challenge.

## How the values were read

Computed styles and custom properties were read in the T3 preview tab on https://www.threads.com/ and https://www.threads.com/login, signed out, on 2026-11-19. Dark is the class the page shipped (`__fb-dark-mode`). Light was read after replacing that class with `__fb-light-mode` on `documentElement`. `--color-scheme` stayed `dark` after the swap. Body background and the named colour roles did flip. Hashed `--x*` custom properties were dropped. Named variables are in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json).

Component sizes (the 337 by 289 login card, 18 px fields, 24 px card radius, dialog padding) were read from `getComputedStyle` and `getBoundingClientRect` in that same tab. Screenshots were taken later in headless Chromium at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, after those URLs had loaded in the preview tab with no Cloudflare challenge. Help pages were opened in the same preview tab, then shot headless. No field was filled. Log in was not pressed. Continue with Instagram was not followed.

## What to distrust

The painted Threads controls do not use the generic Facebook design-system radii. `--button-corner-radius` is 6 px. The login field, the Log in button, the login card, and the Instagram wall button compute to 18, 18, 24, and 16 px. Both numbers are in [tokens.json](tokens.json). Use the measured `core.radius.login-*` and `core.radius.instagram-*` tokens for Threads chrome. The 6 px token is the declared custom property, not the painted control.

`--primary-button-background` is blue (`#2374E1` dark, `#1B74E4` light). The Log in button on `/login` paints white in dark and black in light. `color.control.button-background` is the painted button. The blue value is stored as a declared custom property under the status and border focus tokens, not as the Threads login face.

Light screenshots are a class-forced remap. The capture did not find a theme toggle on the signed-out page. `--color-scheme` did not follow the class.

The login-card crop in [components/crops/card-login-default.png](components/crops/card-login-default.png) sits under the signup dialog scrim, so its pixels read darker than `#1e1e1e`. The token is the computed style, not the crop.

`core.spacing.1` (4 px) and `core.spacing.2` (8 px) are Derived from declared radius tokens (`--radius-image` and `--radius-card`). The other spacing steps were measured on a control or a custom property. Sources say which.

help.instagram.com is a different chrome (12 px body, `#fafafa` page, `#00376b` links). Those colours live only under `light.color.help.*`. They are not Threads app tokens.

The phone-width light theme was not shot. Hover, pressed, error, disabled, and loading were not provoked, because a press on these controls starts Instagram login or submits the form. One focus ring was observed on the dialog's Instagram button after a click that missed a legal link. `shadow.focus` matches that ring and the declared `--focus-ring-shadow-default`. Reduced motion was not emulated. About 230 hashed keyframes were left untokensised.

T3 preview returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" twice. The PNGs are the headless set. Sidecars say so.

## Fonts

The page sets `--font-family-system` to `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. Computed body and controls include `system-ui` twice and omit BlinkMacSystemFont. The token keeps the custom property. Login fields compute to `--font-family-default`: `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. Code is `--font-family-code`: `ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas, Monaco, monospace`. These are system faces. No webfont file was downloaded. Weights in use are 400, 600, 700, and 800. 500 is declared and unused on the signed-out chrome.

## Do and don't

- **Paint the login button as the inverse of the page, not as the blue Facebook primary.** On dark it is white with near-black text. On light it is black with white text. See [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png) and [components/crops/button-primary-light.png](components/crops/button-primary-light.png).
- **Use the measured radii on Threads chrome.** Fields and the Log in button are 18 px, the home login card is 24 px, the card's Instagram button is 16 px, and the dialog's Instagram button is 20 px. The declared 6 px button radius is a different system. See [components/crops/text-field-default.png](components/crops/text-field-default.png) and [components/crops/card-login-default.png](components/crops/card-login-default.png).
- **Keep the signup wall as a wall.** Continue with Instagram and the username form were not entered. Reproduce the chrome, and do not invent the next step. See [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) and [components/crops/dialog-signup-default.png](components/crops/dialog-signup-default.png).
- **Do not mix help.instagram.com type into the app scale.** Help titles are 24 px on a `#fafafa` page. Threads body copy is 15 px on `#101010`. See [screenshots/terms-light-default.png](screenshots/terms-light-default.png).
