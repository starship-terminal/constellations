# Marathon design reference

Tokens and component notes for Marathon (onmarathon.com), captured **2026-11-18**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. The public site paints one light theme (`html.light`). A `.dark` set is declared and was resolved on a probe. It was not painted on a public page.

The site is an archive. The banner says "You're looking at the Marathon archive." `/sign-in` and `/register` redirect to `/`. Those forms were not submitted. Pricing lists Free at $0 and Pro at $49 per user per month billed annually. Get Started links to `/register` and was not used as checkout. No purchase was made.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export marathon` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint a light gray page with a lime archive banner.
2. Do fill actions with ink, not lime.
3. Do spend lime on the banner and the Pro card.
4. Do use pills.
5. Do set the archive headline at 46.2px / 500.
6. Don't invent a painted dark theme.
7. Don't treat Get Started as checkout.
8. Don't style the signed-in app from the agencies demo.
9. Don't submit Book A Demo from this package.
10. Don't copy the logomark.
11. Don't expect the browser focus ring to be a product token.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

Fonts: `core.font.family.sans` is Inter, then 3 fallbacks. Scales, counted as [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds them: 6 type styles (`core.typography`), 4 radius steps (`core.radius`), 6 spacing steps (`core.space`).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-18, light, 1440. Hover is a real pointer. Focus-visible is keyboard. Pressed is a forced `:active` pseudo-class, so no control fired. No cookie banner appeared. The hide path was ready and was never a click. Stylesheets were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Motion was probed on `/`, `/pricing` and `/for-agencies` into [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json). The product ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## What to distrust

- Sign-in and register. Both routes redirect to the archive home. No fields painted. Nothing was submitted.
- Checkout, payment and the billing portal. Get Started points at `/register`. It was not followed into a purchase.
- The app at app.onmarathon.com. Account, dashboard and workspace sit behind sign-in. The agencies page shows a public demo board only.
- Book A Demo. The SavvyCal embed loaded a spinner and was not submitted.
- Dark theme. Declared in CSS, not painted on a captured public page.
- Choc Chip CSS. The stylesheet is cross-origin and unreadable.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes.
- Focus-visible on Get Started. The 2026-11-18 script left the pointer on each control from its hover reading through pressed and focus-visible, so the `focus-visible` readings of `primary-button`, `primary-button-lg` and `secondary-button` in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) carry the hover fill (`rgb(64, 64, 64)`, `oklch(0.92 0 0)`), not the fill at rest. Read only their outline as the focus state. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now moves the pointer away before every state; those readings need a recapture.
- `raw/css-vars.json` `darkDeclared` is empty. The dark values are in its `app` rules (`.dark` and the `:root` inside `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`) and in `resolved.dark`. `tools/capture/vars-live.mjs` now fills it from those rules.
- The plan-card crop. The selector matched a transparent wrapper, not the card face. Use [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- Careers, privacy and terms. Those links leave the site for neverbeforeseen.group and were not followed.

## Fonts

Inter, loaded by Next as a variable face (100 to 900). Computed on body as `Inter, "Inter Fallback", system-ui, sans-serif`. Graphik is declared as `--font-graphik` and was not the painted body face. The files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint a light gray page with a lime archive banner.** The banner is `#d7fd51` and the page field is white in the token, slightly off-white on screen ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
2. **Do fill actions with ink, not lime.** Get Started is `#0b0b0b` with white type, and hovers to `#404040` ([button](components/crops/primary-button-hover.png)).
3. **Do spend lime on the banner and the Pro card.** Popular is an ink pill on that card ([pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)).
4. **Do use pills.** Buttons, the billing segment and the badge are `9999px` radius ([segment](components/crops/segment-selected-default.png)).
5. **Do set the archive headline at 46.2px / 500.** Display on the agencies page is 81.4px / 500 ([heading](components/crops/section-heading-default.png)).
6. **Don't invent a painted dark theme.** `.dark` is declared and was not painted on a public page.
7. **Don't treat Get Started as checkout.** It links to `/register`, which redirects home. No purchase was made ([redirect](screenshots/register-light-redirect.png)).
8. **Don't style the signed-in app from the agencies demo.** That board is a public demo, not the account or dashboard ([agencies](screenshots/agencies-light-default.png)).
9. **Don't submit Book A Demo from this package.** The SavvyCal embed was not submitted ([demo](screenshots/demo-light-default.png)).
10. **Don't copy the logomark.** The mark is Marathon's. Tokens name the system ([logomark](icons/logomark.svg)).
11. **Don't expect the browser focus ring to be a product token.** Focus is `1px` `#005fcc` ([focus](components/crops/nav-link-focus-visible.png)).
