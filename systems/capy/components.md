## Gaps

Hover, pressed, disabled, and loading were not measured. The focus ring was read on the Product menu link (`rgb(229, 151, 0)`, 1px, offset 1px) and is the orange outline in the menu crop. Checkbox, radio, switch, select, toast, table, and avatar were not present on the measured marketing pages. The demo form has text fields and a team-size radio group. Those fields were left empty and were not submitted.
