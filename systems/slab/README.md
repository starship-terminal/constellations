# Slab

Slab (slab.com) is knowledge-base and wiki software. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out login and signup walls. Tokens were measured on 2026-11-19. A 2026-11-22 pass mapped the public control tree into [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and photographed the pages those controls open. No account was created. Google signup was not clicked. The register form asks for an email, a full name and a password, so it was not submitted. `login-extracted` stays not-started. catalog.json was left alone.

Computed styles were read in a T3 preview tab at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, devicePixelRatio 2. The 2026-11-22 photographs are headless Playwright at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, devicePixelRatio 2, after those pages loaded without a Cloudflare challenge.

### Principles

1. Use plum for the home hero and white for interior pages.
2. Use coral for the marketing primary.
3. Use Sentinel for headlines and Whitney for body.
4. Keep the login card separate from marketing chrome.
5. Do not invent a signed-in wiki.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties on https://slab.com/ are only Swiper's `--swiper-theme-color` and `--swiper-navigation-size`. Product colours are plain `rgb()` in CSS modules. Component colour, type, radius, padding and size were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Hover and active button colours were read from the same page's stylesheet rules (`.Button-module--primary` and `.UntitledButton-module--primary`), because those states were not shown. Spacing steps were read from `.Spacing-module--padding-*`. The root `font-size` computed as 10px, so 1rem is 10px.

`preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" twice. The measurements above were kept. Headless Playwright then opened only https://slab.com/ and https://slab.com/pricing/, which had already loaded in the preview tab without a challenge. It did not open login or register.

## What to distrust

The live CSS viewport was 1169, not 1440. The 1440 and 390 screenshots are a later headless pass of the marketing pages, not the viewport the computed styles were read at. Hover, pressed and disabled colours come from stylesheet rules, not from a shown state. The home email field's programmatic focus did not paint the cyan focus ring the stylesheet declares, so that ring is a rule, not a shown state. `core.color.focus` and `light.border.focus` (`#e59700`) are Chromium's default focus ring on a focused link, labelled Observed: the product does not style it, so do not ship it as a brand focus colour. No dark theme was found. `prefers-color-scheme` was dark in the preview tab and the page stayed on the plum and white palette. IBM Plex Mono is declared in an `@font-face` and was not used on the pages opened. Screenshot colours are 24-bit PNGs. Read values from the tokens.

## Fonts

Whitney is the marketing sans (`whitney, system-ui, helvetica, sans-serif`). Sentinel is the marketing serif (`sentinel, georgia, serif`). Untitled Sans is the login and register face. Roboto is loaded only for the "Sign up with Google" glyph. IBM Plex Mono is declared and unused on these pages. The font files are self-hosted on slab.com and are not in this package. Tokens name the computed families and fall back to system-ui, Helvetica, and Georgia.

## Do and don't

- **Use plum for the home hero and white for interior pages.** Home nav and hero are `#42022e`. Pricing nav and page are `#ffffff`. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- **Use coral for the marketing primary.** Get Started is `#ff4143`, 6px radius, white Whitney at 18px and weight 600. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Use Sentinel for headlines and Whitney for body.** The hero is Sentinel 42px. Body is Whitney 20px / 32px. [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- **Keep the login card separate from marketing chrome.** Login uses Untitled Sans, a 2px radius, and a plum Continue button on a `#fcf9f8` page. [components.md](components.md).
- **Do not invent a signed-in wiki.** Register asks for a password. It was not submitted. The product app was not opened.
