FlutterFlow style guide

FlutterFlow, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json, read signed-out from flutterflow.io: the --ff-* design layer (colour roles, type, radii, space, sizes, shadows, motion, the .ff-drip widget kit and z-order), the legacy shorthand the home page's earlier skin still reads, and the values measured on the real components. The header's pick sets data-theme on <html> — the same mechanism as the site's footer switch. The mock controls below are rebuilt from the tokens; the real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Each token's provenance (the declaring selector, or the measurement it came from) is in the tables at the bottom and in tokens.json under $extensions.source. Inter is self-hosted by the site and loaded here from Google Fonts; the mono roles use the platform's ui-monospace stack.

Colour

color.* is the semantic layer every page reads (rebound per theme); legacy.* is the shorthand the home page's earlier skin declares and the legacy sheet re-binds per theme; drip.* belongs to the .ff-drip UI kit used inside the site's own demos and widgets. Flip the theme pick to watch the sets move.

Type

One face, Inter, in every role: the --ff-text-* shorthands are parsed into typography.* tokens. Sizes written as clamp() are shown resolved at 1440px; the 390px reading is on each token's $extensions.responsive.

Radius, space and size

Radii are pill-led (fields and buttons are 999px), cards 20px, page sheets 16. The spacing and content sizes are single-purpose names rather than a numbered scale.

Elevation

Ring-plus-drop shadows: the field lift, its focus variant (a 4px ink ring added), resting and floating chrome.

Motion

Two durations — 120ms fast (hovers, presses) and 240ms base (panels) — over two bezier easings and a linear() spring. Click a track to run it. The measured group is what the elements actually run; prefers-reduced-motion strips most of it (raw/motion.json).
