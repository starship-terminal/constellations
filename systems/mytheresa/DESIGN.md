## Typography

AvenirNextLTPro-Medium is the UI face. OptimaLTPro-Roman is the display face on the hero, the category heading, the product designer, and the product price. Neither file is in this package. The style guide falls back to Palatino and Avenir.

| Style | Size | Weight | Line |

| page title | 70px | 400 | 77px |

Letter-spacing on the measured styles is 0.5px. Nav and department labels are uppercase.

## Geometry

Controls use a 0 radius. The promo ribbon is 32px. The header, including the ribbon, wordmark row, and department row, is 158px. A department link is 73px. Add to bag is 44px by 402px. The size control is 46px. The newsletter email field is 48px.

## Components

Add to bag is ink with white type, 44px, square corners. It was measured and not clicked. The size control reads "Choose your size". An unavailable size is `#757575`. A selected About us tab is `#f2f2f2`.
