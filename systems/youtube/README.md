# YouTube design reference

A token set and component reference for YouTube's iOS app. Use it to build a native iOS video or media feed (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-black or white page, one neutral grey for every raised control, rounded-rectangle filter chips that invert when selected, capsule buttons drawn in the theme's ink, edge-to-edge 16:9 thumbnails with a translucent duration badge, and a five-item tab bar with a round Create button. YouTube red is kept for the brand and watch progress. It was captured from the shipped app through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account on the free plan: dark on 2026-10-23 with Appearance set to Use device theme and the phone in dark mode, light on 2026-10-24 with Settings > General > Appearance > Light theme, put back to Use device theme afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 31 captures: 18 in dark (Premium interstitial, home with an ad and the Shorts shelf, My Ad Centre, search, a Short, the You tab, the sort sheet, Settings, General, Appearance, Subscriptions with chips, the overflow menu, the watch page and the mini player) and 13 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) nothing in them is masked: they show the owner's channel name, handle, avatar, search history and watch history, and no credentials or personally identifiable data. The capture opened one video (now in the owner's watch history) and liked, subscribed, commented and searched for nothing.

This package contains no YouTube code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Google's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)); the thumbnails, Shorts and ads in them are other creators' and advertisers' work and are kept only to show layout. Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, icons or YouTube red.

A public web pass was added on 2026-11-21 from https://www.youtube.com, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop shots are 1440 x 900 at 1x. Phone shots were requested at 390 x 844 and saved at 780 x 1688 because the mobile page reports a 2x device pixel ratio. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). Captured: the empty home, the guide, the settings menu, the voice dialog, search results and the filter dialog, a public watch page, the save sign-in prompt, subscriptions, library and history empty states, /premium, https://about.youtube/, and the empty Google sign-in and create-account walls, in dark and light where the page honours the color scheme. Login and signup were photographed empty and the branch stopped. No email, password, code, phone or address was typed. Next was not clicked. Identity providers were not clicked. Like, subscribe, comment and playlist save were not used. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`. Web values are in [tokens.public.json](tokens.public.json). The iOS tokens.json above is unchanged.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export youtube` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do use one neutral grey for every raised control.
2. Do mark selection and the primary action by inverting, not by colouring.
3. Do keep YouTube red for the brand and progress.
4. Do let the content carry the colour.
5. Don't draw dividers between rows.
6. Do keep blue for links, new-content dots and switches that are on.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

YouTube is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 109 named readings, most in both themes. The light captures are laid out differently on several screens (other videos, other ads, the menu opening elsewhere), so a reading can name a box per theme. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps the readings to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (a material, a tint, a thin mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph or hairline colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal; `corner`, the same for a thumbnail with no flat fill | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of ink bands | Derived | A line height, or a row height when the bands are row labels or icons |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font family |

Two values are alphas worked out from a pair of readings: the scrim (the You tab reads #F1F1F1 and #FFFFFF undimmed and #787878 and #7F7F7F under the sort sheet, black at 50% in both themes) and the duration badge (#666666 over a white frame, black at 60%). Scale steps (spacing, radii, the tab bar height) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in dark only (the comments card, the Subscribe pill, the interstitial's button), the light token is Derived and says what it was filled from.

## What to distrust

- **The font.** Not determinable from pixels. Body text looks like Roboto and headings like a heavier display face; `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured.
- **Tinted blocks.** In dark, the block behind an ad and the watch page's title area take a tint from the artwork: the ad block reads #020F1B (navy) under one ad and a dark plum under another ([13-subscriptions-avatars-chips](screenshots/dark/13-subscriptions-avatars-chips.png)). `surface.ad` and `button.tonal.bg` hold one reading each. Light reads neutral greys.
- **The tab bar is a material.** It is translucent over the feed, so `surface.tabBar` is one moment's reading (46% share in dark).
- **My Ad Centre stays dark in light.** Only its header row turns white ([03 light](screenshots/light/03-ad-centre-sheet-accordions.png)), so its tokens (`surface.adSheet`, `border.accordion`, `text.linkOnSheet`) carry the dark value in both sets. Take sheet values from the sort sheet.
- **Light nav titles are not captured.** On the pushed Settings pages the light title reads nearly white on white ([10 light](screenshots/light/10-settings-icon-list.png), [11 light](screenshots/light/11-general-switches-on-off.png)), likely mid-transition. Use `text.primary`.
- **The player controls are not shown.** 17-player-controls-overlay-scrubber is the same paused watch page as 16; the controls and scrubber had faded before the capture.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: chips read 8.5, thumbnails 7.7 and 6.0, cards and sheets 11.9, the menu 17.1.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em; weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights (helper text 17 pt, a two-line title 20 pt).
- **The light caret may be mid-blink.** It reads #BABABA where the dark caret reads #F1F1F1.
- **The neutral list is a list.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the two themes use, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published ramp.
- **No pressed, disabled or error states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** The captures are stills made with clicks, nothing was liked, subscribed or searched, and iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; thumbnails and the tab bar band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do use one neutral grey for every raised control.** Unselected chips, the search field and the library chips all share `surface.secondary`; text is the theme's ink or one secondary grey. See [13-subscriptions-avatars-chips](screenshots/dark/13-subscriptions-avatars-chips.png), [04-search-focused-history-rows](screenshots/dark/04-search-focused-history-rows.png) and [08 light](screenshots/light/08-you-tab-profile-history-library-chips.png).
2. **Do mark selection and the primary action by inverting, not by colouring.** The selected chip and the filled buttons turn the theme's ink with a page-coloured label; no button is filled red or blue. See [14-chip-selected-video-cards-preview](screenshots/dark/14-chip-selected-video-cards-preview.png), [02-home-ad-shorts-shelf-tabbar](screenshots/dark/02-home-ad-shorts-shelf-tabbar.png) and [02 light](screenshots/light/02-home-ad-shorts-shelf-tabbar.png).
3. **Do keep YouTube red for the brand and progress.** Red appears in the logo, the watch-progress bar and the Subscriptions tab dot, never on a button or an error. See [08-you-tab-profile-history-library-chips](screenshots/dark/08-you-tab-profile-history-library-chips.png) and [02-home-ad-shorts-shelf-tabbar](screenshots/dark/02-home-ad-shorts-shelf-tabbar.png).
4. **Do let the content carry the colour.** Feed thumbnails run edge to edge, and in dark the block under an ad or a playing video takes a tint from its artwork. See [14-chip-selected-video-cards-preview](screenshots/dark/14-chip-selected-video-cards-preview.png), [13-subscriptions-avatars-chips](screenshots/dark/13-subscriptions-avatars-chips.png) and [16-watch-page-actions-comments-teaser](screenshots/dark/16-watch-page-actions-comments-teaser.png).
5. **Don't draw dividers between rows.** Settings, search and menu rows are separated by space alone, on a 50 pt or 56 pt pitch. See [10-settings-icon-list](screenshots/dark/10-settings-icon-list.png), [04-search-focused-history-rows](screenshots/dark/04-search-focused-history-rows.png) and [15-video-overflow-menu](screenshots/dark/15-video-overflow-menu.png).
6. **Do keep blue for links, new-content dots and switches that are on.** The accent never fills a button. See [13-subscriptions-avatars-chips](screenshots/dark/13-subscriptions-avatars-chips.png) and [11-general-switches-on-off](screenshots/dark/11-general-switches-on-off.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/youtube/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/youtube/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_public_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

The public web shots are rewritten by `node tools/capture/public/capture.mjs` with `PLAYWRIGHT` pointing at a Playwright install. Full-colour frames stay in `~/.design-systems/youtube/raw-shots/`.

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.secondary`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system tab bar, sheets and menus with these fills rather than redrawing them; the switch is Material-style (a round thumb overhanging a thin track), so it needs a custom control or the system switch tinted with `switch.onThumb`. Draw tinted blocks from the artwork, not from `surface.ad`. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
