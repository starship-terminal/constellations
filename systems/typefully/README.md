# Typefully

Typefully is a social media scheduler (typefully.com). This package covers the public marketing site, the pricing page, and the signed-out email dialog, read on 19 November 2026. Themes are light and dark, switched with `prefers-color-scheme`. Widths are 1440 and 390. Nothing was submitted. Google, X/Twitter and LinkedIn were not clicked.

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on typefully.com and typefully.com/pricing, at 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2. Custom properties were read from the same-origin stylesheets: the `:root` light set (`--light-*Hsl`) and the dark set under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` on `:root:not(.light-mode)`. Component boxes were `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Probe elements resolved `var(--bg0)` and the other role names to rgb.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Screenshots were taken with headless Playwright on the same two public URLs, which had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. The email dialog was opened by clicking Email on the marketing page. Continue was not clicked. Google, X/Twitter and LinkedIn were not clicked. `/login` is a public 404 and was not used as the sign-in surface.

## What to distrust

The marketing page follows the OS colour scheme. Tokens in `light` and `dark` are the painted surfaces under each scheme. A class of `dark-mode` on `html` was not set by hand.

OAuth borders and the Log in border are `oklab()` colours. The tokens store the nearest opaque hex from the role they match (`--c6`, `--c3`). The computed oklab strings are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

The focus ring is a 20% oklab mix of the accent, painted as a 4px shadow. `border.focus` stores the accent at 20% alpha (`#199AF533`). That is a flattening, not the mix the browser computed.

The dark email field and the dark dialog were not opened. Those two dark component tokens reuse the dark surface colour and are marked Derived.

The 256-colour screenshots band the hero gradient and the product demo. Read colour from the tokens.

Inter, inter-arrows-subset, GT America and Fira Code are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Fonts

Inter is the computed face on the page, the headings and the buttons. The stack continues with system-ui, Helvetica Neue and Segoe UI. The files are not committed.

inter-arrows-subset is the computed face on tweet handles inside the public demo (`--font-twitter`), then GT America. Neither file is here.

Fira Code is declared as `--font-code`. No code specimen was measured.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are the blue pill.** Sign up, Continue and the Business Get started button use `#199AF5` with white type. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Secondary actions are a quiet button.** Log in is a hairline outline. Get started on Free and Creator is a white button with a grey border. [components/crops/button-nav-default.png](components/crops/button-nav-default.png)
- **OAuth choices sit in a row of pills.** Google, X/Twitter, LinkedIn and Email share one height and one radius. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **The page follows the OS scheme.** Light is white. Dark is `#111418`. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Headings are Inter at 700.** The hero is 44px. Section headings are 36px. Plan names are 18px at 600. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Rebuild

python3 tools/package_tests.py typefully
