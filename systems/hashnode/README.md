# Hashnode design reference

A token set and component reference for Hashnode's signed-out surfaces — for building a developer-blogging product, publication pages, marketing sections and reading experiences in the style of the real thing. It was read from **hashnode.com** and **townhall.hashnode.com** on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: every public route the marketing/app surface serves — /, /feed, a public post (/posts/…), a public profile (/@anish78), /pro, /agents, /changelog, /hackathons, /brand, /alternatives/medium, /code-of-conduct, /onboard (rest only) and /blog (which redirects to the blog engine) — plus the Town Hall blog's index, article and archive — in the painted light theme, with verified dark repaints on home, feed, post, pro, changelog and both blog surfaces; the Ctrl+K search dialogs, the Theme and More menus, an opened FAQ accordion, category and install tab switches, the sidebar toggle and the 390px nav sheet as transient states; plus 390px passes on home, feed, post, pro and the blog index.

hashnode.com is worth a capture because it declares a complete token layer to the browser: a Tailwind v4 `@theme` plus a hand-written semantic layer — `--background`/`--foreground`, `--primary`/`--secondary`/`--accent`/`--muted`/`--card`/`--popover` and their `-foreground` partners, the `--destructive`/`--success`/`--warning` families (including the `-wash`/`-cream`/`-border` tints), `--disabled-foreground`, `--highlight`, `--toolbar`, `--border`/`--input`/`--ring`, the `--brand`/`--link` blue (**#2962ff** light / **#6293ff** dark), the `--sidebar-*` and `--chart-1..5` sets, `--code-block(-foreground|-muted)`, `--reading-text`, `--surface`, `--scrim`, `--selection`, `--scroll-shadow`, `--scrollbar-thumb` — plus the scene palettes (`--pro-*` gold, `--buildful` green, `--ai-*` gradient, `--landing-*`, the 36-name `--tod-*` time-of-day hero art), the `--elevation-xs..lg` shadow stacks, the system-stack `--font-app` and next/font `--font-inter` faces, the `--text-xs..6xl` ramp and the `--text-body`/`-label`/`-reading`/`-title-*` prose roles, `--radius`/`-xs`, `--spacing`, `--container-*` and motion vars. The architecture: every page boots **html.light**; hashnode.com's own sidebar **Theme menu (Light / Dark / System)** repaints `html.dark` and `.dark`/`:root.dark` re-point the roles — verified by the painted surface. The hosted blog engine on townhall.hashnode.com shares the same declarations and paints dark under `prefers-color-scheme` (its own "Toggle theme" button produced no repaint headless, so blog dark evidence is the OS-preference path, recorded as such). `--command` is declared under `.dark` only. **Boundaries:** /login and every post-auth surface was never entered; engineering.hashnode.com answered 429; help., blog., docs., api., support., changelog. and weekly. subdomains do not resolve; status.hashnode.com is a Better Stack status page, not the product — all documented as blocked boundaries, never claimed as captures. Nothing was signed into, typed, searched, sent, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides (the search dialogs, the Theme/More menus, FAQ and tab rows, the sidebar toggle, the phone's nav button), and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): every same-origin CSS response from both surfaces plus the inline CSSOM is reparsed, rules are binned theme/skins/app/vendor/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets and the live pages' own generated classes (the next/font classes that scope `--font-inter`), once under `<html class="light">` and once under `class="dark"`. `tokens.json` never publishes a raw `lab()` declaration — every colour is the painted value.
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link can fire), opened/focused states via the component's own toggle.
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`) verify the painted theme before writing a sidecar: HTML class, computed `colorScheme` and sampled background luminance must all agree — the luminance check reads `lab()` backgrounds, not just `rgb()`. The app shell scrolls inside its own panes, so `-full` captures scroll those panes for lazy content, temporarily expand them, shoot, and restore the shell — bounded at eight chunks.
- **Dark states** on hashnode.com are painted by the sidebar's own Theme menu (verified, then switched back); on the blog engine they are painted by emulated `prefers-color-scheme: dark` + reload — the signal the engine honours — and each such state says so in its `via`.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label, and records where each was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.

## What to distrust

- **`app-sidebar` was read with the rail collapsed.** Its default and dark readings are 64px wide, the collapsed rail the "Toggle Sidebar" press leaves behind; the open rail is 232px in the other chrome readings. The measure script never pressed the toggle back, so components read after it on the same page saw the collapsed layout, and components after the /pro FAQ's `opened` state saw the answer open. The script now restores both toggles (not run); these readings need a recapture.
- **Focus rings in components.md.** Before 2026-11-20 the builder mapped every `oklab()` to grey by its lightness, so the 50% blue focus ring `oklab(0.561988 -0.023436 -0.238834 / 0.5)` read `rgba(143, 143, 143, 0.5)`. It now converts OKLab fully: `rgba(41, 98, 255, 0.5)`, the `#2962ff` ring at half opacity.
- **/login and everything behind it**: the auth wall was never crossed — the dashboard, editor, settings and the onboarding submit are boundaries, not captures. The "Sign in" / "Start your blog" / "Get Pro" links were measured, never followed.
- **engineering.hashnode.com** (429), **help./docs./blog./api./support./changelog./weekly.** subdomains (NXDOMAIN or unreachable) and **status.hashnode.com** (a Better Stack page, not the product): blocked boundaries; nothing on those surfaces was captured.
- **The blog engine's own theme button**: pressed headless it produced no repaint and no menu, so blog `dark` states and `th-*-dark-*` shots come from emulated `prefers-color-scheme` — honest evidence of the engine's OS-preference path, not of the button.
- **`--command`**: declared under `.dark` only; the light set has no value for it and none was inferred.
- **Search results**: both ⌘K dialogs were opened and their resting contents measured; no query was ever typed, so result rows are uncaptured (the blog dialog's suggestion rows were absent at rest).
- **Five components read as absent** on this pass — the profile header (its sticky bar did not match the probe's selector), the "Get Pro" plan CTA, the onboarding name field (the page deferred its form), the blog article's `<pre>` (the article's code sits in a styled wrapper the selector missed) and the blog search suggestion row. They are recorded as missing in components.md, not measured as absent from the product.
- **Inter rendering**: the face is loaded through next/font and not shipped here; crops show it, the style guide falls back to system fonts.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Reduced motion**: the probe's per-page results are in `raw/motion.json`.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint light, and let the menu carry dark.** Both surfaces boot `html.light` — white `--background`, near-black `--foreground` ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); dark is a real painted surface behind the sidebar's Theme menu (and the blog's OS-preference path), not a media-query guess ([dark](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)).
2. **Do spend the brand blue on actions and links.** #2962ff is `--brand`/`--link`/`--ring`: CTAs, prose links and focus rings ([CTA](components/crops/app-cta-default.png), [focus](components/crops/app-nav-link-focus-visible.png)); surfaces stay quiet — the app chrome is achromatic.
3. **Do build the feed on the post-card grammar.** Author row, title, muted excerpt, cover, the upvote/comments/bookmark action row on `--border` hairlines ([card](components/crops/post-card-default.png)) — and keep actions ghost-quiet until hovered.
4. **Do keep the code panel dark.** `--code-block` is near-black in both themes, so a light page carries a dark sample ([blog article](screenshots/th-post-light-full.png)).
5. **Do pair the two font chains.** The system stack (`--font-app`) carries the app UI; Inter (`--font-inter`) carries display and marketing; the mono stack draws all code ([article](components/crops/th-prose-h1-default.png)).
6. **Do use the prose roles for reading.** `--text-reading` (18px/28) and `--reading-text` are the blog engine's body pair; `--text-title-2..4`/`--text-subtitle`/`--text-label`/`--text-small`/`--text-body` are the product's own role scale, not invented names.
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** /login and everything behind it is a boundary — dashboard, editor and account surfaces are unmeasured ([Sign in](components/crops/app-signin-default.png)).
8. **Don't present the blog toggle as dark-mode evidence.** The engine's "Toggle theme" produced no repaint headless; blog dark evidence is the prefers-color-scheme path ([th dark](screenshots/th-home-dark-default.png)).
9. **Don't invent the dark-only or missing values.** `--command` exists in dark alone; the five components recorded as absent in components.md are unmeasured, not absent ([measurements](raw/measurements.md)).
10. **Don't fake the search or the feed.** No query was ever typed into either ⌘K dialog ([dialog](components/crops/app-search-dialog-default.png)); the streams are bounded at eight chunks and the bound is in each sidecar.
11. **Don't copy the brand or Inter.** The wordmark, the icon set and the font files are Hashnode's and its licensors' ([wordmark](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.

5. Do pair the two font chains.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

### Using tokens

| Role | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| page | #ffffff `light.root.background` | #0d0f14 `dark.root.background` |
| surface | #f7f8fa `light.surface.default` | #121419 `dark.surface.default` |
| text | #13161c `light.root.foreground` | #e6e8eb `dark.root.foreground` |
| muted | #63666d `light.muted.foreground` | #a2a5aa `dark.muted.foreground` |
| accent | #2962ff `light.brand.default` | #6293ff `dark.brand.default` |
| border | #e2e4e8 `light.border.default` | #272a30 `dark.border.default` |

Fonts: `core.font.family.app` is -apple-system, then 3 fallbacks; `core.font.family.inter` is Inter, then 1 fallback; `core.font.family.code` is ui-monospace, then 6 fallbacks. Scales, counted as [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds them: 9 type styles (`core.typography`), 2 radius steps (`core.radius`), 1 spacing step (`core.space`).
