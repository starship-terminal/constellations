# CALPAK public site tree

Mapped signed out on 2026-11-22 from [https://www.calpaktravel.com](https://www.calpaktravel.com). The first paint was the live Shopify shop, title "CALPAK Travel: Luggage, Bags & More | CALPAK". Global-e had already set the storefront to Singapore and SGD. The promo line reads "Free shipping to Singapore on all orders". This package records that storefront. Desktop viewport measured 1440 by 900 CSS pixels, `devicePixelRatio` 2. The page is a live shop, not a parked domain and not a captcha wall.

Nothing was typed. No email, password, code, phone, or address was entered. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. Add to Bag was not clicked. Cart was not opened. Search was not submitted. Account was not opened. Subscribe was not submitted. `login-extracted` was not flipped. `catalog.json` and `webapps-manifest.json` were left alone. origin/main had no `systems/calpak/`. `systems/cal` is Cal.com and was not touched.

Tracking query strings are not recorded.

A Klaviyo "Receive Free Shipping" email dialog covered the first paint. It was closed. The email field was not filled and Subscribe was not clicked. A OneTrust cookie banner was rejected. A later Klaviyo teaser ("Get Free Shipping") was hidden for the homepage photograph. That teaser is a subscribe wall, not page chrome.
