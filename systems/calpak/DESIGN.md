## Geometry

Search, hero CTA, Add to Bag, and collection chips use a full pill (`9999px`). The header sheet uses 5px. The promo bar is 38px. The nav row is 53px. Add to Bag is 47px tall. The wordmark is 70 by 14.

## Components

The primary button is yellow on ink, 47px tall, full pill, 16px Futura PT 500. The header is a cream sheet with a 5px radius over the hero.
