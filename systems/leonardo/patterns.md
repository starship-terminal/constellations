# Leonardo.Ai patterns

Public marketing pages, 2026-11-21. The signed-in product is a recorded boundary, not a pattern inferred from the marketing site.

## App shell

A black bar. Wordmark at the left, four menus (Create, Industry, Business, Learn), Pricing, Contact, then Tutorial (outline pill) and Log in (white pill). At 390 px the menus collapse into a drawer opened by the menu mark. The drawer lists the same items and pins Log in and Tutorial to the bottom.

Shown in [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) and [mobile-home-dark-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-menu.png).

## Hero

A black field filled with huge violet poster type ("YOUR IDEAS", "YOURS TO CREATE") and a centered white headline, "The creator-first generative AI platform". Two actions: a white Start now pill and a quiet Developer API control. Start now goes to the app host and was not followed into a session.

## Pricing

Three audience tabs (Solo, Team, API), a yearly/monthly pill, then four plan cards. Subscribe is the violet pill. Checkout was not opened.

Shown in [pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png) and [pricing-dark-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-monthly.png).

## Feature page

The image generator, model, industry and API pages reuse the same header, a large headline, and Start now. They are marketing pages, not the generator.

Shown in [image-generator-dark-default.png](screenshots/image-generator-dark-default.png) and [api-dark-default.png](screenshots/api-dark-default.png).

## Contact

A split layout: a violet poster panel and a form. Two segments, Support and billing (green) and Enterprise sales. Fields were left empty. The form was not submitted.

Shown in [contact-dark-default.png](screenshots/contact-dark-default.png).

## News

A card index of articles. One article, the getting-started tutorial linked from the header, was captured as the article template.

Shown in [news-dark-default.png](screenshots/news-dark-default.png) and [article-dark-default.png](screenshots/article-dark-default.png).

## Error

https://leonardo.ai/login is a 404. A giant violet 404, the line "This page is not found", and a white "Back to homepage" pill.

Shown in [not-found-dark-default.png](screenshots/not-found-dark-default.png).

Not present on the public pages. The app's empty states sit behind login.
