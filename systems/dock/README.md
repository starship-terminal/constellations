# Dock design reference

A token set and component reference for Dock's public surface — for building marketing pages and sales-collateral sites in the style of the real thing. It was read from **dock.us** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home, pricing, customers, library, templates, product (Dock AI), solutions (Sales), about and demo-request marketing pages, the login and signup cards on auth.dock.us (measured, never submitted), and a 390px mobile pass on home and pricing.

Dock is worth a capture because it is two surfaces in one domain. The marketing site is a Webflow build that declares a real custom-property layer — the `--ds-*`/`--color-*`/`--space-*`/`--text-*`/`--radius-*`/`--shadow-*` variables scoped to `.dock-shared-shell` and `#dock-native-home`, plus the `_anchor` colour ramp on the shared shell — and paints it with three Roobert-derived families (DockWebflowHome on the home hero and sections, DockWebflowNavigation on the header/footer/menus, Roobert on pricing, customers, templates and /demo). Its actions are 100px pills on the #0068f9 blue ramp (#0060eb hover, #005fe4 pressed), its controls draw a 0.5px inset hairline instead of a border, and the home hero opens on a #002fa6 deep-blue band with white text and white-alpha ghost buttons. The auth app on auth.dock.us runs a second, older Chakra system: Roobert 14px controls, the #4058ff royal-blue fill, 4-6px radii and the #1a202c ink — a different product face, captured at its signed-out cards only. **Boundary:** the Dock workspace — rooms, deal portals, content library, learning paths, billing — lives behind auth.dock.us and was never reached; the HubSpot demo form's fields were read through their iframe, never filled or submitted; the Turnstile human check was never toggled.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export dock` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do make the primary action the Dock blue pill.
2. Do paint the hero on the deep-blue band.
3. Do ring focus in ink.
4. Do draw borders as inset rings.
5. Do keep the families to their surfaces.
6. Do use the measured state washes.
7. Do give cards the painted radii.
8. Don't mix the auth face with marketing.
9. Don't style the workspace from this package.
10. Don't submit from this package.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

Scales, counted as [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds them: no type styles under `core.typography`, 11 radius steps (`core.radius`), 10 spacing steps (`core.space`).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every same-origin stylesheet body off the CSSOM (the Webflow bundles on cdn.prod.website-files.com and the inline `<style>` blocks) plus the cross-origin auth bundles, and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Because the marketing layer is scoped to container classes, not `:root`, resolution runs on a probe element nested inside every scope at once (`.dock-shared-shell > #dock-native-home > .ds-page > #dock-pricing-2026 > .header`), once light and once under `chakra-ui-dark` — the two resolve identically apart from one menu value. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets; a leaf is `{"var": name}` where a declared variable resolves the measured value, else a measured value with its provenance.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state. The header is a `<dock-site-header>` web component — locators pierce its shadow root for reads and the forced-pseudo lookup searches the pierced document's shadow roots. The HubSpot demo form's fields and submit are read through `frameLocator` into the hsforms iframe. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and bounded full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the Product mega-menu on home (`home-light-menu`) and the mobile menu on the phone pass. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, submitted or paid for: the auth cards were measured as surfaces — the subdomain and email fields were focused and given sample text that was deleted again, never sent; every Get-started, Start-for-free, Request-demo, Schedule-demo, Talk-to-sales, Log-in and OAuth action was measured, never followed; the Product dropdown, mobile menu, FAQ rows and customer filter pills were toggled as local UI state; the Turnstile widget was never toggled. No consent or chat control was ever clicked — overlays were hidden before every shot and reading.

## What to distrust

- **The "dark" set is not a theme.** Light and dark resolve identically in raw/css-vars.json apart from one menu value; the dark set is the deep-blue islands the light site paints (hero band, signup pane) — use it for text/fills/rings painted *on* dark surfaces, not as a dark mode.
- **The two font/ink systems are not interchangeable.** Roobert/#4058ff/#1a202c belongs to the auth app; DockWebflowHome/Navigation and #0068f9/#121722 to the marketing site. The pricing and demo pages sit between: marketing chrome with Roobert content.
- **Agent/MCP prompt fields** (input/textarea) are display:none until the widget's own interaction; their values are honest misses — the visible `.mcp-prompt` widget was measured instead.
- **The Turnstile widget** stayed a 1px placeholder headless; its 1x1 reading is what was painted, not the widget's real chrome.
- **Two entries measured a neighbouring control** in the 2026-11-13 run: `signup-submit` read the "Continue with Google" OAuth action (not the email form's submit) and `hero-lede` read the hero eyebrow (not the lede). Both selectors were fixed in `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` (an OAuth match is now recorded missing); the entries carry notes and need a recapture — until then their values describe the neighbouring control. `core.color.brand.blue-deep` no longer cites `hero-lede`; it carries the declared `--color-blue-deep` alone.
- **Eight focus rings were never confirmed.** `nav-trigger`, `nav-link`, `nav-login`, `nav-demo`, `nav-cta`, `menu-link`, `footer-link` and `m-nav-item` focus-visible were recorded `via: focus failed`; their via in raw/components-computed.json was later rewritten by hand, not re-measured. components.md and raw/measurements.md treat them as failed focus, and the focus tokens cite confirmed controls (`mcp-copy`, `hero-cta`) instead. Recapture needed.
- **`dark.surface.pane` #242d7d** was entered by hand from the auth signup pane; no reading in raw/ holds it (its source says Reconstruction).
- **`menu-link`** was not found by the 2026-11-12 run; its states are those of the 2026-11-13 run (`recaptureMissed`), and the four tokens read from it say so.
- **`m-hero`** was read with the mobile menu still open (Escape does not close its `<details>`), so its crop can show the menu over the headline. The script now closes the menu with its own toggle; not re-run.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs** — read exact colours from tokens.json or raw files, never from a screenshot.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Do and don't

1. **Do make the primary action the Dock blue pill.** Primary actions are #0068f9 100px-radius pills with white text — #0060eb hover, #005fe4 pressed, and a 0.5px inset deep-blue ring. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png).
2. **Do paint the hero on the deep-blue band.** The home hero opens on #002fa6 with white text, a transparent ghost secondary (white-alpha hover/pressed, sky inset ring) and the blue CTA. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
3. **Do ring focus in ink.** The keyboard ring on the marketing surface is a 2px rgba(18,23,34,0.6) outline; on the pricing buttons it's 2px #0068f9; on the blue band it's the same ink ring. See [mcp-copy](components/crops/mcp-copy-focus-visible.png) and [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-focus-visible.png).
4. **Do draw borders as inset rings.** White controls carry no border — a 0.5px inset rgba(18,23,34,0.2) ring (nav buttons), 1px rgba(18,23,34,0.1) on fields; real borders are the #dedfe3 hairlines on bars and dividers. See [nav-demo](components/crops/nav-demo-default.png).
5. **Do keep the families to their surfaces.** DockWebflowNavigation on chrome, DockWebflowHome on home sections, Roobert on pricing/customers/templates/demo and the auth app — 400 body, 500 controls, 600 display, 700 the customer-facing numbers. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
6. **Do use the measured state washes.** Light controls take ~#f8f8f9 hover and ~#f2f2f3 pressed; on the blue band ghosts take rgba(255,255,255,0.06)/0.1. See [nav-link](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png).
7. **Do give cards the painted radii.** Menu viewport 19px, hero artwork 18.9px, popular column 12px top, menu items 8px, fields 6px, Chakra controls 4-6px. See [nav-dropdown](components/crops/nav-dropdown-default.png).
8. **Don't mix the auth face with marketing.** #4058ff, 4-6px radii and #1a202c ink belong to auth.dock.us's Chakra cards — putting them on a marketing section is wrong. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png) versus [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
9. **Don't style the workspace from this package.** Every value is the public surface's; the product behind auth.dock.us is uncaptured. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
10. **Don't submit from this package.** The demo form and auth cards were captured read-only — fields measured, never filled or sent. See [demo-field](components/crops/demo-field-default.png).
