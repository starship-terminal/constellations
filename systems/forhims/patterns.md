# Patterns

## App shell

A migration banner sits above a sticky bar. The bar holds the wordmark, Weight, Hair, Sex, Learn, a LOGIN pill, and a hamburger. Treatments, Messaging and Profile are in the DOM and route to the login wall. They are not the visible desktop labels. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

On a 390-wide viewport the same Weight, Hair, Sex and Learn row still paints under the wordmark. The hamburger opens the account drawer. See [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Home

A large headline, one copper phrase, a short grey line, a black pill, then four cream cards. The cards go to assessment URLs. Those URLs were recorded in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and not entered.

## Category page

Weight, hair, ED and premature ejaculation landings share the chrome, a hero, product links, and an FAQ accordion. See [screenshots/weight-light-default.png](screenshots/weight-light-default.png).

## Product page

A product image, a title, a price, a black Get started pill, a safety link, and an accordion. Get started goes to the assessment wall and was not followed. See [screenshots/pdp-light-default.png](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png) and [screenshots/pdp-light-accordion.png](screenshots/pdp-light-accordion.png).

## Chooser

`/c` drops the marketing chrome and asks "What are you looking to do?" with three rows. Each row opens an assessment. Stop. See [screenshots/chooser-light-default.png](screenshots/chooser-light-default.png).

## Error

`/skin` is a styled 404: "Sorry, we seem to be having an issue here." plus a short link list. See [screenshots/notfound-light-default.png](screenshots/notfound-light-default.png).

## Content indexes

Blog and condition guides are card indexes with a next page. One index of each was captured. Individual posts follow the same template and were not all captured.
