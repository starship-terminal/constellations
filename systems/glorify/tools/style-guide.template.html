Glorify, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the marketing site's hashed Framer token layer (--token-<uuid>, emitted under token.*), the app's own semantic vocabulary (--active-*/--hover-*/--icon-*/--neutral-light-*/--disabled-*/--field-*/--error-*/--info-*/--green-*/--orange-*/--pink-*/--cta-300/--glori-gradient), the measured type ramp, the measured radius ladder, and the measured component sizes. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from glorify.com and app.glorify.com, signed out, in headless Chromium. Everything post-login is a documented boundary; the sign-in and sign-up forms were measured and typed into then cleared, never submitted; the anonymous editor at /designer/use-template/0 is the product's own Play-for-free path on a scratch file the SPA creates. The marketing site is light-only — no toggle, no dark media query. The app surface paints its dark chrome, and its tokens live in the separate app theme. The product's served faces are not loaded here: samples fall back to system sans.

The app surface

app.glorify.com declares its own vocabulary on :root and paints dark chrome. The sign-in submit sits disabled until the fields validate — both fills were measured, never submitted. The anonymous editor paints the header (file name, Login/Signup, zoom, Share, Download), the left tool rail and the EDIT/LAYERS/PAGES inspector; the Craft dialog sits behind "Try with Placeholder Image" and its "Add to canvas" was measured disabled and enabled, never pressed on the measurement pass. The swatches in this scope carry the app set.
