# Glorify design reference

A token set and component reference for Glorify's public marketing site and its signed-out product surfaces — for building e-commerce marketing pages, pricing pages and dark editor chrome in the style of the real thing. It was read from **glorify.com** and **app.glorify.com** on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing, /features/background-remover, /templates, /template-marketplace, /learn and a Learn article, /team, /affiliates, /webinars, /submit-template, /canva-alternative, the legal pages /terms-of-service and /privacy-policy, the signed-out auth cards at /dashboard/login and /dashboard/register, the anonymous editor at /designer/use-template/0, plus 390px passes on the home, pricing and login pages.

Glorify is worth a capture because it paints two distinct public surfaces signed out. The marketing site is a Framer build whose stylesheets declare a hashed Framer token layer (`--token-<uuid>`…) — light only, no theme toggle and no dark media query — over a white field with a saturated gradient accent. app.glorify.com is the product SPA and declares its own semantic vocabulary on `:root` outright (`--light-*`/`--dark-*` ramps, `--neutral-light-*`, `--active-*`/`--hover-*` state colours, `--icon-*`/`--field-*`/`--disabled-*` roles, `--error-*`/`--info-*`/`--green-*`/`--orange-*`/`--pink-*` ramps, `--cta-300`, `--glori-gradient` and the `--header`/`--hero`/`--inspector`/`--assets` layout names); the signed-out surfaces paint its dark chrome: the /dashboard/login and /dashboard/register cards, and the anonymous editor the marketing "Play for free" CTA opens — the upload onboarding, the Craft dialog behind "Try with Placeholder Image", and the canvas header, tool rail and inspector. **Boundary:** everything post-login was never reached — the auth fields were typed into and cleared, never submitted; trial, OAuth, Share and Download actions were measured, never pressed or followed; "Add to canvas" was measured by `measure.mjs` but pressed once by the screenshot pass, which ran an AI generation (Do and don't, item 7; a boundary since 2026-11); no purchase or account flow was touched. Any consent overlay is hidden, never answered.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export glorify` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep the two surfaces honest.
2. Do spend the accent on actions.
3. Do reuse the measured geometry.
4. Do treat the declared vocabularies as canonical colour.
5. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
6. Don't read the dark app as a marketing dark theme.
7. Don't fabricate editor flows.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

### Using tokens

| Role | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| page | #ffffff `light.background.page` | #212121 `app.background.page` |
| surface | #ffffff `light.surface.card` | #212121 `app.surface.card` |
| text | #111111 `light.text.ink` | #ffffff `app.text.ink` |

| border | #000000 `light.border.focus-ring` | rgba(0, 0, 0, 0) `app.border.field-focus` |

Fonts: `core.font.family.display` is PolySans Trial Median, then 2 fallbacks; `core.font.family.body` is Inter, then 2 fallbacks. Scales, counted as [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds them: no type styles under `core.typography`, 8 radius steps (`core.radius`), no spacing steps under `core.spacing`.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the two surfaces honest.** Marketing is light-only Framer (`light` theme); the product surface is dark chrome (`app` theme). They never mix — see [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [designer-app-canvas](screenshots/designer-app-canvas.png).
2. **Do spend the accent on actions.** The marketing CTA fill (`light.action.cta-fill`) sits on "Play for free" and "Get Started Now" — the hero's own "Play for free" is an outlined ghost on the dark band (`light.action.hero-cta-fill` transparent, `light.action.hero-cta-border` the 1px white stroke); the app's primary fill (`app.action.primary-fill`) only paints once a form validates. See [nav-play](components/crops/nav-play-default.png) and [login-submit](components/crops/login-submit-enabled.png).
3. **Do reuse the measured geometry.** The 64px marketing bar, 42px CTAs, ~230px plan cards, the 352x384 auth card, the 56px editor header, 48px rail tools and the 280px inspector are all measured: `core.component.*`. See [mkt-header](components/crops/mkt-header-default.png) and [ed-inspector](components/crops/ed-inspector-default.png).
4. **Do treat the declared vocabularies as canonical colour.** The marketing `--token-*` layer (`token.*`) and the app's `--light-*`/`--dark-*`/`--active-*` ramps (`app.*`) are stylesheet declarations; the semantic roles alias to them where they match. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and the swatches in [style-guide.html](style-guide.html), e.g. [login-card](components/crops/login-card-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything post-login is a documented boundary; the signed-out auth cards and the anonymous editor are the evidence, and even they were never submitted. See [login-app-default](screenshots/login-app-default.png).
6. **Don't read the dark app as a marketing dark theme.** There is no marketing dark theme — the site paints light only; `app` is a separate surface's chrome, paired with `core` under its own `$themes` entry. See [designer-app-canvas](screenshots/designer-app-canvas.png).
7. **Don't fabricate editor flows.** The Craft dialog's "Add to canvas" was measured disabled and enabled by measure.mjs; the screenshot pass did press it once, which ran an AI generation, and [designer-app-canvas](screenshots/designer-app-canvas.png) is its result (the script now stops at the button, edited 2026-11, not rerun). Share/Download/Replace and every OAuth button were measured, never activated. See [ed-add](components/crops/ed-add-default.png) and [ed-add enabled](components/crops/ed-add-enabled.png).
