# Patterns

## Marketing shell

Black 64px header, wordmark, five menus, search, Log in, Try ChatGPT. The hero is a pale video wash with a 90px app mark, a 64px headline, and two pills. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

## Pricing

Three cards under "Codex is included in your ChatGPT plan." Plus and Pro are quiet. Business is the filled card. Prices on this capture were SGD 30, SGD 138 and SGD 25. [screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png).

## Docs

The in-page "Learn more in the developer docs" link redirects to https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs. Black docs shell, left rail, a product frame. [screenshots/docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/docs-dark-default.png). Phone: [screenshots/mobile-docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/mobile-docs-dark-default.png).
