# Variant design reference

A token set and page captures for Variant's signed-out web surfaces. Read live from [variant.com](https://variant.com/) on 2026-11-19, and photographed again on 2026-11-22. The host is Variant Design, Inc. The title is "Variant – Endless designs for your ideas, just scroll."

origin/main already had `systems/variant/`. This pass refreshes that folder. The slug did not change. `catalog.json` was left alone, so `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

The signed-out product is one dark screen, not a marketing site. `/` is a 408px column (headline, two chips, a composer) beside a scrolling grid of public design widgets. "Sign up / Sign in" opens `/authentication`, one wall for login and signup. Terms and privacy share `/terms-and-privacy`. There is no public pricing page, no footer, and no light theme: forcing `prefers-color-scheme: light` on 2026-11-19 left the page on `rgb(34, 34, 34)`. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

Desktop shots are 1440×900 CSS at devicePixelRatio 2 (PNG 2880×1800). Phone shots are 390×844 CSS at devicePixelRatio 2 (PNG 780×1688). Nothing was typed on this pass. Google was not clicked. No form was submitted. The signed-in app was not opened.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export variant` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Paint the page near-black and the ink off-white.
2. Set display type in Variant Neue Display at 32/36, weight 400.
3. Use a 6px radius on chips and auth controls, 8px on grid cards, 10px on the composer.
4. Fill actions with white at 10% opacity, not a blue.
5. Hairline borders are 0.5px at white 10%.
6. Do not invent a light theme or a signed-in shell from this package.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were walked from `document.styleSheets` on `https://variant.com/` (11 same-origin sheets, none blocked). The declared list is [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Several names (`--gray1` through `--gray12`, `--error-*`, `--homepage-offwhite`, `--font-inter`) are in the bundle and do not resolve on the homepage root. Tokens that use them cite the declaration. Tokens that describe paint cite `getComputedStyle`.

Component geometry is in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json): homepage at 1440×900, authentication after clicking "Sign up / Sign in", and `/terms-and-privacy` after clicking the terms link. Hover on the enabled email button was a real hover. Focus on buttons and links was a real Tab. Click-focus on the email field painted no ring.

`tools/build_tokens.py` copies those files into `tokens.json`. It does not sample screenshots.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots from 2026-11-22 are 256-colour PNGs. Flat fills survive. The widget photographs band. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from a pixel.
- The legal shot is a crop of the tabs and the heading. The rest of the article prints a contact email, a street address, and a phone line, so it was not committed.
- The phone auth card is 400px wide inside a 390 frame. The overflow in [screenshots/auth-dark-390.png](screenshots/auth-dark-390.png) is what the page painted.
- The user-agent focus outline `rgb(229, 151, 0)` is the browser default (`accent-color: auto`). It is recorded because it was painted. It is not a Variant token.
- Blues, coral, billing past-due colours, and the gray HSL ramps are declared in CSS. They were not painted on the signed-out pages. `core.palette` keeps the declarations and says so.
- `--border-radius` resolves to 12px. The controls on screen were 6px, 8px and 10px. Use the measured radii.
- Sidebar, chat, billing modal, style modal and toast motion live in the same CSS bundle. Those screens were not opened.
- Variant Neue Display, Variant Neue Text and Advercase are proprietary files on variant.com. This package names them and does not ship the files.
- The homepage grid cards are iframes of design widgets. Their inner UI was not measured.
- Pressed states were not taken. A click on "Continue with Email" or the composer Submit would start a flow.

## Fonts

- Variant Neue Display. Homepage h1. Weights 100 through 900, plus italics, served from `/fonts/neue-haas-grotesk/variant-neue-display/`. Not shipped.
- Variant Neue Text. Homepage body, chips and composer. Weights 400, 500 and 700, plus italics. Not shipped.
- Inter, with an Inter Fallback. Auth page, legal page, and the body stack under the Neue families. Variable woff2, 100 to 900. A public family. The files are not copied here.
- Advercase. Declared as `--font-advercase` and as a font-face at weight 400. Not used on the surfaces that were measured.
- The mono stack is the declared `--font-mono` (ui-monospace, Menlo, Monaco, and the rest). No code sample was on the signed-out pages.

## Do and don't

1. **Paint the page near-black and the ink off-white.** The body is `rgb(34, 34, 34)`. The column is `rgb(7, 7, 6)`. The headline is `rgb(240, 237, 229)`, which is the declared `--homepage-offwhite`.
2. **Set display type in Variant Neue Display at 32/36, weight 400.** The homepage headline is that, with `0.32px` tracking. Auth and legal titles switch to Inter 21/32 weight 500.
3. **Use a 6px radius on chips and auth controls, 8px on grid cards, 10px on the composer.** The declared `--border-radius` is 12px and was not the radius of these controls.
4. **Fill actions with white at 10% opacity, not a blue.** "Continue with Google" and the enabled email button are `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Hover on the enabled button moves to `0.15`. The empty email button keeps the same fill and drops the label to `0.5`.
5. **Hairline borders are 0.5px at white 10%.** Chips, the email field, and the composer ring all use that. Do not add a drop shadow. None was painted.
6. **Do not invent a light theme or a signed-in shell from this package.** Light preference did not change the paint. Sidebar and billing CSS was not on screen. The shots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/).
