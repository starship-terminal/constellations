# Nike patterns

Public marketing and shopping chrome on nike.com/sg. Signed out. No bag, checkout, or member account.

## Shell

A 60px white header. Swoosh at the left. Department links centred: New & Featured, Men, Women, Kids, NikeSKIMS, Sale, Gifts, SNKRS. Utility row above it: Find a Store, Help, Join Us, Sign In. Search, heart, and bag at the right. A promo strip under the header uses 16px body text and an underlined Shop Now.

![Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

At 390px the departments collapse to a menu icon. Header stays 60px and white.

![Home, 390](screenshots/home-light-mobile.png)

## Home

Full-bleed photography. White Shop and Explore pills sit on the image. Product rows below use square photos and black prices. The hero type on the photograph is campaign type, not the PDS body stack.

## Listing

Men's Shoes is a filter column plus a product grid. Title is 24px / 500. Category links are plain text. Hide Filters and Sort By sit on the right. Photos have no radius.

![Men's Shoes](screenshots/plp-light-default.png)

## Product

Gallery left, buy column right. Title 20px Helvetica Now Display Medium. Price 16px medium. Size grid, then Add to Bag and Favourite. Add to Bag was measured and not clicked. One size was selected to read the selected style. Nothing was added to the bag.

![Air Force 1 '07 LX](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png)

## Help

Get Help is a large black wordmark, then a search field, then three columns of question links. No ticket was submitted.

![Help](screenshots/help-light-default.png)

## Stores

Find a Nike Store is a location field, a filter pill, and a list of published Nike stores beside a map. No location was typed.

![Stores](screenshots/stores-light-default.png)

## Membership marketing

Nike Membership is a public marketing page with a Sign Up label and a Let's go button on a photograph. The account form was not opened. Join Us and `/sg/register` lead to accounts.nike.com, which returned Access Denied in this capture.

![Membership](screenshots/membership-light-default.png)

## Not captured

Empty, loading, and error pages. A form validation error. A destructive confirmation. Checkout. The signed-in account. A dark theme.
