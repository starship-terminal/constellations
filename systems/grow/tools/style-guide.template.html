Palette

The declared layer is the glue ramp family — blue/green/red/yellow/purple 50-900 plus the grey ramp — from the versioned gstatic bundle; it is the only custom-property vocabulary the site ships. Everything else is a compiled literal read as a computed style: the painted accent #3186ff (brighter than declared glue-blue-600 #1a73e8, which still paints card links and the focus ring), the hover #185abc, inks and surfaces.

Radius, spacing and motion
