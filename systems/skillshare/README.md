# Skillshare design reference

A token set and component reference for Skillshare's public surface. It was read from **skillshare.com** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: home, browse, illustration browse, learning paths, student projects, digital products, 1-on-1 sessions, about, press, blog, affiliates, teach, gift, teams, help, terms, privacy, search, the login wall, and careers on Workable.

Skillshare's public UI is a light marketing shell: GT Walsheim Pro, near-black ink `#0b1215` on white, a green `#55da9b` Sign Up that darkens to `#50cf93` on hover, a purple `#5620c1` link, 4px control radii, and a 1px `#dcdee1` field border. The site declares no design custom properties on `:root`. Every token is a computed style or a flat fill sampled before quantize.

**Boundary:** class pages (`/en/classes/...`) return Cloudflare "Just a moment..." in this client. Header Sign In and Sign Up open Auth0 at auth.skillshare.com. `/en/login` shows email, Google, Facebook, and Apple. Nothing was typed or submitted. Signed-in learning, checkout, and profiles were not reached.

The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## How the values were read

Headless Playwright opened public URLs. Consent banners (DataGrail on skillshare.com, the Workable cookie dialog) were hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked.

- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/shoot.mjs](tools/capture/public/shoot.mjs) saved viewport and full-page shots to a scratch folder, with `.capture.json` sidecars from the kit's screenshot helper. Desktop innerWidth was 1440. Phone innerWidth was 390.
- **Computed styles.** A separate probe read `getComputedStyle` on the Sign Up button, search box, home h1, Continue with Google, Continue with email, the login email field, and the login Sign In button. Hover, pressed, and focus-visible on Sign Up used CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState`.
- **Sampled fills.** The promo bar (`#000000`) and footer (`#020404`) were sampled from raw screenshots before `quantize.py`. Those two are labelled Sampled in tokens.json.
- **Fonts.** `@font-face` on the home page names GT Walsheim Pro and Kumlien Pro, served from static.skillshare.com. The faces are not in the package.

Nothing was signed into. Google, Facebook, and Apple were not clicked. No email was typed. No form was submitted.

## What to distrust

- **This is the public shell.** Lessons, the class player, reviews, and the signed-in home are uncaptured. Class URLs hit Cloudflare in this client.
- **No declared token layer.** Marketing CSS has no `:root` custom properties. Login and Help Center expose Tailwind and Zendesk variables (`--tw-*`, `--zd-*`). Those are framework defaults, not Skillshare's palette, and they are not in tokens.json.
- **Sign Up hover and pressed match.** CDP hover and active both paint `#50cf93`. That is what the browser computed. It may not be a distinct pressed style.
- **The footer colour is sampled.** `#020404` is a pixel on the raw about video shot, not a computed style of `footer`.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Phone login is 1x.** Other phone shots are 2x. The login page reported `devicePixelRatio` 1 while the context was 2, so that shot was saved at CSS scale to pass the dimension check.
- **Careers is Workable.** The first shot was a cookie veil. The committed shot hides that dialog. Accept and Decline were not clicked.
- **A later blog probe hit Cloudflare.** The committed [blog-light-default](screenshots/blog-light-default.png) is the real blog chrome (Articles, Resources, News, Classes). A second visit in the same session titled "Just a moment...". Treat a recapture that shows that title as a failed load.

## Fonts

**GT Walsheim Pro** is the UI face: 46px hero, 18px buttons, 15px nav and fields. Bold and regular (plus obliques) are served as woff2 from static.skillshare.com. **Kumlien Pro** bold is also served. Neither is committed. The style guide falls back to Arial.

## Do and don't

1. **Do put the green on one action.** `#55da9b` is Sign Up and the login Sign In. Social buttons stay white with a dark border. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do keep controls at 4px.** Search, Sign Up, the email field, and social buttons share a 4px radius. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
3. **Do use purple for links.** Continue with email and Sign up are `#5620c1`, the same colour as the focus ring. See [btn-email-default](components/crops/btn-email-default.png).
4. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site paints light. The black promo bar and the footer are chrome, not a theme.
5. **Don't style the class player from this package.** Class URLs did not load. The player, lessons, and reviews are uncaptured.
6. **Don't treat Zendesk or Tailwind variables as the brand.** `--zd-*` and `--tw-*` appear on Help and login. They are not the marketing palette.

## Rebuild

From `systems/skillshare/`:

```sh
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

Live recapture needs Playwright:

```sh
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node tools/capture/public/shoot.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/skillshare/raw-shots screenshots
```

`make design-md` and `make brief` need a catalog.json entry. This capture does not add one.
