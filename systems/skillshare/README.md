### Principles

1. Use the mint for the action and the purple for links.
2. Keep controls at 4px.
3. Invert the nav on photo heroes.
4. Do not invent a dark theme.
5. Do not treat the Workable careers page as the product chrome.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read on the live home page in the T3 preview tab (innerWidth 1169, then confirmed at 1440 in headless Chromium) and on /en/about, /en/teach, /teams, /en/press, /en/careers, and the legal host. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so screenshots and crops are from headless Chromium on pages that loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare challenge. A cookie overlay was hidden for screenshots and never answered. Hover values that differ from rest (Sign Up `#50cf93`, Google wash, email underline) come from a real mouse hover. `CSS.forcePseudoState` did not apply those hover rules, so it was not used as evidence.

No custom properties on `:root` name a colour, type size, or radius. The only custom properties were Tailwind internals. Tokens are the computed values.

## What to distrust

The body font on www.skillshare.com computed as Times on `document.body`. Painted text uses GT Walsheim Pro. Trust the element readings, not the body default.

`core.typography.body` line-height is 24px taken from the 16px OAuth button, because the 16px footer links computed `line-height: normal`.

`core.typography.hero-mobile` records font-size 32px. Its line-height was not a separate computed value.

The careers page is a Workable embed. Its teal `#00756a` and 8px cookie buttons are third-party and are not tokens.

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json and raw/, not from the PNGs. Photographs in the shots will band.

The wordmark SVG uses `fill="black"`, which follows the current color only if the page sets it. On the dark nav the rendered mark is white. The saved SVG is the light-bar source.

## Fonts

GT Walsheim Pro is the marketing face. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Tokens fall back to the system sans, then Arial. legal.skillshare.com names GT Walsheim Pro first and loads DM Sans. Kumlien Pro is declared and did not load. /en/careers loads Montserrat and Proxima Nova inside the Workable embed.

## Do and don't

- **Use the mint for the action and the purple for links.** Sign Up, Get Started, and the legal Search submit are `#55da9b` with `#0b1215` type. Continue with email and legal links are `#5620c1`. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Keep controls at 4px.** Sign Up, OAuth, and search share a 4px radius and a 40px height at 1440. See [nav-signup-default.png](components/crops/nav-signup-default.png).
- **Invert the nav on photo heroes.** About, Teach, and Press paint the 80px bar `#0b1215` and the labels white. The home bar stays white. See [about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png).
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** None was offered on these pages.
- **Do not treat the Workable careers page as the product chrome.** Its teal buttons are the embed.
