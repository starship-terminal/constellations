Palette

A tight palette, not a ramp: signature lime, pure black for text/dark pills/the footer band, white cards on a page-gray field, then the accents — coral for icon links and red for the Save-30% badge.

Type

Two voices: Poppins 700 for every display heading (57/60 with −1px tracking, one size everywhere) and the 500-weight sub-display at 35px; Inter for everything else — 18px buttons and links, 16px body, 15px badges and footer links, plus a 300-weight ghost for Log in.

Radius and geometry

Four shapes: radius.pill 30px on every button/link/badge, radius.card 36px on plan/member/step cards, radius.card-lg 40px on the nav bar, review cards and the feature table, and radius.mega 50px on the features-page mega CTA.

Motion and layout

Motion is minimal: 0.2s ease-in-out colour/border transitions on the atoms, and Tilda's 0.6s nav animation. Standard pills are 60px tall (nav pills 44px, the mega CTA 96px); the floating bar spans a fixed 1200px.
