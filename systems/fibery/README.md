# Fibery design reference

A token set and component reference for Fibery's public surfaces — for building warm, editorial, product-led marketing pages, pricing pages, template galleries and the sign-up/sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **fibery.com** on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /features, /pricing, /templates plus one template detail, /customer-stories, /product-management, /no-code, /ai, /blog plus one article, /download, /getting-started, /about-us, /101-questions-about-fibery, /museum, the fibery.io/sign-up wizard, the fibery.io/login/ form, and 390px passes on home, features, pricing and sign-up.

Fibery is worth a capture because its public site is a fully declared token layer with a voice of its own: a single `:root` rule in the Next.js CSS bundle ships 67 `--fibery-*` custom properties — a culinary named palette (`--fibery-milk`, `--fibery-soup`, `--fibery-tea`, `--fibery-rusty`, `--fibery-terracotta`, `--fibery-mud-text`), four-level `basic`/`secondary`/`tertiary`/`quaternary` ramps for eight hues, and semantic `--fibery-page-*`, `--fibery-text-*`, `--fibery-menu-*`, `--fibery-gray-*`, `--fibery-form-*` and `--fibery-business-*` roles — plus one `--fibery-shadow-m` elevation. The whole public surface paints **one warm light theme**: the "soup" #faebd6 page field, cream #fdf7ef cards, near-black #251d1a ink, dark pill CTAs and the #3b758c website blue spent on links and form controls — no toggle, no dark scope; the signed-in app's dark/system theme sits behind sign-in. Four self-hosted voices: **Suisse Intl** (100–900 static weights, every control and body line), **Fraunces Var** (the serif display face), plus **Casuar** and **Inter Var** on special surfaces. **Boundary:** everything past fibery.io/login was never reached — the sign-up and login forms were measured and typed into then cleared, never submitted; the OAuth POST forms were never pressed; the wizard never advanced; every link and CTA was measured, never followed; the consent layer and Intercom widget were hidden, never answered.

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layer carry the design.
2. Do keep the palette warm.
3. Do use the dark pill CTA.
4. Do pair Suisse with Fraunces.
5. Do open the nav on hover.
6. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
7. Don't fabricate a dark mode or form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

| Step | What ran |

| CSS harvest | `tools/capture/vars.mjs` — every custom property in the site's served stylesheets (network bodies + inline CSSOM, re-parsed), resolved in a light probe: 67 `--fibery-*` values plus 22 @font-face rules across 6 sheets |
| Icons | `tools/capture/icons.mjs` — 140 inline SVG glyphs deduped by hash into `icons/` |
| Measurements | `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` — 45 components on public routes only; hover/focus-visible/pressed/opened/focused/filled states; nothing signed into, submitted or followed |

- **Provenance.** Every token leaf names its source in `$extensions.source`: `css-var --…` for the site's own declared variables, `measured …` for computed-style readings. Two declarations are literally `undefined` on the site (`--fibery-page-secondary-color`, `--fibery-text-selection-color`); they are kept as `other` tokens with the unresolved value rather than invented.

## What to distrust

- **The signed-in product.** Nothing past fibery.io/login was reached; the workspaces, entity views, whiteboards, AI and admin surfaces need an account this capture does not have. The hero product video and the screenshots inside the marketing pages are product mocks, not the product.
- **The forms.** The sign-up prompt and login e-mail field were measured at rest and typed into then cleared — validation, error, loading and success states are unverified because nothing was submitted, and the OAuth POST forms were never pressed.
- **Wizard depth.** The sign-up was never advanced past step one, so the later wizard steps, workspace creation and onboarding screens are uncaptured.
- **The dark theme.** The login page's "Did you know?" hint advertises a dark/system theme for the app; it paints nowhere on the public surface, so there is deliberately no `dark` token set.
- **`undefined` variables.** `--fibery-page-secondary-color` and `--fibery-text-selection-color` are declared as the literal string `undefined` in the served CSS; preserved as evidence, unusable as values.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw values, never the pixels.
- **Unpainted fills are not tokens.** The login Google button computes a transparent `backgroundColor`, so `light.background.oauthFill` was dropped in the 2026-11 audit.

## Fonts

- **Suisse Intl** — the UI voice, served as self-hosted woff2 at weights 100–900 in normal and italic (`SuisseIntl-*.woff2`); every nav item, control and body line.
- **Fraunces Var** — the serif display face on headlines and quotes (a variable italic file plus `Fraunces_72pt_SuperSoft-Italic`).
- **Casuar** and **Inter Var** — served for special and embedded surfaces.
- None is bundled here; the style guide falls back to system faces. Declared in the stylesheets' `@font-face` rules (harvested in `raw/css-vars.json`).

## Coverage

- 18 desktop routes and 4 mobile routes captured signed out, all on the single light theme (the marketing pages, the sign-up wizard and the sign-in share the same stylesheets).
- 45 components measured in 109 states, including the opened header dropdowns, the mobile drawer, the toggled billing switch, the revealed e-mail step, forced-pressed controls and typed-then-cleared fields.
- 140 SVG icons lifted into `icons/`; 103 component crops in `components/crops/`.
- Missing surfaces (declared in the catalog's `capture.gaps`): every post-login app surface, the wizard's later steps, the demo scheduler, and all but one article/template detail per index.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layer carry the design.** Fibery ships its tokens: use the `color.named.*`/`color.basic.*`/`color.secondary-*` scales and the `light.fibery.*` semantic roles rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do keep the palette warm.** The page field is soup #faebd6, cards are cream #fdf7ef, the menu bar is #fff5e8 and ink is #251d1a — the cool gray and blue are accents, never the canvas. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
3. **Do use the dark pill CTA.** Primary actions are near-black pills (#251d1a fill, soup-coloured ink, 40px tall, 6px radius); secondary actions are the same pill outlined. See [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png) and [hero-cta2](components/crops/hero-cta2-default.png).
4. **Do pair Suisse with Fraunces.** Suisse Intl carries the UI; Fraunces carries display headlines and quotes — the editorial serif against the utilitarian sans is the voice. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
5. **Do open the nav on hover.** The header dropdowns are hover-triggered panels of icon tiles, not click menus; the same tree becomes the mobile drawer's accordion. See [nav-panel](components/crops/nav-panel-default.png) and [m-drawer](components/crops/m-drawer-default.png).
6. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything past fibery.io/login — workspaces, views, the dark theme — was never reached; the login and sign-up are the only app surfaces measured. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
7. **Don't fabricate a dark mode or form outcomes.** No dark scope paints publicly, and no submit was pressed: disabled, error, loading and success states are unverified. See [su-submit](components/crops/su-submit-enabled.png).
8. **Don't copy the brand.** The wordmark, the culinary palette names, the illustrations and the product mocks are Fibery's published artwork; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).
