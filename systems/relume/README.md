# Relume

Relume is an AI website builder (relume.io, which redirects to relume.ai). This package captures the public marketing site and the signed-out login page on 19 November 2026.

Surfaces: homepage, pricing, and `/app/login`. Theme: light only. Widths: 1440 and 390 CSS pixels. No account was created. The login form asks for a password and offers Google and SSO, so nothing was typed into it.

2. Keep the page warm grey.
3. Put secondary actions on a quiet border.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://www.relume.io/ and landed on https://www.relume.ai/. Computed styles for body, hero, composer, and buttons were read there at 1169 by 731, then again after a resize to 1440 by 900. `preview_snapshot` then returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames."

Screenshots and the 1440 and 390 readings come from headless Chrome (Playwright, channel `chrome`) on the same public URLs. Those pages loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. `window.devicePixelRatio` was 2. PNG pixel size is 2 times the CSS viewport. Screenshots and crops were quantised to 256 colours after the readings were saved.

Custom properties were read from `:root`. Relume's own marketing palette is `--black`, `--light-grey-*`, `--orange`, `--purple`, and the `--_publish-*` semantic set. Embedded site demos (`--_hipages-*`, `--_landing-*`) are not tokens here.

## What to distrust

The primary button's declared hover colour is `#ff29f6`. A real hover kept the computed background at `rgb(255, 0, 244)` and scaled the button to 1.03. The declared hover is in the tokens and marked as such.

`.p-button` sets `font-variation-settings: "wght" 420` while computed `font-weight` is 400. The login title computed weight 440. The tokens keep the computed weights.

Status colours (danger, warning, success, info) and the disabled colour are declared custom properties. They were not painted on the captured pages.

The pricing page shows dollar amounts. Those are prices, not colour or type tokens, except the 40px amount style.

Geist Variable and Geist are commercial faces. The tokens name them. The style guide falls back to Arial.

[tokens.json](tokens.json) and [style-guide.html](style-guide.html) are maintained by hand, and no builder in this package reproduces them. The capture read the page into the files in [raw/](raw) and typed the tokens from those readings. The package has no token or style-guide builder: the kit's copies of another product's example (`build_tokens.py`, `build_style_guide.py`, `style-guide.template.html`, `motion.py`) were never adapted, could not run on these readings, and were deleted on 2026-11-21. `tools/test_build_tokens.py` checks the tokens against the readings, and checks that every colour the style guide declares is a token value. A change to a value goes into both files by hand, or waits for a recapture through the kit's pipeline.

## Do and don't

- **Use magenta only for the primary action.** "Start for free", "Generate Website", and login "Continue" are `#ff00f4` with white labels. [button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Keep the page warm grey.** Body and theme-color are `#f1f0ee`. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Put secondary actions on a quiet border.** "Log in" is transparent with a 1px `rgba(27, 25, 25, 0.1)` border. [button-secondary-default.png](components/crops/button-secondary-default.png)
- **Set headlines in Geist Variable at weight 400 with tight tracking.** The 1440 hero is 56px, line-height 67.2px, tracking -1.68px. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** Asking the browser for dark left `data-theme` on light. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do not submit the login form from this capture.** It asks for a password and offers Google and SSO. [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png)
