# Relume patterns

Public pages only. Captured 19 November 2026 from https://www.relume.ai/, which redirects from relume.io.

## Marketing home

Sticky navbar, 56px tall, transparent, content column max-width 1280px. Logo, text links, a muted icon button, a secondary "Log in" button, and a magenta "Start for free" button.

The hero is a 640px column. Eyebrow, 56px headline, 18px lede, then a frosted composer card. Below that, two paths (Publish and Export) sit as secondary buttons next to the same magenta primary.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

At 390px the headline drops to 40px / 44px and a 44px menu button replaces the link row. The navbar stays 56px.

[home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png)

## Pricing

Same navbar. The page title measured at 48px. Plan amounts are 40px / 48px, tracking -0.8px. "USD" is muted `#8a8a8a` at 17px. The hosting block is a 12px-radius container. Prices on the page included $0, $12, and $14. This package does not treat those figures as tokens.

[pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Signed-out login

`/app/login` is a centred column, 348px wide, on the same `#f1f0ee` page. Title "Welcome back" is 24px. Actions are "Continue with Google", "Continue with SSO", an email field, a password field, and a magenta "Continue" button. "Sign up" sits in the top bar. "Forgot password" is 12px secondary text.

No email was entered. The form is not email-only.
