# Patterns

Public signed-out pages, measured 2026-11-19. Screenshots were not saved. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames."

## Marketing home

[https://mymind.com/](https://mymind.com/) is a long single page on `light.color.page` (`#f9fafc`). The header is transparent, with the wordmark at the left, Louize links in the centre, and an orange Sign up pill at the right. The hero headline is Louize, fluid with the viewport (about 117px at innerWidth 1169, 50px at 390). A muted Inter lede wraps five coloured keyword bubbles. Store chips sit on `#f5f7f9`.

Lower bands alternate a warm `#f6f4f2` container (padding 100px 30px 140px) and a full orange `#ff5924` band with light copy `#f0f2f5`. Eyebrows are Nunito, tracked out, sometimes in the orange.

## Pricing

[https://mymind.com/pricing](https://mymind.com/pricing) repeats the same header and page colour. A Louize headline ("What kind of mind are you?") sits over a Monthly / Yearly toggle. Plan names are Louize. Prices are orange Louize. Feature cards are white, 22px radius, soft shadow. A basic plan ("The Bookmarker", `$4.99/mo`) sits below the yearly plans. Checkout was not opened.

## Why, How, FAQ, reviews

Why and How keep the page colour `#f9fafc` and the same header. FAQ shifts the page to `#f7fbfe` and sets questions in Nunito 28px. Reviews paints a blue gradient hero (sampled start `rgb(145, 168, 206)`, rest truncated) with a white Louize headline, then quote blockquotes on `#f0f2f5` sections. The gradient stops past 30% were cut by the reader and are not tokens.

## Signed-out access

[https://access.mymind.com/signin](https://access.mymind.com/signin) is a split page. A Louize welcome line ("Welcome to your new private oasis", 141px, `#0b1424`) sits beside a `#f9fafb` panel, radius 10px, headed "Access your mind" at 80px. The panel holds a Google button (orange, 300 by 50px, radius 25px, shadow `5px 5px 10px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`) and an Apple button (white, same size and shadow). A "Sign up here." link is orange. No email field.

## Not found

[https://mymind.com/this-page-does-not-exist-mymind](https://mymind.com/this-page-does-not-exist-mymind) is a white page. "404 - Not found" is Open Sans 36px weight 300. The line under it is "This page slipped our mind." A "Homepage" link is black 18px.

## Phone

At innerWidth 390 the header links hide and a 50 by 36px hamburger shows on `#e2e6ee`. The hero headline drops to 50px. The opened menu was not captured.

## Not captured

Signed-in library, search, empty, loading and error states. Payment, plan checkout, and the Google or Apple provider screens. The onboarding overlay on the access host was visible at the fold and was not stepped through.
