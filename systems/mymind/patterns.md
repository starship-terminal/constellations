# Patterns

How the public pages combine the controls. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

Desktop header: wordmark, What / Why / How with coloured dots, Log in as a blue link, Sign up as an ember pill. Footer repeats product, legal and auth links. The footer Sign in and Create an account controls open the auth wall and were not entered.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Phone shell

At 390px the header is a rounded bar with the wordmark and a Menu control. Menu opens an overlay with What, Why, How, LOG IN and SIGN UP. LOG IN and SIGN UP are auth walls.

[mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

## Hero

A large serif or Avenir display, a grey sentence, and outlined chips for notes, bookmarks, inspiration, articles and images. The primary action is an ember pill. On the phone that pill is "Download on the App Store" and leaves the site.

## Pricing

Three plan cards under a Monthly / Yearly toggle. The toggle stays on the page. SIGN UP leaves for https://access.mymind.com/.

[pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [pricing-light-yearly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-yearly.png)

## Indexes

Mind Palace and Interviews are long lists of links into essay templates. One essay and one interview were captured to show the template. The rest are the same layout.

[palace-light-default.png](screenshots/palace-light-default.png), [essay-light-default.png](screenshots/essay-light-default.png), [interview-light-default.png](screenshots/interview-light-default.png)
