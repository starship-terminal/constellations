# BYBORRE design reference

A token set and component reference for BYBORRE, the Amsterdam textile studio, captured from the public site at [byborre.com](https://byborre.com/) on 2026-11-21. Signed out, headless Chromium, desktop 1440 and phone 390. The Design Studio at `/dashboard` sits behind a login dialog and was not entered.

The public site is cream (`#f5f5f0`) and ink (`#1b1b1b`), with one yellow (`#fae210`) for the New badge and for button hover. Helvetica Neue is the loaded family. Controls are 48px with a 10px radius. The footer inverts to cream on ink and rounds its top corners at 24px.

## How the values were read

Playwright loaded the public pages. Custom properties were read from the live CSSOM (`raw/css-vars.json`). The site declares a `--color-primary-*` green ramp and a `.dark` override. Neither paints the public pages, so those greens stay in the raw file and one unused `primary500` token. Cream, ink, yellow, type, radii and control sizes were read from computed styles on body, header, footer, buttons, fields and the login dialog (`raw/component-tokens.json`, `raw/components-computed.json`). Hover and focus-visible used the pointer and the keyboard. The cookie banner was hidden and not accepted.

## What to distrust

- The hero video failed in headless Chromium (Mux codec error). The home screenshot shows that error overlay.
- `--color-primary-500` (`#00e606`) is declared and unused on the measured controls. Do not treat it as the brand accent. The accent that paints is yellow `#fae210`.
- Focus outline color computed as transparent on several controls while a 2px ring and a yellow hover fill were visible. The ring width is measured. The ring colour is the ink, read from the crop, and is not a separate token.
- The login scrim was not isolated as a computed colour.
- The Design Studio, checkout, and submitted forms were not captured.
- Helvetica Neue is loaded by the site. The package does not ship the font files.

## Fonts

Helvetica Neue, weights 400, 500 and 700, from the page's `@font-face` rules. The computed stack continues Arial, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif. The files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do set type in Helvetica Neue on cream.** Body is 13px ink `#1b1b1b` on `#f5f5f0`. [screenshots/collection-light-default.png](screenshots/collection-light-default.png)
- **Do spend yellow on the badge and on hover.** The New badge and the order bar hover are `#fae210`. Resting primary buttons are ink. [screenshots/textile-light-default.png](screenshots/textile-light-default.png)
- **Do keep controls at 48px with a 10px radius.** Fields and the login button share that size. [components/crops/login-submit-default.png](components/crops/login-submit-default.png)
- **Do invert the footer.** Ink ground, cream type, 24px top radius. [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png)
- **Don't treat the declared green ramp as the accent.** It is in the CSS and does not paint these pages.
- **Don't round the page titles.** Display type is square. Rounding belongs on controls, badges and the footer cap.
