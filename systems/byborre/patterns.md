# Patterns

How the public pages combine. Screenshots are in [screenshots](screenshots). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Shell

A 57px cream header sits under a black sample marquee. Nav links are 16px Helvetica Neue. Collaborations and About open mega menus. Cart opens a 380px drawer. The account icon opens the login dialog. The footer is ink with a 24px top radius and cream type. Phone width replaces the nav with an Open menu button.

Shown in [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [screenshots/home-light-collaborations.png](screenshots/home-light-collaborations.png), [screenshots/home-light-cart.png](screenshots/home-light-cart.png) and [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Catalogue

Textile Room is an 80px title, a color row, a Pattern menu, and a grid of textile cards. A card opens a family page. The family page configures color, tone and repeat in place, then an order bar.

Shown in [screenshots/collection-light-default.png](screenshots/collection-light-default.png), [screenshots/collection-light-pattern.png](screenshots/collection-light-pattern.png) and [screenshots/textile-light-default.png](screenshots/textile-light-default.png).

## Login wall

Design Studio, the account icon and the mobile Login/Sign-up row open a dialog on the home URL. It asks for email and password. Sign up and Forgot password swap the same dialog. Nothing was typed. The signed-in app is not in this package.

Shown in [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [screenshots/login-light-signup.png](screenshots/login-light-signup.png). The field list is [login-review.json](login-review.json).

## Form

Contact is a long cream page with 48px fields, a 104px textarea, two selects and an ink Submit. The form was not filled or submitted, so there is no validation state.

Shown in [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).

## Empty and error

The cart drawer opens empty, with an Explore button back to Textile Room. An unknown path paints a split 404: ink panel, 128px numeral, cream panel, "Go back home".

Shown in [screenshots/home-light-cart.png](screenshots/home-light-cart.png) and [screenshots/notfound-light-default.png](screenshots/notfound-light-default.png).

## Not present

No toast, no destructive confirmation, no onboarding, no data table, no theme switch on the public site. Search is not a header control. Checkout was not started.
