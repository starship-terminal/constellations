# Runey

Runey is invoicing, projects and clients for freelancers and small teams. This package records the public marketing site at runey.app and the signed-out auth page at runey.app/auth, measured on 19 November 2026. No account was created. Nothing was typed into the form and nothing was submitted. Google was not followed. The live pages stayed in light.

### Principles

1. Paint the page pale and the type near-black.
2. Use the grey gradient pill for the one action.
3. Keep See features as the bordered pill.
4. Put plans on white cards with a soft border.
5. Do not paint the declared dark theme as if the public site showed it.
6. Do not invent a signed-in dashboard, an error, or a payment step.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview tab opened https://runey.app/ and https://runey.app/auth. Computed styles and the public stylesheet `assets/index-BNow9ey6.css` were read from those live pages. The preview viewport was 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, device pixel ratio 2, visual viewport scale 1. The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`), so the screenshots, the crops, the hover reading and the focus reading were taken in headless Playwright against pages that had already loaded with no Cloudflare challenge. Those shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at device pixel ratio 2, then quantized to 256-colour sRGB.

Colours are the computed `background-color`, `color`, `border` and `box-shadow` of the body, the hero, the buttons, the chips, the plan cards, the footer and the auth fields. A custom property that was declared and not painted is labelled declared. That includes the whole `.dark` block.

## What to distrust

The dark tokens were not painted. The public pages never applied `.dark`. Do not treat them as a live theme.

The hero heading was measured at 1169 CSS pixels, where the `md:text-[4rem]` class had already won, so the heading is 64px. The phone screenshot is 390 wide. The heading size at that width was not computed. The class also names `text-[2.25rem]`, which is 36px. That phone size is a class, not a computed reading.

`--radius` is `.875rem` (14px). The controls that were measured use Tailwind radius classes (pill, 12px, 16px, 24px), not that token.

Status colours (`--destructive`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--orange`, the chart colours) are declared and were not painted on a public control.

The primary shadow token holds one layer. The button paints four. The full string is in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json).

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from the tokens, not from the images. The product demo in the hero is a marketing image, not the signed-in app.

## Fonts

The painted family is Poppins, loaded from Google Fonts at weights 400, 500, 600 and 700, with a sans-serif fallback. Poppins is licensed under the SIL Open Font License. The page also loads Allura, Inter, Playfair Display, Space Mono, Satoshi and Instrument Serif. Those families were not used by the controls that were measured.

## Do and don't

- **Paint the page pale and the type near-black.** The home sits on `light.surface.page` (`#fafafa`) with headings in `light.text.primary` (`#1a1a1a`). [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) shows the pair.
- **Use the grey gradient pill for the one action.** Start for free, Sign In and the trial button share the gradient from `#575757` to `#292929`, white type, and a pill radius. [button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png) shows it.
- **Keep See features as the bordered pill.** It uses the page colour and a 1px `#ebebeb` border, not the gradient. [button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png) shows it.
- **Put plans on white cards with a soft border.** Solo, Team and Agency share one body. [card-plan.png](components/crops/card-plan.png) shows one.
- **Do not paint the declared dark theme as if the public site showed it.** The pages stayed in light. The dark values are in the tokens and labelled declared.
- **Do not invent a signed-in dashboard, an error, or a payment step.** Those were not on the pages that were opened.
