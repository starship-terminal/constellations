Palette

The declared --token-* layer carries the palette in core.color.palette.*; the measured roles resolve in the light and dark sets. Values are the sRGB gamut map of the site's color(display-p3 …) declarations.

Spacing and radius

The standard pill is 40px high on a 20px radius with 12px/18px padding; fields and the newsletter submit are 40px capsules; feature cards round at 18.8px and table rows at 12px. Focus is the browser-auto 1px ring.

Type

One uploaded family, three painted weights: Aquawax Pro Bold (700) on every heading from the 56px ticker to 17px labels, Aquawax Pro DemiBold (600) on buttons and the tag chip, and Aquawax Pro Medium (500) on body and UI. Everything tracks at normal; samples fall back to a rounded system sans.
