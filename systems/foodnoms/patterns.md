# Patterns

Public marketing site. The signed-in app is the iOS app and is not in this package.

## Header and footer

Every Framer page repeats a white (or black, in dark) header and an orange footer band. The header holds the wordmark, three links, and Get the App. The footer holds four columns: Explore, Comparisons, Company, Social. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) for what each control opens. Screenshot: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Hero

A rotating display line (56px Aquawax Pro Bold, one colour per phrase) over a fixed "with Foodnoms" line, a grey lede, and an App Store badge. Phone mockups sit under the hero. They are images. Screenshot: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), dark: [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

## Feature grid

Two-column cards, 25px radius, grey fill, a coloured heading per feature. Screenshot: [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Help center

A search button opens a dialog. Category rows expand to article links. Screenshot: [screenshots/help-light-default.png](screenshots/help-light-default.png), open row: [screenshots/help-light-accordion.png](screenshots/help-light-accordion.png), search: [screenshots/help-light-search.png](screenshots/help-light-search.png).

## News list and insider form

Cards, then View More, then an email field that was not submitted. Screenshot: [screenshots/news-light-newsletter-focus.png](screenshots/news-light-newsletter-focus.png).

## Phone navigation

Below the desktop breakpoint the header links collapse into a menu. Screenshot: [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Missing patterns

Form validation, empty, loading, and error states, destructive confirmation, and onboarding are not on the public site. Search results inside the help dialog are the only filter UI: [screenshots/help-light-search-results.png](screenshots/help-light-search-results.png).
