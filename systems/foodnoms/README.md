# Foodnoms design reference

A token set and component reference for foodnoms.com's public marketing site — for building friendly nutrition-tracker marketing pages, app-store pricing tables, doc-style help centers, press hubs and longform article surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **foodnoms.com** on **2026-11-16**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 (light and the site's authored dark theme via emulated prefers-color-scheme) and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /plus pricing, /help with its search modal and an opened accordion, /news with the newsletter form, /press, /family-plan, /vs/myfitnesspal, a help article, a news article, /terms, /privacy, and the /open/settings/sign-up deep-link interstitial, plus mobile passes on home, /plus and /help.

Foodnoms is a Framer-published marketing site for an iOS/macOS app, and it shows: the stylesheet declares ~90 flat `--token-*` custom properties — the `#ff5406` accent ramp, the neutral ramp, the `#2f2f2f` ink alphas and a per-nutrient hue set — in `color(display-p3 …)` values, and re-declares 39 of them under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` for a real authored dark theme (canvas black, card fill #1a1a1a, accent pills unchanged). One uploaded family, **Aquawax Pro**, paints everything in three weights — Bold 700 for the 56px hero ticker down to 17px labels, DemiBold 600 for buttons, Medium 500 for body and UI. **Boundary:** the product is an App-Store iOS/macOS app — there is no web sign-in and no web product surface. Every Download / Get-the-App link (a JS redirect to apps.apple.com), the press-kit zips, the mailto links and the newsletter submit were measured, never followed or submitted; the help search and newsletter fields were typed into then cleared.

### Principles

1. Do spend the orange once per surface.
2. Do let the hues mean nutrients.
3. Do keep the Aquawax voice.
4. Do capsule the fields.
5. Do card on #f2f2f2.
6. Don't style the app from this package.
7. Don't darken the accent on hover.
8. Don't soften the dark theme.
9. Don't read wrapper colours as the product's.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the orange once per surface.** `#ff5406` (`light.accent`) is reserved for the conversion pills — Get the App in the header, Try Foodnoms, Subscribe — and it hovers to the lighter `#ff9c7b` (`light.accent-hover`), never darker. Everything else is neutral or a nutrient hue. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-hover.png) and [cta-secondary](components/crops/cta-secondary-default.png).
2. **Do let the hues mean nutrients.** Red, yellow, blue, indigo, teal and steel (`light.hue.*`) paint feature-card titles, the hero ticker's rotating word and the goal pills — colour carries the metric, not decoration. See [hero-ticker](components/crops/hero-ticker-default.png) and [feature-card](components/crops/feature-card-default.png).
3. **Do keep the Aquawax voice.** Every heading is Aquawax Pro Bold at 700 with no tracking; buttons sit at DemiBold 600, body at Medium 500. It is a rounded, friendly voice — not a grotesk. See [hero-ticker](components/crops/hero-ticker-default.png).
4. **Do capsule the fields.** Inputs and submits are 40px-radius capsules on the card fill with an inset 1px ring (`light.surface.ring`), not bordered rectangles. See [nl-email](components/crops/nl-email-focused.png).
5. **Do card on #f2f2f2.** Feature, review and news cards sit on `light.surface.card` at 18.8px radius — flat, borderless, never white-on-white with a shadow. (The values trio is the exception: transparent and square, its glyphs painted straight in the nutrient hues.) See [feature-card](components/crops/feature-card-default.png).
6. **Don't style the app from this package.** Foodnoms the product is an iOS/macOS app behind the App Store download; every downloaded surface is a documented boundary, not inferable from the marketing site.
7. **Don't darken the accent on hover.** The accent lifts to a lighter tint on hover (`light.accent-hover`); darkening it is not the pattern.
8. **Don't soften the dark theme.** The authored dark is a true black canvas with #1a1a1a cards — the accent pills and white text stay identical, and the .2/.4 text greys brighten to .8/.6. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
9. **Don't read wrapper colours as the product's.** Several anchors' own `color` stays the browser default link blue while the painted descendant text carries the real value — read each entry's text row, as components.md does.
