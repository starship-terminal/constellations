# Mangomint design reference

Mangomint is salon and spa software. This package captures the public marketing site at www.mangomint.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop captures are 1440x900 at 1x (measured `innerWidth` 1440, `innerHeight` 900, `devicePixelRatio` 1). Phone captures are 390x844 at 2x (measured `innerWidth` 390, `innerHeight` 844, `devicePixelRatio` 2). The site has one light theme. The app login at https://app.mangomint.com/login was opened as a wall and not filled.

The marketing site paints ink (#191e49) on cream (#fcfafa), with one magenta accent (#bc269b) for filled buttons, links, and the outline of Book a demo. Type is TT Commons Pro. Buttons are pills. The login wall is a separate navy surface with a lilac button and is not a theme toggle.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages and computed styles on the live elements. `raw/components-computed.json` is that reading. Colour, type, radius and size tokens are those computed values, labelled Measured. Aliases in the `light` and `login` sets are labelled Derived.

The page CSS declares 49 custom properties. They are layout helpers (`--section-gap`, `--pill-w`, `--gradient-color-1` through `4`, `--swoosh-stop-*`). They are not a colour role system. `raw/css-vars.json` lists the names. They were not promoted to tokens.

Hover is a real pointer hover. Pressed is Chromium `CSS.forcePseudoState` with `hover` and `active`, so no link fired. Focus is `element.focus()`. No form was submitted. No password, code, or profile field was filled.

## What to distrust

- The marketing site has no design-token layer. A role that was not on a measured element is absent, not filled in.
- Focus and pressed often match hover. The product recolours the label or the fill and does not paint a focus ring.
- The homepage product screenshot is a public demo. It is not the signed-in app.
- The login wall uses Muli, not TT Commons Pro. Do not apply the marketing type stack to the app.
- The announcement bar colour was read from the login body, which computes to the same navy. The bar element itself was not isolated.
- `pp-editorial-old`, `geist`, and `tt-commons-mono` are declared as `@font-face`. No captured element used them as its computed family, so they are not tokens.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- Contact, security, legal, and the updates full page were captured and then deleted because they showed an email address or a street address. The site tree still records those URLs.

## Fonts

TT Commons Pro is the marketing face (`tt-commons-pro`, weight axis 100–900). It is proprietary and is not in the repo. The style guide falls back to the system sans. The login wall computes to Muli, also not bundled.

## Do and don't

- **Do use one magenta for the filled action.** Try it now, Start a free trial, and the phone Pricing pill share #bc269b. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do outline the second action.** Book a demo is a 2px magenta inset shadow on a transparent pill, not a second fill. [components/crops/button-secondary-default.png](components/crops/button-secondary-default.png)
- **Do set marketing type in TT Commons Pro, sentence case for headings and small caps for some labels.** The hero is 54px weight 620. Nav labels are weight 540. [components/crops/hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png)
- **Do keep corners as pills on actions.** 48px on header buttons, 5px on the login wall. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Don't treat the homepage calendar as the app.** It is a public demo. The app starts at the login wall. [screenshots/login-light-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-wall.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The marketing site has no theme switch. The navy login wall is a different surface and a different font. [screenshots/login-light-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-wall.png)

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/public.mjs

python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```
