# Mangomint design reference

Tokens and component notes for Mangomint (mangomint.com), captured **2026-11-18**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. One light theme. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing/`, `/features/online-booking/`, `/demo/`, `/trial/`, `/about-us/`, `/contact-us/`, `/blog/`, `/go/customers/`, `/learn/`, `/legal/` (redirects to `/legal/privacy-policy/`), `app.mangomint.com/login`, `app.mangomint.com/forgot-password`, and `booking.mangomint.com/737397`. The salon admin sits behind sign-in. Nothing was submitted, booked, or purchased.

Marketing colour is CSS-module literals, not a token layer. The app host declares `--color-*` and `--mt-duration-*` on `:root`. The booking host declares `--base-color` and `--button-color`.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do paint a cream page and navy type.
2. Do spend magenta on the one filled action.
3. Do keep Book a demo as an outline.
4. Do set the headline at 54px, weight 620.
5. Do keep the bar at 80px.
6. Do paint the login wall navy.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't style the salon admin from this package.
9. Don't book from the public page.
10. Don't submit demo, trial, or forgot password.
11. Don't copy the wordmark or the TT Commons files.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-18, light, 1440. Hover is a real pointer. Focus-visible is keyboard. Pressed is a forced `:active` pseudo-class, so no control fired. A Mira promo strip (`banner-module--banner`) was hidden with CSS. It was never clicked. No cookie consent banner was observed. Stylesheets were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Motion was probed on home, pricing, login and booking into [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json). Page geometry is in [raw/layout.json](raw/layout.json).

## What to distrust

- The salon admin. `app.mangomint.com` redirects to `/login`. Calendar, clients, payments and checkout were never reached. Log In was not pressed.
- Demo and trial. Continue on `/demo/` and `/trial/` was measured and not pressed, so step 2, validation and any account creation were not reached.
- Forgot password. The email field was measured. Continue was not submitted.
- Public booking. `booking.mangomint.com/737397` is Mangomint's own embed (title "Mangomint Internal"). It loaded signed out with an empty service list. Rebook a past service was not pressed. No slot, no checkout.
- The desktop Features menu. The click timed out. The Businesses menu is captured.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes. Four of them are not pressed readings: Book a demo, Try it now, Businesses and Log In (`nav-demo`, `nav-trial`, `nav-businesses`, `login-submit` in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json)) equal their default, because the 2026-11-18 script left the first pressed control's marker in place and forced that control again. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now clears the marker; those four need a recapture.
- Hand-typed values. `tools/build_tokens.py` copies most values from the readings by hand rather than reading raw/ at build time. They match the readings today; after a recapture, check them against the new readings.
- The stylesheet harvest. The app host's sheet list was cut. Measured computed styles are the source of truth. Declared `--color-*` values that were not painted on a captured element are labeled as declared.
- TT Commons Pro, TT Commons Mono and IvyPresto. The family names are measured. The files are proprietary and not in this package.
- Quantised screenshots. Read colours from the JSON, not from the 256-colour PNGs.
- Login and booking phone shots are 1x. Those hosts report `devicePixelRatio` 1.

## Fonts

TT Commons Pro (400, 540, 620) on marketing, TT Commons (500) on the login button and booking page, TT Commons Mono (400) on "Watch a video tour", Muli on the password visibility control. Loaded from Typekit. Licensed, not shipped. Tokens fall back to `sans-serif` or `monospace`.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint a cream page and navy type.** Page is `#fcfafa`, type is `#191e49` ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
2. **Do spend magenta on the one filled action.** Try it now is `#bc269b`, cream label, 48px pill ([crop](components/crops/nav-trial-default.png)).
3. **Do keep Book a demo as an outline.** Magenta ink, transparent fill, same pill ([crop](components/crops/nav-demo-default.png)).
4. **Do set the headline at 54px, weight 620.** Line-height is 60px ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
5. **Do keep the bar at 80px.** Log in is 17px / 540. Businesses opens a local menu ([menu](screenshots/home-light-businesses.png)).
6. **Do paint the login wall navy.** The button is lilac `#b28eff`, 5px radius, not the marketing magenta pill ([login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)).
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site does not paint one.
8. **Don't style the salon admin from this package.** Calendar, clients and payments sit behind sign-in ([login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)).
9. **Don't book from the public page.** The embed lists no services, and Rebook was not pressed ([book](screenshots/book-light-default.png)).
10. **Don't submit demo, trial, or forgot password.** Continue was measured and not pressed ([demo](screenshots/demo-light-default.png)).
11. **Don't copy the wordmark or the TT Commons files.** The typefaces are licensed. Tokens name the families.
