# Burrow

A signed-out capture of the public Burrow shop at [https://burrow.com](https://burrow.com), prices in USD, on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The page is `#ffffff`, department type is Suisse Intl at `#262626`, and the hero is Suisse Intl at 68px. Shop Sale is a 44px white button.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

## Type

Suisse Intl on body at 16px and on department links at 14px, weight 400, letter-spacing `0.14px`. The homepage h1 is Suisse Intl at 40px, weight 500. The hero title is 68px, weight 500, white. Eyebrows and the hero kicker are DM Mono. Font files are not in this package.

## Controls

Shop Sale is 44px with a `3px` radius and a white fill. Add to cart is 45px with a `2px` radius and an ink fill. The 35% OFF badge is 34px, coral, radius `2px`. The promo bar is 41px. The header, promo plus main bar, is 106px.
