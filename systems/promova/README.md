# Promova design reference

A token set for Promova's public signed-out site. Read from **promova.com** on **2026-11-19**, signed out, at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Surfaces: home, blog, the English course page, who we are, the public sign-up form, and the site's own 404. No account was created. No card, phone, or paid plan was entered.

Promova paints one light theme on a black page. `body` is `#000000`. White and pastel islands (nav, hero, sections, footer) sit in a 20px gutter with a 30px radius. Type is Nekst for display and buttons, Manrope for body. Filled actions are black squares. The sign-up submit is the exception, at 10px. Fields are 5px with a 30% black hairline.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page black and put content on white and pastel islands.
2. Do keep filled actions black and square.
3. Do set display type in Nekst and body in Manrope.
4. Do use 30px radius for desktop islands and 20px at 390.
5. Don't invent a second accent.
6. Don't round the marketing buttons.
7. Don't ship a dark theme from these tokens.
8. Don't invent a focus ring from these tokens.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read from the live signed-out pages. The T3 preview browser loaded `https://promova.com/` and `https://promova.com/sign-up` and returned computed styles, custom properties, and stylesheet hover rules. `preview_snapshot` failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so the PNGs come from headless Chromium on public pages that had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. Login was not opened in that headless session.

Custom properties on `:root` are thin: `--black`, `--white`, the Nekst and Manrope stacks, body size steps, and a yellow route-progress bar. Section colours are painted as backgrounds, not as named colour tokens. Those fills were read with `getComputedStyle`.

The entry promo was closed with its close button. Nothing was submitted.

## What to distrust

- The signed-in app, checkout, and any paid plan were not opened.
- `/log-in` is a 404. Log in is a control on `/sign-up`, and that control was not followed into a second form.
- Focus on the email field did not paint a ring, so the package publishes no focus token.
- Nav hover and pressed colours are stylesheet rules, not a forced computed hover. The rules were read in the preview browser and not saved (`rules` in raw/extra.json is empty), so `core.color.ink-hover`, `ink-pressed`, `gray.200` and the hover use of `gray.100` are labelled Reconstruction. So are the other values only the preview browser read: `core.color.ink.50`, `progress`, the 26px and 34px body line heights, and the 0.5s body transition.
- Screenshots are headless Chromium, not preview-browser frames. The sign-up viewport shot was taken before the form painted; the card crop is the form.
- Quantisation was not run. Read colours from tokens.json.
- No dark theme exists. The black page is the light theme's ground.

## Fonts

Nekst (`--heading-font`, `--nekst-font`) for titles and buttons. Manrope (`--body-font`, `--manrope-font`) for body and fields. Fallbacks in the computed stack are Rubik, Arial, Lucida Grande, and the system sans. No font file is in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the page black and put content on white and pastel islands.** The ground is `body` `#000000`. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do keep filled actions black and square.** Marketing CTAs are 0 radius. See [button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Do set display type in Nekst and body in Manrope.** The hero is 70px/700. See [heading-hero-default.png](components/crops/heading-hero-default.png).
- **Do use 30px radius for desktop islands and 20px at 390.** See [nav-default.png](components/crops/nav-default.png).
- **Don't invent a second accent.** The yellow `#fff584` is the announcement strip and the route-progress bar, not a CTA colour.
- **Don't round the marketing buttons.** The 10px radius belongs to the sign-up submit only. See [signup-card-default.png](components/crops/signup-card-default.png).
- **Don't ship a dark theme from these tokens.** The black ground is the light theme.
- **Don't invent a focus ring from these tokens.** Focusing the email field changed nothing visible, so there is no focus token.
