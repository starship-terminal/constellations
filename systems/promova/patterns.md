# Promova patterns

Signed-out pages, 2026-11-19. Desktop shots are 1440 CSS pixels. The phone shot is 390.

## Home

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

The page ground is black, painted on `body`. A yellow announcement strip sits above a white nav island (30px radius, 20px inset, 100px tall). The hero is a lavender `#bec8ff` island with a 70px Nekst title, a 24px Manrope lede, and a square black TRY NOW. Later islands reuse that radius with their own fills: white numbers, `#f5f5f5` features, `#fff8a7` confidence, `#bcf0d0` quote, `#f2f4ff` reviews, `#ebe0d3` resources, `#f5a3d0` awards, `#f4f4f4` FAQ. The footer is a white 30px island.

An entry promo (`#dfe3ff`, 30px radius) covers the page until its close button is used. It was closed for the shots. It was not submitted.

## Sign up

[screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png) and [components/crops/signup-card-default.png](components/crops/signup-card-default.png).

`/sign-up` is a public form: Google, Apple, email, and password. The page ground is `#eeeeee`. The card is white, 400px, 20px radius. The form was not submitted. No card, phone, or SSO company login was entered.

`/log-in` returned the site's own "page not found" page. The sign-up card links to log in as a button on the same form, not as `/log-in`.
