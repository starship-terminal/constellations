# Promova patterns

Public marketing only. The signed-in app was not reached.

## App shell

The desktop page is a black frame. A white bar with a 30px radius holds the wordmark, four menu buttons, SIGN UP and GET STARTED. Content sits in rounded panels of the same width (1400px at 1440). See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

On a phone the bar keeps the wordmark and a menu button. That button opens a white drawer with the four menus, store badges and the same two actions. See [mobile-home-light-drawer](screenshots/mobile-home-light-drawer.png).

## Hero

A lilac panel, 30px radius, holds a 70px Nekst headline, a short paragraph and a square black TRY NOW. A phone mock sits on the right at desktop and below the copy on a phone. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Menus

Languages, Courses, Why Promova and Resources each open a menu of links. They do not navigate until a link is chosen. See [home-light-menu-languages](screenshots/home-light-menu-languages.png).

## FAQ

A pink page, a search field with a black SEARCH button, then white topic cards. The search was focused and not submitted. See [faq-light-default](screenshots/faq-light-default.png).

## Auth and onboarding walls

SIGN UP opens `/sign-up`, which rendered no email form. GET STARTED and TRY NOW open `/foxtrot/org-main`, a 15-step language quiz. Both branches stopped. See [signup-light-wall](screenshots/signup-light-wall.png) and [onboarding-light-wall](screenshots/onboarding-light-wall.png).

## Not captured

A form with a validation error, empty, loading and error states, a destructive confirmation, feedback after an action, search results, a data table, and onboarding past screen 1.
