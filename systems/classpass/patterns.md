# ClassPass patterns

Public marketing site. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs in [screenshots](screenshots).

## App shell

Desktop: sticky white header, wordmark left, text links, blue pill at the right, yellow trial banner under the header. Phone: wordmark and a hamburger that opens a full-height list ending in the same blue pill. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Menus

How it works is a small menu under the nav item. The language control in the footer opens "Select your language" over a dimmed page. See [home-light-how-menu](screenshots/home-light-how-menu.png) and [home-light-language](screenshots/home-light-language.png).

## Plan picker

A tall blue hero, then credit cards with a monthly price, a change-location link, and an FAQ accordion. See [plans-light-full](screenshots/plans-light-full.png).

## Auth wall

Login is a white card on a grey page: Apple, Google, or email and password. Signup is a separate page with Apple and Google. Both were photographed and not submitted. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [signup-light-default](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).

## Trial explainer

`/walkthrough` is a short carousel (Next) before signup. See [walkthrough-light-default](screenshots/walkthrough-light-default.png) and [walkthrough-light-slide-2](screenshots/walkthrough-light-slide-2.png).

## Content pages

About, credits, gifts, blog, brands, press, partners and legal pages share the same header and a dark footer. See [about-light-default](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [features-light-default](screenshots/features-light-default.png), [blog-light-default](screenshots/blog-light-default.png).

## Not reached

Signed-in booking, search results without a precise address, empty states behind login, destructive confirmation, and onboarding after account creation.
