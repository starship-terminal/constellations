# Playbook

Playbook is file infrastructure for creative teams. This package records the public marketing site at playbook.com and the signed-out sign-in page, measured on 19 November 2026. No account was created. The home and pricing pages were read signed out, at 1440 CSS pixels and at 390. `/login/` redirects to `/sign-in`. The email field was already focused. Nothing was typed into it and nothing was submitted.

The marketing pages set their type in Stabil Grotesk. The sign-in page sets its type in Akkurat and publishes a large custom-property layer (`--primaryColor`, `--textColor`, `--borderRadiusXl`). The marketing page also sets `--primary-color` to `#599ffe`, a different blue from the auth coral.

4. Do not paint the auth coral on the marketing page.
5. Do not invent a hover, an error, or a signed-in screen.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview tab opened https://www.playbook.com/, https://www.playbook.com/pricing/ and https://www.playbook.com/login/. The login URL redirected to https://www.playbook.com/sign-in. Computed styles, custom properties and font faces were read from those live pages. The preview viewport was 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, device pixel ratio 2, visual viewport scale 1. The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`), so the screenshots and the 1440 and 390 shots were taken in headless Playwright against pages that had already loaded with no Cloudflare challenge.

Marketing colours are the computed `background-color`, `color`, `border` and `box-shadow` of the body, the header, the CTAs, the prompt, the plan cards and the billing toggle. Auth colours are the computed style of the heading, the social buttons, the email wrapper and the disabled continue button, checked against the custom properties on the same page. A custom property that was not painted on a measured control is labelled declared.

## What to distrust

The home headline is in the DOM at 56px, and the type crop shows it, but the first viewport of the home screenshot is the pinned canvas. Scrolling the window did not bring the headline into that first screen.

The preview measurements were taken at 1169px, not 1440. The 1440 and 390 labels on the screenshots are the Playwright viewport, recorded in each `.png.capture.json`. A control size measured in the preview (the prompt box at 760px) is that viewport's size.

`--primary-color` on the marketing page is `#599ffe`. `--primaryColor` on the sign-in page is `#ff5d64`. They are different tokens. The coral was declared and not painted on the disabled continue button.

The plan card radius came back as 17.535px and the New badge as 4.41882px. Those are the computed values, not a designed step.

Stabil Grotesk and Akkurat are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

The sign-in art board is a published sample collage. Its fill is an image. No flat panel colour was measured.

## Fonts

Stabil Grotesk is the marketing face, loaded as `stabil_grotesk` at weights 400, 500 and 600. The sign-in sheet names the same face StabilGrotesk. The tokens fall back to `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`.

Akkurat is the sign-in face. The computed stack is Akkurat, then system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue and Arial. `--akkuratFontFamily` names the same stack with BlinkMacSystemFont.

Other faces are declared on the sign-in page and were not painted on a measured control: Victor Serif, Charter, Aktiv Grotesk, DM Sans, Bricolage Grotesque, Labil Grotesk. They are not tokens.

The font files are proprietary and are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the dark page for the marketing home and the white page for pricing.** The home body is `#0d0e10`. Pricing is `#ffffff`. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Get started is the paper pill. Schedule a demo is the ink pill.** Both are 10px radius. [components/crops/button-get-started-default.png](components/crops/button-get-started-default.png) [components/crops/button-schedule-demo-default.png](components/crops/button-schedule-demo-default.png)
- **Plan cards share one body.** The last card is the only ink button. [components/crops/card-plan.png](components/crops/card-plan.png)
- **Do not paint the auth coral on the marketing page.** Marketing blue is `#599ffe`. Auth primary is `#ff5d64`, and it was not painted on the disabled button. [components/crops/button-continue-disabled.png](components/crops/button-continue-disabled.png)
- **Do not invent a hover, an error, or a signed-in screen.** Those states were not captured.
