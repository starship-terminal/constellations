# Patterns

How the public pages are put together. The shots are the first viewport of each page.

## Marketing home

[screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) is the home page at 1440 by 900, scroll 0, refreshed 2026-11-21. The page background is `#0d0e10`. The headline in the first viewport is "Intelligent infrastructure for every file in your app", Stabil Grotesk 56px / 600, white, letter-spacing -2.52px. ## Pricing

[screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) is `/pricing/` at 1440 by 900. The page is white. The same header is dark text on white. The heading is 56px / 600, `#222222`, letter-spacing -2.24px: "We have a plan for all your files". A billing toggle sits under the lede, with Yearly selected. Four plan cards share one body and change the name, the price and the last button. Enterprise is the only ink button.

The phone shot is [screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png). The cards stack. The grid was not measured at 390 beyond the screenshot.

## Sign in

[screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) is `/login/`, which redirects to `/sign-in`. The form column is 350px wide, padded 35px, in Akkurat. The heading is "Log in or sign up", 30px / 600, `#0d1a26`. Then Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, an "or" divider, a Work email field, and Continue with email, which is disabled. Terms, Privacy and Community Guidelines sit under the form. A language button reads EN. The right half is a rounded art board of published sample files. Its fill is an image, not a flat colour.

Nothing was typed. Google, Apple and the language menu were not opened. `/sign-up` paints the same wall: [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png). The phone wall is [screenshots/mobile-login-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-login-light-default.png). The contact page is an empty demo form, not submitted: [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).
