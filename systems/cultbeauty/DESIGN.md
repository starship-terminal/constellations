Public shop, signed out, 2026-11-22. The browser landed on the EU storefront, prices in EUR. Values are measured from the live page. Font files are not in this package.

## Type

Inter carries the chrome, nav, buttons, and product title. Department labels are 16px, weight 400. The selected department is weight 700, white on black. The promo strip is 16px, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.8px, uppercase. MAKE UP is 20px, weight 800, letter-spacing 1px. The product title is 30px, weight 400, line-height 36px. The price is 24px, weight 500. The "25% OFF / 20 Brands" hero title is painted in the campaign image, not a DOM text node.

## Geometry

Utility strip 50px, `#efefef`. Promo strip 44px, black. Header 122px. Wordmark rendered 250 by 40. Search 46px tall, 482px wide, radius 0, 1px `#8c8c8c` border. ASK CULT is 40px tall, 115px wide, radius 0. SHOP NOW is 48px, radius 0, 1px black border, padding 0 16px. ADD TO BAG on the product page is 60px tall, 300px wide, radius 0, 2px black border. It sits below the 900px fold.

## What was not captured

Hover, focus, pressed, and disabled states. Phone width. Dark theme. Account, bag, checkout, search submit, shade select, and email signup. ASK CULT was not opened. ADD TO BAG was not clicked. The hero title size is not a CSS reading.
