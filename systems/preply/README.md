# Preply design reference

Tokens and component notes for Preply's signed-out site. Read on **2026-11-19** from preply.com, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 (2x). The T3 preview tab loaded the homepage and returned computed styles, then failed screenshots with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Screenshots are from headless Chromium on public pages that had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge.

Captured: the marketing home, /en/online/english-tutors, /en/teach, /en/business-language-training, /en/proven-progress-platform, /en/stand-with-ukraine, a made-up 404, and /en/login chrome. Login was measured and screenshotted. It was not submitted. Signup asks for a student or tutor path plus Google, Facebook, Apple, or corporate SSO, so no account was created.

Preply paints one theme, `tokyo-ui` / `ds-scheme--base`. Forcing `prefers-color-scheme: dark` left the pink and the ink unchanged. Custom properties on `.ds-tokyo-ui__PkpFv` are content-hashed, so roles here are computed styles of the controls that use them.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export preply` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the hero pink and the action black or pink.
2. Do keep buttons at 8px radius and a 2px border.
3. Do set headings in Platform at 700.
4. Don't invent a dark theme.
5. Don't ship Figtree or Platform files, or the wordmark.
6. Don't treat this as the signed-in product.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM: button variants, text roles, headings, the field wrap, the checkbox box, cards, header, footer. Focus-visible was a real keyboard focus on "Find your tutor" (2px solid #006af5, offset 2px). Hover was a real pointer on that button and on header Log In. The Ukraine bar's black is a screenshot pixel at (720, 20): its computed background was transparent. Hashed custom properties were dumped from `.ds-tokyo-ui__PkpFv` and `.ds-scheme--base` into raw/tokens-tokyo.json and raw/tokens-base.json. Status colours in the token file are those declared colours, marked as not seen on a status component.

## What to distrust

- The Ukraine bar colour is sampled, not a computed style.
- Header computed background is #ff7aac, and the logo pixel matches. A white nav in a description of the screenshot is the illustration sitting on the pink, not a second header fill.
- Status colours (#a3120a, #067560, #966208, #0450b4) are declared in the scheme and were not seen on a component.
- Pressed, disabled, and loading were not captured.
- Field focus was not re-measured. A second headless load of /en/login hit Cloudflare.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Read values from tokens.json.
- Figtree and Platform are not shipped. The style guide falls back.
- components.md is typed into tools/build_components_md.py at capture time, not generated from raw/, and tools/build_tokens.py types its values rather than reading raw/. A recapture updates neither; compare a value with raw/home-measured.json or raw/login-fields.json before relying on it.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the hero pink and the action black or pink.** The hero and header compute to #ff7aac. "Find your tutor" is a black button with white type. "Log in" is a pink button with black type and a black border ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)).
2. **Do keep buttons at 8px radius and a 2px border.** Large controls are 56px, medium 48px, the header Log In is 40px ([button](components/crops/button-oncolor-default.png)).
3. **Do set headings in Platform at 700.** The home hero is 63px / 68px at 1440 and 40px / 36.8px at 390 ([hero](components/crops/heading-hero.png)).
4. **Don't invent a dark theme.** Forcing dark colour-scheme did not restyle the page.
5. **Don't ship Figtree or Platform files, or the wordmark.** Names are evidence. The files and the logo are Preply's.
6. **Don't treat this as the signed-in product.** Login, signup, booking, and checkout were not entered.
