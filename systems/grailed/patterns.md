# Patterns

How the public pages combine the controls in [components.md](components.md). The map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Shop chrome

The homepage stacks a white header, a mint fee ticker, a full-bleed photo hero, and white module rails. Desktop is [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png). Phone is [screenshots/home-light-390.png](screenshots/home-light-390.png): a menu icon, search icon, wordmark, Sign up, and Log in, then the same ticker and hero.

## Category

Men's Tops puts a breadcrumb and a 32px title over a horizontal card row. Cards in the kept shot are Grailed's own listings of books and magazines, with a designer name, a size, a dollar price, and a heart. See [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Designers index

A title, logo tiles with item counts, an A to Z index, and a three-column name list. See [screenshots/designers-light-1440.png](screenshots/designers-light-1440.png).

## About

A photo hero with a white 34px sentence, then a text section. See [screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Auth wall

Log in and Sign up open a centered dialog on the same URL. The dialog offers Google, Facebook, Apple, and an email button. Those branches stop. See [screenshots/login-light-1440.png](screenshots/login-light-1440.png) and [screenshots/signup-light-1440.png](screenshots/signup-light-1440.png).
