The chrome

Two different top bars were measured. The 72px home bar carries the wordmark, the quiet Log in, the solid dark Sign up pill and the round menu button that opens the site-menu drawer. The 56px bar on the content pages sits on a sage wash and carries the wordmark, the rounded-full pill links (About, FAQ, Blog, Online Treatment, Wall of Love, LegitScript) and the dark "Try Doctronic For Free" CTA. Buttons are 999px pills: the accent action paints blue-600 (light) / blue-400 (dark) with the brand-glow shadow; the dark solid button is the inverted solid-bg/solid-fg pair.

Type

Three self-hosted faces: fraunces for display and italic text, geist for heading/body/label, geistMono for mono. The --text-* tokens are composite font shorthands — display at /1.17, italic display at /1.25, everything else at /1.4 — 400 body and display, 500 label, 600 heading and mono-bold. The webfonts are licensed and not loaded here; samples fall back to Georgia/system sans.
