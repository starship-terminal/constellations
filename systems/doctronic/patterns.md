# Patterns

## Home composer

The first screen is a centered greeting, a short lede, and one composer card. The card holds a textarea, an Anonymous chip, and a blue Start chat pill. Log in and Sign up sit in the header, not in the card.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Marketing sidebar

A left drawer, not a persistent app sidebar. It groups Dashboard (New chat), Explore (About Chat, Health Record, Health Information), and Company (About us, Careers, For Partners). Sign up for free and Log in sit at the bottom. Version v1.40.575 is printed under the drawer.

[screenshots/home-light-sidebar.png](screenshots/home-light-sidebar.png)

## Public chat

A narrow icon rail, an empty "Chat for free" greeting, a composer, and four suggestion chips. Health record and Appointments are in the rail and stop at the login wall.

[screenshots/chat-light-default.png](screenshots/chat-light-default.png)

## Auth dialog

One card, centered, over a dimmed page. Title, then a blue email button, then Google, Apple, and Facebook outline buttons, then a switch between log in and sign up. The email step replaces the provider list with one empty email field. Nothing in that step was filled or submitted.

[screenshots/home-light-login.png](screenshots/home-light-login.png)

## Phone

The same home stacks in a 390px viewport. The sidebar becomes a full-height menu. The login dialog fills more of the screen.
