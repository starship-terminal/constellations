## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the live home stylesheet `:root` rule: `--white`, `--black`, `--black-ish`, `--light-grey`, `--v1-gold`, `--v1-dark-grey`, `--gold-on-white`, `--dark-grey`, `--light-grey-hover`, `--surface-green`, and `--100-red-l`. Component colours, type, radius, padding, and transition were read with `getComputedStyle` on https://www.vsco.co/, https://www.vsco.co/subscribe/plans, and https://vsco.co/user/login.

The home body class was `background-color-black-ish js-logged-out`. The plans body class was `background-color-gray js-logged-out`. The login page is a separate app shell with a longer VSCO Gothic stack. Focus was applied with `element.focus()` on the identity field. An invalid email was written into the field and blurred. No error painted.

A cookie preference center was open on first load. Close was clicked. The center nodes stayed in the document. No account form was submitted. No checkout was opened. Google and Apple login were not clicked.

## What to distrust

Display and title sizes are the computed sizes at 1169px, including fractional pixels. They will differ at another width. The 390px phone width was not measured.

`--dark-grey`, `--gold-on-white`, `--surface-green`, and `--100-red-l` are declared and were not painted on the captured elements. Hover colours are declared in CSS and were not forced on a live control.

The empty login submit is #aaaaaa. That is the empty state, not a brand accent. The pink #ff0877 bar is a promo, not a named custom property.

VSCO Gothic is served from the VSCO Webflow CDN. This package does not include the font files.

## Fonts

VSCO Gothic is the product face. Computed on the marketing pages as `"VSCO Gothic", sans-serif`. Computed on login as `"VSCO Gothic", -apple-system, system-ui, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`, plus an emoji fallback stack that the token shortens. Weights seen: 400, 500, 600, 700. The files are woff2 on cdn.prod.website-files.com. They are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Paint the home band in #111111.** The marketing body class is `background-color-black-ish` and the computed background is rgb(17, 17, 17). [tokens.json](tokens.json)
2. **Keep the nav white.** The marketing nav computed rgb(255, 255, 255) at 88px tall with 40px of side padding. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
3. **Use gold for the free-trial pill.** Try VSCO for free and Copy code computed rgb(255, 188, 60) with black text. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
4. **Use black for the solid pill.** Explore VSCO and Select Pro computed a black fill and white text at 999px radius. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
5. **Don't treat pink as the brand accent.** Learn more in the Studio Pro bar computed rgb(255, 8, 119). It is a promo fill, not a `:root` custom property. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
6. **Don't submit the login form from this package.** The page offers email and password, Google, and Apple. Nothing was submitted. [patterns.md](patterns.md)
7. **Don't invent a painted dark theme.** No theme toggle was used, and no dark set was painted. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
8. **Don't ship VSCO Gothic.** The face is on the VSCO CDN and is not in this package. [style-guide.html](style-guide.html)
