# VSCO design reference

A token set and component reference for VSCO's public marketing site (`www.vsco.co`), captured 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium. One light theme. Desktop 1440×900 at 1x and phone 390×844 at 2x. The signed-in app on `vsco.co` stops at the login and signup walls. Nothing was submitted. Google and Apple were not clicked.

The marketing page is white type on black in the lower band, and black type on white in the header and hero. Buttons are pills. Gold is the trial button. Pink is the top promo. Ink is the default button. The family is VSCO Gothic.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the home page stylesheets (`raw/css-vars.json`): `--black-ish`, `--v1-gold`, `--light-grey`, `--v1-dark-grey`, `--dark-grey`, `--gold-on-white`, `--surface-green`, `--100-red-l`, `--banner-h`. Button fills, type, radii, and padding were computed styles on the live elements (`raw/components-computed.json`), including hover forced with `CSS.forcePseudoState`. Pink is a computed banner-button fill, not a custom property. Screenshots were taken with Playwright at the measured viewports and quantized to 256 colours. Full-colour originals stay in `~/.design-systems/vsco/raw-shots`.

## What to distrust

- The body element background is `#111111` while the header and hero paint white. `light.background.page` is white because that is the page a visitor reads. The inverse band is `light.background.inverse`.
- `--surface-green`, `--gold-on-white`, and `--100-red-l` are declared and were not seen painted on the captured pages.
- Gold hover and pink hover come from a forced pseudo-class, not a pointer resting on the control.
- The ink Try for free button did not change colour under forced hover.
- `https://vsco.co/user/login` showed email and password on the first probe and on the screenshot. A later probe of the same URL showed an email-only Continue step. Both are the public wall. Neither was submitted.
- Nav line-height 22.5px and body line-height 24px are derived as 1.5 of the measured font size. The element boxes include padding.
- VSCO Gothic is proprietary and is not in the style guide. The guide uses Helvetica.
- Article, learn, effects, and state pages in the sitemap share this shell. They were not each screenshotted. The site tree lists the families.

## Fonts

VSCO Gothic is the computed family on the marketing pages and on the login wall. It is proprietary. The tokens name it, then `sans-serif`. The style guide falls back to Helvetica.

## Do and don't

- **Do use pills.** Every captured button has a 999px radius. [components/crops/button-gold-default.png](components/crops/button-gold-default.png)
- **Do spend gold on the trial and pink on the promo.** The header button is ink. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do set display type in VSCO Gothic at a heavy size and a tight line height.** The hero is 100px at weight 500. [components/crops/hero-heading-default.png](components/crops/hero-heading-default.png)
- **Do keep the lower band black and the header white.** [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The site has no theme toggle.
- **Don't treat the sitemap articles as separate components.** They share this shell. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md)

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/vsco
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

Capture again with `PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/public.mjs` and `node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`. Quantize with `python3 tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/vsco/raw-shots`.

This capture does not register the package in catalog.json. Brief, Health, and DESIGN.md need a later catalog commit and `make design-md`.
