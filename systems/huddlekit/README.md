# Huddlekit design reference

A token set and component reference for Huddlekit's signed-out surfaces — for building a design-feedback / website-annotation product, agency-marketing pages and sign-in surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **huddlekit.com** and **app.huddlekit.com** on **2026-11-17/18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the marketing site's main templates — /, /features, /agencies, /customers (+ the /customers/leadfront case study), /pricing, /integrations (+ /integrations/linear), /compare (+ /compare/pastel-alternative), /mcp, /manifesto, /blog (+ /blog/introducing-huddlekit), /support (+ /support/getting-started), /tools (+ /tools/color-contrast-checker), /contact, /privacy — plus **app.huddlekit.com/login**, the only surface the app serves anonymously — in the painted **light** theme the site boots (it has no theme control; the footer's, testimonials' and MCP card's nested `.dark` panels are captured as component-level dark surfaces), with verified **dark** repaints of the login via the app's own `prefers-color-scheme` path; the consent banner, the hover-open Solutions/Resources nav menus, FAQ accordions, the feature tabs, the testimonials 'Show more', the billing switch, the login's 'Continue with email' reveal and the phone's nav drawer as transient states; plus 390px passes on home, pricing, the contrast checker and the login.

huddlekit.com is worth a capture because it declares a complete token layer to the browser: a Tailwind v4 `@theme` plus a shadcn-style semantic layer — `--background`/`--foreground`, `--card(-foreground)`, `--popover(-foreground)`, `--primary(-foreground)` (the **#ea580d** orange), `--secondary`, `--muted(-foreground)`, `--accent(-foreground)`, `--destructive`, `--border`/`--input`/`--ring`, `--upgrade(-foreground|-muted-foreground)` (the #7357ff pro purple), `--sidebar-*`, `--chart-1..5` — plus the marketing site's `--color-page` warm wash (**#f6f3f2**), `--color-primary-dark`, `--scroll-mask-*-from`, the app's `--canvas` and `--mode-glow-strength`, the `--radius` (.75rem) + `--radius-xs..2xl` scale, `--spacing`, `--container-*`, `--section-padding/-gap`, `--card-gap`, the `--text-xs..7xl` ramp with `--leading-*`/`--tracking-*`, the `--font-weight-*` scale, the next/font faces (**Inter** `--font-sans`/`--font-inter`, **Geist Mono**, the ui-monospace `--font-mono`), `--ease-*`/`--animate-*`/`--default-transition-*` motion vars and `--blur-sm/-md`. The architecture: **huddlekit.com paints light only** — its `.dark` rules re-point the same names inside nested dark panels (the footer, the dark testimonials wall, the MCP card), never on `<html>`; **app.huddlekit.com paints `html.dark` + `data-theme="dark"` under prefers-color-scheme** — verified against the app's painted dark login. Both token themes resolve from one declaration set. **Boundaries:** everything past /login (the annotation widget, dashboard, workspaces, billing) 307-redirects an anonymous visitor and was never entered; Google OAuth and the email form's Continue were never submitted; **try./demo./status./docs./help.huddlekit.com** and **app.huddlekit.io** do not resolve — all documented as blocked boundaries, never claimed as captures. Nothing was signed into, typed, searched, sent, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides (the consent banner's Decline, the nav menus, FAQ and tab rows, the billing switch, Show-more, the email reveal, the phone's menu button), and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): every same-origin CSS response from both surfaces plus the inline CSSOM is reparsed, rules are binned theme/skins/app/vendor/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets and the live pages' own generated classes (the next/font classes that scope `--font-inter`/`--font-geist-mono`), once under `<html class="light">` and once under `class="dark"` — the classes the app paints per theme. `tokens.json` never publishes a raw `oklch()`/`oklab()`/`color-mix()` declaration — every colour is converted to the sRGB value the browser paints (CSS Color 4 math in `build_tokens.py`'s `norm_color`).
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link or submit can fire), opened/focused states via the component's own toggle (the email reveal, the menus, the accordions, the switch, the drawer).
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`) verify the painted theme before writing a sidecar: HTML class, `data-theme` attribute and computed `colorScheme` must agree. `-full` captures walk the document for lazy content and bound at eight chunks; mobile pages taller than one GPU texture are shot as part tiles and stitched by `quantize.py`.
- **Dark states** exist only where the product paints them: the marketing site has no theme control (its nested `.dark` panels were measured at rest as component surfaces), so `app-login-dark-*` states come from emulated `prefers-color-scheme: dark` + reload — the app's own OS-preference path — verified against the painted surface.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label, and records where each was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.
- **Nothing was submitted.** The consent banner's Decline was pressed once to dismiss it; 'Accept', 'Continue with Google', the email form's Continue, copy-to-clipboard and every sign-up CTA were measured, never pressed.

## What to distrust

- **Everything past /login**: the app (annotation widget, dashboard, workspaces, billing) redirects an anonymous visitor to /login and was never entered — the signed-in product is a boundary, not a capture. No token or component here claims to describe it.
- **`dark` is the app's painted dark, not the site's.** huddlekit.com has no theme control: the `dark` token set is the `.dark` re-pointed declarations the app paints under a dark OS preference, verified on the login; the marketing site's dark footer/testimonial/MCP panels paint the same re-pointing inside components, not a page theme.
- **Oklch is converted, not copied.** Computed colours come back as `oklch()`/`oklab()` (Tailwind v4); tokens carry the painted sRGB equivalents — a colour that still reads `oklch(` in a raw file was converted for the token it feeds.
- **The fields are resting.** The support search, the contrast-checker inputs and the login email field were focused, never typed into — validation, suggestion and error states are uncaptured.
- **Long-tail templates are covered by one instance each**: one blog article of twelve, one compare page of sixteen, one support article of ~40, one integration page of eight, one case study of two, one tool of ~30; /privacy stands in for /terms and /dpa, /agencies for /freelancers, /product-teams and /qa-teams.
- **Inter and Geist Mono render in the crops** but their woff2 files are licensed and not shipped; the style guide falls back to system fonts.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Reduced motion**: the site only partly honours `prefers-reduced-motion` — /pricing, /features and the login ignore it and the marquee/ping loops keep running; the per-page verdicts are in `raw/motion.json` and `core.motion.$extensions`.
- **The /pricing readings after the billing switch were taken with billing off.** `billing-switch` `off` pressed the switch and never pressed it back, though its `via` says it did, so `plan-cta` and the components after it on /pricing were read in the monthly layout. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now restores the switch after the reading (edited in the 2026-11 audit, not run); a recapture is needed.

## Fonts

- **Inter** — the product face (`--font-sans` `"Inter","Inter Fallback",sans-serif`, `--font-inter`): next/font with a metrics-matched fallback; the woff2 under /_next/static/media is licensed, declared and measured, not shipped. The style guide falls back to the system sans.
- **Geist Mono** — the code face (`--font-geist-mono`, next/font); `--font-mono` is the `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace` system stack.
- **The wordmark serif** ("Huddlekit" in the header and footer) is a brand face/logo mark — the product's artwork, not a tokenised text face.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint light on the marketing surface.** `--background` #ffffff on the `--color-page` warm wash #f6f3f2, `--foreground` ink #111827 ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); the app login is the same tokens on `--canvas` ([login](screenshots/app-login-light-default.png)).
2. **Do spend the orange on actions.** `--primary` #ea580d fills the CTAs and the Google button; `--ring` #ff6900 is the focus ring ([CTA](components/crops/cta-primary-default.png), [focus](components/crops/cta-primary-focus-visible.png)) — surfaces stay warm-neutral.
3. **Do let the nested `.dark` panels carry dark.** The footer, testimonial wall and MCP card re-point the same tokens — #0e0b0d ground, #e3e0df ink, white-alpha borders, the orange kept ([testimonial](components/crops/testimonial-card-dark-default.png), [footer](screenshots/home-light-full.png)).
4. **Do match the app's dark under an OS preference.** `html.dark` + `data-theme="dark"` is the painted path; `--primary` stays #ea580d while `--border` goes `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` ([dark login](screenshots/app-login-dark-default.png)).
5. **Do compose the chrome as bar → header → sections → dark footer.** Announcement bar, sticky header with hover-open menus, warm content sections, the giant wordmark footer ([full page](screenshots/home-light-full.png)).
6. **Do keep the login card square.** `app-login-card` paints a 0px radius while buttons and the pill email field stay rounded ([login card](components/crops/app-login-card-default.png)). The pricing slabs look square in the page shots ([pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-full.png)); `plan-card` itself was not measured (the saved reading was the "Free forever" label, now marked missing).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything past /login is a boundary — widget, dashboard, workspace ([login](screenshots/app-login-light-default.png)).
8. **Don't invent typed, error or result states.** No field was ever typed into and nothing was submitted; focus rings are captured, validation is not ([email field](components/crops/app-email-input-focused.png)).
9. **Don't fake a site dark mode.** The marketing site paints light only — `dark` is the app's `.dark` re-pointing, not a site theme ([dark panel](components/crops/mcp-card-dark-default.png)).
10. **Don't copy the brand, icons or the Inter/Geist files.** The wordmark, the icon set and the font files are Huddlekit's and its licensors' ([wordmark](screenshots/app-login-light-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.

1. Do paint light on the marketing surface.
2. Do spend the orange on actions.
3. Do let the nested `.dark` panels carry dark.
4. Do match the app's dark under an OS preference.
5. Do compose the chrome as bar → header → sections → dark footer.
6. Do keep the login card square.
7. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
8. Don't invent typed, error or result states.
9. Don't fake a site dark mode.
10. Don't copy the brand, icons or the Inter/Geist files.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).
