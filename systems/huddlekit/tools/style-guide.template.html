huddlekit.com, declared and measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. huddlekit.com and app.huddlekit.com share a Tailwind v4 @theme plus a shadcn-style semantic layer: --background/--foreground, --card, --popover, --primary (the #ea580d orange) and its foreground, --secondary, --muted, --accent, --destructive, --border/--input/--ring, --upgrade (the #7357ff pro purple), --sidebar-*, --chart-1..5, the marketing site's --color-page warm wash (#f6f3f2), and the app's --canvas. Radii, spacing, containers, the Inter and Geist Mono faces, and the measured header and control sizes sit in core.

Read from huddlekit.com and app.huddlekit.com/login, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 (mobile pass at 390×844). The marketing site paints light only. Its nested .dark panels (footer, testimonials, the MCP card) re-point the same names. The app paints html.dark under prefers-color-scheme. There is no theme menu on the marketing site. Inter is loaded through next/font and is not bundled here, so samples fall back to system fonts.

Palette

light.* is the painted default (white ground, #111827 ink, #ea580d primary, #f6f3f2 page wash). dark.* is the same names re-pointed for the app's dark OS preference and the nested dark panels (#0e0b0d ground, #e3e0df ink, the orange kept). Swatches in the dark block sit inside .dark so they resolve that set.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

Spacing is the Tailwind --spacing unit (0.25rem). --radius is 0.75rem. component.* holds geometry that was measured on the right element: header height, CTA height and radius, the login card, the consent banner. Plan-card width and mobile-drawer width are absent until a recapture reads those containers. motion.* is the reduced-motion probe: durations, easing, and what prefers-reduced-motion changes.
