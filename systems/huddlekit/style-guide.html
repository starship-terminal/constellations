Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the shadcn-style semantic roles (--background/--foreground, --primary/--secondary/--accent/--muted/--card/--popover + foregrounds, --destructive/--success/--warning families, --border/--input/--ring), the --brand/--link blue (#2962ff), the --sidebar-* and --chart-* sets, the --code-block/--reading-text/--surface/--scrim/--selection roles, the scene palettes (--pro-* gold, --buildful green, --ai-*, the --landing-* and --tod-* hero art), the --elevation-* shadow stacks, the system-stack + Inter faces on the --text-* ramp and the --text-body/-reading/-title-* prose roles, and the measured geometry (component.*) read off the live DOM. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from hashnode.com and townhall.hashnode.com, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 (mobile pass at 390×844). Palette

The semantic roles under light.* are the painted default (white ground, near-black ink, the #2962ff brand/link blue); dark.* resolves the same declarations inside html.dark (#0d0f14 ground, #e6e8eb ink, #6293ff blue) — including the dark-only command name. sidebar.*, chart.*, codeBlock.*, selection.* and scrollbar.* are their own role sets; pro.*, buildful.*, ai.*, landing.* and tod.* are the scene palettes (Pro gold, the green accent, the AI gradient, the landing and time-of-day hero art), all re-pointed per theme.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

Spacing is the Tailwind --spacing unit (0.25rem); radii are --radius .5rem and --radius-xs .125rem (pills clamp to 9999px); container.* and breakpoint.* carry the layout scales and component.* the measured chrome — sidebar width, CTA height, dialog and card sizes, the mobile drawer. motion.* is the measured set from the reduced-motion probe (docs/decisions/0001): the durations and easing curves elements actually run, the transitions and animations, and what prefers-reduced-motion changes.

Type

Two font chains: --font-app (the system sans stack, the UI face) and --font-inter (Inter through next/font, the product face), plus --font-mono for code. The --text-* ramp runs xs–6xl with paired line-heights and the prose roles --text-body/-label/-reading/-small/-subtitle/-title-2..4 carry size, weight, tracking and leading; weights are the named --font-weight-* steps (300–700). Below that, the core.typography.* composites — the article title, headings, body, code and control styles measured off the live DOM. Samples fall back to system sans because Inter is not bundled.
