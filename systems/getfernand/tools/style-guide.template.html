Palette

The declared palette runs the white/grey-light/grey/grey-medium/grey-strong/black grey ramp with the blue #00a0ff action accent and orange/red/green status family; the app re-resolves the grey family a step lighter, and the docs dark mode remaps the whole ramp. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius and motion

Radii climb 6px (header controls) → 8px (buttons, fields) → 10px (cards) → 16px (pricing panels) → 40px (eyebrow pills) with a full-radius launcher bubble. Components are padded in eights (24px card padding); the control transition is measured on the live buttons.

Type

Self-hosted Inter carries everything: display headings and body at 400, buttons, nav links and eyebrow pills at 500, plan eyebrows and card titles at 600 — the heaviest weight the public surfaces set. The marketing sizes are measured (the sheets declare no type vars); the app's declared --viewport-height shows under its surface. Samples fall back to system sans.
