# Patterns

Public site only. The signed-in inbox was not captured.

**Shell.** A white header with the wordmark, About us, Pricing, Log in, Book a demo, and Start for free. The same header is on every marketing page, including the 404. On a 390px phone the header stays a row. There is no hamburger. The phone header shows the wordmark, About us, Pricing, Log in, and Start for free. Book a demo is visible in the hero at that width, next to a dark Start for free control.

**Footer.** Product, Integrations, Alternatives, and Legal columns, then a contact row. The map is in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

**Auth wall.** Log in leaves the marketing host for https://app.getfernand.com/auth/login. Start for free leaves for https://app.getfernand.com/create. Both were opened and not submitted. https://getfernand.com/login is a 404.

**Demo.** Book a demo opens a Cal.com modal on the same page. Watch a 2 minute demo opens a YouTube dialog. Neither was submitted.

**Install with AI.** A menu of three outbound agent links (Cursor, Codex, Claude Code). Not followed.

**Search.** No search field on the public marketing pages.
