# Patterns

## App shell

A navy universal bar names the Gap Inc. brands and holds account and bag. A blue promo strip sits under it on category pages. The white department bar carries the wordmark, search, Ask Navy, and eleven departments. See [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Category

Women opens a public grid. A circle row of subcategories sits under the title. Filter chips sit above the products. See [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Product

A public product opens from a category tile. Photos sit left. Title, price, color, fit, and size sit right. Add to Bag is the navy control. It was not clicked. See [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## About

Our Values is a public page: a photo and three tabs, Opportunity, Inclusivity, and Sustainability. See [screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).
