Glassnode, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the shared GDS palette primitives (palette.* — the grey ramp, ultramarine #4559dc, the green accent #16cc8c, status ramps and the registry's chart colours), the marketing site's --light_*/--button-* roles (light.theme.*), the studio shell's painted light --gds-* roles (light.studio.*), the Ghost research publication's painted --rf-* roles in both themes (light/dark.research.*), the tokenization registry's --color-* roles (dark.tokenization.* painted by default, light.tokenization.* via its theme button), and the measured roles under light.measured.* plus the geometry under core.component.*. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from glassnode.com and its public subdomains, signed out, in headed Chromium under Xvfb. The signed-in Studio workbench and every post-login surface is a documented boundary; the newsletter, contact and Auth0 username fields were typed into and cleared, never submitted; the Auth0 landing URL's volatile ?state= is never recorded. The studio's html[data-theme="dark-theme"] layer is declared but has no public theme control — every dark.studio.* token is labelled declared-not-painted. Inter, fraktion, JetBrains Mono and Roboto are served faces and are not loaded here: samples fall back to system fonts.

Palette

core.palette.* holds the declared primitives the surfaces share: the grey ramp, ultramarine (the brand blue), green/red/yellow/rose status ramps, the darken alpha steps, the lone indigo, the marketing accent and second grey ramps, the Ghost palette steps and the registry's chart series. The semantic roles under light.theme.* (marketing), light/dark.studio.*, light/dark.research.* and light/dark.tokenization.* resolve to them; measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

The space ramp is 4px-based (--space-* on marketing, --gds-spacing-* on the shell, --spacing 4px on the registry). Radii run 0-24px plus full. The marketing grid caps at --grid-width; the shell sizes its pages through --gds-page-width-*, --gds-navbar-height and --gds-sidebar-width. Motion is the marketing --transition_* shorthands and the registry's duration/easing pair.

Type

Five faces across the surfaces: fraktion is the marketing display face over an Inter body; Inter/Inter Variable carries the studio shell, the research publication and the registry; JetBrains Mono is the registry mono; Roboto and IBM Plex Mono are the GitBook docs fonts. The GDS --gds-font-size-* ramp below is the shell's declared scale; the measured marketing sizes are the font.size.measured.* entries. Samples fall back to system sans.
