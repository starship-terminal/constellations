# Patterns

How the public Glassnode surfaces combine. Signed out, 2026-11-21. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.

## Marketing header

White bar. Wordmark left. Products dropdown, Pricing (external), Data, About, and a near-black Launch Studio button right. Phone width collapses this into a black drawer labelled Menu.

Shown in [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Marketing hero

Grey page (`#f5f5f5` layout wash on Studio; marketing body is a light grey section). Fraktion display headline, Inter body, a lavender Contact us link, and a chart illustration. The chart is a public demo, not the signed-in Studio workspace.

Shown in [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Studio shell

`glassnode.studio` wordmark, search, a flat nav (Home, Dashboards, Charts, Market Compass, Alpha Lab, Research, Tokenization, Resources), a changelog icon, Log in, and Sign up. Below that, a card grid and an asset table. Public market numbers are visible without an account. Charts, dashboards, and Market Compass also load signed out.

Shown in [studio-home-light-default.png](screenshots/studio-home-light-default.png).

## Search

The search icon opens a field with suggestion chips and a keyboard hint row. Escape closes it. Nothing was submitted.

Shown in [studio-home-light-search.png](screenshots/studio-home-light-search.png).

## Pricing

Two plan cards, Advanced and Professional, with a feature table under them. Sign up and Configure sit on the cards. Configure was not followed into a purchase.

Shown in [studio-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/studio-pricing-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

Log in and Sign up leave Studio for `auth.glassnode.com`. Empty email and password, Google, and a reCAPTCHA. The branch stops there. See [login-review.json](login-review.json).

Shown in [login-light-empty.png](screenshots/login-light-empty.png) and [signup-light-empty.png](screenshots/signup-light-empty.png).
