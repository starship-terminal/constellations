# Glassnode design reference

A token set and component reference for Glassnode's public surfaces — for building crypto-analytics marketing pages, pricing grids, docs-site chrome, a research publication and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **glassnode.com** and its public subdomains on **2026-11-16**, signed out, in headed Chromium under Xvfb at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the marketing home page, /products/studio, /products/data, /products/research, /products/studio/market-compass, /products/studio/alpha-lab, /pricing/vector, /about, /contact, /customer-stories and both /solutions pages, the header's Products dropdown, the studio.glassnode.com shell (/pricing, /charts, /dashboards, /market-compass, /get-listed, /contact), the Auth0 universal login at auth.glassnode.com/u/login reached through the studio's real "Log in" button, the research.glassnode.com Ghost publication (index plus the current first article, including its own dark theme toggle), docs.glassnode.com (the GitBook landing and the metric catalog, including its theme buttons), the dark-by-default tokenization.glassnode.com registry (plus its painted light theme), and 390px passes on home, studio pricing, research, docs and the registry.

Glassnode is worth a capture because it is really five stacks that share one design system: the marketing Next.js + SCSS-modules site declares `--base_grey_*`/`--base_ultramarine_*` palette ramps plus `--light_*` semantic roles, `--button-*`, `--space-*`, `--shadow-*`, `--transition_*` and `--z_index_*`; the studio shell declares the real GDS layer — `--gds-color-*` palette + semantic roles, `--gds-font-*`, `--gds-spacing-*`, `--gds-border-radius-*`, `--gds-shadow-*`, `--gds-zindex-*`, `--gds-opacity-*` and the page/sidebar widths — on `:root`, and a full dark redeclaration plus Ant Design `--ant-*` vars under `html[data-theme="dark-theme"]` that **no public control reaches** (captured as declared-not-painted tokens, never as a screenshot); the Ghost research site paints `--rf-*` roles in both themes via its own toggle; the tokenization registry paints `--color-*` roles dark by default and light via its button; and the GitBook docs carry the platform's own token layer, kept out of the GDS tree as `component.docs.*`. Four self-hosted voices: **fraktion** display and **Inter** on marketing, **Inter/Inter Variable** across the shell, research and registry, **JetBrains Mono** on the registry, **Roboto/IBM Plex Mono** on the docs. **Boundary:** the signed-in Studio workbench — charts editor, saved dashboards, alerts, API keys, billing — was never reached; the newsletter, contact and Auth0 username fields were typed into then cleared, never submitted; every link and CTA was measured, never followed; the Auth0 landing URL's volatile `?state=` is never recorded.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do let the declared palettes carry the colour.
2. Do spend ultramarine on the action.
3. Do keep surfaces white behind hairlines.
4. Do pair the voices on purpose.
5. Don't style the signed-in Studio from this package.
6. Don't treat `dark.studio.*` as a painted theme.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand or partner artwork.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What was captured

Marketing pages paint the light `:root` layer only — dark in-page sections, no global theme control. The Products dropdown is a local UI toggle (hover opens it). The Vector pricing page's billing select and FAQ accordions are local controls; nothing was bought or downloaded. The studio shell's signed-out nav is Home, Dashboards, Charts, Market Compass, Alpha Lab, Research, Tokenization, Resources, "Log in" and "Sign up"; "Log in" navigates to the Auth0 card. Research, docs and the registry each carry a real theme control — the switched surfaces are the `theme-*` screenshots and the corresponding token roles. The registry is dark by default.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- `dark.studio.*` is a stylesheet declaration (`html[data-theme="dark-theme"]`, plus the `--ant-*` layer), not a painted surface — no public control reaches it signed out. Never present it as a verified dark theme.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs — read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw files, never the pixels.
- `component.docs.*` values belong to GitBook's stack, not Glassnode's GDS — keep them namespaced.
- The `res-article-tag` entry measured a zero-size element (the article carries no visible tag link row at capture); `docs-code` was not found on the metric catalog page.
- The footer `newsletter-btn` fill is the footer's muted grey (#dedfe1 on the dark band), not the accent — the accent CTA lives on the subscribe band elsewhere.
- The Auth0 card's `auth-card` background read transparent at the wrapper — the white fill sits on an inner layer (see "Child layers"/paint rows in raw/measurements.md).

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared palettes carry the colour.** Five surfaces share the GDS primitives: the grey ramp, ultramarine, the status ramps and the registry's `--c1`–`--c7` chart colours — use `core.palette.*` and the `light.*`/`dark.*` roles rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do spend ultramarine on the action.** `#4559dc` (`palette.ultramarine.50`, `light.studio.background.accent.default`) belongs to studio links, active marks and the Vector "Download sample" control — primary CTAs on marketing stay ink-dark `#1a1a1a` pills (`measured.ctaFill`), and `#16cc8c` (`palette.accent`) marks subscribe and positive states. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and [vec-download-btn](components/crops/vec-download-btn-default.png).
3. **Do keep surfaces white behind hairlines.** The page field is `#ffffff` (`light.theme.bg.default`), tinted sections `#f7f8fa` (`theme.bg.tinted`) and cards carry `#dedfe1` hairlines at 2px radii — the dark `#1a1a1a` is spent on in-page sections, the footer and CTA pills, never the field. See [contact-category](components/crops/contact-category-default.png) and [footer](components/crops/footer-default.png).
4. **Do pair the voices on purpose.** fraktion is the marketing display face over an Inter body; Inter/Inter Variable carries the shell, research and registry; JetBrains Mono is the registry mono; Roboto and IBM Plex Mono belong to the GitBook docs. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in Studio from this package.** The chart editor, saved dashboards, alerts, API keys and billing were never reached — the signed-out navbar, pricing gate, public chart views and the Auth0 card are the only product-shell evidence. See [studio-pricing-light-default](screenshots/studio-pricing-light-default.png) and [auth-login-light-default](screenshots/auth-login-light-default.png).
6. **Don't treat `dark.studio.*` as a painted theme.** The `html[data-theme="dark-theme"]` `--gds-*/--ant-*` layer is declared-not-painted — no public control reaches it. The painted darks are the Ghost research toggle ([research-home-light-theme-dark](screenshots/research-home-light-theme-dark.png)) and the registry's default ([tokenization-dark-default](screenshots/tokenization-dark-default.png)).
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** The newsletter, contact and Auth0 username fields were typed into then cleared — validation, error and success states are unverified. See [auth-email](components/crops/auth-email-filled.png).
8. **Don't copy the brand or partner artwork.** The glassnode wordmark, product glyphs, theme-toggle icons, partner logos and report cover art in the captures are Glassnode's published content; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).
