# Glassnode design reference

A token set and component reference for Glassnode's public surfaces: the marketing site at glassnode.com and the signed-out Studio shell at studio.glassnode.com. Captured 2026-11-21 in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. One light theme. The auth wall at auth.glassnode.com was photographed empty and not entered.

Glassnode sells on-chain and market data. The public site is a light marketing page with a Fraktion display headline and a near-black Launch Studio button. Studio, signed out, is an Ant Design shell: near-black primary (`#1a1a1a`), white cards, a public market table, and search. Signed-in workspaces were not reached.

## How the values were read

Studio colour roles, radii, type sizes, motion, and control heights come from Ant Design custom properties resolved on the theme holder at studio.glassnode.com (`--ant-color-primary` and the rest in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json)). They are not on `:root`. Marketing display type (Fraktion, 51.2px, `#333333`) is a computed style on the home `h1`. Component states were read with `getComputedStyle` in [tools/capture/measure.mjs](tools/capture/measure.mjs). Hover is a real pointer. Pressed is `CSS.forcePseudoState`.

The Usercentrics banner was denied. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked.

## What to distrust

- One theme. No dark switch was found. Do not invent a dark set.
- Ant status colours (success, warning, error, info) are declared tokens. The public pages also paint gain and loss colours in chart artwork. Those chart colours were not tokenised.
- Pressed crops are forced pseudo-states, not clicks.
- Search-metrics and search-assets readings hit the placeholder text node, not the field chrome.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- Auth0 `state` query strings were stripped from recorded URLs.
- The login wall shows a reCAPTCHA. It was not solved, so error and disabled states of that form were not captured.
- `https://studio.glassnode.com/login` is a 404. The real login is the Log in button, which opens auth.glassnode.com.

## Fonts

**Inter** and **Inter Variable** are the UI faces (marketing buttons, Studio shell). **Fraktion** (file `FraktionSans_Variable`) is the marketing display face. **ulp-font** is the Auth0 wall face. None is distributed. The style guide falls back to the system sans. Studio code face is the Ant stack `SFMono-Regular, Consolas, Liberation Mono, Menlo, Courier, monospace`.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep primary actions near-black.** Launch Studio and Sign up are `#1a1a1a` on white. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do keep controls square.** Ant radius is 2px. [studio-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/studio-pricing-light-default.png).
- **Do use Fraktion for the marketing headline and Inter for UI.** [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do leave the public Studio table readable without an account.** [studio-home-light-default.png](screenshots/studio-home-light-default.png).
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** None was switched on. [studio-home-light-default.png](screenshots/studio-home-light-default.png).
- **Don't treat the auth wall as the app.** It is an empty form with Google and a reCAPTCHA. [login-light-empty.png](screenshots/login-light-empty.png).
