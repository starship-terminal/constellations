# Handshake design reference

A token set and component reference for Handshake's signed-out web — for building marketing pages, pricing tables, lead forms and auth-gate cards in the style of the real thing. Values were read from **joinhandshake.com** and **app.joinhandshake.com** on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. On **2026-11-21** the public homepage was mapped into [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) before a refresh of the public pages that tree names (home, students, employers, career centers, AI skills, Handshake AI, about), plus the employers menu, FAQ, segment tab, privacy dialog, and the phone nav sheet. That pass stopped at `app.joinhandshake.com/access`. Nothing was typed or submitted. See [login-review.json](login-review.json). Captured: the marketing routes /, /students/ and /students/whos-hiring/, /employers/, /employers/pricing/ and /employers/request-more-info/, /career-centers/, /career-centers/contact/ and /career-centers/marketing-toolkit/, /careers/, /about/, /ai/, /ai/enterprise/ and /ai/opportunities/, /research/ai/ (the one Framer-built page), /blog/ and an article, /learn/, /legal/tos/, /privacy-policy/, /newsroom/, /accessibility/ and /security/; the nav mega menu, an employers segment tab, FAQ and the careers departments filter and the 390px "Open menu" nav sheet as transient states; mobile passes on home, students, employers, pricing and blog; and the consumer app's "Log in or sign up" card at app.joinhandshake.com/access on the loads where its Cloudflare challenge cleared, desktop and phone.

joinhandshake.com declares a real variable layer to the browser: the Tailwind theme's `--font-noi-grotesk` / `--font-sans-plomb` (the proprietary NoiGrotesk and SansPlomb faces), `--spacing`, `--container-3xs..7xl`, `--leading-*`, `--tracking-*`, `--font-weight-*`, `--ease-*`, `--default-transition-*`, `--animate-*`, `--blur-*`, `--drop-shadow-*` and `--breakpoint-lg`, plus the site's own geometry variables — `--desktop-nav-height` 3.5rem and its `--desktop-nav-top-offset`, `--mobile-nav-height`, `--notification-banner-height`, `--full-*-nav-height`, `--container-padding` and `--content-max-width` — and the arbitrary-property utility declarations `--cta:#2e6aff` / `--cta-hover:#1552d6` / `--cta-active|visited:#6fbb28` and `--bg:#14151c` / `--text:#14151c` (the ink). The app surfaces a different, larger layer: the Rosetta design system's `--rosetta-bg-*`/`--rosetta-fg-*`/`--rosetta-interactive-*`/`--rosetta-shadow-*`/`--rosetta-size-*`/`--rosetta-spacing-*`/`--rosetta-type-*`/`--rosetta-font-family-*` plus `--nav-*`, `--popover-*`, `--modal-*`, `--listbox-*`, `--select-*`, `--tag-*`, `--avatar-*`, `--slider-*`, `--tooltip-*`, `--day-picker-*`, `--segmented-toggle-*` and friends, served from handshake-production-cdn.joinhandshake.com. Both surfaces paint **light only** — no theme class, no theme control, no prefers-color-scheme rule — so there is one `light` token set for the marketing site and one `access` set for the app, and no dark pass exists because no dark surface exists. **Boundary:** the consumer app's signed-in surfaces — /explore, /signup, the student registration flow — never cleared the Cloudflare managed challenge across fresh-context retries, and support.joinhandshake.com answered Cloudflare 403 from the capture host. Nothing was signed into, typed, searched, sent, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides, and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): every same-origin marketing CSS response plus the app CDN sheets (when the /access challenge cleared) and the inline CSSOM are reparsed, rules are binned theme/app-theme/app/vendor/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets. `tokens.json` never publishes a raw channel triplet — every colour is the painted value. Utility declarations like `--cta:#2e6aff` resolve only on their own utility classes, so they are emitted from the rule that declares them, with that selector as provenance.
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link can fire), opened/selected states via the component's own toggle. A lookup that misses on a rerun never erases a state a previous run measured.
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`) verify the page actually painted (title, url, a real element) before writing a sidecar; `-full` captures lazy-scroll the document, then tile at CSS scale, bounded at eight chunks with the boundary logged. The marketing pages scroll the document — no inner panes.
- **The Ketch consent banner** is captured where the page mounts it (a page's own first-load UI), then hidden with CSS for every subsequent measurement — never accepted, rejected or answered.
- **Layout** (`tools/capture/layout.mjs`) probes fixed/sticky furniture, the 12-column grid, content max-widths and section bands at 1440, 1920, 1024 and 390.
- **Motion** (`tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs`) samples transitions, keyframes and the prefers-reduced-motion behaviour on seven marketing routes.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label and records where it was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.
- **The app surface** is reached through fresh contexts with bounded retries; a load that stays on the challenge is logged and retried, never worked around.

## What to distrust

- **app.joinhandshake.com/explore, /signup, registration and support.joinhandshake.com**: blocked at the edge — the managed challenge never cleared across fresh-context retries and support returned 403. Nothing signed-in was captured; do not present this package's tokens as the consumer app's authenticated design system — the `access` set is the signed-out Rosetta layer as served.
- **mkto-select** is the only specced component never found: Marketo renders picklists as scripted dropdowns, not native `<select>` elements, so the lead forms' select furniture is a documented absence.
- **The nav's scrolled state**: the pill is measured at rest over the hero (transparent, white items); the solid white state it paints under scroll is visible in the page screenshots but was not separately state-measured.
- **Search results**: the hero, blog and careers fields were measured at rest; no query was ever typed, so result states are uncaptured.
- **The Google SSO button** is Google's own rendered widget (GSI) — vendor furniture, recorded as such; its internals are not Handshake design.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Reduced motion**: the probe saw prefers-reduced-motion ignored on all seven probed routes — see `raw/motion.json`.
- **The faces** — NoiGrotesk, SansPlomb, Chicago FLF/Geneva (marketing-toolkit) and the Rosetta stack — are self-hosted and not shipped here; crops show them, the style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do float the nav.** The pill sits 24px (`--desktop-nav-top-offset`) below the viewport top over every page, transparent with white items over the hero and switching to the banner offset when the notification band shows ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [nav](components/crops/hs-nav-default.png)).
2. **Do spend the lime on the one action.** `light.button.primary.bg` #d3fb52 fills only the sign-up pill (hover #a1dd3b); CTAs elsewhere are ink pills, ghosts or the blue `--cta` #2e6aff utilities ([Sign up](components/crops/hs-signup-button-default.png), [CTA](components/crops/hs-cta-default.png)).
3. **Do pair the two voices.** NoiGrotesk carries the brand moments and SansPlomb the body; the uppercase SansPlomb hero ("LET'S FIND YOUR NEXT JOB" — 160px at desktop, 86px on the phone) sits over the 920px rounded-3xl search field ([hero](components/crops/hs-hero-h1-default.png), [form](components/crops/hs-search-form-default.png)).
4. **Do band the page by fill.** Long indexes alternate `bg-neutral-0` white and `bg-neutral-10` #f3f4fa sections — grouping by fill, not by rules ([blog](screenshots/blog-light-full.png)).
5. **Do let opacity carry selection.** The segment switcher's unselected tabs sit at muted opacity; the selected tab and the carousel dots are the same control at full strength ([tab](components/crops/hs-segment-tab-selected.png)).
6. **Don't style the signed-in app from this package.** /explore, /signup, registration and support.joinhandshake.com are blocked boundaries; `access` describes the sign-in card's Rosetta layer only ([access](screenshots/access-light-default.png)).
7. **Don't answer the consent banner.** The Ketch dialog is part of the first-load UI and is captured where it mounts; it was never accepted or rejected, and its preference centre is uncaptured.
8. **Don't invent a dark theme.** Both surfaces are light-only — no class, control or media query exists; there is no dark token set to copy.
9. **Don't ship the fonts or the brand.** The NoiGrotesk/SansPlomb files, the wordmark and the icon set are Handshake's; the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.
