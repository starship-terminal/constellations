# Krisp design reference

A token set and component reference for Krisp's public marketing site. It was read from **krisp.ai** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. Captured: the home page, pricing, AI meeting assistant, noise cancellation, customers, about, security, blog, careers, the 404, and a 390px pass on home and pricing.

Krisp paints a white field, ink `#131032`, and one indigo action `#614efa` (hover `#4a3bbe`). The header is 73px: wordmark, product menus, Sign in, a near-black Book a demo pill, and an indigo Get Krisp pill, both at 8px radius. Display type is Plus Jakarta Sans at 52/72 600 on the home hero and 40/700 on pricing. Plan cards are white, 16px radius, 1px `#e7e7ea` hairline; the highlighted plan takes a 2px indigo border and a Most popular badge. The billing control is a `#f4f4f5` pill. **Boundary:** the product lives at app.krisp.ai. Sign in, sign-up, Get Krisp, Try it Free and Start with a plan were measured or seen as links and never followed. Book a demo opens /contact-sales/, a form that was not submitted. OneTrust was hidden, never answered.

### Principles

1. Do spend the indigo on the one primary action.
2. Do keep Book a demo in ink.
3. Do set display in Plus Jakarta Sans.
4. Do mark the popular plan with a 2px indigo border.
5. Do use the pill for the billing toggle.
6. Don't style the app from this package.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't follow Get Krisp or Book a demo as if they were measured screens.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Playwright opened krisp.ai on 2026-11-18. Colours, type and radii were read from declared CSS rules in the krisp-v4 theme and from `getComputedStyle` on the painted controls (the indigo lives on `.button.btn--primary a`, not on the wrapper div). Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs; read values from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from the images. OneTrust was hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked, no form was submitted, and no sign-in link was followed.

Theme version string on the stylesheets that day was `17.18` to `17.19`. The font files are self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans under `/wp-content/themes/krisp-v4/fonts/Jakarta/`.

## What to distrust

The site does not publish a custom-property token sheet. Ramps in `core.color` are the colours that actually paint, not a 10-step scale the product named. `light.border.focus` is the accent recorded because no designed focus ring was declared on the public buttons. `light.text.link` is the indigo the 404 link paints; header links stay ink. `light.text.disabled` reuses the muted grey because no disabled control was painted. Green and red status colours are single stylesheet hues, not fill/text/border triples, and they were not the main paint of the pages shot. The contact-sales form was opened only far enough to see that it is a form; its fields are a boundary, not a captured pattern. Marketing heroes include a product demo illustration; that illustration is not the signed-in app. The icon set is three cost-chip SVGs from the noise-cancellation page.

## Fonts

Plus Jakarta Sans, weights 400, 500, 600, 700 and 800, self-hosted by krisp.ai as woff files. The tokens fall back to the system sans. The files are not shipped in this package.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the indigo on the one primary action.** `#614efa` is Get Krisp and the highlighted plan border. See [get-krisp-default](components/crops/get-krisp-default.png).
2. **Do keep Book a demo in ink.** The near-black pill is the secondary action, white text, same 8px radius. See [book-demo-default](components/crops/book-demo-default.png).
3. **Do set display in Plus Jakarta Sans.** The hero is 52px at weight 600, line-height 72. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do mark the popular plan with a 2px indigo border.** The other cards stay on a 1px hairline. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
5. **Do use the pill for the billing toggle.** Monthly and Annually sit in a `#f4f4f5` capsule. See [pricing-light-monthly](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png).
6. **Don't style the app from this package.** app.krisp.ai was never opened.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** Dark pills and the indigo are actions inside the one light theme.
8. **Don't follow Get Krisp or Book a demo as if they were measured screens.** They leave the public site or open a form.
