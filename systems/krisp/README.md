# Krisp design reference

A token set and component reference for Krisp's public marketing site and the signed-in web app. Marketing was read from **krisp.ai** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844.

A 2026-11-22 pass mapped the public website into [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and photographed the marketing pages the header and footer open, plus the empty login and signup walls. No account was used. Nothing was typed. Google, Microsoft, Apple, and SSO were not clicked. `login-extracted` stays as it was. catalog.json was left alone. The app pass used a free account at **app.krisp.ai** the same day (passwordless email OTP), at 1440×900 and 390×844, light and dark (`data-theme=auto` follows `prefers-color-scheme`). Captured: the marketing home, pricing, AI meeting assistant, noise cancellation, customers, about, security, blog, careers, the 404; and in the app, setup/download, My Notes (empty), Action Items (Core paywall), account settings, AI Note Taker settings, billing on the Free plan, connect-calendar (not connected), and the settings shell.

Krisp paints a white field, ink `#131032`, and one indigo action `#614efa` (hover `#4a3bbe`). The marketing header is 73px: wordmark, product menus, Sign in, a near-black Book a demo pill, and an indigo Get Krisp pill, both at 8px radius. Display type is Plus Jakarta Sans. The signed-in app uses Inter, named canvas/surface tokens, and a dark set. **Boundary:** `/meetings` and recordings redirect to `/setup` (desktop app download) for this free account. Upgrade, Download, Connect Google/Outlook, Delete account, Enable 2FA and OneTrust were not pressed. Book a demo still opens /contact-sales/, not submitted.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export krisp` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do spend the indigo on the one primary action.
2. Do keep Book a demo in ink.
3. Do set display in Plus Jakarta Sans.
4. Do mark the popular plan with a 2px indigo border.
5. Do use the pill for the billing toggle.
6. Don't treat marketing Plus Jakarta Sans as the app face.
7. Don't invent recordings.
8. Don't follow Get Krisp, Book a demo, Upgrade or Download as if they were measured screens.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Playwright opened krisp.ai on 2026-11-18. Colours, type and radii were read from declared CSS rules in the krisp-v4 theme and from `getComputedStyle` on the painted controls (the indigo lives on `.button.btn--primary a`, not on the wrapper div). The signed-in pass injected a stored session into Chromium, opened app.krisp.ai, and read named custom properties (`--canvas-lighter-1`, `--background-primary-dark-1`, status triples) from the computed style. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs; read values from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from the images. OneTrust was hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked. Sign-up used email OTP only. Download, Upgrade, Connect calendar, Delete account and Enable 2FA were not pressed.

Theme version string on the marketing stylesheets that day was `17.18` to `17.19`. The font files are self-hosted Plus Jakarta Sans under `/wp-content/themes/krisp-v4/fonts/Jakarta/`. The app loads Inter from Google Fonts.

## What to distrust

The marketing site does not publish a custom-property token sheet. Ramps in `core.color` are the colours that actually paint, not a 10-step scale the product named. `light.border.focus` is the accent recorded because no designed focus ring was declared on the public buttons. `light.text.link` is the indigo the 404 link paints; header links stay ink. The app does publish named custom properties; `dark` is that set under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. App light values live in [raw/app-css-vars.json](raw/app-css-vars.json) and were not folded into marketing `light`. The contact-sales form was opened only far enough to see that it is a form; its fields are a boundary, not a captured pattern. Marketing heroes include a product demo illustration; the signed-in notes shell is [app-notes-light-default](screenshots/app-notes-light-default.png). The icon set is three cost-chip SVGs from the noise-cancellation page. Recordings were not captured: `/meetings` redirects to `/setup`.

Every marketing value in `tools/build_tokens.py` is typed in by hand from the stylesheet harvest and the computed reads. The harvest (`raw/foundation.json`) was never committed, so seven values that no committed reading holds are labelled Reconstruction: `core.color.neutral.300` `#d8d8d8`, `core.color.coral.600` `#f75246`, `core.color.green.500` `#68b631`, `core.fontWeight.extrabold` 800, `light.text.muted` and `light.text.disabled` `#757585`, and `light.action.inverse-hover` `#23232e`. The other marketing literals match a reading in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) but are still typed in, so a recapture does not update them.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the indigo on the one primary action.** `#614efa` is Get Krisp and the highlighted plan border. See [get-krisp-default](components/crops/get-krisp-default.png).
2. **Do keep Book a demo in ink.** The near-black pill is the secondary action, white text, same 8px radius. See [book-demo-default](components/crops/book-demo-default.png).
3. **Do set display in Plus Jakarta Sans.** The hero is 52px at weight 600, line-height 72. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do mark the popular plan with a 2px indigo border.** The other cards stay on a 1px hairline. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
5. **Do use the pill for the billing toggle.** Monthly and Annually sit in a `#f4f4f5` capsule. See [pricing-light-monthly](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png).
6. **Don't treat marketing Plus Jakarta Sans as the app face.** The signed-in app is Inter. See [app-notes-light-default](screenshots/app-notes-light-default.png).
7. **Don't invent recordings.** `/meetings` redirects to the desktop download gate on a free account. See [app-setup-light-default](screenshots/app-setup-light-default.png).
8. **Don't follow Get Krisp, Book a demo, Upgrade or Download as if they were measured screens.** Upgrade sits on notes and billing; it was not pressed. See [app-billing-light-default](screenshots/app-billing-light-default.png).
