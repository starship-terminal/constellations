# Jasper patterns

How the public pages combine the measured pieces. Captured 2026-11-18, signed out.

## Marketing shell

The header is a white bar with the red wordmark, five text items (Platform, Solutions, Resources, Company, Pricing), Log In, Free Trial, and a navy Get a Demo button. Hovering Platform, Solutions, Resources, or Company keeps the URL on `/` and opens a panel. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). The footer is a dark island, background near `#262627`, with the wordmark in flame and quiet legal links.

See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-menu-platform.png](screenshots/home-light-menu-platform.png), [footer-default.png](components/crops/footer-default.png).

## Hero

A light-blue band holds a webinar chip, a Feature display headline, and two actions: a navy Get a Demo (`/demo`) and an outlined Start Free Trial (`https://app.jasper.ai/auth/signup`). The trial is a signup wall. It was not followed.

See [hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [hero-cta-default.png](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).

## Pricing chart

`/pricing` is two plan cards under a Monthly / Yearly toggle. Yearly is selected in [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png): Pro is $59 per month per seat, Business is custom. Start Free 7-Day Trial opens the signup wall and was not followed. Contact Sales was not submitted. There is no FAQ accordion.

See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [price-card-default.png](components/crops/price-card-default.png).

## Demo form boundary

`/demo` mounts a HubSpot form (email, first name, last name, company) beside marketing copy. The field and the submit button were measured at rest. The form was not submitted.

See [demo-light-default.png](screenshots/demo-light-default.png), [demo-field-default.png](components/crops/demo-field-default.png).

## Login boundary

`https://app.jasper.ai/` redirects to `https://app.jasper.ai/auth/login`. The card is white, with the red wordmark, Continue with Google, an empty email field, and a blue Continue with email. That blue is product auth chrome, not the marketing flame or navy. There is no password field. On 2026-11-21 Continue with email was not pressed and Google was not clicked. The empty wall is [login-light-empty.png](screenshots/login-light-empty.png). The record is [login-review.json](login-review.json). Canvas, agents, Brand IQ and campaigns were not reached.
