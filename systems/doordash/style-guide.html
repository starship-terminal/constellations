DoorDash, measured

doordash.com's consumer web (consumer-web-next) declares the prism token system on :where(.prism-theme): --base-* primitives — the DoorDash red ramp, the caviar/dashpass brand palettes, neutral and warm/cool greys, twelve hue ramps, a 32-step size scale and a 28-step DD Norms type scale — --usage-* semantic roles over them (stateful colour, type, space, size, radius, elevation and motion), and a --comp-* component layer over the roles. The same sheet ships sibling brand themes (deliveroo, plus — each light and dark) under [data-dt-theme][data-dt-mode] scopes; a doordash.com visitor gets only the unscoped doordash light set tokenised here. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The --base-color-* ramps: the DoorDash brand ramp feeding action-primary, the caviar and dashpass palettes, neutral/warm/cool greys and twelve hue ramps. The light roles are the --usage-color-* semantic layer — stateful (default/hovered/pressed/disabled) and aliased to the ramps.

Spacing, radius and motion

The --usage-space-* roles (none → xxx-large) alias the 32-step --base-size-* scale; radii are roles (none → full — DoorDash pills are radius-full); motion is the --usage-motion-* ladder of auto/functional durations and easings.
