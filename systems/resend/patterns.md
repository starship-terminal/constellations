# Resend patterns

Public site only. The signed-in dashboard was not entered.

## App shell and navigation

Desktop header is sticky, 58px, transparent over a black page. Logo, six hover menus, Pricing, Log in, Get started. Phone replaces the menus with one "Open main menu" button that opens a sheet. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png), [mobile-home-dark-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-menu.png).

## Marketing page

Black page, domaine display headline at 96px, one white pill for the primary action, quiet text links beside it. A product render sits to the right of the headline. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

## Pricing

Two tabs, Transactional emails and Marketing emails, then plan cards. Get started on a plan goes to signup and was not followed. [pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png), [pricing-dark-marketing-tab.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-marketing-tab.png).

## Docs

Light by default, with system, light, and dark switches. Left sidebar, article, right "On this page" rail. Search opens a dialog. [docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png), [docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/docs-dark-default.png), [docs-light-search.png](screenshots/docs-light-search.png).

## Login wall

Observed and not used. Email field, Google, GitHub. Nothing was typed. Google and GitHub were not clicked. The placeholder in the field was masked. [login-dark-default.png](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).

## Not captured

Empty, loading, and error states of the app. Destructive confirmation. Feedback after an action. Search inside the product. Onboarding. Any form submit.
