# Patterns

## App shell

A 65px ink header on every page: hamburger, Stories, Shop, Courses, centred wordmark, three social icons, Search. The hamburger opens a full-width category panel. The footer repeats the site pages, the two stores, and a newsletter form. Interior pages are white. The home featured band inverts to the same ink as the header.

## Navigation

Primary destinations are Stories, the category panel, and search. Shop and Courses leave the magazine for external stores. Category archives share one template.

## Form

Contact is a Contact Form 7 form: name, email, message, a terms checkbox, a black submit. It was not filled and not submitted, so the error state is not captured. Newsletter forms repeat in the rail, the footer, and a closed modal. They were not filled.

## Empty, loading, error

`/login` is the error pattern: crimson Roslindale heading "Oooops! Page not Found", a slate line, then a Recommended card row. Search with an empty query shows the search field and no result grid. No loading skeleton was seen. Load more is a button, not a spinner.

## Destructive confirmation

Not present.

## Feedback after an action

Not captured. No form was submitted.

## Search and filtering

Search is a header button that navigates to `/?s=`. The field is 600px on desktop. A results page (`/?s=monet`) uses the same shell and a card grid. There is no filter rail.

## List

Latest, category, trending, long reads, quizzes, and author pages are card grids.

## Onboarding

Not present. There is no account and no sign-in form.
