# DailyArt Magazine design reference

A token set and component reference for DailyArt Magazine, the public art-history magazine at https://www.dailyartmagazine.com. Captured signed out on 2026-11-21 in headless Chromium at 1440 and 390. WordPress 7.1.5, theme `idvetmp-child`. One light theme. The home featured band uses the same ink as the header.

The magazine has no sign-in. https://www.dailyartmagazine.com/login is a 404 page. Shop and Courses are external stores and were not entered. Newsletter and contact fields were not filled.

This package is not registered in catalog.json. That file was left unchanged on purpose. `make design-md` and `make brief` were not run, because both rewrite from the catalog.

### Principles

1. Do set display titles in the condensed face and UI in the sans.
2. Do spend crimson on badges, bylines and footer links.
3. Do keep the header and the home featured band on the same ink.
4. Do keep interior pages on white with black titles.
5. Don't round the search field.
6. Don't follow Shop or Courses into a store from a magazine screen.

Each rule links a screenshot under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

| Theme | text on page | text on surface | muted on page | muted on surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| light | 21:1 pass | 21:1 pass | 7.4:1 pass | 7.4:1 pass |

Black on white passes. Slate `#4d5761` on white passes. Crimson `#c6002f` on white passes. White on the header ink passes. The search field paints no focus ring (`light.focus` is the outline colour, and the outline width is 0).

## How the values were read

Computed styles in headless Chromium, written to [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) and [raw/extra-readings.json](raw/extra-readings.json) by [tools/capture/public/capture.mjs](tools/capture/public/capture.mjs). `theme-color` is `#C6002F`. The theme stylesheets declare almost no design tokens (three unrelated custom properties). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) maps [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json) into [tokens.json](tokens.json). Screenshots were quantised after the readings.

## What to distrust

- **Body background is transparent.** Interior pages photograph as white. `light.page` is that photographed white, not a computed body fill.
- **Donate panel position.** The dialog's scrim is the viewport. The crimson panel is in normal flow below the fold, so [home-light-donate-modal](screenshots/home-light-donate-modal.png) shows the scrim more than the panel. Panel colours come from computed style.
- **No focus ring.** The focused search field reports `outline: none`. `light.focus` records the colour that would have painted. It does not paint.
- **Fonts are licensed.** Basier and Roslindale are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system sans and a serif.
- **Home headlines change.** Card titles in the screenshots are whatever the front page showed on 2026-11-21.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Published contact addresses were painted out** of [about](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [advertise](screenshots/advertise-light-default.png) and [contribute](screenshots/contribute-light-default.png). The lines are white. The addresses are not in the package.
- **Load more** was not confirmed. The button click timed out.

## Do and don't

1. **Do set display titles in the condensed face and UI in the sans.** Headings are Roslindale Condensed. Nav, buttons and body are Basier. See [article](screenshots/article-light-default.png).
2. **Do spend crimson on badges, bylines and footer links.** `#c6002f` is the theme-color, the badge fill, and the link colour. It is not the button fill. See [login](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
3. **Do keep the header and the home featured band on the same ink.** Both are `#19191c`, with white type. Interior pages drop back to white. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [about](screenshots/about-light-default.png).
4. **Do keep interior pages on white with black titles.** Article, contact and the 404 use black Roslindale on white. The 404 heading is the exception: it is crimson. See [article](screenshots/article-light-default.png) and [login](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
5. **Don't round the search field.** It is square, 48px, with a pale border. Buttons and badges are 3px. Contact fields are 2px. See [search-light-empty](screenshots/search-light-empty.png).
6. **Don't follow Shop or Courses into a store from a magazine screen.** Both leave the site. Record the link. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Rebuild

From this folder. Full-colour shots stay in `~/.design-systems/dailyartmagazine/raw-shots/`.

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/capture.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py screenshots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

`capture.mjs` writes readings and screenshots. Quantise after reading values. The builders do not need a network.
