# DailyArt Magazine design reference

A token set and component reference for DailyArt Magazine's public surface — for building editorial pages, article layouts and newsletter/contact forms in the style of the real thing. It was read from **dailyartmagazine.com** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, the /latest index, an Art Movements category (/category/art-movements/impressionism/), a full article (/10-japanese-woodblock-prints-you-need-to-see/), about, the Contact Form 7 page, search results, plus a 390px mobile pass on home and the article.

DailyArt Magazine is worth a capture because a WordPress art-history magazine paints an unusually editorial system: a white field crossed by near-black `#19191c` bands — the 65px fixed nav, the ~799px home hero, the mid-page donation sections — with one accent, `#c6002f` crimson, on every chip, tag, footer link and ghost-button border, plus a brighter `#f3003a` on inline article links. Three self-hosted voices carry the type: RoslindaleCondensed at 56px/500 on display headings, RoslindaleNarrow at 20px/700 on card titles and menu rows, and Basier at 400/600/700 on everything else. Interactive language is one ghost button: transparent with a 1px `#c6002f` border, filling crimson on hover and pressed. **Boundary:** the magazine is read-only — Shop and Courses lead to external stores and were measured as links, never followed; the newsletter and contact forms were filled with sample text and cleared, never submitted; comment threads and ad content are third-party, not part of the evidence.

### Principles

1. Do band dark over light.
2. Do spend the crimson on labels and actions.
3. Do ghost the secondary actions.
4. Do pair the serif voices.
5. Do keep it square.
6. Don't style a signed-in app from this package.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't treat ad slots as design.

## Do and don't

1. **Do band dark over light.** `#19191c` is a band — nav, hero, donation — never the whole page; the editorial field stays white. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do spend the crimson on labels and actions.** `#c6002f` paints category chips, tags, footer links and ghost-button borders; body copy stays `#4d5761` slate and headings `#000` or white. See [post-card-h](components/crops/post-card-h-default.png).
3. **Do ghost the secondary actions.** Search, Send and Load more are transparent with a 1px `#c6002f` border at rest, filling crimson with white text on hover/pressed. See [load-more](components/crops/load-more-hover.png).
4. **Do pair the serif voices.** RoslindaleCondensed at 56px/500 owns display headings and 28px tabs; RoslindaleNarrow at 20px/700 owns card titles and menu rows; Basier carries everything else. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
5. **Do keep it square.** 3px radius on chips, buttons and inputs; 2px on the contact Send; cards and panels paint at 0. See [search-btn](components/crops/search-btn-default.png).
6. **Don't style a signed-in app from this package.** The magazine is read-only — every surface here is public; Shop and Courses are external stores, never captured.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The dark bands are surfaces inside the single theme, not a toggle; there is no dark token set.
8. **Don't treat ad slots as design.** The third-party placements are measured as layout space only.

## Rebuild

Everything derived regenerates offline from the raw readings (run from `systems/dailyartmagazine/`):

```sh
python3 tools/build_component_tokens.py        # raw/components-computed.json -> raw/component-tokens.json
python3 tools/build_tokens.py                  # -> tokens.json
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py             # -> style-guide.html
python3 tools/build_components_md.py           # -> components.md
node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs  # -> raw/measurements.md
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-dir>    # full-colour PNGs -> screenshots/
```

Then `make brief && make design-md && make grade && make check` from the repo root refreshes the generated sections and validates the catalog.

Live recapture (needs the site; Playwright at `PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs`):

```sh
node tools/capture/vars.mjs                    # stylesheet harvest -> raw/css-vars.json
node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs          # computed styles + crops -> raw/components-computed.json
RAW=<scratch> node tools/capture/public/public.mjs   # screenshots -> <scratch> (incl. the mega-menu/mobile-menu toggles)
```

dailyartmagazine.com loads headless from this client. The newsletter modal opens via the site's own `.modal-r--show` class; nothing was submitted or followed off-domain.

## How the values were read

The site is a WordPress publication, so there is no app to sign into: the capture is the public magazine itself. `tools/capture/public/` holds the kit copy — `public.mjs` shoots the routes (home with the hamburger-opened mega-menu, latest, a category index, an article, about, contact, search, and a 390px pass on home + article), `measure.mjs` reads bounding boxes and computed styles for every component in `raw/components-computed.json` across default/hover/focus-visible/pressed/focused/filled states plus the locally opened mega-menu (the site's `#hamburger` trigger) and the newsletter modal (the site's `.modal-r--show` class). `capture/vars.mjs` resolves the declared custom properties; `build_component_tokens.py` turns the measurements into the token spec, `build_tokens.py` resolves it into `tokens.json`, `build-measurements.mjs` writes `raw/measurements.md`, and `build_components_md.py` + `build_style_guide.py` generate the docs. Nothing was submitted, signed into, downloaded or followed off-domain; third-party ad slots were measured as layout only.
