## Gaps

- Hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, loading, and error were not held. Declared tokens exist for several of them and are in [tokens.json](tokens.json) with that note.
- Checkbox, switch, select, textarea, tabs, menu, tooltip, dialog, toast, avatar, and badge were not on the pages that were measured. The consent dialog was present before OK and was not measured as product chrome.
- Search was not opened. The navigation menu was not opened.
- The email field was not focused and was not submitted.
