# The New Yorker design reference

A token set for The New Yorker's public signed-out pages. Measured on **2026-11-19** in the T3 preview browser, signed out, at **1169 by 731** CSS pixels (devicePixelRatio 2, visual viewport scale 1). Pages: the homepage, one magazine article, the digital offers page, and the Condé Nast email sign-in step. Nothing was submitted.

The site sets colour and type as custom properties on `body`. Article text is **TNYAdobeCaslonPro** at 21px / 31.5px. Headings are **IrvinHeadingPro**. UI labels are **Graphik**. Rubrics are **IrvinText** in #db3334. Bylines are **NeutrafaceNewYorker**. The subscribe fill is #0879bf. **Boundary:** no card, no account, no trial. The signed-in app was not opened.

### Principles

1. Do set article body in Caslon at 21px on 31.5px.
2. Do set headings in IrvinHeadingPro, not in the body face.
3. Do paint rubrics in #db3334.
4. Do make Subscribe the blue chip.
5. Don't treat a missing screenshot as the layout.
6. Don't send a reader to checkout from this package.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser loaded newyorker.com signed out. `preview_evaluate` read `getComputedStyle` on the header, Subscribe, section links, rubric, dek, byline, article heading, article paragraphs, the offers button, and the sign-in email step. Colour roles were also read from declared custom properties on `body, .theme-standard`. Type roles were read from `--size-*` and `--font-*` on `body`, then checked against the computed sizes on the live elements.

`preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." No PNG was saved. A headless Playwright session was not started after that error.

## What to distrust

The preview viewport was 1169px, not 1440 or 390. Type sizes that the stylesheet changes at 768px and 1024px are recorded at the base rule in [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json). The article heading's 42px is the computed size at 1169px.

`--color-action-brand-pressed` declared `rgba(56.6,121.4,162.2,1)`. That is a computed mix, not a hex the stylesheet authored as a flat token. It is not in `tokens.json`.

The homepage lead wrapper computed background `#121212`. The header, the offers page, and the sign-in page were white. `light.page` is the white surface, not that dark band.

Continue with e-mail: the button element's computed color was `#000000` and the label span's computed color was `#ffffff`. The token uses the span.

Fallback faces in [style-guide.html](style-guide.html) are not the product faces. The product faces are proprietary and are named only.

No screenshot in this package shows the product. Do not sample colour from a missing image.

## Fonts

TNYAdobeCaslonPro, IrvinHeadingPro, IrvinText, Graphik, and NeutrafaceNewYorker are proprietary faces served by the site. Tokens name them and fall back to Times New Roman, Helvetica Neue, or Georgia. They are not committed as font files.

## Do and don't

- **Do set article body in Caslon at 21px on 31.5px.** The magazine article paragraphs computed to that size in a 521px column. The wordmark that sits above that column is [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg).
- **Do set headings in IrvinHeadingPro, not in the body face.** The article h1 was 42px Irvin. The homepage feature hed was 36px Irvin. [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg)
- **Do paint rubrics in #db3334.** Dept. of Medicine computed to that colour in IrvinText at 12px. [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg)
- **Do make Subscribe the blue chip.** Homepage Subscribe was #0879bf, white Graphik 12px, 1px radius, 23px tall. The offers CTA used the same blue at 13px, 4px radius, 48px tall. [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg)
- **Don't treat a missing screenshot as the layout.** This package has no live PNG. [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg)
- **Don't send a reader to checkout from this package.** GET DIGITAL ACCESS and Continue with e-mail were measured and not clicked. [logo.svg](icons/logo.svg)
