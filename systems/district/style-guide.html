Palette

The declared layer carries the full DDS hue scales under color.* (grey, purple, pink, blue, red, yellow, green, ember, peach) and the semantic roles (--color-background-*, --color-surface-*, --color-border-*, --color-icon-*, --color-text-*, --color-button-*, --color-gradient-*) resolved per theme — html[data-theme=light] is the default paint, data-theme=dark the measured dark pass. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing and radius

Controls are 12px (pills, CTAs, search), cards and auth 8px, chips 10px, the marketing nav pill 16px and the Nitro card a 96px slab. Hairlines are rgba(151,151,159,0.12) on cards and rgba(255,255,255,0.08) on the auth submit; focus rings are 2px #6aa8f4 (app) or #5865f2 (marketing).
