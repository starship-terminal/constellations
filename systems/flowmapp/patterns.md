# FlowMapp patterns

Public site only. The signed-in app was not captured.

## Marketing shell

A white rounded bar sits on a `#f6f8fa` page. Logo left, demo link, two hover menus, Pricing, Help, Blog, then Log in (outline) and Try for free (black pill). Phone replaces the link row with a menu button that opens a white sheet. Shot: [desktop-home.png](screenshots/desktop-home.png), [phone-home-menu-open.png](screenshots/phone-home-menu-open.png).

The blog uses a second header: category links instead of Features and Use cases. Shot: [desktop-blog-header.png](screenshots/desktop-blog-header.png).

## Hero CTA

One blue pill, "Start planning", centered under the headline. The painted fill is a child of the anchor, with a blue shadow. It opens sign-up. The walk stopped there.

## Pricing

Four plan cards and a monthly/annual tab. Annual is the default and shows a green 45% off chip. Plan buttons open sign-up with a plan query. Students and Education are separate pages that send the reader to Contact us. Shots: [desktop-pricing.png](screenshots/desktop-pricing.png), [desktop-pricing-student-top.png](screenshots/desktop-pricing-student-top.png).

## Help search

A dark hero with one search field and a Search button. Popular topics are links into articles. The cookie bar sits over the lower right until hidden. Shot: [desktop-help-with-chrome.png](screenshots/desktop-help-with-chrome.png).

## Contact form

A two-column page: copy and staff illustrations on the left, an unfilled form on the right. The form was not submitted. Shot: [desktop-contact.png](screenshots/desktop-contact.png).

## Auth wall

Sign-in is a centered card: Google, then email and password. Sign-up adds a username. The submit stays disabled until the fields have values. No field was filled. Shot: [desktop-signin-with-chrome.png](screenshots/desktop-signin-with-chrome.png).

## Empty, loading, error, destructive, onboarding

Not present on the public pages that were opened. The 404 at `/login` is a marketing missing-page, heading "Oops, looks like there's nobody here", with Go to Main Page. Signed-in empty, loading, error, and onboarding states were Not captured.
