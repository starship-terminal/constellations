# FlowMapp design reference

A token set and component reference for FlowMapp's public surfaces — for building marketing pages, pricing tables and signed-out app/auth surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.flowmapp.com** and **app.flowmapp.com** on the capture date, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing, /product-overview, three /features/ pages, one use case, /reviews, /blog plus one article, /security, /privacy, /terms, /contact-us, /sitemap-generator, the signed-out /signin, /signup and /restore forms, the published read-only demo project at /share/projects/215285 (sitemap and user-flow viewers), and 390px passes on home, pricing and the shared sitemap.

FlowMapp is worth a capture because its two public hosts declare **separate** vocabularies on `:root`, which the token sets keep apart: the Webflow marketing site ships a legacy flat palette (`--dodger-blue` #0080ff, `--primary-blue`/`--basic-blue` #0050ff, `--bg` #f6f8fa, `--alice-blue` #e8edf2, `--service-grey` #8c9baa, `--yellow-green` #99dd2c) plus the Webflow `_apps---` variable layer (typography, the 20px radius, the shadcn-style colour roles, the chart ramp — including `-dark` twins that are declared but never painted); the React/Ant Design app ships its own ramp (`--primary-basic-blue` #0050ff, `--primary-basic-gray-880` #1f1f1f, the `--secondary-*` hue families with 8/20/32/56/64 alpha steps, the `--neutral-gray-*` greys). Self-hosted Inter carries everything, from the 72px/700 mastheads to the 12px trust eyebrow. The signature look: white cards on the #f6f8fa field, black ink, giant dodger-blue **pills** — the hero and plan CTAs are 1600px-radius buttons wrapped in a translucent `rgba(0,128,255,0.2)` halo — and an inverted black Team plan card. **Boundary:** everything post-login — the sitemap/user-flow editors, wireframes, content, estimation, dashboards, billing — was never reached; the auth and contact forms were measured and typed into then cleared, never submitted; every link and CTA was measured, never followed; no consent banner was observed on either host.

## How the values were read

Every colour, radius, length and font on this package is one of two kinds, and `$extensions.source` on each token says which:

- **Declared** — `css-var <name>`: a custom property in the host's own stylesheet, harvested by `tools/capture/vars.mjs` (the marketing site's Webflow CDN bundle plus inline blocks; the app's CSS-module chunks loaded by /signin and the share viewer) and resolved in a probe document carrying that host's real sheets at 1440 x 900.
- **Measured** — `measured/computed <what> of <component> <state>`: a computed style or bounding box read from the live DOM by `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`, with a matching crop in `components/crops/`.

The marketing palette names are emitted under `core` at their natural paths (`black`, `dodger.blue`, `apps.colors.primary`, `alice.blue`, `service.grey`…); the SPA's ramp lives under `app` (`primary.basic.blue`, `neutral.gray.40`, `secondary.green`…). Measured roles (`light.text.ink`, `light.action.cta-fill`, `app.action.primary-fill`…) **alias** the declared token whose value they match, so a role that paints #0050ff reads `{primary.basic.blue}`, not a duplicated literal. The marketing `--_apps---colors--*-dark` variables are emitted verbatim under `core.apps.*`: they are declarations nothing signed out switches to, not a captured dark surface.

## What to distrust

- **Nothing dark paints.** Every signed-out surface is light-only (no toggle, no `prefers-color-scheme` rule observed; `color-scheme: normal`). The `apps.*.dark` tokens are template evidence — do not present them as a captured dark theme.
- **The two blues differ.** Marketing CTAs paint `--dodger-blue` #0080ff; the app primary is `--primary-basic-blue` #0050ff (which marketing also declares as `--primary-blue`/`--basic-blue` but does not paint on the measured buttons). Check `light.action.cta-fill` vs `app.action.primary-fill` before mixing.
- **Presses are forced.** `pressed` states were read with `CSS.forcePseudoState` — any script-driven mousedown look is uncaptured.
- **The sign-in submit's two fills are both measured:** disabled #9fb8f9 at rest, `--primary-basic-blue` #0050ff once sample text sat in both fields — it was never clicked.
- **Share-viewer geometry is zoom-dependent.** Card sizes on the demo canvas were measured at the viewer's captured zoom (60%); they are real px at that zoom, not a canonical size.
