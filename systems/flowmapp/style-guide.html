Palette

The marketing palette is a two-layer declaration: the flat legacy names the components are styled with, and the Webflow _apps layer (colours, the chart ramp, sidebar and typography vars — the -dark twins are declared but never painted). The app re-declares nothing: it ships its own --primary-*/--secondary-*/--neutral-* ramp. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Radius and motion

Radii climb 8px (nav links, flyout rows) → 12px (header and share-viewer buttons) → 16px (auth fields, share cards) → 24px (pricing cards) → the 1600px pill that makes every CTA fully round; the Webflow layer declares the 20px --_apps---sizes--radius. The control transition is the measured 0.3s on nav links and buttons.

Type

Inter carries everything: display headings at 700 (96px feature pages, 72px home mastheads), nav links and buttons at 600, the trust eyebrow and app headings at 500, body and plan rows at 400 — the marketing sizes are measured (the sheets declare no size vars), and the app loads a narrower weight band from Google Fonts.
