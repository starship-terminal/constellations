Coinbase, declared and measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. coinbase.com ships the Coinbase Design System (CDS): the whole theme is declared inline on div.coinbase.light (~830 custom properties) and div.coinbase.dark (~210) — eleven primitive ramps (--light-blue*/--light-gray*/--light-green*/…, blue60 #0052ff the brand), the 54-role --color-*/--lightColor-*/--darkColor-* semantic layer (fg, bg, bgAlternate, bgLine, washes, accents), the --space-*/--borderRadius-*/--borderWidth-*/--fontSize-* scales, the --shadow-elevation* ladder and the --cds-font-* faces (CoinbaseDisplay, CoinbaseSans, CoinbaseText, CoinbaseMono, CoinbaseCondensed). Roles are measured on the painted chrome: the blue Sign-up pill, the white nav over its hairline, the dark asset band's chips, the grey footer band and the sign-in card.

One light scheme across the marketing routes with a .coinbase.dark asset band as a theme island; login.coinbase.com/signin is a separate app surface measured at rest as the sign-in boundary (Singapore locale, en-SG — the site geolocates). Read from coinbase.com, signed out, in headed Chromium under Xvfb (Cloudflare gates headless). Nothing was signed into, typed into, or submitted.

Palette

The page ground is light.surface.page (bg #fff); raised bands are light.surface.raised = bgAlternate #eef0f3 (the promo row and footer); the asset panel is light.surface.dark = bgInverse #0a0b0d. Ink is light.text.primary = fg #0a0b0d with light.text.muted = fgMuted #5b616e for secondary copy; the accent is light.accent = bgPrimary = blue60 #0052ff. Status colours come off the declared role layer: fgPositive #098551, fgNegative #cf202f, fgWarning #cf470e.
