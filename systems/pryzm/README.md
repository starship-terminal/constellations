# Pryzm

Pryzm is a browser studio for gradients, glass, grain and animated loops, at https://pryzm.design/. This package is the public site, captured 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop is 1440×900 at 1x. Phone is 390×844 at 2x. The pages pin `class="dark"` on `html`. `prefers-color-scheme: light` and `dark` both paint that same dark canvas. No account was created. No email was typed. No password was submitted. Google was not clicked. No form was submitted.

The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). It was written before the screenshots.

## How the values were read

Headless Playwright opened the public pages. `tools/capture/vars.mjs` read the CSSOM: custom properties on `:root` and `.dark`, font faces, and the computed root values. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` read computed styles on marked elements and saved crops. Screenshots went to `~/.design-systems/pryzm/raw-shots` and were quantised to 256 colours for `screenshots/`. Colours in tokens.json are the hex declarations in the stylesheet, not samples from the PNGs.

## What to distrust

- The `light` set is declared CSS. The public pages do not paint it. Do not treat it as a captured theme.
- Hover and focus-visible crops often match the default. The computed style on the marked element did not change. A change may sit on an inner layer. It was not measured.
- Display type size in `core.typography.display` is Derived from the hero, not a stored computed style.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Gradients and photos band. Read colours from tokens.json.
- The support page screenshots had the published support address painted over. The address is not in this package.
- Sign-in screenshots show empty fields. Nothing was typed.

## Fonts

Inter is the UI face (`--font-sans`), loaded from the site. Poppins is the heading face (`--font-heading`). Geist Mono is the mono face (`--font-mono`). They are the product's fonts. This package does not commit the font files. The style guide falls back to system faces of the same role.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the canvas black and the action near-white.** The page is `#000`. The button that matters is a light pill. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Do put the product in a left rail once you are in the studio.** Studio, Lab, Flow and Inspiration share that rail. Marketing pages do not. [studio-dark-default.png](screenshots/studio-dark-default.png)
- **Do round cards and dialogs more than buttons.** Cards and the export sheet are 18px. Buttons and fields are 8px. [gallery-dark-default.png](screenshots/gallery-dark-default.png)
- **Do set headings in Poppins and UI in Inter.** The hero and the gallery title are the heading face. Controls are Inter. [home-dark-pricing-yearly.png](screenshots/home-dark-pricing-yearly.png)
- **Don't invent a light theme from the `:root` declarations.** The public site forces `dark`. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
- **Don't follow Sign in, My work, checkout, or Google.** Those branches stop at a wall. [signin-dark-wall.png](screenshots/signin-dark-wall.png)

## Rebuild

```sh
cd systems/pryzm
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node tools/capture/public/public.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node tools/capture/vars.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/pryzm/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

Full-colour shots stay in `~/.design-systems/pryzm/raw-shots`. They are not committed.
