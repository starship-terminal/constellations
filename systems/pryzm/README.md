# Pryzm

Pryzm is the background and visual studio at [pryzm.design](https://pryzm.design/). This package records the public signed-out surfaces measured on 2026-11-19.

The live page puts `dark` on `html` and paints black. A light `:root` palette is declared in the stylesheet and was not on screen. Values are in [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/measured.json](raw/measured.json). Desktop CSS viewport for the screenshots is 1440x900. The phone screenshot is 390x844 CSS, saved at devicePixelRatio 2 (780x1688).

## How the values were read

The attached T3 preview tab loaded `https://pryzm.design/` with no Cloudflare challenge. Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the homepage, `/signin` (including the Sign up form), `/gallery`, `/lab`, `/about` and `/docs`. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so the screenshots are a later headless Playwright pass of the same public URLs. Those requests returned HTTP 200 and the product title, with no challenge form.

The homepage h1 is Poppins 48px, weight 600, line-height 50.4px, letter-spacing -0.96px at 1169px and at 1440px. At 390px the same heading computed to 30px / 31.5px / -0.6px. Body is Inter 16px / 24px. Primary actions (Get Pryzm Pro, Continue, Create account) use `--primary` `#e5e5e5` with `--primary-foreground` `#171717` and an 8px radius.

## What to distrust

The light hex block in [raw/measured.json](raw/measured.json) is declared on `:root` and was not painted. `html` has class `dark` on every page that loaded. Do not treat that block as a captured theme.

Gallery card images are content. Their colors are the looks, not brand tokens. `--chart-1` through `--chart-5` and `--sidebar-primary` (`#1447e6`) are declared and were not painted on these pages.

Sign-in fields paint `--background` (`#000000`), not `--input` (`#ffffff26`). `--input` is recorded because it is declared and computed, and it is not the field fill.

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` did not shorten the 150ms button transition or the 300ms card transform. That is a finding, not a missing reading.

The phone hero "Open studio" looks filled in [screenshots/home-dark-phone.png](screenshots/home-dark-phone.png). Its computed style was not read at 390px. The desktop header "Open studio" is a ghost button (white at 15% fill, 24px tall).

## Fonts

Inter is the UI family (`--font-sans`). Poppins is the heading family (`--font-heading`, `--tracking-heading` -0.02em). Geist Mono is `--font-mono` and was not the resolved family on the measured text. The wordmark is an image at `/logo.png`, not a type style.

## Do and don't

- **Do use Inter for UI and Poppins for headings.** Body is 16px Inter. The homepage h1 is 48px Poppins at weight 600 with -0.96px letter-spacing. See [screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png).
- **Do use the light primary button for the main action.** Fill `#e5e5e5`, label `#171717`, 8px radius, weight 500. Get Pryzm Pro is 32px tall. Sign-in Continue is 44px tall. See [screenshots/pricing-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-desktop.png) and [screenshots/signin-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/signin-dark-desktop.png).
- **Do keep the app shell as a 256px black sidebar and a dark inset canvas.** Gallery cards are `#141414` with an 18px radius and a 1px border at white 11%. See [screenshots/gallery-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/gallery-dark-desktop.png).
- **Do not invent a light theme from the stylesheet.** `:root` declares a light palette. The painted pages are `html.dark`.
- **Do not treat gallery art as brand color.** Looks such as Ink Facet are content. The brand accent on these pages is the light primary button.

## Gaps

- Light theme was declared and not shown. No theme toggle was found.
- Account creation, Google sign-in, checkout, newsletter submit, and Lab Save / Share / Export were not entered.
- Hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, error, empty and loading states were not captured.
- The preview browser could not produce screenshot frames. Screenshots are headless Playwright of the public pages.
- Reduced motion is ignored by the measured transitions.
- Icon set was not extracted. [icon.png](icon.png) is the wordmark.
