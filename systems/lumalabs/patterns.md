# Patterns

How the public site combines the components. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/).

## Shell

A fixed wordmark and a single row of uppercase links. On a black hero the links are white and Sign In is a white pill. On a white page the links are black and Sign In is a black pill. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

At 390 the same links open as a full-height black sheet. [mobile-home-light-nav-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-nav-open.png).

## Hero

Black full-bleed band, white Graphik headline, one white pill CTA. A prompt card sits under the fold with suggestion chips. Chips fill the field and do not submit. [home-light-chip.png](screenshots/home-light-chip.png).

## Pricing

A Monthly/Yearly pill toggle, then three plan cards. The middle card is inverted (black ground, white type). Start Now leaves for the signup wall and was not followed. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [pricing-light-yearly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-yearly.png).

## FAQ

A rounded row. Opening it expands the answer in place and swaps the plus for a close mark. [api-light-faq-open.png](screenshots/api-light-faq-open.png), [app-light-faq-open.png](screenshots/app-light-faq-open.png).

## Form

Sales, partner, educator, and whitepaper pages show empty fields. None were filled or submitted. [contact-sales-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-sales-light-default.png), [enterprise-light-full.png](screenshots/enterprise-light-full.png).

## List

News is a long index of article links. Careers is a filterable role list. Case studies is a card grid with category chips. [news-light-default.png](screenshots/news-light-default.png), [careers-light-default.png](screenshots/careers-light-default.png), [case-studies-light-default.png](screenshots/case-studies-light-default.png).

## Loading, empty, error

Not shown on the public pages. The signed-in app that would show them is behind `auth.lumalabs.ai`.
