# Article

A signed-out capture of the public Article shop at [https://www.article.com/](https://www.article.com/) on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The page is `#ffffff`, type is Proxima Nova, the site message is `#141414`, and the sale accent is `#ff6458`.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

## Type

Proxima Nova regular on the body, 16px, weight 400. Department links are the same. Sale by Room is Proxima Nova bold at 32px. The homepage display is 110px with 11px letter-spacing. The Timber title is 24px bold. Font files are not in this package.

## Controls

Search is a 40px field with a 4px radius and a 1px `#c2c2c2` border. Shop the sale is a 44px coral pill. Add to cart and Subscribe are 42px coral pills with a `9999px` radius. Add to cart letter-spacing is 1.6px.
