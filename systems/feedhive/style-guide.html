Palette

The brand indigo #4457ff with its #3347fb hover step and the #eef2ff/#84a1ff/#5a84ff selected tint, against the warm neutral ramp: #23252b ink, muted grays #666c77–#8b9098, subtle surfaces #f4f3f2/#f6f6f8/#fbfaf7 and hairlines #dfdfe4–#e6e2dc. The declared layer carries only --color-black/--color-white — everything else was measured.

Spacing, radius and motion

A 4px declared spacing base (--spacing). Radii: 9px option chips, 11px header pills, 12px fields, 15px CTAs, 16px cards and media clips, 18px dropdown items, 20px on the Chakra card, full pills elsewhere. Controls transition over the declared 150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1); the gradient ring's --cta-border-angle animates 0deg → 1turn.

Type

Three served faces: Inter (variable wght 100–900) carries every control and body; Plus Jakarta Sans draws the display headings and plan names; Sometype Mono sets the tracked caps eyebrows and card kickers. Display sizes are measured arbitrary-px steps — 68/64/62/60/58/55px, 46px on the phone — with negative tracking throughout and +0.14–0.2em on the mono caps. The Chakra app paints 14.72px controls.
