# FeedHive components

Public marketing site, signed out, 2026-11-21. Crops are from the screenshots in [screenshots/](screenshots/). Values are computed styles, named as tokens in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Button

Sign Up is the marketing primary. 40px tall, 11px radius, Inter 14/21 medium, white on `#4457ff`. Hover paints `#3347fb`. Crop: [button-signup-default.png](components/crops/button-signup-default.png).

Login is the quiet pair. Same height and radius, `#272b34` on `#f4f2f0`. Crop: [button-login-default.png](components/crops/button-login-default.png).

Get started for free is the hero CTA. 50px tall, 15px radius, Inter 15/22.5 medium, same primary fill. Crop: [button-cta-default.png](components/crops/button-cta-default.png).

The sign-in submit is a different blue, `#3b82f6`, 59px tall, pill radius. It was not clicked. Crop: [dialog-signin-default.png](components/crops/dialog-signin-default.png).

Pressed, disabled, and loading were not shown.

## Link

Header links are Inter 14/21 medium, `#4f5562`, no underline. Footer links repeat the same family on white.

## Text field

The public tools pages have textareas (username generator, font generator, bio generator, slop detector). They were not typed into and not submitted.

The sign-in email and password fields are pills, 61px tall, 1px `#edf2f7` border, Inter 14.72px, text `#1a202c`. Focus shadow is `rgba(27, 25, 121, 0.05) 0px 2px 8px 0px`. Nothing was typed. Crop: [field-email-default.png](components/crops/field-email-default.png).

## Menu

Desktop Product, Agents & Automation, Socials, and Learn are header dropdowns. Crop: [menu-product-default.png](components/crops/menu-product-default.png). Phone uses the same links inside a drawer. Crop: [header-mobile-default.png](components/crops/header-mobile-default.png).

## Tabs

Home marketing demo has Composer, AI Assistant, Automation, and Post Conditions. Pricing has Monthly and Yearly pills. Yearly starts selected: white on `#4457ff`. Monthly is `#757b86` on a clear fill. Both were clicked. They do not submit.

## Card

Pricing shows Creator, Brand, Business, and Agency. Business is the filled primary card with a Popular chip. Crop: [card-pricing-default.png](components/crops/card-pricing-default.png).

## Dialog

The sign-in wall is a white card on `#f7fafc`, with a Google button, an OR divider, email, password, Sign in, and Forgot your password. Not submitted. Crop: [dialog-signin-default.png](components/crops/dialog-signin-default.png).

## Gaps

Icon button, select, checkbox, radio, switch, tooltip, toast, list row, table, avatar, and badge were not present on the public pages opened, or sit behind the sign-in wall.

Unread token values are Not captured. The signed-in app was not captured.
