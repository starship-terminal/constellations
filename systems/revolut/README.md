### Principles

1. Use the pill buttons.
2. Use Aeonik Pro for display and Inter for UI text.
3. Keep the two accents apart.
4. Do not treat the hero size as the display token.
5. Do not invent a screenshot.
6. Do not copy the wordmark, card art, or Aeonik Pro into something that ships.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed style in the T3 preview browser, on the live page. Colour tokens are `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` for every `--rui-color-*` that resolved. Type, radius, space, size, shadow, duration, and easing are the same read on `:root` of the home page. Button, heading, footer, and SSO field values are `getComputedStyle` on the painted element.

Home: `https://www.revolut.com/`, title "Banking & Beyond | Revolut United Kingdom", `color-scheme: light`. Pricing: `https://www.revolut.com/our-pricing-plans/`, title "Compare Revolut Plans | Revolut United Kingdom", `html[data-theme=dark]`. SSO: `https://sso.revolut.com/signin`, title "Single Sign On | Revolut", `data-theme=dark`, `color-scheme: dark`. The query string on that URL holds an OAuth `code_challenge` and was not copied into the package.

The preview snapshot call returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Measurements already read were kept. No screenshot was invented.

## What to distrust

The hero `h1` computed to 82.6351px with tracking -2.08px at this viewport. The token scale's marketing display1 is 3.5rem (56px). Both are measured. The token is the declared role. The 82px size is the fluid heading at 1169px, recorded on `light.text.hero`'s source string.

Resolved font weights on the marketing site differ from the declared custom properties. Body declared 400 and computed 500. Display declared 700 and computed 800. Tokens use the resolved weights.

`--rui-easing-linear` computed to the quoted string `"linear"`. It is listed under `$extensions.notTokenised`, not stored as an easing.

SSO `Continue with email` was a 40px icon button. Its accessible name was "Continue with email". The visible word "Email" sat in a sibling. The 40px circle is the measured control.

`preview.cover` is [icon.png](icon.png) because no page screenshot exists.

- **The foundation scales have no file behind them.** The type, radius, space, size, shadow and motion scales and most painted-chrome values were read in the capture session, but no `raw/foundation.json` was kept. Only the `--rui-color-*` dumps are in [raw/](raw). The builder labels every Measured token whose value raw/ does not hold, with its opacity, "Reconstruction" (87 tokens). A coincidental match, such as a 4px step that also appears in a colour dump, keeps the old label.

## Fonts

Aeonik Pro is the marketing face (`--rui-font-marketing`). It loaded at weight 500 on the home page. Weight 700 was declared and not loaded. Inter is the brand face (`--rui-font-brand`) and loaded. The system stack is `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, Arimo, sans-serif`. Neither Aeonik Pro nor Inter is committed. They are Revolut's fonts.

## Do and don't

- **Use the pill buttons.** Log in is black on white type, Sign up is white on black type, both `9999px`, padding 10px 24px, Aeonik Pro 500. See `light.button` in [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- **Use Aeonik Pro for display and Inter for UI text.** The SSO Continue button is Inter 500 at 15px, not Aeonik. See `core.font`.
- **Keep the two accents apart.** Light marketing accent is `#234fa6`. Dark marketing and the SSO wall use `#6fa0ff`. See `light.accent` and `dark.accent`.
- **Do not treat the hero size as the display token.** 82.6351px was the fluid heading at 1169px. The role token is 56px.
- **Do not invent a screenshot.** The frame failure is recorded above. A grey PNG would look like evidence.
- **Do not copy the wordmark, card art, or Aeonik Pro into something that ships.**
