# Kraken patterns

How the public pages combine the components. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots).

## Marketing shell

A 64px transparent header over a light page (`magic-light`, page `#f6f5f9` in the token layer, the hero paints near-white). Product links sit left of the actions. Sign up is the purple pill. Log in is the purple wash. Search is an icon button. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

On the phone the same header collapses. The unlabeled button at the right opens a full-height nav list (Kraken, Pro, Wallet, Prime, VIP, Markets, About, Log in, Sign up). The Search button beside it opens the trade overlay, not the nav. See [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png).

## Hero with an account field

The home hero is a centred Kraken-Brand headline, a subline, an email field and a purple Try Kraken button, then Apple and Google marks. The field shows Kraken's published placeholder. Nothing was typed and the button was not submitted. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Dark product band

Home drops into a near-black band for Kraken Pro: white headline, purple Sign up now. /pro, /wallet, /prime, /vip, /app and /institutions are whole pages in `magic-dark`. The high-emphasis button on those pages is near-white, not purple. See [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png) and [pro-dark-default.png](screenshots/pro-dark-default.png).

## Market table

/prices and the home markets strip are a filter row (Crypto, Spot / Margin, Futures, xStocks) over a table of asset, price, 24h change and market cap. Green and red are the only status colour on the public surface. See [prices-light-default.png](screenshots/prices-light-default.png) and [home-light-tab-futures.png](screenshots/home-light-tab-futures.png).

## Sign-in card

id.kraken.com/sign-in is a centred white card on a pale ground: wordmark, title, email, password, purple Continue, then Google, Apple and passkey rows. It was photographed at rest. No credential was entered and no provider was followed. See [signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).

/sign-up is the same family of card with Personal / Business, email, optional invite code and Continue. See [signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).

## Legal and editorial

/legal, /learn, /careers, /why-kraken and /features stay in the light marketing shell: headline, body, footer. They were captured as pages. Their inner components were not measured one by one. See [legal-light-default.png](screenshots/legal-light-default.png) and [learn-light-default.png](screenshots/learn-light-default.png).

## Search

The header Search button opens a local overlay titled "Find your next trade": a field, a market list, and Esc to close. Nothing was typed into it. See [home-light-search-open.png](screenshots/home-light-search-open.png). On the phone the same overlay is the Search button, not the nav menu.

## What is not here

The trading app (pro.kraken.com/app), wallets, checkout, and any submitted form sit behind sign-in. Those surfaces are named in the README coverage table and in the catalog `capture.gaps`.
