# Kraken design reference

A token set and component reference for Kraken's public surface, for building crypto-exchange marketing with the purple `#7132f5` primary, Kraken-Brand headlines, 12px controls and the magic-light / magic-dark pair of the real site. It was read from **kraken.com** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

Kraken publishes its design system as custom properties. The body of the marketing home carries `data-theme="magic-light"` and about 1,187 resolved names. `/pro` carries `data-theme="magic-dark"` and the same names remapped. The brand primitive is `--colors-ds-brand` `#7132f5`. Semantic roles live under `--colors-ds-semantic-*` (surface page/canvas/raised/overlay, content primary/neutral/dimmed, action primary and its hover, status positive/negative/warning/info). Type roles are `--fontSize-ds-display*` through `--fontSize-ds-caption*`, set in Kraken-Brand, Kraken-Product and Kraken-Mono with IBM Plex fallbacks. Radius is a 0 to 32px scale plus a 9999px round step. Elevation is `--box-shadow-ds-0` through `--box-shadow-ds-7`.

**Boundary.** The trading app, wallets and any submitted account flow sit behind sign-in. `id.kraken.com/sign-in` and `/sign-up` were photographed and measured at rest. Fields were focused for their computed style. Nothing was typed, submitted, or followed into Google, Apple or passkey. The OneTrust banner was hidden and never answered.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export kraken` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do use purple `#7132f5` for the one primary action on a light page.
2. Do switch the primary to near-white on a magic-dark page.
3. Do keep controls at radius 12.
4. Do set headlines in Kraken-Brand and UI in Kraken-Product.
5. Don't invent a third theme.
6. Don't infer the trading product from these pages.
7. Don't copy the Kraken wordmark, the tentacle mark, or partner glyphs into anything that ships.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Declared values come from the CSSOM of the live public pages. `tools/capture/harvest-vars.mjs` opens `/`, `/pro` and `/sign-up`, hides OneTrust, and records every custom property `getComputedStyle(document.body)` resolves. Those land in `raw/css-vars.json`. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps the semantic names onto `light` and `dark`, and the shared scales onto `core`.

Computed component values come from `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`: hover is a real mouse hover, focus-visible is keyboard focus, pressed is `CSS.forcePseudoState` so the control never fires. Screenshots come from `tools/capture/public/public.mjs` at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, then `quantize.py` writes 256-colour PNGs.

The home hero email field shows Kraken's published placeholder `satoshi@email.com`. That string is the input's placeholder attribute, not a typed address. No field was filled.

## What to distrust

- The 4px spacing scale in `core.spacing` was picked by hand at capture time and is labelled Reconstruction. Kraken does not publish a space scale as custom properties. The steps were chosen to fit the measured button padding (8, 12, 16) and the 2px radius increment. Control gaps that are declared (`--gap-ds-checkbox-*`, `--spacing-ds-radio-*`) are measured.
- `core.motion` comes from [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json). The probe's reduce verdict is partial: most moving elements keep their timing under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Pressed colour is a forced `:active`. If Kraken swaps the node in script on mousedown, that swap is missing.
- The email field's focus ring is weak on the input itself. The wrapper carries more of the focus paint, and that wrapper was not cropped separately.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from `tokens.json`.
- Kraken-Brand, Kraken-Product and Kraken-Mono are not in the repo. The tokens name IBM Plex as the fallback. The style guide loads the fallback, not the proprietary faces.
- `/pro` "Create free account" in the measurement pass resolved to a 36px nav control, not the 48px hero button. The hero size is on the screenshot.

## Do and don't

- **Do use purple `#7132f5` for the one primary action on a light page.** Sign up, Try Kraken and Continue share it. Hover is `#5b1ecf`. See [nav-signup-default.png](components/crops/nav-signup-default.png).
- **Do switch the primary to near-white on a magic-dark page.** Pro's Create free account is `#f7f7fa` on near-black, not another purple pill. See [pro-cta-default.png](components/crops/pro-cta-default.png).
- **Do keep controls at radius 12.** Buttons, inputs and the sign-in card use the `--border-radius-ds-5` step, not a pill. See [signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Do set headlines in Kraken-Brand and UI in Kraken-Product.** The hero is Brand at 48/56. Buttons are Product at 14 or 16, weight 500. See [hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
- **Don't invent a third theme.** The public site is magic-light or magic-dark. A dark band inside a light page is the dark theme, not a new palette. See [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).
- **Don't infer the trading product from these pages.** pro.kraken.com/app was not opened. See [pro-dark-default.png](screenshots/pro-dark-default.png).
- **Don't copy the Kraken wordmark, the tentacle mark, or partner glyphs into anything that ships.** See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
