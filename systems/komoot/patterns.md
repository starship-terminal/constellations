# Patterns

Public pages photographed on 2026-11-22. Signed out.

## Marketing shell

A 66px header: pin and wordmark, Routes, Planner, Features, Updates, an orange-wash App control, and a green Login or Signup pill. On a photograph the nav is near-white. On a cream page the nav is ink. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png) and [screenshots/help-light-1440.png](screenshots/help-light-1440.png)

## Homepage

A full-bleed trail photograph. A yellow peak sits above a white 72px heading, "Explore beyond the map". A white Signup for Free pill sits beside a green Login pill. A small caps line under them reads "WHERE WILL YOU EXPLORE NEXT?". An orange round control sits in the bottom right. Same homepage shot.

## Help Guides

Cream page, green rule, 36px ink heading, 18px lede, then a row of book-shaped cards. The first viewport has eight cards and no person's name. [screenshots/help-light-1440.png](screenshots/help-light-1440.png)

## Stopped

Login or Signup, Signup for Free, Login, the App store dialog, gift, planner, and any page that prints a person's name were not used. Features was measured and not photographed.
