Palette

The ground is dark.page #000000. color.neutral is a five-step grey ramp (#191919, #262626, #808080, #bfbfbf, #e0e0e0) plus black and white that builds every surface, text level and border; buttons hover from gray-2 to gray-3. color.accent holds the declared greens and orange the signed-out pages never paint.

Component metrics

Measured boxes: the 72px header, 44px nav buttons and 56px large controls, the 270px (= --cols-3) soundscape card, the 62px sign-in field and the player's 56px transports.

Measured extras
