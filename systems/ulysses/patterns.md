# Patterns

## App shell

A white bar, 70px tall, with the wordmark on the left and four links on the right: Why Ulysses, Pricing, Stories, Help. The current link gets a yellow underline. On a 390px width the links collapse into a drawer. The drawer lists the same four links and a close control.

## Page

A white page, 48px Interstate headings, 15px body. Long homepage sections alternate a product photograph with a short column of copy. The dark sections paint white type. The action there is a yellow outline, not a fill.

## Pricing

One public price: $39.99 per year, with $5.99 per month under it. A check list, then App Store and Setapp badges. A second block points businesses to volume licensing. Checkout on the marketing host does not exist. The volume-licensing host's Continue to Checkout was not clicked.

## Footer

Three columns (Company, Explore, Help & Licensing), a language switch, a newsletter outline button, and three social marks. Yellow links, white column labels, near-black ground.

## Empty states

The marketing 404 is a centered heading, one sentence, and Back to Homepage. The login wall is a heading, one sentence, two empty fields, Log In, and Forgot your password. Both were photographed. Neither control was used to go further.

## Forms

Contact and newsletter show empty fields. They were not submitted. The signup wall is the start of a purchase. It was photographed and not continued.
