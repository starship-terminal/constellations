## App shell

A 60px bar, `#1a1a1a`, holds the white wordmark, league links, Log In, the red trial button and search. League pages add a 40px `#2b2b2b` secondary nav and a `#f7f7f4` score ribbon. The footer repeats the dark bar (`#1a1a1a`) with a 1px `#323232` top border and 12px `#c4c4c0` links. Page ground is `#ffffff`.

## About
