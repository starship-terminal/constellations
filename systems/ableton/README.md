# Ableton design reference

A token set and component reference for Ableton's public marketing site, for building a music-product marketing page or account-entry screen in the style of a real music software company. It was read from www.ableton.com on 2026-11-08, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 wide and 390 wide: the home page, the product pages (Live, Push, Move, Note, Link), the trial, shop and Packs catalogue, the blog index, the about, help and jobs pages, and the log in / register page. The look is famously restrained: black Futura PT on white, one signature `#0000ff` blue for every action, zero border radius anywhere, and full-bleed photography doing the decorating.

Ableton's own products (Live, Push, Move, Note) are a different world; this package is the marketing and account-entry surface only. Nothing was signed into, bought, downloaded or submitted, and the cookie consent card was captured but never answered.

### Principles

1. Do let one blue do all the work.
2. Don't round anything.
3. Do keep type to Futura bold at a few sizes.
4. Do put white type straight on photography.
5. Don't invent hover states.
6. Do keep the two focus rings.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Ableton's marketing pages are a custom server-rendered front end (no React, no Next/Nuxt; BEM-style `abl-*` and `main-nav__*` classes), so the kit selects by semantic selectors rather than a framework's atoms. Its SASS build declares no CSS custom properties at all: [vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) read every same-origin stylesheet and found zero declarations to promote (the two unreadable sheets were the cross-origin Adobe Typekit kit, recorded as skipped in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json)). Every token is therefore a literal measured off a real element's computed style, and each leaf's `$extensions.source` names the component entry it came from.

Component values are computed styles read per state with Playwright ([measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs)). Hover used the pointer, focus-visible a real keyboard Tab, and pressed was forced through DevTools (`CSS.forcePseudoState`), so no button was clicked for real. Text fields were clicked into and left empty. The only presses were the nav's "More" disclosure and the Live page's own feature-collapse toggles, both local UI state. Nothing was signed into, bought, downloaded or submitted; the consent card's Accept, Close and More info were measured but never clicked.

The cookie overlay (`#biscuits-overlay`) is hidden by CSS for the clean captures ([lib.mjs](tools/capture/public/lib.mjs) adds a `display: none` rule), which is a hide, not a consent: it is captured and measured as found in `home-light-cookie-banner` and the `cookie-*` entries. Screenshots were lazily scrolled to settle lazy-loaded cells, then stored quantised to 256 colours; the unquantised originals and per-capture metadata stay in [raw/full/](raw/full).

## What to distrust

- **Readings past a masked bot signal.** The committed screenshots and readings were taken with `navigator.webdriver` masked, the flag bot checks read to spot automation. The capture scripts no longer mask it (not run since), so a recapture may record a walled route as a boundary instead of a reading.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Gradients and photography band slightly; read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw files.
- **Hero cells carry video.** Two captures of the same page can differ in the frame shown.
- **The `main` rect in `raw/layout.json` is the whole page column** (Ableton wraps content in a single `<main>`), so "content x 0, 1440 wide" does not mean text spans 1440: the 1200px text column sits inside it.
- **`palette.lime-stripe` is read off a patterned cell**, not a flat fill: the promo card's background alternates lime and pale-blue stripes, and the token is the lime of the brighter band, approximate by eye.
- **Hover reads "same" on most controls by design.** Ableton's `abl-button`s genuinely do not restyle on hover (verified on the crop pairs); the entries are not missing a state.
- **The register form's `ul` paints `#ffc2ca`** in the child-layers note: that is the password-strength meter's track, not the form's background.
- **`trial-edition-card` and `pack-card` were read from the wrong elements.** `trial-edition-card` is the 720 x 655 hero column of /trial and `pack-card` is the 1440 x 348 page banner of /packs, not a single edition panel or Pack tile. Both need a recapture. `packs-green` now cites only `packs-title`, which holds the same value.
- **`hero-cta` has no keyboard-focus reading.** Its selector matched the `<p class="homepage-teaser__cta">` around the "See what's new" link, and keyboard focus never reached it (the raw state says `focus failed`), so the stored focus-visible style is the element at rest. The builders leave that state out of components.md and raw/measurements.md, and the focus summary lists it as not captured. It needs a recapture that focuses the link itself.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let one blue do all the work.** `#0000ff` is the only accent: every primary button, every text link, the scroll chevron and the consent Accept are the same flat blue, with `#0032e6` reserved for the boxed Buy button. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [trial-light-default](screenshots/trial-light-default.png) and [packs-light-default](screenshots/packs-light-default.png).
2. **Don't round anything.** Buttons, fields, badges, cards and the consent card are all 0px-radius rectangles; the signature is the hard square edge. See [login-submit-default](components/crops/login-submit-default.png), [newsletter-field-focused](components/crops/newsletter-field-focused.png) and [blog-category-badge-default](components/crops/blog-category-badge-default.png).
3. **Do keep type to Futura bold at a few sizes.** Display headlines run 60px, panels 40px, and almost everything else — nav, body, every button — shares 20/30 bold; badges drop to 14/21. Nothing is letter-spaced or transformed. See [live-title-default](components/crops/live-title-default.png) and [nav-link-default](components/crops/nav-link-default.png).
4. **Do put white type straight on photography.** Teaser cells and product heroes pin white Futura titles low on the image with no scrim card behind them; colour comes from the photo, not from panels. See [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png) and [push-light-full](screenshots/push-light-full.png).
5. **Don't invent hover states.** Most buttons and links measure identical on hover; the boxed Try button swaps its text from blue to black and the deep-blue Buy button darkens to `#042b9f`, and that is the whole vocabulary. See [btn-try-hover](components/crops/btn-try-hover.png) and [btn-buy-hover](components/crops/btn-buy-hover.png).
6. **Do keep the two focus rings.** Keyboard focus draws a deliberate 2px `#ff764d` orange ring on boxed buttons and the browser's 1px `#005fcc` auto ring on links — keep both rather than replacing them. See [btn-try-focus-visible](components/crops/btn-try-focus-visible.png) and [nav-link-focus-visible](components/crops/nav-link-focus-visible.png).

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
```

```bash
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

```bash
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

`build_tokens.py` reads `raw/component-tokens.json` and writes `tokens.json`; it refuses a value that is not the DTCG type it claims and an alias that points at nothing. `build_style_guide.py` renders `style-guide.html` from `tokens.json` and `tools/style-guide.template.html`, turning every token into a CSS variable so the page cannot drift from the file. `build_components_md.py` writes `components.md` from `raw/components-computed.json`, `raw/layout.json`, `tokens.json`, the screenshots and `icons/INDEX.tsv`. All three are deterministic: re-running them on this commit reproduces their files byte for byte. Edit the raw inputs or the builders, never the generated files.

`tools/capture/` holds the kit the captures came from. The Playwright scripts take `PLAYWRIGHT` as the path to a Playwright install's `index.mjs` when `playwright` is not resolvable from where they run.

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=/path/to/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/public.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT=/path/to/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs home live mobile
```

`public.mjs` re-takes the screenshot set into `RAW` (a scratch dir outside the repository, defaults to `raw/full`); `python3 tools/capture/quantize.py raw/full` stores the quantised copies in `screenshots/`. `measure.mjs` takes page names and merges into `raw/components-computed.json`; `node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs` then rewrites `raw/measurements.md`. `vars.mjs`, `layout.mjs` and `icons.mjs` rewrite their raw or icon files whole. `lib.mjs` holds the shared helpers, including the cookie-overlay hide (it no longer masks `navigator.webdriver`).

## Use in Figma

Import `tokens.json` with Tokens Studio and choose the Light theme. Set every font to Futura PT (or the closest geometric sans where it is not installed) and keep every corner at 0 radius.

## The screen to get right

The home page is the screen to get right. A 69px white bar carries the 60px-wide grid-mark logo, the product links in 20/30 bold black, "Try Live 12 for free" in `#0000ff` and "Log in or register" in 16px. Below, a grid of full-bleed photo and video cells (720px squares at desktop, single column on a phone) each pins a white Futura title low on the image; a striped lime promo card and a blog-teaser panel with salmon category badges break the rhythm. The footer is white again: a grey newsletter field with a blue Sign up, then columns of 20px links and the six social icons. The only card shadow anywhere is on the black consent card, and the only border is the promo card's 1px black rule. Everything the capture could not reach is listed under Gaps in [components.md](components.md#gaps).
