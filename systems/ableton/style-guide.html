Ableton design tokens

Measured from www.ableton.com's public marketing pages in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Every value below is a computed style read off the running site (the site's SASS build declares no CSS custom properties, so these are literals, each with a provenance note in the token file). One theme; the cookie consent was never answered.

The product itself (Live, Push, Move, Note) is a separate world: this package is the marketing and account-entry surface only. Screenshots and per-component readings are in components.md.

Palette

Ableton's marketing palette is famously restrained: black text on white, one signature blue (#0000ff) for every CTA and link, a deeper blue for the boxed Buy button, grey field fills, and a salmon pink kept for blog category badges. Content tiles add whatever their photography carries.

Core palette
Semantic (light)

Type

One family, Futura PT, in two weights: 700 for nav, headlines, buttons and badges; 400 for long copy and the consent card. Nothing is letter-spaced and nothing is transformed. The display step is 60px; body text and every button share 20/30.

Futura PT comes from Adobe Typekit on the live site; this page previews in Nunito Sans where Futura is not installed.

Space, radius & structure

The signature decision: zero radius. Buttons, fields, badges, cards and the consent card are all hard rectangles. Buttons pad 30px on the flanks and stand 47–48px; fields are 42–46px of grey fill with 15px side padding; the content column sits at 1200px under a 69px top bar.

Motion

Hover transitions are ~0.3s ease-in-out on colour properties; the scroll-down chevron eases its bottom offset over 0.15s; the mobile menu trigger changes colour over 0.35s.

Buttons & links

Every button is a hard square at 20/30 bold. The primary is signature blue with white text and no hover change; the boxed Buy button is the deeper blue and darkens on hover; outline buttons carry a 2px stroke; text links are plain blue or underlined deep blue with an arrow. Keyboard focus draws a 2px #ff764d orange ring on boxed buttons and the browser's 1px #005fcc auto ring on links.
