# WhatsApp design reference

A token set and component reference for WhatsApp's public marketing site, for building a cream-and-green product page in the style of the real thing. It was read from **whatsapp.com** on **2026-11-19**, signed out. Desktop captures are 1440 x 900 at 1x. The phone pass measured innerWidth 390 at 2x. Captured: `/`, `/download`, `/privacy`, `/calling`, `/contact` and `/security`, plus a phone pass on home and download.

WhatsApp's marketing site paints one light theme with no custom properties on `:root`. The field is `#fcf5eb`, the ink is `#1c1e21`, the footer and the privacy hero are `#111b21`, and the Download pill is `#25d366` with a `#1c1e21` label. Type is WhatsApp Sans Var, 80px on the desktop hero and 44px on the phone hero. Controls are 50px pills. **Boundary:** web.whatsapp.com was not opened. No phone number was entered. No QR pairing surface was opened. Download links to the app store and the Mac app were not followed. The blog, help center and WhatsApp Business are other domains and were not captured.

### Principles

1. Do paint the field cream.
2. Do keep the green for the action.
3. Do set the hero at 80px.
4. Do round the controls to 50px.
5. Do use one family.
6. Don't style the messenger from this package.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't follow a download or log-in link from a reconstruction.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read from the live DOM in headless Chromium (Playwright) at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. The same home page was also opened in the T3 preview browser. That tab loaded `https://www.whatsapp.com/` (title "WhatsApp | Secure and Reliable Free Private Messaging and Calling", h1 "Message privately", no Cloudflare challenge) and returned the same green, ink, cream, type and 50px radius. It did not produce screenshot frames, so the package screenshots are the headless captures.

whatsapp.com declares no custom properties on `:root`. The three readable stylesheets are hashed CSS from static.whatsapp.net. Colours in [tokens.json](tokens.json) are computed `rgb()` values normalised to hex. The contact-us link colour is the exception: it was sampled from the contact screenshot pixels (`#0373e9`), and the token says so.

Nothing was neutralised except a menu that a later navigation left open. Those shots were retaken with the menu closed. No cookie banner was present. No account, phone number, or QR code was entered.

## What to distrust

- The Features menu radius and shadow are not in the tokens. The DOM walk landed on a transparent wrapper. The crop shows the white list; do not invent a radius from it.
- `#0373e9` is a pixel sample of the contact-us link, not a computed style.
- [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [components.md](components.md) were written by hand from [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md); no builder regenerates them from a measurement file, so a recapture must update them by hand. `tools/build_tokens.py` checks that every token has a source and that every colour labelled Measured is in raw/. Two colours are not: `core.color.green-deep` (`#103928`) and `core.color.muted` (`#5e5e5e`) were read on the home page but not saved, and are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time.
- Hover on Download did not change the fill. Do not invent a hover green.
- The dark footer and the privacy hero are surfaces inside the one light theme. The `dark` token set is empty on purpose.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from a photo in a screenshot.
- WhatsApp Sans is not in the package. The style guide falls back to Helvetica Neue.
- The T3 preview tab measured the home page at innerWidth 1169, where the header padding computed to 12px 24px. The token uses the 1440 reading (16px 60px).

## Fonts

WhatsApp Sans Var is the only family. The page loads three `@font-face` rules, weights 400, 500 and 700, from one woff2 at `/rsrc.php/yN/r/fQtw5exLIdT.woff2`. It is WhatsApp's face and is not committed. Tokens name `"WhatsApp Sans Var", sans-serif`.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the field cream.** `#fcf5eb` is the header and the home sections. White is the contact page and the Log in pill. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do keep the green for the action.** `#25d366` fills Download and colours the privacy accent word. The label on the pill stays `#1c1e21`. See [button-download-default](components/crops/button-download-default.png).
3. **Do set the hero at 80px.** Desktop "Message privately" is 80px / 400, white, over the photo. The phone hero is 44px. See [heading-hero-default](components/crops/heading-hero-default.png).
4. **Do round the controls to 50px.** Download and Log in are pills, not a 999px token and not a square. See [button-login-default](components/crops/button-login-default.png).
5. **Do use one family.** WhatsApp Sans Var at 400 for reading and 500 for nav and pills. See [header-default](components/crops/header-default.png).
6. **Don't style the messenger from this package.** web.whatsapp.com was never opened. No chat bubbles, no QR login, no phone field.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** `#111b21` is the footer and the privacy hero inside the light theme. See [privacy-light-default](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).
8. **Don't follow a download or log-in link from a reconstruction.** Those destinations were measured, never opened.

## Rebuild

Run from `systems/whatsapp/`:

```sh
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

`build_tokens.py` checks the measured roles. It does not rewrite [tokens.json](tokens.json), because the site has no custom-property layer to harvest. Then `python3 tools/catalog.py design-md whatsapp` from the repo root refreshes [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).
