# WhatsApp patterns

How the public marketing pages combine the measured components. Screenshots are the pages. The style guide's boxed mocks are reconstructions.

## Chrome

Every captured page shares an 80px `#fcf5eb` header: green wordmark, Features, Privacy, Blog, Apps, Help Center, For Business, a white Log in pill, a green Download pill. Features opens a white list. At 390 the header is 58px with a menu glyph and a round download icon.

See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png) and [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Hero

Home puts an 80px white headline and a white Log in pill over a photo, on a cream field. Privacy uses the same headline on a `#111b21` field and paints "privately" in `#25d366`. Calling and security use the same split: headline left, product image right.

See [privacy-light-default](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png) and [features-light-default](screenshots/features-light-default.png).

## Download

`/download` keeps the cream field, a 60px headline, and a white card for WhatsApp Web with its own Log in pill. Further down, device cards sit on a lighter band. The full page is [download-light-full](screenshots/download-light-full.png).

## Contact

`/contact` is a white page of headings and sentences. The actions are blue text links ("contact us", "survey"), not pills. No form was on the page. See [contact-light-default](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).

## Footer

A `#111b21` band with 12px white column labels and 18px white links. See [footer-default](components/crops/footer-default.png).

## Phone

At 390 the hero headline drops to 44px and the Download pill sits inside the photo. The header collapses to a menu glyph, the wordmark, and a round green download icon. See [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) and [mobile-download-light-default](screenshots/mobile-download-light-default.png).
