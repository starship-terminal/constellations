# Monarch Money design reference

Tokens and component notes for Monarch Money, captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. One light theme. Public marketing lives on `https://www.monarch.com` (`https://monarchmoney.com` redirects there). The sign-in wall is `https://app.monarch.com/login`. The sign-up page at `https://app.monarch.com/signup` was photographed and not submitted. No account was created. No bank was linked. No card, phone or government ID was entered.

The marketing site is Next.js. Colour is a gray ramp and an orange ramp on `:root`. `data-brand` was unset, so the default `:root` paints the page. A `data-brand="refresh"` palette is declared and was not applied. ABC Oracle is the UI face. Copernicus sets the home hero. ABC Otto sets pricing headings. Beltram Mono is the accent face. Weight 350 is the book role.

1. Do paint the page gray 2.
2. Do spend orange on one filled action.
3. Do use pills in the marketing bar.
4. Do set display at 48px, weight 350, tracking -3.2px.
5. Do keep the sign-in card at 8px radius.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.

## Health


## How the values were read

Headless Chromium, Playwright, 2026-11-19. Desktop viewport 1440×900 at 1x. Phone viewport 390×844 at 2x, except the sign-in host, which reports devicePixelRatio 1, so that phone shot is 1x. Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle`. Custom properties were read from the resolved `:root` on `https://www.monarch.com`. The Osano consent window was hidden with CSS. Accept, Reject and Save were not clicked.

The sign-in host at `https://app.monarch.com/login` was measured the same way. Nothing was typed. Apple, Google, Forgot password, Stay signed in and Sign up were not pressed. The sign-up page was opened, photographed, and left. The email field already showed the site's placeholder `email@address.com`. Sign up with email was not pressed.

Cloudflare did not block the headless launch. A preview tab was opened on `https://www.monarch.com/` and showed the same marketing page, with no challenge.

## What to distrust

`core.color.refresh` is the `data-brand="refresh"` palette. It was declared and not painted. Do not use it for a screen of the live site.

The app stylesheet at `https://static.monarch.com/static/css/main.6ee92ac8.css` declares hundreds of custom properties. Cross-origin CSSOM blocked them on the painted sign-in page. They are not tokens.

The plan-card crop is the plan name heading, 331×38, not the card chrome. The password-field crop is the inner input, 310×46, transparent, not the bordered wrapper. The sign-up heading selector missed the page.

The home hero video is Mux. Headless Chromium cannot decode it and paints a Media Error card inside the player shadow root. That card was hidden before the shot. The video is not evidence. The photograph under the type is.

Status colours (`danger`, `success`, `warning`, `info`) are declared on `:root` and were not shown as form errors. The sign-in submit is half orange (`rgba(255, 105, 45, 0.5)`) while the password is empty. That is the resting state, not an error.

`core.space.1` (4px) is derived from the spacing step the stylesheet publishes. The other space steps were read off padding.

## Fonts

ABC Oracle, Copernicus, ABC Otto and Beltram Mono are licensed. The files are not in this package. Tokens name the families and fall back to `sans-serif`, `serif` or `monospace`. Weight 350 is the book role on display and body. Weight 500 is the eyebrow and the trial link.

## Coverage

Desktop, light: home, pricing, tracking, budgeting, planning, for couples, for professionals, download, about, security, blog, what's new, careers, press, Canada, the Mint comparison, the mortgage calculator, privacy, terms, help, sign-in, sign-up.

Phone, light: home, pricing, about, sign-in.

Not captured: the signed-in app (accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, reports, advice, settings), bank linking, checkout, the Features menu open, FAQ rows open, the billing menu, dark theme, disabled, loading and field-error states.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the page gray 2.** The ground is `#f6f5f3`. Cards and the nav pill are white. [Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do spend orange on one filled action.** Sign up and the trial link are orange 9 (`#ff692d`). Hover is orange 10 (`#f35b16`). [Sign up pill](components/crops/sign-up-button-default.png).
- **Do use pills in the marketing bar.** Nav links, Sign in and Sign up are 36px with radius 9999px. [Nav](components/crops/nav-link-default.png).
- **Do set display at 48px, weight 350, tracking -3.2px.** The home hero is Copernicus. Pricing headings are ABC Otto. [Hero](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
- **Do keep the sign-in card at 8px radius.** Fields are 48px. Apple and Google are 40px white buttons. [Sign in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The captured surfaces publish one light theme.
- **Don't style accounts, budgets or settings from this package.** The wall is the last measured screen. [Sign in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Don't follow Sign up, Apple or Google.** Those start an account or OAuth. [Sign up page](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).
- **Don't copy the butterfly, the wordmark or the licensed font files.** [Icon](icon.png).
- **Don't treat the half-orange submit as an error.** It rests there while the password is empty. [Submit](components/crops/signin-submit-default.png).

## Rebuild

From the repo root, with the package's readings already in `raw/`:

```bash
python3 systems/monarch-money/tools/build_tokens.py
python3 systems/monarch-money/tools/build_components_md.py
python3 systems/monarch-money/tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/catalog.py design-md monarch-money
python3 tools/brief.py monarch-money
```

A fresh capture, public pages only:

```bash
node systems/monarch-money/tools/capture/public/public.mjs
node systems/monarch-money/tools/capture/public/measure.mjs
node systems/monarch-money/tools/capture/harvest.mjs
```

Do not submit a form. Do not open a bank link. If a launch lands on a Cloudflare challenge, stop that browser and do not start another headless one against it.
