# Patterns

Public pages only. The signed-in library was not entered.

## App shell

Desktop marketing uses a 64 px frosted bar: wordmark, Features, Teams, Pricing, then Instagram, X, Log in, Join for free. Dark pages swap the middle labels for Marketplace and MCP and add Get started. `--nav-height` is 100 px. The rendered bar is 64 px.

Phone collapses that bar to the wordmark and an unnamed button. The button opens a full-height drawer. [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Marketing page

Home is a light `#fdfdfd` page on a `#f3f3f3` shell, with a black pill and a grey pill under a 96 px headline. FAQ, contact, team, MCP, search and the public item paint `#0e0e0e` and flip the pill to white. There is no theme toggle. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). [screenshots/faq-dark-default.png](screenshots/faq-dark-default.png).

## Browse

Search at `/search/?q=typography` and a public item at `/i/hcOfvOK/` load without an account. The item page is a dark stage with the save in the centre and a more-options menu. A join strip sits under the fold. [screenshots/search-dark-default.png](screenshots/search-dark-default.png). [screenshots/item-dark-default.png](screenshots/item-dark-default.png).

## Empty and missing

A profile slug that does not exist paints "Not found. This may have been deleted, or it's private." with Back to Home. [screenshots/notfound-dark-default.png](screenshots/notfound-dark-default.png).

`/explore/` does not stay public. It redirects to the login wall with `next=/pop/`.

## Login wall

One wall for log in and sign up. Email or username, no password, Continue disabled until the field has text, plus Google and Apple. [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Pricing

Three plan cards and a yearly/monthly radio. The comparison sits further down the same page. Buy buttons were not clicked. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Footer

Four groups: About us, Downloads, Features, Social, then Terms, Privacy, Copyright, and the line "Made with love by Immigrants." [screenshots/home-light-footer.png](screenshots/home-light-footer.png).
