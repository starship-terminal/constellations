# Superlist design reference

Superlist's public marketing site and the signed-out app chrome, measured on 2026-11-19. Marketing pages: `https://www.superlist.com/`, `/pricing`, `/blog`, and `/updates`. The signed-out app at `https://app.superlist.com/` was opened and read through its accessibility tree. Theme seen: light. Widths measured: 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, both at device pixel ratio 2. The T3 preview viewport during the first read was 1169 by 731.

Nothing was submitted. "Continue with email" was opened. The magic link was not sent. "Continue with Apple" was not followed. Pricing upgrade links were not opened. No card, phone, or company SSO was entered.

The preview browser did not produce screenshot frames. Page screenshots and crops are from headless Playwright on the public marketing URLs, which loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare challenge. The Flutter login has no screenshot.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page cream.
2. Do paint the primary action as a coral pill.
3. Do set headlines in Haffer XH and body in Inter.
4. Don't send the magic link or follow Apple to finish a capture.
5. Don't treat the Flutter input's CSS as the product field.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` in the T3 preview browser on the home page and on pricing, then checked again in headless Playwright at 1440. Type roles come from the live h1, h2 presets, plan names, prices, nav labels, and footer links. The primary fill is a `color(display-p3)` gradient. The preview canvas clipped those stops, so the token stops are pixels sampled from the 2x pill crop. Framer custom properties on `body` (`--token-*`) were recorded where a computed colour matched one.

## What to distrust

The pill tokens `#ff502f` and `#dc0000` are sampled pixels, not the authored display-p3 stops. The gradient still runs through a white label, so a sample through the middle of the control is the label, not the fill. Hover changed scale, not the gradient string. The Flutter email field's DOM styles are the browser default and are not product colours. The app body computed `rgb(35, 34, 51)` while the canvas was up. The declared page background in the app HTML is `#fafafc`. Those two disagree, and neither is stored as a marketing token. No dark theme was on the site.

## Fonts

Haffer XH (Regular, SemiBold, Bold, and italics) and Inter, both loaded from `framerusercontent.com`. The computed stacks include a Placeholder face that maps to local Arial. The files are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide falls back to Georgia and Inter.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the page cream.** Body background is `#fffdf9`. Sand cards and the peach band sit on it. The evidence is the computed body in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).
- **Do paint the primary action as a coral pill.** The fill is a vertical gradient. Sampled ends are `#ff502f` and `#dc0000`. Radius is 100px. The crop is [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Do set headlines in Haffer XH and body in Inter.** The home h1 is 68px Bold with -3px tracking. Hero body is Inter 18px.
- **Don't send the magic link or follow Apple to finish a capture.** Those controls were opened or seen and left alone.
- **Don't treat the Flutter input's CSS as the product field.** It computed as a default browser input.
