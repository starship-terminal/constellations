## Docs shell

A top bar holds the wordmark, a centered search field, Support, and Start a Project. Under it, Docs, Features, and Changelogs sit on a hairline. The left column is the page list. The current page is amber text with an amber icon. Content is a 749 px column at the 1169 px read, with the title at 36 px and the lead at 18 px.
