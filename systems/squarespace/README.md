## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export squarespace` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use black for the action on a light page and white for the action on a photo.
2. Keep marketing controls at 4px and login controls square.
3. Underline login fields.
4. Use the blue only for the offer and the recommended mark.
5. Do not invent a dark theme.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview tab on the home page (innerWidth 1169, then 1440), on /pricing, on /templates, and on login.squarespace.com. Cross-origin stylesheets on assets.squarespace.com blocked `cssRules`, so custom properties were not enumerated from those sheets. The live document exposed no colour, type, or radius custom properties. Tokens are the computed values.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so screenshots and crops are from headless Chromium on pages that loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare challenge. Hover on Get Started is from a real mouse hover in the preview tab.

## What to distrust

**Most values are hand entries.** [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) records the viewports, the pages and seven notes, and the notes hold four colour readings: the Get Started hover (`#dddddd`), the login error text (`#c32d38`), and the settled plan CTA and its text (`#000000`, `#ffffff`). Those four tokens keep their Measured label. The other 80 tokens were labelled Measured at capture with no reading kept, and on 2026-11-21 they were relabelled Reconstruction ("entered by hand at capture time; no raw/ file holds this reading"), with the original wording kept after it. No value changed. A recapture that writes computed-style readings into raw/ is needed to measure them, and `tools/test_tokens.py` fails if any other token claims a reading.

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json and raw/, not from the PNGs. The home hero photograph will band.

`core.typography.hero-mobile` rounds 42.4066px, 44.1028px, and -2.54439px to 42.41px, 44.10px, and -2.54px.

The login email node arrived with `aria-invalid=true` and an empty error span. `#c32d38` is that span's computed colour. No error sentence was shown.

The first pricing screenshot was taken while plan buttons were still gray. A later computed read showed those buttons at `#000000` with white type. The saved pricing shot is the recaptured frame.

Clarkson Serif, Canela, and the Noto faces were declared and did not load. Trust Clarkson, which `document.fonts` reported loaded at 300, 400, and 500.

The wordmark SVG uses `fill="currentColor"`. On the photo hero the painted mark is white. The saved SVG has no baked fill.

## Fonts

Clarkson is the marketing and login face. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Marketing tokens fall back to Helvetica, then the generic sans. Login tokens name Helvetica Neue before Helvetica and Arial.

## Do and don't

- **Use black for the action on a light page and white for the action on a photo.** Pricing and templates paint Get Started `#000000`. The home hero paints it `#ffffff`. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- **Keep marketing controls at 4px and login controls square.** Get Started is 60px with a 4px radius. Login fields, the LOG IN submit, and OAuth buttons have no radius. See [nav-get-started-default.png](components/crops/nav-get-started-default.png) and [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
- **Underline login fields.** The resting line is 1px `#e7e7e7`. The focused line is 2px `#0e0e0e`. See [login-email-default.png](components/crops/login-email-default.png).
- **Use the blue only for the offer and the recommended mark.** `#0072f0` is the promo badge and the Recommended pill, not the primary button.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** These pages did not offer one.
