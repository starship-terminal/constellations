# Squarespace design reference

A token set and component reference for the Squarespace marketing site (www.squarespace.com), captured signed out on 2026-11-21 in headless Chromium at 1440x900 and 390x844. The capture covers the homepage and its menus, pricing, templates, get started, website design, ecommerce, the feature index (viewport only), blog, contact, about, tools, and domains.squarespace.com. The login host was opened, recorded as a wall, and not committed.

The site paints one light page. The header and the open menu invert to black. Clarkson is the display face. Primary actions are black 60px bars with 4px corners. A blue wash badge marks the discount.

## How the values were read

Pages were loaded in headless Chromium with Playwright. Each shot has a `.png.capture.json` sidecar with `innerWidth`, `innerHeight` and `devicePixelRatio`. Desktop shots measure 1440 by 900 at 1x. Phone shots measure 390 by 844 at 2x.

Stylesheets are cross-origin (`assets.squarespace.com`). The CSSOM walk recorded `varCount` 0, so custom properties were not read. Tokens come from `getComputedStyle` on live elements: the homepage h1, h2 and eyebrow, the header GET STARTED control, the open Products menu, the promo banner, and the pricing trial button, buy button, badge, price and billing track. Those readings are in [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json). `tools/build_tokens.py` writes [tokens.json](tokens.json) from that file.

Nothing was submitted. Email was not typed. Google, Apple and Facebook were not clicked.

## What to distrust

- Hover, pressed and focus colours were not read. The homepage CTA hover shot exists. Its computed colour was not saved.
- The blue link role is the discount badge colour. The savings line on pricing looks the same blue in the screenshot. Its own computed colour was not isolated.
- Card fill on pricing was not isolated from the button group. Cards read as paper on paper.
- The feature-index full-page stitch was 44740px and was not kept. The viewport shot is the evidence for that page.
- The blog viewport shot stayed blank below the fold after a second capture. The header and the MAKING IT title painted. The post grid did not. Treat [screenshots/blog-light-default.png](screenshots/blog-light-default.png) as a partial.
- Contact cards painted as empty boxes with an icon and a label. Their bodies did not fill.
- Clarkson did not load in the headless run for some controls (the billing option computed as Arial). Those readings were not used as the type family.
- Currency on pricing rendered as SGD. That is the locale the headless browser got. It is not a token.
- Login screenshots were taken and then deleted from the package. They show the email and password form. They are not in `screenshots/`.

## Fonts

Clarkson (with a "Clarkson Fallback" face named on the pricing h1) is the marketing face. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Helvetica is the measured fallback. Body computed as the generic `sans-serif`.

## Do and don't

- **Do set display type in Clarkson light on paper.** The homepage h1 is 64px, weight 300, tracking tight, on white. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do invert the header when a menu opens.** The bar and the panel go black, the CTA goes white. [screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png)
- **Do make the primary action a 60px black bar.** Uppercase 14px medium, 4px corners, 23 by 28 padding. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Do keep the secondary action quiet.** BUY NOW has no fill. Its edge is a 2px inset shadow. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Do mark a discount with the blue wash badge.** Blue text on a light blue pill, not a second brand colour for chrome. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Don't treat the black menu as a dark theme.** The page under it stays paper. [screenshots/home-light-solutions-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-solutions-menu.png)

## Rebuild

```sh
cd systems/squarespace
PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs \
  node tools/capture/public/capture.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/squarespace/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

Remove `login-light-default.png` and `mobile-login-light-default.png` from `screenshots/` after quantize. They are the login wall.

Then at the repo root, `make design-md` refreshes the Brief and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). `make grade` rewrites Health. `make check` fails if either is stale. This capture does not edit [catalog.json](../../catalog.json). Register the package in a follow-up before those commands can pass for this folder.
