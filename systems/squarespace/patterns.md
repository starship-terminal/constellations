# Patterns

Public marketing pages and the signed-out login page, captured 2026-11-19. Desktop shots are innerWidth 1440. The phone shot is innerWidth 390.

## Home

The header is fixed, 80px tall, padding 0 40px, transparent over a photo hero. The wordmark is 208 by 30. PRODUCTS, SOLUTIONS, and RESOURCES sit in the center. LOG IN and a white Get Started sit on the right. The hero title is Clarkson 64px weight 300, white, letter-spacing -3.84px. A promo pill sits over the lower hero: surface `rgba(36, 36, 36, 0.9)`, radius 120px, blue badge `#0072f0`.

![Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

At 390 the header is 68px with 16px side padding. The wordmark collapses to the mark. Get Started stays white. The hero title computes to about 42.41px.

![Home, phone](screenshots/home-light-mobile.png)

## Pricing

The header inverts: black wordmark, black LOG IN, black Get Started. The title is 56px weight 300. Plan cards are 443px, radius 24px. The billing toggle is a pill track. Recommended is a blue pill on the Core card.

![Pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Templates

Same light header as pricing. The title is 56px weight 400, `#0e0e0e`. Search is a 660px shadowed field. Template cards are 8px radius on `#f9f9f9`. Filter checkboxes sit in a left column.

![Templates](screenshots/templates-light-default.png)

## Login

A white page. The mark is top left. CREATE ACCOUNT is top right, 12px uppercase. The form is two columns: underlined email and password on the left, OAuth buttons on the right, with OR between them. The LOG IN submit stays gray until the fields are filled. No card field is on this page.

![Login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)
