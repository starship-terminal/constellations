# Squarespace patterns

Public marketing site only. The signed-in editor, account dashboard and checkout were not entered.

## Shell

A sticky header, 80px, transparent over the hero and black once a menu opens. Logo left, PRODUCTS / SOLUTIONS / RESOURCES in the centre, LOG IN and GET STARTED on the right. A promo pill sits at the bottom of the first viewport.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

On a phone the header is a logo and a menu button. The menu is a black drawer with three accordions, LOG IN and GET STARTED pinned at the bottom.

[screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

## Pricing

A title, a savings line, a pill toggle (annual / monthly), then three plan cards. Each card has a name, a discount badge, a price, a black START FREE TRIAL and a quiet BUY NOW. The trial link goes to `/templates/get-started`. It was not followed into account creation.

[screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Get started

A split page. Left: a question and a search field that opens a topic list. Right: a photograph. NEXT sits at the bottom. Nothing was typed and NEXT was not pressed. This is the public onboarding step, not a submitted account.

[screenshots/get-started-light-default.png](screenshots/get-started-light-default.png)

## Contact

A title and two cards, Contact Support and Security Escalations. The cards were not opened. The footer is the black link grid used on short pages.

[screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png)

## Templates

A catalogue with a search field. Search was focused and not submitted.

[screenshots/templates-light-search-focused.png](screenshots/templates-light-search-focused.png)

## What was not captured

| Pattern | Why |
|---|---|
| Form validation | No form was submitted |
| Empty state | Not reached |
| Loading | Not held |
| Error | Not reached. The login wall is a boundary, not an error page |
| Destructive confirmation | Not present on the public site |
| Feedback after an action | Not reached. Nothing was submitted |
| Signed-in editor | `login.squarespace.com` shows email and password, or Google, Apple, or Facebook. Nothing typed |
