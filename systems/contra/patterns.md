The hero is "Get more creative" at 58px, with a prompt box and suggestion chips, then a row of topic cards. The phone home replaces the nav links with a menu icon and keeps Sign up.

Payments, partners, and challenges use an 80px marketing headline in GT Standard 6 L Regular. Log in is a white card on `#f5f6f9` with Continue with Google, an email field, and a pale Log in button.
