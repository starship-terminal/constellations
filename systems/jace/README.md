# Jace AI design reference

A token set and component reference for Jace AI's public surface, for building a cream marketing page with Gelica display type and pill controls, or a dark product shell that matches the sign-in card. It was read from **jace.ai**, **app.jace.ai/signin** and **app.jace.ai/signup** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

Jace is an AI email assistant for Gmail and Outlook. The marketing site is a Next.js / Tailwind v4 build that paints one light cream page (`#fffefd` sand, `#2d2d2d` ink, Geist Sans body, Gelica display). The product design system is a separate custom-property layer that loads on the same origin: bare `:root` is the dark product theme (`--surface-default` `#333437`, `--text-body-color-default` `#fff2e7`), and light overrides live in `:root:where(.light, .light *)`. The marketing site does not add class `light`. Sign-in at `app.jace.ai/signin` paints the dark product theme. The product light set is measured from that selector, not observed on a marketing page.

**Boundary:** the inbox, drafts, labels, Flows, settings and billing sit behind Google or Outlook OAuth. Sign-in offers Google, Outlook and an SSO portal only. There is no email field. Signup was photographed at the landing Continue button and not continued. Google, Outlook and SSO were never pressed. The sales form at `/contact/sales` was shown empty and not submitted. `login-extracted` is not-started.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export jace` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint marketing on sand with Geist body and Gelica display.
2. Do use 9999px pills on the marketing site and 6px to 8px radii on product controls.
3. Do treat lemon and ember as the brand, and zinc as the shadcn accent.
4. Do keep the product shell dark unless `html.light` is set.
5. Don't treat the marketing page as `html.light`.
6. Don't follow Get started, Google, Outlook or SSO and call the result public.
7. Don't treat "work email" as a field.
8. Don't copy the wordmark or the homepage SVGs into anything that ships.
9. Don't assume reduced motion stops the marketing motion.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Declared values come from the stylesheets. `tools/capture/vars.mjs` walked the sheets on `jace.ai` on 2026-11-21 and resolved custom properties twice: once on bare `:root` (dark product) and once with `html.light` applied (light product). Sign-in resolved 839 names. Prefixes kept include `--primitives-color-sand/obsidian/lemon/ember/deepsea`, `--primitives-spacing/radius/font-*`, `--theme-new-colors-*`, `--button-*`, `--surface-*`, `--text-*`, `--elevation-*` and `--motion-*`. A name the reader did not return is Not captured.

Computed values come from the running page. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` settled each component into default, hover, focus-visible and pressed (pressed via `CSS.forcePseudoState`, so no OAuth button was activated). Signup Continue was measured and not clicked. The sales field was focused empty. Nothing was typed, submitted, signed in, or sent through OAuth.

Marketing paint that the product tokens do not name is stored under `core.color.marketing` and `core.font.marketingSans` / `marketingDisplay`, from computed styles on `jace.ai/`.

Motion was probed with `prefers-reduced-motion` emulated, then written into `core.motion` by `tools/motion.py`. Home and pricing ignore reduced motion. Sign-in is partial. The combined verdict in [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json) is partial (112 moving, 6 removed, 106 kept).

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from the tokens.

## What to distrust

- **Signup readings taken past a consent step.** [signup-light-privacy](screenshots/signup-light-privacy.png), [signup-light-oauth](screenshots/signup-light-oauth.png), their `mobile-` twins, and the `signup-privacy-title`, `signup-privacy-continue`, `signup-oauth-title` and `signup-google` readings are from 2026-11-18, after an earlier capture clicked Continue and then Continue to signup. The 2026-11-21 refresh did not click either, so those screens were not recaptured. Treat them as a prior boundary, not as this refresh.

- **Marketing is not the product light theme.** `light.*` is `html.light`. The cream page uses Tailwind utilities and the computed marketing tokens. Do not treat `#fffefd` as `light.surface.default` (that role is `#fffefd` only because the light product surface happens to share sand-50). Body ink on the marketing site is `#2d2d2d` (`core.color.marketing.ink`), not `light.text.body.default` (`#3e3f40`).
- **`theme.accent` is not the brand.** `--theme-new-colors-accent` resolves to `#27272a` in both themes (a shadcn zinc). The painted brand is lemon `#ffdc61` (`--button-background-primary`) and ember `#ff7d55` (`core.color.ember.500`, `surface.brand`).
- **Product type is not what marketing paints.** Tokens name Inter Variable and Instrument Serif. The marketing page computes Geist Sans and Gelica.
- **Sign-in and signup have no email field.** Both cards are Google and Outlook (sign-in also has SSO). "Work email" is the heading, not a field. A work-email field in the style guide is a specimen of the sales field.
- **Pressed states are forced.** `CSS.forcePseudoState` applied `:active:hover`. Script-owned mousedown styles are missing.
- **The Stacey Helbig quote on the sign-in panel is published marketing copy.** It is not a private account.
- **The sales form ships with sample placeholders.** The 2026-11-21 page shot clears those placeholders before capture so the form reads empty. Nothing was typed into the fields. A filled state is Not captured.
- **Reduced motion is mostly ignored.** Home (62) and pricing (41) keep every moving element. Sign-in removes 6 of 9.
- **Elevation is declared, thinly painted.** `elevation.raised`, `floating` and `modal` come from `--elevation-*`. Marketing pills mostly use borders, not those shadows.
- **Five icon files are unnamed.** [icons/INDEX.tsv](icons/INDEX.tsv) labels seven by nearby button text and five as `icon`.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint marketing on sand with Geist body and Gelica display.** Page `#fffefd`, ink `#2d2d2d`, hero at weight 300. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do use 9999px pills on the marketing site and 6px to 8px radii on product controls.** See [nav-cta-default](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and [signin-oauth-default](components/crops/signin-oauth-default.png).
- **Do treat lemon and ember as the brand, and zinc as the shadcn accent.** Primary pill `#ffdc61`, ember `#ff7d55`. `theme.accent` `#27272a` is not the brand. See [hero-cta-default](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
- **Do keep the product shell dark unless `html.light` is set.** Sign-in and signup are `#333437` with `#fff2e7` text. See [signin-light-default](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Don't treat the marketing page as `html.light`.** The class is absent. Product light tokens are a stylesheet reading. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Don't follow Get started, Google, Outlook or SSO and call the result public.** Those leave the signed-out surface. See [signin-oauth-default](components/crops/signin-oauth-default.png).
- **Don't treat "work email" as a field.** Sign-in is Google, Outlook and SSO only. See [signin-light-default](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Don't copy the wordmark or the homepage SVGs into anything that ships.** They are Jace's marks. See [logo-default](components/crops/logo-default.png).
- **Don't assume reduced motion stops the marketing motion.** Home and pricing keep their transitions. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
