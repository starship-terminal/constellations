# Patterns

How the public pages combine. The map of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

A black page (`html` background `#000`), a wordmark, four links (How it works, Plugin, API, Pricing), a help icon, Sign in, and Start free. Desktop 1440 and phone 390. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png), [mobile-home-dark-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png).

The phone collapses the links behind Open menu. [mobile-home-dark-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-menu.png).

## Mix hero

A 72px headline, a mad-lib sentence whose words are tappable chips, Roll the dice, Presets, and Make my mix. The bowl on the right plays a published demo. This is the only interactive public surface. [home-dark-rolled.png](screenshots/home-dark-rolled.png), [home-dark-presets.png](screenshots/home-dark-presets.png).

## Signup gate

Make my mix, Download and Save open a dialog on the same page. Sign up free leaves for AuthKit and was not followed. [home-dark-signup-dialog.png](screenshots/home-dark-signup-dialog.png).

## Pricing

Plan cards in a row, one featured button in the sand accent, the others ghost. FAQ summaries expand in place. [pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png), [pricing-dark-faq-open.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-faq-open.png).

## Plugin download

Mac and Windows toggle the download target. The file links were not followed. [plugin-dark-default.png](screenshots/plugin-dark-default.png), [plugin-dark-windows.png](screenshots/plugin-dark-windows.png).

## Signed-out walls

`/account` and `/workspace-next` say sign in. The workspace hop continues to AuthKit. [account-dark-default.png](screenshots/account-dark-default.png).

## Empty

`/blog` returns the site's not-found page. [not-found-dark-default.png](screenshots/not-found-dark-default.png).
