# Patterns

## Marketing shell

A dark navy bar, #060419, holds the wordmark, three menus, Enterprise, Ad Network, Pricing, then Log in, Get a demo, and a white Sign up for free pill. The page below is the same navy. Display type is Clash Grotesk at 60px on the homepage and 64px on pricing. Body type is Satoshi. Pink #ff5ec4 is the one accent in the hero. Primary actions in the page body use fusion blue #2f39ba.

Shot: [home-desktop.png](screenshots/home-desktop.png), [home-phone.png](screenshots/home-phone.png).
