Clearful, declared and measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. Clearful's Webflow stylesheet declares a real design-token palette — the ten-step --base-color-brand--blue-* ramp, the grey ramp, four radius steps and a full semantic layer — so the core colors are those declarations resolved, and the roles are measured on the painted chrome: the #1953ff brand controls, the #1b1bbc hover step (shipped under a deleted variable), the #adc2ff CTA halo and the #d6e0ff brand-light bands.

One light theme across the Webflow marketing routes and the Tailwind /library surface; app.clearful.com is a Flutter canvas — its shades are pixel-sampled off the captured screenshot and live under core.sampled.app.*.
