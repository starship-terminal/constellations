# Clearful design reference

A token set and component reference for Clearful's public surface — for building clean light marketing pages and a simple blue-on-white control language in the style of the real thing. It was read from **clearful.com** on **2026-11-16**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844: the Webflow marketing routes (home, pricing, what's new, widgets, flow editor, contact, about, press, terms and privacy), the Tailwind `/library` guided-journals surface, `/journal-prompts`, and the public sign-in card on **app.clearful.com** as a boundary.

Clearful is worth a capture because it runs three honest stacks on one product. The **Webflow marketing site** (Manrope UI, Literatatt display) declares a real design-token palette in its stylesheet — a ten-step `--base-color-brand--blue-*` ramp, a grey ramp, four radius steps and a semantic `text-color--text-*`/`background-color--background-*`/`border-color--border-*`/`button-color--*`/`tabs-color--tabs-*` layer — one of the few captures where the declared names and the painted values can be checked against each other. It paints a **single light theme**: white `#ffffff` ground, `#f7f7f7` secondary bands, `#d6e0ff` brand-light accent sections, `#222` ink on `#1953ff` brand pills ringed by a 1px brand border — and the hero CTA carries a 6px `#adc2ff` halo. The **/library route** is a separate Tailwind build in Mulish with its own h1 and prompt cards on the same light theme. The **sign-in card** is a Flutter canvas app: controls were located and measured through its accessibility-semantics overlay at devicePixelRatio 1 (400x56 buttons, real rects), but the canvas paints its own colours — CSSOM reads only UA defaults there, so the app-surface shades are pixel-sampled off the captured screenshot and labelled as such. **Boundary:** the journal app itself, checkout and every OAuth action sit behind sign-in and were never crossed; nothing was typed, submitted or authenticated.

### Principles

1. Do keep one light surface.
2. Do put the brand blue on pills, not text.
3. Do ring the hero CTA.
4. Do pair Literatatt display with Manrope UI.
5. Don't underline links.
6. Don't fake the product.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Layout

The 1440 px page is a white canvas: a 1440 x 72 nav bar carries the wordmark, a Guided-Journals link, Log In and the 225 x 50 blue pill. The hero centres a 1024 px Literatatt headline — "Be a more thoughtful, resilient, and happier you with daily journaling" — with no lede, over the 289 x 82 haloed CTA. Sections alternate white, `#f7f7f7` secondary bands and one `#d6e0ff` brand-light band; feature cards are 1280 x 720 rounded-24 grey blocks. Pricing is a clean comparison: cadence tabs, a Free/Premium table on alternating grey rows, `$0`/`$9.99/mo` prices and blue Get-started pills. The `#f7f7f7` footer closes every page. At 390 px the nav collapses to a fixed bar and the menu opens as a full-height overlay.

## How the values were read

Every public surface was captured signed-out in headless Chromium (Playwright, `tools/capture/public/`). Per component the kit records the computed style plus the first painted child layers (`paint`), so fills living on child nodes are measured where they actually paint. States: `hover` real pointer, `focus-visible` real keyboard focus, `pressed` forced via CDP (never a real press), the billing tabs and mobile menu opened by their own toggles — nothing navigated, submitted or signed in. The stylesheet harvest reads the cross-origin Webflow CDN sheet by fetching and reparsing it (CSSOM alone cannot). The sign-in card is a Flutter canvas: its controls were measured through the semantics overlay after mounting the local "Enable accessibility" switch, at devicePixelRatio 1 (under DSF 2 Flutter lays out at half scale); its colours were sampled off the screenshot, not computed. Screenshots were quantized to 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from the tokens or raw files.

### Honest gaps

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **The journal app itself** — entries, editor, settings — sits behind sign-in; never crossed.
- **The sign-in card's computed styles** are unreadable by design: the canvas paints, so its values are pixel-sampled, labelled Sampled and kept under `core.sampled.app.*`.
- **/journal-prompts** threw a transient human-verification challenge on one pass and captured clean on retry; if it recurs the route is a boundary.
- **Disabled, error and loading states** do not appear on the captured public surface.
- **Several CTA anchors point at `#`** (e.g. the nav pill) — their destinations resolve in-app; the `#` targets were never followed.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep one light surface.** White `#ffffff` ground, `#f7f7f7` secondary bands, `#222` ink — no dark mode exists on the public site. Don't invert it. ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [feature card](components/crops/feature-card-default.png))
- **Do put the brand blue on pills, not text.** Buttons and active tabs take `#1953ff` with a 1px same-blue border and white label; body text stays `#222`. Don't tint paragraphs blue. ([nav CTA](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png), [plan CTA](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png))
- **Do ring the hero CTA.** The primary action carries a 6px `#adc2ff` (blue-200) halo proud of the pill. Don't ship the big CTA bare. ([hero CTA](components/crops/cta-default.png))
- **Do pair Literatatt display with Manrope UI.** Display headings set 60-72px/700 Literatatt; everything interactive sets Manrope; /library sets Mulish — a separate stack, not a third font choice. ([hero title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [library title](components/crops/library-h1-default.png))
- **Don't underline links.** Nav and footer links take colour/fill feedback — `#f7f7f7` wash on nav, `#1953ff` text on footer hover — never an underline. ([nav item hover](components/crops/nav-item-hover.png), [footer link hover](components/crops/footer-link-hover.png))
- **Don't fake the product.** The journal, editor and entry data live behind sign-in; the package holds only what the public pages paint. ([sign-in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png))
