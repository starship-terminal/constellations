# Patterns

## Marketing shell

A 72px white nav, logo left, one text link, Log In, and a blue pill. Footer repeats product links, legal links, and social marks. Desktop: [home-default](screenshots/home-default.png). Phone: the same links move into the drawer in [mobile-home-menu-open](screenshots/mobile-home-menu-open.png).

## Get-app gate

Every "start a journal" control opens the same overlay instead of creating an account on the marketing host. The overlay offers store buttons and a Web link that lands on the sign-in wall. [home-get-app](screenshots/home-get-app.png).

## Editorial page

Display type in Literatatt at 72px/700 on the home hero, Manrope for everything else, blue used once as the action. Library, prompts, about, and press follow the same shell. [library-default](screenshots/library-default.png), [about-default](screenshots/about-default.png).

## Help center

A second host, `support.clearful.com`, with its own nav (Browse, Popular, What's new, Open Clearful) and a search field. [support-default](screenshots/support-default.png).

## Sign-in wall

The app host stops at email, Apple, or Google. [sign-in-wall](screenshots/sign-in-wall.png). The branch was not continued.

## Form, empty, loading, error, destructive, onboarding

The contact form is the only public form. It was not submitted, so validation, empty, loading, and error states were not shown. No destructive confirmation and no onboarding appear signed out.
