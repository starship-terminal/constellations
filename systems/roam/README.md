# Roam Research

Roam Research is a note-taking tool for networked thought. This package captures the public site at roamresearch.com, signed out, on 22 November 2026.

Surfaces: the marketing homepage (hero, quotes, pricing, downloads, footer), the empty sign-in and sign-up walls, and the public `help` graph that Docs and the footer open. Theme: light. The marketing page offered no dark toggle. Widths: headless Chromium at 1440×900 (devicePixelRatio 1) and 390×844 (devicePixelRatio 2). No account was created. No email, password, name, phone, address, or code was typed. Google and Apple were not clicked. Trial and Join were not clicked. Desktop installers were not downloaded.

The public help graph shows a banner: "No changes to the help database will be saved. Sign up to get your own." That graph is published docs, not an account.

## Capture notes

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium opened `https://roamresearch.com/` at 1440×900. Computed styles were read on the live page: body, `#header`, the wordmark span, `.rm-nav-link`, the hero title and subcopy divs, `.rm-signup-button`, `.rm-chrome-callout`, `.rm-desktop-dl-button`, `.rm-plan`, `.rm-price-big`, `.rm-pricing-button`, `#footer`, and the empty `#/signin` `.bp3-button` and input. Phone width was 390×844 at devicePixelRatio 2. The T3 preview browser (incognito) mapped click handlers before the shots. A direct load of `#/app/help` paints only the astrolabe until the app boots from `/`, so help shots were taken after loading the homepage and then setting the hash.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from the files in [screenshots/](screenshots/). The hero product mock shows sample graph text from the marketing page ("October 12th, 2019", public names in the product art). That is the site's own artwork. The help graph is Roam's published docs, including staff-written pages. Neither is a user account.

The homepage declares a `--dark-*` custom-property set for the signed-in graph. Those names were not tokenised. No dark toggle was offered on the marketing page. Hover, focus-visible, and pressed were not measured. The favicon source was 48px, scaled to 128 with nearest neighbour.

## Fonts

Inter is the painted marketing face, loaded from the site's own `@font-face` (woff2). It is not committed. The wordmark uses Times New Roman. The body element computes to Segoe UI, Roboto, sans-serif, while the visible headings and controls compute to Inter. Fira Code loads for the graph editor and was not used on the marketing page.

## Do and don't

- **Keep the header dark and the page white.** The bar is `#343a40` and the page is `#ffffff`. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Use one blue for Sign Up and links.** `#137cbd`. The trial button is the lighter `#48aff0`. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Set the wordmark in Times New Roman and the UI in Inter.** [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Leave auth as an empty centred card.** Do not fill the fields. [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png)
- **Don't style a user graph from this package.** The help graph is public docs. The signed-in product was not entered. [screenshots/docs-light-sidebar.png](screenshots/docs-light-sidebar.png)
