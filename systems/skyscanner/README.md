# Skyscanner

Skyscanner is a travel search product. This package covers the public marketing homepage and its search form, signed out, on 19 November 2026. The start URL https://www.skyscanner.com/ redirected to https://www.skyscanner.com.sg/. Help was also read at https://help.skyscanner.net/hc/en-gb. Theme: light only. Widths measured: 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. No account was used. The search field and the login email field were not filled. No booking, card, phone or SSO was entered.

## How the values were read

The homepage was opened in the T3 preview browser. `getComputedStyle` on `document.documentElement` returned the Backpack custom properties (`--bpk-*`). Declared `:root` rules in the sheets were a dark canvas. The computed values on the painted page were the light theme, and those are the ones stored.

Component values were read from the painted elements at innerWidth 1440, innerHeight 900, device pixel ratio 2, visual viewport 1440 by 900 at scale 1. The same page was then read at innerWidth 390, innerHeight 844, device pixel ratio 2. Help was read at innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731.

The cookie banner was dismissed with "Accept essential only". The origin control was opened and closed. The travellers popover was opened and left. A login modal that appeared was closed. No form was submitted.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are missing. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so layout claims rest on computed boxes, not images.

The body background is transparent. Page white is `Homepage_backgroundContainer`. The hero and footer are `#05203c`.

`--bpk-radius-nav-tabs` is 1.125rem (18px). The painted tab radius is 18px, so the token matches the paint. `--bpk-radius-sm` is 0.5rem and the buttons are 8px.

`--bpk-private-button-dimension-min-height-large` is 3rem (48px). The desktop Search button is 72px. The phone Search button and the modal Continue button are 48px. The 72px height is a search-control size, not the Backpack large-button token.

The first painted `.bpk-text--body-default` was a field label at weight 700. Body copy on the page is 16px at weight 400, line-height 20.8px.

Ease and ease-in-out in the motion tokens are the CSS keyword expansions. The page reported the keywords, not cubic-bezier control points.

Help Centre is a Zendesk skin. Its link blue `#0a6cff` is not the homepage accent `#0062e3`.

Skyscanner Relative files are on js.skyscnr.com (`SkyscannerRelative-Book`, Medium, Bold, Black, and italics). They are not in this package.

## Fonts

Skyscanner Relative is the only family on the homepage and on Help. Weights read: 400, 700 and 900. A 500 face is declared and was not the computed weight of a measured element. The family is proprietary. Tokens name it and fall back to system-ui. The files are not committed.

## Do and don't

**Put the search form on an ink hero.** The homepage search sits on `#05203c`, with white fields and a blue Search button. [patterns.md](patterns.md) shows the shell.

**Use blue for the one search action.** Search and the selected Flights tab are `#0062e3`. Log in stays on the canvas fill. [components.md](components.md) has the button sizes.

**Keep field rows at 72px on desktop and 48px on phone.** The desktop Search button matches the row. The phone button is full width. [components.md](components.md) records both.

**Round the search group at 12px.** Fields, Search, cards and the modal share that radius. Chips and the smaller buttons are 8px. Tabs are 18px.

**Leave booking and account creation out.** This capture stopped at the empty form and the closed login modal.
