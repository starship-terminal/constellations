# Patterns

## App shell

The homepage hero is `#05203c`. At 1440 by 900 it is 476px tall. At 390 by 844 it is 420px tall with 16px padding. The page behind it is white. The footer is the same ink, text `#eff3f8`, padding 0 48px on desktop and 0 16px on phone.

The wordmark, Help, culture (English UK, Singapore, SGD) and Log in sit on the hero. Flights, Stays and Cars are a tab group under the heading.

## Search

The search form is the product. Desktop fields are a 72px white row: origin radius 12px on the start, travellers radius 12px on the end, Search as a 72px featured button with radius 12px. Phone stacks the fields at 48px with radius 12px on the top of each block, and Search is full width.

Opening origin focuses an input labelled "Country, city or airport". The field was not filled and Search was not submitted. No booking, card or account was created.

## Cookie banner

A banner titled "We value your privacy" covers the bottom. Accept all is primary. Accept essential only is secondary. Essential only was the choice used. Privacy Settings in the footer was not opened.

## Login boundary

A modal offers Continue, Log in with Google, Apple and Facebook. It was closed. The email field was present and not filled. No phone, card or SSO was entered.

## FAQ

The homepage lists questions in an accordion. Rows were measured closed.

## Help

help.skyscanner.net is a separate Zendesk skin. Body 20px on 30px, ink `#05203c`, white page. The search field is 60px, radius 24px. Links are `#0a6cff`. It does not reuse the homepage button sizes.

## Empty, loading, error, destructive

Not shown. Search was not run, so there is no results list, no empty state and no error state.
