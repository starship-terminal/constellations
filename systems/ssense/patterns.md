## Home

A 55px fixed header sits over a white page. Left links are Menswear, Womenswear, Everything else, and Search. The wordmark is centred. Right links are language, Login, Wishlist, and Bag (0). Below the header, editorial tiles use a 68px label column and Favorit SSENSE Inter1 headlines at 19px, with JHA Times Now decks at 20px. Shop menswear and Shop womenswear are the only rounded controls on the page (10px). The footer repeats the 11px uppercase chrome: customer care, location, editorial archive, careers, country, email signup, social links, and legal links.

## Listing

Menswear is a three-column page at this width: a left rail (Sale, Categories, Designers), a product grid, and empty right space in the grid template. Cards are 2:3 images with an 11px brand, name, and price stack. Sale prices keep the current price black and strike the previous price in rgb(136, 136, 136). A "Search Designers" field sits in the rail at 150 by 30px on rgb(251, 251, 251).

## Product

The buy box is a brand link, a two-line 11px title, the price with "Taxes and duties included." in rgb(136, 136, 136), a 36px size select, then a 35px row with Add to bag (black) and Add to wishlist (text). The size value was not changed and Add to bag was not clicked. Item copy and a "You may also like" heading (11px uppercase) sit further down.

## Search

Opening Search mounts a 450 by 151px dialog under the header: department text buttons, a black-bordered field with a grey placeholder, Close, and a SALE ONLY checkbox. Results for the public query `rick` used the same card as the listing and added the line "SHOWING RESULTS FOR 'RICK'" at 11px.

## Login

The page is one column, 380px wide, starting at x 395. The heading is "LOGIN OR CREATE ACCOUNT" at 11px uppercase with 0.09px letter-spacing. Under it, an email label, a 30px field, and a 37px Continue button, 25px below the field. Continue was not clicked.

## Customer care

A left rail of 11px links under 13px weight-700 group titles, and a 680px answer column. The FAQ title is 15px. Questions are bold 11px paragraphs; answers are regular 11px paragraphs with 10px bottom margin. The first answer was already expanded.

## Editorial

The article header is a 525px column. The title is Favorit SSENSE Inter1 at 49.875px / 56px, letter-spacing -2px, uppercase, weight 100, color rgb(51, 51, 51). The deck is JHA Times Now at 40px / 44px. Body copy in the article is JHA Times Now at 30px / 34px. The byline is 20px Times Now.
