Hitchd, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. hitchd.com declares almost no custom properties of its own (~10 radio-control flags in the guest registry app), so the token layer is measured: computed styles and bounding boxes read on real elements of the signed-out public pages, each leaf naming its measurement. The voice is a hairline display serif (DDNR) over Inter Variable on warm paper; the guest registry app sets its own scale. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

Hitchd's measured palette is restrained: a near-black ink on white and warm cream, white type over photography, the grey of the disabled submit and field borders, and whatever the registry's cover art carries. Measured role values alias the palette entries they match.

Type

Two voices: the DDNR hairline serif at display sizes (88px hero) and Inter Variable for everything functional — 21px leads, 16px UI, 14px helper. The guest registry sets its couple names in its own classic display face (96px on the demo registry) — one of ~20 registry faces couples pick from. The DDNR and Inter Variable woff2 files are linked from hitchd.com, not vendored.
