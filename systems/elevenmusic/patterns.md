# ElevenMusic patterns

How the measured components combine on elevenmusic.io's public surface, read from the screenshots and the component entries in [components.md](components.md). The app paints dark by default (`html.dark`) — the light theme is a footer toggle that rebinds the same roles — so values below are the dark set unless a light shot is named.

## The app shell

Every page is the same shell ([home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)): a fixed **248px left rail** ([sidebar](components/crops/sidebar-default.png)) on the `--sidebar` field, a **52px header** ([header](components/crops/header-default.png)) over a translucent `lab(0 0 0 / .95)` strip, and the page content scrolling inside one inner element — the document itself never scrolls, which is why the `-full` shots expand the inner scroller first.

The rail carries, top to bottom: the **ElevenMusic wordmark** ([logo](components/crops/logo-default.png), 124x32), the 30px **Search** icon button ([icon-button](components/crops/icon-button-default.png) — it opens the sign-in dialog, so search itself is gated), the primary nav — 224x36 rows ([sidebar-nav](components/crops/sidebar-nav-default.png)) of Explore / Create / Library / Tools / Sounds plus the gated Composer and Chat buttons ([sidebar-action](components/crops/sidebar-action-default.png)) — the **Publish your music** promo card ([sidebar-promo](components/crops/sidebar-promo-default.png)) on the surface-1 fill, and the Playlists list of 224x50 media rows ([playlist-row](components/crops/playlist-row-default.png)). On a page's own route its row holds the selected fill ([sidebar-nav-selected](components/crops/sidebar-nav-selected-selected.png)). A collapse icon and a `New playlist` plus sit in the chrome.

The header holds the nav pills — **Explore / Resources▾ / Pricing** at 28px tall ([nav-link](components/crops/nav-link-default.png)) — then **Sign in** ([signin-btn](components/crops/signin-btn-default.png), a quiet surface-2 pill) and **Start free** ([cta-btn](components/crops/cta-btn-default.png), the inverted white primary). Resources opens a 274x194 popover dropdown ([nav-dropdown](components/crops/nav-dropdown-default.png)) of 20px menu links ([menu-link](components/crops/menu-link-default.png)), captured open in [home-dark-menu](screenshots/home-dark-menu.png).

## The composer

The home page is a creation surface, not a marketing page: the waldenburg-light **"Make music yours."** headline ([hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), 40px at 1440) over a muted lede, then the composer itself — a hairline pill row holding the black **+** Attach circle ([attach-btn](components/crops/attach-btn-default.png), 36x36), the 556px transparent prompt field ([composer-field](components/crops/composer-field-default.png), 36px tall), and the white **Create** send circle ([send-button](components/crops/send-button-default.png)) — over a row of suggestion chips ([prompt-chip](components/crops/prompt-chip-default.png), 28px `lighten-100`/`composer-chip` pills: Trap beat, Loop to chop, Topline…). Under it, the Songs/Sounds segmented control ([seg-tab-on](components/crops/seg-tab-on-default.png), the selected segment on a lighter fill), a demo track list of 614x52 rows ([track-row](components/crops/track-row-default.png)), and stem cards ([demo-card](components/crops/demo-card-default.png)). The same composer pattern repeats on /explore ([explore-input](components/crops/explore-input-default.png)) under a gated Create/Composer/Styles/Sounds pill nav ([create-nav](components/crops/create-nav-default.png)). Every send and Attach was measured, never pressed — a prompt is a generation request.

## Shelves, cards and the carousel

Explore is shelves of 208px media cards ([track-card](components/crops/track-card-default.png)): an artwork square, a title line, a muted artist line ([artist-link](components/crops/artist-link-default.png)), the `v2.5` model badge ([version-badge](components/crops/version-badge-default.png), 34x16) and a Remix chip ([remix-chip](components/crops/remix-chip-default.png)). Hover raises the white 32px play circle ([play-track](components/crops/play-track-hover.png)). The featured shelf is a 328px carousel card ([feature-card](components/crops/feature-card-default.png)) driven by frosted 32px arrows ([carousel-prev](components/crops/carousel-prev-default.png)) and 6px dots ([carousel-dot](components/crops/carousel-dot-default.png), the active one stretched into a 32x6 pill). Genres & Moods is the same pattern as 192px artwork tiles ([genre-card](components/crops/genre-card-default.png)) with a 25px play chip ([play-genre](components/crops/play-genre-default.png)). Shelf headings are small caps-quiet text ([shelf-heading](components/crops/shelf-heading-default.png)).

## The player bar

Pressing a card's own Play mounts the **72px bottom transport** ([explore-dark-playing](screenshots/explore-dark-playing.png), [player-bar](components/crops/player-bar-default.png)): track art and title at the left, the white pause circle ([player-play](components/crops/player-play-default.png)) between unlabeled prev/next ([player-prev](components/crops/player-prev-default.png)/[player-next](components/crops/player-next-default.png)), then repeat, volume, the gated Remix, a React-to-this-moment button, queue, visualizer and details at the right ([player-repeat](components/crops/player-repeat-default.png), [player-volume](components/crops/player-volume-default.png), [player-queue](components/crops/player-queue-default.png), [player-remix](components/crops/player-remix-default.png)). A 4px seek rail spans the bar's top ([player-progress](components/crops/player-progress-default.png), 328px of it visible). Playback is local; every social or library control on the bar is gated and was never pressed.

## Charts

/charts is a ranked list under an 83px headline ([charts-title](components/crops/charts-title-default.png)) with the white 40px **Play** pill ([chart-play](components/crops/chart-play-default.png)), a 40px **Shuffle** ([shuffle-btn](components/crops/shuffle-btn-default.png)) and a 32px **Share** ([share-btn](components/crops/share-btn-default.png)). Each 810x40 row ([chart-row](components/crops/chart-row-default.png)) is rank, art, title, artist ([chart-artist](components/crops/chart-artist-default.png)) and the gated Like/Remix icon buttons ([like-btn](components/crops/like-btn-default.png), [remix-btn](components/crops/remix-btn-default.png)) — measured, never pressed. The page scrolls past one GPU texture, so the full shot is tiled `charts-dark-full.part0/.part1`.

## Pricing

/pricing pairs two **472x909 plan cards** ([plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png), [plan-card-pro](components/crops/plan-card-pro-default.png)) under "Free to start. Pro when you're ready." — Free on the dark surface with a hairline, Pro as the product's one inverted white island with the `$9.99` price figure ([plan-price](components/crops/plan-price-default.png)) and checked 47px feature rows ([plan-feature](components/crops/plan-feature-default.png)). The CTAs invert with their cards: **Start free** is white-on-dark, **Get Pro** dark-on-white, both 44px pills ([plan-cta-free](components/crops/plan-cta-free-default.png), [plan-cta-pro](components/crops/plan-cta-pro-default.png)). Below, the FAQ is a 960x68 border-top accordion row ([faq-item](components/crops/faq-item-expanded.png), measured expanded).

## The auth wall

/login and /sign-in show the same centred **384px column** ([auth-card](components/crops/auth-card-default.png), [login-dark-default](screenshots/login-dark-default.png)): the "Log in or sign up" title ([auth-title](components/crops/auth-title-default.png)), the 384x40 surface-1 SSO buttons ([sso-google](components/crops/sso-google-default.png), [sso-apple](components/crops/sso-apple-default.png)), the legal line with underlined terms links ([tos-link](components/crops/tos-link-default.png)), and **Show other options** ([options-btn](components/crops/options-btn-default.png)) which expands the email form in place — the 376x36 field ([email-field](components/crops/email-field-default.png)), the 16px terms checkbox ([tos-checkbox](components/crops/tos-checkbox-default.png)) and the disabled Continue ([continue-btn](components/crops/continue-btn-default.png)) — captured open in [login-dark-options](screenshots/login-dark-options.png). The sidebar Search button opens the same gate as a two-pane dialog — "Studio-grade. Fully licensed." artwork beside the form ([home-dark-search](screenshots/home-dark-search.png)). Nothing was submitted; no OAuth button was pressed.

## The footer

Every page ends in a footer inside the shell ([footer](components/crops/footer-default.png), 1186x708): the English locale button ([lang-btn](components/crops/lang-btn-default.png)), Browse/Genres/Company/Legal/Social columns of 36px links ([footer-link](components/crops/footer-link-default.png)), the Cookie-settings button ([cookie-btn](components/crops/cookie-btn-default.png)) and the **System/Light/Dark segmented control** ([theme-seg](components/crops/theme-seg-default.png)) whose 24px segments ([theme-dark-btn](components/crops/theme-dark-btn-default.png)) produce the light theme — see [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) for the same shell on warm paper.
