# ElevenMusic patterns

## App shell

A left sidebar (Explore, Create, Library, Tools, Sounds, Composer, Chat, playlists) plus a top bar (Explore, Resources, Pricing, Sign in, Start free). The main column is a rounded panel on black. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

At 390 the sidebar is gone. Search, Start free, and a menu button sit in the top bar. [screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png).

## Create prompt

A single line, suggestion chips, and an attach control. Chips fill the line. Attach asks for a library file, an upload, or a recording, then stops at login.

## Catalog

Explore, tracks, charts, artists, genres, and playlists are public. Playback works signed out. Like, Follow, Remix, and Download stop at the login wall.

## Pricing

Two plans, Free and Pro, with Start free and Get Pro. Both stop at [https://elevenmusic.io/login?redirect=%2Fstudio](https://elevenmusic.io/login?redirect=%2Fstudio). [screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

One wall, used as a dialog and as `/login`. Google and Apple first. Email is behind Show other options and stays disabled until an address is entered. That step was not taken. [screenshots/login-dark-default.png](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).

## Empty, loading, error, destructive confirmation, onboarding

Not present on the public pages, or not reached because they sit behind the login wall.
