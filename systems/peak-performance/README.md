# Peak Performance

Peak Performance is a retail outerwear shop. This package covers the public signed-out Sweden English storefront at [https://www.peakperformance.com/se/en/](https://www.peakperformance.com/se/en/), which is where [https://www.peakperformance.com/](https://www.peakperformance.com/) redirected on 2026-11-19. Surfaces read: the home page and the women's jackets collection, including the filter sheet. Desktop only, measured at 1440×900, `devicePixelRatio` 2. One theme: light. No account was created. Nothing was added to the bag.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://www.peakperformance.com/` and landed on `https://www.peakperformance.com/se/en/`. After a freeform resize request, `window.innerWidth` was 1440, `window.innerHeight` was 900, `devicePixelRatio` was 2, and `visualViewport.scale` was 1. `color-scheme` was `normal`. `document.documentElement.lang` was `en-SE`.

Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live page: body, header bands, nav links, search, hero title, pills, product tiles, prices, footer links, then the jackets collection h1, breadcrumb, toolbar and filter sheet. The OneTrust banner was dismissed with "Use only necessary cookies" before those reads. "Accept all cookies" was not clicked.

The same-origin stylesheet `frontend/Peak/Peak2024/default/css/styles.min.css` (`version1794987467`) was readable in the CSSOM and again over HTTP after the browser was blocked. Type-scale classes (`.t-xs` through `.t-9xl`), `.btn`, `.btn-sand`, `.field._error`, `.field._success` and the transition durations come from that file. A copy is in [raw/styles.min.css](raw/styles.min.css).

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on the first snapshot, so no PNG was saved. A later navigation to a product URL, and then a fresh tab, returned PerimeterX "Access to this page has been denied". Readings taken before that block were kept. Nothing after it was invented.

## What to distrust

The hero title's 96px size was computed on a live node. The 80px `.t-8xl` step and the Mercury Display quote sizes were only in the stylesheet. They are labelled that way in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

Body text at 14px reports letter-spacing `-0.36px` on the product name and price, and `normal` on `document.body`. The body token uses the name and price reading.

`light.background.page` is `#ffffff` because the nav, bottom bar and filter sheet paint white. `body` and `.page-wrapper` themselves compute to transparent. A hero photograph shows through the transparent page behind the title. That photograph is not a token.

Hover italic, the primary hover veil, and the error and success border colours are stylesheet rules. No pointer was held on a control while those computed.

The filter colour dots' fills (`#000000`, `#255793`, `#7b4e2d`, `#686a4e`, `#828588`) are per-product inline styles, not a brand ramp. They are not tokens.

[screenshots/tokens-light-plate.png](screenshots/tokens-light-plate.png) is a plate of the measured colours (lime banner, white header, black, sand). It is not a photograph of the shop. The preview browser never produced a frame.

## Do and don't

**Set the promo bar in lime `#effda4`, 30px, black 12px uppercase.** That is `.header__rotating-banner` on the home page. The plate is [screenshots/tokens-light-plate.png](screenshots/tokens-light-plate.png).

**Keep the page white and the type black.** Nav, bottom bar and the filter sheet are `#ffffff`. Text is `#000000`. The muted line is `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`.

**Use the 24px pill for every action.** Hero links, Clear filters and Show results share radius 24px, 12px uppercase Helvetica Now Text, letter-spacing -0.36px. The filled one is black with white type. The outline one is transparent with a 1px black or white border.

**Set display type in Helvetica Now Display, weight 400, tight tracking.** The hero is 96px with letter-spacing -2.88px. The collection title is 64px with -0.64px. Section titles are 32px with -0.32px. All uppercase.

**Leave product tiles square and flush.** The jackets grid is four 360px cards with no gap, no radius and no shadow. The image is about 359×479.

**Do not invent a dark theme.** The public shop did not ship one. `color-scheme` on the document was `normal`.

**Do not draw a drop shadow on the filter sheet.** The 450px sheet is flat white with a 1px `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)` edge.
