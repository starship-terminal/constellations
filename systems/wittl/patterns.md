# Wittl patterns

The only rendered surface is the public homepage from the 2025-11-08 archive. Screenshots: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) at CSS 1440, [screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png) at CSS 390.

## Page

A white header holds the wordmark, Features, Pricing, Blog, Updates, and a stone Login button. The hero is centred: a fluid FF More Pro headline, a muted 20px lede, a forest "Get started" button and a "Talk to Founders" mail link. Below that, a two-column companion block, a grid of feature cards, a testimonial row, an FAQ accordion, and a fixed ink footer that the live page reveals by scrolling. In this render the footer stayed at opacity 0.

The page background is `#fcfcfc`. The scrolling shell is white with a 40px bottom radius and a 100vh margin, which is the gap the footer is meant to fill.

## Widths

At 1440 the nav content width is 1024px. At 390 the display size drops to 31.842px, feature grids stack, and a 46px menu button replaces the inline nav links. The phone drawer itself was not opened.

## What is not here

Pricing columns, job pages, career-site blocks and the signed-in pipeline were linked from the homepage and not captured. There is no email form on the homepage, so no signup was attempted. `app.wittl.co` was not opened.
