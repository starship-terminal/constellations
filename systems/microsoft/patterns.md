# Patterns

Public homepage only. Signed out. 2026-11-21.

## App shell

A 54px white universal header: logo, product links, All Microsoft, Search, Cart, Sign in. A purple promo bar sits under it. The hero is a pale wash with a 48px heading and a blue Shop now. A carousel control and five shop links sit under the hero. The footer is six link columns on a grey band, then a legal row and a black Back to top pill.

## Navigation

All Microsoft opens in place. It does not navigate. See [home-light-menu-all-microsoft.png](screenshots/home-light-menu-all-microsoft.png). At 390 the hamburger opens a stacked list. See [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Search

Search replaces the product links with a field and Cancel. See [home-light-search.png](screenshots/home-light-search.png). The field was not submitted.

## Product index

Explore Microsoft products opens /en-sg/microsoft-products-and-apps on the same host. It keeps the universal header and adds category links and an Animation switch. See [products-light-default.png](screenshots/products-light-default.png).

## Login wall

Sign in leaves www.microsoft.com for a Microsoft account wall. That wall was not photographed and nothing on it was submitted. See [login-review.json](login-review.json).
