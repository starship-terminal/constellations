helloivy.co, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. Hello Ivy ships two unrelated design layers on its public surfaces: the marketing site (a Framer build) whose whole theme is five hashed --token-* design tokens declared on <body> and re-pointed per theme — the cream ground #faf6f0, the black ink, the muted ink, and the inverse surface/ink pair the pill buttons paint — plus the --framer-link-* roles; and the app (a Vue SPA) whose semantic layer arrives as an inline theme block on the share page's section.main_section: the #3F40F2 indigo (--main-color/--link-color/--checkbox), the --app-color/--canvas/--sidebar/--hover/--book/--comment/--project ramp, --selection/--text-selection, --scrollBar*, and the shell dimensions --sidebar-width/--header-height. The measured geometry and type composites (component.*, core.typography.*) are read off the live DOM. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from www.helloivy.co, app.helloivy.co (/login at rest — never signed in — and the public share route) and the hosted /docs, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 (mobile pass at 390×844). The marketing site boots light (html[toggle-theme=light]) and its own footer "Change theme" control repaints it dark (localStorage.currentToggleState) — that verified repaint is the dark set. The app paints light only on its public routes; its core.app.* values are the share page's own default palette, no dark inferred. /docs is a third platform (General Sans) that shares neither layer and was captured light-only. Unbounded, Inter and General Sans are not bundled: samples fall back to system fonts. Post-auth surfaces are a documented boundary, not captures.

The surfaces

www.helloivy.co is the Framer marketing site: a 116px nav strip (wordmark, links, a black pill "Start for free" CTA) over Unbounded display headings on the cream field, with a full-screen Framer menu overlay and a footer "Change theme" control. app.helloivy.co is the product shell the public share route exposes: a --sidebar-width 242px rail beside the document canvas under a 66px header, in the app's own semantic palette.

Palette

The roles under light.* are the marketing site's painted default (cream ground #faf6f0, black ink, the #0099ff body link); dark.* resolves the same five --token-* declarations after the footer "Change theme" control repaints the page (ground #16161a, ink #edecda, muted #666662) — verified on the repainted page, not emulated. core.app.color.* is the app's own semantic layer — the share page's inline workspace theme (the #3F40F2 indigo, the warm-grey canvas/sidebar/hover/book ramp, the #fdffe4 comment tint, the selection pair, the scrollbar ramp) — light-only: the product paints no dark pass on its public routes, so none is inferred.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

No spacing scale is declared on the public surfaces: what exists is two corner values — the marketing pill (radius.pill, 9999px) and the product's control corner (radius.field, 10px on inputs and buttons) — the font.size.* ramp measured off painted text, the app shell's declared dimensions (app.layout.*: 242px sidebar, 66/65px headers), and component.*, the measured chrome: the 116px nav strip, the 56px CTA pill, the 500px login card, the share header. motion.* is the measured set from the reduced-motion probe (docs/decisions/0001): the durations and easing curves elements actually run, and the probe's verdict on what prefers-reduced-motion changes — on this site's sampled movers, nothing.

Shadows

Type

Three faces across three surfaces: Unbounded is the marketing display face (headings only), Inter is the marketing UI face and the whole product face (--main-font-family on the app), and General Sans belongs to the hosted docs platform. The measured ramp runs 12–82px (font.size.*), weights 400–700 (font.weight.*). The core.typography.* composites are the styles measured off the live DOM: the 82/90 hero display, the 27px lead, the nav link, the FAQ row, the app's field and button labels, the docs nav link, the share document body. Samples fall back to system sans because the faces are not bundled.
