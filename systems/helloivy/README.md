# Hello Ivy design reference

A token set and component reference for Hello Ivy's signed-out surfaces — for building a project-management product, marketing pages and document-sharing experiences in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.helloivy.co**, **app.helloivy.co** and the hosted **/docs** platform on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the marketing routes — /, /about, /features plus /features/tasks, /features/annotate and /features/client-portal, /pricing, /project-management, /project-collaboration-software, /notion-alternative, /blog and /blog/announcing-hello-ivy-2-0, /release-notes, /terms — the hosted docs index and the 'create a project' guide, the app /login card at rest, and the public share document a link exposes — in the painted light theme, with verified dark repaints on home, about, features, pricing and blog through the footer's real 'Change theme' control; the Framer 'Menu' overlay (desktop and the 390px drawer), an opened pricing FAQ row, the share page's settings popover and the footer theme control as transient states; plus 390px passes on home, features, pricing, blog and the app login.

helloivy.co is worth a capture because its public surfaces declare two unrelated design layers. The **marketing site** is a Framer build whose whole theme is five hashed design tokens declared on `<body>` — the cream ground **#faf6f0** (`--token-8cbad9a1…`), the black ink (`--token-3ea41072…`), the muted ink (`--token-38cd3004…`), and the inverse surface/ink pair (`--token-fb7fb35d…` white/`--token-c6b5aded…` black) the pill buttons paint — re-pointed under `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` and by the footer's own **'Change theme'** control (`html[toggle-theme=dark]`, `localStorage.currentToggleState`), plus the `--framer-link-*` roles (#0099ff body links). The **app** is a Vue SPA on another system entirely: its semantic layer arrives as an inline `style=` theme block on the share page's `section.main_section` — the **#3F40F2** indigo (`--main-color`, `--link-color`, `--checkbox`), the warm-grey ramp (`--app-color` #37352f ink, `--canvas` white, `--canvas-color` #323235, `--sidebar` #F5F6F4, `--hover` #F3F5F7, `--book` #F5F6FA, `--comment` #fdffe4, `--selection`/`--text-selection`, the `--project` greys, `--scrollBar*`, `--image-shadow`, `--book-icon`), `--main-font-family` Inter, and the shell dimensions (`--sidebar-width` 242px, `--header-height` 66px, `--main-header-height` 65px). The **/docs** platform is a third surface — General Sans, its own chrome — light-only. **Boundaries:** the workspace and every post-auth surface behind /login was never entered; the login card was measured at rest, fields never typed, 'Continue' never pressed; the share document's settings popover was opened through its own control and nothing was submitted, printed or mailed. Nothing was signed into, typed, searched, sent, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides, and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): every same-origin CSS response plus the inline CSSOM (Framer's SSR inline sheets, the app's index-*.css) is reparsed, rules are binned theme/skins/app/vendor/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets under emulated `prefers-color-scheme: light` and `dark` — the same re-pointing the footer control paints live. The app's semantic names resolve through the share page's inline `section.main_section` theme, recorded under `appTheme`. `tokens.json` never publishes an unverified value — every colour is the painted value.
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link can fire), opened/focused/dark states via the component's own control (the FAQ row, the menu overlay, the settings popover, the footer 'Change theme' control).
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`) verify the painted theme before writing a sidecar: the html `toggle-theme` attribute and the sampled background must agree. `-full` captures are bounded; the bound and any stitching are recorded in each `.capture.json` sidecar.
- **Dark states** are painted only through the footer's real 'Change theme' control — verified by the repainted surface (html attribute + computed `rgb(22,22,26)` body) before each reading, then switched back. The app and docs surfaces paint light only; no dark values were inferred for either.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label, and records where each was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.

## What to distrust

- **The workspace and everything behind /login**: the auth wall was never crossed — the real product surfaces (projects, clients, files, settings) are boundaries, not captures. The login card's fields were measured at rest and 'Continue' was never pressed.
- **The app's semantic layer on /login**: the `--*-color` names resolve only where `section.main_section` carries its inline theme — the public share route. The login card's roles are measured paint, not the themed layer.
- **`--vc-*` and `--fc-*` variables**: v-calendar and FullCalendar vendor internals from the app bundle — captured in `raw/css-vars.json` but kept out of tokens as widget internals, not product roles.
- **Reduced motion**: the probe's sampled movers kept moving under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — recorded as behaviour `ignores` in `raw/motion.json`, not suppression.
- **Font rendering**: Unbounded, Inter and General Sans are declared and measured, not shipped; crops show them, the style guide falls back to system fonts.
- **Body link colour**: `color.link` `#0099ff` is the `--framer-link-text-color` the default rich-text preset declares (five rules); two other presets point it at the ink tokens, and which preset paints a body link was not measured. `font.weight.bold` 700 and `font.size.xs` 12px were entered by hand (no text node paints them); every other size, weight and family is read from [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json), and `font.size.xl` is the plan-card title's measured 20px.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Uncaptured marketing routes**: the remaining feature and alternative pages were probed (same chrome and theme layer); three feature pages and the Notion alternative stand in for the set.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint light, and let the footer carry dark.** The marketing site boots `html[toggle-theme=light]` — cream `color.ground`, black `color.ink` ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); dark is a real painted surface behind the footer's 'Change theme' control, not a media-query guess ([dark](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)).
2. **Do keep the two design layers apart.** The Framer marketing tokens and the app's `core.app.*` semantic layer are unrelated systems — never mix a marketing role into the product shell or vice versa ([share](screenshots/app-share-light-default.png)).
3. **Do spend the indigo on actions.** #3F40F2 is the app's `--main-color`/`--link-color`/`--checkbox` — links, primary actions, checked accents; the product chrome stays on its warm-grey ramp ([login](screenshots/app-login-light-default.png)).
4. **Do pair the faces to the surface.** Unbounded draws marketing headings only; Inter carries marketing UI and the whole app; General Sans belongs to the docs platform ([hero](components/crops/hero-heading-default.png)).
5. **Do use the real radii.** Marketing buttons are pills (`radius.pill`, 9999px); the product's controls take the 10px `radius.field` — fields and buttons share it ([CTA](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png), [input](components/crops/login-input-email-default.png)).
6. **Do read dark from the repainted tokens.** `dark.*` is the measured repaint — the pill flips to `inverseInk` fill with its `#9e9eff` border ([dark CTA](components/crops/nav-cta-dark.png)).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** /login and everything behind it is a boundary — the workspace is unmeasured ([login card](components/crops/login-card-default.png)).
8. **Don't infer an app dark theme.** The product paints light only on its public routes; `core.app.*` is the share page's own default palette — no dark values exist to copy ([share shell](screenshots/app-share-light-default.png)).
9. **Don't treat the docs platform as the same system.** /docs is a hosted surface with its own chrome and General Sans — measured where captured, light-only ([docs](screenshots/docs-light-default.png)).
10. **Don't fake form behaviour.** No field was typed and no button submitted ([login](screenshots/app-login-light-default.png)); the share settings popover was opened through its own control only.
11. **Don't copy the brand.** The wordmark, the icon set and the font files are Hello Ivy's and its licensors' ([wordmark](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.
