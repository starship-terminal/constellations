# Understory design reference

A token set and component reference for Understory, the booking system for experience businesses. Captured from the public marketing site at understory.io on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop captures are 1440x900 at 1x. Phone captures are 390x844 at 2x. The marketing site paints one light theme.

The reason Understory is worth a capture: moss `#022c12` does both the text and the primary button on a cream page `#f6f4e8`; citrus `#f1f97e` is the small accent chip; National 2 Condensed sets the uppercase headlines; Inter sets the UI at 13.5px.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the live homepage stylesheets (`raw/css-vars.json`). Computed styles of buttons, headings, the nav, the field, the open menu, and plan cards were read in the page (`raw/components-computed.json`, `raw/pricing-extra.json`). `tools/build_tokens.py` maps those readings. Screenshots were taken with Playwright, then quantised to 256 colours. Flat UI colours were read from CSS, not from the PNGs.

The cookie banner (`#coiOverlay`, Cookie Information) was hidden with CSS. Accept and Decline were not clicked.

## What to distrust

- `--beige` (`#ede8d0`) is declared, but the painted page is `#f6f4e8`. Use `light.background.page`.
- `--border-radius` is `0.75rem`. Buttons are 9px. Plan cards are 15px. The declared token is not the control radius.
- Body weight is 450, an Inter variable-axis value, not 400.
- Hover on the header primary button did not change its computed background. Do not invent a hover colour.
- The homepage calendar is a public demo, not the signed-in product.
- developer.understory.io dark frame is that site's theme. It is not in `tokens.json`.
- Phone full-page shots are stitched from tiles and are very tall. Treat them as layout evidence, not as a crop source.

## Fonts

Inter (variable, Webflow CDN) sets body, nav, buttons, and fields. National 2 Condensed (bold and regular) sets the uppercase headlines. PP Editorial New sets the step headings. All three are proprietary. The style guide falls back to Arial, Arial Narrow, and Georgia. Fonts were not committed.

## Do and don't

- **Do set text and the primary button in moss.** Headlines, body, and Try for free share `#022c12`. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do keep the page cream, and the cards a step darker.** The page is `#f6f4e8`. Plan cards are `#eae8dd`. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Do set headlines in condensed caps.** National 2 Condensed, uppercase, tight leading. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do use citrus as a small chip, not as a button fill.** The arrow on Try Understory for free is the yellow. The button itself is moss. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do open menus as a moss panel.** Booking system and Marketing drop a dark green panel with white labels. [screenshots/home-light-menu-booking.png](screenshots/home-light-menu-booking.png)
- **Don't round the headlines or square the buttons.** Display type is square. Buttons are 9px. Cards are 15px. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
