## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://www.understory.io/` in its own tab. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so no screenshot was saved. Computed styles and stylesheet rules were read with `preview_evaluate` on the live page.

The cookie banner was dismissed with the visible Decline all button. After that, the home page, `https://understory.io/pricing`, `https://backoffice.understory.io/login?language=en` and `https://backoffice.understory.io/signup` were read at a measured viewport of 1440 by 900 CSS pixels. The host `prefers-color-scheme` was dark. The marketing body stayed beige. The backoffice attribute stayed `backoffice-light`.

Marketing colours come from `:root` custom properties on the Webflow stylesheet and from computed styles on the home and pricing pages. Type sizes for the display headings come from the declared rem rules, checked against the computed pixel size at a 12px root. Backoffice colours, radii, spacing and shadows come from resolved custom properties on `html` plus the computed login card, fields and buttons.

## What to distrust

The first home-page read happened at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, before the viewport was resized. Later reads at 1440 by 900 replaced the size-sensitive numbers. A few letter-spacing values in the raw file still carry the earlier subpixel reading.

`.heading-style-h4` computed 36px, weight 700, uppercase. Its line-height and letter-spacing were not isolated, so it is not a type token.

Marketing `--lilac` is `#d1bee7`. A works tile computed `rgb(203, 183, 219)`, which is a different colour. The token keeps the declared variable.

The marketing site and the backoffice do not share one accent. Marketing primary buttons are moss `#022c12` with white text. The backoffice Log in button is `#d5f285` with text `#02401a`.

No screenshot exists. The coverage rows that need an image are marked not captured.

## Fonts

National 2 Condensed is the marketing display face, loaded as woff2 from the Webflow CDN at weights 400 and 700. Inter is the marketing body face, a variable font on the same CDN, with Arial as the fallback. PP Editorial New is loaded and used on cookie-banner category headings and on some language variants of the alternate heading class. It is not the English home hero face.

The backoffice promotion heading uses National2Condensed, then Inter and Helvetica. Body and fields use Inter, then Helvetica and Arial. The page also loads Poppins, DM Sans, Montserrat, Open Sans, Raleway and Source Sans Pro. Those extra families were not the computed face of the login controls.

The font files are proprietary. The tokens name the families and the fallbacks. The files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

1. **Set display headings in National 2 Condensed, uppercase, at the declared rem size.** The home h1 computed 63.75px at a 12px root. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. **Use moss `#022c12` on beige `#f6f4e8` for the marketing page.** That pair is the body colour and background. See [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json).
3. **Keep the backoffice primary button on citrus `#d5f285` with text `#02401a`.** It is a different accent from the marketing moss button. See [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json).
4. **Round marketing buttons to 0.75rem and the login card to 32px.** Those are the computed radii. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
