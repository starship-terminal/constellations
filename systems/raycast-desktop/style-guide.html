Raycast 2.9: Raycast Light and Raycast Dark

Swatches in each theme's column are drawn over that theme's painted launcher window (page), so translucent roles preview as the app paints them. Every colour and size is Raycast's own declaration, read from the stylesheets and theme definitions shipped in the app (Measured), with the alpha steps worked out from them (Derived) and checked against the window's pixels (raw/pixel-check.json). The window fills (page, settingsPage, sidebar) are pixels (Sampled): the windows are translucent over the macOS window material, so no declaration gives them. Specimens are drawn from tokens only; none of Raycast's icons or artwork is used (the coloured squares stand in for app icons).
