Palette

The core set carries the declared --color-* primitives (the gray-0→1000 ramp, blue/green/amber/orange/red steps and the warm grays); light and dark sets mirror the declared --light-* and --dark-* roles one for one. The public surface paints light only — dark is the declared, un-painted twin.

Spacing and radius

Cosmos is a pill system: rounded-full (the measured 3.36e7px maximum) on every CTA, tab, menu button and search field; the declared ramp runs --radius-xs .125rem through --radius-4xl 2rem plus the 3px --radius-element on tiles; the QR card alone paints a 25% radius. Borders are 12%/10% ink color-mix hairlines.
