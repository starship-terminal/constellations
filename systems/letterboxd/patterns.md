# Letterboxd patterns

Signed out. Desktop 1440 by 900 unless noted.

## App shell

A dark bar with the three-dot wordmark, uppercase nav, and a pill search field. The page under it is a full-bleed still or a poster grid. Footer links sit on the same `#14181c` field. [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [film-light-default](screenshots/film-light-default.png).

## Film page

Poster on the left, serif title, year, director, synopsis, then a sign-in prompt for logging. Ratings show as a histogram and a green average. Tabs under the poster switch cast and details. [film-light-default](screenshots/film-light-default.png).

## List

A public list is a stacked poster grid with a title and counts. [list-light-default](screenshots/list-light-default.png).

## Pro marketing

Three columns: Free, Pro, Patron. Pro uses the orange brand, Patron the blue. No plan was started. [pro-light-default](screenshots/pro-light-default.png).

## Empty and error

A missing URL paints a film still and the line "Sorry, we can't find the page you've requested." [missing-light-default](screenshots/missing-light-default.png).

## Phone

The nav collapses to a person icon, a hamburger, and search. The hamburger opens the same links. [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png), [mobile-film-light-default](screenshots/mobile-film-light-default.png).

## Walls

Sign in is a centered card. Create account is an overlay. Both were photographed empty and not submitted. [signin-light-default](screenshots/signin-light-default.png), [signup-light-default](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).
