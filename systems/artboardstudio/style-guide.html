Palette

--color-scheme-1--text/accent (#0d1400) is the ink on every control and line of text; --color-scheme-1--background is the white field, --foreground the #eee wash; the editor's --mg-gree (#aaff00) is the brand green kept for highlights; hovers are #aaa text fades and #f4f3f2 fills.

Layout metrics

Nav bar 106px, controls 42px, tabs/chips 50px, the search field ~65px, mockup tiles ~199px, icon buttons 48px — radii 8 controls, 12 cards.

Type styles

Vend Sans carries the display lines (60.8px h1, 56px library title, 19.2px sections); a generic sans stack carries every control and body line at 16px.
