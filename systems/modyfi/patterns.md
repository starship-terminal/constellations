# Modyfi patterns

Public marketing pages only. The product shut down on 2025-06-13. These notes describe the February 2025 homepage and the later public pages that still rendered.

## Marketing shell

A black page, an 80px nav, a white Jokker headline, a grey Jokker lede, and a white pill as the primary action. Sign up is the only magenta fill in the chrome. See [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

The nav links to Product, Effects, Roadmap, Careers, and About. Log in and Sign up point at `app.modyfi.com`, which no longer resolves. Desktop version points at a todesktop download. Neither was followed.

## Hero demo

Under the headline the page shows a picture of the editor: a layers panel, a dark canvas, and an overlay inspector. That picture is marketing art on the public page. It is not a signed-in session.

## Community grid

Further down, project cards link to `/projects/...` with a Remix label and a creator name. Those names are published on the marketing site.

## Other public pages

About, modifiers, contact, roadmap, opportunities, and made-with-modyfi use the same black field and the same nav. Modifiers adds a "Modyfi is joining Figma" line. Contact is a short "Contact us" page. None of these pages was submitted.

## Phone

A 390px pass is in `screenshots/mobile-*-dark-default.png` when the mobile capture succeeded. The menu button is in the DOM at desktop width with a 0px box, so the phone menu is the place it is meant to show.
