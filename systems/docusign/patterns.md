# Patterns

## Marketing header

Utility bar, then a logo, three mega-menu buttons (Products, Solutions, Resources), text links (Enterprise, Plans & Pricing), and three CTAs (Contact sales, Buy now, Try for free). Plans & Pricing and Buy now leave for ecom.docusign.com. Try for free leaves for trial.docusign.com. Those branches stop.

## Hero

Large light-weight headline, a work-email field joined to a Get Started button, a region select, and a marketing checkbox. The form was not submitted.

## Sand footer

Footer band on `#f8f3f0`, with a deeper `#ede9e4` locale strip. App-store badges are links only. They were not opened.

## Product and solution pages

Same header and footer. Several pages expose FAQ buttons that toggle `aria-expanded`. One item was opened on pages where the crawler found a closed question.

## Phone

At 390 the same destinations move into a menu drawer. The drawer was opened on the homepage.
