Palette

The declared --ds-* brand set (cobalt, inkwell, deep-violet, mist, poppy, ecru) anchors the palette; the measured ramp holds the painted hover/pressed steps, ink alphas, the account-page deep-violet button and the sierra.chat assistant's own violet. The assistant-ramp group is the only 10-step ramp in evidence — declared in the embedded assistant agent's stylesheet, kept with vendor provenance.

Spacing and radius

Corners stay tight: 4px on links/toggles/submits, 8px on buttons and fields, 6px on generator tabs, 10px as the generator's declared --radius, 16px on ecom plan cards, 9999px only on the floating assistant launcher. Spacing runs a 2-96px grid with 80-96px section padding.

Shadows

Shadows are restraint: a 1px ink-15 inset hairline for ghost hovers, a 1px cobalt inset for focused fields, the storefront's white + cobalt double focus ring, and only the floating launcher lifts.

Type

DS Indigo everywhere, weight 300 for display sizes (80/86.4 h1 down through 48/51.84), 400-600 for UI. Sizes are measured; samples fall back to system sans.
