# Docusign design reference

A token set and component reference for Docusign's public surface — for building marketing pages and agreement flows in the style of the real thing. It was read from **docusign.com** on **2026-11-12/13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home, eSignature product, free-trial, signature-generator, customer-stories and company marketing pages, the ecom pricing storefront (geo-redirected to `/en-SG/` and captured as served), the public sign-in card on account.docusign.com (measured, never submitted), and a 390px mobile pass over home, the generator and pricing.

Docusign is worth a capture because its public face is three surfaces with one voice. The marketing site is Next.js + Emotion — atomic `css-*` classes, almost no custom properties — so its values are measured computed styles: inkwell `#130032` text on white, deep-violet `#26065d` bands, cobalt `#4c00ff` actions that step to `#4000cc` on hover and `#330099` pressed, squared 4-8px radii, and DS Indigo display type at weight 300 with negative tracking. The signature generator is the only surface that *declares* a token layer — the `--ds-*` brand set (cobalt, inkwell, deep-violet, mist `#cbc2ff`, poppy `#ff5252`, ecru `#f8f3f0`) plus shadcn-style roles. The ecom storefront and the account sign-in card run a Tailwind + Emotion "DXP" layer that declares `--dxp-primary #0069ec` and paints the white + cobalt double focus ring. **Boundary:** the product workspace (envelopes, templates, CLM, billing, admin) lives behind account.docusign.com on apps.docusign.com and was never reached; no form was ever submitted, no consent control ever answered, nothing purchased.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export docusign` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do anchor pages in white or ecru and paint bands in deep violet.
2. Do keep corners tight.
3. Do ring focus in cobalt 2px at 2px offset.
4. Do set display headings in weight-300 DS Indigo with negative tracking.
5. Don't take cobalt to black on interaction.
6. Don't style the signed-in workspace from this package.
7. Don't treat vendor surfaces as the palette.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

`tools/capture/vars.mjs` loads `/`, `/signature-generator`, the ecom storefront and `apps.docusign.com` (which lands on account.docusign.com), harvests every stylesheet — inline Emotion blocks plus linked vendor sheets (sierra.chat assistant, Guideflow, flag-icons) — re-parses them and resolves each custom property in a probe carrying the real sheets. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` reads computed styles on 73 components in default/hover/focus-visible/pressed/focused/filled/open states; pressed is CDP-forced `:active:hover`, never a real click on anything that submits. `tools/capture/public/public.mjs` takes the bounded screenshots. `tools/build_component_tokens.py` maps the spec (declared vars where they exist, measured values everywhere else) and `tools/build_tokens.py` emits tokens.json; `build_style_guide.py` and `build_components_md.py` render the docs. Consent, chat and promo overlays were hidden, never answered.

## What to distrust

- **Regional content.** The pricing storefront geo-redirects to `/en-SG/`; plan names, prices (SGD) and copy are as served for Singapore, not the US site.
- **No error colour.** The old `color.error` #ff0000 cited `sg-canvas`, which reads rgb(19, 0, 50) and holds no red; it was dropped. The only #ff0000 in raw/ is the logo link's pressed colour, the browser's own active-link red.
- **Entered by hand, no reading behind them:** `core.font.size.h4` 40px, `core.font.lineHeight.h4` 48px, `core.font.tracking.h4` -0.5px and `core.font.tracking.eyebrow` 0.8px. No captured component reads them; their source says Reconstruction.
- **`color.focus-green` #089b57** (and `light.color.focus.ring-green`) was read from `assistant` focus-visible, a state recorded where keyboard focus never landed (`via: focus failed`); recapture needed.
- **Wrong element measured; recapture needed:** `blog-card` read the 184 x 24 "Docusign Moment" text link, not a card; `story-logo` read a 328 x 328 story thumbnail, not a logo tile; `m-menu-open` read the 390 x 77 header nav, not the opened menu.
- **The assistant-ramp palette** is declared in the sierra.chat agent's own stylesheet (Docusign's "kraken" org theme): real declarations, but a vendor surface — use for the assistant, not the marketing site.
- **Emotion hashes** in selectors are build-dependent; treat any `css-*` class in raw/ as evidence, not an API.
- **The `#005fcc` 1px auto outlines** on ecom/generator controls are the browser's default focus ring, not a designed token — the designed ring is the cobalt 2px+2px pair.
- **Sampled pixels** — screenshots are 256-colour PNGs; exact values come from tokens.json or raw/.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Fonts

**DSIndigo** (marketing, weights 300/400/500/600 + italics) and **"DS Indigo"** (the spaced-name variant the generator, storefront and account stacks lead with) are served woff2 from docucdn akamaihd. **7_Docusign** and **2_Docusign** icon fonts, **Noto Sans Mono**, and six signature script faces (STFelicetteScript, TDNStockinette, TDNRhubarbTart, STAmpersandist, STConstant-UltraItalic, STNebulith-Display) are also declared. The style guide falls back to system sans; no font files are in this package.

## Coverage

- **Pages:** `/`, `/products/electronic-signature`, `/free-trial`, `/signature-generator`, `/customer-stories`, `/company`, ecom `/en-SG/plans-and-pricing/esignature`, and the account.docusign.com sign-in card — plus mobile home, generator and pricing at 390.
- **States:** default, hover, focus-visible (real keyboard focus), pressed (CDP-forced), focused and filled on fields, open on menus/dialogs/accordions — 209 states across 73 components.

## Do and don't

1. **Do anchor pages in white or ecru and paint bands in deep violet.** Marketing grounds are white or `#f8f3f0` ecru with `#26065d` band islands — there is no third dark and no dark theme. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [signgen-light-default](screenshots/signgen-light-default.png).
2. **Do keep corners tight.** Controls paint 4-8px radii, plan cards 16px, and 9999px belongs only to the floating chat launcher — never the marketing pill radius on forms. See [plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png) and [assistant](components/crops/assistant-default.png).
3. **Do ring focus in cobalt 2px at 2px offset.** Every interactive control draws the same ring — mist `#cbc2ff` on the violet bands, inkwell on the account card, and the white+cobalt double ring on the ecom storefront. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-focus-visible.png) and [ec-billing-annual](components/crops/ec-billing-annual-focus-visible.png).
4. **Do set display headings in weight-300 DS Indigo with negative tracking.** 80px display at -1.5px, 58px sections at -1.25px; CTAs run uppercase 600 with +0.2px. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png) and [h2-section](components/crops/h2-section-default.png).
5. **Don't take cobalt to black on interaction.** The action ladder steps `#4c00ff → #4000cc hover → #330099 pressed` — darker violets, never ink or grey. See [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-pressed.png).
6. **Don't style the signed-in workspace from this package.** Envelopes, templates and billing sit behind account.docusign.com and were never captured — the sign-in card is the boundary, not a sample. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
7. **Don't treat vendor surfaces as the palette.** The sierra.chat assistant ramp and Guideflow variables are declared by embeds, kept at vendor provenance — the marketing site's colours are measured, not declared. See [assistant](components/crops/assistant-default.png).
