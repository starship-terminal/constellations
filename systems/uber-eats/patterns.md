# Patterns

Public signed-out pages only. No screenshots. Layout numbers are from `getBoundingClientRect` and computed style at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels.

## Home

[https://www.ubereats.com/](https://www.ubereats.com/)

A 64 px header holds Get a ride, Log in and Sign up. The hero heading is "Order delivery near you", UberMove 52 / 64 / 700, with 24 px under it. Under that, a 540 px address pill and a 48 px search circle. Category sections follow: Restaurants, Grocery stores, Alcohol, Retail and convenience, each with a 32 px heading and a gray pill (Find restaurants, Shop groceries, Shop alcohol, Browse stores). A popular-chains list uses gray 16 px links. The footer is white, padding 64 px 64 px 48 px, and links to help, promotions, cities, pickup and legal pages. A "$0 Delivery Fee with Uber One" link sits in light type. Its band colour was not isolated.
