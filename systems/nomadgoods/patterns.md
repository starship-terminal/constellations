# Patterns

How the public Nomad storefront combines the pieces in [components.md](components.md). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Storefront chrome

A black announcement strip sits above a white rounded nav bar. Desktop nav items are text links. Cases, Watch, Charging, Tracking & Wallets and More Gear open a mega menu on hover. Sale is a link with no menu. Search, notification, account and cart are icons at the right. Account leaves the store for `account.nomadgoods.com` and was not entered.

Shown in [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-menu-cases](screenshots/home-light-menu-cases.png).

## Hero and deals

The homepage hero is a full-bleed photograph with a white Gotham-Bold headline and a white pill. Below it, feature cards and a community strip. The footer is the same page ground (`#f3f3f3`) with link columns and an email field that was not filled.

Shown in [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Browse and product

Collection pages are a grid of product cards. The product page measured here is the Modern Leather Case for AirPods Pro 3 (Horween black). Its primary action read "Sold Out | Join the Waitlist". The waitlist was not opened.

Shown in [cases-light-default](screenshots/cases-light-default.png) and [pdp-light-default](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png).
