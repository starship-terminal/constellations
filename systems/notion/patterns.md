# Patterns

Public Notion pages, signed out, 2026-11-21. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

White page, black wordmark, a centered header, and one blue pill. Product and Resources open dropdowns. Log in is a text link. Get Notion free is the only filled control in the header. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Hero

A 96px semibold headline, tight tracking, and two actions: a blue Get Notion free and a pale Request a demo. A product mock sits under the headline. The mock is a marketing demo, not a signed-in workspace. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Pricing

A monthly/yearly pill, then two bands: Free and Plus on white, Business and Enterprise on a pale blue panel. Primary actions are the same blue. Secondary actions are pale blue fills. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Login and signup walls

www.notion.com/login and /signup redirect to app.notion.com. The walls are empty email forms plus identity-provider buttons. Those buttons were not clicked. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).

## Demo request

Request a demo opens a work-email step on the marketing host. Next was not clicked. See [screenshots/contact-sales-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-sales-light-default.png).

## Phone

The drawer replaces the header links. Request a demo is a black pill. Log in is a pale pill. See [screenshots/home-light-mobile-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-mobile-menu.png).
