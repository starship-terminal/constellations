# Klarna design reference

A token set and component reference for Klarna's public marketing site, for building shopper-facing marketing pages, help centres and country pickers in the style of the real thing. Tokens were read from **www.klarna.com** on **2026-11-18**, signed out. Screenshots were refreshed on **2026-11-21** in headed Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Headless Chromium received Access Denied. The US market (`/us/`) is the design surface. The country picker at `/international/` is a separate public page. The public tree is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

Klarna paints one light theme from an inline Klarna UI custom-property sheet (`.klarna-ui-DEFAULT-light` on `:root`). The page is white (`#ffffff`), the ink is `#0b051d`, and the brand mark is a pink pill (`#ffa8cd`) used on "Get the app" and the QR card, not as the page ground. Controls are full pills (`99999px`). Type is Klarna Title on display headings and Klarna Text on nav, buttons and body. A dark sheet (`.klarna-ui-DEFAULT-dark`) is declared and tokenised. The public marketing pages do not paint it.

**Boundary:** the product (payments, purchases, account, checkout) sits behind app.klarna.com. On 2026-11-21 Sign in and Sign up both landed on an empty country-of-residence wall ("Select your main country of residence"). Nothing was typed. No country was chosen. No identity provider was clicked. No form was submitted. The query string on those URLs is not stored. Login extraction is not started. The OneTrust banner was hidden, never answered.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export klarna` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page white and the ink near-black.
2. Do spend the pink on brand actions, not on the page.
3. Do round every control to a pill.
4. Do pair Klarna Title with Klarna Text.
5. Do wash the nav on hover.
6. Don't style the app from this package.
7. Don't invent a painted dark marketing theme.
8. Don't treat the hero sentence as a token.
9. Don't answer the cookie banner.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Colour, type scale, space, radius and shadow values come from Klarna UI custom properties declared in inline `<style>` blocks on `https://www.klarna.com/us/`, `/us/shopping/`, `/us/help/` and `/international/`. `tools/capture/vars.mjs` wrote [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The light selector is `:root, .klarna-ui-DEFAULT-light`. The dark selector is `.klarna-ui-DEFAULT-dark`. Cross-origin sheets (including `owp.klarna.com` fabric CSS) were not the token source.

Component geometry (nav link, Sign in, the pink "Get the app" pill, ink buttons, search field, menu item, help tabs) was read with `getComputedStyle` in headless Chromium and stored in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Motion was probed with `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` versus no preference) and stored in [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json).

Before each reading the OneTrust banner (`#onetrust-banner-sdk` and related nodes) was hidden with CSS. No Deny, Allow, or Cookie settings control was clicked. A phishing-warning announcement strip was closed with its own close control. That strip is a public notice, not a consent dialog.

The 2026-11-18 readings still hold the tokens. The 2026-11-21 refresh replaced the viewport screenshots. Headless Chromium was refused with Access Denied. Headed Chromium loaded the public pages. The T3 preview tab loaded `/international/` and could not take screenshots ("The browser is not producing screenshot frames"). Desktop shots are 1440 x 900 at 1x. Phone shots are 390 x 844 CSS pixels at devicePixelRatio 2, so the PNG is 780 x 1688. PNGs were quantised to 256 colours. Scratch files stayed in `~/.design-systems/klarna/` and are not in git.

## What to distrust

The pink "Get the app" pill lives in a horizontal scroller. Its computed style is in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) (127 x 52, fill `#ffa8cd`, text `#0b051d`, radius `99999px`). Playwright could not crop the painted pill because the matching node sat off-screen (`x` about -1360). [components/crops/primary-button-default.png](components/crops/primary-button-default.png) is a crop of the pink QR "Get the app" card from the homepage screenshot, not that 52 px pill. Cite the screenshot and the computed JSON for the pill. Do not treat the crop as a button specimen.

[components/crops/ink-button-default.png](components/crops/ink-button-default.png) is a blank white frame from an off-screen clone. The ink button's computed style is still in the JSON. Do not cite that PNG as the button.

The homepage hero rotates. One reading showed "Your money treated right" at 84 px Klarna Title. The stored homepage screenshot shows "Introducing Apple Upgrade". Both are the same display role. The campaign copy is not a token.

`icon.png` is a 48 px ICO upscaled to 128 px. The K is pixelated. `tools/fetch_icon.py` found no higher-resolution source. The wordmark SVG was not extracted: a later browser session was refused with "Access Denied".

The phone home full-page stitch ([mobile-home-light-full.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-full.png)) is 780 x 23340 after tile stitching. The raw full-page PNG was 780 x 9340 against an expected 780 x 9352, so the stitch is not a clean 390 CSS-pixel page. Use the viewport shot [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) for the 390 layout.

Known wrong, recapture needed (the pk-k recapture tracking issue, 2026-11 audit): `sign-in-button`, `primary-button`, `ink-button` and `icon-button` have a `pressed` reading equal to their default. `forcePseudo()` in `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` never cleared the marker it set on the first pressed element (the nav link), so every later forced `:active` landed there. The script is fixed by edit and was not run. `nav-link` and `icon-button` `focus-visible` are `focus failed` readings of the element at rest, not a focus look.

Dark tokens are declared, not painted on these public pages. Status colours (`--colors-bg-negative`, warning, positive) are declared. This capture did not see them as filled status chips with text and border.

Search overlay text in [home-light-search.png](screenshots/home-light-search.png) is the placeholder "Search for any product or brand" plus suggestion rows. A query was not submitted. The filled search crop may still show the placeholder.

## Fonts

Klarna Title, Klarna Text and Klarna Mono are Klarna's proprietary faces. They are named in the tokens with the site's own fallback stacks (system sans, then Roboto, Arial). This package does not ship the font files. The style guide falls back to the system sans for both Title and Text, and to `ui-monospace` for Klarna Mono.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page white and the ink near-black.** `#ffffff` is the page. `#0b051d` is text, the Sign in pill, and the selected help tab. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do spend the pink on brand actions, not on the page.** `#ffa8cd` paints "Get the app" and the QR card. It is not a background wash. See [home-light-search](screenshots/home-light-search.png).
3. **Do round every control to a pill.** Nav links, Sign in, hero CTAs and help tabs use `99999px`. See [tab-selected](components/crops/tab-selected.png) and [sign-in-button-hover](components/crops/sign-in-button-hover.png).
4. **Do pair Klarna Title with Klarna Text.** Title carries the display heading. Text at 14/500 carries the nav and at 16/500 the buttons. See [hero-title-default](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
5. **Do wash the nav on hover.** A nav link hover is the lavender `#e5e3f5`, not an underline. Sign in hover goes eggplant, a darker ink, not pink. See [nav-link-hover](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png).
6. **Don't style the app from this package.** Payments, purchases and the account sit behind app.klarna.com. The empty country wall is the boundary. See [signin-boundary-light-default](screenshots/signin-boundary-light-default.png).
7. **Don't invent a painted dark marketing theme.** Dark roles exist in the token sheet. These public pages paint light.
8. **Don't treat the hero sentence as a token.** The carousel swaps campaigns. Use the display type role, not one headline.
9. **Don't answer the cookie banner.** This capture hid OneTrust. Do not click Deny, Allow, or Cookie settings when rebuilding evidence.
