# SeatGeek design reference

SeatGeek is a ticket marketplace. This package records the public signed-out marketing and browse surfaces on seatgeek.com, measured 19 November 2026 from a live browser tab. No account was created, no ticket was bought, and no card was entered. Themes recorded: light, as painted, and dark, as declared in `[data-theme=dark]` and not painted. Widths measured: `1169` CSS pixels and `390` CSS pixels, both at device pixel ratio 2.

### Principles

1. Brand coral is the accent, ink is the action.
2. Pills for filters, a slab for price.
3. The hero is a dark field, the page below is white.
4. Do not invent a dark paint.
5. Do not treat partner green as SeatGeek.

## How the values were read

The preview tab loaded https://seatgeek.com/ and then `/about`, `/nfl-tickets`, `/concert-tickets`, `/cities` and `/help`. Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live DOM. Cross-origin stylesheets on seatgeekassets.com were fetched from inside that page (a curl from this host got HTTP 403 DataDome) and the `:root` and `[data-theme=dark]` blocks were copied into [raw/declared-tokens.json](raw/declared-tokens.json). `tools/build_tokens.py` maps those blocks to [tokens.json](tokens.json).

Viewport at the desktop pass: `innerWidth` 1169, `innerHeight` 731, `devicePixelRatio` 2, visual viewport scale 1. Phone pass after a freeform resize: `innerWidth` 390, `innerHeight` 844, same ratio and scale.

## What to distrust

The dark set is declared, not painted. `prefers-color-scheme` was dark in the browser and the marketing pages still painted light content surfaces. Button hover and pressed colours are stylesheet declarations (`--theme-background-color-button-*`); those states were not held. The help h1 size (`81.83px`) is a computed reading at 1169px and is not a token step. Spotify's `#1ed760` Connect button is a partner colour. The 19 November pass saved no screenshots. A 22 November walk added first-viewport shots in [screenshots/](screenshots/) and the map in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). Those shots were not used to remeasure tokens.

Only two component readings were saved: `raw/declared-tokens.json` holds the Sign in button (`light.component.button-glass`) and the price pill (`light.component.price`) under `computed`. The other component tokens were read in the preview and typed in, so they are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time. `light.component.field` mixes two elements: its white fill and 20px radius come from the Help textarea wrap, and its 46px height from the home search wrap (translucent, 100px radius). Recapture needed: read one field in full and save it to raw/.

## Fonts

RoobertVF is SeatGeek's variable face, weights 300 to 900, with a local Arial fallback named RoobertVF Fallback. The public pages computed `RoobertVF, "RoobertVF Fallback", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, Helvetica`. The font file is proprietary and is not in this package. `[data-density=compressed]` switches the declared family to Inter. That density was not painted. The style guide falls back to Helvetica Neue, Arial, Helvetica.

## Do and don't

- **Brand coral is the accent, ink is the action.** The wordmark, suggestion chips and Help chat button are `#ff5b49`. Primary actions such as Get updates are black on white, radius 8px.
- **Pills for filters, a slab for price.** Location and date filters are 32px pills, radius 100px, border `#dedddb`. The selected location pill inverts to `#181818`. The price reads "From $42" in a 6px-radius slab.
- **The hero is a dark field, the page below is white.** Home and category headers sit on `#120100` with white type. About, Help and the footer are `#ffffff` with `#181818` type.
- **Do not invent a dark paint.** The dark tokens are the stylesheet's `[data-theme=dark]` overrides. The public pages did not show a theme switch.
- **Do not treat partner green as SeatGeek.** The Spotify Connect button is `#1ed760`.
