### Principles

1. Use the near-black page and the white trial button.
2. Keep Join Prime as the translucent blue nav action.
3. Treat the yellow Continue button as Amazon's wall, not Prime Video's.
4. Do not invent a light theme for the marketing site.
5. Do not ship Amazon Ember as if it were licensed here.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The page was open in a T3 preview tab (`reuseExistingTab` false) on 2026-11-19. `preview_evaluate` read `getComputedStyle` on body, nav, headings, links, the trial CTA, the footer, and the Amazon sign-in card, field and Continue button. Named custom properties on `:root` (`--fable-*`, `--dv*`) were read the same way. Colour roles in the stylesheets sit behind hashed `--__*` names, so those names were not mapped onto tokens. A value in [tokens.json](tokens.json) is a computed style or a named custom property listed in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json).

## What to distrust

The light set is Amazon's sign-in page, not a Prime Video light theme. The marketing page did not offer a theme switch. `text.secondary` on that wall is the same computed colour as `text.primary`, because the page did not paint a second text colour. The email field's blue border is the focused state. The unfocused password border was `#888c8c`. Nav `lineHeight` of 18px comes from `--fable-typography-label-100-line-height`. The computed value was `normal`. Heading 600 and heading 400 were on `:root` and were not used by a visible heading. Dialog radius was a custom property. No dialog was opened. Prices on the page are Singapore dollars.

## Fonts

Amazon Ember is the marketing page's computed family, with Amazon Arabic Ember, then Arial, then sans-serif. It is Amazon's font and is not included here. The Amazon sign-in wall computed Arial, sans-serif. Ember had not loaded there.

## Do and don't

- **Use the near-black page and the white trial button.** The marketing page paints `rgb(0, 5, 13)` on the body and a white 60px button with 8px radius for "Start your 30-day free trial". The frame that should show this was not produced: [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png).
- **Keep Join Prime as the translucent blue nav action.** Its computed background is `rgba(26, 152, 255, 0.8)`, 14px medium Ember, 8px radius, 38px tall. See [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png).
- **Treat the yellow Continue button as Amazon's wall, not Prime Video's.** It is `#ffd814`, pill-shaped, on a white card with a `#d5d9d9` border. See [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png).
- **Do not invent a light theme for the marketing site.** None was on the page. See [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png).
- **Do not ship Amazon Ember as if it were licensed here.** Name the family. Do not add the font files. See [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png).
