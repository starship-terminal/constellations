# Patterns

## Marketing page

`https://www.primevideo.com/` lands on `/offers/nonprimehomepage` for this region (Singapore). A 60px nav sits on `#00050d` with 26px horizontal padding. It holds the wordmark (rendered 80 by 16, source PNG 320 by 62), Home, Movies, TV shows, Sports, a language control reading EN, and Join Prime.

Below that, full-bleed sections use a black-to-transparent gradient over a 1440 by 675 merchandising image. Copy sits in a 529px column: a 36px heading, an 18px / 25px paragraph, and a white 60px trial button. The first section also has "Prime Member? Sign in", a 14px "or", and fine print at 14px / 20px. The footer is `#222222` with `#8197a4` text and `#79b8f3` links.

## Signup wall

Join Prime (`/signup`) redirects to `https://www.amazon.sg/ap/signin`. The page is white, Arial, with a 350px card. The heading is "Sign in". The field label is "Enter mobile number or email". Continue is a yellow pill. Under it, Amazon's conditions line, "Need help?", and "Create your Amazon account". The form was not filled and not submitted. That wall is the gap: account creation, payment and playback sit behind it.
