Colour

Fourthwall names colours as primitives and surface roles. Ink is the text and the declared action fill. Paper is the page. The older grey, blue, green, red and yellow ramps sit beside that system. There is no dark theme to flip.

Ramps

Declared ramps: grey, blue, green, red and yellow. Twitch purple is a partner accent, not the product action.

Typography

Suisse Intl sets body and controls. PP Telegraf is the heading face. Saans is the display primitive. Static body is 16px. Display sizes are fluid clamps and are not tokenised as single lengths.

Density, space and shape

Space is a px step scale. Radius runs 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 18 and a 999px pill. The hero Start now button measured 117 by 43 with 12px radius and 12/20 padding.

Radius

Elevation

Rings, not borders. A 1px ring of the 10% ink is drawn as a box-shadow: outset in light (--cds-ring-outer) and inset in dark (--cds-ring-inner), with a soft drop under menus and the composer.

Motion

Fast and springy: buttons press in 60 ms, most UI answers in 120 ms on a snap curve, sheets take 300 ms. Click a track to play it on its own curve.

Layout

A 288 px sidebar on its own slightly darker ground, then the content column 8 px further on. Pages hang from a 48 px header: the home centres a 672 px composer under the greeting, a chat a 768 px transcript, and list pages a 960 px column.
