# ROVR design reference

A token set and component reference for ROVR's public radio site. It was read from **www.rovr.live** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: home (player, quick menu, drawer, Alexa dialog, playing), moods, schedule, curators, one curator page, archive, one show page, search, about, privacy, terms, and the sign-in and login walls. Phone captures cover home, moods, schedule, curators, archive, show, about, and sign-in.

ROVR paints one dark theme: a black page, yellow chrome (`#fff057` on the MENU pill, play button and track strip), italic kernStandard nav, and a yellow drawer. The site declares a shadcn token layer. On the live dark page `--primary` paints `#ffff57`, a nearby yellow. Both readings are kept. The signed-in library (liked tracks, saved shows, curator chat) stops at a login wall. No password was typed. No form was submitted.

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium, signed out, on 2026-11-21.

- **Declared tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) fetched the stylesheets and wrote every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). That probe does not carry `html.dark`, so its resolved colours are the light defaults. Dark role colours were read on the live page: each `--*` channel was applied as `hsl()` and `getComputedStyle` returned the painted sRGB value. Those readings are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) under `roles.painted`.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) read computed styles for the nav, MENU pill, play button, drawer, search field, archive tab, about headline, and the empty sign-in field. Hover, focus-visible and pressed were forced through the Chrome DevTools protocol. Nothing was typed into email or password. Google and Apple were not clicked.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) shot each public route at 1440 x 900 and the phone set at 390 x 844. Sidecars record `innerWidth` 1440 and 390. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) wrote 256-colour PNGs.

The site tree was mapped before the screenshot run. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## What to distrust

- **Two yellows.** The chrome paints `#fff057`. Computed `--primary` paints `#ffff57`. Use the token whose source names the element you are matching.
- **Stylesheet probe is not the dark theme.** `raw/css-vars.json` resolved values are light defaults. Dark roles come from `roles.painted`.
- **Status colours were not painted.** Error, success, info and warning tokens are derived from declared `hsl()` values. No captured control showed them.
- **Auth walls are boundaries.** `/sign-in` and `/login` show email and password. The password was not submitted. Liked tracks, saved shows and curator messages were not entered.
- **Quantised screenshots.** Read colours from tokens.json, not from the PNGs.
- **kernStandard and gtAlpina are not in the package.** The style guide falls back to system-ui.

## Fonts

**kernStandard** is the face on the body, nav, titles and buttons. The computed stack is `kernStandard, "kernStandard Fallback", system-ui, sans-serif`. It is declared as `--font-kern-standard`. **gtAlpina** and **Roboto** are declared (`--font-gt-alpina`, `--font-roboto`) and were not read on a captured element. None of the webfonts are committed.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the chrome yellow on black.** The MENU pill, play button and track strip are `#fff057` on `#000000`. See [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do set the nav in italic kernStandard.** Header links are 15px italic at half-yellow until hover. See [components/crops/nav-link-default.png](components/crops/nav-link-default.png).
3. **Do open account actions into a wall.** My tracks, saved shows and curator messages stop at sign-in. See [screenshots/sign-in-dark-default.png](screenshots/sign-in-dark-default.png).
4. **Do keep the drawer yellow.** The navigation drawer is a yellow panel, not a dark overlay. See [screenshots/home-dark-menu.png](screenshots/home-dark-menu.png).
5. **Don't treat --primary as the painted yellow.** Computed `--primary` is `#ffff57`. The controls paint `#fff057`.
6. **Don't invent a light theme.** The live page is `html.dark` only.
