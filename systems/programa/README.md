# Programa

Programa is interior-design studio software (programa.design). This package captures the public marketing site and the signed-out login and register pages on 19 November 2026.

Surfaces: home, pricing, features, and contact on programa.design, plus login and register on app.programa.design. Themes: light only. The marketing pages stayed light while the machine preference was dark. Widths: the preview browser measured 1169 by 731, then 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. No account was created. No email or password was typed.

## How the values were read

`preview_open` loaded https://programa.design/ in a new tab. `preview_evaluate` read computed styles and the same-origin custom properties. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Measurements from that tab were kept.

Public marketing screenshots were taken with headless Playwright Chromium at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, device scale 2, colour scheme light. Those pages are marketing HTML. They loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. Login and register returned 403 Forbidden in that headless browser, so those screenshots were not kept. The same login and register URLs loaded in the T3 preview browser, and their styles were read there.

The first preview viewport was 1169 by 731, devicePixelRatio 2, visualViewport scale 1. A later resize reported 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. Screenshot PNGs are 256-colour, at twice the CSS viewport.

## What to distrust

raw/ keeps the root custom properties and the capture notes, not every computed style read in the preview browser. 32 tokens carry a value no raw file holds: the CTA and app text colours, several rings and borders, six computed box-shadows, the Inter faces, a few type sizes and control sizes, and the switch duration. They are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time, and each source names the missing value. A recapture should save those readings.

The hero h1 computed line-height is 64px on an 80px font-size. That is the value `getComputedStyle` returned. The screenshots show the headline wrapping onto two lines, so the used line box is taller than that number.

Start free computed rgb(252, 253, 161). Start 7 day trial computed rgb(253, 255, 162). Both sit next to the declared `--color-yellow-light` `#fdffa2`. They are recorded as separate tokens.

The phone menu Start free computed color rgb(2, 85, 77). The desktop Start free computed rgb(127, 46, 27). Both are recorded.

The email field's first computed shadow was a zinc focus ring. After blur, the rest shadow was a black 1px ring plus a 1px 2px drop. Both readings are in the tokens.

Hover, pressed, disabled, and loading were not forced. The focus ring on `.form-control:focus` is a declared stylesheet rule, labelled as such, and was not measured on a focused control.

Rhymes Display, Rhymes Text, and Rhymes Dynamic are commercial faces. InterVariable is the app field face. The tokens name the families that computed. The style guide falls back to Georgia and Inter.

Headless screenshots of login and register are 403 pages and are not in this package. The login panel on the left of the form did not expose a background colour in computed style. Its quote type is a gap.

## Do and don't

- **Use the pale page and the yellow trial button together.** The marketing page is white and the trial button is `#fdffa2`. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) shows that pair.
- **Set headlines in Rhymes Display at weight 300.** The home h1 is 80px with letter-spacing -1.5px. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) shows the face.
- **Keep the signed-out app on zinc and Inter.** Login title colour is `#18181b` at 22px weight 600. The marketing yellow does not appear on that form.
- **Leave the billing switch mint when it is on.** The on track computed `#a7f3d0`. [components/crops/switch-on-default.png](components/crops/switch-on-default.png) shows it.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/programa && python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/catalog.py design-md programa
python3 tools/package_tests.py programa
```

`build_tokens.py` checks the token file. It does not invent values. `design-md` rewrites DESIGN.md from the tokens and this README.
