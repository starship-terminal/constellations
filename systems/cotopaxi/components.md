Mont, 14px, weight 800, letter-spacing `0.35px`, line-height 40px, padding `0 15px`. Black Friday is `#8a0010`. Gifts is `#005f9e`. Sale and the other departments are `#25282a`.

## Footer

Ink `#25282a`, type `#ffffff`. Columns for shop, retail, impact, sales, support, and legal.
