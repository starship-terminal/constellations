## Typography

Body is Gotham, 16px, weight 400. Nav is Mont, 14px, weight 800, letter-spacing `0.35px`. Shop buttons are Gotham, 14px, weight 700, uppercase, letter-spacing `1.5px`. The announcement is 12px.

## Shape

Controls are square. `--bs-border-radius` is `0px`, and the Shop Packs link matches. Search is a pill, radius `99px`, 300 by 30.

## Layout

Announcement 34px. Wordmark 114 by 37. Department row 40px. Shop button 41px, padding `8px 25px`. Footer is ink with white type.
