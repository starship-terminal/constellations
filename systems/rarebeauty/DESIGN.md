# Rare Beauty

A signed-out capture of the public Rare Beauty shop at [https://www.rarebeauty.com/](https://www.rarebeauty.com/) on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The page is cream `#fdfbf8`. Headings are Ogg Regular. UI type is Neue Hass Unica Regular. Burgundy `#6f0936` carries the wordmark, the nav, the hairline, and the filled Add to bag.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

## Colour

| Role | Value | Where |

| Page | `#fdfbf8` | body and header |

| On burgundy | `#ffffff` | product Add to bag label, hero heading |
| Chat | `#802549` | chat bubble, sampled, not opened |

## Type

Ogg Regular for Coming Soon at 56px, Bestsellers at 48px, the collection title at 30px, and the product title at 40px. Neue Hass Unica Regular for body at 16px, nav at 12px, and buttons at 12px. The shipping Shop now uses Neue Hass Unica Bold at 10px. Font files are not in this package.

## Controls

The shipping strip is 47px. A 1px burgundy hairline sits under it. The header is 62px. The wordmark sprite is 175 by 32px. Sneak peek, card Add to bag, and product Add to bag are 36px with a square radius. Product Add to bag is filled burgundy. Card Add to bag is an outline.
