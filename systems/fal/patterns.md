# fal patterns

Public, signed out, 2026-11-21.

## Marketing shell

A white logo chip and a grey nav island sit on a bronze hero. Products and Resources are hover menus. Get Started is a black pill in the header and a neon pill in the hero. Both open the login wall. Contact Sales is the quiet grey pill and opens a form that was not submitted.

Shown in [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png).

## Pricing

A large Focal display, then plan cards. Plan CTAs that need an account were not followed. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Docs shell

A left sidebar, a top tab row, and a theme switch (system, light, dark). Dark paints a near-black page `rgb(13, 12, 16)` with light text. Search opens in the page. Login in the sidebar is a wall and stops. [docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/docs-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

One wall for login and sign up. Three OAuth buttons and Use SSO. No email field. A generated still sits on the right. [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Explore

A public model index with search, filters, and cards. Trying a model or opening workflows can hit the login wall. Those branches stop. [explore-light-default.png](screenshots/explore-light-default.png).

## Not on the public surface

Empty, loading, and error states of the signed-in app. Destructive confirmation. Onboarding. A form validation error was not provoked, because no form was submitted.
