# Soundtrap patterns

How the public pages combine. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

Desktop header on a near-black or purple bar: logo, Music Makers, Features, Education, Blog, Pricing, Log in, Get started pill. Phone collapses the links into the menu button. Footer repeats the product links, social icons, legal links, and the English language menu.

Seen in [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-nav.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-nav.png).

## Dark hero, light body

Home, features, originals, and storytellers open on a near-black hero (#1a1a23 section) with a light grey headline and a light pill CTA, then drop into light content sections. This is a section style. It is not `.st-dark-theme`.

## Pricing

A purple header, Individual / Education links, a Monthly / Yearly toggle, four coloured plan cards, then a comparison grid. Yearly is the loaded state. Monthly is a local toggle.

[pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png), [pricing-education-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-education-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

Login and signup are centred forms on white: social buttons, an "or" rule, then email fields. Signup adds confirm email, password rules, birth date selects, and a Cloudflare check. The branch stops there.

[login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png), [signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png), [edu-login-light-default.png](screenshots/edu-login-light-default.png).

## Education landing

A separate header (pricing points at education pricing, login points at the school login) and role CTAs for admin, teacher, and student. Those CTAs open account or invite flows and were not filled.

[edu-light-default.png](screenshots/edu-light-default.png).

## Help and blog

Support is a Zendesk home with a search field and category cards. The blog is a post grid with a search button. Neither search was submitted.

[support-light-default.png](screenshots/support-light-default.png), [blog-light-default.png](screenshots/blog-light-default.png), [blog-light-search.png](screenshots/blog-light-search.png).
