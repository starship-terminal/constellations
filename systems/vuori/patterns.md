# Patterns

Public pages only, signed out, 2026-11-22.

## App shell

A 34px promo bar sits on a 80px white header. Departments are text links on the left. Search, Ask AI, Account, Saved, and Bag sit on the right. The footer repeats a newsletter, then Support, Company, and Other. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Department menu

New, Women, Men, Accessories, Gifting, and Explore each open a menu from the keyboard. The menu is a list of collection and page links. Those links were read and not visited, except Women and Explore, which are also the photographed collection and Our Story. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md)

## Collection

A centered title, a category carousel, then Filters and Sort above a product grid. Filters was not opened. [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png)

## Product

A two-column gallery on the left. Title, price, colour, size, and Select Size on the right. Select Size was not clicked, so the bag and checkout never started. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png)

## Story

A full-bleed photograph with a white display line, then a short statement. [screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png)

## Account

The header Account button opens a menu before any wall. Sign In or Create Account is one control. It opens one empty email wall. Continue, Shop, and Google were not clicked. [screenshots/login-light-1440.png](screenshots/login-light-1440.png)

The promo Sign Up and the footer email ask for an address. Neither was submitted.
