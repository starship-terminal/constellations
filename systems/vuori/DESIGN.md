# Vuori

Hand copy of the measured public-store tokens. Not written by `make design-md`, because that command needs a catalog.json entry and this pass does not edit catalog.json.

## Theme

Light only. No theme switch was on the public pages.

## Type

aktiv-grotesk at 14px, weight 300, tracking `-0.14px` on the body. Nav is the same size at weight 400. The visible hero line is 50px, weight 500, tracking `-2.5px`, line height 60px, white. Women's Collection is 28px, weight 400, tracking `-0.84px`. The product title and Sign in are 21.008px, weight 400, tracking `-0.63024px`. Ask AI reports Arial. These files are not in the package.

## Shape

The promo bar is 34px. The header is 80px. The wordmark is 112 by 18. Search and Ask AI are pills, 36px, radius `3996px`, 1px ink border. Hero shop links are square, 60px, radius `0px`. Select Size and the login email are 44px and 40px, radius `2px`. Continue with Google is 40px, radius `4px`.
