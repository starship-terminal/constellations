# Framer design reference

A token set and component reference for Framer's public surfaces — for building website-builder product marketing pages, pricing grids, showcase/gallery indexes and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **framer.com** on **2026-11-15**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the marketing pages /, /ai/, /agents/, /design/, /mcp/, /pricing, /enterprise/, /about, /updates, /academy/, /developers/, /blog/, /stories/, /careers/, /contact/, /downloads/, /compare and /solutions/designers/; the community stack's /community/gallery/ and /marketplace/templates/; the light /login auth shell; the header's Platform/Solutions/Resources mega-dropdowns; and 390px passes on home, pricing, the gallery and contact, plus the phone nav overlay.

Framer is worth a capture because the marketing site is itself a Framer site: its only declared token layer is the project's colour styles — **576 `--token-<uuid>` custom properties on `body`** (not `:root`, which carries only two internals) that deduplicate to **68 distinct painted values**: an achromatic ramp from the #000 page field through ~25 grey steps to #fff ink, a translucent `alpha-white.*`/`alpha-black.*` hairline ladder, the **#cbff00 lime** and a set of vivid accents (#0099ff blue, violets, greens, rose) used in gradient art. The uuid names are opaque project ids — no semantic names can be observed — so the palette tokens name their painted colour and bind one declaring var, and every other value (sizes, radii, weights, roles) is measured on the live pages. The community/marketplace stack is a different app that also paints dark; /login is a light third stack. **Boundary:** the dashboard, editor and every post-login surface was never reached; /login was measured as a form, never submitted; Sign up and every CTA were measured, never followed; the "We use cookies…" consent card was hidden, never answered.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export framer` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint on the black field with white-alpha hairlines.
2. Do use the white pill as the primary action.
3. Don't paint the lime as the default button.
4. Do keep the community stack its own look.
5. Don't style the signed-in product or fabricate a light marketing theme.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

- **Declared layer.** `tools/capture/vars.mjs` loads www.framer.com / and /pricing, reads every stylesheet body (the linked /sites/…/*.css bundles plus the Framer runtime's inline `<style>` blocks), re-parses them with `CSSStyleSheet` and resolves every custom property on `document.body` — `body` is the selector that declares the `--token-<uuid>` palette; `:root` declares only `--overflow-clip-fallback` and `--one-if-corner-shape-supported`. The harvest found 576 token declarations (the colour styles), 102 component-scoped vars (`--framer-font-*`, `--framer-text-*`, `--framer-link-*`, `--framer-input-*` text-style plumbing, hashed component vars like `--17kkcf8`, `--selection-*`) and 378 `@font-face` rules (Inter, Inter Variable, GT Walsheim, Azeret Mono, "Inter Display", walsheim and friends).
- **Palette dedup.** `tools/build_component_tokens.py` groups the 576 `--token-<uuid>` names by resolved value: 68 distinct paints. Each `color.palette.*` token names its painted colour (`neutral-<rr>`, `alpha-white.<pct>`, `alpha-black.<pct>`, `accent.<name>`) and binds the alphabetically-first uuid declaring it; the full uuid→value map stays in `raw/css-vars.json`. No semantic name is invented — the uuid names are opaque project ids.
- **Measured values.** `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` reads computed styles, geometry and text styles on the live pages (37 components in hover, focus-visible, pressed, opened, focused states — DevTools-forced for pressed), and `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` reads transitions/animations twice (no-preference and reduce) on four pages. Measured roles alias the declared paint whose value they match.
- **Screens.** `tools/capture/public/public.mjs` takes 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 screenshots; `tools/capture/quantize.py` stores them as 256-colour PNGs with `.capture.json` sidecars (viewport, sanitized URL, clip for stitched `-full` shots).
- **Locale.** The capture ran from a Singapore IP: /pricing rendered plan prices in **SGD** and the footer locale select read English/Japanese. Prices are the product's content, not tokens.

## What to distrust

- **The uuid palette is a paint list, not roles.** `neutral-1f` is "a declared #1f1f1f paint", not "the card colour" — Framer gives every project colour a uuid, and 71 of them are the same white-alpha. Role semantics are the `dark.color.*` measured aliases, and they are what one component painted, not a contract.
- **The hero CTA is white, not lime.** The #cbff00 lime is a real declared accent (visible on badges and accents in the shots) but the measured "Get started for free" and "Sign up" pills are #fff on #000 ink; the Pro plan CTA is #0099ff. Do not assume the lime is the primary button.
- **SGD prices.** The plan prices are localised content captured in SGD; they say nothing about other locales' numbers or styling.
- **Pressed is forced.** `CSS.forcePseudoState` :active:hover, so any script-applied mousedown look is missing.
- **Quantised screenshots.** 256-colour PNGs; gradients and the demo videos band a little. Read exact colours from tokens.json or raw/.
- **Measured-once geometry.** Component dims are one read at one moment; marketing pages animate and personalise.
- **`auth` is not a light theme.** /login is a separate light app that declares none of the www `--token-<uuid>` layer; its field, Google button and card readings are the `auth` set, as measured literals. Do not read it as a light mode of the marketing site.
- **The community stack's own declared layer.** /community/gallery and /marketplace are a different app whose sheets were not separately harvested — its values are measured component tokens.

## Fonts and fallbacks

The marketing pages measure **GT Walsheim Medium** on the display heading (self-hosted "GT Walsheim Medium" + "…Placeholder" fallback), **Inter Variable** on nav links, body and CTAs ("Inter Variable Placeholder" fallback), with **GT Walsheim Bold** preset and **Azeret Mono** for code styling declared in the sheets (the 378 @font-face rules are in raw/css-vars.json). The community stack measures **walsheim**. The auth shell measures **Inter Variable UI / Inter / system-ui**. The style guide falls back to system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint on the black field with white-alpha hairlines.** The page field is `dark.color.background.page` `#000`; panels and cards read as `neutral-*` greys separated by `alpha-white.*` strokes, not shadows. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do use the white pill as the primary action.** The measured CTAs are #fff fill with #000 ink and an 8px radius; hover is a soft fade, the ghost is `alpha-white.10`. See [pricing-dark-default](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png).
3. **Don't paint the lime as the default button.** `#cbff00` (`color.palette.accent.lime`) is a declared accent for badges and art; the hero and header pills are white and the only saturated plan CTA is `accent.blue` #0099ff on Pro. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
4. **Do keep the community stack its own look.** The gallery and marketplace are a different app (walsheim masthead, 30px filter chips, dark Open buttons) — don't style it from the marketing tokens. See [community-gallery-dark-default](screenshots/community-gallery-dark-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in product or fabricate a light marketing theme.** The dashboard, editor and CMS were never reached; the only light surface is the /login shell. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
