# Girlfriend Collective

Hand copy of the measured public-store tokens. Not written by `make design-md`, because that command needs a catalog.json entry and this pass does not edit catalog.json.

## Theme

Light only. The body class is `palette--light`. No theme switch was on the public pages. The promo bar is `#0b0b0b`. That is a strip, not a second theme.

| Role | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|

| Ink | `#000000` | Body and `--COLOR-TEXT` |
| Footer ink | `#0b0b0b` | Footer, `rgb(11, 11, 11)` |
| Wordmark | `#0d0d29` | Logo.svg path fill |

## Type

Neue Haas Grotesk Text Roman at 16px for body and 11.2px for the header links. Neue Haas Grotesk Text Medium at 12.8px for pills. Canela Thin at 68.64px for the homepage heading and 44px for the product title. Space Mono at 13.2px for the promo. These files are not in the package.

Nav tracking is 0.56px. Button tracking is 0.64px. Promo tracking is 1.32px. Header links and pills are uppercase.

## Shape

Primary and quiet actions are pills, 30px radius, 41px tall. The footer email is 54px, 30px on the left corners, and the subscribe control takes the right corners. The header bar is 60px. The promo bar is 42px.
