# Weekday patterns

Signed-out shop, 2026-11-22. Worldwide English at `/en-ww/`, shipping Singapore.

## Header

Promo, then a single 56px row. Department buttons open a mega-menu in place. Search stays in the row and was not submitted. Account, favourites, and cart stay in the row and were not opened.

## Homepage

A split hero, women on the left and men on the right, each a full-bleed photograph with a white label chip. Further copy links to jeans, suiting, underwear, and graphic prints. JOIN NOW is an outline button that opens a newsletter form. That form was not submitted.

## Category

A white grid of product cells. The first paint on women's clothing opened a newsletter popup. Escape closed it. The photograph is the grid after that close. Filters were not submitted.

## Product

Photography on the left, buy column on the right. The buy column ends at ADD TO CART. That button was not clicked, because clicking it starts checkout. FIND IN STORE was not opened, because it prints addresses.

## About

A text subnav, a black hero, then a white split of copy and photography. The page title sits on the black band in black type, so the readable heading in the photograph is THIS IS WEEKDAY.

## Stop

Login, create account, favourites, cart, checkout, add to cart, search submit, newsletter submit, store locator, find in store, and track order.
