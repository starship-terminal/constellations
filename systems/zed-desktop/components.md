# Zed components

Generated by `tools/build_components_md.py` from its inventory, [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [components/crops/](components/crops). Colour paths are in the `light` and `dark` sets (they alias Zed's own theme keys under `theme.*`); other paths are in `core`. Crops are cut from the 256-colour screenshots by `tools/crops.py`, so they show layout and state, never exact colour.
