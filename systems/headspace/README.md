# Headspace design reference

A token set and component reference for Headspace's signed-out public surfaces — for building marketing pages, pricing cards, content hubs and signed-out auth surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.headspace.com**, **organizations.headspace.com**, **checkout.headspace.com** and **auth.headspace.com** on the capture date, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /app, /meditation, /mindfulness, /sleep, /online-therapy, /coaching, /ai-mental-health-companion (Ebb), /mental-health, /mental-health/anxiety, /science, /about-us, /articles plus one published article, the free-preview /content library and a topic page, /subscriptions, /family-plan, /buy/gift, /share-headspace, the /code redeem form, /app/mindful-families, the B2B home and /employers on the organizations host, the self-serve checkout shell, and the Auth0 universal /login and /signup; 390px passes on home, /subscriptions, /meditation and /content.

Headspace is worth a capture because its marketing surface has **almost no custom-property layer**: the Next.js site compiles to generated Emotion classes and inline `<style>` blocks (275+ per route), and the harvest found only a small `:root` palette — 27 names, `--secondary-blue #0061ef` through `--orange #ff7e1d` and the warm greys. So every role token here is a *measured* reading — computed styles, bounding boxes and painted layers — with provenance in `$extensions.source`. One family carries the whole surface: **Headspace Apercu** in weights 200/400/600/700 (plus Apercu Pro Mono in the served `@font-face` list), on a warm #f9f4f2 ground with blue #0061ef primary pill CTAs, dark #2d2c2b secondary pills and the topic pages' pastel tints — the signature orange surfaces on the B2B CTA's hover and the declared --orange. The header's For You / For Business / Our Plans categories open a hover **mega-menu**, captured as a local state; a phone gets a hamburger nav sheet instead. **Boundary:** everything post-login is out of scope — the Auth0 forms were measured and never filled, checkout was viewed and never touched, /code was never redeemed, no trial was started, nothing was submitted anywhere, and the OneTrust consent layer was hidden, never answered.

2. Do keep the ground warm and light.
3. Do make the primary action the blue pill.

5. Don't sign in, subscribe or redeem from this package.

## Do and don't

1. **Do set everything in Headspace Apercu.** The whole signed-out surface runs on one family in weights 200–700; there is no serif display voice. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
2. **Do keep the ground warm and light.** The marketing pages paint a warm off-white field; pure white belongs to cards and fields. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
3. **Do make the primary action the blue pill.** The Get-50%-off CTA and every primary button is a fully-round pill in `#0061ef` (hover `#0040ea`) with a soft 2px drop; secondary actions are dark `#2d2c2b` pills. The signature orange appears on the B2B CTA's hover. See [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
4. **Do open the mega-menu on hover.** Desktop categories (For You, For Business, For Providers, Our Plans, Resources, About) reveal a shared panel; a phone shows a nav sheet instead. See [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png) and [mobile-home-light-drawer](screenshots/mobile-home-light-drawer.png).
5. **Don't sign in, subscribe or redeem from this package.** The auth, checkout and redeem forms were measured empty and never submitted — their success, error and post-submit states are recorded gaps. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
6. **Don't style the member app from this package.** Only signed-out surfaces were captured; the member meditation player, progress and settings are behind auth and unevidenced.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** Headspace paints light only — no control, no dark class or attribute, `colorScheme: "normal"` in every screenshot's `.capture.json` `theme` block. There is no dark token set.
8. **Don't treat the pastel topic tints as a palette system.** The meditation/sleep/anxiety pages tint sections per topic; the tints are per-surface readings, not a universal accent ramp. See [meditation-light-default](screenshots/meditation-light-default.png).
9. **Don't read `/pricing` as a plan page.** It answers a real "Whoops! Something's changed" 404 — the plans live on `/subscriptions`. See the Gaps in [components.md](components.md).
10. **Don't present the organizations host's palette as the member app's.** organizations.headspace.com is B2B marketing on the same stack; its readings stay in their own component group.

## How the values were read

Every colour, radius, length and font on this package is a **measured** value — `measured/computed <what> of <component> <state>` in `$extensions.source` — a computed style or bounding box read from the live DOM by `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`, with a matching crop in `components/crops/`. The exception is the small `declared` set: the few custom properties the signed-out stylesheets declare, harvested by `tools/capture/vars.mjs` from the inline Emotion blocks and the linked font CSS bundles across 21 routes, resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets at 1440 x 900, emitted as `css-var <name>` provenance. `raw/css-vars.json` keeps the full harvest verbatim (the `@font-face` list, the per-selector declaration counts, the resolved `:root` values) as the evidence that no usable variable layer exists to read.

## What to distrust

- **Nothing dark paints.** No theme control, no dark attribute, no applied `prefers-color-scheme: dark` rules on any captured page. The token file ships no dark set.
- **There is no variable layer.** Values are what the renderer computed, not names a stylesheet declared — two components that share a colour share the *value*, not a named token upstream.
- **Presses are forced.** `pressed` states were read with `CSS.forcePseudoState` — any script-driven mousedown look is uncaptured.
- **Forms are empty.** `focused` was a real click into the field; `filled` states that exist were typed and deleted again, with nothing sent. Success, validation-error and post-submit appearances are uncaptured by design.
- **The consent layer is hidden, not answered.** OneTrust's banner/preferences were set `display: none` for the captures; no consent state was chosen.
- **Full pages are bounded.** `-full` screenshots tile at most 8 chunks; taller pages truncate from the bottom and their sidecars note the document height.
- **`/pricing` is a 404.** Headspace serves its own "Whoops! Something's changed" page there; it is a recorded absence, not a capture.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read exact colours from the tokens or the raw files, never from a screenshot pixel.
- **`hero-h1` is the /share page's headline.** The capture read `hero-h1` on 16 pages under one key and kept only the last one, so the home headline reading was lost. `core.font.family/size/lineHeight/weight.display` and `light.color.ink.primary` come from /share and say so in their source; a recapture is needed. `auth-card` was read on /login and /signup under one key; the kept reading is the /signup card ("Create your account"), so the /login card was lost. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now keeps the bare key for the first page that lists it (home's hero, /login's card) and prefixes the rest (edited, not run).
- **Four Lottie icons were dropped.** `mindfulness-for-kids`, `start-therapy` and the two `process-thoughts` SVGs referenced `<use>` targets the icon harvest stripped, so they rendered blank. They are deleted with their `icons/INDEX.tsv` rows; they were animation frames, not interface icons.
