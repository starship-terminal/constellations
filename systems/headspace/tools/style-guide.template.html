Headspace, measured

headspace.com compiles to generated Emotion classes with almost no custom-property layer — the signed-out stylesheets declare only a small :root palette (the declared set: --secondary-blue #0061ef, --primary-blue #0040ea, --orange #ff7e1d, --primary-warm-grey-700 #2d2c2b, --2ndary-warm-grey-100 #f9f4f2 and friends), so the role tokens are computed-style measurements and the declarations cross-check them. One family carries the surface: Headspace Apercu 200–700 (Apercu Pro Mono in the @font-face list). The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from www.headspace.com, organizations.headspace.com, checkout.headspace.com and auth.headspace.com, signed out, in headless Chromium. Everything post-login is a documented boundary; the auth, newsletter, redeem and checkout forms were measured empty, never submitted; the OneTrust consent layer was hidden, never answered. No dark theme exists signed out — no control, no attribute, no applied dark media rules. The Apercu faces are licensed and not loaded here: samples fall back to system sans.
