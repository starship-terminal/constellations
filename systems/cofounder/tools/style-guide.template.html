Cofounder, declared and measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. cofounder.co declares a Tailwind v4 theme layer on :root — --background/--color-surface #f5f5f2, the --color-lime-* accent pair, --color-ink-ui-solid, the --font-* families (TT Neoris, Inter, Departure Mono, IBM Plex Mono) and the --radius-*/--spacing/--blur-*/--ease-* primitives — while its semantic palette (ink #262323 at three opacities, surface-raised #fbfbf8, border-card #dee2de, the #1a6fd1 tools band) ships as literal utility colours. Roles are measured on the painted chrome: the dark-gradient CTAs, the glass nav and hero prompt button (backdrop-blur 16–24px), the whole-card plan anchors and the logo tiles. Brand art is pixel-art scenery.

One light theme across the marketing routes, with the #1a6fd1 tools band and the pixel-art hero inside it; app.cofounder.co/os/login is a separate app surface measured at rest as the sign-in boundary. Palette

The page ground is light.surface.page #f5f5f2 (color.surface = declared --background); raised surfaces are light.surface.raised #fbfbf8 and the tools band light.surface.band #1a6fd1. Ink runs light.text.primary #262323cc through muted/faint alphas; the accent control is the dark CTA light.accent (~#202020 gradient base = color.ink.ui), and lime color.lime.300 #bbf451 is the declared accent pair. Borders are light.border.card #dee2de.
