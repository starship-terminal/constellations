# Cofounder patterns

Public pages only. The signed-in product was not entered.

## App shell

A fixed header (91px) over a full-bleed pixel illustration, then long paper sections (`#f5f5f2`) and a footer on the same paper with a grass strip. Phone collapses the nav into an overlay opened by "Open menu". See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png).

## Primary action

"Run a company" is a white 41px pill in the header and a white pill on the hero. Every instance leaves the marketing host for `app.cofounder.co`, which shows the login wall. The page does not collect an email first.

## Capability browser

Home sections 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 list numbered buttons. Each opens a right-hand dialog with a stage map, a close button, and "Do this in Cofounder", which is the login wall. See [screenshots/home-light-capability-dialog.png](screenshots/home-light-capability-dialog.png).

## Pricing and FAQ

One public price ($40 per month per company, including $40 of credits) and an FAQ that expands in place. The plan CTA is the login wall. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Login wall

Centered wordmark, three identity buttons, a legal line, a pixel grass footer. No field. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [login-review.json](login-review.json).

## Not on the public surface

Empty, loading and error states of the product, destructive confirmation, search on the marketing site, and onboarding. Docs have a search field. That surface was listed in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and not measured.
