# Patterns

## Home

The home page is a cream canvas with a fixed header. An orange announcement strip sits above the header and links to an ebook. The header is 64px, `#fdf9f4`, with the wordmark, Product, Solutions, Resources, Slite Agent plus a New badge, Pricing, Sign in, Book demo, and Start for free.

The hero is centered. The h1 is Garnett 56px / 500, `#3f434a`, 900px wide, line-height 67.2px. A UniversalSans paragraph sits under it. Talk to us is the orange pill, with AICPA, HIPAA, and GDPR marks beside it. Below the fold, a product demo card (white, radius 12px, `--box-shadow-small`) shows a help-center note. That demo is a marketing illustration, not the signed-in app.

See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Pricing

Three cards in a 1200px row. Basic and Enterprise are white, radius 16px. Pro is wrapped in an 18px orange frame with a RECOMMENDED label. Prices are 24px / 600. A switch under each paid price says Billed yearly. Get started is a ghost pill on Basic and a filled pill on Pro. Contact Sales is a ghost pill. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Features

The features page leads with a Garnett 36px heading, "Keep your knowledge fresh." Three tabs sit under the copy. The selected tab has a 1px ink border and a 50px radius. A white panel below holds tinted stat cards. See [features-light-default](screenshots/features-light-default.png).

## Sign in

`/organization/select` is the signed-out gate. The page is moon dust (`#f9efe4`). A 560px card holds "Sign in", an email field, a disabled arrow button, and a workspace URL field. Submitting would email a workspace list. That submit was not pressed. See [card-signin-light-default](components/crops/card-signin-light-default.png).

## Create account

`/organization/create` uses the same card and page colour. It lists Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, Continue with Slack, and Continue with email. The first three are grey chips. Email is a blue chip (`#2e77e5`). None were pressed. Terms and privacy are underlined links under the rows.

## Phone

At 390 the header keeps the wordmark and a menu button. The hero type stays 56px and wraps. The open menu is a full-width list with an orange Book demo pill at the bottom. See [home-light-390](screenshots/home-light-390.png) and [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png).
