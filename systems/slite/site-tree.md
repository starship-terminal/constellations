# Slite public site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-22 from [https://slite.com/](https://slite.com/) in an incognito preview tab, signed out. Desktop shots are 1440 × 900 CSS pixels at 2× (PNG 2880 × 1800). Phone shots are 390 × 844 CSS pixels at 2× (PNG 780 × 1688). They are the first viewport, not the full page. The homepage title was "Slite | The AI knowledge base that stays accurate". The first visible heading is "A source of truth for your team. And the AI they work with." This is a live marketing site, not a parked domain and not a captcha wall. `prefers-color-scheme` was light. The page painted cream.

Nothing was typed. No email, password, code, phone, or address was entered. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. No workspace was created. No plan was purchased. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

origin/main already had `systems/slite/` and did not have `systems/slite/site-tree.md`. This walk adds the site tree and screenshots. It does not remeasure tokens. catalog.json and webapps-manifest.json were left alone. `systems/slab` was not touched.
