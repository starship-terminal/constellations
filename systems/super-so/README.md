# Super.so

Super.so turns a Notion page into a website. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out login and signup chrome, measured on 2026-11-19 from a live T3 preview tab. Signup asks for a first name, last name, email, password and a terms checkbox. It was not submitted. Google, Apple and passkey were not followed. No card was entered.

The live CSS viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2. Marketing pages ship with `html` class `theme-light`. The app at https://app.super.so/login ships with `html` class `dark` and `color-scheme: dark`.

## How the values were read

Custom properties and computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on https://super.so/, https://super.so/pricing, https://super.so/features, https://app.super.so/login and https://app.super.so/signup. Declared custom properties came from the same-origin stylesheets. Two cross-origin sheets on assets.super.so could not be read. Colours in the tokens are the computed sRGB values, written as hex. A value that stayed a raw `rgb()` string in the page is recorded as that hex.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on `preview_snapshot`. No screenshots were invented. Headless Playwright was not launched against the login page.

## What to distrust

The CSS viewport was 1169, not 1440 and not 390. Do not treat these readings as a 1440 or 390 capture. Marketing `--color-bg-default` is declared `#ffffff` and computes to `#fdfdfc` (`--base-warm-gray`). `--color-text-default` is declared `#37352F` and computes to `#262a2e`. `--navbar-height` is declared `56px` and the live nav is `80px`. The tokens use the computed values. The nav background is `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`, so the painted bar is not a flat white. The Personal plan button radius is `6px`. The nav and hero CTAs are `8px`. Those are different controls. Auth amber `#ffb224` is not the marketing amber `#ffbe3c`. Screenshot folders are empty on purpose.

## Fonts

Beausite is the marketing face, with Inter behind it. The computed stack is Beausite, Inter, Helvetica, then emoji faces, then the system sans stack. Auth pages compute `beausite` with a `beausite Fallback`, then Inter, Helvetica, Arial. The font files are not in this package. Tokens keep the measured stacks and fall back through Inter.

## Do and don't

- **Use the warm white marketing page.** Home, pricing and features render on `#fdfdfc` with `#262a2e` text and `#66707a` body copy. [patterns.md](patterns.md) describes that shell.
- **Use amber for the primary action.** "Get started free" is `#111111` on `#ffbe3c`, 8px radius, 42px in the nav and 53px in the hero. [components.md](components.md) lists the sizes.
- **Keep the app chrome dark.** Login and signup render on `#151514` with `#ffb224` for Log in. Do not paint the marketing page with the auth amber. [tokens.json](tokens.json) keeps both.
- **Keep signup as a signed-out form.** https://app.super.so/signup asks for a name, email, password and a terms checkbox, plus Google and Apple. Do not invent a signed-in dashboard behind it. [components.md](components.md) records the gap.
- **Do not invent a 1440 screenshot.** The preview browser produced no frames. [screenshots/](screenshots/) says so.
