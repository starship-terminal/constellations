# Patterns

## App shell

Orange header, white page, department strip, then a card grid. Footer is one tan row. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/home-light-footer.png](screenshots/home-light-footer.png).

Phone width keeps the orange header, a search field, and a yellow "Sign in securely" pill. See [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Product page

Photo column, title, price, then yellow Add to cart and orange Buy Now stacked in the right rail. See [screenshots/product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png).

## Empty cart

Illustration, "Your Amazon Cart is empty", yellow Sign in and a quiet Sign up. See [screenshots/cart-light-default.png](screenshots/cart-light-default.png). Those buttons were not clicked.

## Deals

Filter chips, then a product grid with deal badges and prices. See [screenshots/deals-light-default.png](screenshots/deals-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

Centered card on white. Logo, heading, one empty field, yellow Continue. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
