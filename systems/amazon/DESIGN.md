## Type

Amazon Ember is the face on the sign-in wall and the product title once it loads. The homepage body computed to Arial, sans-serif. The font files are not in this package.

The sign-in heading is Amazon Ember 28px, weight 400, line height 36px. Body copy is 14px, line height 20px. The product title is 18px, weight 400. The price whole number is 28px.

## Radius and size

Primary buttons are pills: radius 100px, height 32px. The search field radius is 8px. The search bar form radius is 4px. The header is 60px tall.
