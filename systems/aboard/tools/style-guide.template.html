Aboard design tokens

Measured from aboard.com's public marketing pages in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Most values alias the WordPress theme's own custom properties (the --wp--preset--* palette/type presets and the --rd-* tokens, resolved live and recorded with their provenance in the token file); the rest are computed styles read off the running pages. One theme; the cookie consent was never answered and the newsletter signup was never submitted.

There is no product app behind this site: it is the consultancy's whole public surface. Screenshots and per-component readings are in components.md.

Palette

An editorial warm palette: an off-white cream ground, warm-black and ink text, deep browns and one forest-green preset behind the dark bands, and a single loud accent, bright citrus (#de4c00), which the pill buttons and the whole footer carry.

Type

Two faces: Work Sans for the interface (nav, buttons, fields, eyebrows) and Tobias, a light serif, for display headings and card titles. Eyebrow labels are small caps at 12.8px semibold with ~1.9px tracking; the Tobias display steps run 28.8px row titles to a 123.8px index headline, tracked about -2.5% near a 1.0-1.04 line height.

Tobias is a commercial face served by the theme; this page previews the serif in Source Serif 4 where Tobias is not installed.

Space, radius & structure

Every button, field and chip is a full pill (999px radius); media cards and thumbnails carry a small 10-12px radius; text blocks stay square. The WordPress spacing scale runs 4–96px; the fixed header is 78px (80px declared); pill buttons pad 18-22px on the label side.

Motion

The site ships a small motion vocabulary: --rd-ease (0.32,0.72,0,1) for hovers, --rd-ease-out (0.16,1,0.3,1) for the word-by-word hero reveal and section reveals, --rd-ease-io for in-out moves. Headline words stagger in at 50ms steps; reveals move 48px over ~1.2s.

Buttons & links

Every button is a full pill. The primary is bright citrus with off-white text: on dark bands it darkens to dark-citrus (#c44300), and in the nav it darkens to near-black (#291503). The ink variant sits on light sections and flips to citrus on hover. Hairline buttons (share rows, toggles) draw a 1px ink-at-10% stroke.

Forms

The one public form is the newsletter signup, rendered light (off-white field, ink hairline, ink submit that flips citrus) and dark (translucent cream field, cream hairline, citrus submit). Focus swaps the border to orange-peel and draws a 3px halo, never an outline; the submit's own focus ring is a 2px orange-peel outline offset 3px.

Cards & feeds

Feed rows are a three-column grid (kind tag, light serif title, date) on a warm hairline. Case cards are a 12px-radius media block with an all-caps industry tag and a serif title, lifting on a deep shadow at hover.
