## How the values were read

The marketing theme lives in `@layer theme` on `:root, :host` in the Next stylesheet. Those custom properties were copied from `cssRules`, then checked against `getComputedStyle` on body, the h1, the header Book a demo link, the nav, the product menu, the footer, and the pricing cards. Login colours are HSL custom properties on `app.sharewillow.com/login`, resolved by wrapping each value in `hsl()` and reading the computed colour. Component sizes are `getComputedStyle` on the live elements.

The preview browser could not take a screenshot. Measurements were still read from the attached page.

## What to distrust

The hero and footer palette on the light page is the `light.band` group. It was a `dark` token set, but the site did not switch themes, so it is not registered as a theme. Hover and pressed colours are stylesheet rules (`espresso/90`, `ivory`, `scale: 0.985`), not a hovered element. The marketing focus ring was declared as `ring-espresso/30` and was not forced. Keyboard focus on Product painted the browser outline `auto 1px rgb(229, 151, 0)`. The login checkbox was already checked, so the unchecked fill is missing. Display sizes that use `clamp()` are recorded at 1440, not at 390. Matter and Mackinac are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide falls back to system fonts.

## Fonts

Marketing loads Matter (weights 300 to 700) and Mackinac (P22 Mackinac Pro, weights 400, 500, 700) from `/fonts/*.woff2` on www.sharewillow.com. The login app loads Matter 100 to 900, including italics, from `/fonts/Matter/`, and computes the display face as `p22-mackinac-pro`. Both faces are licensed to ShareWillow. Tokens name them. The style guide uses `ui-sans-serif` and `Georgia`.

## Do and don't

- **Espresso does the asking, lemon marks the exception.** Primary buttons are `#180600` pills. Lemon `#faf082` is the accent chip and the occasional button, with espresso ink on it.
- **Display is Mackinac, UI is Matter.** Headlines use the serif at weight 500 and tight tracking. Labels, nav, and buttons stay in Matter.
- **Pills for actions, 24 and 32 for surfaces.** Buttons and nav links are fully rounded. Menu and pricing cards use 24px or 32px.
- **One light page, with a dark footer.** Do not invent a dark theme. The dark band is `#0d0a07` with `#f5f2e0` ink.
- **Leave the app alone.** Login is a second palette (`#1a1a1a` on white, blue links). Do not mix it into the marketing ivory page.
