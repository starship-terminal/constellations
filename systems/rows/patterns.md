# Patterns

## Marketing shell

Every public page opens with a 60px ink bar (`#3a032d`, padding `10px 0`) that says Rows joined Superhuman. Below it, a cream header (`#fbf9f0`) holds the wordmark, Product, Pricing, Templates, Community, and a text link to Superhuman Docs. The header measures 111px at 1440px. Content width inside the page is 1224px.

[screenshots/product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png)

At 390px the hero drops from 64px to 40px and the same yellow CTA stays 52px tall. [screenshots/product-light-mobile.png](screenshots/product-light-mobile.png)

## Hero

Two-line GT Flexa headline, a short Output Sans paragraph, then one yellow button and one ghost button. A product demo image sits under the fold. It is a marketing illustration, not the signed-in app.

## Banded sections

The home page stacks cream, pale yellow (`#fff4cc`), ink, and yellow (`#ffc800`) bands. Type on ink is white. Type on yellow and cream is `#3a032d`.

## Pricing

Four plan cards (Free, Plus, Pro, Enterprise) on cream, then a comparison table below the fold. The Monthly / Annually switch sits above the cards. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Directories

Integrations, templates, docs, community and blog use a white page, a search field, and cards or posts. Community can show "No results found!". [screenshots/community-light-default.png](screenshots/community-light-default.png)

## Missing page

Unknown paths, including `/login` and `/auth/sign-up`, render the marketing 404: "Oops! Seems like we have an empty cell", with a yellow "Go to homepage" button. [screenshots/not-found-light-default.png](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png)

## Footer

A tall cream footer with a darker panel (`#e8e4d9`) and columns of links. The outline CTA repeats "Continue with Superhuman Docs".

## Signed-out login

Not a Rows pattern anymore. The call to action leaves the site. The Superhuman identity page was opened and not used: Google, Microsoft, Apple, an email field, and a reCAPTCHA notice. No form was submitted.
