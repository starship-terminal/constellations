## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in a live preview tab on `https://rows.com/product` and again in headless Playwright on the public pages listed under Coverage. The site ships hashed CSS modules and no custom properties (`varCount` was 0 on every page). Colours, type, radii, padding, shadows and control heights are `getComputedStyle` readings. Motion durations and easings were counted from the stylesheets loaded on `/product`. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. The preview browser did not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`), so the images are from the headless pass on pages that had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge.

## What to distrust

Template card anchors compute to `rgb(0, 0, 238)`, the user-agent link colour, because the card title sits in an unstyled child. Do not treat that blue as a brand link.

The yellow CTA hover is `filter: brightness(0.95)` with no change in `background-color`. A later hover reading that targeted the first matching link hit a zero-size duplicate and reported a transparent background. The crop and `raw/components.json` use the visible hero button.

Pricing "Get Started" labels compute to `rgba(16, 16, 16, 0.3)`. On the screenshot the coloured border is visible and the label is faint. That colour is recorded as measured, not as a designed disabled state.

`ease-in-out` and `ease` cubic beziers are the CSS keyword expansions, marked Derived. The two declared cubic beziers are Measured.

There is no dark theme on these pages (`color-scheme: normal`).

## Fonts

Display is GT Flexa. Body, nav and controls are Output Sans. Both are commercial faces and are not bundled. Tokens fall back to Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif, which is the stack the page itself declares.

## Do and don't

- **Yellow is the only filled action.** The hero CTA is `#ffc800` on `#3a032d` text, 2px radius, 52px tall. Secondary actions are text or a 2px outline. [screenshots/product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png)
- **Ink is the brand colour, yellow is the accent.** Page ground is `#fbf9f0`. The announcement bar and the "Connected to your data" band use `#3a032d`. Do not invert this pair.
- **Display type is GT Flexa with tight tracking.** The 64px hero uses weight 600 and `-1.28px` tracking. Body stays Output Sans at 18px.
- **Corners stay small.** Buttons are 2px, fields 4px, cards 6px or 8px. Do not round the marketing chrome into pills.
- **The product is no longer behind this domain.** Do not draw a Rows login form. The site sends people to Superhuman Docs, and that login is company SSO plus email behind reCAPTCHA.

## Gaps

- Rows login and signup are gone. `https://rows.com/login`, `/signin`, `/sign-up`, `/auth`, `/auth/sign-up`, `/auth/login`, `/app` and `/dashboard` return the marketing 404. `app.rows.com`, `auth.rows.com` and `id.rows.com` do not resolve.
- The visible handoff is "Continue with Superhuman Docs", which lands on `https://id.superhuman.com/signin` for client `superhumanDocs`. That page offers Google, Microsoft, Apple and "Continue with email", and it says the site is protected by reCAPTCHA. Company SSO and a captcha are outside this capture. Nothing was submitted. No email, card, phone or KYC was entered.
- No dark theme, no signed-in spreadsheet, no dialog, toast, checkbox, radio, switch, text area, or validation error.
- Preview screenshots were unavailable. Images are headless Playwright on public pages that loaded without a Cloudflare challenge.
