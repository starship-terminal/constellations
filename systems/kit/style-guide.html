Palette

Action blue #44b1ff (--color-blue-500) on soft-black #1e1e1e and warm white #f2efe9. The gray, orange, green, purple, red and yellow ramps are declared in the theme layer. The log-in card paints the same soft-black as a fill, with white type.

Spacing, radius and motion

A 4px declared spacing base (--spacing). Declared radii run from --radius-xs (2px) to --radius-3xl (24px). Measured controls sit at 8px (nav and trial pills), 10px (log-in fields), and 12px (log-in submit and pricing CTAs). Controls transition over the declared 150ms cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1).

Type

Two served marketing faces: KitSansFont draws the display headlines (80px on the home hero, 64px on interior mastheads, 48px on the phone). Libre Franklin draws UI and body at 14px and 16px, weight 600 on controls. The log-in card uses Inter at 14px. Samples here fall back to system fonts.
