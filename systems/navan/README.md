Pages read: the homepage, pricing, expense management, and the sign-in boundary at login.navan.com. Themes: light only. Widths measured: 1440 and 390. No account was used. The email field was not filled.

## How the values were read

The homepage was opened in the T3 preview browser. `getComputedStyle` on `document.documentElement` returned 254 product custom properties (Tailwind `--tw-*` names left out). Component values were read from the painted elements on the homepage at 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2, then on `/pricing` and `/product/expense-management`. The expense page was also read at innerWidth 390. `/signin` redirected to the Auth0 identifier page on login.navan.com. That page was measured and left. Cookies were dismissed with "Reject All Cookies". No form was submitted.

The main stylesheet is `https://navan.com/_next/static/css/b9bdd45fb4f1a61e.css`. The Next build id on the homepage HTML was `TPZ67uXuDD8_LITwPLM6o`.

## What to distrust

The cookie banner was dismissed with Reject All Cookies before the readings; every reading was taken after that click.

Screenshots are missing. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so layout claims rest on computed boxes, not images.

The body background is transparent. Page white is the nav and the cards, not the body itself.

`--global-announcement-bar-height` is 55px. The painted strip was 50px.

Seven type styles carry a line height raw/ does not hold: `title` (the hero prompt, not read at all), `price`, `feature`, `faq`, `footer`, `login-heading` (40px, the font size repeated) and `login-label`. They are labelled Reconstruction: those line heights were typed in by hand at capture time.

The login label computed to 13.92px, and the token carries 13.92px. `core.typography.heading` is the h2 reading, 45px / 54px.

Sanomat files are loaded from `/fonts/Sanomat-Regular_2219886656.woff2` and `Sanomat-Semibold_2219886655.woff2` but the computed weight on headings is 600. Inter is declared as a 300–700 range face. Those files are not in this package.

## Fonts

Inter (body, UI) and Sanomat (headings) ship from navan.com `/fonts/`. The login page uses `nvn-regular` and `nvn-heading`. Its `@font-face` src names a Sanomat file on a CloudFront host. The families are proprietary. Tokens name them and fall back to `ui-sans-serif` and `ui-serif`. The files are not committed.

## Do and don't

**Use the blurple pill for the primary action.** Get started is `#6307f8` on a 9999px radius, 44px tall. The dark pill (`#15002c`) is the second action, Request a demo.

**Set headings in Sanomat and UI in Inter.** The homepage h1 is 45/54 at 600. Body copy is Inter 300 at 16/24 with -2% letter-spacing.

**Keep the plan card's purple stroke.** Both Business and Enterprise cards use a 1px `#6307f8` border and a 20px radius, even when the price word is "Free".

**Leave the login page on its own palette.** The identifier page is `#f5f5f8` with a near-black Continue pill (`#131316`) and a `#a14be3` input ring. It does not reuse the marketing blurple.
