# Gumroad design reference

A token set and component reference for Gumroad's signed-out public surfaces — for building marketplace landing pages, product grids, product/creator pages, auth cards and public checkout frames in the style of the real thing. It was read from **gumroad.com** and one creator subdomain, **ichigo.gumroad.com**, on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: every public marketing route the site serves — /, /discover, a category page (/design), /features, /pricing, /about, /blog, /gumclaw, /help, /terms, /login and /signup — plus a real product page, its creator profile and the public checkout its "Subscribe" link opens, in the painted light theme, with verified `prefers-color-scheme: dark` repaints on the landing, Discover, category, features, pricing, help, login and signup; the Discover featured-products carousel, the phone nav menu, the product Share popover, the Monthly→Yearly billing-term switch and the empty checkout form as transient states; plus 390px passes on the landing, Discover, login and the product page.

gumroad.com is worth a capture because it declares a complete token layer to the browser: a Tailwind v4 `@theme` plus a hand-written semantic layer — the six brand colours `--pink #ff90e8`, `--purple #90a8ed`, `--green #23a094`, `--orange #ffc900`, `--red #dc341e`, `--yellow #f1f333`, semantic roles declared as RGB channel triplets (`--accent`, `--accent-with-text`, `--primary`, `--color`, `--filled`, `--active-bg`, `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger`, `--info`, `--indicator` and the `--contrast-*` partners), `--body-bg`, `--border`/`--border-alpha`/`--border-width`, `--border-radius-1..3`, the hard offset shadows `--box-shadow-1`/`--box-shadow-2`, `--spacer-1..8`, `--form-element-height`, `--button-hover-offset`, `--main-stack-width`/`--spacing-product-page`/`--dynamic-grid`/`--grid-cols-sidebar`, the proprietary face **ABC Favorit** on `--font-family`, the `--text-xs..9xl` ramp with `--leading-*`, `--font-weight-*`, `--transition-duration`/`--ease-*`/`--animate-*` motion and `--z-index-*` steps. The architecture: every page declares `color-scheme: light dark` and boots **light** — the `light` token set is that painted surface — and a real `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block repaints the roles (`--body-bg` #f4f4f0→#242423, `--color` #000→#ddd, `--border-alpha` 1→0.35; the accent and status colours hold). There is no in-page toggle, so colour-scheme emulation is the product's own theme mechanism and every dark shot and state was verified against the painted surface. The product subdomain inlines a `:root` block that pins its own light palette over the shared sheet, so product and creator pages never repaint — recorded as observed behaviour, not a third theme. **Boundary:** everything behind login — the seller dashboard, the buyer library, payouts and settings — stayed signed out; the checkout was captured empty and never submitted. Nothing was signed into, typed, searched, sent, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides (the carousel arrow, the nav menu button, the Share popover, the billing-term select), and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): the cross-origin Vite sheet's response body plus the inline CSSOM is reparsed, rules are binned theme/darkscope/app/vendor/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in a probe document carrying the real sheets, once under light emulation and once under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` — the product page's light-pinned inline styles resolve in their own `product-light`/`product-dark` profiles. `tokens.json` never publishes a raw channel triplet — every colour is the painted value.
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link can fire), opened/focused states via the component's own toggle.
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`) verify the painted theme before writing a sidecar: the computed `color-scheme`, `matchMedia` and the sampled background luminance must all agree. `-full` captures scroll the document for lazy content and are bounded at eight chunks, recorded in the shot's `.capture.json`.
- **Dark states** are painted by `page.emulateMedia({ colorScheme })` — the site's own media-query theme; there is no in-page toggle. Product and creator pages were probed for a repaint, found none, and were left light — an observed absence.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label, and records where each was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.
- **Motion** (`tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` → `raw/motion.json`) reads computed transitions and animations at rest, then again under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; `tools/motion.py` turns the readings into the `core.motion` group.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **Post-login surfaces**: the seller dashboard, buyer library, payouts and settings were never reached — the capture was signed out end to end. Do not present this package's tokens as the dashboard's.
- **Product/creator dark**: the subdomain's inline styles pin a light palette; no `product-dark-*` or `creator-dark-*` shots exist because the live page does not repaint. Any dark product page is a guess, not evidence.
- **Search results**: the marketplace search fields were measured empty; no query was ever submitted.
- **Checkout beyond the form**: the public checkout page was captured empty — payment, confirmation and post-purchase screens were never reached.
- **The checkout readings were taken past a state change.** Reaching the checkout page meant pressing Subscribe / opening a checkout URL for a real product, which starts a checkout session (a server-side cart) on gumroad.com. The committed `checkout-*` readings and the checkout screenshot stay as evidence; the capture scripts now stop at the button (edited 2026-11, not rerun).
- **Two pressed readings equal their default.** The capture forced `:active` on the topmost node at each control's centre (its label span or icon), not on the control, so the control's own `:active` rule could not apply: `home-search-button` and `login-submit` read pressed identical to default. The script now forces the control itself (edited, not rerun); those pressed states need a recapture.
- **ABC Favorit rendering**: the face is served from assets.gumroad.com and is not shipped here; crops show it, the style guide falls back to system fonts.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Reduced motion**: the probe saw prefers-reduced-motion partially honoured (three of 185 moving elements removed) — see `raw/motion.json`.
- **Partial readings**: `discover-load-more` and `product-buy` pressed states failed at the CDP layer, and the `product-ratings` and `nav-category-active` crops timed out — the state entries in components.md say so; the values that were read stand.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the marketing surface light, and let the OS scheme carry dark.** Every page boots light — cream `--body-bg` #f4f4f0, black `--color` ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); dark is a real painted surface behind `prefers-color-scheme`, not a guess ([dark](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)).
2. **Do spend the pink on hover and accent, not on surfaces.** #ff90e8 fills the "Start selling" button on hover and tints the price flag; surfaces stay cream, white and black — restraint is the pattern ([Start selling hover](components/crops/nav-start-selling-hover.png), [price flag](components/crops/discover-card-price-default.png)).
3. **Do keep the borders hard.** 1px solid-black borders on `--border-width`, 4px radius on cards and controls, and `4px 4px 0` offset shadows on hover — the neo-brutalist grammar is the system ([button](components/crops/nav-login-hover.png), [card](components/crops/discover-card-default.png)).
4. **Do let one face carry everything.** ABC Favorit draws the wordmark, the 96px hero display, body and controls; code falls back to the system mono stack ([hero heading](components/crops/home-h1-default.png)).
5. **Do respect the measured geometry.** `--form-element-height` is 48px and the header, search, buttons and selects all land on it ([header](components/crops/nav-bar-default.png), [login card](components/crops/login-card-default.png)).
6. **Don't style the dashboard or buyer library from this package.** They sit behind login and were never captured — the signed-out marketing, marketplace, product and checkout surfaces are all this package claims ([login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)).
7. **Don't invent a dark product page.** The product subdomain pins a light palette over the shared sheet — it does not repaint under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` ([product](screenshots/product-light-default.png)).
8. **Don't fabricate checkout or payment states.** The form was captured empty and never submitted; payment, confirmation and library screens are unverified ([checkout](screenshots/product-light-checkout.png)).
9. **Don't copy the brand or the face.** The GUMROAD wordmark, the icons and the ABC Favorit files are Gumroad's ([wordmark](components/crops/nav-logo-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.

### Using tokens

| Role | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| page | #f4f4f0 `light.ground.bodyBg` | #242423 `dark.ground.bodyBg` |
| surface | #ffffff `light.component.card.bg` | #000000 `dark.component.card.bg` |
| text | #000000 `light.role.primary` | #dddddd `dark.role.primary` |
| muted | rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5) `light.role.muted` | rgba(221, 221, 221, 0.5) `dark.role.muted` |
| accent | #ff90e8 `core.color.brand.pink` | #ff90e8 `core.color.brand.pink` |
| border | #000000 `light.role.border` | rgba(221, 221, 221, 0.35) `dark.role.border` |

Fonts: `core.font.family.product` is ABC Favorit, then 6 fallbacks. Scales, counted as [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) holds them: 9 type styles (`core.typography`), 11 radius steps (`core.radius`), 9 spacing steps (`core.space`).

### Using components

Read [components.md](components.md) for the component inventory; [patterns.md](patterns.md) for how components combine into screens; 107 element crops in [components/crops/](components/crops); [style-guide.html](style-guide.html), which renders every token and the main components.
