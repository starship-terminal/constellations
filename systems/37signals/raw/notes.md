# Readings

`public-readings.json` is the first Playwright pass: links, computed samples, custom properties. Page colours in that file are the random theme of that visit.

`extra-readings.json` holds the jobs form fields and the button CSS.

`states.json` holds the 1440 measurements used in tokens.json: body 34.4px, header 71px, button 36px, email field empty.

Screenshots were recaptured with `--rgb-theme` pinned to `139, 119, 255` (`--rgb-theme-3`). The podcast page was left on its own white stylesheet.
