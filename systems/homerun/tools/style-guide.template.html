Homerun, declared and measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. homerun.co is a Webflow build whose :root carries TWO palette layers — the current --_updated-colors-v2---* set (beige grounds, the #4f75fe blue ramp, the warm brown ink scale, secondary and tertiary accents) and a large legacy named layer it replaced — so the core colors are the updated declarations, with every legacy name kept under color.named.*, and the roles are measured on the painted chrome: the blue primary buttons, the dark #2d2323 demo buttons and CTA band, the beige-tinted bands and inputs.

One light theme across every surface, with dark #2d2323 content bands inside it. jobs.homerun.co is the employer careers site on the same system (its own #466af6 button blue); app.homerun.co/login and app.homerun.hr/Account/Logon are sign-in boundaries measured at rest — nothing was typed, sent or submitted. Palette

The page ground is light.surface.page #ffffff (color.white); tinted bands take light.surface.band #f5f1ec, fields light.app.field #f5f3ef (color.beige.500) and the dark band light.surface.dark #2d2323 (color.brown.500). Brand controls take light.accent #4f75fe (color.blue.500) and darken to light.accentHover/light.accentPressed #254cda (color.blue.600); the dark buttons lift toward light.darkFillHover #4a3e3e (color.brown.300, also the body ink). The careers site runs its own light.careers.accent #466af6 and the HR card its light.app.hrAccent #00b16b.
