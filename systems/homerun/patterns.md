# Homerun patterns

How the public site combines the measured pieces. Values are in [tokens.json](tokens.json). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

A white page, a 90px header at 1440, and a 1080px content column. The wordmark sits left. Nav links are GT America 16/22 in `#4a3e3e`, with a beige hover wash. The right cluster is an outlined Log in pill, a coffee-black Book a demo pill, and a blue Start free trial pill.

At 390 the nav collapses to the wordmark, the blue trial pill, and a hamburger. The hamburger opens a white sheet with the same links, Log in, and Book a demo.

- Screens: [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

## Hero

Display is GT Walsheim 64/64 weight 900 in `#382d2d`. The lead is GT America. Two pills sit under it: blue Try free for 15 days (52px) and coffee-black Book a demo. The right side is a product illustration, labelled as a public demo, not the signed-in app.

- Screen: [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Product pages and lists

ATS, HR, pricing, features, blog, guides, templates, and integrations share the same header and footer. Pricing adds FAQ disclosures. Integrations adds category checkboxes. Blog and customer stories add category radios and cards.

- Screens: [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [blog-light-default](screenshots/blog-light-default.png), [integrations-light-default](screenshots/integrations-light-default.png)

## Login wall

Log in in the header opens a menu. Organise your hiring goes to app.homerun.co/login. That page asks for email and password, or Google or Microsoft, and offers an HR tab. The branch stops there. Start free trial goes to /trial, which redirects into registration. That branch stops too.

- Screen: [login-light-wall](screenshots/login-light-wall.png)
