# Homerun design reference

A token set and component reference for Homerun's public surface — for building clean light marketing pages and a warm-brown-and-blue control language in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.homerun.co** (a Webflow marketing build), **jobs.homerun.co** (Homerun's own employer careers app), **app.homerun.co/login** (the Angular ATS sign-in) and **app.homerun.hr/Account/Logon** (the .NET HR sign-in) on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

Homerun is worth a capture because the hiring/ATS product runs **one design system across four different stacks**: the Webflow marketing sheet carries two declared palette layers at once — the current `--_updated-colors-v2---*` set (beige grounds `#faf9f7`–`#dbd9d5`, the `#4f75fe` blue ramp, the `#a3a2a2`–`#131313` warm-brown ink scale, secondary and tertiary accents) and a large legacy named layer it replaced (coffee-*, hr-*, ~60 one-off names, kept verbatim under `color.named.*`). The Angular sign-in declares its own `--ui-*` control roles (10px radii, 44px control heights, the same beige and brown roles) and the card paints exactly those values; the careers site and HR card reuse the same palette with their own accents (`#466af6` careers blue, `#00b16b` HR green). It paints a **single light theme**: white `#ffffff` ground, `#f5f3ef` beige input fills and `#2d2323` dark content bands inside it. The type is **GT Walsheim 900 for headlines and GT America for everything else** — both Grilli Type faces served from the product's CDN. **Boundary:** the ATS and HR apps sit behind sign-in; both cards were measured at rest, nothing was typed or submitted, and the public application wizard was never advanced past its first step.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do keep it light.
2. Do pair the two button fills.
3. Do press the blue darker, not lighter.
4. Do set headlines in GT Walsheim 900 and everything else in GT America.
5. Do round the content, not the chrome.
6. Do use the beige scale for quiet structure.
7. Don't style the signed-in products from this package.
8. Don't reuse the careers blue on the marketing site.
9. Don't treat the wizard as measured past its first step.
10. Don't invent a dark theme or disabled states.
11. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every number in [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md) is either a **declared** CSS custom property harvested from the live stylesheets (`tools/capture/vars.mjs` — the Webflow `:root` layers on www, the careers app's sheets on jobs, the Angular bundles' `--ui-*` layer on app, each surface's names resolved on a probe document carrying that surface's own sheets) or a **measured** computed style read off the painted DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`). Screenshots come from `tools/capture/public/public.mjs`, a bounded Playwright shooter at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 that tiles pages over the GPU-texture cap into `.partN` files (the careers and ATS fulls run past 17k px at DPR 2). Component crops are element screenshots taken at measurement time. States: `hover` is a real pointer hover (the Webflow nav dropdowns open on `data-hover`, so panels were held open through a real mouse path), `focus-visible` a real keyboard focus (or `el.focus()` where noted), `pressed` a `CSS.forcePseudoState` CDP call, `opened` the component's own toggle — each state's `via` field in the JSON says which. The mobile menu's button is unreachable by `tap()` through the nav's pointer-event layering, so it is opened by an in-page `el.click()` and the open is verified before reading. The cookie banners and the Intercom launcher were hidden without answering them; consent controls were never clicked.

## What to distrust

- **`pricing-table`, `pricing-table-head` and `pricing-table-row`** kept earlier readings — the feature table sits below the lazy-load line and had not painted on the recapture pass; their JSON entries carry `recaptureMissed` provenance.
- **`ats-tab`** reads 0 x 0 (the tab labels paint through a script that had not run at measure time) and **`apply-file`/`apply-checkbox`** read 0 x 0 (the wizard hides its real inputs under custom controls) — recorded, not invented.
- **The careers accent `#466af6` is not `blue-500`.** jobs.homerun.co paints its own blue a step off the marketing `#4f75fe`; both are tokenised separately rather than merged.
- **`apphr` declares no custom properties at all** — the HR sign-in is plain Tailwind utility CSS, so its tokens are measured values, not declarations.
- **Pixel colours in screenshots are quantized** (256-colour PNGs); read exact colours from the tokens or the raw files.
- **The `color.named.*` layer is a declaration dump, not a curated scale** — it holds every legacy name the sheet still declares, including duplicates and aliases (e.g. `named.royal-blue` = `blue.500`).

## Fonts

- **GT America** (buttons, nav, body — measured on every marketing control) and **GT Walsheim** (hero and section headlines, weight 900) are Grilli Type commercial faces served from the product's CDN (`assets.website-files.com`). They are not redistributable; the style guide falls back to a system sans.
- **GTAmerica-Regular** is the careers site's own face; the job list and apply wizard paint **Inter** (Google Fonts). The app bundle names the same family `GT-America`; the HR card paints the `ui-sans-serif, system-ui` stack.
- 66 `@font-face` records were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json).

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep it light.** Every captured surface paints the same light theme — white ground, brown ink; the dark bands are content inside it, not a switchable theme ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [cta-band](components/crops/cta-band-default.png)).
2. **Do pair the two button fills.** The blue `#4f75fe` (`light.accent`) and the dark `#2d2323` (`light.darkFill`) are one language at `radius 12px` — primary action in blue, secondary in dark, never a third hue ([nav](components/crops/nav-trial-default.png), [hero pair](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png)).
3. **Do press the blue darker, not lighter.** Hover/pressed on blue controls reads `#254cda` = `color.blue.600`; dark controls lift toward `#4a3e3e` ([pressed](components/crops/hero-cta-pressed.png)).
4. **Do set headlines in GT Walsheim 900 and everything else in GT America.** The display face carries hero and section titles (64px/48px); UI, body and controls carry the text face ([hero](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [h2](components/crops/section-h2-default.png)).
5. **Do round the content, not the chrome.** Cards and accordions run 10–32px radii while controls stay at 10–12px and pills reach 30–40px ([plan card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png), [CTA band](components/crops/cta-band-default.png)).
6. **Do use the beige scale for quiet structure.** Inputs sit on `#f5f3ef` (beige-500), card hairlines on `#e3e1dd` (beige-700), section tints near `#f5f1ec` ([field](components/crops/signin-email-default.png), [band](components.md#testimonial-card)).
7. **Don't style the signed-in products from this package.** Both sign-in cards are boundaries measured at rest — the ATS and HR apps behind them are unmeasured ([sign-in](components/crops/signin-card-default.png), [HR sign-in](components/crops/hr-card-default.png)).
8. **Don't reuse the careers blue on the marketing site.** `#466af6` is jobs.homerun.co's own accent; marketing CTAs are `#4f75fe` — the two are tokenised separately ([careers](components/crops/careers-all-jobs-default.png)).
9. **Don't treat the wizard as measured past its first step.** Nothing was typed, uploaded or submitted; the file input and checkbox are custom-painted over 0x0 inputs ([apply](screenshots/apply-light-default.png)).
10. **Don't invent a dark theme or disabled states.** Neither exists on the signed-out surface; `light.surface.dark` is the dark band, not a theme ([measurements](raw/measurements.md)).
11. **Don't copy the brand.** The wordmark, the Grilli Type faces, the testimonial photography and the customer logos are Homerun's and its licensors' ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.
