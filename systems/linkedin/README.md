# LinkedIn design reference

A token set and component reference for LinkedIn's public guest site (https://www.linkedin.com/). Captured signed out, in headless Chromium, on 2026-11-21. Desktop captures are 1440 by 900 at 1x. Phone captures are 390 by 844 at 2x. The signed-in feed, messaging, and profile were not reached.

The guest homepage paints a marketing page: white page, `#0a66c2` filled Join now, an outline Sign in, and a `#f3f2f0` band of topic chips. The product app sits behind https://www.linkedin.com/login/, which asks for email and password, or Sign in with Apple. That wall was recorded and not entered.

This package contains no LinkedIn code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show belong to LinkedIn ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)).

## How the values were read

- Screenshots were taken by [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) in headless Chromium. Viewport sidecars record `innerWidth` 1440 or 390. They live in [raw/capture/](raw/capture).
- Computed styles were read by [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) on the live pages. Hover and active on the Join now button were forced with `CSS.forcePseudoState`.
- The guest homepage stylesheet `https://static.licdn.com/aero-v1/sc/h/assets/DD4d0Fjc.css` declares 412 palette colours on `:root` and 381 `light-dark()` aliases on `:root, .cfsa`. Those names are hashed (`--cfsa41` is `#0A66C2`). The extract is [raw/css-extract.json](raw/css-extract.json).
- Page and surface colours were sampled from the unquantised homepage shot, because the body background is transparent and the gray band is not a computed style on `body`.
- No form was submitted. Language was not changed. Google, Apple, and Facebook were not clicked.

## What to distrust

- **The app is absent.** Feed, messaging, notifications, and profile need an account. Login and signup screenshots show the wall and nothing past it.
- **Dark is declared, not painted.** The stylesheet has `light-dark()` and `[data-color-scheme="light"]`. No captured page paints dark. `dark` in tokens.json has no colour values.
- **Hashed names are not a public token API.** `--cfsa41` is the stylesheet's name for the brand blue. It is not a documented role name.
- **The jobs screenshot includes a sign-in dialog** that opened on its own. The list underneath is the page. The dialog was not submitted.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Phone home is covered by Google and Agree & Join sheets.** Those sheets were not dismissed, because dismissing them can start a sign-in.

## Fonts

The painted UI uses the system stack read from `body`: `-apple-system`, `system-ui`, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, and the rest of that stack. The stylesheet also declares `--cfsc` as a monospace stack. Neither face is vendored here.

## Do and don't

- **Do use `#0a66c2` for the one filled action.** Join now on the guest home is that blue, white label, 24px radius, 48px tall. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do keep the page white and the next band `#f3f2f0`.** The hero is white. The topic band is the warm gray. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do set body text at 16px on the system stack, and the guest headline at 48px / 60px, weight 400.** [raw/layout.json](raw/layout.json)
- **Don't treat the login wall as the product.** It asks for email and password, or Sign in with Apple. [screenshots/login-light-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-wall.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme from the hashed `light-dark()` aliases.** They are declared and were not painted.
