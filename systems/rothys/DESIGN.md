## Geometry

The announcement bar is 37px. The header is 71px, with a 1px `#dcdbdd` rule. The wordmark is 140 by 20, inset 40px. Painted controls in this pass use 0 radius. The declared small radius is 4px.

## Components

The primary mark is roth on white. The sale price is `#da3522` beside a struck midnight compare price. Select a size is a midnight outline, and it was not clicked.
