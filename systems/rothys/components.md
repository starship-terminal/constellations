# Components

Values are computed styles from the signed-out store on 2026-11-22. Crops were not taken. The screenshots are the evidence.

## Announcement bar

37px, `#9a1b2c`, 12px Maison Neue Book, `#ffffff`. The live slide says the sale is up to 40% off and links to `/collections/all-shoes-bags`. Previous and next were not clicked.

Shown in [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Header

71px, white, 1px `#dcdbdd` bottom rule. Department labels are 12px Maison Neue Medium, weight 500, tracking 2px, uppercase, `#03143b`. The wordmark is an inline SVG, fill `#9A1B2C`, 140 by 20.

Search, country, wishlist, log in, and cart sit at the right. Log in and cart were not opened.

## Product title and price

Collection and product titles are 40px Grifo S Light, weight 300, `#03143b`. The sale price is 16px `#da3522`. The compare price is 16px `#03143b` with a line-through, weight 100.

Shown in [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Select a size

A full-width outline control, midnight border, white face, uppercase tracking. It was not clicked. Size chips were visible and were not selected. Add to bag was not on screen and was not clicked.

## Collection card

A photo, a white Best Seller chip, the style name, a struck compare price, a sale price, and a Shop everything on sale outline. Filters were not applied.

Shown in [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Gaps

- No hover, focus, or pressed crops.
- Log in, wishlist, cart, country, search, subscribe, and stores were not opened.
- Phone width was not captured.
- The footer was read from the DOM and was not photographed, because it holds the subscribe field.
