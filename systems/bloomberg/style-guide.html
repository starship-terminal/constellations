Palette

The chrome is near-achromatic: black bars on white, body text near-black on white. The accent blue carries subscribe and paywall actions; market data adds gain green, loss red and the Bloomberg red live dot.

Spacing and radius

A tight editorial grid: small 4–8px gaps inside components, larger gutters between story blocks. Corners stay square on chrome; pills round on ticker chips and buttons.
