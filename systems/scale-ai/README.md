## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://scale.com/` in its own tab
(`reuseExistingTab` false). `getComputedStyle` read the home page, then
`https://dashboard.scale.com/login`, then `https://scale.com/demo`. Custom
properties were read from the resolved `:root`. A few hover and focus rules
were read from the page's stylesheets and are labelled as stylesheet rules
in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json). The demo email field was focused
in the page so its focus ring is a live computed value.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot
frames", so no PNG was saved. Colours, type, radii, and spacing still come
from the attached page. Nothing was invented to fill a missing screenshot.

## What to distrust

The viewport is the pane's fill size, 1169px, not a requested 1440. Phone
width was not set. Social-login pills report a transparent anchor
background. Their face colour is a gap. Spacing steps 2 and 5 are multiples
of the measured `--spacing` and are marked derived. The mono stack's generic
tail is derived. The chart green is the most common non-transparent fill in
the home DOM. It is decorative, not a button colour. Login and marketing
are different products: Inter on the dashboard, Aeonik on the marketing site.

## Fonts

Aeonik is the marketing face, loaded at weight 400. The page also names
`aeonik Fallback`. Mono is the eyebrow face, loaded. Inter is the login
face, with `-apple-system` and Arial behind it. Aeonik and the mono face
are commercial. The tokens name them and fall back to system stacks. The
font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use black for the primary action.** Get Started, Submit, and the login
  button are `#000000` with white type. The outline Log In stays grey on a
  hairline.
- **Keep the two products apart.** Marketing type is Aeonik at a tight
  negative tracking. The login page is Inter, with 24px pills and a grey
  field fill.
- **Leave the rotating word colours on display type only.** Sand and pine
  appear on the home headlines, not on buttons.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** The page set `data-theme` to light and
  did not offer a switch on these surfaces.
- **Do not submit the demo form as if it were signup.** It asks for a name,
  a company, and a job title.
