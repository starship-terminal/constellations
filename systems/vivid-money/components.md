# Components

Measured on the public marketing site and the signed-out business login, 2026-11-19. Element crops were not cut. The page screenshots show the controls.

## Button

Get started on the home page is a pill. Fill `#7d33f6`, label `#ffffff`, radius `100px`, 16px weight 500, padding `15px 23px`, height 56px. Hover and focus set the fill to `#a372fa` (stylesheet rule `.kk6taH:not(:disabled):hover`). Login is an outline pill: 1px solid `#7d33f6`, label `#7d33f6`, height 48px, padding `11px 23px`. Focus on that link filled it with `#f4edff`.

The login Continue button is a different control: fill `#6a26d9`, radius `16px`, height 56px, Inter 16px weight 400.

## Link

Nav items are 16px weight 500, colour `#333333`. The login page link "Services and Privacy info" is `#6a26d9` at 14px. Underline was `none` on the nav links that were read.

## Text field

The signup wall fields are 56px tall, 480px wide, fill `#f5f5f4`, border `#f5f5f4`, radius `10px`. The login email field is a white box, 1px solid `#eceef2`, radius `8px`, height 58px, Inter 14px. Nothing was typed into either.

## Tabs

Pricing uses a track of `#f5f5f4`, radius `50px`, padding `2px`, height 52px. The selected tab is white, radius `48px`, padding `12px 36px`, 16px weight 500. The login Phone/Email control is smaller: track `#f0f0ef`, radius `10px`, selected pill white at radius `8px`.

## Menu

The Products menu is a white panel, radius `12px`, padding `24px`, shadow `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08) 0px 8px 32px`. [screenshots/home-light-desktop-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-desktop-menu.png)

## Dialog

The cookie dialog was on the first load. Accept is the purple pill. Reject non-essential is fill `#f5f5f4`, radius `100px`. The heading "Cookies" is 28px weight 500. [screenshots/home-light-desktop-cookie.png](screenshots/home-light-desktop-cookie.png)

## Card

Plan cards are radius `24px`, padding `24px`. Free Start and Basic are white with a 1px `#eceef2` border. Pro is `#f4edff`. [screenshots/pricing-light-desktop.png](screenshots/pricing-light-desktop.png)

## Badge

"NEW" sits next to menu items in purple. Its exact computed style was not isolated, so no badge token is recorded.

## Gaps

- Checkbox, radio, switch, select, text area, toast, table and avatar were not measured as product controls. The pricing page shows a monthly/annual switch. Its computed style was not isolated, so it stays a gap.
- Hover was read from the stylesheet for the primary button. Pressed, disabled, loading and error states were not opened.
- Focus on the primary button changed the fill to the hover colour and left outline at `none`. No separate focus ring was measured.
- The signed-in app, card entry, phone OTP and KYC were not entered.
- Element crops were not cut. The screenshots are the evidence.
