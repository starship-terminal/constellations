# Deezer design reference

A token set and component reference for Deezer's public marketing site and its signed-out web app — for building music-streaming marketing pages, catalog shells, player bars and auth forms in the style of the real thing. It was read from **deezer.com** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /en/offers, /en/offers/premium, /explore/features/, /explore/download/, the public catalog pages /en/artist/27, /en/album/302127 and /en/playlist/3155776842, the account.deezer.com login and signup forms, plus 390px passes on the artist page and home.

Deezer is worth a capture because it ships its real design system to the browser: every stylesheet declares the **Tempo** token layer outright — 675-step palette scales (`--tempo-colors-*`), full semantic background/text/icon/border role sets per theme, spacing, sizes, radii, shadows, transitions, letter-spacings, line-heights, font weights, breakpoints and `--ease-*` curves — on top of a Chakra-UI app. The surfaces split into a light marketing hero (white field, `#0f0d13` ink, `#a238ff` accent, 140px Deezer Brand display) and a dark everywhere-else theme (`#0f0d13` canvas, `#141216` sidebar, `#242326` fields, `#ad47ff` accent on Mix/Sign-up/play, `#bb73ff` hover, 2px accent focus borders), with a lavender `#ead1ff` signup banner pinned over an 80px player bar. Four served voices: Inter for UI, Deezer Product for marketing headings, Deezer Brand for the display, Deezer Headline VF for app headings. **Boundary:** everything behind account.deezer.com was never reached — the login and signup forms were measured as forms, never submitted; Try-for-free, Mix, Play, queue and volume actions were measured, never pressed or followed; the GDPR cookie modal was hidden, never answered.

### Principles

1. Do let Tempo carry the design.
2. Do spend the purple on actions.
3. Do keep the two painted themes honest.
4. Do pair the four voices.
5. Do use the 16px control pill.
6. Do treat focus as a border.
7. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
8. Don't fabricate playback.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Do and don't

1. **Do let Tempo carry the design.** The product ships its token layer: use `color.tempo.<palette>.<step>` scales and the per-theme `color.<role>` semantics rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. **Do spend the purple on actions.** `#a238ff` (light) and `#ad47ff` (dark) fill Sign-up, Try-for-free, Mix and play buttons; body surfaces stay neutral. See [a-signup](components/crops/a-signup-default.png).
3. **Do keep the two painted themes honest.** The home hero is the only light surface; offers, features, download, the catalog app and the account forms all paint dark. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [artist-dark-default](screenshots/artist-dark-default.png).
4. **Do pair the four voices.** Deezer Brand owns the marketing display (140px hero), Deezer Product marketing headings and the phone surface, Deezer Headline VF app page headings, Inter every control. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png) and [artist-name](components/crops/artist-name-default.png).
5. **Do use the 16px control pill.** CTAs, Mix, search fields and banner buttons share it; small pills drop to 12px, cards to 4-8px, popovers lift to 24px, toggles and round buttons go full. See [mix-btn](components/crops/mix-btn-default.png).
6. **Do treat focus as a border.** Fields draw a 2px `#ad47ff` border, not an outline ring. See [a-search](components/crops/a-search-focused.png).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything behind account.deezer.com was never reached; the login/signup forms and the signed-out catalog shell are the evidence.
8. **Don't fabricate playback.** Play, Mix, queue and volume were measured at rest; what playing does signed out is unverified.

## Known gaps

- `light.background.page` (`#ffffff`) is a screenshot sample: no measured element reports the page field behind the home hero. Its source says so.
- `menu-popover`, `artist-avatar` and `menu-item` were not found by the 2026-11-13 re-run (`recaptureMissed`); the readings kept from the earlier run feed `core.radius.popover`, `core.shadow.popover`, `core.component.popover-width` and `core.component.artist-image`, and their sources say so.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.
