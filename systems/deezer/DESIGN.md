# Deezer

Public signed-out capture, 2026-11-21. Take values from [tokens.json](tokens.json). This file is a one-theme summary of the dark player plus a few marketing measurements.

## Colors

Dark page is black. Surface is `#141216`. Accent in the player and on the login wall is `#ad47ff`. Marketing Sign up is `#a238ff`.

## Typography

Body is Inter. Player headings are Deezer Product. Marketing hero is Deezer Brand at 140px, weight 800. Those display faces are proprietary and are not in the repo.

## Layout

Marketing pages are a black header on a white page. The channel player is a dark shell with a left nav, search, cards, a signup banner and a bottom player. Phone width is 390. The burger opens a full-screen drawer.

## Components

Buttons, fields, search, cards, the cookie dialog and the phone drawer are in [components.md](components.md).

## Do's and Don'ts

Use the measured purple for primary actions. Do not treat the login wall as a signed-in app. Read [README.md](README.md).
