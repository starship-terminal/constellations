# The New York Times

Public signed-out marketing and login chrome for The New York Times, captured 2026-11-19 from https://www.nytimes.com/, https://www.nytimes.com/subscription, and https://myaccount.nytimes.com/auth/login. No account. English. The homepage and the offer page carried `tpl-always-light`. Desktop readings are at innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731, devicePixelRatio 2. Phone readings are the login page at innerWidth 390, innerHeight 844, devicePixelRatio 2. A 1440 CSS-pixel width was not reached.

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export the-new-york-times` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use the ink button for the next step on the offer and the login form.
2. Use the blue masthead face only for Subscribe and Log in on the homepage.
3. Set headlines in Cheltenham and UI in Franklin.
4. Keep the page white.
5. Do not invent a dark theme from the always-dark custom properties.
6. Do not treat the advertisement slot as product chrome.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://www.nytimes.com/ with `reuseExistingTab` false. Styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on the live page: computed style on the elements named in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json), and resolved custom properties in [raw/tokens-resolved.json](raw/tokens-resolved.json). The offer page was a same-tab navigation to the Subscribe href. Login was a same-tab navigation to the email form. Phone width used `preview_resize` freeform 390 by 844, then `window.innerWidth`.

`preview_snapshot` failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." No screenshot or crop was invented. Headless Playwright was not launched. The pages had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge, and the frames error is why screenshots are missing.

30 stylesheets were readable and 28 were cross-origin. 521 custom properties were collected from the readable sheets. Cheltenham, Franklin, and Imperial are named in computed `font-family` and are not shipped in this package.

## What to distrust

- Dark colour pairs exist as `--tpl-color-*-always-dark`. The page class was `tpl-always-light`. Those dark values were not painted.
- Button pressed, disabled, and hover colours in `--tpl-theme-button-*` were not painted.
- The selected offer card computed border was 0. Do not invent a selected border from the unselected `#dbdbdb` line.
- Search focus set outline to none. The 2px accent outline is the `:focus-visible` rule, not the painted search field.
- BEST VALUE letter-spacing computed as `8%`. That string is stored as measured. It is not converted to em.
- [screenshots/home-light-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-light-unavailable.png) is a white label, not a capture of the page. Do not sample colours from it.
- The login URL carried a `redirect_uri` query. That value is not stored here.
- The "$0.50/week" line is campaign copy, not a token.
- The first 271px of the homepage is an advertisement slot.

## Fonts

Cheltenham (`nyt-cheltenham`, then Georgia and Times New Roman) for headlines. Franklin (`nyt-franklin`, then Helvetica and Arial) for UI. Imperial (`nyt-imperial`, then Georgia) for story summaries. All three NYT families are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide uses Georgia and Helvetica.

## Do and don't

- **Use the ink button for the next step on the offer and the login form.** Continue is `#121212` with white Franklin.
- **Use the blue masthead face only for Subscribe and Log in on the homepage.** That face is `#567b95` with a `#326891` border, 11px Franklin, uppercase.
- **Set headlines in Cheltenham and UI in Franklin.** Summaries are Imperial.
- **Keep the page white.** Body text on the homepage is `#363636`. Headlines are `#121212`.
- **Do not invent a dark theme from the always-dark custom properties.** Those pages forced light.
- **Do not treat the advertisement slot as product chrome.**

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

`build_tokens.py` checks tokens.json against raw/: every token has an evidence label, every literal colour is a raw/ reading (or labelled Reconstruction), every alias resolves without a loop, and the painted masthead, ink, accent and login values have not drifted. It does not rewrite tokens.json. `build_style_guide.py` rewrites style-guide.html from tokens.json.
