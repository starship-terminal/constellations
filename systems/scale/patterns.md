# Patterns

How the public site combines the controls. Screenshots are 256-colour copies of the Playwright captures.

## App shell

A fixed white header: wordmark, four menu buttons, a quiet Log In link, a black Get Started pill. The header is 88px tall at 1440. On a 390-wide phone the menus collapse into an Open menu button and Get Started stays in the bar.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png).

## Navigation menus

Each header button opens a mega-menu over the hero. Products splits into Build AI, Apply AI, and Evaluate AI. Solutions splits into Industries and Use cases. Research leaves for labs.scale.com. Resources mixes company pages and a featured eBook. Escape closes the menu.

[home-light-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png), [home-light-solutions-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-solutions-menu.png), [home-light-research-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-research-menu.png), [home-light-resources-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-resources-menu.png).

## Hero

The homepage hero is a full-bleed photograph with a white sentence over it and a "Scroll to explore" control. Product pages repeat the same header and a large Aeonik headline on white or on a dark photograph.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [enterprise-light-default.png](screenshots/enterprise-light-default.png).

## Form

Get Started, Contact, and /pricing all land on /demo: a two-column page, fields on the left, a quote panel on the right. Fields are white, 8px radius. The form was not submitted.

[demo-light-default.png](screenshots/demo-light-default.png), [demo-light-company-focus.png](screenshots/demo-light-company-focus.png).

## Search

The blog search control opens a dialog with a search field, recent posts, and keyboard hints. Nothing was typed into it.

[blog-light-search-open.png](screenshots/blog-light-search-open.png).

## Docs shell

scale.com/docs is a separate product shell: sidebar, search, assistant, and a theme menu (System, Light, Dark). Choosing Dark paints the page. Customer Dashboard in that sidebar is the login wall and was not followed.

[docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png), [docs-light-theme-menu.png](screenshots/docs-light-theme-menu.png), [docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/docs-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

https://dashboard.scale.com/login is a centred card on a pale gradient: Google, GitHub, email, password. The branch stops there. No password was typed.

[login-light-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-wall.png).

## Footer

Every marketing page ends in a link index: products, industries, company, legal, guides, and social links to LinkedIn and X. Those social hosts were not captured.
