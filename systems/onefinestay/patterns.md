# Patterns

Public marketing pages only. Nothing was booked, enquired, or submitted.

## Home

[https://www.onefinestay.com/](https://www.onefinestay.com/)

A transparent header, 80px tall, sits over a full-bleed hero. The hero heading is `core.typography.display` in white. Under it, a 752 by 56 px white search bar with a 2px radius and `light.shadow.search` holds Dates, Guests and a 40px `light.accent` search square. The page then stacks Tiempos section headings at 40px/48px, Akkurat body at 16px/24px, and listing cards. The footer is `light.surface.footer` with 64px 48px padding.

At innerWidth 390 the same header is 80px with 24px inline padding. The hero heading drops to 36px/44px. The page heading drops to 32px/40px. The link row is replaced by a 40px menu button.

## Destination

[https://www.onefinestay.com/destination/london](https://www.onefinestay.com/destination/london)

Same transparent header and search bar. An eyebrow in `light.accent`, uppercase, 14px, 0.35px tracking, sits above a Tiempos section heading. Price lines are Akkurat 700 at 16px. The word "from" is Akkurat 400 at 14px.

## Listing

[https://www.onefinestay.com/home-listing/cadogan-street-iv-cad126](https://www.onefinestay.com/home-listing/cadogan-street-iv-cad126)

The header paints solid white and the wordmark and links paint black. A photo stack carries a white bordered "View all photos" button. The title is `core.typography.heading`. A 324px Check Availability button uses `light.brand.900`. Dates were not chosen. Availability was not checked.

## Search

[https://www.onefinestay.com/search/london/500](https://www.onefinestay.com/search/london/500)

Filter controls sit in a row: 50px select buttons at 2px radius, and 38px chips at 24px radius. A promo line uses 12px uppercase tracking. The count is an h1 at body size. Filters were not applied. No home was opened from the result list beyond the listing already measured.

## Journal

[https://www.onefinestay.com/journal](https://www.onefinestay.com/journal)

A separate compiled stylesheet. Body text computed to AkkuratStd-Regular and `rgb(15, 24, 39)`. Story tags use `#fe7f63`. This page does not share the Next.js token layer. Treat it as a second surface, not as a theme.

## Not captured
