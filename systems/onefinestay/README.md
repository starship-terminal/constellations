The preview tab reported innerWidth 1440 and innerHeight 900 at devicePixelRatio 2 after a 1440 by 900 request, and innerWidth 390 and innerHeight 844 at devicePixelRatio 2 after a 390 by 844 request. The product painted light only.

### Principles

1. Set headings in Tiempos at weight 400, and UI text in Akkurat.
2. Use coral `#ff8065` for search and eyebrows, and rust `#c84908` for the availability button.
3. Keep control corners at 2px, and the search square square.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://www.onefinestay.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. The page loaded signed out. The title was "Luxury Vacation Rentals, Luxury Villa & Home Rentals | onefinestay". No Cloudflare challenge appeared. Computed styles and custom properties were read with `preview_evaluate` on the live page. Lab colours were rasterised to sRGB through a 1 by 1 canvas with `colorSpace: srgb`. Those hex values are in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Element readings are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

`preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Measurements already read were kept. No screenshot was invented. The cookie banner was hidden in the DOM after a close click left it on screen. Accept was not clicked.

The enquire form was opened and measured empty. It asks for a phone number. Nothing was typed into it. The listing Check Availability button was not pressed. Search filters were not applied.

## What to distrust

There are no screenshots in this package. The coverage table names the pages that were measured. It cannot show them.

`--accent` resolves to `#f5f5f5`. The painted coral is `--brand-500` (`#ff8065`). The catalog swatch uses brand 500. A reader who follows the shadcn `--accent` name will paint a grey page.

`--primary` resolves to `#171717`. Painted headings and buttons use `--neutral-900` (`#26232a`) and, for the booking control, `--brand-900` (`#c84908`). The token `light.text.primary` is neutral 900, because that is what the headings computed to.

The journal at `/journal` loads `_compiled/app.css`, not the Next.js sheets. Its body colour computed to `rgb(15, 24, 39)`. Its coral tag computed to `#fe7f63`, which is `--brand-400`, not `--brand-500`. Do not treat the journal as the same token layer.

`--font-heatwood` is declared. No captured element computed to it.

The search button radius is 0. The search bar, field shells and text buttons are 2px. `--radius` is 0.625rem (10px) and was not the painted control radius.

Focus was read by calling `focus()` on the Garden chip. The box-shadow was `rgb(0, 102, 204) 0px 0px 0px 2px`. A keyboard-only pass was not done.

Akkurat's computed generic family was `serif`. The font files are Akkurat Std. The stack in the tokens copies the computed family string.

## Do and don't

1. **Set headings in Tiempos at weight 400, and UI text in Akkurat.** The home hero computed to 64px/72px Tiempos 400. Body copy computed to 16px/24px Akkurat 400. Labels computed to 12px/16px Akkurat 700. The wordmark is in [icons/wordmark.svg](icons/wordmark.svg).
2. **Use coral `#ff8065` for search and eyebrows, and rust `#c84908` for the availability button.** The home search square computed to `rgb(255, 128, 101)`. Check Availability computed to `rgb(200, 73, 8)`. The filled submit on enquire computed to `rgb(38, 35, 42)`. The favicon raster is [icon.png](icon.png).
3. **Keep control corners at 2px, and the search square square.** Field shells, text buttons and the search bar computed to 2px. The search button computed to 0. Filter chips computed to 24px. The menu icon is in [icons/menu.svg](icons/menu.svg).
