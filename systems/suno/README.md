# Suno

Suno is an AI music generator. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out sign-in dialog, measured on 2026-11-19 from `https://suno.com/`, `https://suno.com/careers` and `https://suno.com/about`. The account was signed out. The only theme the pages showed was dark. The CSS viewport was 1169 by 731, device pixel ratio 2. No phone width was measured.

The 2026-11-19 pass read computed styles after the preview browser failed to produce frames. On 2026-11-20 the same default profile produced frames. Desktop measured 1440 by 900 at devicePixelRatio 2. Phone measured 390 by 844 at devicePixelRatio 2. The session was still signed out. No birthday screen appeared. Create, Library and Earn credits redirected to sign-in. No provider was clicked and no song was generated.

Values still come from computed styles and from the custom properties on `:root`. The 2026-11-20 dump is [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). It matches the 2026-11-19 roles. Screenshots are layout evidence. Do not sample colours from them.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://suno.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. The page loaded signed out, title "Suno | AI Music Generator", with no Cloudflare challenge. `preview_snapshot` failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". After that, `preview_evaluate` read `getComputedStyle` on `:root`, `body`, the nav, the hero, the prompt, the pricing cards and the sign-in dialog.

Custom properties were read from the computed style of `:root` (674 names) and saved in [raw/root-vars.json](raw/root-vars.json). Component boxes were read from `getBoundingClientRect` at 1169px CSS width. Nothing was typed into the prompt. "Log in" was clicked, the dialog was measured, then Close was clicked. Pricing buttons were not followed. `/careers` and `/about` were loaded the same way.

Light values in `tokens.json` are the declared `--color-*-light` and `--color-*-on-light` custom properties, read while the page's `color-scheme` was dark. They are not computed styles of a light surface.

## What to distrust

The 2026-11-20 screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/root-vars.json](raw/root-vars.json), not from the PNGs.

The light set was not shown on screen. Use it as the product's own token names, not as a measured light theme.

The hero "Create" control computed to a black background. `--aura-primary` and `--aura-secondary` are in the raw dump and did not paint that control's computed background.

Platinum's card background is an image gradient (`--plan-platinum-stops`). The card's computed background color was the same translucent white as Free. The gradient is in the raw dump, not in a colour token.

Tailwind's `lab()` ramps (`--color-amber-*`, `--color-blue-*`, `--color-neutral-*` and the other default hues) are in the raw dump and were left out of the brand palette.

Hover, focus-visible, pressed and reduced-motion were not measured. Nav links declare `motion-reduce:transition-none`. That is a class name, not a reduce verdict.

The 2026-11-19 readings were at 1169px. The 2026-11-20 screenshots measured 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. Width-specific type below the `md` breakpoint was still not re-read as computed styles.

## Fonts

Neue Montreal is the UI face (`--font-sans`). Neue Montreal Book is the marketing body and plan face (`--font-book`). Editorial New is the display serif (`--font-serif`). Input Sans is the mono face (`--font-mono`). All four are commercial. The tokens keep the product's own stacks, including the system fallbacks. The style guide does not load the commercial files. It falls back to system-ui, Georgia and ui-monospace.

## Do and don't

- **Use the cream pill for the primary action.** "Join Suno for free" is `#f7f4ef` on `#101012`, radius pill, Neue Montreal 15px weight 500. The black aura pill is a second action, not the default.
- **Set display lines in Editorial New at weight 300.** Section headlines such as "Mind blowing song quality" are 66px with letter-spacing -1.32px. Page titles on `/about` go to 96px.
- **Keep the page on `#101012` with foreground `#f7f4ef`.** Borders are white at 10% (`#ffffff1a`). Cards are white at 4%.
- **Use strawberry `#fd429c` as the brand accent, not as the primary button.** The primary button is cream. Pink paints the Premier plan mix and the accent token.
- **Do not invent a light theme from the dark page.** The light names exist as custom properties. They were not computed on a light surface.
- **Do not draw the signed-in studio from this package.** `/create`, Studio and checkout were not entered.
