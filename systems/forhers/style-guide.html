Palette

core.palette is the literal colour census of the archived inline sheets: the ink and its alpha steps, the paper/grey washes, the sage and cream surfaces, the #338585 teal ramp, the dark forest greens, the sage greens, the lime promo ramp and the clay/terracotta accents. The light set layers the semantic roles — bg, text, action, accent, promo, border, focus — over it. The site paints one theme; the dark set is empty.

Spacing, radius and layout

core.space.unit is the --unit-u* element scale (3–78px) and core.space.section the --spacing-s* rhythm (41–163px). core.layout.grid is the 12-column, 14px-gutter, 1251px grid with the chrome heights beside it; core.layout.breakpoint the fluid-type staircase. core.radius is measured: the 48px icon circle, the 9999px CTA pill, 24px cards, 16px dialogs and 11px fields. core.motion is what the probe measured — durations, easings and the reduced-motion behaviour.

Elevation

The pages are nearly flat — layering is the ink hairline, the 6% wash and the modal scrim (the continuity dialog at z-900). Any shadows that appear were measured per component.

Type

Two voices: 'CareSans' — the soft-serif display face for heroes, card names, deks and prices, sized fluidly by the --scale staircase between 360px and 1392px — and 'sofia-pro/SofiaProWeb' for UI, prose, nav and footer.
