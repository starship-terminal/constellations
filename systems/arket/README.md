# ARKET design reference

A token set and component reference for ARKET's public storefront (https://www.arket.com), the H&M-group brand's Next.js + Tailwind v4 shop. Use it to build a commerce or editorial surface that borrows its look: a pure-white page, a warm `#faf8f3` beige used for notice and promo bands, everything set in one monospaced grotesque (arketSansMono), pure-black text, 2px-radius outlined controls that invert to black on hover, black 56px call-to-action blocks, a four-step grey ramp, and a 538px drawer pattern for menu, search, cart, filter and location. It was captured signed out, in headed real Chrome at 1440 x 900 (mobile at 390 x 844) on 2026-11-11.

A 2026-11-21 public refresh added [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) (which header control opens which menu, page, or dialog) and [login-review.json](login-review.json). New shots cover the homepage, the Women, Men, Children and Homeware menus, the account menu, search, the cookie card, the location dialog, and the phone menu. Sign-in, signup, and checkout were not submitted. The root `/en-ww/` later returned Access Denied, so those header states were photographed on `/en-ww/women/`.

It holds 40 screenshots across the public storefront, including nine desktop routes and two phone routes from the first capture — /en-ww/women/, /en-ww/women/new-arrivals/, /en-ww/women/clothing/, /en-ww/men/clothing/, /en-ww/gift-shop/, /en-ww/editorial/, /en-ww/customer-service/, /en-ww/account/, /en-ww/previous-collections/women/ — plus transient states (the consent card and location dialog as they arrive, the search field focused, the empty cart drawer, the PLP filter panel, the mobile menu), and 56 measured components with 130+ states and one crop each. Two surfaces are boundaries, not gaps: Akamai answers every `/en-ww/product/<slug>-<id>/` URL and the `/en-ww/` root with Access Denied (403) for this client, so the product page and the root landing were never captured — the shell was measured on /en-ww/women/, and PDP-looking values come from the listing cards. The account sits behind the Login/Signup gate; nothing was bought, sent, submitted or signed into (see [components.md](components.md) > Gaps).

This package contains no ARKET code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots, product photography and visual system they show belong to ARKET ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, artwork or brand assets.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export arket` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page white and the bands beige.
2. Do set everything in the one mono.
3. Do invert controls on hover.
4. Do radius small: 2px controls, 4px fields, full pills.
5. Do reuse the 538px drawer for every panel.
6. Don't ship storefront tokens as the product.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

- Every component was located on the live page by [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) in headed real Chrome (`channel: 'chrome'`): Akamai serves headless Chromium — bundled and real — an `Access Denied` (403) page on every route, and blocks every `/en-ww/product/<slug>-<id>/` URL even headed (document and RSC fetch alike) plus the `/en-ww/` root for this client. Selectors key off structure, roles and stable text; the classes are Tailwind utilities. Computed styles were read per state — default, hover, focus-visible, pressed (forced via `CSS.forcePseudoState`), focused/filled on fields — and one 2x crop saved per state. The card quick-add and carousel controls only exist while the card is `:hover`ed; they were captured under a CDP-forced hover on the card cell.
- CSS custom properties were read from the live stylesheets by [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json): the Tailwind v4 `@theme` layer declares ~124 names (`--color-*`, `--spacing-*`, `--text-*`, `--leading-*`, `--radius-*`, `--tracking-*`, `--container-*`, `--ease-*`, `--default-transition-*`, `--font-*`, `--animate-*`), resolved in a probe document at 1440 x 900.
- Screenshots were taken on the same pages by [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs), quantised to 256 colours by [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py). Full pages taller than one GPU texture were stitched from `.partN` tiles.
- The `.cookie-consent-popup`, its `.tcc-pc-dark-filter`, the `.settings-page` cookie settings panel, the auto-mounted `<dialog>` "Please confirm your location" and the `.z-drawer`/fullscreen-modal dimmers were hidden before shots — hidden, never answered: "Accept all", "Only required cookies", "Continue to <country>" and "Stay in <country>" were never pressed.

## What to distrust

- **Readings past a masked bot signal.** The committed screenshots and readings were taken with `navigator.webdriver` masked, the flag bot checks read to spot automation. The capture scripts no longer mask it (not run since), so a recapture may record a walled route as a boundary instead of a reading.
- **The product page and the account are absent.** PDP URLs 403 for this client and /en-ww/account/ stops at the gate; no PDP, checkout, filled cart or signed-in screen is in this package — only the signed-out storefront that links to them.
- **The root landing is the women landing.** `/en-ww/` was blocked at capture time; every shell, dialog and footer reading is labelled `/en-ww/women/` in the raw JSON.
- **The focus ring is the browser's.** Every control shows the same 1px `auto` `#005fcc` outline on keyboard focus — arket.com declares no `:focus-visible` style of its own. `light.focus-ring` records it labelled as such.
- **Hover is an inversion, not a tint.** Outlined controls and nav items flip to black fill with white text; black buttons barely lift (to `#080808`). Don't substitute a grey hover.
- **Some font names differ per surface.** The shop shell paints `arketSansMono`; the consent card and account gate fall back to `"Arket Sans"`/`"Arket-Sans-Mono"` stacks. The `+N` colour chips use the small-caps `arketSCSansMono`.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs** — read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw JSON, never from a screenshot pixel.
- **`service-link` is the service page's ARKET logo.** Two specs share the key: the service page's loose `main a` probe overwrote the home page's Customer service link with the logo (43 x 40, "ARKET"). Needs a recapture; the script now gives the second probe its own key, `service-page-link` (not run since).
- **`breadcrumb`, `gate-continue` and `m-search` were read from the wrong elements.** `breadcrumb` is the main category nav ("Women Men Children Homeware Gift shop"), `gate-continue` is the gate's "with Google" button, and `m-search` is the mobile menu button. Each needs a recapture; the script now targets the breadcrumb and the Continue button by their own markers (not run since).

## Fonts

- **arketSansMono** — every shell string, field, button, price and body line; one 400 face, self-hosted woff from `/_next/static/media/`, with an Arial-metric fallback (`ascent-override 92.07%`, `size-adjust 103.07%`).
- **arketSCSansMono** — the small-caps mono on chips and counts (the "+2" colour marker), same licensing posture.
- Licences: both are ARKET's proprietary faces; they are not kept here and the tokens name the stacks as measured, with system fallbacks in the style guide.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the page white and the bands beige.** Page `#ffffff`, notice/promo bands `#faf8f3`, hairlines `#eaeae8`; ink is pure black, never grey text on white. ([header](components/crops/header-default.png), [promo-strip](components/crops/promo-strip-default.png))
- **Do set everything in the one mono.** arketSansMono carries headings, nav, prices and buttons alike at 400 weight; only chips switch to arketSCSansMono small-caps. Don't introduce a second family. ([plp-title](components/crops/plp-title-default.png), [more-colors](components/crops/more-colors-default.png))
- **Do invert controls on hover.** Outlined 2px pills and nav items flip to black fill with white text; black buttons only deepen to `#080808`. Don't reach for a grey or coloured hover. ([nav-item](components/crops/nav-item-hover.png), [filter-button](components/crops/filter-button-hover.png))
- **Do radius small: 2px controls, 4px fields, full pills.** The square-ish control edge is the look; the only large radii are the 99990px pills (read-more chip) and 538px rounded drawers. ([filter-button](components/crops/filter-button-default.png), [read-more](components/crops/read-more-default.png))
- **Do reuse the 538px drawer for every panel.** Menu, search, cart, filter and location all arrive in the same right-side sheet on a beige dimmer. Don't invent a second modal shape. ([cart-drawer](components/crops/cart-drawer-default.png), [filter-panel](components/crops/filter-panel-default.png))
- **Don't ship storefront tokens as the product.** Nothing here was measured on a PDP or inside the account; a screen claiming ARKET's product page from these tokens would be invented. The honest gap is recorded in components.md.
