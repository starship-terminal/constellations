What this page is

The drawings below are rebuilt from those tokens; the real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/. The toggle sets data-mode on this page; TokenTracker itself switches with a .dark class on <html>.

Do and don't

Do: let surfaces do the work

Wash, panel and card differ by one neutral step and a hairline (in dark, panel and card share one fill). No coloured fills behind content; the only resting shadow is 0 1px 2px at 5%.

Do: keep green for money and "on"

The brand green marks cost, the usage fill and the heatmap. Primary buttons are black in light and white in dark, never green.

Don't: copy the uppercase labels

Older cards and the sidebar groups use tracked uppercase ("ACTIVITY HEATMAP", "GENERAL"). TokenTracker's newer cards use sentence case at 14/500. Use sentence case.

Don't: copy the missing focus ring

TokenTracker removes every focus ring, so keyboard focus is invisible. Add a 2px accent ring at 2px offset.
