# Patreon

Public marketing site and the signed-out login wall of [patreon.com](https://www.patreon.com/), measured on 19 November 2026. No account was created. No email was submitted. No pledge or card was entered. The signup wall is recorded as a gap.

The marketing pages use a fluid root (`html` font-size 7.5px at 1440, 10px at 390) and a `--color-*` token layer. The login page is a separate app: near-black page, ABC Oracle Plus Variable, 16px root, and no readable custom properties.

Themes seen: light marketing, and a dark login wall. Widths measured: 1440x900 and 390x844, both at devicePixelRatio 2.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://www.patreon.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. Computed styles were read from that live page with `preview_evaluate`. The viewport was then set to 1440x900 and 390x844. The same tab opened `/pricing`, `/login` and `/product/paid-membership`, then returned to `/`.

Custom properties were read from the marketing stylesheet `af9939d57fba6ce8.css` and resolved on `:root`. Colour mixes were resolved by assigning each value to an element's `color` and reading the computed colour. Component sizes are `getComputedStyle` on the visible control.

`preview_snapshot` failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". A headless Playwright session against the same public URLs received HTTP 403 and the title "Just a moment...". That session was stopped. No screenshot was drawn to fill the gap.

Nothing was neutralised before reading. The Creators menu was opened with a click so its link size could be read, then the viewport changed and the menu closed with the resize.

## What to distrust

The home h1's `innerText` was empty. The size, weight, line-height and letter-spacing were read from the element. The words themselves were not.

Section band colours (`#94bbff`, `#95adcb`, `#71a0ff`) are computed backgrounds of large divs. They are not the `--color-primary` token (`#5fc1f1`).

`--focus-outline` is `1px solid #0e87eb`. A focused header button painted `outline: rgb(229, 151, 0) auto 1px`. Both readings are kept. The orange outline is not turned into a token, because it was one focused control and the token says blue.

Neutral steps are `color-mix` of white into black. The hex in tokens.json is that mix rounded to sRGB (`0.1` to `#1a1a1a`). The browser reported `color(srgb 0.1 0.1 0.1)`.

At 390, Find a Creator's input had a 60px radius and a 0x0 box. Its padding is a gap.

The login stylesheets were cross-origin (13 blocked). Login values are computed styles only.

Screenshots are missing, declared under layoutGaps. Do not treat the style guide specimens as captures of the live page.

tokens.json is written by hand. `tools/build/build_tokens.py` does not write it: it checks that every value appears in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json) and that every alias resolves. Four values have no reading there and say so in their source: the home h2 type (`core.typography.section`) and the Find a Creator radius (`core.radius.search`) are Reconstruction, and the nav line height and the login font's fallback stack were entered by hand.

## Fonts

Marketing: Oracle, then Noto Sans JP, Noto Sans KR, Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans TC, Noto Sans Regular, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif. Oracle is proprietary and is not in this package. The style guide falls back to Helvetica Neue.

Login: ABC Oracle Plus Variable. Also proprietary, also not shipped. Computed weight 550 on the login buttons is outside the usual 100 to 900 steps, and it is recorded as measured.

## Do and don't

- **Use the black pill on a light section and the white pill on the dark hero.** The header Get Started is white with black text. The pricing Get started is black with white text. Both are 47px with a 30px radius at 1440. See [patterns.md](patterns.md).
- **Keep the login wall separate from the marketing scale.** `/login` uses a 16px root, ABC Oracle Plus Variable, an 8px field radius and a 4px SSO radius. Do not reuse the marketing 30px pill there. See [components.md](components.md).
- **Read sizes from the raw file when a frame is missing.** The frame capture failed. Read sizes from [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json).
