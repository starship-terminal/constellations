# Patreon design reference

Public Patreon, captured signed out on 2026-11-20 in headless Chromium. Two public surfaces share `https://www.patreon.com/`: the marketing site (pricing hero, white Get Started pill, PATREON wordmark) and the logged-out app (Explore rail, pill search, topic chips, creator pages). Login, signup, Apple, and Facebook were recorded as walls and not opened. No form was submitted. No email, phone, or location was typed.

Desktop shots are 1440×900 at 1x. Phone shots were requested at 390×844 at 2x. The phone Explore shot's `innerWidth` came back 398; its visual viewport stayed 390. See [What to distrust](#what-to-distrust).

## How the values were read

The logged-out app declares studio tokens as `--global-*` aliases of `--light-global-*`, `--dark-global-*`, and `--shared-global-*` on `.QbDuzW_definitions`. Marketing declares `--color-*` colour-mix aliases and the Oracle stack on `:root`. Help Center is Zendesk and uses its own ramps.

[tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) read stylesheet declarations into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) keeps the first matching declaration on `.QbDuzW_definitions` or `:root`. Component sizes in [components.md](components.md) are computed styles from a later Playwright pass on pricing, Explore, membership, and Help Center. Search fields were typed with the public words "podcast" and "payout" and not submitted.

Screenshots were scrolled to settle lazy media, capped at four extra chunks, then quantised to 256 colours. Full-colour originals and `.capture.json` sidecars stay in `~/.design-systems/patreon/raw-shots/` and are not committed. Cookie banners were hidden, not clicked.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- `https://www.patreon.com/` sometimes serves the logged-out app (`Home | Patreon`) and sometimes the marketing homepage. This screenshot set has the marketing homepage. The app home, its account menu, and a live dark appearance click are not in the screenshots. Dark tokens are still declared in CSS and are in `dark.*`.
- `light.accent` is the computed fill of Join for free on one public membership page, rgb(175, 2, 16). It is that creator's accent, not a studio token named coral. Marketing Get Started is white.
- `--shared-global-radius-md` is declared twice on the same selector, 8px then 10px. The token keeps 8px.
- Marketing body computed font-size was 7.5px because the root rem scale is tiny. That number is not a type token. App body is 14px.
- Phone Explore `innerWidth` was 398 while the requested viewport and visual viewport were 390×844. The other phone shots reported `innerWidth` 390.
- Explore topic click timed out. [explore-light-topic.png](screenshots/explore-light-topic.png) is the same Explore page, not a confirmed topic filter.
- Infinite feeds were capped. A short screenshot is not the whole page.

## Fonts

App UI uses ABC Oracle Plus Variable, then system sans. Marketing body uses Oracle, then Noto Sans JP, KR, SC, TC, and Regular, then Helvetica Neue. Marketing heading declares PatreonOracle_Regular. Help Center loads PatreonOracle Book, Light, Regular, and Thin. None of these files are in the package. They are proprietary. See [docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md).

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep marketing CTAs as white or outlined pills on photography.** Get Started on pricing is a white pill, Oracle, not a coral fill. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
2. **Do use the creator accent only on that creator's Join control.** Join for free on the sample membership page is a red pill. That red is not the marketing brand fill. See [creator-membership-light-default](screenshots/creator-membership-light-default.png).
3. **Do set Explore on the gray page ground with a pill search and a black selected chip.** See [explore-light-default](screenshots/explore-light-default.png).
4. **Don't treat the marketing homepage and the logged-out app home as the same chrome.** The same URL serves both. This package's homepage shot is marketing. The app home was mapped in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and is not in the screenshots.
5. **Don't submit Find a Creator, Explore search, or Help search, and don't follow Log in.** Those fields stay on the page. Log in, Get Started, and Join open a wall. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
6. **Don't treat a capped feed screenshot as the full page.** Explore and creator feeds were scrolled a limited number of times. See [explore-light-default](screenshots/explore-light-default.png).

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/patreon
PLAYWRIGHT="$HOME/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs" node tools/capture/public/public.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT="$HOME/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs" node tools/capture/vars.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py "$HOME/.design-systems/patreon/raw-shots" screenshots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

`tools/build_components_md.py` is still the kit's Gauth builder. Do not run it. It expects files this package does not have. [components.md](components.md) was written from the live measurements.
