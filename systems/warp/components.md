| Nav link | Pricing | Default. |

| Pill, outline | Get started | Default and open menu. |

| 404 | Missing path | Default. |
| Phone menu | 390 homepage | Open. |

Not present on the public pages photographed: select, checkbox as a product control (the login "Remember me" box was already checked and was not toggled), radio, switch (the pricing Monthly/Annual control was left on Annual), toast, table, avatar, badge as a system component.

Not captured: hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, loading, error after a submit. Submitting a form was out of scope.
