# Patterns

How the public pages are put together. Screenshots are viewport shots, not full-page scrolls.

## Marketing shell

A black announcement bar, a white header, a near-white page, a white footer. Desktop header: wordmark, three menus, Pricing, then Sign in, a yellow Book a demo pill, and an outline Get started pill. Phone header drops the menus into a dialog and keeps Get started plus a menu button.

Shown in [screenshots/home-desktop.png](screenshots/home-desktop.png) and [screenshots/home-phone.png](screenshots/home-phone.png).

## Homepage

Headline, two stacked actions (request early access, download warp terminal), then a public factory-grid demo. The grid is a marketing illustration. Task names in it are sample copy.

## Pricing

Two product tabs on one URL family. Factories is the default. Agent pricing is `?product=warp`. Cards sit in a row: price, cadence, a primary action, a feature list. The annual toggle was left on.

## Auth edge

Login and signup are the same dark card on `app.warp.dev`, with the heading swapped. Email on top, a divider, then four identity buttons. Stop at the empty card.

## Empty

The missing-path page keeps the marketing shell and says "Page not found", with Go back home and Get Warp Terminal.
