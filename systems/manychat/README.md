# ManyChat design reference

A token set and component reference for ManyChat's public marketing surface, for building chat-marketing pages, pricing tiers and guide cards in the style of the real thing. It was read from **manychat.com** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop captures are 1440 x 900 at 1x. The pricing phone pass measured innerWidth 390. The home phone pass measured innerWidth 392 (visualViewport 390). Captured: `/`, `/pricing`, `/product/instagram` and `/resources/how-to`, plus a phone pass on home and pricing.

ManyChat is worth a capture because a Next.js + Tailwind marketing site paints a loud, small system: a white field, `#0f0f0f` ink, a `#fa0cf7` neon-pink hero pill, and a 68px black Creator World strip over a 92px nav. The site sets `html { font-size: 10px }`, so 1rem is 10px and the 1440 hero is 7.2rem (72px) ManyChatGravity. Nav labels are CoFo Sans Mono at 12px. CTAs are 999px pills. The Free plan card is 369 x 662 with a 24px radius. **Boundary:** the bot builder and the in-app template library sit behind sign-in at app.manychat.com. Log in, Get started and Start for free were measured, never followed. Checkout was not submitted. The Usercentrics privacy layer was hidden, never answered.

### Principles

1. Do spend the pink on the hero.
2. Do keep the ink for price actions.
3. Do set the root to 10px.
4. Do round the controls.
5. Do pair the three voices.
6. Don't style the bot builder from this package.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't follow a sign-up or checkout link from a reconstruction.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **Automation was hidden during the committed capture.** The readings in [raw/](raw), the crops and the screenshots were taken with a `navigator.webdriver` mask and Chromium's `AutomationControlled` flag turned off. The capture scripts no longer hide automation. A rerun that meets a bot check records that page as blocked.
- **Button label colours.** Each ManyChat button carries a transparent duplicate label span. [components.md](components.md) reports the button's own computed colour. On the nav and product CTAs that colour is white on a white fill, so the visible label's colour there was not read.
- **Type sizes are converted.** The stylesheet authors the type ramp in rem against a 10px root, and the tokens hold px (7.2rem is 72px). Letter-spacing is authored in em and converted to px at each step's own size.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the pink on the hero.** `#fa0cf7` is the hero Get started pill. The nav Get started pill on the home photo is white. See [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
2. **Do keep the ink for price actions.** `#0f0f0f` paints Start for free and the Annual pill. See [price-cta](components/crops/price-cta-default.png).
3. **Do set the root to 10px.** Display sizes are rem against that root. A 7.2rem heading is 72px. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
4. **Do round the controls.** Nav and price CTAs are 999px pills. The Free card is 24px. See [price-card](components/crops/price-card-default.png).
5. **Do pair the three voices.** ManyChatGravity for display, Rooftop for UI, CoFo Sans Mono for nav labels and pill text. See [nav-link](components/crops/nav-link-default.png).
6. **Don't style the bot builder from this package.** app.manychat.com was never entered.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The black strip and the Annual pill are dark surfaces inside the one light theme.
8. **Don't follow a sign-up or checkout link from a reconstruction.** Those destinations were measured, never opened.

## Rebuild

Run from `systems/manychat/`:

```sh
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-dir>
```

Then `make brief && make design-md && make grade` from the repo root refreshes the generated sections.
