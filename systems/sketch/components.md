## Gaps

- Product menu dropdown was open in [screenshots/home-light-nav-product.png](screenshots/home-light-nav-product.png). The measurer did not keep it visible long enough for a crop. Menu link values are Not captured.
- Badge (the NEW pill) was visible inside that menu screenshot. No crop. Colour Not captured.
- Focus ring: focus-visible did not change outline on the header link or primary button. Not captured as its own token.
- Checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, toast, table, avatar, text area: not present on the measured public pages, or not reached.
- Dark theme: not present on the public routes.
- Signed-in web app, Mac app, iOS app: behind https://www.sketch.com/signin/ or the App Store. Branch stopped. Password not submitted.
- Contact form fields were not filled. The page itself is in [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).
- Newsletter field was focused and left empty.
- Sitemap articles beyond the section indexes and one blog post were not walked.
