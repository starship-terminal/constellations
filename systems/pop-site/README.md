# Pop Site design reference

Tokens and component notes for Pop Site, captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. One theme: light. Signed out the whole time.

Pop Site (pop.site) is a personal-site builder. The marketing site is a Framer page (generator `Framer fb64017`). Sign in and sign up live at app.pop.site and use a different stylesheet. Nothing was submitted. No password was typed. Google SSO was not entered. No card, phone, or government ID was requested on these screens.

### Principles

1. Do paint the marketing page white, with ink at `#171717`.
2. Do draw Claim your site as a 40px pill on a `#00a8f5` to `#3b82f6` gradient.
3. Do set marketing type in Satoshi at weight 500, with negative letter-spacing.
4. Do keep the auth card on `#f3f3f3` with a 20px white shell.
5. Don't invent a dark theme.
6. Don't treat the phone mockups as the signed-in app.
7. Don't submit the auth form to capture an error.
8. Don't ship Satoshi or Google Sans Flex.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Marketing colours were read from the Framer custom properties on `body` at pop.site, and from `getComputedStyle` on the heading, nav, claim button, claim field, and section cards. The claim fill is a `background-image` gradient, not `background-color`.

Auth colours were read at app.pop.site/?signin. The page declares Radix-style `--blue-1` through `--blue-12`, and the same for slate, grass, amber, and red, on `:root` and `.light`. Blue and slate also declare a `.dark` ramp. The painted page did not apply that dark ramp when the browser asked for a dark colour scheme.

Screenshots were taken in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844, device pixel ratio 2, then quantised to 256 colours. Motion was read with `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` on the home page, pricing, and the sign-in URL.

The T3 preview browser opened pop.site and app.pop.site/?signin and could read the DOM. It did not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`). The shots in this package are from the same URLs in headless Chromium, which did not hit a Cloudflare challenge.

## What to distrust

Framer token names are hashes. The marketing colour group uses role names. The hex values are the declared ones.

The claim button's `background-color` is transparent. The blue is the gradient.

The sign-in button radius computed as `16777200px`. The token records the pill as `999px`, which is how the marketing field spells a pill. The painted result is a full pill.

`oklch` and `oklab` values in the focus ring, the Google label, and the placeholder are the computed colours the browser returned. They are not converted.

Google Sans Flex is loaded as 95 `@font-face` rules. Visible text computes to Satoshi. Satoshi is not shipped here.

Dark ramps for blue and slate are in the stylesheet and not in `tokens.json`, because the page did not paint them.

Hover on the claim button did not change its computed gradient. The hover crop matches the rest crop.

## Fonts

Marketing text is Satoshi, then "Satoshi Placeholder", then sans-serif. Auth text is satoshi, then -apple-system, system-ui. Satoshi is a commercial face and is not included. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the marketing page white, with ink at `#171717`.** That is the computed heading colour. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do draw Claim your site as a 40px pill on a `#00a8f5` to `#3b82f6` gradient.** The radius is 26px. [components/crops/button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png)
- **Do set marketing type in Satoshi at weight 500, with negative letter-spacing.** Display is 51px at -3.82px. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do keep the auth card on `#f3f3f3` with a 20px white shell.** The sign-in face is a slate gradient, not the marketing blue. [screenshots/signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** Asking the browser for dark did not change the page. [screenshots/home-dark-scheme.png](screenshots/home-dark-scheme.png)
- **Don't treat the phone mockups as the signed-in app.** They are marketing images. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Don't submit the auth form to capture an error.** That creates an account or sends a reset. [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png)
- **Don't ship Satoshi or Google Sans Flex.** Satoshi is the computed face and is commercial. Google Sans Flex is loaded and unused by the visible text. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
