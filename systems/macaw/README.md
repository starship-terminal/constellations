# Macaw design reference

Tokens and component notes for Macaw (macawhq.com), captured **2026-11-18**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. One light theme. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing`, `/contact-us`, `/free-tools/blog-title-generator`, `/terms-and-conditions`, `/privacy-policy`. `/blog`, `/docs`, `/documentation` and `/affiliate` redirect to `/`. Blog and Documentation in the Resources menu are `href="#"`. `app.macawhq.com` and `docs.macawhq.com` did not resolve. Signed out the whole time. No form was submitted. No consent control was clicked. No purchase was started.

Macaw's marketing site is Webflow. Colour is custom properties in `macaw-prod.webflow.7735a213d.min.css` (`--gray*`, `--primary*`, `--error*`, `--warning*`, `--success*`). The page ground is cream `#fffdf9`, which is not one of those variables. Type is Inter.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page cream and the one filled action coral.
2. Do keep Log in and Google as quiet white buttons.
3. Do set display in Inter at 53.6px bold.
4. Do tint the three plans, not the page.
5. Don't invent a dark theme.
6. Don't style the editor from the home samples.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium, signed out, 2026-11-18. Desktop viewport 1440×900 at 1x. Phone viewport 390×844 at 2x. The Webflow stylesheet was fetched from `cdn.prod.website-files.com` because the CSSOM blocks cross-origin `cssRules`. Component colours, type and sizes were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live pages (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`). Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Token colours come from the stylesheet and the computed styles, not from the PNGs.

Overlays (consent, Intercom, leftover tingle modals) were hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked. The contact form was focused and filled with the word Sample, then cleared. It was not submitted. Sign-up, Log in, Get started and Start free with Google were not followed.

## What to distrust

- Status colours (`light.success`, `light.warning`, `light.danger`, `light.info`) are authored `--success600`, `--warning600`, `--error600` and `--blue500`. They were not painted on the captured pages.
- The Resources menu screenshot was taken after forcing `.w-dropdown-list` to `display:block`. At 1440 the toggle itself is `display:none`, so that open state was not reached by hover.
- `/blog` and `/docs` are not pages. The menu items point at `#`.
- `app.macawhq.com` did not resolve, so the product app was not measured. The home writing-example cards are marketing samples, not the editor.
- The free-tool pages describe a generator and render no input. The generator was not captured.
- Pressed on the primary button is a forced `:active` and matches hover (`--primary700`). It is not a separate pressed paint.
- Focus outline `#005fcc` is the browser default, not a Webflow token.
- Privacy h2 is 60px on that page only. Do not treat it as the display role. Display is the 53.6px h1.
- Six type values have no reading in [raw/](raw): `font.size.section` (27.2px), `font.size.privacy-h2` (60px), `font.lineHeight.heading` (43.2px), `font.lineHeight.section` (32.64px) and the two matching trackings. They were typed in at capture time, and their sources say Reconstruction.

## Fonts

Inter, loaded from Google Fonts on the live site (`Inter:regular,500,600,700`). The tokens name `Inter, sans-serif`. The style guide does not load the font files. A custom property in the sheet named `--1` holds `"Open Sans", sans-serif`. Computed body text is Inter.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the page cream and the one filled action coral.** The ground is `#fffdf9`. Start for free is `--primary600` (`#f66658`) and turns `--primary700` on hover. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do keep Log in and Google as quiet white buttons.** White fill, gray-300 border, gray-700 ink, same 8px radius and 46px height as the coral button. [components/crops/button-google-default.png](components/crops/button-google-default.png)
- **Do set display in Inter at 53.6px bold.** The home h1 is that size in gray-900. Do not use the privacy page's 60px h2 as the display role. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do tint the three plans, not the page.** Starter is lavender, Pro is peach, Scale is the same blue as the examples band. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site does not paint one.
- **Don't style the editor from the home samples.** The writing-example cards are marketing. The app host did not resolve. [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png)
