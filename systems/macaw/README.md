# Macaw design reference

A token set and component reference for Macaw, the AI writing product, taken from the public marketing site at `https://macawhq.com/` on 2026-11-21. Signed out. One light theme. Desktop 1440×900 at 1x and phone 390×844 at 2x. The app host `https://app.macawhq.com/sign-in` did not resolve (`net::ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED`). Log in, Start for free, and Start free with Google were not followed.

The site is Webflow. The page is a warm off-white (`#fffdf9`). Body text is a grey-ink (`#475467`). Headlines are near-black (`#101828`). The one action colour is coral (`#f66658`), used on primary buttons. A lilac wash marks the hero highlight. Plan cards use three tints. Inter is the computed face. Corners on controls are 8px.

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium via Playwright, public pages only. `tools/capture/public/public.mjs` shot each page at 1440×900 (deviceScaleFactor 1) and 390×844 (deviceScaleFactor 2). Each PNG has a `.png.capture.json` sidecar in the scratch folder `~/.design-systems/macaw/raw-shots/`. `tools/capture/vars.mjs` read the CSSOM. The only custom properties are a Senja embed's Tailwind internals (`--sj-tw-*`), so they are evidence in `raw/css-vars.json` and not tokens. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` read computed styles on the live DOM for nav links, buttons, the example dialog, plan cards, and the contact fields. A second pass read the body, the h1, the hero highlight, and the three plan-card fills into `raw/foundations.json`. `tools/build_tokens.py` turns `raw/component-tokens.json` into `tokens.json`.

No form was submitted. No password, code, or profile field was typed. Cookie banners were not present. The Resources dropdown is `display: none` at 1440, so it was not opened on desktop. On the phone it sits inside the menu overlay.

## What to distrust

- The hero dashboard (search, filters, table, names) is a picture on the marketing page. It is not the signed-in app.
- `light.accent.pressed` is the nav-link hover colour (`#f26557`). The button hover is `#de4f31`. The pricing-toggle hover is `#de4f30`. A held press was not captured.
- Nav and button line heights in typography are Derived. The computed value was the keyword `normal`, which is not a dimension.
- `light.border.default` aliases the Starter card's own fill, because that card's 1px border computed to the same colour as its background. A neutral hairline token was not read.
- `light.border.strong` is `#000000`, the Pro card's border. It is not a general strong border.
- Legal screenshots have painted rectangles over the company contact lines. The layout of those lines is broken on purpose.
- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from `tokens.json`.
- Free-tool pages did not render a generator. The "free tool" is a landing page plus a sign-up link.
- `/affiliate`, `/affiliate-partner-terms`, and `/go` returned the home document. They are not separate pages.
- The capture date in `raw/css-vars.json` and `raw/components-computed.json` says 2026-11-20 because the script used UTC. The session date is 2026-11-21.

## Fonts

Inter, computed on `body`, `h1`, and the buttons (`Inter, sans-serif`). Webflow also activates Open Sans on the `html` element (`wf-opensans-*`). Open Sans was not the computed face of the measured controls. The style guide loads Inter from Google Fonts. The site's own font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do put actions on coral.** Start for free, the hero CTA, and Send message share `#f66658` with white labels ([button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)).
- **Do keep the page warm off-white.** Body background is `#fffdf9`, not pure white ([home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
- **Do mark the hero line with a lilac wash.** The highlight is `#efccff` with a violet bar, not a second button colour ([home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
- **Do round controls to 8px.** Buttons, fields, and the pricing toggle share that radius ([text-field-default.png](components/crops/text-field-default.png)).
- **Don't treat the hero dashboard as the app.** The table, search, and names are a marketing render ([home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
- **Don't follow Log in.** It points at `https://app.macawhq.com/sign-in`, which did not resolve ([site-tree.md](site-tree.md)).
