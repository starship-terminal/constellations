# Patterns

How Macaw's public pages are built. Values are in [tokens.json](tokens.json). The product app was not opened.

## Marketing frame

A cream page, an 80px frosted nav (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.3)` plus a 26px backdrop blur), and a coral Start for free. Desktop: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone: [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png). The phone nav is 72px and opens a drawer from the menu button: [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Pricing

Three tinted cards (lavender, peach, blue) over a comparison table. Yearly is selected at rest and shows "Save 34%". Monthly is a second label, not a tab. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png). FAQ rows sit under the table: [screenshots/pricing-light-faq-open.png](screenshots/pricing-light-faq-open.png). Get started and Contact our sales team leave for sign-up or a sales path and were not followed.

## Contact

A short form: first name, last name, email, message, Send message. Fields are 8px radius, 44px tall, gray-300 border, and pick up a 4px primary-100 halo when focused. [screenshots/contact-light-field-focus.png](screenshots/contact-light-field-focus.png). The form was not submitted.

## Free tool

`/free-tools/blog-title-generator` is a long explanation plus Try Macaw free. No generator input rendered. [screenshots/tool-light-default.png](screenshots/tool-light-default.png).
