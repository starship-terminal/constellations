# Macaw patterns

Public marketing pages only. The product demo in the hero is a picture on the marketing page, not the signed-in app.

## Shell

A sticky bar: logo, Product, Writing examples, Pricing, Become an affiliate, Log in, Start for free. At 390 the same links sit in an overlay opened by the menu button. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero and product demo

The headline sits on a lilac wash with a violet tag. Below it, a coral primary and a white Google button. The lower half of the first viewport is a rendered dashboard (search, filters, a table, names in the demo). That dashboard is artwork on the public page. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Writing examples

A carousel of five published sample cards. Clicking a card opens a tingle dialog with the sample article. [home-light-example-dialog.png](screenshots/home-light-example-dialog.png).

## Pricing

Three tinted cards (lilac, blush, blue) and a Yearly / Monthly toggle. Yearly is the default. Monthly changes the prices on the same page. A comparison block of rows sits under the cards. [pricing-light-yearly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-yearly.png), [pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png).

## Contact form

A two-column page: fields on the left, an illustration on the right. Fields were focused, not filled, not submitted. [contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png), [contact-light-field-focused.png](screenshots/contact-light-field-focused.png).

## Legal

Terms and privacy are long text pages in the same shell. Company contact lines on those screenshots are painted out. [terms-light-default.png](screenshots/terms-light-default.png), [privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).

## Free tools

Four tool landings share the shell and a "Try Macaw free" link. No generator input rendered. [tool-title-light-default.png](screenshots/tool-title-light-default.png).

## Not on these pages

Empty, loading, and error states of the app. A destructive confirmation. Feedback after a submit. Search and filtering as a working control (the demo picture shows them). Onboarding. A form validation error.
