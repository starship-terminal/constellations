# Patterns

Public signed-out pages. No screenshots.

## Home

Fixed header, white, 85px at 1440 and 58px at 390, 2px bottom border `#eaecf0` on desktop. Logo is the 40px coral mark. Nav links are Best Products, Launches, Community, MCP, Advertise. Search sits in the header. Subscribe is an outline pill. Sign in is a coral pill.

The page is a ranked list. Each row has a title, a tagline, topic links, and a 52px vote control. Sections are Today, Yesterday, Last Week, Last Month. The h1 is 24px at 1440 and 20px at 390.

The footer is `#101828` with category columns.

## Product

`/products/s-space`. Name at 24px weight 600. Tagline at 18px `#344054`. A white Visit website pill. Follow was visible and not clicked. Tabs are pills. Gallery images are 220px tall with a 12px radius.

## Category

`/categories/productivity`. Breadcrumb, then a 48px weight 600 heading. A Filter pill, padding `6px 12px`. Related categories and a product list.

## Leaderboard

`/leaderboard/daily/2026/11/18`. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly as text tabs. A day strip. The selected day uses a 10% coral wash.

## Login

`/login` renders a signup card. Copy says the community discovers and shares products. Six OAuth buttons. No email field, so AgentMail signup was not possible.

## Newsletter

`/newsletters`. An email field and a coral Subscribe button. Submitting a filled address replaced the form with "Check kaichen@agentmail.to to confirm your subscription." The confirmation link from the mail did not stay on the URL, and the page did not show a confirmed state.

## Stories, about, apps

Stories is a list of titles at 30px weight 700. About is one heading and a short description. Apps offers an Add to chrome pill. None of those leave the public site.
