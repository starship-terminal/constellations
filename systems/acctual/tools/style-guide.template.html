Acctual design tokens

Measured from acctual.com's public marketing pages in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. acctual.com is a Framer site: its design tokens are hashed --token-<uuid> custom properties resolved live and recorded with their provenance in the token file; component values are computed styles read off the running pages. One theme; the app.acctual.com sign-in was never opened and the home page's invoice mock is a marketing demo, not the product.

The invoicing product itself sits behind a sign-in at app.acctual.com and is not part of this package. Screenshots and per-component readings are in components.md.

Palette

A quiet ground with one bright accent: #fafafa paper under everything, #1e1e1e ink text (with 70%, 60% and 90% steps for links and hovers), #8d8d8d and #666 for secondary copy, and #0d111b near-black navy for every button. The single saturated colour is #0098f2: links, check bullets, the announcement strip and the count pill; a green, purple, red, magenta and navy sit in the declared token sheet but only green shows up inside the demo artwork.

Type

Open Runde does almost everything: display 64/72 and interior titles 48/56 at semibold with -2 to -3% tracking, card titles 20/28, body and controls 16/24 medium, captions 14/20. The programmatic landing pages swap to Selecta Medium (72/72 display, 56/56 sections) and legal body runs Selecta Regular 18/28; Inter appears only in the announcement strip.

Open Runde ships through Google Fonts now, so this page previews in the real face; Selecta and the demo fonts stay as fallbacks.

Space, radius & structure

Buttons are 40px capsules with 14px side padding on a 1px inner ring; cards round to 16px on a 24px inset; the floating nav pill is 80px tall with 20px bottom corners over a blurred page. The content column centres at 1024px inside a 1072px container. Everything else stays square.

Motion

There are no CSS transitions to token: every measured component computes transitionDuration 0s, and the only declared transition lives on Framer's editor bar. acctual.com animates in JavaScript (Framer springs) — hovers, reveals and the mobile menu are runtime moves the measurement layer cannot time. The one honest duration token is core.duration.instant = 0ms.

duration-instant 0ms (click)

Layout

A marketing page at 1440: the 80px frosted nav pill floats over a centred 768px hero holding the 64px display, the count pill and the dark CTA; below, a 1024px column carries section bands, persona cards, the fee table and the FAQ accordion before the quiet footer. At 390 the bar collapses to 48px with a menu trigger, display drops to 40px, and the column goes single-rail with a 16px gutter.

Cards, rows & accordion

Cards borrow the page colour (#fafafa, 16px corners, 24px inset) so they read as pressed panels rather than containers.
