# Resume.io design reference

A token set for Resume.io's signed-out surfaces. Read on **2026-11-19** from **resume.io** in the T3 preview browser (innerWidth 1169, devicePixelRatio 2) and, for screenshots only, headless Chromium at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844. Captured: the marketing home, resume templates, resume examples, cover-letter examples, pricing, about, blog, the AI builder page, a 404, and the signed-out sign-in shell at `/app/auth/sign-in` through the email step. One theme. The product paints light. The footer is a dark band inside that theme.

The marketing CSS declares colour ramps (`--neutral-*`, `--blue-*`, `--indigo-*`, `--green-*`, `--red-*`, `--orange-*`, `--amber-*`, `--beige-*`) and a few brand colours. The signed-in shell's builder CSS adds a spacing scale, a type scale, font weights, and shadow tokens. Semantic roles (page, surface, action, text, border) were read from computed styles on the painted controls, then matched to those ramps. Nothing here was guessed from a screenshot.

**Boundaries:** the email field was focused and left empty. Continue was not pressed. Facebook, Google, and LinkedIn were not followed. Create, upload, template, and pricing buttons were not followed. No card, phone, download, or checkout was started. help.resume.io was not opened.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page `#eff2f9` and the cards white.
2. Do use `#1a91f0` for the one primary action.
3. Do set body type at 19px / 24px in TT Commons.
4. Do keep the footer as a dark band.
5. Don't style the signed-in builder from this package.
6. Don't invent a dark theme or a missing ramp step.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on `https://resume.io/` (101 names) and on `https://resume.io/app/auth/sign-in` (155 names, including `--spacing-*`, `--font-*`, `--line-*`, `--weight-*`, and the shadow tokens). Stylesheet rules for `.button`, `.field__input`, `.checkbox`, and `.modal__content` were read from the same-origin CSSOM. Component geometry was read from computed styles on the live elements. Headless Playwright with a Chrome user agent saved the screenshots after clicking "Essential only". The first headless attempt, without that user agent, received CloudFront "The request could not be satisfied" and those images were discarded.

## What to distrust

- **The preview browser could not screenshot.** It returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Computed styles from that tab are kept. Screenshots are the later headless pass. Do not treat a screenshot pixel as a token.
- **Viewport.** Token geometry that depends on width (the home hero at 67px / 64px, the about h1 at 50px / 52px) was read at innerWidth 1169, not at 1440. The screenshots are 1440 and 390. Those two widths were not re-measured in the preview tab.
- **Hover and active** on `.button` come from stylesheet rules. They were not hovered live, except the cookie Accept button, which was already `rgb(16, 82, 155)`.
- **Disabled** is the stylesheet rule only. No disabled control was on screen.
- **The AI builder hero** paints a dark indigo in the screenshot (`rgb(26, 28, 106)` sampled from the PNG). That colour was not read as a computed style, so it is not a token.
- **Modal shadow** in the stylesheet dump was cut off after the first layer. The token file does not invent the rest.
- **Social button fills** (`rgb(19, 113, 240)`, `rgb(219, 68, 55)`, `rgb(40, 103, 178)`) are computed colours on the chooser. They are not in the `--blue-*` ramp. They are recorded in `raw/measured.json` and not promoted to semantic tokens.
- **TT Commons, TT Talent, and TT Tricks** are proprietary. The style guide falls back to the system stack named in the tokens.
- **Link blue on the page ground** is about 2.95:1. Muted text on the page ground clears 4.5:1. Secondary text on the page ground is about 3.04:1. These are the product's colours.

## Fonts

- **TT Commons** (400, 500, 600, 700) is the marketing face. `@font-face` points at `/assets/fonts/TTCommons-*.woff2`. Not shipped.
- **TT Talent** (400, 500, and the weights the sign-in shell computed) is the app shell face, including the sign-in screen. Not shipped.
- **TT Tricks** is declared in the landing CSS (400, 700, italics). It was not the computed body or heading face on the pages measured.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the page `#eff2f9` and the cards white.** Home, pricing, and about use that ground. Cards and the nav bar are `#ffffff`. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do use `#1a91f0` for the one primary action.** "Create my resume" and "Build Resume" are that blue with white text and a 4px radius. The second action is a pale blue fill, not a second blue. See [pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
3. **Do set body type at 19px / 24px in TT Commons.** Headings on the marketing pages are the same family at large sizes and weight 400, except the templates title, which is 38px / 700. See [templates](screenshots/templates-light-default.png).
4. **Do keep the footer as a dark band.** `#0f141e` with white text. It is not a theme. See [about](screenshots/about-light-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in builder from this package.** The email step was measured and not submitted. Social sign-in was not followed.
6. **Don't invent a dark theme or a missing ramp step.** The ramps stop where the CSS stopped. Beige, amber, and indigo are in the file because they were declared, not because a screen used every step.
