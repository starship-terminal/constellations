# Purple

Handwritten copy of the measured tokens. Generation needs a catalog entry, and `catalog.json` was left alone.

## Colors

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| page | `#f8f5ef` |
| text | `#181831` |
| accent | `#ca2b16` |
| on-accent | `#ffffff` |
| wordmark | `#1f1a5e` |
| promo bar | `#0f0f3a` |

## Typography

Graphik, arial, sans-serif. Body 16px, weight 400. Nav 14px, weight 500. Hero 44px or 54px, weight 300.

## Rounded

Pill `48px`. Promo bar `8px`. Badge `4px`. Field `16px`.

## Do's and Don'ts

Keep the page cream and the type ink. Use a 48px pill for the primary button. Mark a sale in promo red. Stop before account, cart, and the email field.
