# Patterns

How the public pages combine the components. Screenshots are in [screenshots](screenshots).

## App shell

Every `weave.figma.com` page shares a dark nav bar and a lemon "Start Now" block fixed to the top right. Desktop links are Collective, Enterprise, Pricing, Request a demo, Sign in. Phone keeps Pricing, Sign in and Start Now. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

The footer is `#252525` with company, connect and resource links. It is in the full-page shots.

## Pricing

Individuals and Teams are tabs. Annual and Monthly are a switch. Plan cards sit in a row, then a credit table, then an FAQ whose answers are in the DOM. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png), [pricing-light-teams.png](screenshots/pricing-light-teams.png).

## List

The changelog is a dated list with two filter menus. [changelog-light-default.png](screenshots/changelog-light-default.png).

## Search

The knowledge center is a dark page with a search field and collection cards. The field was focused and not submitted. [help-light-default.png](screenshots/help-light-default.png).

## Not captured

A form with a validation error, empty, loading and error states, a destructive confirmation, feedback after an action, and onboarding all sit in the app or on an external form. Sign in, Start Now, Start free and Get this plan open `https://app.weavy.ai/signin` or `https://app.weavy.ai/pricing`. Those branches stopped. Apply now opens a Notion form and was not submitted. Request a demo and Contact sales open `figma.com` and were not followed.
