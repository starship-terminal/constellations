# Patterns

## Marketing home

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) is the public home at 1440. The nav sits on a pale grid. Links are small uppercase white type. Start Now is a lemon block in the top right with one rounded corner. The headline is about 94px Figma Sans with tight negative tracking. Body copy is 18px `#333333`.

[screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png) is the same page at 390. Collective, Enterprise, and Request a Demo drop out. Pricing, Sign in, and a smaller Start Now stay. There was no hamburger in the DOM.

## Pricing

[screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) shows Individuals and Teams as a tab pair, a Monthly / Annual switch, and four plan cards. The selected tab is richer black. Cards share one grey surface. The plan button is rich black until hover, which turns it lemon. Get this plan was not followed. Contact sales was not followed.

## Enterprise

[screenshots/enterprise-light-default.png](screenshots/enterprise-light-default.png) is a video hero under a 50% black gradient. The white headline uses the same 94px display size. Contact Sales is the lemon-100 button. The grey field in the still is the video, not a page token.
