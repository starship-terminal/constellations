# Grow with Google design reference

A token set and component reference for Grow with Google, the public skills site at grow.google. Captured 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 and 390. `https://grow.google/` redirects to `https://grow.google/intl/ALL_sg/` (Singapore). The English home is `/intl/en_sg/`. A second shell serves the US marketing pages (`/certificates` and the sitemap tree). There is no signed-in surface and no dark theme.

Grow with Google is worth a capture because the public site is two shells on one Glue palette: grey-900 `#202124` on white, Google Sans at 60px on the APAC home, and a black pill, while the US shell spends a brighter blue `#3186ff` on 48px pills and one word of the headline. The four logo colours sit in the Glue ramps (`--glue-blue-500` `#4285f4`, red `#ea4335`, yellow `#fbbc04`, green `#34a853`).

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium loaded public pages. The CSSOM could not read cross-origin sheets, so stylesheet response bodies were parsed with a constructed `CSSStyleSheet`. Glue colours are `:root` custom properties (`--glue-grey-*`, `--glue-blue-*`, and the red, yellow and green ramps). Component colours, sizes and the focus ring are computed styles in [raw/live-readings.json](raw/live-readings.json). `prefers-color-scheme: dark` did not change the body.

## What to distrust

The US accent `#3186ff` is a computed button fill, not a Glue ramp step. APAC pills are grey-900, not that blue. Disabled, pressed, loading and form-error colours were not on screen. `--md-sys-color-*` on `.glue-mwc3` is Material Web, present in the sheet, barely used on these pages. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json. The phone 404 capture measured innerWidth 980, not 390, so it is not a 390 shot. Google Sans and Google Sans Flex are not in the package. The guide falls back to Arial.

## Do and don't

- **Do set APAC type in grey-900 on white.** The home greeting is 60px Google Sans, weight 400. [screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png](screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png)
- **Do use a black pill on the APAC shell and a blue pill on the US shell.** They are not the same accent. [screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png](screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png), [screenshots/us-certificates-light-default.png](screenshots/us-certificates-light-default.png)
- **Do keep the four logo colours for the wordmark and the ring illustration.** They are the Glue 500 steps. [screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png](screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png)
- **Do open the US header as a mega-menu, not a new page.** Hover on "Career Certificates" reveals the certificate list. [screenshots/us-certificates-light-nav-career.png](screenshots/us-certificates-light-nav-career.png)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The dark media query left the page white. [raw/live-readings.json](raw/live-readings.json)
- **Don't treat this as the signed-in product.** There is no account UI on these URLs. [login-review.json](login-review.json)

## Rebuild

```bash
export PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs
export RAW=~/.design-systems/grow-google/raw-shots
node systems/grow-google/tools/capture/public/public.mjs
python3 systems/grow-google/tools/capture/quantize.py "$RAW"
python3 systems/grow-google/tools/build_tokens.py
```

`catalog.json` is left for the batch merge. After that entry exists, `make design-md` rewrites DESIGN.md and the Brief.
