# Patterns

Public pages only. The map of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## App shell

Two shells on one domain.

APAC: white header, Google wordmark, a flat link row, a blue underline on the current item, a large 60px greeting, a black pill, and a concentric-ring illustration in Google blue, red, yellow and green. [screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png](screenshots/apac-home-light-default.png). Phone folds the same links into a drawer. [screenshots/mobile-apac-home-light-nav-open.png](screenshots/mobile-apac-home-light-nav-open.png).

US: the same wordmark, four header groups, a blue promo banner, a 48px headline with one blue word, a blue pill, and a rounded in-page tab strip. [screenshots/us-certificates-light-default.png](screenshots/us-certificates-light-default.png). The header group opens a mega-menu. [screenshots/us-certificates-light-nav-career.png](screenshots/us-certificates-light-nav-career.png).

## Navigation

Recorded in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). `/` redirects to `/intl/ALL_sg/`. English Singapore is `/intl/en_sg/`. `/intl/en/` is a 404.

## Form with a validation error

Not captured. `/partnerapply` and `/government-form` are public forms with an empty captcha field. They were not filled and not submitted.

## Empty, loading, error

The only error page reached is the 404 at `/intl/en/`: "Error: Not Found. The requested URL /intl/en/ was not found on this server." [screenshots/us-404-light-default.png](screenshots/us-404-light-default.png). No empty state and no loading state were shown.

## Destructive confirmation, feedback after an action

Not present on the public site.

## Search and filtering

Not present. The APAC home greeting is not a search field.

US sitemap is a link index. [screenshots/us-sitemap-light-default.png](screenshots/us-sitemap-light-default.png). Certificate and story indexes are card lists, not tables.
