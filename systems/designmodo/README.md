# Designmodo design reference

A measured reference for Designmodo's public signed-out site (designmodo.com), captured 2026-11-21 in headless Chromium at 1440x900 and 390x844. The site is a WordPress theme. It does not publish a semantic token layer. Colours and type below are computed styles from named elements.

The capture stays on marketing pages, pricing, the email-template index, the sign-in form (not filled), and the Postcards gallery URL. The gallery paints a Welcome back dialog. Closing it did not leave a usable signed-out gallery. The builder canvas and every post-login surface were not entered.

## How the values were read

Playwright loaded the public pages. `tools/capture/harvest.mjs` read stylesheets (13 custom properties, all Swiper or landing layout, none a colour role) and `getComputedStyle` on body, h1, the hero paragraph, the Sign Up link, the Open App link, the Login button and the email input. Hover on Open App was a real pointer hover. Focus on Sign Up was `element.focus()` and painted no ring. Colour counts on the home page are in `raw/components-computed.json` under `counts.home`.

`theme-color`, mask-icon colour and a web manifest were absent.

## What to distrust

The hero ground looks near-black in the screenshots. It was not read as a computed background on a named element, so it is not a token. Body's own background is transparent.

The phone menu shot did not settle. Do not treat the blue band in [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png) as the menu's design.

Screenshots are full colour, not quantised. A street address is printed in the site footer and on the contact page. Those full-page shots and the contact page are not in this package. Viewport shots can still clip a footer line on a short page. If a shot shows an address, drop it.

Sign Up focus did not change the fill. That is a failed focus ring, not a measured ring of 0 that the product intends. Recorded as not captured.

## Fonts

Body, headings and buttons compute to InterVariable, then -apple-system, system-ui, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif. The theme also loads an Inter stylesheet, plus Google fonts Archivo, IBM Plex Mono and Prata. Those three were not the computed family on the measured elements. Inter's licence was not read. The tokens name the computed stack and do not embed the font.

## Do and don't

1. **Do use the green pill for the main product action.** Open App is #27ae60, 999px radius, 53px tall. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do use the orange pill for Sign Up.** #f49a40, 999px, 38.5px. Same shot.
3. **Do set body text in InterVariable at 16px on #313942.** [tokens.json](tokens.json) `core.typography.body`.
4. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public pages report `colorScheme: normal` and no toggle.
5. **Don't style the builder from this package.** The canvas was not entered. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
6. **Don't treat the phone menu shot as the drawer.** [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png) did not settle.
