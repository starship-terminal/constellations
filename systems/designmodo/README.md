# Designmodo design reference

A token set and component reference for Designmodo's public marketing surface and the signed-out Postcards app shell — for building SaaS marketing pages, pricing pages, email-builder dashboards and auth modals in the style of the real thing. It was read from **designmodo.com** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /postcards/pricing, /email-templates, /postcards, /slides, the public sign-in form at /my-account/sign-in/, the Postcards app route /postcards/app/folder/templates behind its Welcome-back modal, plus a 390px mobile pass on home and the app.

Designmodo is worth a capture because it runs two different design systems on one domain, and both are readable signed out. The **marketing surface** is InterVariable over a dark `#071c26`/`#17242b` hero band: a 56px display heading (91.5px on /postcards/pricing and /postcards, 63px on /email-templates), the `#f49a40` orange Sign Up Free pill that deepens to `#f18110` on hover, the `#27ae60` green Open App CTA, near-black `#0d1d24` product CTAs that flip to `#fb754b` coral on hover, and a white footer carrying `#313942` ink. The **Postcards app** is a Fira Sans styled-components product: the same `#071c26` navy on a 60px app header, `#2198ed` blue on the Log in pill and the selected sidebar item, `#485f6b` slate on Sign Up, `#e8ecf0` secondary fills, the green `#27ae60` + Create action, a 241px workspace sidebar, filter labels, and 266x592 template cards carrying 9px `#2198ed` FREE and `#27ae60` NEW badges. The Welcome-back modal that gates the gallery is real UI — Escape dismisses it and the whole gallery sits in the DOM behind it; every modal action was measured, none submitted. **Boundary:** the builder canvas, saving and every post-login surface need an account; the sign-in form was measured, never submitted.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export designmodo` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep the two systems separate.
2. Do let the accent own the action.
3. Do pair the radius with the surface.
4. Do keep badges tiny.
5. Don't darken the page into a dark theme.
6. Don't style the builder from this package.
7. Don't invent hover inversions.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every number in this package was read off the live site in headless Chromium, signed out: computed styles from the running DOM on each route (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`), stylesheet custom properties resolved on a probe (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`), and full-page screenshots stitched from inner-scroll tiles where the page scrolls inside a nested container (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs` + `tools/capture/quantize.py`). Interactive states were produced locally — hover, Tab focus, and DevTools-forced `:active`. The app gallery was reached by dismissing the Welcome-back modal with Escape — a local, safe toggle — and every modal and form action was measured, never pressed. Nothing was submitted, signed into or followed to an authenticated surface.

Unlike most captures, designmodo.com ships almost no token declarations: the stylesheet resolves to 13 custom properties, all Font Awesome internals. So this package is measured end to end — every colour, size, radius and dimension carries the computed-style reading and the component it came from rather than a declared-variable source, with the one exception listed under Known gaps.

## Known gaps

- `light.background.paper` (`#f4f4f4`, the app gallery canvas) was entered by hand at capture time: the recon read the app body background as rgb(244,244,244) but never saved that reading, so no file in `raw/` holds it. Its source says so; a recapture of the `app` page should record the body background and let the builder read it.
- `tpl-card` was not found by the 2026-11-13 re-run (`recaptureMissed`); `core.component.tpl-card-width` and `-height` are the readings kept from the earlier run and say so in their source.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the two systems separate.** Marketing is InterVariable with dark hero bands and orange/green pill actions; the app is Fira Sans over the `#f4f4f4` canvas with the navy header and blue/slate buttons. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [app-light-default](screenshots/app-light-default.png).
2. **Do let the accent own the action.** `#27ae60` green carries Open App, + Create, the modal Login and Create Account, and the NEW badge; `#f49a40` orange is the one marketing Sign Up pill. See [openapp-cta](components/crops/openapp-cta-default.png) and [nav-signup](components/crops/nav-signup-default.png).
3. **Do pair the radius with the surface.** Marketing CTAs are 999px pills; app controls are 5-8px radii; product CTAs 12px. See [app-login](components/crops/app-login-default.png) and [pc-cta](components/crops/pc-cta-default.png).
4. **Do keep badges tiny.** FREE and NEW are 9px Fira Sans chips — `#2198ed` blue and `#27ae60` green — on the template cards. See [free-badge](components/crops/free-badge-default.png).
5. **Don't darken the page into a dark theme.** The navy header and dark hero band are surfaces inside the single light theme; the export keeps `dark` empty. See [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).
6. **Don't style the builder from this package.** Everything behind the account — the canvas, saving — was never reached; the Welcome-back modal is the boundary. See [app-light-default](screenshots/app-light-default.png).
7. **Don't invent hover inversions.** Actions deepen on hover (`#27ae60` → `#1e8449`, `#f49a40` → `#f18110`, `#2198ed` → `#485f6b` on the app pill); the product CTA flips to `#fb754b` coral. See [pc-cta-hover](components/crops/pc-cta-hover.png).
