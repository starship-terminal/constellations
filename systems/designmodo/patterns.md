# Patterns

How the public pages combine. Screenshots are layout evidence. Values are in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Marketing shell

The home page stacks a Black Friday banner, a 70px header (logo, Apps, Articles, Log In, Sign Up), a dark hero with a product tab and an Open App pill, then a light section of product cards. Shot: [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone width keeps the banner and the logo, and replaces the centre links with a hamburger. Shot: [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Menus

Apps, Articles, the account panel and the cart are dropdowns already in the page. They open over the hero. Shots are named in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Sign-in wall

[https://designmodo.com/my-account/sign-in/](https://designmodo.com/my-account/sign-in/) is a centred card on a light page: Google, SSO, then email and password, then a blue Login and a green Create Account. Shot: [sign-in-light-default.png](screenshots/sign-in-light-default.png). The form was not filled.

## Pricing

Postcards pricing is a light page of plan cards. Shot: [postcards-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/postcards-pricing-light-default.png). Add-to-cart links were not clicked.

The Postcards template URL paints a Welcome back dialog over the gallery. Closing it did not reveal a usable signed-out gallery. Shot: [postcards-gallery-light-welcome.png](screenshots/postcards-gallery-light-welcome.png). The builder canvas was not entered.
