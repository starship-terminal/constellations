# ChatGPT for iOS design reference

A token set and component reference for ChatGPT's native iOS app (OpenAI). Use it to build a native iOS chat or assistant app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a pure black or near-white page, replies set as plain text on the page with only the owner's messages in a bubble, a two-row glass composer at the bottom, a sidebar instead of a tab bar, iOS grouped settings, and one user-chosen accent colour that tints the owner's bubble, the send button and the primary pills. It was captured from the shipped app on iOS 26 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account on a workspace seat: dark on 2026-10-23 (the app's Appearance is System and the phone is dark) and light on 2026-10-24 (Settings > Theme > Appearance > Light, put back to System afterwards). The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 41 captures: 22 in dark (new chat in both modes, the composer, plus and chat menus, the sidebar, a thread, find in chat, Settings, General, Personalization with an error alert, Usage, the effort slider, search and Scheduled) and 19 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) nothing in them is masked: they show the owner's chats, suggestions and work email (on the allow list), and no credentials or personally identifiable data.

[systems/chatgpt](../chatgpt/README.md) is ChatGPT's web app, captured separately from its CSS. This package takes no values from it; [Compared with the web app](README.md#compared-with-the-web-app) says how the two differ.

This package contains no ChatGPT code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are OpenAI's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the icons, the logo or the accent palette as a brand.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export chatgpt-ios` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep replies on the page and only the owner's messages in a bubble.
2. Do let one accent carry the primary actions.
3. Do float controls as glass over the content instead of drawing bars.
4. Do use iOS grouped settings as they are.
5. Don't fill destructive actions red.
6. Don't tint either button of an alert.
7. Do show focus with the caret alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

ChatGPT for iOS is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/chatgpt-ios/raw-shots/`, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 156 named readings, 243 values in all, most in both themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a gradient, a thin mark such as the caret) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height or a row pitch |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font families |

Scale steps (spacing, the 26 pt card radius, the 23 pt caret) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in one theme only (the search field and the thread's date line in dark, the Share Message banner in light), the other theme's token is Derived and says what it was filled from.

## What to distrust

- **The fonts.** Not determinable from pixels. The text matches the iOS system face, and the measured sizes land on 22, 20, 17, 15, 14 and 12 pt, which fits it, but `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured. Rendered maths uses a serif italic close to KaTeX's (`fontFamily.math`, also Not captured).
- **Glass.** The round buttons, the Chat / Work control, the composer, menus, the alert, the search field and the effort slider are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it: the chat menu reads navy over the owner's bubble (`color.surface.menuTinted`). In light the glass is near-white on a near-white page and carries a soft shadow that was not measured, so the style guide's light menus look flatter than the app's. Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: grouped cards 25.6, composer and bubble 22.2, alert 32.4, chat menu 30.7. The plus menu's 44.4 reads high because its glass rim softens the corner (plus or minus 4 pt). The search tile's 12 pt is judged, not read.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **The accent is the owner's choice.** The account uses Blue. Every accent-coloured token (`accent.default`, `accent.pill`, `surface.userBubble`, `text.onUserBubble`, `text.accent`, `focus.caret`) was read with Blue applied; the other nine options were seen only as menu swatches, so what they do to the bubble is unknown. The usage meter (#339CFF) and the unread dot (#0090FF dark, #0088FE light) are other blues that may not follow the accent.
- **The 18 captures are not loading states.** Both were planned as loading skeletons, but the skeletons had resolved by the capture: `dark/18-search-loading-skeleton-chips` matches 19 (search, no results) and `light/18-scheduled-loading-card-skeletons` matches 21 (Scheduled) apart from the clock and caret. No value is read from either, and no loading state is captured.
- **The dark caret was caught mid-blink** in 01 (#2E4085). The caret token comes from the search field in 19 (dark) and the composer in 02 (light), both at full strength.
- **The scrim differs by theme**: black at 46% in dark and 20% in light, each from one field under one alert.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no switch off.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; every switch on the captured pages was on.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass and the light composer gradient band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep replies on the page and only the owner's messages in a bubble.** Replies run the full text width in `text.reply`, with maths, bold lines and an action row under them; the owner's message is a right-aligned rounded block in the accent's bubble colour. See [06](screenshots/dark/06-chat-thread-blue-bubble-math.png), [08](screenshots/dark/08-find-in-chat-no-results.png) and [06 light](screenshots/light/06-chat-thread-blue-bubble-math.png).
2. **Do let one accent carry the primary actions.** Send, voice, the sidebar's Chat pill, New task and the effort slider's fill all use the chosen accent, and the owner's bubble and a selected menu item are tinted from it; switches stay iOS green. See [03](screenshots/dark/03-composer-typed-send.png), [21](screenshots/dark/21-scheduled-tabs-cards-primary-pill.png), [04](screenshots/dark/04-plus-menu-selected-item.png) and [11](screenshots/dark/11-general-switches-on-popup-rows.png).
3. **Do float controls as glass over the content instead of drawing bars.** The round sidebar, close and back buttons, the Chat / Work control and the composer have no bar behind them; the thread scrolls under them. See [01](screenshots/dark/01-work-mode-segmented-composer-focused.png), [06](screenshots/dark/06-chat-thread-blue-bubble-math.png) and [11](screenshots/dark/11-general-switches-on-popup-rows.png).
4. **Do use iOS grouped settings as they are.** Cards on the grouped page, 52 pt rows with an outline icon, values and pop-up chevrons in grey on the right, hairlines inset to the label, section headers and helper text outside the card. See [09](screenshots/dark/09-settings-grouped-popup-values.png), [09 light](screenshots/light/09-settings-grouped-popup-values.png) and [13](screenshots/dark/13-personalization-popups-text-field-empty.png).
5. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Delete is red text with a red outline icon at the end of an ordinary glass menu. See [07](screenshots/dark/07-chat-menu-destructive-glass.png) and [07 light](screenshots/light/07-chat-menu-destructive-glass.png).
6. **Don't tint either button of an alert.** The error alert's Cancel and Retry are two equal glass capsules with the same label colour. See [12](screenshots/dark/12-error-dialog-cancel-retry.png) and [12 light](screenshots/light/12-error-dialog-cancel-retry.png).
7. **Do show focus with the caret alone.** A focused composer or search field keeps its glass and gains only an accent caret. See [01](screenshots/dark/01-work-mode-segmented-composer-focused.png), [20](screenshots/dark/20-search-focused-empty-prompt.png) and [02 light](screenshots/light/02-chat-mode-suggestions-composer.png).

## Compared with the web app

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass buttons, menus, alerts, switches, pop-up selects and the grouped list rather than redrawing them, and let the accent come from a setting the way ChatGPT's Accent color does. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
