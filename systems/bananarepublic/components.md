# Components

What was on the signed-out shop on 2026-11-22. States that were not measured are listed as gaps.

## Wordmark

SVG, fill `#ad280a`, 300 by 60 on the white bar. [icons/mark.svg](icons/mark.svg). [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Department link

Banana v1, 14px, weight 400, tracking 2px, black, no underline in the resting state. Sale uses BananaSerif at 1.68px tracking. Menus were read from the DOM and were not photographed open.

## Search

Empty field, 270 by 32, white, 1px bottom rule `#5e5c5a`, Lynstone v1 14px weight 350. The search button was disabled while the field was empty. Not submitted.

## Hero chip

White, black type, 280 by 56, radius 0, 12px weight 600, tracking 1px. Default only.

## Category title

Linotype Didot BR, 72px, weight 300, black, centered. Fabric chips under it are the same family at 16px. [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Filter chip

White, 38px tall, 1px outline `#e5e4e4`, Lynstone v1 12px weight 350, radius 0. Not opened.

## Product card

Name and price in Lynstone v1, 12px, weight 350, black. Best Seller is a 24px chip, `#f2f1ee` at 80% opacity, weight 600. A struck price stayed black. Decoration was not a line-through on the measured node.

## Product title

Banana v1, 18px, weight 400, tracking 1px. The page renders it in capitals. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Colour swatch

30px square, 1px `#999999`, inside a 36px cell, radius 0. Selected state was the red square with a heavier frame. That frame's computed style was not isolated from the resting swatch.

## Size chip

Label, 53 by 39, white, Lynstone v1 12px, radius 0. Not selected.

## Add to Bag

Black fill, white type, 318 by 56, radius 0, Lynstone v1 14px weight 350. Not clicked.

## Sign up

Footer button, white, 56px tall, radius 0, Lynstone v1 14px weight 350. Not clicked.

## Gaps

Hover, focus, pressed, disabled, loading, and error were not measured. No native select, checkbox, radio, switch, tooltip, dialog, toast, table, or avatar was opened. The account menu, bag, email form, and credit-card apply were not opened.
