Palette

The declared --color-* layer carries the named neutrals, brand blues, hue accents, ramps and status colours; the booking group holds the invitee application's own ramp plus its measured painted blues. Light and dark roles are the same declared variables, re-bound by the .dark scope.

Spacing and radius

The --spacing/--gap scales run 4-128px with 96-120px section padding; the --radius-ui scale runs 4-48px, and the components paint inside it — 16px CTAs, 20px SSO pills, 40-48px cards, full-circle day cells. Borders are quiet: #d8dce0 subtle, #c6ccd2 default, #93a0aa strong.
