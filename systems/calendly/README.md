# Calendly design reference

A token set and component reference for Calendly's public surface — for building marketing pages and invitee flows in the style of the real thing. It was read from **calendly.com** on **2026-11-12**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home, scheduling, pricing, about and enterprise-solution marketing pages, the login and signup cards (measured, never submitted), a 390px mobile pass, and the public invitee booking pages under `/calendly-demo` — a separate application from the marketing site — including the month calendar with an enabled day selected but never a confirmed time.

Calendly is worth a capture because it is two products in one domain. The marketing site declares a complete token layer in its Astro/Tailwind bundles — the named palette (midnight `#071a31` ink, magnolia `#fcfbf8` field, cornflower `#6bb1ff` focus rings, cobalt `#1e5ea7`, plus linen/steel/silver neutrals, hue accents, ramps and badge/status roles), the `--text-ui` ramp from 72px huge to 12px overline, the `--radius-ui` and `--spacing`/`--gap` scales, and three served faces (Calendly Sans, Geist, Wulkan Text) — then paints it light with `.dark` islands that re-bind the same roles (hero card, footer card, dark sections). The invitee surface runs a second, older design system: the booking-kit application's camelCase layer (`--coreColorBlue*`, `--primary-color*`, `--fontFamily*`) under Proxima Nova 14/21 on `#0a2540` ink, painting a calendar whose actual blues — `#006bff` selected fill and slot borders, `#004eba` link ink, `#f4f8ff` day wash — exist only as compiled atomic classes and were measured, not declared. **Boundary:** the product workspace (event types, availability, meetings, integrations, billing) lives behind `/app/login` on app.calendly.com and was never reached; the invitee confirmation form past a time slot was never opened; nothing was booked, paid or submitted.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

### Principles

1. Do paint the field magnolia, not white.
2. Do make the primary action midnight.
3. Do ring focus in cornflower.
4. Do run the type ramp tight.
5. Do give cards big soft radii and quiet shadows.
6. Do invert inside .dark islands.
7. Do keep the booking blue inside the booking app.
8. Don't mix the two systems.
9. Don't style the workspace from this package.
10. Don't book from this package.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body (the Astro `/_astro/*.css` bundles and the cross-origin `assets.calendly.com` booking bundle from network responses, inline `<style>` blocks re-parsed with `CSSStyleSheet`) and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json), resolved on two probes: the marketing page once plain and once under `.dark` (the island scope), the booking page once light and once `.dark` (declared but unpainted). Both token layers are genuinely declared — the marketing `--color-*`/`--text-ui-*`/`--radius-ui-*`/`--spacing-*` system and the booking `--coreColor*`/`--primary-color*`/`--fontFamily*` system — so most tokens are declared variables; the blues the booking calendar actually paints are atomic classes with no variable, so those are measured computed values. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets (booking leaves resolve on the booking pass via `"resolve": "bookingLight"`).
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state, across the marketing pages, the auth cards, the booking profile/event pages and mobile. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md). Calendly's buttons and cards keep their fill on painted child layers (the hero card's gradient, the plan cards' radius), so the readings record painted layers rather than trusting the wrapper.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the mega-menu on hover, the mobile menu, the Monthly/Yearly billing radio on /pricing, and an enabled day selected on the booking calendar (`booking-event-light-day`) — the last read-only: no time slot was ever confirmed. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, booked or submitted: the login and signup cards were captured as surfaces, never filled or submitted; every Get-started, Sign-up, Talk-to-sales and plan CTA was measured, never followed; the billing radio, mega-menu, mobile menu and the booking day were toggled as local UI state; the invitee confirmation form was never reached. The OneTrust cookie UI, Ketch and Intercom launchers were hidden, never answered — no consent button was ever clicked and no consent POST was sent.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **This is the public shell, not the product.** The Calendly workspace — event-type editor, availability, meetings, routing, workflows, contacts, integrations, analytics, billing — sits behind /app/login and is uncaptured. Treat the product's own design system as unknown.
- **Two applications, not a theme toggle.** The marketing site and the booking pages share a domain and some variable names but are different codebases with different fonts, palettes and geometry. `tokens.json` keeps booking roles under `light.booking.*` (they paint light); `dark.*` is the marketing `.dark` island scope, not a user-selectable dark mode, and the booking bundle's dark scope is declared but never paints.
- **The marketing `.dark` islands invert the primary action.** Inside them the primary button is magnolia-on-midnight with midnight text — the mirror of the page's midnight pill. Copy the scoped roles, not the page defaults.
- **The booking blues aren't declared.** `#006bff` (selected day, slot borders), `#004eba` (links/day numbers), `#f4f8ff` (day wash), `#d4e0ed` (hairlines) and `#a6bbd1` (month nav) are compiled into atomic classes; they are measured values, labelled as such in the tokens.
- **A selected day locks.** Once picked, a booking day paints solid `#006bff` and goes `disabled` — the selected state is a committed choice, not a hover.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **One failed state.** `menu-link` pressed timed out — the mega-menu closes on the forced press — and is recorded as a failed state, not a measured absence. `email-field` focus-visible was not recorded.

## Fonts

Four served faces do everything. **Calendly Sans** runs marketing headings, the wordmark, nav and button labels. **Geist** runs the marketing body and form text (the `--font-geist` var). **Wulkan Text** (400/500/600) runs the editorial serif headings — the 48px section h2s. **Proxima Nova** runs the entire booking application at 14/21 body and 16/700 time slots. All are served from Calendly's own CDN; none is distributed with the package — the style guide falls back to system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the field magnolia, not white.** Marketing surfaces sit on `#fcfbf8` with `#f5f3ee` linen alternates; pure white is reserved for cards, fields and the booking surface. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do make the primary action midnight.** Buttons are `#071a31` pills with `#fcfbf8` text at a 16px radius — no brand blue on marketing chrome. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png).
3. **Do ring focus in cornflower.** The keyboard focus indicator is a 2px solid `#6bb1ff` outline — the same declared `action-focus-border` on every interactive element. See [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-focus-visible.png).
4. **Do run the type ramp tight.** Display and heading steps carry -2px tracking at ~1.1 leading (72px huge, 48px serif h2); body runs 14-20px at 1.4. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png) and [h2-serif](components/crops/h2-serif-default.png).
5. **Do give cards big soft radii and quiet shadows.** Auth and hero cards paint 48px, plan cards 40px, on the multi-layer `shadow-ui-*` scale — never heavy borders. See [login-card](components/crops/login-card-default.png) and [plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png).
6. **Do invert inside .dark islands.** Scoped sections flip the same roles — midnight field, magnolia text and primary fill, cornflower info badges — via the same `--color-*` variables, not new values. See [hero-card](components/crops/hero-card-default.png).
7. **Do keep the booking blue inside the booking app.** `#006bff` selected fills, `#004eba` links and the `#f4f8ff` day wash belong to the invitee surface's own system — the marketing site never paints them. See [bk-day-selected](components/crops/bk-day-selected-default.png).
8. **Don't mix the two systems.** Proxima Nova on magnolia or Geist on the booking calendar is wrong — they are separate applications with separate tokens. See [bk-timeslot](components/crops/bk-timeslot-default.png) versus [nav-link](components/crops/nav-link-default.png).
9. **Don't style the workspace from this package.** Every value is the public surface's; the product behind /app/login is uncaptured. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
10. **Don't book from this package.** The invitee flow was captured read-only — past the time slot is a recorded gap, not a design source. See [booking-event-light-day](screenshots/booking-event-light-day.png).
