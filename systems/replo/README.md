# Replo

Replo is a landing-page and store builder. This package records the public marketing site at www.replo.app and the signed-out login form at dashboard.replo.app, measured on 19 November 2026. No account was created. Nothing was typed into a field. Nothing was submitted.

The marketing pages paint one dark theme: black page, cream text (`#f5fad9`), Archivo for UI, editorialNew for plan names. The login form is a second system: Inter on `oklch(0.16 0.0046 72)`, with 8px radii. There is no theme switch on the pages that were opened.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview tab opened https://www.replo.app/, https://www.replo.app/pricing, https://www.replo.app/contact, https://www.replo.app/this-page-does-not-exist-capture, and https://dashboard.replo.app. The dashboard URL redirected to https://dashboard.replo.app/auth/signup. A click on Sign in moved to https://dashboard.replo.app/auth/login. Computed styles and custom properties were read from those live pages at 1440 by 900 CSS pixels, device pixel ratio 2, visual viewport scale 1.

The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`). Screenshots of the public marketing pages were taken in headless Playwright after those pages had already loaded with no Cloudflare challenge. Playwright was not pointed at the login page. Login has measurements and no image.

Marketing colours are the computed `background-color`, `color`, `border` and `box-shadow` of the body, the header buttons, the plan card, the billing track, the open Product menu and the contact field. Auth colours are the computed style of the heading, the field wrapper, Continue and Continue with Google. A custom property that was not painted on a measured control is labelled declared in [raw/readings.md](raw/readings.md).

## What to distrust

The shadcn tokens on `:root` (`--background: #fff`, `--foreground: #0a0a0a`, `--primary: #171717`) resolve to a light palette. The painted page is black and cream. Those light tokens are not in tokens.json.

`body` on the login page reports `color: rgb(0, 0, 0)` while the heading and inputs are `oklch(0.986 0.0031 72)`. The painted text colour is the heading's.

The filename says `light` because the catalog uses that label. The product did not show a light theme. Accent green, pink, magenta, purple and blue are declared custom properties. They were not sampled as a control fill.

Plan-name, price and auth-display tokens omit line-height. It was not recorded. Contact title is the same.

The word Replo inside the home display line is italic in the screenshot. Its computed style was not separated from the h1, so there is no italic type token.

Monthly prices `$119` and `$599` are visible in [pricing-light-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-light-monthly.png). They were not read as computed text. Annual `$99` and `$499` were.

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json, not from the PNG.

## Fonts

Archivo is the marketing UI face, loaded by the site as a Next.js font with an `archivo Fallback`. It is proprietary to the site's bundle and is not committed. The tokens name the measured stack: archivo, "archivo Fallback", Arial, sans-serif.

editorialNew is the plan-name and badge face. The file was not fetched. The tokens name the family that computed style reported.

Inter is the login face, with Helvetica Neue and Arial. It is not committed.

Space Mono is declared as `--font-space-mono` and was not sampled on a painted control.

## Do and don't

- **Use the cream pill for the primary action.** Start for free and Get started for free are `#f5fad9` with `#121212` text. [button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png) shows the header one.
- **Use the black pill for the secondary action.** Book a demo keeps the cream label and a 24% cream border. [button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png).
- **Keep plan cards on one body.** Free, Starter, Pro and Custom share `#0b0b0b`, a 16px radius and an 8% white border. [card-plan.png](components/crops/card-plan.png).
- **Keep login off the marketing palette.** The form uses Inter, an 8px radius and `oklch(0.16 0.0046 72)`. Do not paint Archivo pills on that screen.
- **Do not invent a hover, an error, or a signed-in screen.** Those states were not on the page.
