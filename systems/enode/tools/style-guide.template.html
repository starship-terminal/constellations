Palette

The declared --color-brand-* ramp runs pale-green #f0fde8 (75) to the signature #b0f87c (225) next to warm --color-gray-* neutrals (#f4f2f1 paper to #252322 ink) and the alpha ramps the glass surfaces are built from; the semantic --color-background-*/--color-foreground-*/--color-card-*/--color-border-*/--color-line-*/--color-diagram-* roles resolve per data-mode in the light and dark sets. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius and motion

Radii are small — 2/4/6/10px for pills, nav items, cards and fields — against full pills for the CTAs (33.5M px --radius-full). Controls transition color/background/border/shadow/transform over 150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1); the layout is a 1308px grid with 20px base gutters.
