### Principles

1. Do paint a white page and slate type.
2. Do spend purple on the mark and the one filled action.
3. Do use pills in the bar.
4. Do set display at 60px with tight tracking.
5. Do round the letter card at 24px.
6. Do keep the sign-in card quiet.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't style lifelogs or settings from this package.
9. Don't follow Continue with Google.
10. Don't copy the star or the Greycliff files.
11. Don't expect a new email to open the app.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- Lifelogs, settings and account. This pass stopped at the empty sign-in wall. Continue with Google was not pressed. The email field was focused and left empty.
- Pendant checkout. The dialog and the marketing site both say the Pendant is not sold to new customers. No purchase flow was opened.
- Support chat on `/support`. It is a third-party widget. It was not opened.
- Help search results and article pages. The field was focused and not queried.
- Ashby careers and the Slack community invite. Both leave the site.
- Greycliff files. The family name is measured. The files are not in this package.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes.
- Reduced motion. The product ignores it. That is recorded, not corrected.
- Hand-entered values. `core.color.slate.200` (`#e2e8f0`, so `light.border`), `core.color.zinc.500` (`#71717a`), `light.helpBand` (`#8a54e1`), `core.elevation.popover` and `core.elevation.dialog`, `core.space.1`, `.3`, `.5` and `.16`, and `core.layout.page`, `.phone` and `.navHeight` were entered by hand at capture time. No raw reading holds them: the Solutions panel, the dialog close icon, the help header band and the top bar were not measured, and the measured dialog card computes no box-shadow. They are labelled Reconstruction in tokens.json.
- Values corrected to the readings (2026-11 audit): `core.font.size.menu` 13px to the measured 12.8px; `core.typography.heading` line-height 41px to 41.4px, with the measured -0.9px tracking; `core.typography.body-lg` line-height 29px to 29.25px; `core.elevation.card` colour slate 900 to the measured slate 800 at 15%; `core.elevation.control` from one invented 0 1px 2px layer to the carousel button's two measured layers; `core.font.family.mono` now carries the whole measured stack.
- The Sign Up Unavailable readings in [raw/gated-dialog.json](raw/gated-dialog.json) were taken on 2026-11-18 after an email was submitted. This refresh removed those screenshots. The JSON remains as a labelled prior reading, not as a state shown on 2026-11-21.
- components.md was written at capture time; no builder in tools/ regenerates it.
- The phone sign-in shot is 1x. That host reports `devicePixelRatio` 1 while Playwright still writes a 2x PNG, so the shot was taken at 1x to match the page.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint a white page and slate type.** Page is white, titles are slate 900, body is slate 600 ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)).
2. **Do spend purple on the mark and the one filled action.** Purple 500 is the star, the Copy button and developer anchors ([Copy](components/crops/copy-button-default.png)).
3. **Do use pills in the bar.** Nav links and Solutions are 9999px radius, 16px/600, with an 8% slate wash on hover ([nav](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png)).
4. **Do set display at 60px with tight tracking.** The acquisition headline and solution titles are 60px, tracking -1.5px ([hero](components/crops/hero-title-default.png)).
5. **Do round the letter card at 24px.** The acquisition letter is a soft card, not a flat column ([card](components/crops/letter-card-default.png)).
6. **Do keep the sign-in card quiet.** White, 8px radius, one Google button, one email field, on a pale field ([sign-in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)).
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site does not paint one.
8. **Don't style lifelogs or settings from this package.** Those screens were not reached ([sign-in wall](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)).
9. **Don't follow Continue with Google.** OAuth was not used ([sign-in wall](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)).
10. **Don't copy the star or the Greycliff files.** The mark and the typeface are Limitless's. Tokens name the system.
11. **Don't expect a new email to open the app.** This pass did not submit one. The empty wall is the boundary ([sign-in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)).
