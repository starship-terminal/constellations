# Stripe design reference

Tokens and component notes for Stripe, the payments company at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com/). Captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. Public routes only: the marketing site, docs.stripe.com, and the signed-out walls at `dashboard.stripe.com/login` and `dashboard.stripe.com/register`. Signed out the whole time. No email was typed. No account was created. No card, bank, or KYC step was opened.

The home page is the current marketing system (HDS). Colour roles live as custom properties (`--hds-color-action-bg-solid` resolves to `#533afd`). Pricing still paints an older chrome: page `#f6f9fc`, pill buttons at radius 16.5px, accent `#635bff`. The dashboard login paints a third purple, `#675dff`, on a 6px field and a 12px card. Sohne Variable is the marketing face. The files are not shipped.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export stripe` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Paint the home primary button at 4px radius, fill `#533afd`, label in white at 14px / 400.
2. Keep the home Sign in as a quiet button: transparent fill, brand text, 1px border `#d6d9fc`.
3. Set the home hero in Sohne at 48px, weight 300, tracking -0.96px.
4. Keep pricing on its own page colour `#f6f9fc` and its own pill accent `#635bff`.
5. Stop at the login card.
6. Do not invent a dark theme.
7. Do not use the hero's computed green as a brand colour.
8. Do not treat the blurred dashboard behind register as a signed-in capture.
9. Do not type into the email field or follow Google, passkey, or SSO.
10. Do not ship the Sohne files.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://stripe.com/` and redirected to `https://stripe.com/en-sg`. It returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so the screenshots come from a later headless Chromium pass on the public pages. Computed styles were read in both places. The marketing stylesheets are cross-origin, so custom properties were taken from the fetched CSS (`70d739dff01e0964.css`) and checked by resolving them on `documentElement`. A cookie banner, where it appeared, was hidden in CSS and never clicked. Login and register were photographed and not submitted.

## What to distrust

The hero headline's computed colour is a canvas blend (`rgb(129, 184, 26)` on one layer, `rgba(0, 14, 255, 0.5)` on another). That is the wave graphic, not a text token. Read the headline size and weight from the tokens, and the colour from the screenshot.

Hovered Contact sales computed `rgb(72, 53, 221)`. The declared hover token `--hds-color-action-bg-solidHover` resolves to `#4032c8`. Both are recorded. The computed hover is not the token.

Pricing and the dashboard are a different generation from the home page. Do not flatten `#635bff` and `#675dff` into `#533afd`.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Pages taller than 6000px are cropped to the top. Phone shots are one viewport, 780×1688 physical pixels. The cookie banner is hidden in the headless shots.

## Fonts

Sohne Variable (`sohne-var`) is Stripe's marketing face, weight axis 1 to 1000, with SF Pro Display and then sans-serif as the fallback. The dashboard adds `sohne-woff` and Helvetica Neue. Docs use the system sans. Source Code Pro Medium is the code face. None of the files are in this package. The tokens name the stacks and fall back.

## Do and don't

- Paint the home primary button at 4px radius, fill `#533afd`, label in white at 14px / 400. See [components/crops/nav-default.png](components/crops/nav-default.png).
- Keep the home Sign in as a quiet button: transparent fill, brand text, 1px border `#d6d9fc`. See [components/crops/nav-default.png](components/crops/nav-default.png).
- Set the home hero in Sohne at 48px, weight 300, tracking -0.96px. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- Keep pricing on its own page colour `#f6f9fc` and its own pill accent `#635bff`. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- Stop at the login card. The wall is the capture. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
- Do not invent a dark theme. These pages did not paint one. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- Do not use the hero's computed green as a brand colour. It is the wave graphic. See [components/crops/hero-default.png](components/crops/hero-default.png).
- Do not treat the blurred dashboard behind register as a signed-in capture. See [screenshots/register-light-default.png](screenshots/register-light-default.png).
- Do not type into the email field or follow Google, passkey, or SSO. See [components/crops/login-card-default.png](components/crops/login-card-default.png).
- Do not ship the Sohne files. The tokens name the stack and fall back. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
