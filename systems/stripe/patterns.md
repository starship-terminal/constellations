# Stripe patterns

Public pages only. The signed-in product starts behind the login wall.

## Home

A 36px announcement bar (`light.announce`, `#0d1738`) sits on a 76px header. The header is transparent over the wave. The wordmark is 60×25, fill `light.text`. Primary and Sign in sit at the right, both 40px tall, radius 4px.

The hero headline is 48px / 300. Its painted colour comes from the wave graphic, not from a text token. Below the hero the page is white, with bento cards and a footer on `light.pageMuted` (`#f8fafd`).

Screenshot: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone: [screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png).

## Pricing

An older header, 76px, background `light.pricingPage` (`#f6f9fc`). Sign in is a text link in `light.pricingAccent`. Contact sales is a 16.5px pill in the same purple. The title is 56px / 500 in `light.pricingText`.

Screenshot: [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Login wall

`dashboard.stripe.com/login` is a white page with a colour wave on the right and a 540px card. Email and password are empty. Sign in, Google, passkey and SSO were not pressed. Create account links to the register wall.

Screenshot: [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Register wall

`dashboard.stripe.com/register` asks for email, full name, password and country, then Create account or Google. A blurred dashboard sits behind the card. That image is the wall's backdrop. The app was not entered. No field was filled.

Screenshot: [screenshots/register-light-default.png](screenshots/register-light-default.png).

## Docs

`docs.stripe.com` uses the system sans, a white search chip at radius 8px, and a Sign in button at radius 6px. It is a separate surface from the marketing HDS page.
