# TIDAL

TIDAL's public signed-out web chrome, read on 2026-11-19. The surfaces are the signed-out player at [https://tidal.com/](https://tidal.com/) and [https://tidal.com/view/pages/explore](https://tidal.com/view/pages/explore), the OAuth login chrome at [https://login.tidal.com/authorize](https://login.tidal.com/authorize), and the download page at [https://offer.tidal.com/download](https://offer.tidal.com/download). No account was created. No plan was started. The only theme on these surfaces was dark. Widths measured were 1440 by 900, a 1169 by 731 fill viewport, and 390 by 844.

2. Keep the accent off the chrome.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://tidal.com/ in its own tab. `getComputedStyle` was read on body, the navigation aside, sidebar links, Log in, Create a free account, Download TIDAL, Get started, the transfer-card title and body, the home h1 and h2s, artwork images, and the footer. Custom properties were resolved on `:root`, including the `--wave-color-solid-*` palette, `--wave-border-radius-*`, `--desktop-navbar-height` and `--frosted-glass-nav-surface`.

The same tab then opened Explore, the authorize page (by clicking Log in, which navigates), and the download page. Login field, Continue, the "or" divider and the marketing h1 were read there. A 1440 by 900 viewport and a 390 by 844 viewport were set with the preview resize tool, and `innerWidth` was read back as those sizes.

Stylesheets on tidal.com were readable. The Square Sans stylesheet on squarecdn.com was cross-origin and was not parsed. Its family name comes from the computed font stack.

## What to distrust

The readings were not saved. [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) holds the pages, viewports, theme and what was not entered, but no style or custom-property value. So every token read in the inspector is labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time, and the two values worked out from those stay Derived. A recapture that writes the computed styles and `:root` custom properties to raw/ would make them Measured.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Nothing in screenshots/ is a capture of the product. Headless Playwright against https://tidal.com/ returned HTTP 403 and a block page, so that shot was deleted and not used.

`dark.color.button.loginDisabled` is white at 0.4 alpha, derived from the disabled Continue opacity. The field stroke is an inset box-shadow, recorded as a border colour. Explore's h1 line-height was not re-read, so the home h1 line-height is stored on that style. The login button's computed line-height was the button height, so the body line-height is stored instead.

Hover on Log in was one computed colour after a hover action. It is not a token, because the browser returned a `color(srgb ...)` value rather than a stable hex. Focus-visible is a declared rule, not a forced state.

`--headliners-green` resolved on `:root` and was not seen on a control. The cookie banner buttons were in the DOM with a box on the viewport edge. Their colours are noted in components.md and are not tokens.

## Fonts

Square Sans Text VF and Square Sans Display VF are the player faces, from square-fonts-production-f.squarecdn.com. They are proprietary and are not in this package. The token stacks fall through to Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

Nationale (`nationale, nationale-regular, helvetica, arial`) is the login and download face. It is proprietary and is not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the black page and the white pill for the primary action.** Create a free account is white on black, 32px, fully round. The secondary action is the frosted grey pill.
- **Keep the accent off the chrome.** The cyan `#33FFEE` is a text-link and wave accent. The signed-out buttons do not use it.
- **Use 12px corners on the login column, and pills on the player.** Continue is 12px. The email field wrapper is 16px. Player buttons are 1000px.
- **Do not invent a light theme.** These surfaces shipped one dark palette.
- **Do not ship a screenshot from this package as evidence of layout.** screenshots/ is empty because the browser did not paint.
