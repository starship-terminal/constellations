# Pocket

Pocket was a read-it-later app. On 2026-11-19 the public site at [https://getpocket.com/](https://getpocket.com/) is a shutdown notice. The page says the web, Android, iOS, and macOS apps, and the browser extensions, are being phased out. This package measures that notice and the error page that `/privacy` and `/tos` return. No account was created. The signed-in app was not opened.

Themes: light, painted. Dark tokens are in the stylesheet and were not painted. Widths: 1440 and 390 CSS pixels, plus a 1169px preview-tab reading.

### Principles

1. Use the coral pocket mark for the brand, and teal for actions.
2. Set headlines in the serif stack and UI in Graphik Web.
3. Keep the shutdown page as a notice.
4. Leave dark unpainted unless the page applies it.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened [https://getpocket.com/](https://getpocket.com/) on its own tab. It redirected to `/home`, title “Pocket”, `html.colormode-light`. `getComputedStyle` on the document element returned 208 custom properties. The two stylesheets are cross-origin, so their rules were fetched as files (`f421bff56e9ba7e2.css` and `85b2c0276c955df5.css` on `assets.getpocket.com`) and parsed. Computed styles on the live page confirmed the painted values: body, header, h1, h3, lede, footer, the secondary button, the language select, and the primary button on the error page.

The preview browser could not produce screenshot frames. The public page had already loaded with no challenge, so the screenshots and crops were taken with headless Playwright at 1440×900 and 390×844, device pixel ratio 2, then quantized to 256 colours. Hover and focus were read with `getComputedStyle` after `hover()` and `focus()` on the live controls.

## What to distrust

Dark tokens are declared on `.colormode-dark`. The live page never applied that class, and there was no control to switch it. Do not treat the dark set as a painted theme.

The secondary button stretches the content column. Its crop is the whole control at 390px, not a small button.

`icon.png` is the 32px favicon scaled 4×. The apple-touch icons returned 503. The sharp wordmark is [icons/wordmark.svg](icons/wordmark.svg).

Doyle computed as the h1 family even though `document.fonts` reported Doyle 500 loaded and the heading weight was 700. Blanco OSF is the `--fontSerif` token and was not used on this page.

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json) and [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Fonts

Graphik Web is the UI face (`--fontSansSerif`), with Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif behind it. It is a commercial face. The tokens name the stack, and this package does not ship the font files.

Doyle is the heading face on the shutdown page (`--fontSerifAlt`), with Garamond, Times, serif behind it. Also commercial. Weight 500 was the face the browser loaded. The headings computed at weight 700.

Blanco OSF is `--fontSerif`. It was declared and not painted here.

## Do and don't

- **Use the coral pocket mark for the brand, and teal for actions.** The wordmark’s pocket is `#EF4056`. The painted primary button and the focus outline are teal (`#008078`, focus `#009990`). See [the wordmark](components/crops/logo.png) and [the primary button](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Set headlines in the serif stack and UI in Graphik Web.** The shutdown h1 is Doyle 38px bold. Body, buttons, and the footer are Graphik Web. See [the first fold](screenshots/home-light-fold.png).
- **Keep the shutdown page as a notice.** The header is a wordmark and a shadow. There is no nav, no list, and no save button on this surface. See [the full page](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Leave dark unpainted unless the page applies it.** The page stayed `colormode-light` while the OS preference was dark. See [the phone capture](screenshots/home-light-phone.png).
