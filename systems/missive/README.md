# Missive design reference

Tokens and component notes for Missive (missiveapp.com), captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. Marketing is one light theme. The signed-out mail app at mail.missiveapp.com paints light and dark through `data-theme`. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing`, `/features`, `/customers`, `/download`, `/security`, `/blog`, `mail.missiveapp.com/#login`, `#signup`, and `#sso`. Signed out the whole time. No form was submitted. No demo was booked. No purchase.

Missive marketing is Webflow. Colour is a named swatch palette on `:root` (`--swatch--neutral-*`, `--swatch--blue-*`, cumin, green). Display is Tiempos Headline. UI is Inter. The mail app is its own stylesheet, a system font stack, and `--brand-color` `rgb(34 102 237)`, the same blue as marketing `--swatch--blue-600`.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export missive` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the marketing page white and the hero in Tiempos Headline.
2. Do fill Try for free and Log in with brand blue, #2266ed, at 8px radius.
3. Do keep Book a demo and Log in in the nav as ghost links in Inter 14px.
4. Do use the system stack on the mail screens, and the inset 1px ring on the social buttons.
5. Do switch the mail app with data-theme, and leave marketing light.
6. Don't invent a dark marketing theme.
7. Don't submit the login, signup or SSO form.
8. Don't follow Book a demo off the site.
9. Don't treat the inbox painting as the product.
10. Don't ship the Tiempos Headline or Inter files.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-19, light, 1440, plus a dark pass on the mail login and signup by setting `data-theme="dark"`. Hover is a real pointer. Focus on the email field is a real focus. Stylesheets were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json): the Webflow sheet `missive.shared.7c139a59c.min.css` and the mail sheet `application-6d1a731ae9d5888b2691.css`. Custom properties were resolved in both themes. The privacy sheet ("Your Privacy Choices") was hidden with CSS and never clicked. Motion was read with `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` on the home, pricing and login pages.

Headless Playwright loaded the marketing site and the mail app. A Cloudflare "Verify you are human" widget appeared on the dark signup screen and was not checked. No payment, phone or government ID was asked for on these routes.

## What to distrust

- The signed-in inbox. `#login` and `#signup` were left unsubmitted. Google and Apple were not clicked. SSO asked for an organization keyword and was not submitted.
- Password reset. `#forgot`, `#forgot-password` and `#reset` redirect to login. `/forgot` is a 404.
- Book a demo. The href is a Calendly URL. It was not followed.
- The product paintings on the marketing pages and the auth quote column. They are published demos, not a signed-in workspace.
- Dark marketing. The marketing site has no dark theme. The dark set is the mail app only.
- Tiempos Headline and Inter files. The family names are measured. The files are not in this package.
- The mail font stack. It names Segoe UI, Roboto, Oxygen and Ubuntu as fallbacks. The painted face on this Mac is the system sans.
- Pressed states. None was captured. A click on Try for free leaves for signup, so pressed had to be forced, and the 2026-11-19 script enabled CSS before DOM on its DevTools session, which Chromium refuses. Every `pressed` entry in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) is null, although that file's source line says pressed was forced. `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now forces `:active:hover` on the element in the kit's order; the readings need a recapture.
- The `hero-try` reading in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Its selector, `a.button-base.w-button`, matched the first nav link (Pricing at y 8), not the hero's Try for free. [components.md](components.md) uses it only as the nav link, so no token comes from it. The script now marks the hero button by its text and position; the hero reading needs a recapture.
- [components.md](components.md) is written by hand from the readings. `tools/build_components_md.py` only checks that the readings and crops still exist, so after a recapture the tables need a hand update.
- The signup Cloudflare widget on the dark shot. It is a challenge, not a Missive control.
- Motion. Home and pricing ignore `prefers-reduced-motion`. Login is partial. That is the reading, not a failed capture.
- `--textMuted` on light. The hero body is neutral 500. Neutral 400 is the next declared step, used as the muted role, and was not the colour of a measured caption.

## Fonts

Tiempos Headline is the marketing display, weight 400, declared in the Webflow sheet. Inter is the marketing UI, weights 400 and 500. Neither file is shipped. The mail app uses `--font-family`, a system stack starting at `-apple-system`. The tokens name the families and fall back to Times New Roman and the system sans.
