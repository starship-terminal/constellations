# Ripple design reference

A token set and component reference for Ripple's public marketing site. It was read from ripple.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. The site paints one light theme. Captured pages: home, payments, one use-case page, customers, insights, FAQ, company, contact, terms, and the Portuguese home, plus the Products menu, search dialog, language menu, and the phone menu.

The signed-in product was not reached. Footer "Customer Login" opens https://home.ripple.com/, which redirects to Auth0 at ripplenet-prod-eu.eu.auth0.com and asks for a username and a password. The password was not submitted. That branch stops there.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the homepage CSSOM in headless Chromium. Ripple declares two palettes as space-separated RGB channels: `--bds-*` (the brand system the buttons use) and `--color-*` (the marketing sections). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) converts those channels to hex. Component colours, type, radii, and the button gradients were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Screenshots came from [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs). The cookie banner was hidden and never clicked.

## What to distrust

- The signed-in payments app is unknown. Auth0 stopped the branch.
- The primary button is not flat `#006aff`. That hex is the focus ring and `--bds-primary-500`. The button fill is a radial gradient from `#005bcc` to `#008cff`.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json.
- `icon.png` is the 46 px favicon scaled to 128 px.
- Hover was read. Pressed was not held, so pressed is Not captured.
- No dark theme was found. Do not invent one.

## Fonts

`rippleFont` is the display and body face (weights 350 and 500 on the homepage). `rippleBdsFont` is the UI face on nav triggers and Contact Sales. Both are served by ripple.com as woff2. They are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **Keep the page white and the type near-black.** The header is `#ffffff` and the display is `#141a1f`. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Paint the primary action as a radial blue pill.** The fill runs from `#005bcc` to `#008cff`, with light text. [hero-cta-default.png](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png)
- **Keep secondary actions as quiet pills.** Contact Sales is a gray fill with a blue gradient border. [nav-cta-default.png](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png)
- **Drop the footer to the same ink as the display.** `#141a1f` background, `#fafafa` text. [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png)
- **Don't style the payments app from this package.** Customer Login stops at Auth0. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md)
- **Don't fill `#006aff` into the primary button.** That value is the focus ring.
