### Principles

1. One blue for the action.
2. Headlines are tight.
3. Pills for controls, small radii for content.
4. Dark bands, light page.
5. Stay on the public site.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on https://ripple.com/ in the T3 preview tab (innerWidth 1440, innerHeight 900, devicePixelRatio 2, visualViewport scale 1). Component sizes, colours, radii and shadows were read with `getComputedStyle` on the same live page and on the public routes listed below. Stylesheet rules (type classes, `.button-dark`, `.bds-focus-ring:focus-visible`, max-widths) were read from the CSSOM. Channel triples such as `--bds-primary-500: 0 106 255` are stored as `#006aff`.

Screenshots were taken in headless Chromium after the public pages returned HTTP 200 with no Cloudflare challenge. The T3 preview tab loaded the same pages but did not produce screenshot frames. The Osano cookie banner was hidden in the DOM for screenshots. It was not accepted or denied.

## What to distrust

The primary button's computed `background-color` is transparent. Its blue comes from animated circle layers. The token `light.component.button-primary.background` is `--color-blue-60`, the declared stop, not a flat fill.

`--breakpoint-xs`, `--breakpoint-md` and `--breakpoint-xl` are unresolved Sass strings (`$xs-breakpoint`). They are listed under notTokenised, not used as lengths.

`core.motion.easing.ease` expands the CSS keyword `ease` to `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`. The sheet declared the keyword. The control points are Derived.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.

The search field's computed font-weight was 350 while its class includes `font-normal`. The measured weight is the one in the notes.

## Fonts

`rippleFont` and `rippleBdsFont` are TT Ripple, shipped as woff2 (Book 350, Regular 400 and 500, Medium 500, Bold 700). The fallback faces are local Arial. The BDS stack also names San Francisco, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica and Arial. The files are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide falls back to Avenir Next and Segoe UI.

## Do and don't

- **One blue for the action.** The primary button and the focus ring use the blue ramp (`#006aff`, `#005bcc`). Secondary actions are black pills or text with an arrow. See [button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Headlines are tight.** Display type uses weight 500 and negative letter-spacing, down to -1px. Body copy uses weight 350 (Book). See [type-display.png](components/crops/type-display.png).
- **Pills for controls, small radii for content.** Buttons and icon buttons are fully round. Search fields are 4px. FAQ rows and insight images are 8px. Mega-menu cards are 12px. See [text-field-search-focus.png](components/crops/text-field-search-focus.png).
- **Dark bands, light page.** The page and the hero are white. The footer and the closing band are `#141a1f`. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Stay on the public site.** Account, custody and payment flows were not opened. See [contact-sales-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-sales-light-default.png).
