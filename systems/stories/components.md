# Components

What was on the signed-out shop on 2026-11-22. States that were not measured are listed as gaps.

## Wordmark

SVG sprite symbol `logo_v2_na`, viewBox `0 0 912.22 350.1`, fill `currentColor`. Rendered 100 by 38. Desktop header paints it black. Phone homepage paints it white over the photograph. [icons/mark.svg](icons/mark.svg). [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Promo strip

`#f3f3f3`, 34px desktop, 52px at 390. Quadrant 12px, black. The strip is one Read more button. Opening it starts the newsletter form. That form was closed. Not submitted.

## Header icon button

Shop, search, account, favourites, and bag are 72 by 72 hit areas with a 16px icon. Account is absent at 390. None of account, favourites, or bag was opened.

## Shop drawer link

Quadrant, black, except Last chance at `#cf0b00`, 14px. The gift shop carries a New badge in Quadrant 14px, black. Clothing, Shoes, Bags, Accessories, and Trending have a chevron. Trending was not expanded. [screenshots/shop-menu-light-1440.png](screenshots/shop-menu-light-1440.png).

## Hero title

QuadrantMono, 34px, white, uppercase, line-height 37.4px. "NEW IN".

## Shop now

SansStories, 12px, uppercase, tracking 0.96px, height 40, padding 0 18px, radius 2px, white fill, black type, 1px white border. Default only. It links to `/en-ww/newin/`. It does not add to the bag.

## Product card

Name and price in Quadrant 12px, black. Image well `#f2f2f2`. Example on the homepage: Oversized Wool Coat, $459.

## Product title and price

Quadrant 14px, black. Velvet Lace Dress, $159. Colour name "Dark Brown" sits above two image swatches. The selected swatch frame was not isolated. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Size chip

48 by 40, Quadrant 10px. Resting state only. Size 42 had an orange low-stock dot. Size 46 had a bell. Neither was clicked.

## Choose size

Disabled until a size is picked. Background `#949494`, type `#d9d9d9`, SansStories 12px uppercase, tracking 0.96px, 382 by 40, padding 0 26px, radius 2px. Not clicked.

## Footer heading

QuadrantMono 16px, `#949494`, uppercase. Links under it are Quadrant 12px, black. Sign up in the footer is a subscribe wall and was not clicked. [screenshots/about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Gaps

Hover, pressed, loading, error, selected size, filled search, and a dark theme setting were not captured. Focus was observed once as a 2px `#757575` outline and was not photographed as its own state.
