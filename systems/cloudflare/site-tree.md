# Cloudflare public site tree

Mapped from https://www.cloudflare.com/ on 2026-11-22, signed out, before the public screenshot pass. Each row is a control a visitor can reach, and what it opens. Login, signup, identity providers, and lead forms stop at the wall. Nothing in this map was submitted.

The marketing site is one public surface. The dashboard at dash.cloudflare.com is a separate signed-in product. This pass photographs the public site and the empty login and signup walls. It does not enter the dashboard.

| Control | Opens |
|---|---|

| Under attack? (nav, when shown) | https://www.cloudflare.com/under-attack-hotline/ |
