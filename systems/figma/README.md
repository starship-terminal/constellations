# Figma design reference

A token set and component reference for Figma's public surfaces — for building design-tool marketing pages, product landing pages, pricing grids, community index pages and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **figma.com** on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /design/, /figjam/, /slides/, /dev-mode/, /make/, /pricing/, /community, /community/plugins, /blog/, /customers/, /enterprise/, /careers/, /contact/, /downloads/, /design-systems/, /release-notes/, /login and /signup, plus the header's Products and Solutions dropdowns, and 390px passes on home, pricing, community and contact.

Figma is worth a capture because it ships a complete token layer to the browser: the marketing Next.js + Emotion app declares `--fig-theme-*` semantic roles (bg, fg, fg-muted, border, btn-primary/secondary/transparent/ghost, card, badge, form-input) redeclared under `:root[data-theme="dark"]`, a `--fig-typography-*` variable-font ramp (12px formLabel through 88px display1, with unitless line-height steps, negative `-0.078125rem` display tracking and `font-variation-settings` axes for wght 400/600/700, wdth 96/98/100 and slnt -6), a `--fig-space-*` scale on a 4px base out to 120px, `--fig-radius-*` steps 2–28px plus the 9999px full pill, `--fig-grid-*` (12 columns, 16px gutter, 40px margin, 1520px max), the `--f-*` page-frame grid (48 columns over a 1440px max width), `--fig-elevation-*` shadows, `--fig-section-*` block padding and `--fig-animation-*` durations. Two self-hosted variable voices: **figmaSans** and **figmaMono**. The dark roles are declared but nothing public painted them while captured — every public page resolves the light `:root`. **Boundary:** the editor and every post-login surface was never reached — the login, signup, contact and Make prompt forms were measured as forms, never submitted; every link and CTA was measured, never followed; the consent layer was hidden, never answered.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export figma` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layer carry the design.
2. Do spend ink as alpha, not grey.
3. Do keep the CTA a black slab.
4. Do pair the two voices.
5. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
6. Don't treat the dark set as photographed.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **The signed-in product.** The editor, files, boards, Make projects, profiles and admin surfaces need an account this capture does not have. /community, /login and /signup are the product app shell — a different stack that does not share the marketing `--fig-*` layer; its measured values sit under `component.productShell.*`, honestly labelled.
- **The dark set.** `:root[data-theme="dark"]` declarations were resolved in a probe document; no public page carried that attribute while captured. The `dark` set is declared-not-painted evidence, not a photographed dark surface.
- **Animated surfaces.** Heroes and product demos hold live canvas/video content; a screenshot freezes one frame. Sparse-looking panels are a capture artifact, not the design.
- **The forms.** Contact, login, signup and Make prompt fields were typed into then cleared — validation, error, loading and success states are unverified because nothing was submitted. `/contact`'s `name=topic` input is a display:none honeypot; the measured `field-topic` is the visible unnamed text input beside it.
- **Detail pages.** The community, blog, customers and careers indexes were captured; individual community files, posts, case studies and job ads were not opened (careers links out to an external ATS — out of scope).
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw values, never the pixels.
- **Unpainted fills are not tokens.** The product shell's Google button and community search compute a transparent `backgroundColor`, and the plan card's reading matched only the transparent `--fig-theme-btn-ghost-bg`; `core.component.productShell.google-btn-fill`, `comm-search-bg` and `light.surface.planCardBg` were dropped in the 2026-11 audit. The declared transparent theme variables stay: they are what the sheet declares.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layer carry the design.** Figma ships its tokens: use the `theme.*` roles and the `core.font`/`core.spacing`/`core.radius` scales rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do spend ink as alpha, not grey.** Hierarchy is translucent black: muted text at 0.54, hairlines at 0.16, hover deepening to 0.85 — on white. On the dark declaration the same ladder runs in white alphas. See [nav-pricing](components/crops/nav-pricing-default.png).
3. **Do keep the CTA a black slab.** The primary action is a 46px-high black button at `radius.cta` 8px, white ink, hover softening to 85% black — never a pill, never a brand colour. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png).
4. **Do pair the two voices.** figmaSans carries everything from the 88px display1 to 12px labels on one variable face; figmaMono owns code and metric surfaces. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** The editor and every post-login surface was never reached; `component.productShell.*` marks the app-shell measurements that do not share the marketing `--fig-*` layer. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
6. **Don't treat the dark set as photographed.** Every `dark.*` token resolves a declaration the site ships under `:root[data-theme="dark"]` but no public page painted; the only black-painted surface on the light pages is the footer's field. See [footer](components/crops/footer-default.png).
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** Contact, login, signup and the Make prompt were typed into then cleared — validation, error and success states are unverified. See [field-email](components/crops/field-email-filled.png).
8. **Don't copy the brand.** The Figma mark, product glyphs and the demo imagery in captures are Figma's published artwork; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).
