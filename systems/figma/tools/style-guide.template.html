Figma, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the site's declared --fig-typography-* ramp (formLabel 12px to display1 88px, with the unitless --fig-typography-lh-* steps, the negative --fig-typography-ls-* tracking and the --fig-typography-variation-* font axes), the --fig-space-* 4–120px scale, the --fig-radius-* 2–28px radii and 9999px full pill, the 12-column --fig-grid-* and 48-column --f-* marketing frame, the two --fig-elevation-* shadow steps, the 180/250/400ms --fig-animation-* durations, and the --fig-theme-* semantic roles that re-resolve under :root[data-theme="dark"]. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The semantic --fig-theme-* roles are the palette: white bg on black fg, the 54%-alpha fg-muted, the 4%-alpha card-bg and 8%-alpha form-input-bg/badge-bg washes, the 16%/40% border steps, the black/white btn-primary pair and the #972121 form-error — each re-resolving under the dark scope. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius and motion

The space scale runs 4–120px; radii run 2–28px against the 9999px full pill (CTAs, the billing toggle, badges). The marketing grid is 12 columns on 16px gutters and 40px margins inside 1520px; the 80px section block padding separates page bands. Durations are 180/250/400ms.

Shadows

Type

Two served faces: figmaSans carries every heading, control and body — display1 runs 88px at lh-tight 1.1 with the -0.078125rem tracking — and figmaMono draws code and numerals. The variation axes (wght 400/600/700, wdth 96/98/100, slnt -6, mono) ride the variable face. Sizes are the declared --fig-typography-size-* steps; samples fall back to system sans.
