### Principles

1. Set display type in Crimson Pro at weight 300.
2. Use the quiet gray for the marketing action.
3. Use ink, not a brand blue, for the paid-plan action.
4. Keep body copy in Inter at 14px.
5. Leave prices out of the system.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` in the T3 preview browser on 2026-11-19, on `https://www.workflow.design/` at 1169 by 731, device pixel ratio 2. Eighty-four properties are declared once on `:root`. They are in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Control metrics are computed styles in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

Stylesheets were same-origin, so `cssRules` could be read. Nothing was sampled from a screenshot. The wordmark fill `#0f0f0e` is the fill in the SVG the nav loads.

## What to distrust

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Home, pricing and features screenshots were taken later in headless Playwright on the same public URLs, which had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. They are full colour. Read colours from the tokens.

The features heading computed at `60.992px` at 1169px. That value is kept. The home heading at the same width is 32px. Dark mode was not present. The OS preferred dark in the preview tab and the page stayed light.

`a.nav--menu.w-button` computed Webflow blue, `rgb(56, 152, 236)`, at height 0. That is the hidden menu button, not a brand colour. Prices on `/pricing` are not tokens.

## Fonts

Crimson Pro and Inter are loaded from Webflow's CDN as variable fonts. The tokens name them and fall back to Times New Roman, Arial and sans-serif. They are not shipped in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Set display type in Crimson Pro at weight 300.** Headings on the home and pricing pages are 32px with a 32px line. See [screenshots/home-light-desktop.png](screenshots/home-light-desktop.png).
- **Use the quiet gray for the marketing action.** Try now and Get started are `#f6f6f6` with ink text and an 8px radius. See [components/crops/button-secondary-default.png](components/crops/button-secondary-default.png).
- **Use ink, not a brand blue, for the paid-plan action.** Get started for free on pricing is `#1a1a1a` with `#f6f6f6` text.
- **Keep body copy in Inter at 14px.** The line height is 21px. Muted text is `#1a1a1a` at about 65% opacity.
- **Leave prices out of the system.** The yearly figure on the pricing page is regional.
