## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from `https://cdn.prose.com/build/client/assets/root-LQqObtzz.css` (`:root, :host`) and confirmed with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on https://prose.com/. Component values were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements listed in [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md). The promo dialog was closed. No form was submitted.

## What to distrust

There are no screenshots. The preview snapshot failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Do not treat the style guide as a picture of the site.

The focused white "GET YOUR FORMULA" showed a browser outline of `rgb(229, 151, 0)`, which is not a Prose custom property. Prose's own focus utilities (`focus-visible:outline-primary-300`) were not the computed result on that control.

The footer "Blog" link computed as `rgb(0, 0, 238)`. Neighbouring footer links computed as white. The blue is what the browser returned.

`--font-weight-bold` is declared as 600. The Simplon Norm stylesheet also ships a 700 face. Headings use Saol at 300.

Hover backgrounds are declared (`any-hover:bg-primary-100` on the filled pill) and were not computed, because hover was not applied.

## Fonts

Saol (heading), Simplon Norm (body), and Simplon Mono (labels) are served from cdn.prose.com as woff2. They are proprietary. Tokens name them and fall back to Times, Helvetica Neue, and Courier New. Those fallbacks are what [style-guide.html](style-guide.html) can render.

## Do and don't

**Use the olive ink and the cream wash.** Page text is #323429, headings #4d523c, cream surfaces #f9f7f2, footer #161716.

**Set display type in a light serif and labels in a tracked mono.** Saol 300 at tracking -0.03em for headings. Simplon Mono, uppercase, tracking 0.08em, for pills and eyebrows.

**Make the action a 48px pill.** Radius 60px, padding 15px 32px, fill #323429 or the inverse white pill on a dark hero. The promo fill is #f69371.

**Keep fields square.** The promo email is radius 0 with a 1px #eaeaea border. The sign-in email is an underline, 2px #323429.

**Do not invent a dark theme.** The stylesheet has none.

**Do not ship the wordmark or the social marks.** They are in [icons/](icons/) as evidence.
