# Spyglass design reference

A token set and component reference for Spyglass's public marketing site and its signed-out sign-in page. Read from **spyglass.so** on **2026-11-19**, signed out, at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 (both at 2x). The live theme on every public page is `html.light`. Captured: `/`, `/features`, `/pricing`, `/faq`, `/about`, `/compare`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, and a missing route that returns the framework 404. Local UI only: the Product menu, the phone menu, one FAQ row, and focus on the email field. Nothing was submitted.

Spyglass paints a white marketing page in Geist, with Familjen Grotesk for the display headlines and a near-black primary button (`#171717`, 36px, 6px radius). The sign-in page is email plus Google. Continue stays disabled until an email is present. The stylesheet also declares a `.dark` shadcn set and a `[data-spyglass-theme]` product set (teal in light, lime `#d0fe1d` in dark). Those selectors are not mounted on the public pages, so their tokens are declarations, not computed styles of a visible screen.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export spyglass` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Paint the page white and the action near-black.
2. Keep secondary actions outlined.
3. Set display type in Familjen Grotesk.
4. Open menus as a frosted panel.
5. Leave sign-in at the email step.
6. Do not invent the lime app theme on the marketing page.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the live home stylesheet (`https://spyglass.so/_next/static/chunks/daecd961b511cdd8.css` and `4a0b564f14bbd1c7.css`) and from `getComputedStyle` on the attached page. Hex declarations were kept. The same rules also emit `lab()` duplicates. Component sizes, colours, radii and shadows were computed on the live elements at 1440 x 900, and the phone header was computed at 390 x 844. Hover was a real pointer hover. Focus was a real focus on the email field and the header links. The email field was not filled and no form was submitted.

The T3 preview browser loaded `https://spyglass.so/` and returned computed styles, then failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Screenshots were taken in headless Chromium against the same public pages, which loaded with no Cloudflare challenge. Sign-in was not driven in a new headless session beyond a read of the already-public form.

## What to distrust

- **`.dark` and `[data-spyglass-theme]` are declarations.** The public pages set `class="light"` on `html` and do not set `data-spyglass-theme`. Do not treat `dark.color` or `light.spyglass` as a screen that was open.
- **Keyboard focus did not paint a ring** on the header links (`outline-style: none`). Do not invent a focus ring.
- **Pressed, loading, error, checkbox, radio, switch, select, textarea, toast, dialog, tooltip, avatar and table states** were not on these pages.
- **The sign-in carousel quotes name people.** Those names are on the public page. This package does not transcribe them into tokens.
- **Fonts are not shipped.** Geist and Familjen Grotesk load from the site. The style guide falls back if those families are absent.
- **The 404 is the framework's plain page**, Times on white, not the marketing shell.
- **Plan CTAs were not followed.** They go to `/sign-up?planTier=`. No card was entered.

## Fonts

- **Geist** is the UI face, a variable woff2 (weight 100 to 900) with a size-adjusted Arial fallback named Geist Fallback.
- **Familjen Grotesk** is the display face on h1 elements, with its own fallback. `--font-familjen-tracking` is `-0.03em`.
- **Geist Mono** is declared and not used for body text on these pages.
- **Playfair Display** is linked from Google Fonts. It was not the computed face of the measured headings.

## Do and don't

1. **Paint the page white and the action near-black.** The primary control is `#171717` with `#fafafa` text, 36px tall, 6px radius. [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
2. **Keep secondary actions outlined.** Sign in and Explore the product are white with a 1px `#e5e5e5` border. Hover fills `#f5f5f5`. [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
3. **Set display type in Familjen Grotesk.** The home h1 is 70px, weight 500, line-height 70px, tracking -2.1px, colour `#212121`. Body stays Geist 16/24.
4. **Open menus as a frosted panel.** The Product menu is 380px wide, radius 24px, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`, shadow `0 24px 60px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.18)`. [menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png)
5. **Leave sign-in at the email step.** Google is a 44px outline button. Continue is the primary button at opacity 0.5 until an email is present. Do not submit it from this reference. [sign-in](screenshots/sign-in-light-default.png)
6. **Do not invent the lime app theme on the marketing page.** `#d0fe1d` and the teal `#69a097` live on `[data-spyglass-theme]`, which these pages do not set.
