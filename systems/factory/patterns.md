# Factory patterns

Public patterns only. The signed-in app was not entered.

## Header

A fixed light bar: logo, Product, Enterprise, Pricing, News, Company, Docs, a locale globe, Sign in, Contact sales, Download. Menus open under Product, Enterprise, and Company and do not change the URL. At 390 the bar collapses to logo, globe, and Open menu. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero

Uppercase Geist headline, a short sentence, a split primary download button, an outlined Book demo, and a CLI install line with Copy. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Pricing

Five plan columns. Pro has a Pro / Plus / Max segment. Paid CTAs leave the site for the auth wall and were not followed past the empty fields. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Docs shell

A left sidebar of expandable groups, a top bar with search and a light/dark/system switch, and path cards. Dark is a real painted theme. See [docs-dark-default.png](screenshots/docs-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

Sign in leaves factory.com for auth.factory.ai: email, Continue with email, Continue with Google, Sign up. The email field was left empty. See [login-dark-empty.png](screenshots/login-dark-empty.png). The older factory.ai/login URL is a Vercel wall and was not submitted. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
