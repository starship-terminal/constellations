The scoped dark sections

factory.com never paints a whole dark page: <html data-theme="light"> ships on every route and dark lives inside [data-dark-hero] heroes, .invert-color-scheme panels and the footer — near-black fields with light ink and the orange accent. docs.factory.com's theme selector flips a whole page to this same dark set; the auth.factory.ai sign-in is a separate dark surface outside this token layer.

Palette

The declared --neutral-* warm ramp runs #1f1d1c (1000) to #d6d3d2 (100) beside the orange --accent-100..300 steps and the Tailwind --color-<hue>-* scales; the semantic --color-base-100..1000/--color-background/--color-foreground/--color-surface-raised/--color-*-base-* roles invert between the light and dark scopes. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius and motion

Radii are small — 2/3/4/6/8/10/12px for pills, fields, cards and panels. Controls transition over 150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1); the layout is a 1440px content column with a 36px side gutter at the desktop breakpoint (declared steps 1rem/1.5rem/2.25rem/5rem).

Type

Two served variable faces: Geist (wght 100–900) carries every heading, control and body — the hero masthead runs 60.48px at weight 500, body 16px — and Geist Mono draws code, eyebrows, labels and the install command; Geist Pixel Square is the pixel label face. Sizes are the declared --text-* steps; samples fall back to system sans.
