# Tilda

Tilda is a website builder (tilda.cc). This package covers the public marketing site and the signed-out login page, read on 19 November 2026. The marketing surface is light. No dark theme was on screen. Widths are 1440 and 390. The login form was not submitted.

### Principles

1. Primary actions are the coral rectangle.
2. Secondary actions are a 1px black box.
3. Headings stay at weight 300.
4. Nav type is 13px, weight 700, uppercase.
5. Plan cards are a 3px black frame.
6. The sign-in page is a coral gradient, not the marketing white.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on tilda.cc, tilda.cc/pricing and tilda.cc/login. The first reading was at 1169 by 731. After a freeform resize the pages were 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2. Component boxes were `getComputedStyle` on the live elements.

Cross-origin sheets on static.tildacdn.net were blocked from the CSSOM. They were fetched with curl and the declarations that match a live element are in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The sign-in sheet is `t-sign.min.css?ver=v26092505`.

The cookie banner was dismissed with Reject all before the screenshots. The login form was focused and not typed into, and not submitted. Google, phone, card and company SSO were not present.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on both snapshot attempts. Screenshots of tilda.cc and tilda.cc/pricing were taken with headless Playwright after those pages had already loaded in the preview browser without a Cloudflare challenge. The login host was not opened in Playwright. Shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at 2x, then quantized to 256 colours.

## What to distrust

html and body compute to a transparent background. The painted page is white. `light.background.page` is that paint.

The sign-in field underline computed to solid white. The stylesheet declares white at 30% at rest and solid white on focus and hover. The live read is what the tokens store.

`core.color.sign-button-hover` and `core.color.sign-placeholder` are declared and were not forced on screen. Their descriptions say so.

The 256-colour screenshots band the illustration. Read colour from the tokens.

TildaSans is not in this package. The style guide falls back to Arial.

The sign-in page has no screenshot. Its values are computed styles only.

## Fonts

TildaSans is the marketing and sign-in face, a variable font from weight 250 to 1000, loaded as woff2 from static.tildacdn.com. It is proprietary and is not committed. The computed fallback is Arial, then sans-serif.

html and body compute to Times until a TildaSans rule applies. That is the grid reset, not a designed face.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are the coral rectangle.** The hero Create button is `#FA8669` with white type and a square corner. [components/crops/button-accent-default.png](components/crops/button-accent-default.png).
- **Secondary actions are a 1px black box.** Overview and Sign up share that border and a transparent fill. [components/crops/button-ghost-default.png](components/crops/button-ghost-default.png).
- **Headings stay at weight 300.** The hero is 46px, the pricing title is 60px, section titles are 48px and 78px. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Nav type is 13px, weight 700, uppercase.** Letter-spacing on the home Pricing link is 0.5px. [components/crops/nav-links-default.png](components/crops/nav-links-default.png).
- **Plan cards are a 3px black frame.** Radius stays 0. The plan button inside is a 30px pill on `#ECECEC`. [components/crops/card-plan-default.png](components/crops/card-plan-default.png).
- **The sign-in page is a coral gradient, not the marketing white.** The button there is peach and fully round. Do not reuse the marketing coral rectangle on that screen.
