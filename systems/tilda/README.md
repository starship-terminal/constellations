# Tilda design reference

A token set and component reference for Tilda's public English site, read from [https://tilda.cc/](https://tilda.cc/) on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. Captured pages: home, pricing, features, templates, made-on-Tilda, FAQ, color library, AI landing page, Tilda Sans, terms, and the login and registration walls. The walls were photographed and not filled.

Tilda is a no-code website builder. The public marketing shell is a Tilda block page in TildaSans. The primary marketing button is coral `#fa8669` with white type. Auth screens use a cream pill `#ffe8c7`. There is no dark theme switch on the public site.

Boundary: the editor, projects, and account settings sit behind [https://tilda.cc/login/](https://tilda.cc/login/). That URL showed an email field and a password field. The password was not submitted. There is no Google button. Registration at [https://tilda.cc/registration/](https://tilda.cc/registration/) was not filled.

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium via Playwright, signed out. [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) read stylesheets, including cross-origin sheets from static.tildacdn.net. The pages declare `--t-headline-font`, `--t-text-font`, and button `--height` steps. They do not declare a colour token layer. Colours in [tokens.json](tokens.json) are computed styles from [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

The cookie banner was hidden with CSS. Accept, Reject, and Cookie settings were not clicked. No form was submitted. The FAQ search field received the word "domain", then it was cleared.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.
- Header and menu backgrounds computed as transparent. The menu shadow was read. A solid header fill was not.
- Hover and pressed on the coral CTA and the cream auth button did not change the computed background.
- Full pages taller than 24000 CSS pixels were truncated. The capture log names features, templates, colors, and terms on phone, and the desktop terms page was stitched from tiles.
- Login and registration are walls, not the product editor.

## Fonts

TildaSans is the declared family (`--t-headline-font` and `--t-text-font`), with Arial then sans-serif. Weights 300, 400, 600, and 700 were measured. The face is served from Tilda's CDN and is not in this package. The FAQ search also named `tfutura` ahead of Arial in one computed stack.

## Do and don't

1. **Do set marketing type in TildaSans.** Headlines are weight 300. Nav labels are 13px and weight 700. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do use coral for the primary marketing button.** `#fa8669` fill, white 15px type, 50px height, square corners. See [hero-cta-default](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
3. **Do use a cream pill on the auth screens.** `#ffe8c7` fill, black 18px type, 100px radius. See [login-submit-default](components/crops/login-submit-default.png).
4. **Don't treat the login page as the editor.** The editor is behind the wall and was not captured.
5. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public English pages did not offer one.
