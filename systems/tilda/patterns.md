## Hero

Two columns at 1440. The left half is `#F0F0F0` with an illustration. The right half is white, with a 46px light heading, a 16px description, an outline button and a coral button. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Pricing

A 60px light heading, a two-tab billing switch, then three cards with a 3px black border. Prices on screen were Free, $10/month and $20/month for annual billing. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Sign in

A full-viewport gradient from `#EF6B50` to `#F8AD70`, a frosted card (white at 10%, radius 8px) and a peach pill button. Email and password only. Not submitted, and not screenshotted: the preview browser was not producing frames, and a headless session was not opened against the login page.

## Footer

`#111111` with white links, then a `#171717` bar. Measured on the pricing page. Below the fold of the viewport shots.
