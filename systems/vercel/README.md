# Vercel

Vercel (vercel.com) is a frontend cloud. This package is a refresh of the public marketing site, captured on 2026-11-21 from current origin/main, signed out, in headless Playwright. Desktop is 1440 by 900 at devicePixelRatio 1. Phone is 390 by 844 at devicePixelRatio 2.

The site paints light by default. The footer has system, light, and dark labels. Clicking dark adds `dark-theme` and paints the page black. Login and signup stay light. They were photographed empty. Nothing was typed. Google, GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, ChatGPT, Apple, SAML SSO, and Passkey were not clicked. No form was submitted. `login-extracted` stays not-started.

An older signed-in dashboard walk was removed from this package. Those frames named an account. They are not in this refresh.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages and read `getComputedStyle` on `html`, `body`, the homepage `h1`, the header Sign Up and Log In anchors, the login email input, and the login buttons. Custom properties were read from the CSSOM on the homepage. Dark values were read after clicking the footer `dark` label, which set `html` class `dark-theme`. Screenshots were quantised to 256 colours after the values were read. Read colours from tokens.json, not from the PNGs.

## What to distrust

The email input's own radius is 0. The 8px radius is on the login buttons, and `--geist-marketing-radius` is 8px. The wrap around the email field was not measured on this pass. Dark `--ds-gray-400` (`#2e2e2e`) is carried from the 2026-11-19 reading. It was not recomputed on 2026-11-21. Focus on the email field did not change its computed colour. The focus token is the link colour, not a measured ring. `--geist-success` is `#0070f3` in both themes. The primary button is not that blue. It is near-black in light and near-white in dark. Signup "Show other options" did not reveal a new provider. It kept Google, GitHub, ChatGPT, and Apple, and added a Continue with Email link that was not followed. The style guide still renders the earlier dark-only specimen. Light roles are in tokens.json.

## Fonts

GeistSans is the UI face, computed as `GeistSans, "GeistSans Fallback"`. Geist Mono is the code face, from `--font-mono` and from the docs `pre`. Font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the near-black page in dark and the off-white page in light.** Dark body background is `#000000`. Light body background is `#fafafa`. [screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png) and [screenshots/home-light-desktop.png](screenshots/home-light-desktop.png).
- **Fill the primary action, ring the secondary.** Sign Up is the solid button. Log In is the quiet one. [components.md](components.md).
- **Keep login empty.** The email field stays blank. Provider buttons are labels, not paths to follow. [screenshots/login-light-desktop.png](screenshots/login-light-desktop.png).
- **Open Products and Resources as one dialog.** Both sets of links sit in the Navigation menu. [screenshots/products-menu-light-desktop.png](screenshots/products-menu-light-desktop.png).
- **Use GeistSans for UI and Geist Mono for code.** The homepage `h1` is 64px weight 400. [tokens.json](tokens.json).
