Palette

The declared layer carries the full Discord hue scales per theme under color.* (neutral, blurple, plum, the *-new families, illo-*, opacity-*); the class-scoped semantic names (--background-base-*, --text-*, --border-*, --button-*) resolve per theme in the light and dark sets — the marketing light and the app .theme-darker both paint signed out. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing and radius

Controls are 12px (pills, CTAs, search), cards and auth 8px, chips 10px, the marketing nav pill 16px and the Nitro card a 96px slab. Hairlines are rgba(151,151,159,0.12) on cards and rgba(255,255,255,0.08) on the auth submit; focus rings are 2px #6aa8f4 (app) or #5865f2 (marketing).
