# Make patterns

Public pages only. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

| Pattern | Where |
|---|---|
| App shell and navigation | Sticky announcement bar, logo, four mega menus, Pricing, Enterprise, Talk to sales, Sign in, Get started free. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-product-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png), [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png) |
| Marketing hero | Centered headline, primary and secondary buttons, two reassurance checks, a product demo still. The demo is a public marketing animation, not the signed-in app. |
| Plan grid | Credit slider, monthly and annual toggle, five plan columns. [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) |
| Search and filtering | Integrations search, category chips, verified and community switches. [integrations-light-search.png](screenshots/integrations-light-search.png) |
| FAQ accordion | One answer open. [home-light-faq.png](screenshots/home-light-faq.png) |
| Error | Styled 404. [not-found-light-default.png](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png) |
| Auth wall | Login asks for email and password, or Google or SSO. [login-light-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-wall.png). Branch stopped. |
| Form | Talk to sales and contact pages show lead forms. They were photographed and not filled or submitted. [talk-to-sales-light-default.png](screenshots/talk-to-sales-light-default.png) |

Not present on the public surface: destructive confirmation, toast feedback after an action, empty app state, loading state of the product, onboarding.
