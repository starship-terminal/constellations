# Make patterns

Public pages only, 2026-11-18. Desktop 1440×900, phone 390×844.

## Marketing frame

Home, pricing, product and templates share a white page, a purple wordmark, and a bar with Product, Solutions and Resources menus. Get started free is the filled action. Sign in is a quiet outline. A Waves '26 announcement strip sits above the bar. OneTrust was hidden and never clicked.

![Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

The Product menu is a three-column panel: platform, what's new, and an announcement card. Opening it does not navigate.

![Product menu](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png)

On a 390-wide phone the same links stack in a full-height menu. Get started free is a full-width purple button.

![Phone menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

## Pricing

Plans that grow with you. A credit slider reads 10,000 credits/mo at the captured position. Monthly billing and Annual billing sit in a segmented control. Free, Core, Pro, Teams and Enterprise are five columns. Pro is marked Popular. Annual shows Save 15%. Prices on the captured annual view: $0, $9, $16, $29, and Custom. Get started links to register. Talk to sales is the Enterprise action. Neither was followed.

![Pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Templates

Templates load signed out. A search field and category chips (AI, App Families, Built-in Apps) filter the library. Typing "gmail" into search was captured and cleared. The search was not submitted as a form post. Opening a template into the editor was not done: that path asks for sign-in.

![Templates](screenshots/templates-light-default.png)

## Sign-in wall

`/login` paints the sign-in form without an account. Email and password fields, a Cloudflare "Verify you are human" widget, a magenta-to-purple Sign in button, Continue with Google, and Continue with SSO. Nothing was submitted. The CAPTCHA was not passed. Google and SSO were not pressed.

![Sign in](screenshots/login-light-default.png)

`/register` is the same wall with a sign-up form and a CAPTCHA. It was not submitted.

## What this does not cover

Scenarios, the visual editor, Make Grid, billing, and account settings sit behind sign-in. The hero on home is a public demo of Maia, not the signed-in product.
