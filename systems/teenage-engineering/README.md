# Teenage Engineering design reference

Public marketing capture of [teenage.engineering](https://teenage.engineering/), measured signed out on 2026-11-19. Pages read: home, products, one product story (`/products/ep-35`), the store grid, a store category (`/store/pocket-operators`), newsletter, and a 404. Desktop viewport was 1440 by 900 at 1x. The product stage is black. The store and the newsletter are white. Type is te-20 at weight 300, with TechnoType on the home price. The primary action is a square near-black button. The discount mark is a black pill. Desktop fields have no radius.

The T3 preview browser opened the homepage and returned `The browser is not producing screenshot frames` twice. The tab had already loaded (title teenage engineering, no Cloudflare challenge). Values and screenshots below come from a headless Playwright pass on the same public pages.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://teenage.engineering/` in a new tab. Snapshot failed twice with `The browser is not producing screenshot frames`. The page title was already `teenage engineering` and the HTML had no Cloudflare challenge, so the same public URLs were read in headless Playwright.

Custom properties were read from `:root` (152 names) and confirmed against `root.CyKTWa8o.css`, `button.B8x4Racb.css`, and `form.DWmR17wv.css`, fetched from the same URLs the page linked. Component colours come from `getComputedStyle` on the live nodes: subscribe, email, checkbox, discount badge, search field, prices, and the phone menu.

A viewport-relative length (`calc(0.0102041 * 100vw)`) is stored as the pixel size computed at 1440. Phone shots confirm the under-768 rules (larger type ratio, 7.959px control radius).

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Photographs band. Read colour from [tokens.json](tokens.json), not from the image.

There is no dark token set. `color-scheme` was `normal`. The black product stage is the page background for home and product stories, not a theme switch.

`--te-grey-400` is named in the field focus rule and is not declared in root.css. Focused email border computed as 1px solid `#000000`.

`--te-blue`, `--te-red`, and the error red `#c0262c` are in the stylesheet. Blue and red were not the colour of a control that was open. Green and orange were painted on the 404 header icons. Yellow was painted on the 404 illustration.

The phone join button computed a `#b2b2b2` face. button.css maps disabled to that grey. The button was not marked disabled in the DOM. Treat the grey as what was painted, not as a confirmed disabled state.

Spacing, type, and header height scale with the viewport. The pixel tokens are the 1440 reading. A 390 layout uses the under-768 ratios in root.css, about twice the desktop ratio.

`--theme-bc` computed to the string `null` on the product stage. The header background there is transparent over black. Do not use `null` as a colour.

## Fonts

te-20 (weights 100 and 300) and te-40 (weights 100 and 300) load as woff2 from `/assets/TE20T`, `TE20L`, `TE40T`, and `TE40L`. TechnoType (400, 500, 700) loads from the same origin. Unicode is a local CJK stack (Hiragino, Meiryo, MS PGothic). The tokens fall back to sans-serif, which is what the `@font-face` stack names. The font files are the product's and are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Black square, white page.** The subscribe control is `#0f0e12` with a `#f5f5f5` label, 73px tall, radius 0, on a white page. See [newsletter-light-default.png](screenshots/newsletter-light-default.png).
- **The product stage is black.** Home and product stories use `#000000` with `#f1f2f2` chrome. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **One pill.** The discount mark is a black pill, radius 9999px, white label. Prices that are reduced keep a `#767676` strikethrough. See [store-light-default.png](screenshots/store-light-default.png).
- **te-20 for chrome, TechnoType for the price.** Store titles and the subscribe label use te-20 at weight 300. The home price uses TechnoType at weight 700.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** The public site did not ship a theme switch in this capture.
- **Do not submit the newsletter or open checkout.** The email field was focused and left empty. `/store/checkout` was not opened.
