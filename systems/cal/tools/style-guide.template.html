Cal.com, measured

The chrome

The same dark fill draws the hero's sign-up buttons; a 1px-white-gap + 3px-grey ring is the keyboard focus every control draws. A near-black footer closes every marketing page.

Palette

The declared --color-hp-* layer carries the marketing neutrals and accent; the declared --cal-* layer carries the booker/app semantics in both themes; the measured color.paint.* readings carry the near-black paints the variables do not name.

Spacing and radius

Controls sit on 10px radii, CTAs on 12px, hero buttons and cards on 14px, the glass bar and menu cards on 18px; the billing switch is a capsule. Borders are hairlines: #e5e7eb/#dee1e6 on marketing, #d1d5db on the booker, #4d4d4d in dark.
