Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export webflow` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use #146ef5 for the action and the announcement.
2. Move the primary button to #0055d4 on hover.
3. Keep controls at 4px and cards at 8px.
4. Paint the page #080808 in dark and white in light.
5. Ring focus in the same blue, 2px, offset 2px.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview tab on the home page and on /pricing. The page starts in dark (`u-mode-dark` on `<html>`, `color-scheme: dark`). Light mode was switched with the header button labelled Light mode. Custom properties on `:root` were read in both modes. `:root` exposed 234 custom properties, including `--colors--*` and the `_color`, `_typography`, `_components`, and `_layout` groups.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so screenshots and crops are from headless Chromium on pages that loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare or PerimeterX challenge. Hover on Start for free is from a real pointer hover in the preview tab. Focus is from `element.focus()` in that tab.

[https://webflow.com/login](https://webflow.com/login) returned the title "Access to this page has been denied" (PerimeterX, "Confirm you're not a bot"). That page was not measured as product chrome and was not held through.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/](raw/), not from the PNGs.

The home hero on the first dark read, at innerWidth 1169, computed to 71.2893px. The pricing h1 at innerWidth 1440 computed to 80px. Both are recorded. The 80px value is the 1440 reading.

`core.typography.nav` rounds 15.9808px, 19.177px, and -0.159808px. `core.typography.hero-home` rounds 71.2893px, 74.1409px, and -0.712893px.

The cookie dialog was visible in the first headless frames and in the preview text, then absent on later loads. Its colours were not read as a separate computed node. Do not treat the dialog crop as a measured component. There is no cookie crop.

Light `--colors--primary-accent-text` resolved to `color-mix(in hsl, #146ef5 92%, #080808 8%)`. That mix is not stored as a colour token. The painted accent text role in dark is `#3b89ff`.

WF Visual Sans Variable loaded from the Webflow CDN. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Inconsolata loaded from Google Fonts at 400 and 700 and is not the body face.

## Fonts

WF Visual Sans Variable is the marketing face, stacked with Arial and sans-serif. It is proprietary and is not bundled. Tokens fall back to Arial, then the generic sans.

Inconsolata is linked from Google Fonts and is named in [tokens.json](tokens.json) as a loaded family, not as body type.

## Do and don't

- **Use #146ef5 for the action and the announcement.** Start for free, Get started, the Campaigns bar, and the yearly toggle track are `#146ef5` with white type. See [home-dark-desktop.png](screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png) and [button-primary-dark.png](components/crops/button-primary-dark.png).
- **Move the primary button to #0055d4 on hover.** The hovered Start for free button is `#0055d4` and gains the four-layer shadow in [tokens.json](tokens.json). Resting buttons have no shadow.
- **Keep controls at 4px and cards at 8px.** Buttons, the NEW pill, and the email field are 4px. Pricing card shells and the Enterprise band are 8px. The toggle track is 32px. See [pricing-light-desktop.png](screenshots/pricing-light-desktop.png).
- **Paint the page #080808 in dark and white in light.** Text flips with it. The blue does not. Secondary text is `#ababab` in dark and `#5a5a5a` in light.
- **Ring focus in the same blue, 2px, offset 2px.** Nav links and the primary button use `outline: 2px solid #146ef5`.
