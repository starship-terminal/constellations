# Patterns

Public marketing pages, signed out, 2026-11-19.

## Home

Announcement bar at 46px, `{light.background.accent}`, then a 68px header. Hero heading uses `{core.typography.hero}` at 1440 on pricing and a slightly smaller computed size on the home read at 1169. Primary and secondary buttons sit under the heading. Page background is `{dark.background.page}` or `{light.background.page}`.

![Home, dark, 1440](screenshots/home-dark-desktop.png)

![Home, light, 1440](screenshots/home-light-desktop.png)

![Home, dark, 390](screenshots/home-dark-mobile.png)

The phone frame is innerWidth 390, innerHeight 844, devicePixelRatio 2. The PNG is 780 by 1688.

## Pricing

Heading "Our pricing" at 80px / 600. A yearly/monthly toggle sits under it. Site plans (Starter, Basic, Premium) and Platform plans (Team) are columns. Enterprise is a dark band. Prices use `{core.typography.price}`.
