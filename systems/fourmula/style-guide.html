Palette

The marketing site inverts: light paint is ink #020108 on #fff with ink-filled CTAs, dark paint is white on #020108 with white-filled CTAs; hairlines and muted inks are the --fonts-* alpha scale and --lines/--menu-colors. The app's declared ramp is the base-100…900 neutral stack with the #ff6b02 accent.

Spacing, radius and motion

The app's declared 4px spacing base and 4–40px radius steps; the marketing site paints fluid radii (~1318px pills, 6.65px chips, 26.6px icon rings). Controls transition over the measured 0.2s ease on marketing and the declared 150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) in the app; the site ignores prefers-reduced-motion (337 moving elements kept running).
