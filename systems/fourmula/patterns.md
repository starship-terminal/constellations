# Fourmula patterns

How the measured components combine on each captured surface. Every value cited is in [tokens.json](tokens.json); the components are in [components.md](components.md) and the full-page evidence in [screenshots/](screenshots). The marketing site and the app are two separate design systems — the marketing `light`/`dark` sets and the app `appColor`/`component.app` values never mix.

## Marketing chrome (both paints)

A ~92.5px header band (`component.marketing.header-height`, ~26.6px side padding) holds the square Fourmula mark left and, right: the dark **Menu pill** (78 x 35, `component.marketing.menu-pill-*`, `light.background.menu` #020108 — white ink on the ink fill in both paints) carrying the moon **theme toggle** and a translucent "0%" **counter** (`component.marketing.icon-btn` 26.6px squares, `light.background.counter` rgba(255,255,255,.2), `light.border.toggleRing`), a 26.6px **account ring** (`component.marketing.icon-ring`, `light.border.iconRing` — the `fonts-20` hairline) and the ~40px ink-filled **Get started** CTA (`component.marketing.cta-*`, `light.action.fill` #020108 in light, white in dark, `light.action.ink` the inverse). The Menu pill opens a full-width ~529px **nav overlay** (`component.marketing.nav-overlay-height`) of 20px/500 overlay links (`font.size.measured.navOverlay`, `light.text.overlayLink`).

**The theme is inverted by name.** The header toggle writes `isDarkMode` to localStorage and flips `dark` on `<body>`: `.body.dark` paints the light surface (the first-visit default — white `light.background.page`, #020108 `light.text.ink`) and plain `.body` paints dark (#020108 page, white ink). Both paints mirror: `light.action.fill`/`dark.action.fill` swap #020108 ↔ #fff exactly, hairlines swap the `fonts-20` alpha between the two inks, and only the menu fill and rules differ (`light.background.menu` #020108 → `dark` #272727; `light.border.rule` #d7d7d6 → #2a2a30). The toggle itself was never pressed during measurement — both paints were reached through the site's own storage key, which the toggle writes.

## Home (light paint)

The hero runs the 73.2px "Your catalog, instantly re-shot." masthead (`font.size.measured.hero`, `font.tracking.hero` -2.2px, "Sf Pro Display") with the second line in `light.text.muted` (the fonts-64 alpha), beside a radial grid of rounded product-photo tiles around an "Upload or drop your assets" centre tile — the marketing site shows published product imagery, not the live product. Rotating-word **chips** (58 x 32, `component.marketing.chip-radius` 6.65px, `light.background.chip`, `light.border.hairline`) swap words in the copy; a 53.2px white circular **AI button** (`component.marketing.ai-btn`, `light.background.aiButton`) sits on the hero band, and a "SCROLL DOWN" hint anchors the fold. The how-it-works band inverts inside the light page: 53.2px step headings paint white (`font.size.measured.step`) with white **inverse CTAs** (`light.action.fillInverse`/`light.action.inkInverse` — the same inversion the dark theme applies globally). Below: logo strips driven by the `heroSlider` --tx/--ty variables and a dotted field (`heroSlider.dots*`), then the **FAQ accordion** — 75px rows (`component.marketing.faq-height`) ruled by `light.border.rule` (#d7d7d6) that grow to ~118px open (`component.marketing.faq-open-height`) — and the ~812px footer (`component.marketing.footer-height`).

## The app shell (dark only)

app.fourmula.ai paints a single dark theme (`appColor.base-900` #020108 under a photo grid): a floating pill header — the white-circle mark, a 32px square **menu button** (`component.app.menu-btn`), a 32px **Create asset** accent pill and **Sign in** accent-tint pill (`component.app.create-btn`/`component.app.signin-btn`; `component.app.accent-fill` #ff6b02 = `appColor.accent-500`, `component.app.accent-tint` ≈ accent at 20%, `component.app.accent-ink` #020108). Controls are full pills (Tailwind's rounded-full sentinel) at `font.size.app.sm` 14px, `sfProFont` everywhere.

Inside: the accent-tint **Continue with Google** pill (`component.app.google-height` 40px), an "or" divider, two 48px fields (`component.app.field-height`; email ~286px, password ~262px with the 16px visibility eye in `component.app.muted-ink` #686a6e), "Forgot password?" (underlined, `component.app.muted-ink`), the accent **Sign in** submit (`component.app.submit-*`, full pill) and the "Don't have an account? Sign up" switch (`component.app.link-accent` #ff6b02). ## Motion and focus

Marketing controls transition over the measured **0.2s ease** (`duration.marketingControl`); the app declares **150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)** (`easing.appDefault`, `duration.appDefault`) and paints a 0.2s cubic-bezier transition on its controls. The motion probe (`core.motion`) found 337 moving elements across three pages — the hero strips (`columnanima`, `hero-row`), the logo `marquee`, the `spinStep`/`spinner-leaf-fade` loaders — and **none honour prefers-reduced-motion**: every element kept running under `reduce` (behaviour `ignores`, an honest finding). Keyboard focus paints almost nothing on either surface: all 19 focused components show no measured outline change (see Shared values in components.md).
