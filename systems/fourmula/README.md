# Fourmula design reference

A token set and component reference for Fourmula's public surfaces — for building marketing pages, legal pages and the signed-out app edge (start, sign-in, the PDP generator) in the style of the real thing. It was read from **fourmula.ai** and **app.fourmula.ai** on **2026-11-15**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page in both painted themes (default, menu overlay, FAQ accordion, cookie consent), /privacy-policy and /terms-of-service, the app's /start, /auth (the sign-in and create-account views; the forgot-password control is a validating submit, so the reset view is uncaptured) and /project/create-pdp (menu, What's-a-PDP and Recommendations dialogs), plus 390px passes on home, start, auth and create-pdp.

Fourmula is an AI product-photography product for e-commerce brands, and its public surface is **two design systems**. The marketing site is a Webflow build with a small declared variable layer — a fluid `--_fonts---*` type ramp (the steps resolve to fractional px at the capture width — 73.2px hero, 99.8px section — and shrink on the phone), a five-step `--fonts-*` alpha ink ramp, the themed `--mainbg`/`--secondbg`/`--lines`/`--menu*` colours, and "Sf Pro Display", Arial — behind a header toggle that writes `isDarkMode` to localStorage and flips a `dark` class on `<body>`. **The class names are inverted against the paint:** `.body.dark` paints the light surface (the first-visit default) and plain `.body` paints dark. Both themes invert the same way: light is ink **#020108** on white with ink-filled CTAs, dark is white on #020108 with white-filled CTAs. The app is a separate **dark-only Next.js/Tailwind v4 app** with a real declared `@theme` ramp — `base-100…900` neutrals (#fff→#020108), the **#ff6b02** accent, `--text-*` 9–52px, `--radius-*` 4–40px — painting a 384px/28px-radius sign-in card on #111, accent/20% tint pills, 48px fields and full-pill controls, all measured, never submitted. **Boundary:** nothing was signed into; the dashboard and every post-login surface need an account; uploads, the Shopify connection and generation were never run; consent was never answered; every CTA and link was measured, never followed.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do invert with the theme.
2. Do spend the orange only in the app.
3. Do keep the two systems apart.
4. Do name the paint, not the class.
5. Do keep pills honest.
6. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't carry the type ramp to another width.
9. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **The signed-in product.** Nothing behind app.fourmula.ai's auth wall was reached; the dashboard, generation results and every account surface need an account this capture does not have. The product photos on the marketing pages are published imagery, not live product.
- **The two token layers.** `appColor.*`, `font.size.app.*`, `radius.app.*` and `component.app.*` are the app's Tailwind system read at app.fourmula.ai — never mix them with the marketing `light`/`dark` roles (the app's #ff6b02 accent does not exist on the marketing site).
- **The forms.** The auth fields were measured at rest and typed into then cleared — validation, error, loading and success states are unverified because nothing was submitted. The PDP "Start generation" pill's dim accent/15% fill is its resting (not-ready) style, not a measured disabled state.
- **The fluid marketing scale.** Marketing px values are fluid-rem readings at 1440 (and 390): the declared `--_fonts---*` steps are the tokens; measured `font.size.measured.*` names what the page painted — the two differ by design.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw values, never the pixels.
- **Sidecar theme shape.** 28 of the 29 committed `screenshots/*.capture.json` record `actual.theme` as `{root: {...}, body: {...}}`, an older shape of this package's `tools/capture/screenshot.mjs`; `auth-dark-forgot` has the kit's flat shape. The script now writes the kit's shape (`classes`, `attributes`, `colorScheme` from `<html>`) with `<body>`'s markers in `actual.theme.body`, so a reader of `actual.theme.classes` works on new captures. The committed sidecars keep the old shape until a recapture rewrites them (the script was edited, not run).
- **Reduced motion.** The marketing site and app ignore `prefers-reduced-motion` (337 moving elements kept running, including the hero strips and marquee loops) — an honest finding, recorded in `core.motion.$extensions.reducedMotion`.

## Fonts

- **"Sf Pro Display", Arial** — the marketing face: hero, section and step headings, buttons, nav overlay links.
- **sfProFont, "sfProFont Fallback"** — the app's local next/font SF Pro build, every app control.
- Neither is bundled here; the style guide falls back to system faces. Declared in the stylesheets' `@font-face` rules (harvested in `raw/css-vars.json` and `raw/css-vars-app.json`).

## Coverage

- 10 desktop routes and 4 mobile routes captured signed out; both public surfaces covered (the two-paint marketing site and the dark-only app edge).
- 78 components measured in 176 states, including the opened menu overlay, the FAQ accordion, the consent manager, typed-then-cleared auth fields, the auth card's sign-in and create-account views (the reset view needs a submission and is recorded as uncaptured), both create-PDP dialogs, the format radiogroup and forced-pressed controls.
- Missing surfaces (declared in the catalog's `capture.gaps`): every post-login app surface (dashboard, uploads, Shopify connection, generation), any pricing page (none was found in the site's navigation, footer or menus), the marketing dark paint of the privacy/terms pages beyond the home pass, and the submit/error/loading states that need a submission.
- Icons: layout gap — no standalone SVG set lifted; the Fourmula mark, header glyphs and app icons are visible in crops/screenshots.

## Do and don't

1. **Do invert with the theme.** Both marketing paints mirror each other — ink #020108 on white with ink-filled CTAs in light, white on #020108 with white-filled CTAs in dark; `action.fill`/`action.ink` flip together. See [header-cta](components/crops/header-cta-default.png) and [header-cta-dark](components/crops/header-cta-dark-default.png).
2. **Do spend the orange only in the app.** `#ff6b02` marks the app's primary actions and its /20% tint fills the secondary pills; it never appears on the marketing site. See [auth-submit](components/crops/auth-submit-default.png) and [auth-google](components/crops/auth-google-default.png).
3. **Do keep the two systems apart.** The Webflow marketing vars (`--_fonts---*`, `--fonts-*`, `--mainbg`) and the app's Tailwind `@theme` (`--color-*`, `--text-*`, `--radius-*`) are separate layers from separate apps. See [auth-card](components/crops/auth-card-default.png).
4. **Do name the paint, not the class.** `.body.dark` paints light and plain `.body` paints dark — the site's class name is inverted against what it paints; the `isDarkMode` storage key says what the toggle wrote. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
5. **Do keep pills honest.** Marketing spells full pills as fluid ~1318px radii (it scales with the rem factor), the app as Tailwind's rounded-full sentinel; chips are 6.65px, icon rings 26.6px, app cards and dialogs 28px. See [cookie-check](components/crops/cookie-check-default.png) and [pdp-reco-dialog](components/crops/pdp-reco-dialog-default.png).
6. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything behind the auth wall was never reached; /start, /auth and /project/create-pdp are the only app surfaces measured. See [auth-dark-default](screenshots/auth-dark-default.png).
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** Sign in, Continue with Google, Upload, Check connection and Start generation were measured, never pressed; what a submission returns is unverified. See [pdp-start-bottom](components/crops/pdp-start-bottom-default.png).
8. **Don't carry the type ramp to another width.** `--_fonts---*` is a fluid rem scale resolved at the 1440 capture viewport — a narrower window paints it smaller. `font.size.marketing.*` is that resolution; `font.size.measured.*` is what the page painted. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
9. **Don't copy the brand.** The Fourmula mark, the product photography and the Awwwards badge are Fourmula's published artwork; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).

## Rebuild

From the repo root, Playwright at `PLAYWRIGHT` (index.mjs) and Pillow at `PYTHON`:

```sh
cd systems/fourmula
RAW=~/.design-systems/fourmula/raw-shots PLAYWRIGHT=$PW node tools/capture/public/public.mjs   # -> RAW full-colour shots
PYTHON=$PY python3 tools/capture/quantize.py ~/.design-systems/fourmula/raw-shots             # -> screenshots/
PLAYWRIGHT=$PW PYTHON=$PY node tools/capture/vars.mjs                                       # -> raw/css-vars.json (marketing)
PLAYWRIGHT=$PW PYTHON=$PY VARS_URL=https://app.fourmula.ai/start VARS_OUT=css-vars-app.json \
  VARS_BODY='body' node tools/capture/vars.mjs                                              # -> raw/css-vars-app.json (the app)
PLAYWRIGHT=$PW PYTHON=$PY node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs                             # -> raw/components-computed.json + crops
PLAYWRIGHT=$PW node tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs --urls https://fourmula.ai,https://app.fourmula.ai/auth,https://app.fourmula.ai/project/create-pdp   # -> raw/motion.json
node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs                                            # -> raw/measurements.md
python3 tools/build_component_tokens.py                                                     # -> raw/component-tokens.json
python3 tools/build_tokens.py                                                               # -> tokens.json
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py                                                          # -> style-guide.html
python3 tools/build_components_md.py                                                        # -> components.md
make -C ../.. design-md grade check                                                         # repo-level (the Makefile is at the root): DESIGN.md, health, validation
```

Capture conduct, edited in the 2026-11 audit and not run since:

- The capture scripts no longer hide automation: the `navigator.webdriver` mask is removed. The committed captures were taken with it.
