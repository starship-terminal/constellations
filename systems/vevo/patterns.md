# Patterns

How the public site combines the components. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## App shell

A transparent header over a full-bleed photo. The wordmark sits left. Six text links sit right: About, Originals, People, For Advertisers, For Artists, Newsroom. At 390 the links collapse into a black drawer opened by the hamburger. The footer is a black band: Contact, Brand, Careers, social icons, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Home

A display headline over a portrait, a white pill (About Us), then a crimson stats band (22 Billion, 1.5 Billion, 1M), artist and advertiser blocks, a video still that opens a lightbox, and a row of press cards. The page is one light theme. Section grounds change: black hero, crimson band, later photo bands.

[home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png), [home-light-video.png](screenshots/home-light-video.png).

## Section pages

About, Originals, People, For Advertisers, For Artists, Newsroom, Blog, Vevo 101, Distribution, and Glossary are the same shell with a new hero and a long scroll. Full pages were captured for the main ones.

[about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [advertisers-light-default.png](screenshots/advertisers-light-default.png), [artists-light-default.png](screenshots/artists-light-default.png), [newsroom-light-default.png](screenshots/newsroom-light-default.png).

## Form

The advertiser contact page is a slate ground with a two-column form: labels above square fields, a country select, a textarea, a newsletter checkbox, and a black pill Submit. The artist support page is the same form on a pink ground. The feedback page is radios and text areas on a lilac ground. None of these forms was submitted. No validation message beyond the empty-field border was captured.

[advertiser-form-light-default.png](screenshots/advertiser-form-light-default.png), [advertiser-form-light-name-focused.png](screenshots/advertiser-form-light-name-focused.png), [artist-support-light-default.png](screenshots/artist-support-light-default.png), [feedback-light-default.png](screenshots/feedback-light-default.png).

## Policy

Privacy and terms were reached. The pages are long text on black, with a Language button and an underlined section list. Screenshots were not kept: the terms page paints a published office address, and this package does not store locations.

## Error and walls

## Empty, loading, destructive, onboarding

Not present on the public site. There is no signed-in app in this capture, no empty library, no destructive confirm, and no onboarding.
