### Principles

1. Set headlines in the display face, white on the photograph, tight line-height.
2. Use the white pill for the primary action on a dark still.
3. Paint each section as one full-bleed band.
4. Keep the header transparent over the still.
5. Mark the current phone-menu item in violet, not with a background.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles and custom properties were read on the live page at 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2, and again at 390 by 844. The Wix tokens (`--wst-color-*`, `--wst-font-style-*`, `--font_*`, `--cookie-banner-*`) came from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue`. Component colour, type, radius and size came from the elements named in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Hover was forced on the About Us pill. The skip link's focus ring was read after Tab.

The T3 preview browser loaded `https://www.hq.vevo.com/` and returned those styles, then both snapshot calls failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Screenshots were taken with headless Playwright on the same public URLs after they returned 200 with no Cloudflare challenge. Screenshots are viewport captures, quantized to 256 colours. Read colour from the tokens, not the PNGs.

## What to distrust

Declared font sizes are not the sizes on a 1440 screen. `--font_0` is 88px and the hero computes to 166.3px. The anchor of a pill reports 10px Arial and `rgb(0, 0, 238)`; the visible label is the inner span at 16px black. `--wst-button-color-fill-primary` is blue and is not the pill on this site. Indexed enumeration of custom properties returned an empty list in the Playwright Chromium, so [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) holds the names that were requested, not every variable on the root. The display font is a Wix-hosted file and is not in this package. Arial is the fallback.

## Fonts

The display family is `wfont_08392a_c1f9747dda3c4c4889c5132122c1d70a`, served from `static.wixstatic.com` as woff2. It is proprietary and not committed. The cookie banner uses a second Wix face, `wfont_08392a_3b7a707e0480499385ea560e682a43c6`. DIN Next and Poppins are declared in unused `@font-face` rules and were not the computed family of the text that was read. Tokens fall back to Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.

## Do and don't

1. **Set headlines in the display face, white on the photograph, tight line-height.** The hero is four stacked lines, not a sentence in a card. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
2. **Use the white pill for the primary action on a dark still.** Black label, 24px radius, 46px tall. Hover inverts it to near-black. [button-default.png](components/crops/button-default.png)
3. **Paint each section as one full-bleed band.** Crimson, slate, sand and black, with the type colour flipped to stay readable. [home-light-cookie.png](screenshots/home-light-cookie.png)
4. **Keep the header transparent over the still.** White wordmark left, 14px links right, no bar fill. [nav-default.png](components/crops/nav-default.png)
5. **Mark the current phone-menu item in violet, not with a background.** Home is `rgb(149, 7, 255)` on black. [menu-open.png](components/crops/menu-open.png)

## Surfaces

This package is the public marketing site, not the YouTube network player. Signed-out video chrome was looked for at `/watch` and a video path. Both redirect to the originals marketing page. A player, an account, and a dark theme are gaps.
