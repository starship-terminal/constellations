# The Leap

The Leap (theleap.co) is a link-in-bio store for creator products. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out login chrome, measured on 2026-11-19 from a live T3 preview tab. Signup at accounts.theleap.co/signup redirected to the login page, so no account was created. The login form already held an email and a masked password from an earlier session. Those values were not changed, not copied here, and the form was not submitted. Google and Thinkific were not followed. No card was entered.

The live CSS viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2, visualViewport scale 1. Marketing pages use a light warm gray page. No dark theme was found.

## How the values were read

Custom properties and computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` and stylesheet walks on https://www.theleap.co/, https://www.theleap.co/pricing/, https://www.theleap.co/features/, https://www.theleap.co/blog/, https://www.theleap.co/contact/, https://www.theleap.co/changelog/, and the login page at https://make.theleap.co/accounts/login. Colour strings the browser returned as `rgb()` were converted to hex from the same channel values. Font files were named from `@font-face` rules in the theme CSS. Inline SVGs were copied from the live header and footer.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on `preview_snapshot`, three times. No screenshots were invented. Headless Playwright was not launched against the login page.

## What to distrust

The CSS viewport was 1169, not 1440 and not 390. Do not treat these readings as a 1440 or 390 capture. The brown band heading on home computed as `#4c2116` on a `#4c2116` background, so that heading does not separate from the band in the reading. Pricing "Sign up" buttons measured radius 20px while home yellow buttons measured 100px. Both readings are kept. Auth pills measured `9999px`. Login fields measured radius 0. The catalog accent is the yellow button fill `#ecf956`, not the rainbow hover gradient. Hover shadow and the `::before` rainbow are stylesheet rules. They were not shown. Screenshot folders have no page images on purpose. The login form contained a prefilled email and password. Those strings are not in this package.

## Fonts

Tobias Light is the display face, weight 300, from `Tobias-Light.woff2`. ABC Favorit ships as Favorit at weights 300 (Book), 400 (Regular) and 700 (Bold). Computed `font-family` is `Favorit` on body, nav and buttons, and `"ABC Favorit"` on subtitles, plan names and prices. The font files are not in this package. Tokens keep the measured stacks and fall back to sans-serif.

## Do and don't

- **Use the warm gray page and brown ink.** Home, pricing, features, blog and contact render on `#f0edee` with `#482317` text and `#4c2116` secondary text. [patterns.md](patterns.md) describes that shell.
- **Use the yellow pill for the primary action.** "Explore features" is `#ecf956` with a 1px `#482317` border, 100px radius, 20px Favorit, uppercase. [components.md](components.md) lists the sizes.
- **Keep login as a signed-out form.** https://make.theleap.co/accounts/login asks for email and password, plus Google and Thinkific. Do not invent a signed-in store or a checkout behind it. [components.md](components.md) records the gap.
- **Use Tobias for headlines and Favorit for UI.** The two faces do not collapse into one stack. [tokens.json](tokens.json) keeps both.
- **Do not invent a 1440 screenshot.** The preview browser produced no frames. [screenshots/](screenshots/) says so.
