# Tango

Tango (tango.ai) turns a workflow into a shareable how-to guide. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out login and signup on app.tango.us, measured on 2026-11-19 from a live T3 preview tab. The email field was set to kaichen@agentmail.to. A password field appeared on sign-in and was not filled. Signup continued to a name and password form and was not submitted. Google was not followed. No card was entered.

The live CSS viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2. Marketing pages are light (`#fafafa` page, `#383838` text). The app login uses the same light Topaz palette. `prefers-color-scheme` was dark and the marketing page did not switch.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the Webflow stylesheet on https://www.tango.ai/ and from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`. The app pages on https://app.tango.us/sign-in and https://app.tango.us/sign-up expose a `--topaz-*` set, also read from computed style. Component colour, type, radius, padding and size were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Named hex tokens (`#6f26d0`, `#fafafa`, `#f9650f`) matched the computed `rgb()` values, so those hex strings are the tokens.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on `preview_snapshot`. No screenshots were invented. Headless Playwright was not launched against the login page.

## What to distrust

The CSS viewport was 1169, not 1440 and not 390. Do not treat these readings as a 1440 or 390 capture. Marketing pill radius computed as 731px because the token is `100vh`. At another viewport height that pill changes. The `100vh` declaration is listed under `core.$extensions.notTokenised`, since a DTCG dimension holds one px length. Ten declared values have no reading in [raw/foundations.json](raw/foundations.json): `core.color.purple.700` and `100`, `black`, `border`, `error`, the secondary and tertiary families, `core.motion.navDropdown`, `light.border.default` and `light.status.error`. They are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time. The hero gradient is assigned as a background image with `background-clip: text`, and `-webkit-text-fill-color` stayed black, so the headline was painted black in this browser. Pricing "Monthly" was not clicked, so the monthly prices on the cards were visible in the DOM text and were not measured as a separate selected state. Disabled Continue and enabled Continue were both measured. Hover, pressed and loading were not. `.button:hover` in the stylesheet is a declared rule, not a shown state. The AnyApp panel on the login page is a public demo. It is not the signed-in app. Screenshot folders are empty on purpose.

## Fonts

Gelica is the display face (marketing h1, app h2). Apercu Pro is the marketing UI face. The app computes the family as Apercu, the same cut under a shorter name. Inter Variable and Lato are declared as secondary and tertiary families and were not the painted UI. These families are named as computed. The font files are not in this package. Tokens keep the measured stacks and fall back to Arial and ui-sans-serif.

## Do and don't

- **Use the warm white page and purple pill.** Home, pricing and enterprise render on `#fafafa` with `#383838` text. "Get started for free" is `#6f26d0` with white text and a full pill radius. [patterns.md](patterns.md) describes that shell.
- **Use Gelica for headings and Apercu for UI.** The hero and the sign-in title are Gelica. Body, nav, fields and buttons are Apercu. [tokens.json](tokens.json) keeps both stacks.
- **Keep login on app.tango.us as a signed-out form.** Email reveals a password field. Signup then asks for a name and a password of at least 8 characters. Do not invent a signed-in dashboard behind it. [components.md](components.md) records the gap.
- **Use orange for the selected pricing pill and the sales button.** Annual pricing is `#f9650f`. "Talk to sales" is `#ff8c49`. Those are not the purple primary. [components.md](components.md) lists both.
- **Do not invent a 1440 screenshot.** The preview browser produced no frames. [screenshots/](screenshots/) says so.
