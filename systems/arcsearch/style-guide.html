Arc Search style guide

Arc Search for iOS, read from stills captured through iPhone Mirroring on 2026-10-23 (dark) and 2026-10-24 (light). Every value below comes from tokens.json; points are 1:1 with CSS pixels. The font is not known, so the page uses the platform sans. Every glass surface is drawn as one reading of a translucent material that takes the colour of the page behind it.

Light and dark side by side. The badge says how each value was read: Sampled (a flat fill, exact), Sampled (text) (the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill), Derived (worked out, or a reading of a material, tint or gradient). Hover a swatch for its capture.
