# Arc Search design reference

A token set and component reference for Arc Search, The Browser Company's iOS browser. Use it to build a native iOS browser, search or reader app (SwiftUI) that borrows its structure: a web page edge to edge with no address bar, a glass toolbar along the bottom, a launcher whose search field sits low on the screen, one glass page menu that holds every page action, and Settings as a sheet of inset groups with coloured icon tiles. Arc's own colour is one blue-violet accent, kept for a confirm button, ticks, text actions and the caret. It was captured from the shipped app through iPhone Mirroring, not signed in: dark on 2026-10-23 with the phone in dark mode, light on 2026-10-24 after the owner switched the phone to light mode (Arc Search follows the phone and has no theme setting of its own). The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 48 captures: 24 in dark and the same 24 in light (launcher, typed search, a page with the toolbar, the tab switcher, Settings and three of its sub-pages, a pop-up menu, the App Icon sheet, the page menu expanded and collapsed, the find bar in three states, the space sheet, a long-press menu, the Archive, its Edit menu and its search in three states). The light pass shows different pages behind the chrome (YouTube instead of Google, and a government voucher page in 03); the chrome is the same. Masked under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md): a private hostname in the long-press menu (19) and the Launcher's history row, and two history searches (20, 21, 24), in both themes. The owner's other history titles and pinned sites show as captured.

This package contains no Arc code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are The Browser Company's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)); the web pages inside the browser (YouTube, Google, other sites) belong to those sites and are kept only to show layout. Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, the app icons, the icon tiles' glyphs or a space's gradient.

### Principles

1. Do put chrome at the bottom, in glass.
2. Do gather a page's actions into one menu.
3. Do fill controls on a sheet with the same grey as its groups.
4. Do keep the accent for confirming and choosing.
5. Don't make destructive actions buttons.
6. Don't draw dividers outside grouped cards.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Arc Search is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 124 named readings, most in both themes. The pop-up menu and Settings' subtitle row sit lower in the light captures, so a reading can name a box per theme. No reading box touches a masked region. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps the readings to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

Most of Arc's chrome is iOS 26 glass: a translucent material that takes the colour of the page behind it. Two methods were added to the kit for it (tested in `tools/test_measure.py`): `mean`, the average colour of a box, for a glass surface or a gradient, and `span`, the first and last sharp step along a line, for the size of a glass control whose fill drifts too much for a background point.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `mean` | Derived | The average of glass or a gradient: one moment's reading |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph or hairline colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `span` | Derived | The size of a glass control, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal; `corner`, the same against what lies behind the shape | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of ink bands | Derived | A line height, or a row height when the bands are row labels or icons |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font family |

Scale steps (spacing, radii, rounded sizes) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a control's light copy could not be bounded (a white glass button on a white sheet), its size comes from dark and says so.

## What to distrust

- **Every glass colour is one moment.** The launcher, the search field, the toolbar, the page menu and its panels, the find bar, the pop-up menu and the Archive's search field take their colour from the page behind them: the page menu reads brown and maroon over a YouTube thumbnail in dark and pink over a pink video in light ([12-page-menu-address-actions](screenshots/dark/12-page-menu-address-actions.png), [12 light](screenshots/light/12-page-menu-address-actions.png)). The `color.glass.*` tokens are means of one box each. Build glass as a material, not as these fills.
- **The space gradient is Main's.** Each space has its own colour; `color.space.top` and `bottom` are means over a grainy gradient, plum to green ([17-space-sheet-gradient](screenshots/dark/17-space-sheet-gradient.png)).
- **Two neutral systems.** The Settings sheet uses its own greys (#2A2A2C sheet and #444444 groups in dark, #F5F5F5 and #DDDDDD in light); the Archive uses the iOS grouped colours (#1C1C1E and #2C2C2E, #F2F2F6 and #FFFFFF). Take each from its own token, not one from the other.
- **The accent shifts between themes.** The confirm button reads #768AFF in dark and #2D36F8 in light (light is a glass tint, 88% share); ticks, links and the caret read the same pair within a few units.
- **The dark sheet's radius is not read.** The dark Settings sheet is too close to the dimmed page behind it to bound; `radius.sheet` is the light reading (47.8 pt).
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: groups and cards 25.6, square buttons 19.6, panels 14.5, Browse for Me 10.2, icon tiles 4.3 (at 28 pt the tile's reading is the least certain).
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em; weights are judged by eye. Only the two-line helper has a line height (22 pt).
- **The neutral list is a list.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the two themes use, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published ramp.
- **No switch off, no pressed, disabled or error states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** Every switch in Arc's settings is on and none was changed, the captures are stills made with clicks, and iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass and gradients band visibly. Read values from tokens.json.
- **A blank slot on the YouTube comments teaser is a mask.** The commenter's letter avatar beside "Comments" on the YouTube page and its tab cards was painted out with the card colour on 2026-11-21 ([arcsearch.json](../../tools/redact-rules/arcsearch.json)), because it is another person's avatar; the channel, video and page content stay.

## Do and don't

1. **Do put chrome at the bottom, in glass.** The page runs edge to edge with no address bar; the toolbar, the launcher, the page menu and the find bar all sit low and are translucent. See [03-page-bottom-toolbar](screenshots/dark/03-page-bottom-toolbar.png), [01-launch-search-focused-pinned](screenshots/dark/01-launch-search-focused-pinned.png) and [15-find-bar-typed-match light](screenshots/light/15-find-bar-typed-match.png).
2. **Do gather a page's actions into one menu.** Address, actions, display options and site settings all live in the page menu, stacked as panels. See [12-page-menu-address-actions](screenshots/dark/12-page-menu-address-actions.png) and [13-page-menu-collapsed](screenshots/dark/13-page-menu-collapsed.png).
3. **Do fill controls on a sheet with the same grey as its groups.** Downloads, Archive and every grouped card on the Settings sheet share `surface.group`; colour comes only from the icon tiles. See [05-settings-sheet](screenshots/dark/05-settings-sheet.png) and [05 light](screenshots/light/05-settings-sheet.png).
4. **Do keep the accent for confirming and choosing.** The blue-violet fills one round confirm button and colours ticks, text actions and the caret; it never fills a capsule or a row. See [20-archive-history-list](screenshots/dark/20-archive-history-list.png) and [08-settings-search-engine-checklist](screenshots/dark/08-settings-search-engine-checklist.png).
5. **Don't make destructive actions buttons.** Remove Favorite and Clear All are red text with a red glyph inside a glass menu or capsule. See [19-longpress-favorite-menu](screenshots/dark/19-longpress-favorite-menu.png) and [21-edit-menu-destructive-item](screenshots/dark/21-edit-menu-destructive-item.png).
6. **Don't draw dividers outside grouped cards.** Launcher rows, suggestions and the page menu's rows are separated by space alone; only rows inside a group get an inset hairline. See [18-search-sheet-pinned-rows](screenshots/dark/18-search-sheet-pinned-rows.png), [02-search-typed-suggestions](screenshots/dark/02-search-typed-suggestions.png) and [09-site-settings-switches](screenshots/dark/09-site-settings-switches.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/arcsearch/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/arcsearch/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.group`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Draw every `color.glass.*` surface as a system material (`.glassEffect` or `.regularMaterial`) over the page rather than as its fill, and use the system switch, menus and sheets. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme; give glass a background blur over the page instead of the fill.
