# Pastel design reference

A token set and component reference for Pastel's public website, measured from usepastel.com on 2026-11-20, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440x900 and 390x844. The site loaded (HTTP 200). Captured: the marketing pages, the blog shell, the customers shell, the public tools, the help center, the status page, and the login and signup walls as surfaces. The annotation canvas was not entered.

Pastel's marketing pages use Figtree on white, ink `#111111`, and one blue CTA `#165dfb` at a 10px radius. A second shell (customers, teams, about) uses the same family on `#f5f5f4`. The blog shell uses Inter and a declared colour list. There is no public dark theme on the marketing site.

The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## How the values were read

Headless Playwright opened each public URL, waited for DOM content, hid the Intercom launcher with CSS, and read `getComputedStyle`. Screenshots used the kit's screenshot helper and were quantised to 256 colours. Custom properties were read from the CSSOM on `/blog`, `help.usepastel.com`, and `status.usepastel.com`. The marketing homepage declares almost no colour tokens. Its colours are computed styles.

Nothing was typed into a field. No form was submitted. Login, signup, Google, and SSO were not clicked. The login and signup pages were loaded only to record the wall.

## What to distrust

- This is the public site, not the annotation canvas. Projects, comments, and billing sit behind `/login`.
- Hover on the primary CTA did not change its computed background. Do not invent a hover colour.
- The annual pricing shot is a real toggle click. The first click on the label did not move the prices. The recapture clicked `.toggle-switch`.
- Status colours belong to Instatus. Help colours belong to Intercom. They are labelled that way in tokens.json.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json.
- Login field metrics are Not captured. The page was not interacted with.
- `DESIGN.md`, Brief, and Health were not generated. `make check` requires a catalog entry, and this capture was told not to edit catalog.json.

## Fonts

Figtree is the marketing and customers face (computed `Figtree, sans-serif`). Inter is the blog face. Help uses system-ui. Status uses Inter. None of these files are in the package. The style guide loads Figtree from Google Fonts as a stand-in for the same family name.

## Do and don't

1. **Do use one blue.** The only accent on the marketing pages is `#165dfb`, on the announcement bar and the primary CTA. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do keep the headline large and light.** The home h1 is Figtree 60.2px, weight 400, line-height 66.22px, letter-spacing -0.8px. See [type-display](components/crops/type-display.png).
3. **Do pair a blue pill with a quiet ghost.** Start a free trial is filled. Watch demo is a 1px `#d7d3d1` outline. See [button-primary-default](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
4. **Do use the ink pill for Sign up.** The header signup is `#454745`, not the blue. See [button-signup-default](components/crops/button-signup-default.png).
5. **Don't style the canvas from this package.** The product behind login was not captured.
6. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public marketing site did not offer one.
