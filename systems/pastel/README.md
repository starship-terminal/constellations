# Pastel

Pastel (usepastel.com) is a visual feedback tool for websites and files. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out login and signup cards. Captured 2026-11-19 from a signed-out T3 preview tab at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, device pixel ratio 2. Light is the only theme on those pages. No account was created. No form was submitted.

### Principles

1. Use Figtree at weight 400 for headlines and 500 for controls.
2. Use `#165dfb` for the marketing accent.
3. Keep the page white and the band stone.
4. Round controls at 10px on the marketing site and 6 to 8px on the account card.
5. Leave the password forms alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

I opened https://usepastel.com/ in a T3 preview tab (`reuseExistingTab: false`) and read `getComputedStyle` on the live page. The marketing CSS is one Webflow sheet, `pastel-marketing.webflow.shared.28fda8ef8.min.css`. Same-origin rules were readable in the CSSOM. Google font sheets were cross-origin and blocked. I also fetched the Webflow sheet and read the class rules that matched the painted nodes.

Pages measured: home, https://usepastel.com/plans, https://usepastel.com/features, https://usepastel.com/login, https://usepastel.com/signup. Login and signup are a different app shell (`body.pastel-app`) on the same host. I focused the login email field and read the focus ring. I did not type into it.

Viewport at read time: `innerWidth` 1169, `innerHeight` 731, `devicePixelRatio` 2, `visualViewport` scale 1. That is the preview pane, not a requested 1440 or 390.

## What to distrust

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." There are no screenshots and no crops. Layout claims come from DOM measurements and the stylesheet, not from a reviewed PNG.

`.button-primary` (`#1a1b1f`, 12px, uppercase, 2px letter-spacing) is in the sheet. The painted marketing buttons were `.button` and `.navbar-button`, not that class. Webflow's default `.w-button` is `#3898ec`. A hidden mobile Login link still computed that blue at 0 size. Do not treat it as the desktop button.

Inter and Oswald are loaded by the marketing page (`wf-inter-*` and `wf-oswald-*` on `html`). Body, headings and buttons computed Figtree. I did not find a painted Inter or Oswald node on the pages I opened.

The first footer anchor computed as default link blue with an underline. The declared `.footer-link` is `#222222` with `text-decoration: none`. The sampled node did not carry that class.

`--pricing-toggle-monthly: 600` and `--pricing-toggle-annual: 400` are the only product custom properties on `:root`. They are unitless numbers, not colours.

Login field `box-shadow` includes a 0px white ring. The token drops that layer and keeps the stone ring and the two black shadows.

## Do and don't

- **Use Figtree at weight 400 for headlines and 500 for controls.** The home h1 was 52.605px with -0.8px tracking. Buttons were 16px weight 500.
- **Use `#165dfb` for the marketing accent.** The announcement bar and Start a free trial share it. Pricing CTAs use `#12b981`. The login submit uses `#4f77ff`.
- **Keep the page white and the band stone.** Body is `#ffffff`. `.section-dark` and the login page are `#f5f5f4`.
- **Round controls at 10px on the marketing site and 6 to 8px on the account card.** Nav text links stay at 4px. Pills use 120px.
- **Leave the password forms alone.** Login and signup both ask for a password. This package stops at the signed-out card.
