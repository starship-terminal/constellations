https://www.wix-pages.com/design-system-documentation/#/page/foundations/colors

Wix Design System v1.352.0. The page says "Last updated 2026-10-29" and "Every color token in the theme you pick in the header." The theme picker was not changed. 476 rows were in the table. Semantic rows mostly alias a foundation name (`--wds-color-blue-100`). Gradients and the transparent steps are listed in the gaps: they were read, and they are not stored as colour tokens.
