# Patterns

How the public site combines the components. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Marketing shell

White header, floral-white page, yellow pill for the one primary action. FAQ, Wall of love, Apps and Resources sit left. Search, Log in and Sign up sit right. The same header and footer repeat on every marketing page.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Hero

One display line, "Fund your creative work", at 96px Circular Bold, tracking -2.4px, ink on floral white. One yellow pill under it. Creator cards float beside and below the headline.

[screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png)

## Hover menus

Apps opens a short list (iOS, Android) that leaves the site. Resources opens Creator Tools, Comparisons, Request a feature, and Blog. The panel is a white card with a soft shadow, not a full-screen overlay.

[screenshots/home-light-resources-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-resources-menu.png)

## Search

Clicking Search creators opens a modal on the same URL. The field is empty in the capture. Results were not requested.

[screenshots/home-light-search-open.png](screenshots/home-light-search-open.png)

## Phone drawer

At 390 the header collapses to a menu icon. The drawer lists FAQ, Wall of love, iOS, Android, and Start my page. Apps and Resources are desktop-only.

[screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png)

## FAQ

A question list on floral white. Opening a question expands the answer in place.

[screenshots/faq-light-question-open.png](screenshots/faq-light-question-open.png)

## Wall of love

A carousel of public social mentions. Slide next moves it.

[screenshots/reviews-light-slide-next.png](screenshots/reviews-light-slide-next.png)

## Comparison page

Same shell as home, with a comparison headline. Captured for Ko-fi and Patreon. The other six comparison URLs use the same template and were not each screenshotted.

[screenshots/ko-fi-light-default.png](screenshots/ko-fi-light-default.png)

## Public creator page

A published supporter page, not the dashboard. Quantity radios, a name field, a message, membership tiers. Support and Join start payment and were not clicked.

[screenshots/creator-light-default.png](screenshots/creator-light-default.png)

## Auth wall

Login asks for email, then Google, Facebook, Apple, or Twitter. Signup asks for a name. Both were photographed and not filled.

[screenshots/login-light-auth-wall.png](screenshots/login-light-auth-wall.png)
