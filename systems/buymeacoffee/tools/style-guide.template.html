Palette

The measured color.neutral.* readings carry the ink, cream, fields and hairlines; color.brand.* the yellow paint; color.theme.* the per-creator accent scale declared inline on the page root; color.focus.* the two focus paints. Light roles alias the palette.

Spacing and radius

Buttons and quantity pills are 9999px pills; the marketing bmc-btn's paint layer is a 40px pill; fields and dropdowns sit on 12px, cards on 24px, creator tiles on 16px, menu rows on 10px, tabs on 8px and the checkboxes on 6px. Borders are hairlines: #e5e5e5 on light, theme-25% on the accent pills.
