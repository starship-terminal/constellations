# Teachable

Teachable sells online courses, coaching, and downloads. This package covers the public marketing site at www.teachable.com and the signed-out Teachable Accounts sign-in chrome at sso.teachable.com. Captured 2026-11-19, signed out, light only, at 1440 and 390 CSS px.

The marketing site is Webflow. The accounts page is a separate Rails stylesheet. They share the chartreuse `#e6ff32` and not the type or the button radius.

### Principles

1. Use the chartreuse pill for the primary action.
2. Keep the nav black and the page white.
3. Set display type in Ivypresto Headline at weight 400.
4. Use the accounts radius on the accounts form.
5. Do not invent a dark theme.
6. Do not treat the hero dashboard as the app.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on www.teachable.com and on the signed-out sign-in page, both at 1440 by 900 CSS px and devicePixelRatio 2. Custom properties were read from `:root`. Marketing html font-size computed to `14.9666px`, so a `1rem` length is that size, not 16px. Accounts html font-size computed to `16px`.

Public page screenshots were taken in headless Playwright after the preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Those pages returned the real Teachable title, not a Cloudflare challenge. The login host was not opened in headless Playwright. Login values come from the preview browser's computed styles only.

No stylesheet was rewritten. No theme was switched. The accounts email field was focused and not filled.

## What to distrust

The dashboard frame on the home hero is a marketing illustration. Its sidebar, chart, and buttons are not the signed-in product.

`--_theme---system--success-text` resolves to `#b6f2e8` on a success background of `#fae6b6`. That pair was declared and not seen on a live success message. Several `--_theme---` names contain a Webflow `deleted|variable` suffix. They are recorded in raw readings, not promoted to tokens.

Marketing button radius computes to `900px`. The declared full radius is `100vh`. Both are pills. The accounts Log In radius is `4px`.

Hover on the yellow pill did not change its computed background. A Webflow interaction may still move it. That motion was not measured.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.

## Fonts

Ivypresto Headline (400, regular and italic) and Peridot PE (variable, 100 to 950) are loaded from the Webflow CDN. They are proprietary and are not in this package. Tokens fall back to Georgia and Arial.

Teachable Accounts uses Reckless Light for the heading and a system-ui stack for the form. Reckless is proprietary. The token fallback is Palatino, Garamond, Georgia.

Hotmart Sans is loaded by the cookie panel. It is not the product UI face.

## Do and don't

- **Use the chartreuse pill for the primary action.** The nav and hero "Start for free" buttons are `#e6ff32` with black text, a 1px black border, and a `900px` radius. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Keep the nav black and the page white.** `.nav_wrapper` is `#000000` at `79px`. Body text is black Peridot. The footer is black with `#a0a0a0` links. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Set display type in Ivypresto Headline at weight 400.** The home h1 computes to `74.8332px` with line-height equal to the font size. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Use the accounts radius on the accounts form.** The Log In button is `4px`, not the marketing pill. The email field is `5px`. See [components.md](components.md).
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** Neither surface exposed a theme control. See [screenshots/](screenshots/).
- **Do not treat the hero dashboard as the app.** It is a public demo frame. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
