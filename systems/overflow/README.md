### Principles

1. Use black for the marketing primary button.
2. Use the blue only as a link and as the app action.
3. Keep the marketing type in Inter at weight 800 for display.
4. Keep the app shell on the ice-blue panel.
5. Do not invent a dark theme.
6. Do not treat the disabled Sign in button as the primary.

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium (Playwright) loaded the public pages and the signed-out shell. Marketing CSS is `public-site-homepage.min.css` on `/` and `public-site-v2.min.css` on the other marketing pages. The blog uses `public-site-v2-light.min.css`. The app shell is a React bundle, `main.624d7318.css`, whose custom properties are declared on `body`.

Computed styles were read on the live DOM at 1440x900 and 390x844. Hover was a real pointer. Focus-visible on the header primary button was forced with `CSS.forcePseudoState` so the button could not navigate. The cookie banner was dismissed with Required only. No field was submitted. Google, SSO, Download, and Subscribe were not clicked.

App colour, type, shadow, and motion tokens are the custom properties resolved on `body` at `/n/signin` (`raw/app-vars.json`). Marketing colour and type are computed styles (`raw/deep.json`, `raw/pricing-bits.json`, `raw/focus-visible.json`).

## What to distrust

The forced focus-visible outline is `outline-style: auto` in `rgb(0, 95, 204)`: Chromium's default focus ring. Overflow sets no focus colour, so `core.color.focus-ring` and `light.focus.*` are labelled Observed and are not a brand value to copy.

Five marketing values have no reading in raw/: the h3 eyebrow colour `#acacbc`, the `.btn-secondary` fill `#f0f0f2`, and the `section`, `card-title` and `eyebrow` type styles. They are labelled Reconstruction (typed in by hand at capture time).

The cookie banner was dismissed with its Required only choice before the readings; every marketing reading was taken after that click.

The hero wash has no CSS `background-image` on the section or its ancestors. Its stops are not in the tokens.

`font-weight: 550` is the computed value of `.btn`. It is not a standard weight.

The pricing h2 "Pro" computes to line-height 24px at font-size 40px. That is what the page returned.

Sign-in focus darkens the field border to `#a3a6aa`. The declared `--color-input-focus` is `#a3cdf5`, and that variable did not win on the focused field.

`/n/forgot` renders the app 404, not a reset form. The forgot-password control on the sign-in page was not followed into a working form.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from the tokens.

Inter and Inter var are licensed faces and are not in this package. The style guide falls back down the measured stack.

## Fonts

Marketing: Inter, then Helvetica Neue, Arial, Noto Sans, sans-serif. Weights measured: 400, 500, 550, 700, 800. The faces are self-hosted woff2 under `/assets/public-site-v2/fonts/` and are not shipped here.

App shell: "Inter var", sans-serif, from `--font-family-body`. Weights declared: 300, 400, 500, 600, 800.

An icon font, icomoon 3.03, draws the marketing icons. It is not shipped. The package keeps no icons/ folder for that reason.

## Do and don't

- **Use black for the marketing primary button.** The header and hero Download buttons are `#000` on white or on the hero wash, 8px radius, white type. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Use the blue only as a link and as the app action.** Marketing links and the active nav item are `#0085e4`. The app's Download button is `#0071db`, and its hover is `#0065c5`. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [screenshots/signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
- **Keep the marketing type in Inter at weight 800 for display.** The home headline is 56px, line-height 63.84px, tracking -1.2px. Interior headlines go to 48px or stay at 56px. See [screenshots/features-light-default.png](screenshots/features-light-default.png).
- **Keep the app shell on the ice-blue panel.** `.authpanel` is `#d6f3ff`, 485px wide at 1440, with the form on white. See [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** Both surfaces stayed light under a dark colour scheme.
- **Do not treat the disabled Sign in button as the primary.** It is `#f0f1f3` with `#b7bdc5` text until the fields validate. See [components/crops/button-signin-disabled.png](components/crops/button-signin-disabled.png).
