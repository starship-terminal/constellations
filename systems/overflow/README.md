# Overflow design reference

A token set and component reference for Overflow's public marketing site, for building white marketing pages, a black primary button, and a blue link accent in the style of the real thing. It was read from **overflow.io** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

Overflow is a desktop user-flow app. The public site is a Bootstrap marketing surface: a white header, a gradient hero, a black Download button, a Product mega menu, pricing cards, and a public shared player at `/s/<id>/`. The signed-in product was not reached. https://overflow.io/n/signin and https://overflow.io/n/signup?buy are walls. Those branches stop. https://overflow.io/login/ is a 404 page, not a form.

The site paints one light theme (`color-scheme: normal`, body `#ffffff`). No theme toggle was found. Colours live in compiled class rules, not a token layer. The only CSS custom property on the homepage is the vendor `--swiper-theme-color`.

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium on 2026-11-21. Playwright is the local module at `~/.design-systems/pw`. The T3 preview browser was not used.

- **Site tree.** [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) was written from the homepage, the product menu, the footer, and sitemap.xml before the screenshot pass. Sitemap has 30 URLs. A homepage link crawl hit an 80-page guard with a large queue left, so the capture follows the sitemap plus one blog post and one public `/s/` player.
- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) walked the homepage stylesheets and wrote [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json): one custom property, `--swiper-theme-color: #007aff`, plus 12 `@font-face` rules (icomoon, swiper-icons, Inter 400/500/600/700/800). That proves the palette is not a CSS variable layer. Component colours come from `getComputedStyle` in [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs). [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) writes [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- **Components.** The measure script opens each route in a fresh context and reads computed style, painted box, and states: default, a real pointer hover, focus-visible via Tab, and pressed via CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` so no link is followed and no form is submitted. Results are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours. Full-colour shots stay in `~/.design-systems/overflow/raw-shots` and are not committed.
- **Icon.** The homepage HTML has no favicon link. `https://overflow.io/favicon.ico` returned a 48 px ICO. [icon.png](icon.png) is that image scaled to 128 px. The wordmark SVG at `/assets/public-site-v2/images/logo.svg` is fill `#161637` and is not committed.

The cookie bar was hidden in the DOM. Required only and Allow all were not clicked. No email, password, name, phone, or newsletter field was typed. No form was submitted. Sign in, Sign up, Google, Apple, and SSO were not clicked. Download buttons were not followed. External hosts (support.overflow.io, marketplace, social networks) were not captured.

## What to distrust

- **One painted theme.** Every captured route paints light. There is no dark set.
- **No design-token layer.** `--swiper-theme-color` is a vendor variable and is not painted on the captured controls. `core.color.theme` (`#232d41`) is the `theme-color` meta tag, unread as a painted fill.
- **`core.color.link` is the logo's computed link colour.** The SVG wordmark fill is `#161637` (`core.color.ink-soft`). Do not treat the blue as the mark's fill.
- **`core.color.grey-2` and `core.font.family.mono` are declared, not computed on a specimen.** Sign In computes transparent until pressed. No code block was on the captured pages. `core.motion.button` and `core.motion.lift` are declared stylesheet values, not a computed `transitionTimingFunction` on a measured element.
- **`blog-card` is a filter pill.** The selector missed the article grid. `core.color.muted` and `core.radius.chip` come from that pill.
- **feature-card and integration-card matched the page body.** Those crops are not component evidence.
- **Homepage Canvas / Prototype / Story shots are the demo dialog.** The hash links open later frames of the same YouTube dialog. They are not switched page sections. See [home-light-canvas-view.png](screenshots/home-light-canvas-view.png).
- **Focus rings.** Several controls show no outline change on Tab. demo-link and faq-item could not be focused. Do not invent a focus token.
- **Hero gradient.** The wash is painted artwork. The style guide's gradient is a reconstruction, labelled as such in the template. It is not a token.
- **Plan label colours.** Pro reads blue and Enterprise reads purple in the pricing shot. Those colours were not measured as roles and are not tokens.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` and crops are 256-colour PNGs. Read exact colours from tokens or the raw JSON.
- **Shared player.** `/s/<id>/` is a public document, including a prototype of someone else's marketing page. It is not the Overflow marketing system and not the signed-in editor.
- **Chat widget.** Pricing shows a blue circle at the bottom right. It is a third-party widget, not a token.

## Fonts

Declared in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json): **Inter** at 400, 500, 600, 700, and 800, served from `/assets/public-site-v2/fonts/Inter-*.woff2`. Computed UI text uses `Inter, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, "Noto Sans", sans-serif`. **icomoon** is the icon font. **swiper-icons** is the carousel vendor face. A mono stack is declared in the homepage CSS and was not measured on a specimen. The webfont files are not committed. Their licence is not stated in the captured pages.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the page white and the primary button black.** Header, cards, and the mega menu are `#ffffff`. Download and the hero CTA are `#000000` with white text, 8px radius. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [hero-cta-default.png](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
2. **Do use one blue for links and the current nav item.** Hover is `#259aef`. Pressed is `#124b74`. See [nav-link-hover.png](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png).
3. **Do open Product as a full-width white sheet.** The menu is 1440 by 393 with a `#dadce0` top border, not a small dropdown. See [home-light-product-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png).
4. **Do set the hero in Inter 56px extra-bold.** White type on the gradient, letter-spacing -1.2px. See [hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
5. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site has no toggle and no dark token set.
6. **Don't read the wordmark as blue.** The logo link computes `#0085e4`. The SVG fill is `#161637`.
7. **Don't treat the homepage hash links as tabs.** They open the demo dialog. See [home-light-demo-dialog.png](screenshots/home-light-demo-dialog.png).
8. **Don't follow Sign in or the trial CTA into the package.** Those branches stop at the wall. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
9. **Don't sample colours from the screenshots.** They are 256-colour PNGs. Read [tokens.json](tokens.json).
