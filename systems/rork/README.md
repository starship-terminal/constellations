## How the values were read

`https://rork.com/` returned HTTP 200. `https://www.rork.com/` redirects there. The T3 preview browser tools were not registered in this session (`search_tool` stayed partial, and `preview_status` was not a callable tool), so the readings come from headless Chrome (Playwright) on the public pages. Those pages loaded without a Cloudflare challenge.

Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` at 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, both at devicePixelRatio 2. `lab()` and `oklab()` colours were resolved to sRGB by filling a 1px canvas. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs at 2 times the CSS viewport. The sign-in dialog was opened and the email step was shown. No email was typed and no code was requested.

## What to distrust

The page colour is `#171717`, not pure black. Several surfaces are the same colour at an opacity (`bg-background/80`, white at 4% and 8%). Those are recorded as 8-digit hex.

Britti Sans is in the computed font stack and was not loaded (`document.fonts.check` returned false). Headlines painted with the system face ahead of it. FAQ headings name Britti Sans first, so they painted the fallback (Roboto, then Arial) in this browser.

The Sign in radius computed as `3.35544e+07px`. That is a pill. The token records `9999px`.

The App nav word is a text gradient (`#ffd230` to `#ff6900`). Its computed `color` is transparent. Do not read the word as black.

The providers screenshot and the email screenshot are the same dialog at two steps. The email field's focus ring was read, and the Sign in button's keyboard focus ring was read. Pressed, disabled, and loading were not on screen.

## Do and don't

- **Keep the page at #171717.** The body background is `rgb(23, 23, 23)`, and the text is white. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Use white pills for the primary action.** Sign in is a white pill, 36px tall, with black 14px medium type. [button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Put the prompt in a 28px card.** The composer is `#262626`, 768px wide, radius 28px. [text-field-composer-default.png](components/crops/text-field-composer-default.png)
- **Reserve the orange gradient for Rork Max.** The Max plan card runs from `#e17100` to `#ca3500`. Free and Pro sit on a 4% white wash. [card-max-default.png](components/crops/card-max-default.png)
- **Do not send the email step.** The dialog says it will email a 6-digit code. That request was not made. [login-dark-email.png](screenshots/login-dark-email.png)
