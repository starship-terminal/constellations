# On public site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-22 from the public homepage, https://www.on.com/. The storefront redirected to https://www.on.com/en-sg/. Title: "On | Swiss Performance Running Shoes & Clothing". The response was 200. This is the live On storefront, not a parked domain and not a captcha wall.

The tree was read in an incognito preview tab. Photographs were taken in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 by 900 CSS pixels and 390 by 844 CSS pixels, both at 2x. Desktop PNGs are 2880 by 1800. Phone PNGs are 780 by 1688. Each shot is the first viewport. `login-extracted` stays not-started. catalog.json was left alone.

Nothing was typed. No email, password, code, phone number, or address was entered. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. Nothing was added to the bag. Checkout was not opened. The newsletter field was not filled. The order tracker was not submitted. The referral form was not submitted.

Login and signup are one wall. Header Account, footer Become a member, and the Sign up button on `/en-sg/login` all open https://auth.on.com/ui/flow-executor/solidus-authentication-flow/. The heading is "Log in or create an account". The email field is empty. Google and Apple sit under Continue and were not clicked. The OAuth query string from the storefront redirect was not copied, and those shots were discarded. The photographs use the path with no query.
