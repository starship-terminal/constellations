# Patterns

Public Substack, signed out, 2026-11-21.

## Logged-out home

A dark page. An orange hero holds a rotating line ("Start a newsletter worth paying for") and a white pill, "Start your Substack for free". The header's primary action is an orange pill, "Start your Substack". Sign in is quiet text. Category chips sit in one scrolling row. Notes are dark cards with a public author name. [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)

## Marketing page

About is light, with an orange hero and a large serif headline. The header is a wordmark, three click menus, a search field, an orange "Start publishing" pill, and Sign in. [about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png)

## Publication post

A light reading page. The publication name is centered. Subscribe is a publication-coloured pill (gold on this post, not Substack orange). The body is Spectral at 19px. [post-light-default.png](screenshots/post-light-default.png)

## Reader shell, signed out

Explore and the technology bestseller list use a left rail (Home, Subscriptions, Chat, Activity, Explore, Profile, Create) and a login card. The list is a ranked column of public authors. [explore-dark-default.png](screenshots/explore-dark-default.png) [leaderboard-dark-default.png](screenshots/leaderboard-dark-default.png)

## Auth walls

Sign-in is a centered column on the dark page: logo, heading, email field, orange Continue, a "Sign in with password" link, and an app download card. Signup is the same column with a terms checkbox and a disabled Continue. [login-dark-default.png](screenshots/login-dark-default.png) [signup-dark-default.png](screenshots/signup-dark-default.png)

## Phone

The homepage stacks the orange hero and keeps Sign in and Get the app in the header. About swaps the menus for a menu button and keeps the orange Start publishing pill. [mobile-home-dark-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png) [mobile-about-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-about-light-default.png)
