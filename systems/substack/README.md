# Substack design reference

A token set and component reference for Substack's iOS app, the reader and writer client for Substack newsletters, notes, podcasts and chats (https://substack.com). Use it to build a native iOS reading or publishing app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-black or white page, a floating glass tab bar, one orange for the app's own actions, the system blue kept for controls, a publication's own colour on its Subscribe buttons, and post bodies set in a serif. It was captured from the shipped app on iOS 26 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account: dark (the app's Night look, with Settings > Appearance > Theme on System and the phone in dark mode) on 2026-10-23, and light (Theme set to Day, then put back to System) on 2026-10-24. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 35 captures: 19 in dark (Home, Read, Saved, the post reader, sheets, search, Podcasts, Activity, the profile, Settings and three of its pages, Chat) and 16 of the same screens in light. They show the owner's profile and email and the publications, writers and podcasts the account follows. Other people are masked with [tools/redact-rules/substack.json](../../tools/redact-rules/substack.json): subscribers' avatars, names, handles and bio on Activity (12) and the profile's subscriber count (13), the suggested people in search (08), a person named in a note and the avatar and name of the last Activity row, which show through the glass tab bar (12; painted in the gaps around the tab icons and labels, which stay; faint fragments of the first name remain behind the Home label), and chat message sender names (18). The crops cut from those screens are rebuilt from the masked copies.

This package contains no Substack code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots, the publications' artwork and the visual system they show belong to Substack Inc. and the publications ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, icons or brand orange.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do keep orange for Substack's own actions.
2. Do give a publication's own colour to its Subscribe buttons.
3. Do use the system blue for controls, never orange.
4. Don't fill destructive actions red.
5. Do set post bodies in the serif and everything else in the sans.
6. Do float the tab bar and nav controls as glass over the content.
7. Don't use pure black for text in Day.
8. Do mark unread with an orange dot at the row's leading edge.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Substack is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 267 readings of 157 named values, most in both themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a thin mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph or rim colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal; `ring`, the same from an outlined shape's outer corner | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The sans family |

Scale steps (spacing, radii) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Three screens exist in dark only (the notifications pre-prompt, search with no results, the note options sheet); the one colour read only there, the orange-tinted pre-prompt button, is filled in light from the same role on the Activity screen and says so.

## What to distrust

- **The sans.** Not determinable from pixels. It is a neo-grotesque, heavy in titles, and the measured sizes land on 28, 27, 24, 20, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13, 12 and 10 pt, but `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured.
- **The serif is named, not measured.** Display Settings lists New York (selected), Messina and Tiempos as article fonts, so `fontFamily.reader` is New York on the app's own word. Messina and Tiempos are licensed faces and are not committed.
- **The publication colour is one example.** `color.accent.publication` (#538BFA) is The System Design Newsletter's colour. Every publication's Subscribe buttons take its own.
- **Two oranges.** The flat primary reads #FF5804 in both themes, but Share on the profile reads #F5661E dark and #FE6D24 light, and the dark compose button #FF5A00. They may be the same orange through glass tinting; the tokens keep what was read.
- **Glass.** The tab bar, nav buttons, the selected top tab and the nav bar's Subscribe pill are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a material that takes colour from what is behind it. Use the system material.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: cards and buttons 12, the publication button 10, small buttons and checkboxes 7, option buttons 8.5, sheets 36. Publication avatars and post thumbnails have continuous corners that `diag` cannot read on artwork, so they have no radius token.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Light feed content differs.** Light 02 (Home), 03 (the featured card and inbox) and 11 (Podcasts) were captured later with other posts at the same layout, so readings there use only the shared chrome.
- **No pressed, off or unchecked states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** Every switch and checkbox was on as found and none was changed; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; Block and Disable all were not tapped.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; artwork and glass band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep orange for Substack's own actions.** Turn on, Subscribe back, Share, the compose button, unread dots and the Subscribe link on a note are orange; nothing else is. See [18-chat](screenshots/dark/18-chat-list-warning-banner-unread.png), [12-activity](screenshots/dark/12-activity-feed-primary-secondary-buttons.png) and [02-home](screenshots/dark/02-home-feed-notes-stories-fab-tabbar.png).
2. **Do give a publication's own colour to its Subscribe buttons.** Inside a post, the body button and the nav bar pill take the publication's colour, not Substack orange. See [05-post](screenshots/dark/05-post-reader-serif-subscribe-action-bar.png) and [05-post light](screenshots/light/05-post-reader-serif-subscribe-action-bar.png).
3. **Do use the system blue for controls, never orange.** Switches, checklist ticks and the selected option button are blue. See [17-appearance](screenshots/dark/17-appearance-switches-checklists.png) and [07-display-settings](screenshots/dark/07-display-settings-option-buttons-selected.png).
4. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Report, Mute, Block and Disable all are red text in an ordinary sheet or card, set apart by a hairline. See [06-post-options](screenshots/dark/06-post-options-sheet-destructive.png), [19-note-options](screenshots/dark/19-note-longpress-options-destructive-group.png) and [16-engagement](screenshots/dark/16-notification-checkbox-grid-destructive-button.png).
5. **Do set post bodies in the serif and everything else in the sans.** Only the reader body (and the font options that preview it) is serif; titles, notes and interface stay sans. See [05-post](screenshots/dark/05-post-reader-serif-subscribe-action-bar.png) and [07-display-settings](screenshots/dark/07-display-settings-option-buttons-selected.png).
6. **Do float the tab bar and nav controls as glass over the content.** The feed and the post scroll under a glass capsule tab bar and round glass buttons. See [02-home](screenshots/dark/02-home-feed-notes-stories-fab-tabbar.png) and [05-post](screenshots/dark/05-post-reader-serif-subscribe-action-bar.png).
7. **Don't use pure black for text in Day.** Primary text is a dark grey (#363636) on white. See [03-read light](screenshots/light/03-read-tabs-featured-card-inbox-unread.png) and [14-settings light](screenshots/light/14-settings-sheet-grouped-icons.png).
8. **Do mark unread with an orange dot at the row's leading edge.** Inbox rows and chats carry the dot outside the content column; avatars carry it at their top right. See [03-read](screenshots/dark/03-read-tabs-featured-card-inbox-unread.png) and [18-chat](screenshots/dark/18-chat-list-warning-banner-unread.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/substack/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/substack/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. Re-quantizing from the raw shots brings back the people masked in `screenshots/`: after `quantize.py`, run `python3 tools/redact_images.py apply systems/substack --rules tools/redact-rules/substack.json` from the repository root, confirm every edited file is still a 256-colour PNG with its alpha and that only pixels inside the rules' rectangles changed (the committed masks were painted that way, on each file's original palette and transparency), and only then run `tools/crops.py`. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass tab bar, nav buttons, sheets, switches and checklists rather than redrawing them; tint controls with `color.accent.control` and keep `color.accent.brand` for the app's own calls to action. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Day or Night theme.
