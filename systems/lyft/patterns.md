# Patterns

## Marketing shell

White header, pink wordmark, one purple pill, text links, then a hero split between a photograph and a display headline. A warm off-white band follows. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

The same header restyles on inner pages: the current section's links move into the bar and the pill changes ("Get a ride" on rider, "Become a driver" on driver). [screenshots/rider-light-default.png](screenshots/rider-light-default.png), [screenshots/driver-light-default.png](screenshots/driver-light-default.png).

## Phone shell

The header collapses to the wordmark and a menu button. The drawer lists Rider, Driver, Business, and Log in. [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png), [screenshots/mobile-rider-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-rider-light-default.png).

## Cities

A search field and a country select over a list of city links. Rider cities lead with a photo hero. Driver cities lead with the search. [screenshots/cities-light-default.png](screenshots/cities-light-default.png), [screenshots/driver-cities-light-default.png](screenshots/driver-cities-light-default.png). Search was not submitted.

## Help

Public help center: search, article cards, category tiles. [screenshots/help-light-default.png](screenshots/help-light-default.png). A region dialog and a "log in to see your rides" toast were on the live page and are not in the kept shot.

## Auth walls

Login is a centered card on a pink wash: empty phone field, consent copy, an unchecked "Keep me signed in", and "Continue with Phone". [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

Driver apply is a marketing page whose first card is an empty phone form and a Submit button. [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png). Submit was not clicked. The header Sign up deep link was not opened, so there is no separate rider signup page in this package.

## Stops

No ride request, no checkout, no identity provider, no typed phone or email. Business and safety are public marketing pages. [screenshots/business-light-default.png](screenshots/business-light-default.png), [screenshots/safety-light-default.png](screenshots/safety-light-default.png).
