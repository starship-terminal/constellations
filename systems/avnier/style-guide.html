Palette

The storefront is achromatic — black ink on white paper, a #f3f3f3 wash on alternate sections — then exactly two hues: the #faf6b5 predictive-search highlight and the #f4ff7a yellow a press wipes across the action buttons. Swatches list the declared var each alias resolves through.

Type

Space Mono (400) everywhere — body, nav, fields, buttons, prices at 12–18px — and the licensed avnier-font (500) on titles and product names at 15–220px, tracked 3% at display size. avnier-font is not loaded here, so the named stacks below are the token values, not the rendered face.

Spacing and radius

Corners are the signature: square (0px) on every button, pill, field and tile. No shadows anywhere but the one-pixel field ring; depth comes from fills alone.

Shadows

None measured — the shop is flat. (The list below stays empty unless a future run finds one.)

Measured extras

Colours no variable declares — the #7a7a7a product-count grey, the rgba(0,0,0,.75) dimmed labels, the rgba(0,0,0,.55) field ring — plus the measured component dimensions.
