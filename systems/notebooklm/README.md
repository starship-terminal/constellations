# Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) design reference

A token set, component reference and pattern notes for Google's Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM), for building mockups that match the product. Recaptured from notebook.google.com on 2026-10-17 while signed in. A public signed-out pass on 2026-11-22 mapped the marketing page and the empty login wall ([site-tree.md](site-tree.md)). catalog.json was left alone., in the UI generation Google ships as "luminous": near-black and off-white surfaces, one variable family (Google Sans Flex) and pill buttons. The 2026-09-25 capture of the earlier lavender UI is replaced, not merged; the product no longer paints those values. One value, `blur.panel-glass`, is carried over from it (see What to distrust).

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export notebooklm` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do separate regions by fill, not shadow.
2. Do keep one accent-filled button per screen.
3. Do show selection with a quiet grey fill.
4. Don't colour destructive actions red.
5. Do draw keyboard focus as a thick outline around the whole pill.
6. Don't put colour in the chrome.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The signed-in pages were copied in the built-in browser with [snapshot.js](tools/capture/signed-in/snapshot.js): the DOM with every same-origin stylesheet inlined, scripts removed, the account photo replaced by a grey disc, and every notebook, source, output and chat text replaced by "Sample ..." before the copy left the browser. [unsnap.py](tools/capture/signed-in/unsnap.py) then applied the account's literal names and ids from a redaction file kept outside the repository, and refused any copy where one survived. 30 copies were taken, all in dark; the light theme is the same copy with `body.dark-theme` swapped for `light-theme`, which is what the in-app Light mode does (the product's own variables are `light-dark()` pairs and Material's are scoped to `.dark-theme`).

Everything else ran headless on the copies, with fonts served from a local cache and nothing requested from notebook.google.com:

- [read-css.mjs](tools/capture/read-css.mjs) resolved every `--nlm-*`, `--nb-*` and `--mat-sys-*` variable on `<body>` in both themes (colours through a probe element), and listed media queries, reduced-motion rules, transitions, z-index, grid columns at four widths and colour usage counts.
- [measure.mjs](tools/capture/signed-in/measure.mjs) found 64 components by role and class and read their computed styles in 336 states: hover with the real pointer, focus-visible with real keyboard focus, pressed with the mouse held down, and selected, open, typed, error and disabled from copies captured in those states. Each state of 47 components was cropped (302 crops); the other 17 (the large and announcement dialogs, the scrim, the avatar, the header, the three panels and the nine type samples) were measured without a crop.
- [build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) maps the readings into tokens. Labels: **Measured** is a declared variable or a computed style; **Derived** is worked out from measured values (the accent hover and pressed colours composite the measured state layer at its measured opacity; the neutral ramp orders measured greys by CIELAB L*; the tonal ramps fill unpainted tones with Material's generator, see below). Nothing is sampled from a screenshot.
- [tonal-ramps.mjs](tools/capture/tonal-ramps.mjs) builds the ten hue ramps the product does not declare, into [raw/tonal-ramps.json](raw/tonal-ramps.json). Each is the Material tonal palette (13 tones, 0 to 100) of a measured seed, built with Material's own colour utilities. A tone the product paints keeps the measured value; the rest are generated and labelled Derived.

The brand evidence (`<meta name="theme-color">` is black in light, white in dark; the manifest names the app "Gemini Notebook") is in [raw/brand.json](raw/brand.json).

## What to distrust

- **Two UI generations live in the CSS.** The legacy `--nlm-*` palette (lavender page `#edeffa`, blue `#4259ff`) is still declared and still paints a few things: the dialog Save button, links, the checked toggle chip, and the 3px focus ring of checkboxes, toggle chips and menu items (`border.focus-strong`: lavender `#c3cafc` in dark, `#4259ff` in light). Everything else is the newer `--nb-sys-*` set. Tokens named `legacy-*` are declared but no longer painted.
- **`--nlm-green-0` to `-40` and `-90` to `-99` are declared as `undefined`.** Only five brand-green steps exist, and their names are not tones (`-50` is tone 83), so `palette.green` stays as declared; the green hue's full ramp is `green-status`.
- **63 of the 137 tonal-ramp steps are generated, not read.** The product declares no ramps beyond grey, blue and green; its other hues are role colours on Material tones (40, 80, 90, 30, 100, 20). `red`, `orange`, `amber`, `yellow`, `green-status`, `cyan`, `sky`, `info`, `purple` and `pink` keep 74 measured colours (at their tone, or as an extra step named by their own tone when they sit between two, such as `info.46` for the blue Studio accent) and fill the other tones from Material's generator, seeded with the measured hue. As a check, of the 80 measured colours that sit on a standard tone, the generator reproduces 44 within 10 RGB units and 59 within 25; the misses are the luminous `nb-sys` colours and the Studio accents, which Google did not take from Material's palettes (code yellow and the dark positive green are the furthest off). 16 measured colours sit in no ramp, listed under `notInRamp` in [raw/tonal-ramps.json](raw/tonal-ramps.json). 13 lose their tone to another measured colour: 6 are near copies (Material error and the legacy red beside Material red), but the other 7 (code yellow, the dark warm Studio accent, the dark Studio green and teal, the dark warning text, and the dark accent navy and its container) are distinct colours. The other 3 (the dark yellow tile text, the dark positive container, the light error container) are off every ramp's hue.
- **The luminous accent changes hue between themes.** It is sky (hue 244) in light and navy (hue 276) in dark. The light values are `palette.sky`, which has only 2 measured steps; of the dark navy, only the surface accent is a step (`info.14`), and the accent fill and its container are under `notInRamp`.
- **The scrim** is 32% black in both themes as painted, although `--nlm-fills-scrim` still says 60% in dark.
- **Success, warning and info** colours are the declared variables; no such message appeared, so their use as fill, text and border is the product's naming, not an observed screen. Danger was observed (the "Blocked by browser" chip and the URL field error).
- **Studio outputs** (audio and video players, mind map, flashcards, quiz, reports) were not opened; they render in other views and iframes. Their tile colours (`studio.*`, `tile.*`) are declared variables.
- **The list-view table header** read "Sample notebook 1" in its copy because the title-column scrub caught the header cell too; that one cell was set back to "Title" (the live wording) by hand before rendering.
- **The tooltip's text** is scrubbed in every image, since tooltips repeat titles; the measured copy reads "Sample text 1". The live tooltip read "Audio Overview - Generate an AI podcast based on your sources".
- **Hover is not measured at 390.** The panel tabs there have focus-visible, pressed and selected readings; the notebook row at 390 is measured at rest only.
- **`blur.panel-glass` (14px) is not from this capture.** It is carried over from the 2026-09-25 read; no file in `raw/` holds it, and none of the 336 measured states has a backdrop filter.
- **The switch** is the only one in the product and is disabled (the browser blocks notifications), so no enabled or on state was seen.

## Colors

The luminous UI is nearly monochrome. Dark: page `#0f0f0f` (`bg.page`), cards and selected pills `#171717` (`surface.card`, `surface.selected`), panels, menus and dialog bodies `#1f1f1f` (`surface.panel`, `surface.overlay`, `surface.raised`), text `#e6e6e6` and 55% white (`text.primary`, `text.secondary`). Light: `#faf9f9`, `#f2f0f0`, `#ffffff`, black and 55% black. The one accent fill is the sky/navy pair on New notebook (`accent.default`: `#9dd2ff` light, `#1f3b9b` dark); dialog confirms and links still use the older brand blue `#4259ff` (`accent.brand`, `text.link`) in both themes. Borders are translucent on-surface (`border.default`: 8% black, 12% white). Colour otherwise comes from content: red PDF icons and one accent per Studio output type (`studio.*`).

## Layout and geometry

Spacing is `--nb-sys-spacing`: 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28px (`spacing.*`), plus container paddings of 16, 24 and 32 and two 40px gaps (`spacing.widget-gap`, `spacing.space-above`). Radii are the `--nb-sys-shape` scale (4 to 56px and full), and the product leans on large ones: pills for every button (`radius.button`), 40px cards, 32px panels and composer, 28px dialogs, 20px menus. Controls are 36px (header, small) or 48px (dialog actions); dialog buttons from Material are 40px. Breakpoints: the product's most used is `599.98px` (15 rules); the other width queries have one to three rules each, and the tokens keep 359.98 (3), 600-959.98 (1), 1024 (2) and 1280 (1); the notebook grid is `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(var(--project-card-min-width), 1fr))`, five columns at 1440, three at 1024, two at 800. Every overlay (dialog, menu, select, tooltip, toast) mounts in one container at z-index 1000; the Google bar sits at 999 and card actions at 10.

Elevation is three tied levels plus none: cards, panels and dialogs have no shadow (`shadow.none`); menus, the composer and the Studio banner share `shadow.level1` (a 20px blur at 28% black in dark, 4% in light); the toast uses Material's level 3 (`shadow.toast`, the same as `core.shadow.m3-level3`), and the select panel a lighter three-layer Material shadow that matches none of `core.shadow` (`shadow.select`). `shadow.level2` is declared but no captured component used it. Focus is a 3px outline in `border.focus` 2px outside each pill (`focusRing.*`); Material controls (checkbox, toggle chips, menu items) draw a 3px ring in `border.focus-strong` instead, and outlined fields thicken their outline to 2px. Motion is Material's: 150-500ms with the standard curve `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` the most common cubic-bezier (34 declarations; the plain `ease` keyword has 35); under reduced motion the backdrop fade drops to 1ms and the segmented slider, search field and card entrance stop animating (`core.reducedMotion`).

## Do and don't

1. **Do separate regions by fill, not shadow.** Cards (`surface.card`), panels (`surface.panel`) and dialogs sit on `bg.page` with no border and no shadow; only floating things (menus, the composer, the Studio banner) cast `shadow.level1`. See [home-dark](screenshots/home-dark.png) and [notebook-dark](screenshots/notebook-dark.png).
2. **Do keep one accent-filled button per screen.** On home only New notebook is filled (`accent.default`); tabs, Add sources and Create notebook are grey tonal or text pills. Dialog confirms are the one exception and use the older brand blue. See [home-light](screenshots/home-light.png) and [notebook-dark-configure-chat](screenshots/notebook-dark-configure-chat.png).
3. **Do show selection with a quiet grey fill.** The selected header tab uses `surface.selected`, and the active 390 tab is a `surface.panel` pill on a `surface.card` track ([raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md)), not the accent; only Configure Chat's toggle chips fill blue with a leading check. See [tab-selected](components/crops/tab-selected.png) and [radio-selected](components/crops/radio-selected.png).
4. **Don't colour destructive actions red.** Delete in the confirmation is the same grey tonal pill as any other action, beside a text Cancel, and it takes focus. See [home-dark-delete-confirm](screenshots/home-dark-delete-confirm.png).
5. **Do draw keyboard focus as a thick outline around the whole pill.** A 3px `border.focus` outline 2px outside the control, following its radius; fields thicken their own outline instead. See [button-accent-focus-visible](components/crops/button-accent-focus-visible.png) and [text-area-outlined-focus-visible](components/crops/text-area-outlined-focus-visible.png).
6. **Don't put colour in the chrome.** Header, panel and menu icons are `text.primary`; colour is kept for content type: red PDF icons on sources and one accent per Studio output type. See [notebook-dark](screenshots/notebook-dark.png) and [mobile-notebook-dark-studio](screenshots/mobile-notebook-dark-studio.png).
