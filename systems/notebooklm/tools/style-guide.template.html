Primitive palettes

grey and blue are the ramps the product declares (--nlm-grey-*, --nlm-blue-*); green is declared with only five steps. neutral gathers every grey the luminous UI declares, ordered by lightness (step = CIELAB L*). red, orange, amber, yellow, green-status, cyan, sky, info, purple and pink are Material tonal palettes (step = Material tone) of a measured seed: steps the product paints show the measured value, steps marked derived are generated by Material's colour utilities.

Typography

One variable family, Google Sans Flex (OFL), for every role, tuned per role with its wdth, opsz and ROND axes and non-round weights (280 to 540); Google Sans Code (OFL) for code and the extended caption; Luminous Symbols for icons; KaTeX for maths. Fallback stacks are the product's own.
