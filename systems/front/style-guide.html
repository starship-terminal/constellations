Front, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. The marketing site's layer is compiled into classes — the hue-utility palette (core.palette.*: violet 50-1000, citron, blue, gray, green, pink, salmon, orange, yellow), the type ramp (core.font.size.*: display1 62px to micro3), the radius and motion scales — and its three section themes ship as the violet, light and gray role tables (surface-l0..l3, text-primary/secondary, the button-* state layer). The product's real token layer is Froyo: the app signin shell's resolved --color-* semantics (app/dark sets, 206 roles over the core.primitive.* ramps) and the full Froyo build the Companion messenger embeds (core.froyo.* scales + the companion/companionDark --colors-* semantics). help.front.com's Knowledge Base vars are the help set. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from front.com, app.frontapp.com, help.front.com and dev.frontapp.com, signed out, in headless Chromium. The workspace and every signed-in product surface is a documented boundary; the demo, signup, auth and search fields were typed into and cleared, never submitted. Suisse Intl, Suisse Intl Mono, Suisse Works and the app's Indivisible are served faces and are not loaded here: samples fall back to system fonts.

Palette

Three honest layers: core.palette.* is the marketing site's class-declared hue ramp; core.primitive.* is the app shell's resolved --color-primitive-* ramps; core.froyo.ramp.* is the Froyo sheet's light/dark/alpha values. The violet/light/gray sets are the marketing section roles, app/dark the signin shell's resolved semantics, companion/companionDark the messenger's, help the Knowledge Base's.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

The marketing radius scale runs 4-40px (.rounded-*); the motion layer is .duration-100..1000 over the .ease-appear curve (0.16,1,0.3,1). The Froyo scales (core.froyo.*) carry the product's spacing/sizes/radii/easings/durations/breakpoints.

Type

Three served Suisse faces on the marketing site: Suisse Intl carries every heading, control and body; Suisse Intl Mono draws figures and code; Suisse Works is the serif display face. The app shell sets "Indivisible" with Inter fallbacks. The type ramp pairs each role with its size, line-height, tracking and weight. Sizes are the declared steps; samples fall back to system sans.
