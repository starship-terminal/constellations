# Kitchen.co design reference

A token set and component reference for Kitchen.co's public marketing site and its signed-in workspace. Kitchen.co is client-portal software for agencies: conversations, tasks, invoices and deliverables in one workspace. This package was read from kitchen.co on 2026-11-18, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Public pages were signed out. The workspace was captured on a free account after email signup. It is not Kitchen Stories (kitchenstories.com).

Public pages were refreshed on 2026-11-21, signed out, and stopped at the login wall. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) maps the homepage controls. [login-review.json](login-review.json) records the walls. The signed-in workspace shots from 2026-11-18 were left as they were.

Captured public pages: home (Solutions menu, hero Conversations tab, cookie settings), features, pricing, buy, reviews, tutorials, integrations, how it works, benefits, about, our story, blog, affiliate program, the four Solutions pages, terms, privacy, DPA, and the cookie policy. Walls, photographed at rest and not submitted: login workspace, find your workspace, get started, get a demo. Phone width on marketing: home (including the open menu), pricing, features, login and reviews.

Captured workspace pages: Home empty, Inbox chats empty, Proposals (DocuMocu dialog, not connected), Clients empty, Tasks table empty, Library tree, More menu, Settings general / members / billing / theme, My Tasks empty, and create-workspace. Phone width is a separate client portal: Home, Inbox, Clients, Profile. Invites, creates, Upgrade, Buy Lifetime Access, DocuMocu connect/buy and Stripe checkout were not submitted.

The marketing site paints one theme: a black promo bar, a white page, Graphik throughout, ink buttons, and one coral action (`#ed553b`). The workspace is a different system: system UI, a `--k-*` token layer, and blue `#0165e1` as the accent. Both surfaces are light-only.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page white and the action coral.
2. Do put the campaign in the black bar.
3. Do use Graphik at 700 for headlines and buttons.
4. Do round controls by 5px.
5. Do style the workspace with system UI and blue `#0165e1`.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.
7. Don't follow Upgrade, Buy Lifetime or DocuMocu.
8. Don't treat the promo copy as permanent.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium (Playwright) opened each public URL. Cookie and consent overlays were hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked. Computed styles were read from the live elements and stored in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Motion was read by [tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs](tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs) on home, pricing and features. The stylesheet `main.css` declares three custom properties (`--about`, `--dark`, `--white`) and the Graphik `@font-face` rules.

The workspace was captured from a persistent Chromium profile after email signup. Account strings were replaced in the live DOM before screenshots. Workspace values come from declared `--k-*` custom properties ([raw/app-vars.json](raw/app-vars.json), 3258 `--k-*` names on the page; primitives only are stored) and from computed styles on rail, jumbo cards, primary buttons, tabs and the billing Upgrade control ([raw/app-computed.json](raw/app-computed.json)).

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json). The promo bar is a campaign strip (Black Friday, code BF20 on this capture date) and will change. Graphik is a commercial face and is not in this package. The 128 px icon is the 32 px favicon scaled up. Hover and pressed on marketing links often share the resting colour. The workspace empty states are a new free account; they are not a designed "zero data" marketing mock. Theme customisation is behind Upgrade.

Seven marketing values were typed in by hand from `main.css` at capture time and no file in raw/ holds them, so tokens.json labels them Reconstruction: `light.background.page` `#ffffff`, `light.background.sand` `#f8f6f4`, `light.text.secondary` `#707070`, `light.text.promo-gold` `#ffdc4d`, `light.border.stroke` `#e5e5e5`, `light.border.field` `#dcdcdc`, and `core.font.weight.black` 900 (Graphik Black is declared in `@font-face`; no element paints it).

## Fonts

Graphik (Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold, Black, plus italics) is served from `/assets/site/fonts/` on the marketing site. It is commercial and is not committed. Marketing tokens fall back to Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif. The workspace uses the system UI stack (`-apple-system`, system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto).

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page white and the action coral.** `#ed553b` is the one warm fill. Ink buttons stay `#1d1c1d`. See [cta-coral](components/crops/cta-coral-default.png).
2. **Do put the campaign in the black bar.** Offers sit in the 56px strip, with gold `#ffdc4d` on the offer words. See [promo-bar](components/crops/promo-bar-default.png).
3. **Do use Graphik at 700 for headlines and buttons.** Body stays 400. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
4. **Do round controls by 5px.** Buttons, fields and the Continue action share that radius. See [login-field](components/crops/login-field-default.png).
5. **Do style the workspace with system UI and blue `#0165e1`.** Marketing Graphik and coral stop at the login gate. See [app-home-light-default](screenshots/app-home-light-default.png).
6. **Don't invent a dark theme.** Theme & Colors is an Upgrade wall. The promo bar is a dark strip inside the one light theme. See [app-theme-light-default](screenshots/app-theme-light-default.png).
7. **Don't follow Upgrade, Buy Lifetime or DocuMocu.** Billing and the proposals dialog were captured closed. See [app-settings-billing-light-default](screenshots/app-settings-billing-light-default.png).
8. **Don't treat the promo copy as permanent.** The Black Friday strip is what this capture was served.
