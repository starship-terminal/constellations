# Kitchen.co design reference

A token set and component reference for Kitchen.co's public marketing site. Kitchen.co is client-portal software for agencies: conversations, tasks, invoices and deliverables in one workspace. This package was read from kitchen.co on 2026-11-18, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. It is not Kitchen Stories (kitchenstories.com).

Captured pages: home, features, pricing, buy, reviews, tutorials, integrations, how it works, about, blog, the login workspace gate, get started, get a demo, and the cookie policy. Phone width: home (including the open menu), pricing, features, login and reviews.

The site paints one theme. A black promo bar, a white page, Graphik throughout, ink buttons, and one coral action (`#ed553b`). The signed-in workspace, signup submit, demo submit and Stripe checkout were not entered.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page white and the action coral.
2. Do put the campaign in the black bar.
3. Do use Graphik at 700 for headlines and buttons.
4. Do round controls by 5px.
5. Don't style the workspace from this package.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.
7. Don't follow Pay Now.
8. Don't treat the promo copy as permanent.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium (Playwright) opened each public URL. Cookie and consent overlays were hidden with CSS. No consent button was clicked. Computed styles were read from the live elements and stored in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Motion was read by [tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs](tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs) on home, pricing and features. The stylesheet `main.css` declares three custom properties (`--about`, `--dark`, `--white`) and the Graphik `@font-face` rules. Colours and sizes in the tokens are computed values, not a declared ramp.

## What to distrust

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json). The promo bar is a campaign strip (Black Friday, code BF20 on this capture date) and will change. Graphik is a commercial face and is not in this package. The 128 px icon is the 32 px favicon scaled up. Hover and pressed on links often share the resting colour. The site does not restyle those states.

## Fonts

Graphik (Regular, Medium, Semibold, Bold, Black, plus italics) is served from `/assets/site/fonts/`. It is commercial and is not committed. Tokens fall back to Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page white and the action coral.** `#ed553b` is the one warm fill. Ink buttons stay `#1d1c1d`. See [cta-coral](components/crops/cta-coral-default.png).
2. **Do put the campaign in the black bar.** Offers sit in the 56px strip, with gold `#ffdc4d` on the offer words. See [promo-bar](components/crops/promo-bar-default.png).
3. **Do use Graphik at 700 for headlines and buttons.** Body stays 400. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
4. **Do round controls by 5px.** Buttons, fields and the Continue action share that radius. See [login-field](components/crops/login-field-default.png).
5. **Don't style the workspace from this package.** `*.kitchen.co` after Continue was never opened.
6. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The promo bar is a dark strip inside the one light theme.
7. **Don't follow Pay Now.** Those links leave for Stripe checkout. They were measured as destinations only.
8. **Don't treat the promo copy as permanent.** The Black Friday strip is what this capture was served.
