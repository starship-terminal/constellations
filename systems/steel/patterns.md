Public pages only. No screenshots: the preview browser did not produce frames.

## Marketing shell

https://steel.dev/ uses `color-scheme: dark`. The page background is `#141414`. A 36px announcement bar sits on `#05a5ff`. The header is 72px tall and transparent, with mono 12px links and two 32px buttons: Contact sales on a dark face, Sign in on white.

The hero band is `#99e1e0` (`--hero-bg`), 366px tall at 1169px width. The h1 is 60px schengenA, line-height 60.6px, letter-spacing -1.2px, colour `rgb(33, 32, 28)`. The primary action is "START FOR FREE", 36px, radius 6px, near-white face, near-black text.

Later bands change the page colour: a use-case section on `rgb(199, 153, 212)` with ink `rgb(76, 26, 93)`, a testimonial section on `rgb(254, 137, 178)` with ink `rgb(82, 0, 0)`, and a closing section on white. Those are page bands, not a second theme.
