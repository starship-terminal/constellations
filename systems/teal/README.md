# Teal design reference

A token set and component reference for Teal's public marketing site and its signed-out sign-in and sign-up pages. Read from **tealhq.com** and **app.tealhq.com** on **2026-11-19**, signed out. The preview viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2. Captured by computed style: `/`, `/pricing`, `/tool/resume-checker`, `app.tealhq.com/sign-in`, and `app.tealhq.com/sign-up`. Nothing was submitted.

Teal's marketing page is white, set in Roobert, with a goldfinch yellow pill button (`#f5b501`) and a deep teal (`#005149`) used for bands, hover text and feature cards. The signed-out app is a different sheet: Inter at 14px, a 36px field with a 3px radius, and a 40px yellow pill. Those two sheets do not share one computed theme.

### Principles

1. Paint the marketing action goldfinch yellow.
2. Keep Log in as a white pill with a black border.
3. Set marketing display type in Roobert.
4. Use the bird colours for bands, not as a second button.
5. Leave sign-in at the empty fields.
6. Do not treat the marketing sheet and the app sheet as one theme.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties and computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` in the T3 preview browser on the attached page. Marketing tokens come from `https://www.tealhq.com/` (`_astro/webflow-thq-layered.*.css` and the home stylesheet), confirmed on `/pricing` and `/tool/resume-checker`. App tokens come from `https://app.tealhq.com/sign-in` (`assets/main-*.css`) and the same sheet on `/sign-up`. Hex declarations were kept. `lab()` duplicates on the marketing sheet were not copied as separate colours.

The email and password fields were left empty. Sign-up was not submitted. LinkedIn was not opened. No card, phone number or company SSO was entered.

The T3 preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on snapshot. Headless Playwright against `https://www.tealhq.com/` and `https://app.tealhq.com/sign-in` received a Cloudflare challenge (403). Those pages were already loaded in the preview browser, so the computed readings stand. `https://www.tealhq.com/privacy-policy` loaded in headless Chromium without a challenge, at 1440 by 900, devicePixelRatio 2. That PNG is [screenshots/privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png). It is the privacy page, not the home page.

## What to distrust

- **There is no dark theme on these pages.** Do not invent one from the marketing bird palette.
- **37 values were typed in and not saved to raw/.** [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) records the main computed readings and part of the declared palette. The other declared custom properties (the secondary bird colours, the hover fills, the mono stack, three weights, the tracking, easing and duration tokens, the app radius and shadow) and some computed sizes (display, price and legal type, footer and testimonial radii, the sign-in form width, the resume-checker CTA height) are not in it. Those 37 tokens are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time; the list is `HAND` in [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py).
- **Marketing yellow and app yellow are two readings.** `#f5b501` is the marketing pill. The sign-in button computed `rgb(244, 179, 1)`, which is the app token `--color-accent-500` (`#f4b301`).
- **App brand `#005249` and marketing teal `#005149` differ by one channel.** Both were read. Do not collapse them.
- **Hover colours are declarations.** Header links declare `hover:text-[#005149]`. Sign up declares `hover:brightness-95`. Those were not a live pointer hover, because the preview snapshot could not drive a visible hover frame.
- **Keyboard focus painted a ring on the email field** (`box-shadow` with `#005249` and a 25% teal halo). Header links reported `outline-style: none` at rest. A focus-visible rule was found on `.ch-post-body .ch-resume-disclosure > summary` (2px solid `#005149`, offset 2px). That control was not on the home page.
- **`oklch` and `lab` values are stylesheet output.** The sign-up legal line computed `oklch(0.707 0.022 261.325)`. It is stored as that string.
- **The viewport was 1169 by 731**, the preview fill size. It is not a 1440 or 390 capture.
- **Fonts are not shipped.** Roobert loads on the marketing site. Inter loads from Google Fonts on the app. The style guide falls back if those families are absent.
- **Untitled UI, Relume and Webflow library variables** are on the marketing root (`--untitled-ui-*`, `--relume-library-lite-*`, `--elements-webflow-library-*`). They are a kit leftover. They are not Teal's bird palette and are not in `tokens.json`.

## Fonts

- **Roobert** is the marketing face, computed on body, headings and buttons. Weights 300, 400 and 600 loaded. The nav also lists Poppins in the stack.
- **Inter** is the signed-out app face, with `system-ui, sans-serif`. Body is 14px / 20px.
- **webflow-icons** loaded on the marketing page as an icon font. The header caret measured here is an inline SVG, not that font.

## Do and don't

1. **Paint the marketing action goldfinch yellow.** The primary control is `#f5b501` with black text, 53px tall, padding 16px 32px, radius 100px, Roobert 20px weight 600, tracking -0.4px.
2. **Keep Log in as a white pill with a black border.** Header Log in is 43px, padding 10px 24px, 1px `#2D2D2D`, text `#2D2D2D`, 15px weight 500.
3. **Set marketing display type in Roobert.** The home h1 is 64px / 64px, weight 600, tracking -2px, colour `#333`. Body is 16px / 20.8px.
4. **Use the bird colours for bands, not as a second button.** Feature panels are `#5a6fec`. The footer is pheasant `#791e3f` with a 72px top-left radius. Teal `#005149` is the band and the declared nav hover.
5. **Leave sign-in at the empty fields.** The app field is 36px, radius 3px, 0.5px `#cccccc` border, Inter 14px. Focus draws a `#005249` ring. Sign in is `#f4b301`, 40px, radius 24px. Do not submit it from this reference.
6. **Do not treat the marketing sheet and the app sheet as one theme.** Roobert and `#f5b501` are the site. Inter and `#f4b301` / `#005249` are the app.
