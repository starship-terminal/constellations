# Navia

Navia is a marketing-plan product for small businesses. This package captures the public signed-out site at [www.thenavia.com](https://www.thenavia.com/) and the signed-out gate at [app.thenavia.com](https://app.thenavia.com/). `thenavia.com` redirects to `www.thenavia.com`. Captured 2026-11-19, signed out, light only, in the T3 preview browser at innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731, device pixel ratio 2.

No account was created. The contact form asks for a phone number, so it was not filled. Sign In and Sign Up on the app gate were not pressed.

### Principles

1. Use the cream page and the ink text.
2. Use yellow for the accent and orange for the action.
3. Keep buttons as pills.
4. Set headings in Lora at weight 400.
5. Leave the phone field alone.

## How the values were read

Computed styles and stylesheet custom properties were read in the T3 preview browser on the live page. The marketing sheet is `navia-7b57fe.webflow.shared.49a8c1270.min.css` on the Webflow CDN. ## What to distrust

The viewport was 1169 CSS pixels, not 1440 or 390. Root font-size there was 15.436px, so a computed 54.026px is 3.5rem, not a fixed pixel size. Token sizes are the authored rem values.

The white Book a demo button computed white text on a white face. That is what the page returned on the hero. It may be a label that sits on a photo the section background did not report.

App colours that were declared and not painted on the gate (purple steps, orange press `#e5703c`, radius-brand 18px) are in the tokens and marked as declared. The marketing focus outline computed to none. The app gate focus outline is the one that showed.

The app stylesheet has a `.dark` block with a blue primary. The gate did not apply it, so it is not a Navia theme in this package.

## Fonts

Public Sans is the body, loaded as `Publicsans Variablefont Wght` on the marketing site and as Public Sans on the app gate. Lora is the heading, loaded as `Lora Variablefont Wght` on the marketing site. Notemakersmark is the handwriting face, used for the yellow script line and the plan names. Adobe Typekit also loads meno-banner. It was not the computed family of any measured element, so it is not a token. The font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the cream page and the ink text.** The body is `#faf4e6` with `#36312a` text on every public page that was opened.
- **Use yellow for the accent and orange for the action.** The nav pill and `--color-scheme-1--accent` are `#e8ff39`. The default button and the article link are `#f88450`.
- **Keep buttons as pills.** Marketing radius is `4rem`. The app gate uses 25px, which is `--radius-button`, not the marketing pill.
- **Set headings in Lora at weight 400.** The marketing h1 is 3.5rem, line-height 1.1. Do not bold them.
- **Leave the phone field alone.** Contact asks for a phone number. This capture did not enter one.
