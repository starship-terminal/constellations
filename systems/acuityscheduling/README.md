# Acuity Scheduling design reference

A token set and component reference for Acuity Scheduling's public marketing site, for building a booking-software marketing page in the style of Squarespace's scheduling product. It was read from acuityscheduling.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. The look is warm and soft: a pastel hero gradient, Clarkson Serif headlines in navy `#283849`, 12px-radius ink buttons, and pastel card fills on white.

The site tree is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md): which control opens which page, menu, or wall. The scheduling product sits behind a Squarespace account and was never entered. Login (`/login.php`) and signup (`/get-started`, which redirects to login.squarespace.com) were recorded as walls and not filled. Nothing was signed into, bought, or submitted. Consent SDK requests were aborted before a banner could mount.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export acuityscheduling` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let one ink do all the work.
2. Do keep the 12px corner.
3. Do pair Clarkson Serif headlines with Clarkson UI.
4. Don't give links a link colour.
5. Don't invent fills for hover.
6. Do keep the three focus rings.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

acuityscheduling.com is Squarespace's scheduling marketing site: server-rendered pages with BEM-ish class names (`menu-container`, `dropdown__`, `frontsite-footer`, `phase2-plan-*`). Its stylesheet declares almost no design-system custom properties. [vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) was run again on 2026-11-21. The cdn-marketing stylesheet denies CSSOM reads but serves CORS `*`, so its rules were refetched. It found carousel frame dimensions and the `--c1/--c2/--c3` hero-gradient variants, recorded in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json), not tokenised. Every token is a literal measured off a real element's computed style. Each leaf's `$extensions.source` names where it was read.

On 2026-11-21 the visible controls were measured again in Playwright at 1440×900, deviceScaleFactor 2: header Get started, How It Works, the Solutions trigger and a menu row, the hero title and the two ghost CTAs, the pricing segment, plan name and plan button, a FAQ title, the login email field (focused empty, never typed), and the enterprise lead field and submit (never filled). Hover used the pointer. Pressed on the nav pill used `CSS.forcePseudoState`. Those readings are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). They match the 2026-11-09 token values (ink `#283849`, nav hover `#3e5772`, plan hover `#1e2d3a`, 12px radius, Clarkson / Clarkson Serif), so [tokens.json](tokens.json) was not rewritten. Unread roles stay Not captured.

The only presses were local UI: the Solutions hover, the pricing monthly segment, one FAQ accordion, the enterprise benefit switch, a logo-carousel arrow, and the phone hamburger. Login and signup walls were photographed and not filled. Google, Apple, Facebook, and Squarespace were not clicked. The enterprise lead form was not submitted.

Consent SDK requests were aborted, so no consent banner mounted. Screenshots were scrolled to settle lazy sections, then quantised to 256 colours. Unquantised originals stay outside the repo at `~/.design-systems/acuityscheduling/raw-shots/`.

## What to distrust

- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** The pastel hero gradient and photography band slightly; read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw files.
- **The home hero repaints per load.** Nine `--c1/--c2/--c3` gradient variants rotate (raw/css-vars.json); the tokens record the variant live during the capture, and the sampled pixels differ from the declared stops because the gradient sits under a white ::after wash.
- **The pricing hero's dark band is a photograph** (`pricing-background.webp`), not a flat fill — its pixels (~#334e53 teal) are image content, not a palette value.
- **`social-link`'s and the wordmark's `rgb(0, 0, 238)` and `rgb(255, 0, 0)` are UA defaults**: those anchors carry no `color` of their own, so rest shows the browser's link blue and a forced `:active` shows its red. Measurement artifacts, not design values.
- **The two ink buttons move in opposite directions on hover**: the header pill lightens to `#3e5772`, the plan button darkens to `#1e2d3a`. Both are measured, verified on the crop pairs.
- **`plan-card` itself paints transparent**: the white hairline-bordered card is its `.plan-wrapper` sibling (see its Child layers).
- **The gate dialog's `Times` font reading** is the browser default on a container that sets no family; its children all measure Clarkson.
- **The testimonial carousel auto-plays**, so two captures of the same section can differ in the slide shown; `testimonial-card` is measured on its first slide.
- **The Squarespace signup gate was not opened on 2026-11-21.** Tokens that cite `gate-*` or `oauth-*` crops come from the 2026-11-09 readings in [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json). Those crops are not in this package. The 2026-11-21 readings that were re-checked (ink, 12px radius, Clarkson, nav and plan hovers) match.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let one ink do all the work.** `#283849` is the only action colour: every primary button, the serif headlines, the ghost CTAs' 1px rules, the FAQ band and the support card. There is no bright accent anywhere. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [nav-cta-default](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png).
2. **Do keep the 12px corner.** Buttons, plan cards and CTAs share the 12px radius; cards step to 16px, panels and the testimonial to 22–24px, and the pricing toggle is a full pill. The gate's square black-ruled rows are the exception — they belong to Squarespace's shared auth surface. See [plan-cta-default](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png) and [plan-card-default](components/crops/plan-card-default.png).
3. **Do pair Clarkson Serif headlines with Clarkson UI.** Serif at 500 sets hero, plan and card headlines; the sans sets everything else at 14/22 or 16/22, mostly regular. The only caps are the gate's 12px uppercase labels tracked 0.5px. See [hero-title-default](components/crops/hero-title-default.png) and [plan-name-default](components/crops/plan-name-default.png).
4. **Don't give links a link colour.** Marketing links are plain text that fades to 50% on hover (nav) or underlines (footer); the only "blue" anywhere is Chromium's own `#005fcc` focus ring. See [nav-link-hover](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png) and [footer-link-hover](components/crops/footer-link-hover.png).
5. **Don't invent fills for hover.** Hovers are opacity fades, the two measured ink steps, or a light `#f2f2f2` row wash — never a new colour. See [dropdown-item-hover](components/crops/dropdown-item-hover.png) and [nav-cta-hover](components/crops/nav-cta-hover.png).
6. **Do keep the three focus rings.** Links and accordions take the browser's 1px `#005fcc` auto ring, the mobile menu draws `#e59700` amber, and the gate's buttons paint a 2px-white/4px-black double box-shadow ring — keep all three rather than unifying them. See [plan-cta-focus-visible](components/crops/plan-cta-focus-visible.png), [m-menu-link-focus-visible](components/crops/m-menu-link-focus-visible.png) and [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
```

```bash
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

```bash
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

`build_tokens.py` reads `raw/component-tokens.json` and writes `tokens.json`; it refuses a value that is not the DTCG type it claims and an alias that points at nothing. `build_style_guide.py` renders `style-guide.html` from `tokens.json` and `tools/style-guide.template.html`, turning every token into a CSS variable so the page cannot drift from the file. `build_components_md.py` writes `components.md` from `raw/components-computed.json`, `tokens.json`, the screenshots and the crops. All three are deterministic: re-running them on this commit reproduces their files byte for byte. Edit the raw inputs or the builders, never the generated files.

`tools/capture/` holds the kit the captures came from. The Playwright scripts take `PLAYWRIGHT` as the path to a Playwright install's `index.mjs` when `playwright` is not resolvable from where they run.

```bash
PLAYWRIGHT=/path/to/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/public.mjs
PLAYWRIGHT=/path/to/playwright/index.mjs node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs home pricing getstarted login mobile
```

`public.mjs` re-takes the screenshot set into `RAW` (a scratch dir outside the repository, defaults to `raw/full`); `python3 tools/capture/quantize.py raw/full` stitches any `*.partN.png` tiles and stores the quantised copies in `screenshots/`. `measure.mjs` takes page names and merges into `raw/components-computed.json`, re-cropping `components/crops/`; `node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs` then rewrites `raw/measurements.md`. `vars.mjs` rewrites `raw/css-vars.json` whole. `lib.mjs` holds the shared helpers, including the consent-SDK request aborts and the `navigator.webdriver` masking.

## Use in Figma

Import `tokens.json` with Tokens Studio and choose the Light theme. Set UI text to a warm grotesque (Clarkson is proprietary; Public Sans is close) and display headlines to a soft serif (Fraunces stands in for Clarkson Serif), and keep buttons and cards at 12px radius.

## The screen to get right

The home page is the screen to get right. An 80px white bar carries the acuity:scheduling wordmark, five Clarkson nav items and the ink Get-started pill. Below, a pastel gradient hero centres a 40/48 Clarkson Serif headline in navy over two 56px ghost CTAs on 1px ink rules.
