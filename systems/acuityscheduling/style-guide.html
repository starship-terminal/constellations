Acuity Scheduling design tokens

Measured from acuityscheduling.com's public marketing pages and Squarespace's public account gate in headless Chromium, signed out, at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Every value below is a computed style read off the running site (the frontsite build declares almost no design-system custom properties, so these are literals, each with a provenance note in the token file). One theme; nothing was submitted or signed into.

The scheduling product itself sits behind the Squarespace account and was never entered: this package is the marketing and account-entry surface only. Screenshots and per-component readings are in components.md.

Palette

A warm, near-pastel palette around one workhorse: navy ink #283849 fills every primary button, colours the serif headlines, rules the ghost CTAs and bands the FAQ section. Near-blacks (#0e0e0e, #222, #2d2d2d) carry text; soft greys (#f2f2f2–#e0e0e0) paint hovers, hairlines and the disabled state; cream and lavender sit on the cards; pale blue #e0ebf4 closes the page.

Type

Two proprietary Squarespace families. Clarkson (400, 500) sets everything working: nav, buttons, fields, body copy. Clarkson Serif at 500 sets every display headline (40/48 hero, 32/40 plan names, 24/28 card titles), with Recoleta in the fallback stack. The gate's labels are uppercase 12/22 tracked 0.5px.

Clarkson and Clarkson Serif are served from the site's own /font/ paths and are not loaded here: this page previews in Public Sans and Fraunces where they are not installed.

Space, radius & shadow

The signature radius is 12px — every button, the plan cards and the CTAs share it. Cards step up to 16px, panels and the testimonial slide to 22–24px, and the pricing toggle is a full 100px pill. Depth comes from soft paired shadows (a hairline ring plus a drop), never from hard borders.
