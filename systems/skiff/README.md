# Skiff

Skiff was a private email, docs, drive and calendar product. Notion acquired it, and the public site now leads with a shutdown notice. This package measures the signed-out pages that still load on skiff.com: the notice at `/`, the product marketing pages, the security whitepaper, and the report form. Captured 2026-11-19, signed out, light only, at 1440 and 390. No account was created.

### Principles

1. Lead with the shutdown notice.
2. Keep the orange for the mark and the one emphasised phrase.
3. Use the raised card for a page of prose.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://skiff.com/ and returned computed styles, then failed to produce screenshot frames. A headless Chromium session (Playwright, viewport 1440x900 and 390x844, deviceScaleFactor 1) loaded the same public pages with no Cloudflare challenge and wrote the screenshots. Colours, type, radii, padding and shadows are computed styles or declared rules in the same-origin stylesheets. Each token's `$extensions.source` names the reading.

Custom properties on the root are mostly Tailwind internals plus `--font-display`, `--font-text`, `--font-mono`, `--font-pixel`, `--f200` through `--f900`, `--bg-overlay-*` and `--shadow-page`. The orange, gray and blue ramps are Tailwind classes (`text-orange-500`, `bg-gray-800`, `text-blue-400`), read from the computed rule values.

## What to distrust

The product faces are next/font names (`__skiffSansDisplay_59b421` and the matching text, mono and pixel faces). The tokens call them Skiff Sans Display, Skiff Sans Text, Skiff Mono and Skiff Pixel. Those commercial names are not in the CSS. Fallbacks in the font faces are Arial.

Hover and focus colours (`orange-600`, the `#0b79af` ring) are declared and were not observed on a hovered or focused control. The Mail, Pages, Drive and Calendar pages are marketing demos with sample names and messages, not the signed-in app. The whitepaper screenshot is the top 900 px of a page that scrolls past 12,000 px.

The preview browser could not produce screenshot frames. The PNGs are the headless captures, quantized to 256 colours. Read values from the tokens, not from the PNGs.

## Do and don't

- **Lead with the shutdown notice.** The home page is the acquisition statement, with the orange phrase on the second line. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Keep the orange for the mark and the one emphasised phrase.** Orange-500 is the mark tile and the accent span, not a fill behind body copy. [components/crops/nav-pill-default.png](components/crops/nav-pill-default.png)
- **Use the raised card for a page of prose.** White surface, 4 px radius, 1 px top border and 2 px bottom border at 8 percent black. [components/crops/notice-card-default.png](components/crops/notice-card-default.png)
- **Set product heroes on the blue gradient.** Mail, Pages, Drive and Calendar open on `#51c4fe` to `#31afee` with white display type. [screenshots/mail-light-default.png](screenshots/mail-light-default.png)
- **Leave the report form empty in a reconstruction.** Fields are sunken, 8 px radius, and the submit button is black with a 14 px radius. [components/crops/form-report-default.png](components/crops/form-report-default.png)
