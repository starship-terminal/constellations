Palette

The palette is declared entirely in named utilities: warm darks (warm-black, basalt, charcoal, stone), warm neutrals (warm-white, cloud, silver, aluminum), the brand hues (orange/carmine, purple, light-purple, lilac, fluorescent green and its darker hover step), status tints at 8%, and the warm-white alpha veils the bundle names 'black4/8/32/64'. Dark roles are measured off the painted components and alias the palette where the value matches.

Spacing and radius

The radius scale runs 6-56px plus full: 12px buttons, 16px fields and header corners, 20px cards, circle pagination. The signature ease is cubic-bezier(0.9,0.1,0.1,0.9) at 350ms; state changes use Tailwind's 150-300ms steps, and interactive-cards fade their sheen over 500ms.
