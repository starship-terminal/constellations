## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://spatial.chat/ with `reuseExistingTab` false. `preview_evaluate` read `getComputedStyle` on the live page at 1440 CSS px, then on https://app.spatial.chat/login, then on https://spatial.chat/pricing, then again at 390 CSS px. Custom properties were read from the marketing root. Button hover colours were read from the Webflow stylesheet rules, because a live hover could not be screenshotted. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" twice. No screenshot was invented.

## What to distrust

The hero section's computed background-color is white, while its stylesheet also sets a multi-stop gradient. Both are recorded. Heading sizes in the stylesheet (h1 50px, h2 38px) are smaller than the computed sizes at 1440 (60px and 48px). Tokens use the computed sizes. The email field was focused before its border was read, so the resting border is not isolated. Login `--dash-*` variables exist in stylesheets and were not the computed style of the login page. The Chatwoot bubble colour is a third-party widget.

## Fonts

Marketing uses Satoshi (computed on body; weights 400, 500, and 700 loaded). The login form uses Euclid Circular B (weights 400, 500, and 600 in `@font-face`). OAuth labels use an Apple system stack. Inter 500 was loaded on the login page and used by the reCAPTCHA links. These families are proprietary or licensed to the product. The tokens name them. The style guide falls back to the system face. Caveat and IBM Plex Mono are requested by a Google Fonts link on the marketing page and were not the computed face of the captured text.

## Do and don't

**Purple is the marketing action colour, and the login Continue button is a different purple.** Start for Free, Book a Demo, and Buy Now use #5727e7. Continue on the login form uses #5b5bf5. Do not paint the login button with the marketing accent.

**Outlined buttons are the quiet pair.** Login and Try it Free are transparent or white with a 1px black border and 12px radius. The filled button is the one purple action in that group.

**The news bar is a full-width purple strip, not a badge.** It is 54px, #5727e7, with white 16px text. The New tag is a separate 8px-radius chip.

**Plan prices use the accent, and plan names stay black.** $399 is #5727e7 at 32px. Free and Business are black at 40px on desktop.

**Do not follow Buy Now or submit the email form when matching this package.** Those paths were measured and not entered. Checkout and the signed-in app are outside this capture.

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
```

components.md is written by hand from raw/readings.json at capture time; `build_components_md.py` only checks that it exists, so a recapture must update it by hand. `build_tokens.py` does not rebuild tokens.json either: it checks the hand-written file against raw/readings.json (sources, literal colours, aliases).

Run from `systems/spatial-chat`. `python3 tools/catalog.py design-md spatial-chat` from the repo root rewrites DESIGN.md.
