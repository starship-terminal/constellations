## How the values were read

A T3 preview tab opened `https://wise.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. The page was not a Cloudflare challenge. Computed styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on that tab, then on `https://wise.com/login` and `https://wise.com/sg/pricing/`.

Custom properties were read from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`, so a name that loses to a later rule is stored as the winning computed value. 321 names matched the colour, type, radius, and spacing filters. The full dump is [raw/css-vars-home.json](raw/css-vars-home.json).

`preview_snapshot` returned: "The browser is not producing screenshot frames. For a desktop tab, show or restore the T3 Code window and try again." Measurements already read were kept. No screenshot was invented. Headless Playwright was not launched, including against the login page, which embeds a Cloudflare Turnstile field.

## What to distrust

Headline sizes are fluid. 85.909px and 77.947px are what the browser computed at innerWidth 1169, not a 1440 layout. Three-decimal rounding is marked on those tokens.

`light.color.border`, `light.color.neutral-fill`, and `light.color.calculator-fill` are alpha colours flattened onto white so the token checker can store a hex. The computed rgba is in [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).

Hover colours declared as `--color-interactive-accent-hover` and `--color-interactive-primary-hover` were not confirmed on a hovered element, so they are not tokens. Focusing the empty email field did not change its inset ring.

`color-scheme` was `normal`. There is no dark set. Bright orange, yellow, pink, blue, and dark purple are resolved custom properties. They were not confirmed as a paint on these three pages.

Motion durations are computed and declared values. `prefers-reduced-motion` was not emulated.

No screenshot backs these numbers.

## Fonts

Wise Sans is Wise's display face, named by `--eds-font-family-display` and computed on the home h1. Inter is the text face, with Vazirmatn, Noto Sans SC, Zen Kaku Gothic Antique, Noto Sans KR, Noto Sans Thai, Noto Sans Hebrew, then Helvetica, Arial, and sans-serif. The files are not in this package. The tokens name the stacks and fall back to Inter and Helvetica.

## Do and don't

- **Use the measured green and forest.** The primary face is `light.color.accent` with `light.color.on-accent`. On a green plate the same button inverts to `light.color.forest` with `light.color.on-forest`. Readings are in [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Use the pill for a marketing action.** Large actions are 48px, radius `core.radius.pill`. The nav Sign up is 32px. The login field is the exception: radius `core.radius.input`.
- **Keep the login fields empty in any reconstruction of this capture.** The email and password controls were measured blank. No address was entered.
- **Stop at the login wall.** Google, Facebook, Apple, passkey, recovery, and signup were not followed. Do not invent the register form.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** These pages painted `np-theme-personal` only.
- **Do not treat the 1169px headline sizes as a desktop token for every width.** They are the fluid computed size at this viewport.
