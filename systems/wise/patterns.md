# Patterns

Public signed-out pages only. Viewport was 1169 by 731 CSS pixels. No screenshots: the preview browser did not produce frames.

## Home

https://wise.com/ redirected the title to Wise Singapore and stayed on `https://wise.com/`. The theme class is `np-theme-personal`. The page background is white. The hero headline is Wise Sans at the fluid size in `core.typography.display-home`, colour `light.color.text`. A paragraph under it is `core.typography.hero-paragraph` in `light.color.muted`.

The header holds a transparent Log in pill and a green Sign up pill. Further down, a calculator uses currency comboboxes and a green Send money button. That button was not clicked. A full-bleed section paints `light.color.accent` and puts a forest pill on it. The footer paints the neutral green wash.

## Pricing

https://wise.com/sg/pricing/ uses the same light theme. The nav Sign up is inverted (forest face, green label). Segment cards sit in a row with a 10px radius and a forest-tinted border. "Sign up today" is the large green pill with a 1px green border and 11px 24px padding.

## Login

https://wise.com/login is a narrow column, 486px form inside a 550px container, on the white page. The heading is "Welcome back" at `core.typography.login-title`. An error banner was already visible. Email and password fields were empty and were left empty. Social buttons (Google, Facebook, Apple) and the passkey button were measured and not clicked. Sign up, Can't log in, and recovery were not followed.

## Not captured

The signed-in app, a transfer, onboarding, phone width, and a dark theme were not opened. A menu, a dialog, and a validation error from a submit were not produced.
