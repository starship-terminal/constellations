Palette

The measured color.neutral.* readings carry the marketing inks and fields, color.brand.* the calm-blue/violet gradient ends, and color.app.* the dark shell. Light roles alias the marketing readings, dark roles the app readings.

Spacing and radius

Pills dominate: nav CTAs, category tabs and the signup pill are fully rounded; content tiles and the rail sit on mid-size radii; the auth cards and upsell bar round at card scale.
