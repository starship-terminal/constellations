# Mesh design reference

A token set and component reference for Mesh's public surface — for building dark glass-on-black product pages and warm-cream pricing surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **me.sh** on **2026-11-16**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844: the marketing routes (home, pricing, integrations, nexus, teams, start, stories, serendipity, notion, story, careers, apple-vision-pro, about and contact), plus the public sign-in card on **app.me.sh** as a boundary. **Provenance:** **clay.earth** 301-redirects to me.sh — the Clay product rebranded to Mesh (an Automattic product, per the footer badge); this package keeps the `clay-earth` slug and captures the live redirect target.

Mesh is worth a capture because it runs three honest surfaces on one product: a **black marketing ground** (`#000` painted by `documentElement` — body itself is transparent) with a floating glass nav pill, warm off-white `#fefef7` VerlagCondensed display type, inverted off-white `#fdfcfa` CTA pills ringed by a blue-violet-amber gradient and haloed in a 98%-white glow, plus the `#f2b98b` tan wordmark accent; a **warm light pricing route** (`#f9f8f6`) with hairline `#eceae8` plan cards under a white sheen, Chronicle-serif prices, an orange `#fb7232` popular ring and filled CTAs; and a **light sign-in card** on app.me.sh (`#fcfcfc`, SF Pro Text, the app's own `#ff6821` orange and the Apple/Google OAuth pair) — a different stack from the marketing site. **Boundary:** the app itself, plan checkout and account creation sit behind sign-in or payment and were never crossed; the sign-in card was measured (field focused and filled with placeholder text only) but nothing was submitted.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export clay-earth` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page black and float glass on it.
2. Do keep the two surfaces honest.
3. Do pair VerlagCondensed display with Verlag UI.
4. Do reserve orange for actions.
5. Do ring the inverted CTA pill.
6. Do use the tan `#f2b98b` for the wordmark and accents only
7. Don't read fills off the element itself
8. Don't underline links
9. Don't fake the product.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Layout

The 1440 px page is a black canvas: a 388 px glass nav pill floats at top-centre; the hero centres a 1232 px VerlagCondensed headline (the final word rotates — organized / searchable / up to date) over an animated relationship counter and a white CTA pill. Sections alternate black and warm-cream bands (the "Never miss what matters." features live on a light band — dark ink `#1d1d1f` on white). Pricing is its own warm-cream route with four 288 px plan columns. The `#0f0f10` footer band closes every page. At 390 px the nav collapses to three fixed pieces (logo, Get-Started pill, hamburger) and a 374 px slide-in drawer.

## How the values were read

Every public surface was captured signed-out: routes were opened in fresh contexts in headless Chromium (Playwright, `tools/capture/public/`). Per component the kit records the computed style plus the first painted child layers (`paint`), so fills living on `::before`/`::after` (the nav pill's sheen, the CTA's gradient ring, the filter pills' wash) are measured where they actually paint. States: `hover` real pointer, `focus-visible` real keyboard focus, `pressed` forced via CDP (never a real press), `filled`/`focused` on the sign-in e-mail field with placeholder text deleted after. The nav dropdowns and mobile drawer were opened by their own toggles — nothing navigated, submitted or signed in. Screenshots were quantized to 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from the tokens or raw files.

### Honest gaps

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **library.me.sh** (the host /terms and /privacy redirect to) sits behind a "Confirm you are human" verification gate — not captured.
- **/press/kit** redirects to a public Notion press kit (clayearth.notion.site) — external, not captured.
- The app interior, checkout, downloads (/start links) and every OAuth/sign-in action — boundaries, never crossed.
- The marketing site mixes themes per route (dark home, light pricing): both sets are tokenised; components measured on their own route.
- CSS-module class names are hashed but stable across loads; the package never selects the random part.
- **Two components were read from the wrong element; they need a recapture.** `menu-panel` is the closed Company panel (opacity 0), not the open dropdown, so `core.component.menuPanel.width` (556px) and `menu-card` ("ABOUT MESH") come from a hidden panel. `hero-sub` is not a hero lede (the hero has none): the first paragraph after the h1 is a section paragraph ("Set reminders..."), so `core.color.gray.400` and `core.font.size.sub` describe section body copy. The token descriptions say so; the capture selectors are fixed by edit (opacity above 0, a sibling paragraph of the h1), not rerun.
- **Seven focus states could not be driven.** Their focus-visible readings were the element at rest (`via: focus failed`); [components.md](components.md) leaves them out of its tables and lists them as not driven.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the page black and float glass on it.** `ink.950` `#000` paints `documentElement` (body itself is transparent); the 388px nav pill floats centred under a 3%-white sheen. Don't render the marketing routes on white. ([header](components/crops/header-default.png), [page ground](screenshots/home-dark-default.png))
- **Do keep the two surfaces honest.** The black marketing routes and the `#f9f8f6` warm-cream pricing route are separate token sets, not a user toggle. Don't ship a switchable theme. ([pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png))
- **Do pair VerlagCondensed display with Verlag UI.** 64px/700 headlines in `#fefef7` on dark and `#1f1f1f` on light, the hero's final word rotating; the sign-in card is a third stack — SF Pro Text — don't set it in Verlag. ([hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [signin-title](components/crops/signin-title-default.png))
- **Do reserve orange for actions.** Marketing `#fb7232` paints the popular-card ring, filled CTA and hovers; the app stack's `#ff6821` lives only on the sign-in card. Don't mix them. ([plan-cta-filled](components/crops/plan-cta-filled-default.png), [signin-email-btn](components/crops/signin-email-btn-default.png))
- **Do ring the inverted CTA pill.** The 205x54 `#fdfcfa` "Get Mesh Free" pill (black 18px/600 text) wears a blue-violet-amber gradient ring inside a 98%-white glow; don't ship it flat white. ([cta](components/crops/cta-default.png), [cta-bottom](components/crops/cta-bottom-default.png))
- **Do use the tan `#f2b98b` for the wordmark and accents only** — the logo, footer icons and drawer link hovers. Don't promote it to a fill. ([logo](components/crops/logo-default.png))
- **Don't read fills off the element itself** — the nav sheen, CTA ring, plan-card sheen and filter-pill wash all paint on `::before`/`::after` layers; the tokens carry the measured layer values. ([plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png), [filter-button](components/crops/filter-button-default.png))
- **Don't underline links** — hovers repaint colour or an 8% white wash only, from nav items to footer columns. ([nav-item](components/crops/nav-item-default.png), [footer-link](components/crops/footer-link-default.png))
- **Don't fake the product.** The Mesh app interior, checkout and library.me.sh sit behind sign-in, payment or a human-verification gate — boundaries documented in components.md, never crossed.
