Mesh, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json. me.sh (clay.earth's redirect target — the Clay product rebranded to Mesh, an Automattic product) declares no design-token CSS variables (its nine custom properties are Bulma column gaps and orb positions), so the whole palette is measured on the painted chrome: the black ground and its translucent whites, the #fb7232 marketing orange, the #f2b98b wordmark tan, the warm creams and the app surface's own #ff6821.

Two page themes on different routes, not a toggle: / and the dark routes paint black; /pricing and the app.me.sh sign-in card paint warm light surfaces.
