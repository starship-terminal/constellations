# Patterns

Patterns are what the opened pages showed. There are no screenshots.

## Home

A transparent header over a seed-green hero. The hero heading is snow, 48px at 1169 and 32px at 390. Two snow pills sit under it: Take the Quiz and Shop DS-01. Later sections switch to snow or foam backgrounds with seed-green type. The footer is seed green with 14px snow captions.

At 390 the header collapses to a logo and a two-bar menu. The tab labels are not in the header.

## Shop index

`/products` opens on a seed-green hero (html background #1c3a13) with a snow heading at 48px. Product prices are 20px. A struck compare-at price uses the disabled text colour, rgba(28, 58, 19, 0.5). Filled Learn More pills are seed green.

## Product

`/daily-synbiotic` is a snow page. A 24px title, a 14px reviews link, a 16px blurb, a 24px price, a bright-green Bestseller badge, and a full-width Start Now pill (304px at this viewport). Benefit and ingredient rows are buttons in the buy column.

## Shop menu

Opening Shop drops a 375px glass panel, radius 32px, under the header. Each product row is 75px. Shop All Products is a 14px / 500 snow link with a pill radius.

## Sign in

The Sign in button opens the same glass panel. Copy says "Welcome back" and "Enter your email to access your account." One email field and a Continue pill. Nothing was typed or submitted.

## Quiz

`/is-ds01-right-for-you` redirects to `/lp/ds01-quiz-baseline`. Step 1 of 13 is a question with four 72px option buttons. The first step asks a health question. No option was selected. The page says health information is handled under their consumer health policy. That is why the quiz stops at the first screen.
