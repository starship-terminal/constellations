# Linktree design reference

A token set and component reference for Linktree's public site. It was read from **linktr.ee** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844: the marketing home, About, Features, public pricing, the official public profile at `/linktr.ee`, and the signed-out login wall. The login form was measured and not submitted.

Linktree is a current reference for a link-in-bio marketing surface: a chartreuse hero band, a forest-green 80px/800 hero, an ink `#1e2330` pill at a 9999px radius, and a dahlia `#502274` footer. The public profile is a second surface, set in Link Sans Product, with a 40px share button, Links and Shop tabs, and a Verified badge.

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) read every stylesheet rule through the CSSOM on `/`, `/s/pricing`, and `/linktr.ee`. The marketing bundle declares a Tailwind v4 theme layer. A dark class on the probe document resolved the same 456 custom properties, so the published theme is light. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes [raw/component-tokens.json](raw/component-tokens.json); [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) renders [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle` per element in default, hover, focus-visible, and CDP-forced pressed states. The claim field was focused and filled with a throwaway string, then cleared. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars; [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.
- **Fonts.** 43 @font-face declarations sit in the bundle ([raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) `fonts`): **Linksans**, **Link Sans Product**, Anybody, DM Mono, Elza Condensed, Roboto Flex, mencken, thatThat, Archivo, AvenirNext. The webfonts themselves are not committed.

Nothing was sent, claimed, purchased, or signed into. Plan buttons, Continue, Google, Apple, Share profile, and social buttons were measured, never followed. Consent overlays were hidden, never clicked.

## What to distrust

- **One painted theme.** Every captured route paints light. The dark probe resolved the same variables, so the `dark` set is empty.
- **`light.page` is the claim field's white fill.** The body and the nav root paint transparent. The home hero band is chartreuse `#d2e823` (`light.hero`, resolved `--color-chartreuse`). Pricing sits on a light grey. The official profile sits on chartreuse.
- **`light.band` is the footer**, dahlia `#502274`, not the page ground.
- **`light.focus` is the outer ring of the Log in link's focus** (`#1e2330`, the 4px box-shadow outside a 2px white gap; the Sign up pill, hero and claim buttons draw the same ring). It used to be `#005fcc`, the Pricing nav link's `outlineColor`, which nothing paints: that link's outline is none/0px and it shows focus as a `#eff0ec` fill. Footer links, plan buttons and the profile controls show the browser's own `auto` ring.
- **`login-continue` focus-visible was not captured.** Keyboard focus never landed (`via: focus failed` in raw); components.md and raw/measurements.md leave it out, and `components/crops/login-continue-focus-visible.png` is the element at rest.
- **Captured past an automation mask.** Every committed reading and screenshot was taken with `navigator.webdriver` masked. The mask was removed from the capture scripts (bot checks are now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); the readings were kept and were not retaken.
- **`plan-card` is the Pro heading**, 65 x 36, not a plan card. The plan buttons are the 336 x 48 pills.
- **Pressed states shrink some pills** (hero CTA and claim submit go from 300 x 60 to 291 x 58.2) because CDP forced `:active`. Nothing was clicked.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs. Read exact colours from tokens or the raw JSON.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the hero band chartreuse and the title forest.** The home hero band is `#d2e823`. The title is `#254f1a` at 80px/800. Pricing and the profile sit on other grounds. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [hero-title-default](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
2. **Do use one ink pill for the primary action.** Sign up free is `#1e2330` with white text and a 9999px radius. See [nav-signup-default](components/crops/nav-signup-default.png).
3. **Do set marketing type in Linksans.** Nav, hero, and footer measure as Linksans. The public profile title measures as Link Sans Product. See [profile-title-default](components/crops/profile-title-default.png).
4. **Do keep the footer a dahlia band.** The footer fills `#502274`. See [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).
5. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The captured routes paint light, and a dark class on the probe did not change the resolved variables.
6. **Don't follow a plan button into checkout.** Try free for 7 days and Get started were measured. They were not clicked. See [plan-select-default](components/crops/plan-select-default.png).
7. **Don't treat the login wall as a signed-in app.** `/login` redirects to the universal login wall. Continue, Google, and Sign up were measured and not submitted. See the Sign-in wall group in [components.md](components.md).
8. **Don't submit the claim field.** The field was filled with a throwaway string and cleared. See [claim-field-filled](components/crops/claim-field-filled.png).
