# Trulia design reference

A token set and component reference for Trulia's public site, read from www.trulia.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headed Chrome through Playwright. Desktop captures are 1440×900 at 1x. The phone homepage, mortgages, and neighborhoods captures are 390×844 at 2x. There is no signed-in surface and no dark theme in this package.

Headless Chromium and curl received HTTP 403 from PerimeterX. Headed Chrome loaded the homepage and several public routes. A second visit in the same minute often returned a press-and-hold wall. Listing pages that show a street address were mapped and not screenshotted. No password was typed, no form was submitted, and Continue with Google and Continue with Apple were not clicked.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from `document.styleSheets` on the homepage in headed Chrome. The palette, type sizes, spacing, radii, breakpoints, focus shadow, and header height are declared on `:where(:root, :host)`. Semantic names such as `--colors-primary` are `var()` aliases of those ramps. [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) writes them into [tokens.json](tokens.json).

Component colours that the stylesheets do not name as a state (login hover, search submit, selected tab) were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live element, including a CDP-forced hover and active state. Those readings are in [raw/component-states.json](raw/component-states.json).

One stylesheet was cross-origin and could not be read. The 184 declarations that were readable include the colour ramps and the scales.

## What to distrust

- **No dark theme.** Do not invent one. The document's `color-scheme` is `normal`.
- **Search fields arrive prefilled** with a location from the network. Captures clear or hide that value. A blank search field in a screenshot is the cleared state, not proof the live field is empty.
- **The mortgage ZIP was painted out** of [mortgages-light-default](screenshots/mortgages-light-default.png) and the full-page shot. The Get Pre-Qualified button was not pressed.
- **The calculator page is not in the screenshots.** A cleanup pass wiped its sample numbers. The first pass saw $200,000, 20% down, 7.30%, and a ZIP. Those numbers are not in the package.
- **Login dialog screenshots include Continue with Google.** That control was not clicked. The Google iframe is Google's button, not a Trulia token.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Figtree is commercial** and is not in the repo. The style guide falls back to Arial.

## Fonts

**Figtree** is the family on `html` and `body`: `"Figtree", "Figtree Fallback"`. Weights 400, 700, and 900 are declared in `@font-face`, self-hosted as woff2. `Figtree Fallback` is `local("Arial")`. The tokens add Arial and sans-serif after that. Figtree is not committed.

## Do and don't

- **Do use teal for the selected tab and the primary submit.** Buy selected and the login Submit both use tarzana.700 (`#007882`). See [tab-buy-selected](components/crops/tab-buy-selected.png).
- **Do use the red-orange submit only on search.** The search button is oakmore.500-a-a (`#d93c23`), not the teal accent. See [search-submit-default](components/crops/search-submit-default.png).
- **Do keep the header at 64px and controls at 8px radius.** See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do set body text to grey.900 on white.** `#3b4144` on `#ffffff`. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The site does not paint one.
- **Don't treat a cleared search field as the live empty state.** The field arrives prefilled. See [What to distrust](#what-to-distrust).
