# Patterns

## Homepage

www.fitbit.com redirects to the Google Store watches and trackers category. A 53px `#050505` promo strip sits above a white header. The Google Store mark is on the left, then Phones, Watches & Trackers, Earbuds, Smart Home, Accessories, and Support. Search, cart, Google apps, and Sign in sit on the right. Sign in is the blue pill. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

Under the header, a horizontal row of product cards names Pixel Watch and Fitbit devices. New is a soft blue pill on Pixel Watch 5 and Fitbit Edge. The page background is `#f9f9f9`. Further down, a photographic band carries the 76px headline "Google Pixel and Fitbit. Healthier for everyone." That region is below the photographed viewport. Shop holiday deals is a white pill on that band.
