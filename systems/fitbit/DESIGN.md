# Fitbit public chrome

Signed-out capture of [https://www.fitbit.com](https://www.fitbit.com) on 2026-11-22. The browser landed on the Google Store watches and trackers category. Values are the ones in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

The page is a white header over a `#f9f9f9` category body. A 53px promo strip sits on top of the header and uses `#050505` with white 16px type. The Google Store mark and nav sit in the white bar under it. Nav labels are Google Sans Flex, 14px, weight 500, `#191c1d`.

Sign in is a pill on the right: 85 by 40, radius 100px, fill `#0b57d0`, white 14px, weight 500. Buy uses the same blue. On the category page the fill is painted on the link's `::after`, 48px, radius 100px, white 16px, weight 500. On the product page the sticky Buy button paints the blue on the button itself, 77 by 56, same radius.

Shop holiday deals is the inverse pill: white fill, ink type, 184 by 48, radius 100px. New is a soft pill, `#d3e3fd`, type `#0842a0`, 12px, weight 500, radius 100px. The selected Overview tab uses that same soft fill at 48px. Tech specs is `#eff1f2` with ink type. Save SGD 60 is `#beefbb` with `#00522c` type, radius 8px.

The category headline is 76px, weight 500, line-height 84px, letter-spacing -1px, `#191c1d`. The product headline is 48px, weight 500, line-height 56px. The product stage is `#e6ebe5`, radius 28px. A struck compare price uses the same ink with a line-through, 14px.

There is no theme toggle on the public pages opened.

Do not follow Sign in, cart, Buy, or a config link. Those leave the public category and product pages.
