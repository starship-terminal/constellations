# Instacart patterns

## Marketing home

White header, forest hero, store logo rail, filter chips, store cards. The hero copy sits on `color.hero` with a green pill. Store cards sit on white. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

Mobile home stacks the same hero, then an app interstitial ("Get the app" and "Continue in browser"). See [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Search and deals

A green promo bar can sit above the header. Search results group products under a store row (logo, name, delivery time) and a horizontal product rail. Prices use a large dollar and a raised cents figure. Yellow chips mark deals. ZIP 94105 is a public example in the header, not a saved street address. See [search-light-default](screenshots/search-light-default.png) and [deals-light-default](screenshots/deals-light-default.png).

## Auth

Login and signup are a white card on the warm wash, carrot wordmark above, one email field, a green Continue pill, then Google, Facebook and Phone. Phone is an alternate path, not a required step on the email path. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [signup-light-default](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).

## Grocery delivery landing

A split hero: headline and ZIP field on the warm wash, a food photo on the right, then a store list. The ZIP field was not submitted in the capture. See [grocery-delivery-light-default](screenshots/grocery-delivery-light-default.png).

## Help

Guest help is a warm page, a short greeting, and stacked white groups of rows. A black "Connect with Instacart" pill floats at the bottom right. See [help-light-default](screenshots/help-light-default.png).

## Business

A separate marketing system: dark purple header, periwinkle pill, cream hero card. Do not mix it into the consumer green. See [business-light-default](screenshots/business-light-default.png).

Checkout, payment, order tracking, a saved street address, the Plus trial, and the signed-in storefront home. The signed-in session was reached and then screenshot capture failed. Company.instacart.com is a different host.
