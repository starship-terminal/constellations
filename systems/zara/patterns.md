# Patterns

Public US storefront, signed out, 2026-11-19. No page screenshots: the preview browser produced no frames.

## Shell

A left column of uppercase links: SEARCH, BAG with a count, LOG IN, HELP. On the cart page the column also shows FAVORITES and PRE-OWNED. The wordmark is an inline SVG (`viewBox="0 0 132 55"` on the 404 logo link). The header element itself is `display: contents`.

## Location gate

www.zara.com/ lists country links before a storefront loads. Choosing United States goes to www.zara.com/us/. A later geo dialog asks to stay on United States or go to the Singapore site. Stay was accepted. The other store was not opened.

## Catalog

The new-arrivals grid is image tiles. Names are not in the tile text until another surface (search recommendations) shows them at 11px uppercase.

## Product

Name at 15px uppercase, price at 15px uppercase (`$ 149.00` on the measured coat), colour and reference at 11px uppercase. ADD opens a size list. The size list was opened and closed by navigation. No size was chosen and the item was not added.

## Search

A section column (Woman, Man, Kids, Home, Massimo Dutti) and a borderless input. Recommendations render under the field. No query was typed or submitted.

## Help

A title, a search field that gains a 1px black underline on focus, FAQ rows, and topic columns. Account topics were not opened.

## Empty bag

The cart URL loads signed out and shows "YOUR BAG IS EMPTY" plus a recommendation row. Nothing was added.

## Logon

LOG IN leaves www.zara.com for account.zara.com. The form is email plus CONTINUE, then social buttons. It was measured and not submitted. No email, card, phone, or address was entered.

## 404

A made-up path renders "PAGE NOT FOUND", "This page is no longer available.", and a secondary GO TO HOMEPAGE button.
