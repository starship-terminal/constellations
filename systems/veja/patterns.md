# Patterns

## Storefront chrome

A gray promo line sits on a white header. The V mark is 65px. Departments are uppercase and black, except Gift card, which is green. Search, account, and cart are icon buttons. See [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Homepage

A full-bleed photo carousel, then a row of category tiles (Men, Women, Kids, Running), then a product strip. The project band and lookbooks sit further down. The footer was read from the DOM and not photographed.

## Collection

`/en_us/men` is a four-column grid under a breadcrumb and a Filters button. Column toggles sit at the right of the breadcrumb. See [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Product

Photo on the left, buy box on the right: uppercase title, price, colour swatches, size chips, a fit note, then Add to cart. Add to cart was not clicked. See [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Help

FAQ is a two-column page: group names on the left, questions on the right, each with a plus. See [faq-light-1440.png](screenshots/faq-light-1440.png).

## Not reached

Account, cart, checkout, gift-card purchase, newsletter submit, search submit, contact, and the store locator.
