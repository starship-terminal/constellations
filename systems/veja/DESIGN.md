## Typography

Helvetica is the UI face. The product title is 30px, weight 700, uppercase. The price is 25px. Nav links are 14px uppercase. Body is 16px.

| Style | Size | Weight | Line |

## Geometry

Add to cart, size chips, and Filters use a 5px radius. The promo bar is 38px. The header is 134px. The department row is 26px. The logo is 65px.

## Components

The primary button is black on white, 40px tall, 5px radius, uppercase 18px. Size chips are 100 by 30, 1px black border, 5px radius.
