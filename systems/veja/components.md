# Components

Public United States storefront, signed out, 22 November 2026. Crops were not taken. The screenshots show the components in place.

## Header

White bar, 1px black bottom border, 65px V mark on the left, uppercase 14px department links, search, account, and cart icons on the right. Gift card is `#065a21`. See [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Promo bar

38px, `#4d4d4d`, white 14px line, close icon at the right. See the same homepage shot.

## Button

Add to cart is black, white uppercase 18px, 40px tall, 5px radius, full width of the buy column. It was not clicked. Filters is a white outline of the same radius. Subscribe is a gray outline and was not clicked.

## Size chip

100 by 30, 1px black border, 5px radius, 14px uppercase. See [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Link

Department links, breadcrumbs (Home, then the page name in 11px), and FAQ rows. Underline is not the resting style.

## Card

Product tiles on the men grid: photo, uppercase name, price in bold. Four columns at 1440. See [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Text field

Newsletter email and header search exist. Neither was typed in or submitted.

## Accordion

FAQ groups and product details use a plus icon and a 1px rule. See [faq-light-1440.png](screenshots/faq-light-1440.png).

## Gaps

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured. No text area, select, checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, tooltip, dialog, toast, table, avatar, or badge was opened. The login dialog, cart, and filter sheet were not opened. No element crops.
