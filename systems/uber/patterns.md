# Patterns

## Marketing header

Every captured desktop marketing page opens with a 64px black bar. The wordmark sits left. Product pills (Ride, Earn, Travel, Business, Uber Eats, About) sit beside it. Drive also shows Hotels. The right cluster is language, Help, Log in (dark pill), and Sign up (white pill).

Shown in [screenshots/drive-light-default.png](screenshots/drive-light-default.png), [screenshots/about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png), and [screenshots/safety-light-default.png](screenshots/safety-light-default.png).

About opens a white menu. Sign up opens a short menu (Ride, Earn, Uber Eats, Business). Language opens a full-page dialog. See [screenshots/about-menu-light-open.png](screenshots/about-menu-light-open.png), [screenshots/signup-menu-light-open.png](screenshots/signup-menu-light-open.png), and [screenshots/language-dialog-light-open.png](screenshots/language-dialog-light-open.png).

## Drive hero

The public driver page is a black hero with a 52px white headline, an empty phone field, and a white "Get started" button. A second nav lists Sign up, Requirements, Vehicle solutions, and the rest of the driver topics. The phone field was not filled.

Shown in [screenshots/drive-light-default.png](screenshots/drive-light-default.png). At 390 the same hero stacks. Shown in [screenshots/drive-light-mobile.png](screenshots/drive-light-mobile.png).

## About and safety

About opens on a full-bleed photograph with a white "About us" title, then a white section. Safety is a white page with a photograph on the right. Help is a white page of grey topic tiles under a second product bar.

Shown in [screenshots/about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [screenshots/safety-light-default.png](screenshots/safety-light-default.png), and [screenshots/help-light-default.png](screenshots/help-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

`https://auth.uber.com/v2/` asks "What's your phone number or email?" then Continue, or Continue with Google, or Continue with Apple. The field was left empty. Google and Apple were not clicked. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

Shown in [screenshots/auth-light-empty.png](screenshots/auth-light-empty.png) and [screenshots/auth-light-mobile-empty.png](screenshots/auth-light-mobile-empty.png).

## Not-found

`/us/en/careers/` on www.uber.com renders the designed not-found page: a large headline, a black "Go to Uber's homepage" button, and columns of popular links. Shown in [screenshots/not-found-light-default.png](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png).

## Footer

The drive footer is black, four columns, then social icons, language, city, and store badges. Shown in [screenshots/drive-light-footer.png](screenshots/drive-light-footer.png).
