## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://quizlet.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. A Ketch cookie banner was dismissed with Reject All. Computed styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on the home page, `https://quizlet.com/login`, the signup form it navigated to, and `https://quizlet.com/mission` (opened by clicking the footer link).

Quizlet's stylesheets are cross-origin, so `cssRules` threw. The same page fetched `https://assets.quizlet.com/_next/static/css/2f945cc419a78f44.css` and the homepage sheet, and the custom properties were parsed from that text. Named scales (`--twilight-*`, `--gray-*`, `--mint-*`, and the other hues) were checked against computed colors on the buttons, fields and body. They match.

`preview_snapshot` failed twice: "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." No screenshot was invented. A direct open of `https://quizlet.com/features/flashcards` returned a PerimeterX press-and-hold page. That challenge was not completed.

## What to distrust

Night colors are stylesheet declarations on `body.theme-night`, not a painted page. This Mac reported `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, and the site stayed on `theme-default`.

Hover, pressed and disabled colors are stylesheet rules. They were not painted.

The homepage h1 computed color is rgb(0, 0, 0). Login and About headings computed gray-800. The black is recorded in raw/readings.json and is not a token.

Two feature-tile fills, rgb(217, 255, 204) and rgb(18, 193, 203), are not steps on the named scales. The teacher band rgb(219, 223, 255) is not a named step either.

The modal overlay's computed background was transparent while its rule targets `--scrim300-scrim300`. The entrance animation had not painted, and the preview pane was not producing frames.

The viewport was 1169 CSS pixels wide. It is not a 1440 or 390 capture.

## Fonts

## Do and don't

- **Use the twilight pill for the main action.** Log in and Sign up for free are #4255ff, white text, radius 200px, weight 600.
- **Keep the page on gray-200 and the nav on white.** Body is #f6f7fb. The sticky nav is #ffffff, 64px.
- **Fields are sunken, not outlined, until focus.** Fill #f6f7fb, radius 8px, 2px transparent border. Focus border is #a8b1ff.
- **Secondary actions use twilight-100.** Teachers is #edefff with #4255ff text, same pill radius.
- **Do not invent a night screen from the light page.** Night pairs live on `body.theme-night` and were not painted here.
- **Do not treat the feature-tile greens as scale steps.** Record Lecture uses twilight-500. Study Guides and Flashcards use one-off fills.
