# Canva design reference

A token set and component reference for Canva's public signed-out surface — for building marketing pages and galleries in the style of the real thing. It was read from **canva.com** on **2026-11-12**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: home, pricing, the /templates category index and the presentations gallery, the product pages (presentations, docs, whiteboard, the create/design-editor marketing page, pro, features), the /login and /signup cards, terms, plus a 390px pass on home and templates. Canva served the **Singapore region** — prices render `S$` — which this package records as geo behaviour, not an error.

Canva is worth a capture because the world's biggest design tool paints a deceptively small public system: Canva Sans at 400/500/600 over a `#0f1015` ink and white surfaces, one `#8b3dff → #7630d7 → #612dae` purple ramp for every action, state layers instead of borders (6%/14% ink washes on light, 7%/15% white on the dark band), a 1px inset `rgba(53,65,90,0.2)` hairline ring on secondary controls, and 9999px pills next to a flat 12px control radius. The home band is genuinely dark — the header and hero sit inside `.theme.dark` over a gradient media layer — and the StyleX bundles declare a real but hash-named custom-property layer with `.light`/`.dark` redeclarations, harvested here from the cross-origin responses. **Boundary:** the editor, Brand Kit, dashboards and everything behind the auth card were never reached and are never inferred.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export canva` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do spend the purple on actions only.
2. Do walk the ramp.
3. Do paint states as washes.
4. Do ring secondaries with the inset hairline.
5. Do pill the tabs, square the controls.
6. Do let the dark band breathe through the fills.
7. Don't style the editor from this package.
8. Don't read a theme toggle into `.theme.dark`.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body — Canva's StyleX bundles are cross-origin (static.canva.com, invisible to the CSSOM), so bodies are read from network responses plus inline `<style>` blocks re-parsed with `CSSStyleSheet` — and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json), resolving each on a probe document once `.theme.light` and once `.theme.dark`. The layer is real but hash-named (`--8jnd6g` is #8b3dff), so tokens var-reference it by resolved value, and everything else — the ink/white neutrals, the purple ramp, the state washes, the radii and the type ramp — is measured computed values from [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context (rapid navigation in one context trips Canva's error/challenge pages) and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md). Two Canva behaviours shaped the run: the pricing grid re-mounts while localised prices resolve (a wait-for-visible guards it), and the plan-audience tabs auto-activate on keyboard focus — measured last so `?tab=education` can't contaminate the rest.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the Design mega-menu and the Presentations tools tab on home, the Monthly→Yearly billing switch on /pricing, and the mobile menu. Full-page shots are capped at eight chunks; mobile-home's full page overflowed the GPU texture limit and stitched from two chunks (the second came back 10px short on a late layout settle — kept, flagged in its sidecar). [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, created, submitted or paid for: the auth card was measured as a surface — Continue with email was pressed once to reveal the username field (a local UI step; the field was focused and given sample text that was deleted again, and Continue was never pressed); every Sign up, trial, Contact-Sales and OAuth action was measured or screenshotted, never followed. The cookie banner (Accept all cookies / Manage cookies) was hidden per capture, never answered — no consent was ever posted.

On 2026-11-21 the public homepage was mapped again into [site-tree.md](site-tree.md), then photographed signed out. New shots in [screenshots/](screenshots/) use the `home-light-*`, `pricing-light-*`, `login-light-empty`, `signup-light-empty`, and `mobile-*` names. `/login/` and `/signup/` are the same empty card (Continue with Google, Continue with Facebook, Continue with email). Nothing on that card was clicked. See [login-review.json](login-review.json). Prices on `/pricing/` rendered `S$` again. `prefers-color-scheme: dark` did not change the homepage paint.

## What to distrust

- **The tool tab and tool panel were read in the wrong state; they need a recapture.** `tool-tab` is an ARIA tab that selects itself when it takes keyboard focus, so its focus-visible and pressed readings are the selected Presentations tab (white label), not an unselected tab, and `tool-panel`, read after it, is the Presentations panel. The capture now measures the tab after the panel (edited, not rerun).
- `dark.surface.page` (#16171d) was entered by hand: no resolved variable holds it, and its source now says so.
- **This is the public surface, not the editor.** The design editor, Brand Kit, dashboards, teams and account sit behind the auth card and are uncaptured. Marketing pages describe the product; nothing here verifies how it behaves. Treat the product's own design system as unknown.
- **"Dark" is a painted surface, not a toggle.** The home header/hero band and feature sections paint dark through `.theme.dark` elements on a light page — nothing on the public surface toggles it. The `dark` token set is the translucent-white state layer those surfaces paint, read as surface roles, not a user-selectable mode.
- **The hero's colour field is media, not CSS.** The purple-to-teal band is painted by an image/video layer behind the hero — no flat-colour token claims to be it. The teal→purple gradient token is the *logo* gradient from the StyleX layer, a different thing.
- **Singapore region.** Prices render `S$` (Free S$0, Pro S$165, Business S$250/year); the footer locale reads English (US). That's what this client was served — other regions may differ.
- **The mega-menu auto-closes.** It dismisses a few seconds after opening; flyout-state readings re-opened it first, so hover/focus values are real but the panel is ephemeral by design.
- **Focus really activates tabs.** The plan-audience tabs use ARIA automatic activation — `plan-tab`'s focus-visible state was measured on the view switching to Education. Truthful, and it swaps the page.
- **Unstyled containers read fallback fonts.** StyleX leaves some wrappers at Times/Arial; the text nodes inside carry Canva Sans. Type tokens cite the text-node readings (`textStyle`), not the wrapper's.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.

## Fonts

One family does everything the styled surface paints: **Canva Sans** at 400 (display and body), 500 (menu rows, prices, footer links) and 600 (every button, nav label and section label). The `@font-face` blocks live in the cross-origin static.canva.com bundles — the harvest lists the woff2 srcs in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The font is licensed and not distributed with the package — the tokens carry the full stack (`"Canva Sans", "IBM Plex Sans Arabic", "Noto Sans Variable", "Noto Sans", -apple-system, …`) and the style guide falls back to system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the purple on actions only.** `#8b3dff` is the Sign up pill, every plan CTA, the billing switch and mobile Log in — never a background or a text colour. See [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png).
2. **Do walk the ramp.** Every purple button darkens `#8b3dff → #7630d7 → #612dae` on hover then pressed; no side steps. See [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-hover.png) and [continue-button](components/crops/continue-button-pressed.png).
3. **Do paint states as washes.** Hover is 6% ink (or 7% white on the dark band), pressed 14% ink (15% white) — overlays, not new colours. See [oauth-button](components/crops/oauth-button-hover.png) and [signup-cta](components/crops/signup-cta-pressed.png).
4. **Do ring secondaries with the inset hairline.** Secondary buttons and fields carry `rgba(53,65,90,0.2)` as a 1px inset shadow, not a border. See [oauth-button](components/crops/oauth-button-default.png).
5. **Do pill the tabs, square the controls.** Tabs and the billing switch are 9999px pills; buttons, fields and cards take 12px — and the hero CTA stretches to 20px. See [tool-tab](components/crops/tool-tab-default.png) and [hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png).
6. **Do let the dark band breathe through the fills.** On `.theme.dark`, buttons are 90% white or transparent ghosts with a 20% white ring — the media shows through. See [login-link](components/crops/login-link-default.png) and [signup-cta](components/crops/signup-cta-default.png).
7. **Don't style the editor from this package.** Every value is the public surface's; the product behind the auth card is uncaptured.
8. **Don't read a theme toggle into `.theme.dark`.** It's a painted section treatment on a light page, not a user mode.
