# Patterns

Public marketing pages on www.raus.life, signed out. Structure from the 2026-11-19 DOM readings. Screenshots from the 2026-11-21 headless pass are in [screenshots/](screenshots). The map of controls is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Page chrome

Every measured page paints `light.surface.page` (`#fdf9ed`) on `html`. A full-width promo strip sits above the header: `light.surface.promo` (`#a6dfff`), 48px tall, padding `8px 48px`, with an orange Book now! pill at the right.

The header is transparent over the cream page. The wordmark is forest green (`light.text.forest`). Desktop nav links are 22px Neue Haas Unica. A language `select` sits at the end of the nav.

The footer is transparent, padding `32px 12px 24px`, same ink text. The newsletter control is a sky pill with orange text.

## Home

The h1 is forest green, 36px, weight 300, letter-spacing -0.54px, in Neue Haas Unica. A later section heading switches to the serif `fonts` family at 40px. Two filled ink pills (Start quiz, and later View all articles) sit under the copy. Cabins near me is the outline twin of Start quiz.

A yellow pill (`light.surface.search`), radius 40px, height 128px, holds Region, dates and guests as text buttons, plus the magenta Book your stay circle. The menu is [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Search

The h1 matches the other interior pages: 40px, weight 300, forest green, Neue Haas Unica. The yellow search pill is present. Under it, white filter chips with a 10% ink border scroll sideways. Filters is a lime pill.

Listings are a grid of photo cards. A card title is the serif at 28px. A price line is 22px ink. A secondary line ("for 2 Nights") is 14px ink at 50% opacity.

Search was not submitted. No dates were chosen.

## Journal, about, staying with us, help

These pages share the same header, promo strip, cream page and 40px forest h1. About and staying-with-us add an ink pill (Learn more, Find a cabin). Help lists questions under 36px section headings. One question row was clicked. It did not open a booking form.
