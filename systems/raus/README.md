# Raus

Raus rents sustainable cabins in Germany and Austria. This package captures the public marketing site at www.raus.life, signed out, on 19 November 2026.

Surfaces: home, about, search, journal, help, and staying with us. Theme: light only. Widths: the preview tab reported innerWidth 1440 and innerHeight 900 at devicePixelRatio 2 after a 1440 by 900 request, and innerWidth 390 and innerHeight 844 at devicePixelRatio 2 after a 390 by 844 request. No account was created. No booking, card, phone, or newsletter address was entered.

### Principles

1. Paint the page cream and the headlines forest.
2. Use ink pills for the main action and orange only on the promo.
3. Keep the search pill yellow and the book circle magenta.
4. Set section headings in the serif, UI in Neue Haas Unica.
5. Do not invent a booking form or a dark theme.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://raus.life/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. The page redirected to `https://www.raus.life/`. The title was "Your sustainable cabins in Germany and Austria | Raus". No Cloudflare challenge appeared. Computed styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on the live page.

`preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Measurements already read were kept. No screenshot was invented.

A Cookiebot banner was on the first paint. Deny was clicked. Allow all was not clicked. The banner text was gone on the next read.

The product does not publish a custom-property palette on `:root`. Colours are computed backgrounds and text colours. Adyen checkout rules in the Next.js CSS were ignored, because those controls were not painted.

## What to distrust

There are no screenshots. The coverage table names the pages that were measured.

`html` paints `#fdf9ed`. `body` computed to transparent. A reader who samples `body` will miss the cream.

The wordmark and h1 are `#006434`. Three spans computed a fill of `#006334`. Both are recorded. Headings use `#006434`.

Listing anchors computed to the browser default blue `rgb(0, 0, 238)`. Their child text computed to ink. The card text token is ink.

Focus on the ink buttons computed to a 3px white outline. That is what `focus()` returned. A keyboard-only pass was not done.

The serif family name in `@font-face` is the literal word `fonts`. The style guide does not load that file. It falls back to a system serif.

Typekit declares Neue Haas Unica at 300, 400, 500 and 600. Measured text used 300 and 400. 500 and 600 were not seen on a painted element.

`ease` in the motion tokens is the CSS keyword expanded to control points. The Book your stay bezier was measured as written.

## Do and don't

- **Paint the page cream and the headlines forest.** `html` is `#fdf9ed`. The h1 is `#006434` at weight 300. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
- **Use ink pills for the main action and orange only on the promo.** Search and Start quiz are `#23212c` with white text and a 20px radius. Book now! is `#dd5000` at 12px radius. [components.md](components.md)
- **Keep the search pill yellow and the book circle magenta.** The home search bar is `#fcbd1c` with a 40px radius. Book your stay is a 128px circle in `#b218a4`. [patterns.md](patterns.md)
- **Set section headings in the serif, UI in Neue Haas Unica.** The serif family name is `fonts`. Do not rename it. [tokens.json](tokens.json)
- **Do not invent a booking form or a dark theme.** Dates were not chosen. No card was entered. No dark rule was declared. [raw/notes.md](raw/notes.md)
