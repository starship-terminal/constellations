# adidas China patterns

How the components combine on each surface, with the screenshots that show them. Captured on adidas.com.cn on 2026-11-10, signed out, at 1440x900 and 390x844.

## Shell
A sticky top bar (~124px): utility strip (在线客服, 请登录), adidas wordmark, bold category nav (男装 男鞋 女装 女鞋 儿童 配件 更多精彩), then a campaign tag strip on #f9f9f9 (11.11 不止5折, 雪境漫游, 降温毛衣精选). [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Overlays
- **Search panel** — opened from the nav search icon. [home-light-search](screenshots/home-light-search.png)
- **Filter overlay** — on /plp/, tapping a refinement row opens the filter surface. [plp-light-filter](screenshots/plp-light-filter.png)
- **Burger drawer** — at 390 the menu button opens the nav drawer. [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)
- **WeChat tooltips** — QR-code tooltips (.lib-tooltip-content) sit behind share/contact icons; captured but not measured.

## Catalogue page
A left filter column (性别, category, price rows) over a grid of tall product tiles (338x454): image, name, ¥ price. [plp-light-default](screenshots/plp-light-default.png)

## Product page
Title, ¥ price (18/22 bold), gallery, a purchase bar (this capture showed the disabled white-with-grey "加入购物袋" state). [pdp-light-default](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png)

## Footer
A 360px foot with link columns (关于adidas…) at 11/16 and the tel:400-999-5999 service line. [plp-light-default](screenshots/plp-light-default.png)

## Mobile
The header collapses to wordmark + burger; tiles go single-column. [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-plp-light-default](screenshots/mobile-plp-light-default.png)
