# adidas China patterns

How the components combine on each surface, with the screenshots that show them. Captured on adidas.com.cn on 2026-11-21, signed out, at 1440x900 and 390x844. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Shell
A sticky top bar (about 124px): a black utility strip (在线客服, 请登录), the adidas wordmark, bold category labels (男装 男鞋 女装 女鞋 儿童 配件 更多精彩), a search pill, a bag icon, and a profile icon. [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

## Overlays
- **Search panel.** The search pill opens a field and hot-search chips. Nothing was submitted. [home-light-search](screenshots/home-light-search.png)
- **Filter row.** On /plp/, 筛选 opens the filter surface. [plp-light-filter](screenshots/plp-light-filter.png)
- **Login drawer.** 请登录 and the profile icon open an adiClub phone login. That branch stops. Not screenshotted.
- **Catalog tree.** The desktop mega-menu did not open in this session. The same links are a page at /siteMap and, on the phone, the 分类 tab. [sitemap-light-default](screenshots/sitemap-light-default.png), [mobile-category-light-default](screenshots/mobile-category-light-default.png)

## Catalogue page
A filter chip row (性别, 产品分类, 品牌, 运动类型, 服饰类型, 折扣力度) over a grid of product tiles: image, name, ¥ price. [plp-light-default](screenshots/plp-light-default.png)

## Product page
Title, ¥ price, gallery, reviews, and a purchase bar. This capture's pinned SKU (JP1477) showed the disabled white-with-grey 加入购物袋 state. The bar was not pressed. [pdp-light-default](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png)

## Empty bag
"您的购物袋是空的" plus a recommendation row. [cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png)

## Footer and policy
Link columns, a published service line, and a privacy statement. The statement's registered-address span is painted out. [privacy-light-default](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png)

## Phone
A search pill and a four-item tab bar (首页, 分类, 购物袋, 我的). No burger. The account tab is a login wall. [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-account-light-default](screenshots/mobile-account-light-default.png)
