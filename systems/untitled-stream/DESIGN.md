# untitled

This file is a short export of [tokens.json](tokens.json). It was written by hand from measured tokens. It was not generated by `make design-md`, because this capture does not edit [catalog.json](../../catalog.json). Take values from tokens.json. This file has no per-value provenance.

## Overview

Public signed-out untitled.stream, refreshed 2026-11-22. The marketing site follows the browser colour scheme. Dark paints `#191919` with a white Enter App pill. Light paints white with a black pill. Type is Untitled Sans. Chrome is bracketed: `[shop]`, `[membership]`, `[starter]`.

## Colors

- page, dark: `#191919` (computed body background)
- page, light: `#ffffff` (light colour-scheme capture; `--color-primary-background`)
- text, dark: `#ffffff`
- text, light: `#191919`
- muted: `#7e7e7e` (computed footer link)
- action, dark: `#ffffff` (computed Enter App fill)
- action, light: `#242424` to `#101010` (declared `--color-button-background-primary`)
- on action, dark: `#191919`
- surface, dark: `#202020`
- surface, light: `#f2f2f2`
- frost: `#262626` at 90% (computed consent card)
- pill wash: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` (computed iOS pill and sign-up buttons)
- untitled blue: `#5a8ff7` (declared `--color-untitled-blue`)
- untitled pink: `#e699ab` (declared `--color-untitled-pink`)

## Typography

Untitled Sans. Super title 26px, weight 500, line height 32px, tracking -0.78px. Body 15px, weight 400, line height 20px, tracking -0.45px. Enter App 13px, weight 500, tracking -0.39px. IBM Plex Mono is loaded and was not used on the measured marketing controls.

## Layout

Enter App is 150 by 44, fully rounded. Sign-up buttons are 342 wide and 52 to 54 tall, radius 28px. Consent card is 500 by 224, radius 16px. Header on the dark homepage measured 152px. Desktop captures are 1440 by 900. Phone captures were requested at 390 by 844 and saved at 780 by 1688 because devicePixelRatio was 2.

## Elevation and depth

Consent card shadow: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2) 0px 4px 10px 0px`. The marketing page is otherwise flat. The bottom pill bar sits over the disc hero.

## Shapes

Pills are fully rounded. Auth buttons are 28px. The consent card is 16px.

## Components

Enter App is the filled action on the dark homepage. [shop] and [membership] are quiet bracketed links. The bottom bar is a row of frosted pills plus one filled hiring pill. Pricing uses two plan cards and a Yearly / Monthly radio. The shop is a two-card grid. Full notes are in [components.md](components.md).
