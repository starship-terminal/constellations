# Patterns

## Marketing chrome

The homepage header is a tagline on the left and `[shop]` `[membership]` Enter App on the right. There is no separate logo on the homepage. The wordmark `[untitled]` appears as an h1 with no visible width on that page, and as a real link on pricing, trust, and the welcome gate.

## Hero and pill bar

A disc carousel fills the first screen. A frosted pill bar sits on top of it: social links, We're hiring, Learn more, and the three store pills. Learn more replaces the discs with a product demo. That demo is public marketing, not a signed-in session.

## Pricing rail

Two plan cards, `[starter]` free and `[membership]` at $49.99 / year, with a Yearly / Monthly radio. Subscribe goes to the login wall and was not followed.

## Shop

A two-up grid, then a product page with a photo, a size label, a quantity stepper, and Add to Cart. The cart drawer opens empty. Nothing was added.

## Auth

`/login` redirects to `/welcome`. Two buttons, no fields. Create account and I have an account each open a provider list. The branch stopped there.

## Phone

At 390 the homepage centers the tagline, swaps Enter App for an App Store badge, and drops the shop and membership links. Pricing stacks the cards.
