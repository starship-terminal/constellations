Palette

The palette is roles, not ramps: primary-label near-black on primary-background white, secondary-label grey, hairline shading/border fills, then the accents — action blue, the untitled brand pink/blue, and the DAW production set (red, yellow, the tape-label pair). Dark mode remaps the same roles.

Type

One voice: Untitled Sans for everything, at a signature tight tracking (−0.39px subheads to −1.64px the mobile display). The type scale is named like a DAW: super-title 26/32, large-title 22/26, title 18/22, body 15/20, subhead 13/16, footnote 12/16, note-mono 12/18 in IBM Plex Mono.

Radius and geometry

Two shapes: radius.card 20px (plan cards, the selected segment) and radius.pill — rounded-full resolving to CSS infinity, so every pill is a full capsule. The consent card rounds at 16px.

Motion and layout

Motion is quiet: the hovers are transition-opacity at 150–300ms on the standard ease curve; a 1200px perspective backs the disc tilts.
