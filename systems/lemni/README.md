# Lemni design reference

A token set and component reference for Lemni, the product that sets up custom AI agents for customer interactions. Refreshed on 2026-11-21 from the public site only, desktop 1440 and phone 390. Home and changelog paint dark (`#0d0d12`). Pricing, docs, blog, company, brand, support, privacy, terms and the sign-in shell paint light (`#f6f6f8`). The marketing site is Framer. The sign-in shell at `https://lemni.com/signin` is a separate React page (styled-components, system-ui). The workplace app was not entered. `login-extracted` stays not-started.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export lemni` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the home page dark and every other page light.
2. Do make the primary control a black pill, not the violet chip.
3. Do set display type in TWK Lausanne 250 with tight tracking.
4. Do keep the sign-in shell on system-ui.
5. Don't treat reduced motion as honoured.
6. Don't invent a workplace sidebar the public site does not show.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Headless Chromium via Playwright on 2026-11-21. `tools/capture/public/public.mjs` shot the marketing routes at 1440 × 900 (device scale 1) and 390 × 844 (device scale 2). Cookie and consent overlays are hidden in CSS if they appear. None rendered, and none were clicked. `vars.mjs` dumped the marketing stylesheets (259 rules, 195 font faces, 44 `--framer-*` names, no `--token-*` palette). The sign-in shell declares no custom properties. `measure.mjs` tagged public controls, forced hover, focus-visible and `:active` through DevTools, and wrote computed styles plus a crop. `motion-probe.mjs` read the public pages, then the same elements under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. `layout.mjs` recorded nav, headline and body ground at both widths.

The sign-in page overwrites `window.devicePixelRatio`. The screenshot helper trusts the scale stored at launch (`window.__captureDPR`) so the phone sign-in PNG matches the 2x capture.

No account was used. Continue with Google was not clicked. The email field was photographed empty. Continue was not clicked. The map of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## What to distrust

- **No published palette.** Framer does not declare a `--token-*` set. The sign-in shell declares no custom properties. Semantic colours are computed styles of real elements, plus two declared hairlines (`--border-color`). The sheets reference `--framer-link-text-color` but never declare it, so the link blue `#0099ff` was entered by hand.
- **TWK Lausanne weight is the family name.** The hero reports `font-weight: 400` and `font-family: "TWK Lausanne 250"`. The 250 is the face, not a computed weight.
- **Auth Continue label.** The black button's computed label colour is `rgb(100, 100, 101)`, not white. The crop is the evidence. Do not recolour it to white.
- **`feature-card` is not a card.** The locator matched a large decorative region. Ignore that crop as a component spec.
- **Spacing 2, 3 and 4 were entered by hand** on a 4 px base and are labelled Reconstruction. 5, 10 and 12 are measured.
- **Hand-entered colours.** `core.color.ground.light-card` and `light.background.raised` (`#f5f5f7`), `light.background.surface` (white), `core.color.accent.violet` (`#5500ff`), `core.color.accent.link` (`#0099ff`) and `core.color.price.struck` (`#cacacd`) were entered by hand at capture time. No raw reading holds them: the pricing card, the violet chip and the struck price were never measured. They are labelled Reconstruction in tokens.json.
- **Roles drawn from other elements.** `text.secondary` (light `#646465`) is the computed label of the sign-in Continue button, and `color.ink.700` (`#20232e`) is that button's hover fill. No pricing subtitle or feature line was measured. tokens.json names the element each value was read from.
- **Readings taken with automation hidden.** Every committed reading and screenshot was taken by scripts that masked `navigator.webdriver` and turned off Chromium's AutomationControlled flag. The scripts no longer do; a recapture records any bot check as a boundary instead.
- **components.md has no builder.** It was written at capture time; no builder in tools/ regenerates it.
- **Pressed and focus were forced.** A script that runs only on a real pointer event is not in these crops. The Google button's pressed shrink is the one clear change.
- **Reduced motion is ignored.** The probe kept 15 moving elements, including the home `dots` loop. There are no authored `prefers-reduced-motion` rules in the readable sheets.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Full-colour originals stay in the scratch directory and are not in git.

## Fonts

- **TWK Lausanne** (250, 300, 400, 500, 600) is the marketing face. It is proprietary. It is not in this package. The style guide falls back to system-ui. Inter is also declared on the marketing sheets and is not the measured UI face.
- **Roboto Mono** is declared as a `@font-face` and is not the UI face. Token `core.font.family.mono` names it.
- **system-ui** is the sign-in shell. No fallback stack is required for that surface beyond what the page already sets.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the home page dark and the product pages light.** Home and changelog are `#0d0d12` with display ink `#e3e8ff`. Pricing, docs and the rest are `#f6f6f8` with black ink. There is no theme switch. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) [screenshots/changelog-dark-default.png](screenshots/changelog-dark-default.png)
- **Do make the primary control a black pill, not the violet chip.** Light pages use a 40 px radius black button. Home uses a glass black pill at 140 px radius. The violet `#5500ff` is decoration. [components/crops/plan-cta-default.png](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png) [components/crops/hero-continue-default.png](components/crops/hero-continue-default.png)
- **Do set display type in TWK Lausanne 250 with tight tracking.** The home hero is 94 px, line-height 81.78 px, tracking -4.7 px. The computed weight stays 400 because the weight is the family name. [components/crops/hero-title-default.png](components/crops/hero-title-default.png)
- **Do keep the sign-in shell on system-ui.** The auth card is 12 px radius, not the marketing pill. The Continue label computes grey on the black bar. [screenshots/signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png) [components/crops/auth-continue-default.png](components/crops/auth-continue-default.png)
- **Don't treat reduced motion as honoured.** The home `dots` loop and the sign-in transitions keep running under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Don't invent a workplace sidebar the public site does not show.** Docs navigation is a section row plus a side list. The signed-in app was not entered on this pass. [screenshots/docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png)
