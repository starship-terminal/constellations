# JetBrains patterns

How the public pages combine the components. Captured 2026-11-21, signed out, at 1440 and 390. Which control opens which page is in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Site header

Every marketing page shares a dark 72px header: the JetBrains wordmark, six text items (Products, For Business, Education, Solutions, Support, Pricing), then search, account, cart and language icon buttons. Products opens a white mega menu. On a phone the items collapse into "Open main menu".

Shown in [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Dark campaign home

The home page is a dark campaign, not a theme the visitor picks. A purple announcement strip sits under the header. The hero is a full-bleed photograph with a near-black "Explore Air" pill and a white text link. Below it, three audience cards (For devs, For teams, For orgs) sit on `#19191c`.

Shown in [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Product page

IntelliJ IDEA's page is a long product story: a hero, feature bands, and Download and Buy links. Download goes to the products index. Buy goes to the store. Neither was followed into a download or a checkout.

Shown in [idea-light-default](screenshots/idea-light-default.png).

## Store and pricing

The store is a light page. A secondary nav (Store, Toolbox, Team Tools, AI, Learning, Plugins, For Business, Contact Sales) sits under the site header. Audience tabs switch Organizations, Individual, Special Categories and Students. Billing toggles yearly and monthly. Price cards carry a title, a short description, a price and a Buy pill. "Best offer" marks the All Products Pack.

Shown in [store-light-default](screenshots/store-light-default.png). Buy was not pressed. Checkout is gated.

## Documentation index

Help is a light index of product documentation cards. No signed-in support console is on this page.

Shown in [help-light-default](screenshots/help-light-default.png).

## Company

Company is a dark long page, "Make it happen. With code.", sharing the same header.

Shown in [company-light-default](screenshots/company-light-default.png).

## Academy

`/education/` redirects to `/academy/`. The page is light grey with a purple "Explore catalog" pill, a Students/Courses switch, and a purple announcement strip.

Shown in [education-light-default](screenshots/education-light-default.png) and [academy-light-default](screenshots/academy-light-default.png).

## Login boundary

account.jetbrains.com/login is a white card on a purple-to-yellow gradient. It offers Google, GitHub and Apple as labelled rows, then GitLab, WeChat and Bitbucket as icon buttons, then "Continue with email" and "Create an account". None of those were clicked. No email field was opened. Profile and licenses were not reached.

Shown in [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [oauth-icon-row-default](components/crops/oauth-icon-row-default.png).

## Search

Search opens from the header icon as a dark field with the hint "⌘K for advanced search". No query was submitted.

Shown in [home-light-search](screenshots/home-light-search.png).
