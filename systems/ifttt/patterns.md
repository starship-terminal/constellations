# Patterns

How the public pages combine the controls. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/).

## App shell

Desktop header is a wordmark, a search field, Explore, Plans, an outlined Log in, and a filled Get started. Phone collapses Explore and Plans into a hamburger drawer with Log in and Get started pinned to the bottom. The footer repeats Explore, Services, Stories, Applets, MCP, Help, and the company links. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Marketing home

A large ink headline, a short subhead, a prompt field, and one black pill. Illustrated service cards sit behind the headline. Further down, tabs swap applet cards, a service logo row, solution cards, and story spotlight tabs. [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-tab-business.png](screenshots/home-light-tab-business.png), [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Search

The header field is always visible on desktop. Typing a public query opens a suggestion list on the page. [home-light-search.png](screenshots/home-light-search.png). Phone opens the same field from the search icon. [mobile-home-light-search.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-search.png).

## Explore

A prompt, example buttons, and tabs for All, Applets, Services, and Stories. Category chips filter the grid. The pages are long. The phone explore full page grew without a useful bound and was not kept. [explore-light-default.png](screenshots/explore-light-default.png).

## Plans

Four cards in a row. Yearly is the default, with a blue switch and a clover SAVE 30% badge. The Pro card is marked RECOMMENDED and uses the filled button. Monthly is [plans-light-monthly.png](screenshots/plans-light-monthly.png). About buttons open tooltips. FAQ summaries sit below. Get started on a paid plan redirects to signup.

## Service, applet, connection

A coloured hero, Back, Copy, and Share. Share opens a small dialog. Ingredient filters (All, Triggers, Queries, Actions) and a Load more control sit under the applet list. [service-light-default.png](screenshots/service-light-default.png), [service-light-share.png](screenshots/service-light-share.png).

## Docs

A left section nav and a long reference. "Open the menu" is the phone-width control for that nav. Code samples have Copy. [docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png).

## Login wall

A centered column: logo, title, two fields, a text link, one black pill, a divider, then Google, Apple, and Facebook. The branch stops there. [login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png), [join-light-default.png](screenshots/join-light-default.png).
