IFTTT, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the few real custom properties the site declares (light.misc.* — --card-color, --hero-unit, --primary-text, the explore cycler's --cycle-*), the light.color.* semantic roles measured off the painted components (the white ground, the near-black ink, the black action pill, the outlined secondary, the hairline borders), the core.palette.* literals the Sass-compiled classes hard-code, the proprietary Museo Sans Rounded face as a name (never shipped), and the measured geometry (component.*) read off the live DOM — the header, the pills, the prompt card, the switch and the cards. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from ifttt.com and ifttt.ai, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 (mobile pass at 390×844). IFTTT paints one theme: there is no theme control on any surface, and the dark grounds on /explore and the service/applet/connect heroes are painted sections — their values live under light.component.dark as the measured values they are; no dark set was fabricated. Museo Sans Rounded is licensed and not bundled: samples fall back to a rounded system face. Sign-in, the ‘Connect’ install control, checkout, help.ifttt.com (403 to automation) and the app-store links are documented boundaries, not captures.

The surface

IFTTT is a light-on-white marketing system built on rounded shapes and one strong action: near-black filled pills (“Get started”, “Start building”, “Connect”) against white grounds and pastel section tints, with dark painted heroes on /explore and the detail pages where the same components invert (“Get started today” is a white pill on near-black). The frame is header.unified-header (wordmark, search field, Explore / Plans, Log in, Get started) and the content primitives are the service-icon tile, the applet and service cards, the prompt card, the featured tabs, the billing switch and the plan cards.

Palette

The semantic roles under light.* are the painted surface — the white ground, the near-black ink ramp, the black action fill and the outlined secondary. light.component.dark.* holds the dark painted sections (the /explore ground and the detail heroes, the on-dark chips and white-pill action) measured in place, and core.palette.* the literals the Sass-compiled classes declare outside the thin token layer — near-blacks, the off-white tints and the brand-pastel section colours.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

IFTTT declares no spacing scale — the stack is Sass classes — so spacing, radius and control geometry are measured: component.* carries the header height, button and field heights, card and control corners (the pills clamp to 9999px), the switch track and the service-icon tile. motion.* is the measured set from the reduced-motion probe: the durations and easing curves elements actually run, the transitions and animations (the explore cycler included), and what prefers-reduced-motion changes.

Shadows

Type

One proprietary face, recorded by name only: Museo Sans Rounded in weights 300/500/700/900 plus italics, served from web-assets.ifttt.com — the 900 black carries the display headlines, 700 the buttons and card titles, 500/400 body text. No type ramp is declared; the core.typography.* composites carry the measured styles — the hero, the explore display, headings, body, buttons, tabs and the applet byline. Samples fall back to a rounded system face because Museo Sans Rounded is licensed and never shipped.
