# Mercari components

What was visible on the signed-out shop on 2026-11-22, and what was not measured.

## Header

77px bar, padding 0 40px. Homepage: navy `#252b60`, white wordmark, white labels. Interior: white bar, primary wordmark, ink labels. Search is a 44px pill, `#f5f5f6`, 1px `#ecedf1` border, placeholder "Search for anything". Download app, Sign up, and Log in are 16px, weight 600. Notifications and Cart are 40px icon buttons. On the homepage a red badge sat on Notifications and read 3. Sell is 64 by 32, white, radius 4px, 14px weight 600. On interior pages that button reads List an item and paints `#5356ee`.

## Category row

Homepage only. 92px, under the header. Each item is a 40px line icon over a 14px primary label. Eighteen items fit at 1440, ending in View all. Interior pages replace this row with a text nav: Brands, Discover Trends, the categories, View all.

## Homepage tiles

A promo banner links to a free-shipping landing page. Under it, three cards name Gaming, Figurines, and Trading Cards, each with a "See more" link. A second row starts Electronics, Toys, Beauty, and Handbags. Section titles such as Top brands are 20px, weight 600, letter-spacing -0.64px.

## Category

Electronics title is 26px, weight 600, letter-spacing -1px, ink. A count sits under it (`+147K`, "New listings each week"). Sale cells show a red percent badge, a photo, a truncated title, a current price, and a struck previous price. The struck price measured `#6b6b6b`, 16px, weight 400. Subcategory chips (Media, Video games & consoles, and the rest) are pills. The seller row under those chips was cropped out of the photograph because it prints usernames.

## About

A primary hero, 322px, holds a 65px white title, line-height 65px, letter-spacing -1.24px. Body copy below is 16px Averta on white, in a centered column. View openings is a primary button, 200 by 44, radius 4px, 16px weight 600. It was not clicked.

## Brands

Title is 26px, weight 600, navy `#252b60`. A letter index runs A to Z and #. Brand names are 14px links in primary. The first name read was Abbott, linking to `/us/brand/abbott-15/`.

## Footer

Navy block. Column labels are 16px, weight 600, white. Links are 14px, weight 400, white. The legal bar is `#f5f5f6`, 69px, with 10px `#6b6b6b` links. It prints a mailing address, so it is not in the photographs.

## Gaps

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured. Phone width was not captured. No error state was shown. Search, Sign up, Log in, Sell, List an item, Notifications, and Cart were not opened. No element crops were taken.
