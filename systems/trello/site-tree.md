# Trello public site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-22 from [https://trello.com/](https://trello.com/) while signed out. Title: "Capture, organize, and tackle your to-dos from anywhere". The first visible heading is "Capture, organize, and tackle your to-dos from anywhere." This is a live product site, not a parked domain and not a captcha wall. The page painted light. `color-scheme` was `normal`.

Desktop shots are 1440 by 900 CSS pixels at device pixel ratio 2 (PNG 2880 by 1800). Phone shots are 390 by 844 CSS pixels at device pixel ratio 2 (PNG 780 by 1688). They are the first viewport, not the full page. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.

Nothing was typed. No email, password, code, phone, or address was entered. No form was submitted. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. No account was created. No board was created. `login-extracted` stays `not-started`.

origin/main had no `systems/trello/`. This walk adds the package. catalog.json and webapps-manifest.json were left alone. `systems/atlassian/` was not touched.

Query strings that carry a campaign or a locale were stripped from the recorded URL.
