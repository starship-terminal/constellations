# Sana AI

Sana is an AI work product from Sana Labs. This package records the public marketing page at sana.ai and the signed-out sign-in at sana.ai/sign-in-to-sana, measured on 19 November 2026. The home page was read at innerWidth 1169 and at 390. The sign-in page was read at 1169, including the email field, the code step and one FAQ item. An AgentMail address was typed into the email field. The one-time code was not entered. Google was not clicked. No company SSO was offered.

The home page paints a light token set. The sign-in page sets `html.dark` and paints the dark set.

### Principles

1. Use ink for the home buttons and fog for the sign-in page.
2. Sign in is a 36px pill and Explore is a 24px pill.
3. The product cards are photographs with a 24px radius.
4. Do not paint the lime accent on these pages.
5. Do not draw a sign-in error.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview tab opened https://sana.ai/ and https://sana.ai/sign-in-to-sana. Computed styles, custom properties and font faces were read from those live pages. The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`). Home screenshots at 1440 and 390 were taken in headless Playwright after the same URLs had already loaded in the preview tab with no Cloudflare challenge. A headless shot of the sign-in page was taken and discarded: the preview tab paints that page dark (`html.dark`, background `#131314`), and the headless shot painted it light, so it is not evidence.

Home colours come from the `--color-*` declarations on `:root`, checked against the computed style of the page, the Sign in link and the Explore button. Sign-in colours come from the same names under `.dark`, checked against the computed style of the page, the email field, the nav pill and the Back button. Focus on Sign in and on the code input was a real focus in the preview tab.

## What to distrust

The home headline size is the computed size at innerWidth 1169, 61.52px. It is not a token. At 390 it is 44px. Neither is the `--text-3xl` value of 34px, which is the sign-in title.

`--font-weight-bold` is declared as 500, the same as medium. The home buttons use weight 450, which is `--font-weight-book`.

`--color-background-accent` is `#cdfe00` and `--color-brand-lime` is `#0d0`. Neither was painted on a measured control. The ink buttons are `#0a1217`.

The sign-in hero panel computed to `rgb(23, 24, 26)`, which is not a named custom property. The image inside it had no painted source.

Sign in focus and the code-input focus are the browser outline `rgb(229, 151, 0) auto 1px`. The email field focus did not change its border. A keyboard-intent rule in the home stylesheet sets a 2px ink outline. That attribute was not on the page.

The Products dropdown was in the DOM and hidden. Its shadow is a stylesheet value, not a dropdown that was open on screen.

A headless screenshot of the sign-in page disagreed with the live page, so it is not in the package. There is no picture of the dark form.

## Fonts

Sana Sans is a variable woff2 at `https://sana.ai/public-static/fonts/SanaSans-Variable-251029.woff2`. Sana Serif Regular and Italic are woff2 files on the same host. IBM Plex Mono is loaded from Google Fonts and was not the computed family of any measured text. None of these files are in the package. The tokens name Sana Sans and fall back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **Use ink for the home buttons and fog for the sign-in page.** Sign in and Explore are `#0a1217` on white. The sign-in page is `#131314` with `#e8e8e8` type. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Sign in is a 36px pill and Explore is a 24px pill.** Both are ink with a white label. See [components/crops/button-signin-default.png](components/crops/button-signin-default.png) and [components/crops/button-explore-default.png](components/crops/button-explore-default.png).
- **The product cards are photographs with a 24px radius.** The title is 20px / 450. See [components/crops/card-product.png](components/crops/card-product.png).
- **Do not paint the lime accent on these pages.** `#cdfe00` is declared and was not on a measured control.
- **Do not draw a sign-in error.** The disabled submit stays disabled until an address is present, and the code was not entered.
