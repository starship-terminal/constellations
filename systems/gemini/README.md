# Gemini design reference

A token set and component reference for Gemini, Google's AI assistant app for iOS (https://gemini.google.com). Use it to build a native iOS chat assistant (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-empty page (pure black in dark, warm off-white in light) with a glow rising behind the composer, iOS 26 glass circles and capsules floating over the content, the owner's messages in a bubble and the assistant's replies as plain text, rimless grouped cards in settings, and Google's Material primary blue for filled actions beside a brighter system-style blue for links and switches. It was captured from the shipped app on iOS 26 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's Google AI Pro account: dark on 2026-10-23 with the app's Theme on System default and the phone in dark mode, light on 2026-10-24 with Settings > Theme > Light, put back to System default afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 42 captures: 23 in dark (onboarding, home, the composer, the + sheet, the model picker, Task mode, the sidebar, a thread, menus, settings, search, Students, Scheduled, Images, the account sheet) and 19 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) nothing in them is masked: they show the owner's name, photo, email address and notebook and chat titles, and no credentials or personally identifiable data.

This package contains no Gemini code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Google's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the Gemini spark, the icons, the sample images or the brand colours.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export gemini` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do keep the page empty and let the glow carry the colour.
2. Do put the owner in a bubble and the assistant in plain text.
3. Do float controls as glass circles and capsules instead of drawing bars.
4. Do give the two blues separate jobs.
5. Do let the mode recolour the screen.
6. Don't fill destructive actions red.
7. Do group settings in rimless cards with inset hairlines.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Gemini is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/gemini/raw-shots/`, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 197 readings of 140 named values, many in both themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, the glow, a thin mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height, or a row pitch when run over the first letters of a list |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights and the font family |

Scale steps (spacing, radii, row heights) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in one theme only (onboarding, the primary button, the sidebar's current row, the new-model dot in dark; the caret in light), the other theme's token is Derived and says what it was filled from.

## What to distrust

- **The font.** `fontFamily.base` is Google Sans, identified by eye from the letterforms, not read from the app. Google may use Google Sans Text or Flex for body sizes; the captures cannot tell them apart.
- **Glass.** The nav circles, the composer, menus, the + sheet and the search field are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it: the chat menu over the video reads warm grey, the + sheet reads blue from the glow. Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **The glow is a gradient.** `bg.glow` and `bg.glowTask` hold only the colour at the bottom edge (29% and 27% of their boxes). Its shape and stops were not measured.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: grouped cards read 25.6 pt (26 used), suggestion cards 23.9, menus 29.0, the account sheet 37.6, the owner's two-line bubble 39 to 41. The bubble radius was read on one two-line bubble; a one-line bubble may be a capsule.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em; the Settings title was read on an 'S', whose overshoot may add a point. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Light covers 19 of the 23 screens.** Onboarding, the permission alert and the Create images intro are dark only, so `button.primary`, `button.signIn` and `surface.signIn` are Derived in light. The light sidebar was scrolled past New chat, so `surface.selected` is Derived in light.
- **The scrim alphas differ by theme**: 66% in dark (read on the menu button, since the dark page is black) and 20% in light (read on the page). Both come from the + sheet only.
- **Two blues.** `accent.primary` (#A8C6FA dark, #0957CF light) is Google's Material primary; `accent.bright` (#3286FE, both themes) is the link and iOS-switch blue. They are separate roles, not a mistake in one.
- **No loading, streaming, error, off or disabled state.** No prompt was sent, no switch was off and no control was disabled; Delete was not tapped.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; the glow, glass and photos band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page empty and let the glow carry the colour.** Home is a black or off-white page with one centred greeting; the only colour is the glow rising behind the composer. See [04-home](screenshots/dark/04-home-greeting-chat-task-toggle-composer.png) and [04-home light](screenshots/light/04-home-greeting-chat-task-toggle-composer.png).
2. **Do put the owner in a bubble and the assistant in plain text.** Replies run full width on the page with an action row under them; only the owner's messages get a container. See [10-thread](screenshots/dark/10-chat-thread-media-bubble-actions.png) and [reply-actions](components/crops/reply-actions.png).
3. **Do float controls as glass circles and capsules instead of drawing bars.** The menu, new chat and avatar sit in 44 pt glass circles at the top, and the composer and the Chat / Task pill float at the bottom; content scrolls under them. See [nav-bar-home](components/crops/nav-bar-home.png) and [composer-default](components/crops/composer-default.png).
4. **Do give the two blues separate jobs.** The Material primary fills actions (Add, the Material switch, Done); the bright blue marks links, the iOS switch, the new-model dot and the avatar ring. See [button-tonal-add](components/crops/button-tonal-add.png), [switch-system-on](components/crops/switch-system-on.png) and [link-inline](components/crops/link-inline.png).
5. **Do let the mode recolour the screen.** Task turns the glow and the voice button purple and changes the greeting and placeholder; Chat keeps them blue. See [08-task](screenshots/dark/08-task-mode-suggestions-purple.png) and [composer-task](components/crops/composer-task.png).
6. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Delete is red text with a red outline icon, last in an ordinary glass menu. See [menu-chat-destructive](components/crops/menu-chat-destructive.png).
7. **Do group settings in rimless cards with inset hairlines.** Cards have no border; dividers start at the row text, not the card edge, and section headers sit outside the card in tertiary text. See [12-settings](screenshots/dark/12-settings-sheet-grouped.png) and [card-grouped](components/crops/card-grouped.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/gemini/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/gemini/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass nav circles, menus, sheets, the iOS switch and pop-up selects rather than redrawing them; the Material switch and the tonal button are Google's own and need drawing. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
