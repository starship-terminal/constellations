Palette

Warm naturals over ink: natural-white (undyed-wool beige) and its steps, the near-black ink family with fixed-opacity variants, then Tailwind's gray/neutral ramps and small accent sets. stroke (#cdcdcd) is the universal hairline.

Type

Two voices: Self Modern for headlines and product titles, Geograph for everything else. Buttons and nav links are uppercase xs medium with wide tracking; the scale is Tailwind's steps plus a fluid ramp that resolves at the viewport.

Radius and geometry

Two shapes: radius.card 20px on hero and photo cards, and radius.pill (full) on every button, field, chip and swatch. Small squares use the 2-12px steps.

Motion and layers

One motion vocabulary: sine easings for small state changes, snappy for the drawers that slide in on z-takeover. Layering is named, not numeric: the header sits at --z-header 80, every takeover at 90.
