# Components

Measured on the public Fantastical pages on 2026-11-22. States that were not on screen are listed as not captured.

## Button

Try for Free in the header is a filled pill. Background `#408fff`, label `#ffffff`, 15px, weight 500, height 32px, radius 24px.

Start for Free in the hero is the same fill and radius at 17px, height 44px, padding `0 31px`.

Sign In in the desktop header is a text button. Colour `#408fff`, transparent background, 15px, weight 500, height 32px.

Hover, focus, pressed, and disabled were not measured.

## Link

Pricing, Support, and Blog are 17px, weight 500, colour `#1d1e1f`, letter-spacing -0.1px.

## Text field

The login wall has empty Email and Password fields. The signup wall has an empty Email field. Neither was typed in. The support page has a search field with placeholder "Ask a question…". It was left empty. The Android page and the mailing-list row have an email field. Both were left empty.

## Not present on the pages opened

Checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, menu beyond the phone header, tooltip, dialog, toast, table, avatar, badge as a component, select. Pricing uses Annually and Monthly as in-page links, not a tab widget that was measured separately.
