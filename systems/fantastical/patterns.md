# Patterns

## App shell

The marketing shell is a fixed 44px header and a four-group footer. At 1440 the header shows the product links. At 390 a menu button opens the same links in a full-width list.

## Pricing

https://flexibits.com/pricing shows Free, Individual, Family, and Team. The annual figures read on the page were $0.00, $4.75, and $7.50. Plan cards link to the signup wall. Those links were not clicked.

## Empty

A missing path shows a 404 page with the heading "404" and the sentence "Page not found."

## Auth wall

Sign In and Sign Up are on hub.flexibits.com. Both were photographed empty. Continue with Apple was not clicked. No field was filled.

## Where it stops

Checkout, downloads, store listings, identity providers, and the signed-in account were not entered. The mailing list was not submitted.
