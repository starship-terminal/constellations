Captured 2026-11-19 from a signed-out T3 preview tab at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, device pixel ratio 2. Light is the Framer marketing pages. Dark is the live `ptf-dark` / `ptf-app` shell on login and Explore. No account was created. No phone number was entered. No event was created.

### Principles

1. Use cream, ink, and a black hero for the marketing site.
2. Set marketing display in Partiful Janky Inter Display Regular at weight 400.
3. Use TWK Lausanne Pan for marketing UI.
4. Keep marketing nav pills at 200px and hero buttons at 4px.
5. Treat the app shell as its own theme.
6. Leave the phone field and create flow alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

I opened https://partiful.com/ in a T3 preview tab (`reuseExistingTab: false`) and read `getComputedStyle` on the live page. The page is Framer. Stylesheets are inline, so custom properties were readable in the CSSOM. Button chrome is painted on an inner `div`, not on the anchor, so I measured that inner node.

I then opened https://partiful.com/about, https://partiful.com/login, and https://partiful.com/explore. Login and Explore are a separate app (`html.ptf-app` / `html.ptf-dark`) with its own custom properties. I resolved those on `:root`. I did not change the theme. Marketing has no theme attribute. The app shell was already dark.

Viewport at read time: `innerWidth` 1169, `innerHeight` 731, `devicePixelRatio` 2, `visualViewport` scale 1. That is the preview pane, not a requested 1440 or 390.

## What to distrust

The 2026-11-19 preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." That pass has no crops. Layout claims in the token notes come from DOM measurements. The catalog cover is still [icon.png](icon.png).

A later public walk on 2026-11-22 photographed the marketing pages and the empty walls. Those files are in [screenshots/](screenshots/). The map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). `catalog.json` was left alone.

`--background-color` resolved to `#252464` and `--primary-color` to `#ab76f6` on login and Explore. The painted page fill was `rgb(17, 17, 17)`. Those two token colours are recorded as declared values. They were not the painted page or the painted buttons on the screens I opened.

`--danger-color` (`#fa304b`) and `--warning-color` (`#ffc562`) resolved and were not painted. Marketing declared `#31c431`, `#ffae00`, `#ffcee3`, `#001666`, and `red`. I did not see those paint on the nodes I sampled.

The marketing favicon is 32 px. [icon.png](icon.png) is that file scaled to 128 px, so it is soft.

Programmatic `focus()` on the phone field did not paint an outline.

The home hero phone mock is a public illustration using SF Pro Text. It is not the product UI.

## Do and don't

- **Use cream, ink, and a black hero for the marketing site.** The page is `#f7f7f7`, ink is `#111111`, the nav is 80% `#fdfdfb`, and the hero is `#000000`.
- **Set marketing display in Partiful Janky Inter Display Regular at weight 400.** The home h1 was 64px on 64px, uppercase. Do not invent a bold weight for that face.
- **Use TWK Lausanne Pan for marketing UI.** Nav is 500 at 16px. Buttons are 600 at 16px. Body is 300.
- **Keep marketing nav pills at 200px and hero buttons at 4px.** Both were on the same page. The 800px pill belongs to the app shell.
- **Treat the app shell as its own theme.** Login and Explore paint `#111111` with Lausanne, not the Framer marketing tokens.
- **Leave the phone field and create flow alone.** Login asks for a phone number. Create an invite starts an event. This package stops at the public page.
