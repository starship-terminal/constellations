Explore, measured

explore.family was an ad-free social platform for creatives — a white feed under a signature mid-grey field (#9d9d9d: fixed header, hero, media placeholders; the auth welcome panel is the darker #717171), one yellow accent (#ffc60a: Sign up free, the about-page CTA, the PRO badge), dark 7px-cornered submits (#262626), UntitledSans for UI with UntitledSansMedium post titles and LabilGrotesk-Medium display. Every value is a hardcoded literal mined from the compiled stylesheet or a computed-style measurement — the site declared no design-token layer.

Read on 2026-11-14 from the archived 2023-02-23 build (the product closed 2023-04-19 and the domain later lapsed); every page was replayed locally from an Internet Archive mirror, signed out, in headless Chromium. Auth forms were measured, never submitted. UntitledSans/LabilGrotesk are proprietary served faces: samples fall back to system sans.

Palette

A warm-neutral ramp (#262626 ink through #9d9d9d field grey to #fff paper) with one brand yellow, plus small status accents (red, green, blue) used by form alerts and icon states.

Spacing, radius and motion

Small radii (7–8px) on cards/inputs/tags/submits against full pills (20px categories, 50px CTA pills) and round avatars; the feed is a 1640px-max 3-column grid with 15px gutters and a 50px row pitch. Controls ease over 0.2s (alerts 0.35s, media fades 0.5s).

Type

UntitledSans (Klim) carries UI text at 16px/22px base, UntitledSansMedium the post titles and tags, and LabilGrotesk-Medium (Labil Grotesk / Kometa) the 40–41px display lines and the wordmark. Weights: 300/400/700, medium expressed through the medium families.
