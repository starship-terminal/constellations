# Patterns

Public marketing site, 2026-11-22.

The shell is a white bar, a product menu that opens on click, and a burgundy footer. The homepage adds a lilac strip ("Looking for Superhuman Mail?") and a full-bleed hero video. Pricing, Mail, Docs, Go, and the stories index sit on warm paper (#f2f0eb) with ink headings.

Primary actions are a violet pill (#714cb6) with a white label. The homepage hero control is the exception: its own fill is transparent, and the dark pill is the video behind it.

Sign in is a menu of three products, not one login page. Go and Docs land on `id.superhuman.com`. Mail admin stays on `superhuman.com` and offers Google and Microsoft only. Signup is the same identity host with "Continue to Superhuman".

Empty states in this pass are the empty email field, the empty sales form, the empty store search, and the 404. The homepage inbox is demo art.
