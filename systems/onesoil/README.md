# OneSoil design reference

A token set and component reference for OneSoil's public marketing site. It was read from onesoil.ai on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium. Desktop captures measured `window.innerWidth` 1440 and `window.innerHeight` 900 at device pixel ratio 1. Phone captures measured 390 by 844 at device pixel ratio 2. The site redirects `/` to `/en`. There is one painted theme, light. `prefers-color-scheme: dark` left the page cream and ink, and no theme switch exists.

Captured pages: home, platform, global analytics, AI agronomist, farmers, soil sampling, agro consulting, machinery dealers, input suppliers, field force assistant, resources, the Learn category, one article (`what-is-ndvi`), contact, schedule-a-demo, the help-center home, and the marketing 404. Header panels (Products, Solutions, Company, language) and the phone menu were opened and closed. The contact form was not filled and not submitted. Try Free and Open Web App go to the app sign-in wall. That URL was recorded and the branch was stopped.

The signed-in product at app.onesoil.ai was not captured. Marketing `/login` is a 404.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages and read `getComputedStyle` on the real controls. Hover used the pointer. Focus used `element.focus()`. Pressed used `CSS.forcePseudoState` with `active` and `hover`, so the button was not clicked. The Agronomist role chip was clicked to read its selected fill. The form was not submitted. Nothing was typed into a field.

OneSoil does not publish a product token layer on the marketing site. The custom properties on the page are Tailwind internals (`--tw-*`) plus `--font-geologica`. Role colours are the computed values.

Two hover fills stayed in oklab in the computed style. Those two were sampled from raw screenshots before quantising: Schedule a demo hover is `#2b2b2b`, Try Free hover is `#3bb56f`. They are labelled Sampled.

## What to distrust

- **Hover and pressed.** The browser reported oklab with alpha, not sRGB. The sampled hex is the painted pixel, not a declared token. Pressed on the black button matched hover. Pressed on the green button was not sampled.
- **Nav and menu card fills.** Computed as oklab at 5% and 10% black. The tokens use `rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.05)` and `0.1`, derived from the measured ink and the measured alpha.
- **Pill radius.** Computed radius was `3.35544e+07px` (Tailwind `rounded-full`). The token stores `9999px` and says Derived.
- **No dark theme.** Do not invent one from the phone menu. That panel is a near-black overlay on the light site.
- **Help center type.** The search field uses Noto Sans. The marketing site uses Geologica.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Read colours from tokens.json.
- **Status colours.** Success, warning and danger were not painted. The green is the brand accent.

## Fonts

Geologica, with a named fallback `geologica Fallback`, then `system-ui, sans-serif`. The family is loaded by the site. This package does not commit the font files. The style guide falls back to the system face when Geologica is not installed.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the page cream and the type near-black.** The page is `#f6f4ef` and the heading is `#151515`. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do use green for the one forward action.** Try Free is `#27ae60`. Schedule a demo stays black. See the same screenshot.
- **Do float the header as a white island.** Products, Solutions and Company open inside it. See [screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png).
- **Do set display type large and tight.** The home heading is 72px with -2.88px tracking. See the home screenshot.
- **Don't style the signed-in app from this package.** Map, fields and account UI were not reached.
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The phone menu is a dark panel on a light site. See [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).
