# Patterns

Public pages only. Captured 2026-11-19, signed out, light theme. Desktop shots are 1440 by 900 CSS pixels at 1x. The phone shot is 390 by 844 CSS pixels at 2x (780 by 1688 PNG).

## Marketing home

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) is the first viewport. [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png) is the page at 6216px tall.

The page is a cream canvas (`{background.page}`), a floating glass nav, a 72px display headline with one green span, a 24px light lead, then three photo tiles. Later bands use white, `#EDE8E2`, green `#27AE60`, and forest `#003A1E`. There is no app shell.
