# OneSoil patterns

Public marketing only. The signed-in app was not opened past the login wall.

## App shell

The marketing shell is a floating header island on a cream page. Products, Solutions, Company and the language control open panels on the same page. Phone width replaces the links with Open menu, which covers the screen in a near-black panel. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Form

Contact and Schedule a demo share one form: name, job title, company, phone, email, country, message, consent, Submit. The demo variant adds role chips. The empty form is in [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png). Validation, error and success were not captured because the form was not submitted.
