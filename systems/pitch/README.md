# Pitch

Pitch is a collaborative presentation product. This package records the public marketing site at pitch.com and the signed-out login at auth.pitch.com, measured on 19 November 2026. No account was created. The home, product, pricing and templates pages were read signed out, at 1440 CSS pixels and at 390. The login form was read at rest and focused. Nothing was typed into it and nothing was submitted.

The marketing site is a Framer page with hashed colour tokens. The templates gallery and the login page are Pitch's own app chrome, in Lato and Mark Pro, with a named custom-property layer (`--action`, `--grey0` through `--grey9`, `--size-*`).

### Principles

1. Use the marketing violet for the Framer pages and the login violet for the app chrome.
2. Sign up is the lime chip, Log in is the white pill.
3. Plan cards share one body and change the header.
4. Do not invent a dark theme.
5. Do not draw an error state.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview tab opened https://pitch.com/ and then https://app.pitch.com/, which redirected to auth.pitch.com. Computed styles, custom properties and font faces were read from that live page. The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`), so the screenshots and the 1440 and 390 readings were taken in headless Playwright against pages that had already loaded with no Cloudflare challenge. `app.pitch.com/login` is an S3 Access Denied page and was not used. The password form at `#/sign-in/email` was opened and measured, and not submitted.

Marketing colours come from the Framer `--token-*` declarations. Login colours come from the auth page's custom properties, checked against the computed style of the field and the buttons. Hover on Log in and Sign up was a real pointer hover in Playwright. Focus on the email field and the templates search field was a real focus.

## What to distrust

The six login colours `core.color.login-ink-deep`, `login-muted`, `login-border`, `login-error`, `login-success` and `login-warning` were read from auth.pitch.com custom properties that raw/ does not hold. They are labelled Reconstruction, entered by hand at capture time.

The home hero is a collage and a gradient, not a flat fill. The violet and violet-deep tokens are the colours the type and the buttons use. They are not a single sampled pixel of the hero.

Log in hover computed to `#5216eb`, one step off the `#5318eb` violet token. Both are recorded. The token is the stylesheet value. The hover is the computed value.

The Continue with email button paints `--action` at opacity 0.5 while it is disabled. A screenshot of it looks lilac. The token is the underlying `#6b53ff`.

The templates gallery is a different surface from the Framer marketing pages. Its type is Mark Pro and Pitch-Lato-UI, and its search button is the login violet `#6b53ff`, not the marketing violet.

No dark theme was on the public pages. `prefers-color-scheme` on the marketing page was `normal`. The login custom properties include dark-looking greys, and they were not applied as a theme on the form that was on screen.

## Fonts

Mark Pro Bold and Mark Pro Regular, and Eina 03 Bold and Eina 03 Regular, are loaded from framerusercontent.com as woff2. They are Pitch's marketing faces. Inter is also loaded, as the Framer bold and italic fallback. Space Mono (Google) is the eyebrow and plan-name face. Lato is the login face, with Helvetica Neue, Helvetica and Arial behind it. The templates page also names Pitch-Lato-UI and Eina01. None of these files are in the package. The tokens name the families and fall back to the system sans or to Space Mono, which is the one face with a public licence.

## Do and don't

- **Use the marketing violet for the Framer pages and the login violet for the app chrome.** Generate is `#5318eb`. Continue with email and the templates Search button are `#6b53ff`. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
- **Sign up is the lime chip, Log in is the white pill.** On the home nav Sign up is `#c4ee87` with a 4px radius, and Log in is white with a 30px radius. See [components/crops/button-signup-default.png](components/crops/button-signup-default.png).
- **Plan cards share one body and change the header.** Free is the lilac wash, Plus is lilac, Team is violet, Business is violet-deep. See [components/crops/card-plan.png](components/crops/card-plan.png).
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** The public pages did not switch. The grey ramp on the login stylesheet is recorded and was not on screen as a theme.
- **Do not draw an error state.** The login form did not show one.
