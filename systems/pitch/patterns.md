# Patterns

## Marketing home

A full-bleed purple hero with a collage of template thumbnails behind the type. The wordmark sits at the left of a 72px bar. Product, Use Cases, Templates, Resources and Pricing sit in the centre in Eina 03 Regular 16px. Log in and Sign up sit at the right.

The headline is Mark Pro Bold, 180px at 1440, white, letter-spacing -7.2px. Under it, a prompt card with a 24px radius and a translucent white face (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`). Generate is the violet button inside that card.

At 390 the nav collapses to the wordmark and a menu button. Sign up for free and Get a demo sit under the hero as a white pill and a lime chip. [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png)

## Product page

The same nav on a light page. The headline is violet-deep, 92px, weight 900. The page background is white. [screenshots/product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png)

## Pricing

An eyebrow in Space Mono, then a 64px violet-deep title. A yearly/monthly pill uses the lime chip for the selected side and a violet 17% saving badge. Four plan cards share one body and change the header colour. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

The phone shot stacks the same content. [screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png)

## Templates

This page is the public templates app, not the Framer marketing template. A white nav, a 30px Mark Pro title, a search field and a violet Search button, then a row of category chips. Template cards are artwork with a title and a New badge. [screenshots/templates-light-default.png](screenshots/templates-light-default.png)

## Login

`https://app.pitch.com/` redirects to `https://auth.pitch.com/login`. The page background is a violet gradient. A 560px white card holds the wordmark, one line of instruction, the email field, Continue with email, and three secondary buttons. Google, Apple and SSO were not followed. [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png)

The password form is a second step at `#/sign-in/email`. It was measured and not submitted.
