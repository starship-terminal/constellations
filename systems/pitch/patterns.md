# Patterns

## Marketing home

A full-bleed purple hero with a collage of template thumbnails behind the type. The wordmark sits at the left of a 72px bar. Product, Use Cases, Templates, Resources and Pricing sit in the centre in Eina 03 Regular 16px. Log in and Sign up sit at the right.

The headline is Mark Pro Bold, 180px at 1440, white, letter-spacing -7.2px. Under it, a prompt card with a 24px radius and a translucent white face (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`). Generate is the violet button inside that card.

At 390 the nav collapses to the wordmark and a round menu button. The menu is a violet drawer with Product, Use Cases, Templates, Resources, Pricing, Log in and Sign up. [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

Product, Use Cases, Templates and Resources open a white hover menu from the desktop nav. [screenshots/nav-product-menu.png](screenshots/nav-product-menu.png) The map of every item is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Login

Log in and Sign up both open the same wall. The page background is a violet gradient. A white card holds the wordmark, one line of instruction, the email field, Continue with email, and three secondary buttons. The signup arrival also shows an empty "Verify you are human" checkbox. Google, Apple and SSO were not followed. Nothing was typed. [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png) [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png)
