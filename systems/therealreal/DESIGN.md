## Typography

The painted body is SuisseIntl, 16px, weight 400, line-height 25.6px, colour `#333333`. Department nav is the same family at 16px, weight 400, sentence case. SELL is 11px, weight 700, uppercase, tracking 3.3px. The promo line is 16px, weight 400, white on black. Footer column headings are 18px, weight 600, uppercase, tracking 5.4px. `--chakra-fonts-heading` is TRRFont. The painted wordmark is an image.

## Shape

Search, SELL, and the department links are square, radius `0px`. `--radius-sm` is `0.25rem` and was not the painted search field.

## Layout

Announcement 40px. Header 229px. Wordmark image 172 by 28. Search 416 by 40, border `1px solid #666666`, padding `8px 96px 8px 8px`. SELL 112 by 32, padding `0px 12px`. Department link height 28px. Footer 1006px.
