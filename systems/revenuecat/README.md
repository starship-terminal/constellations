### Principles

1. One blue for the primary action.
2. Display type is objectSans, body is helveticaNeue.
3. Pills on the marketing site, 8px in the app.
4. Do not invent a marketing dark theme.
5. Do not treat the hero illustration as the product UI.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser with `preview_evaluate` on the live pages. Custom properties on `:root` were resolved with `getComputedStyle`. Lab colours that stayed in `lab()` were filled on a 1x1 sRGB canvas from the same string. The app's `--rc-*` ramps were read as declared hex. The app light sheet was read from the `:root, .light` rule. The live login and signup pages had `html.dark`, so the dark sheet is what was on screen.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on every snapshot. Public marketing screenshots were taken afterwards with headless Playwright, because those pages had already loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare challenge. Login and signup were not opened in that headless session. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## What to distrust

The dark set is the signed-out app, not a dark marketing theme. The marketing site had no `prefers-color-scheme` theme switch.

Hover colours are declared `hover:` classes. They were not held. The app primary button token is the disabled fill that was on screen (`#2d2f35`), not the enabled `#606dff` stylesheet value. That enabled fill is `dark.action.default`.

Pill radius computed to `16777215px`. The component tokens record `9999px` for that sentinel.

`#576cdb` in the ghost-button class is one channel off the computed `--primary-blue` (`#576ddb`). Both are recorded. The wordmark red `#f2545b` is sampled from the screenshot, not a marketing custom property.

The body of the login page also paints an `oklch` wash at alpha 0.09. It is not the page token.

- **The app ramps are not stored in raw/.** The `--rc-*` ramps were read as declared hex during the capture session, but [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json) holds no copy of them. The builder labels every token whose value raw/ does not hold "Reconstruction": 80 of the 90 app ramp steps, `size.content` and `dark.action.hover`. A recapture should store the ramps in raw/.

## Fonts

helveticaNeue and objectSans are self-hosted next/font families on the marketing site. IBM Plex Mono is declared as the mono stack and was not used by a visible home-page element at rest. The app uses Inter Variable, with ObjectSans named on the login heading. None of these files are in the package. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **One blue for the primary action.** Sign up and Start for free share `#576ddb`. Secondary actions are a blue outline or a text link. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and the [primary button](components/crops/button-primary.png).
- **Display type is objectSans, body is helveticaNeue.** Do not set the lede in the display face. The app swaps body to Inter Variable. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Pills on the marketing site, 8px in the app.** A marketing button is a full pill. An app field and submit are 8px. See the [button](components/crops/button-primary.png) and the [field](components/crops/field-default.png).
- **Do not invent a marketing dark theme.** The dark tokens are the signed-out app. The public home is light only: [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do not treat the hero illustration as the product UI.** It is a public demo. Dashboard components were not captured. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
