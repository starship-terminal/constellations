## Where the walk stopped

Account opened a modal on [https://www.madewell.com/](https://www.madewell.com/). The address bar stayed on the homepage, with no OAuth query. The modal offered Sign In and Sign Up, an empty Email field, Continue, Facebook Sign Up, and Apple Sign Up. Copy above the form listed free shipping, a birthday gift, and points. Email was empty. Continue was not clicked. Facebook and Apple were not clicked. The modal was closed. The screenshot was discarded because it is a signup and sign-in wall.

Bag was not opened. Add To Bag was not clicked. Search was not submitted. The email form was not submitted. Wishlist, Orders, Order Status, and Contact Us were not opened. Store locator was not opened, so no street address entered a screenshot.

Contact Us was left closed because a help page can print a phone number. The photographed pages were checked for a phone pattern and a street pattern before they were kept. None printed one.
