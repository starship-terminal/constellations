## Typography

p22-underground at 16px for body, and 11px weight 500 uppercase for nav. Product title is the same family at 16px. Hero title is manop-variable at 40px. Category title is TAYBigBird at 44px. About title is TAYBigBird at 50px. Font files are not in this package.

## Shape

Announcement bar 55px. Header 124px. Search 32px. Hero outline button 48px. Add To Bag 44px. Email field 40px. Every measured control has square corners.

## Do and don't

Keep the page white and the type black. Use the red bar only for the sale line. Stop before the account modal.
