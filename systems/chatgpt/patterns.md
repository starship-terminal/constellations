# ChatGPT patterns

How ChatGPT combines its components for the common jobs, as captured.

**App shell and navigation.** A 340 wide sidebar (at 1440; it clamps between 240 and 520) on the page ground: search and hide at the top, destination rows (New chat, Images, Scheduled, Library, Plugins, Explore), then collapsible Pinned, Projects and Recents sections, and the account row at the bottom. The fixed 52 tall header carries the Chat / Work toggle centred and Upgrade at the end. Hiding the sidebar leaves a 52 wide rail of icon buttons; on a phone the sidebar is a 260 wide drawer over the page, opened from the header. Settings is a page of its own with its own left nav and a Back to app row. See [home-dark](screenshots/home-dark.png), [home-dark-sidebar-collapsed](screenshots/home-dark-sidebar-collapsed.png), [mobile-home-dark-drawer](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-drawer.png) and [settings-dark-keyboard](screenshots/settings-dark-keyboard.png).

**Forms and validation.** Settings rows put the label (and a secondary line) at the start and one control at the end: a switch, a select button that opens a checkbox or radio menu, an outline button, or a hex field. Changes apply at once; there is no Save button and no inline validation on any captured row. A disabled control keeps its colours at 40% opacity. The one form that validates is the signed-out sign-up dialog: an incomplete email and Continue give the field a red border (`aria-invalid`) and a 12px red line under it ("Email is not valid."), and Continue stays enabled; nothing validates while typing. See [onboarding-dark-email-error](screenshots/onboarding-dark-email-error.png),  [settings-dark-data-controls](screenshots/settings-dark-data-controls.png), [settings-dark-notifications-menu](screenshots/settings-dark-notifications-menu.png) and [settings-dark-appearance](screenshots/settings-dark-appearance.png).

**Empty, loading and error states.** Empty is one centred line with a small icon in tertiary text, inside the surface that would hold the results ("No results" in search). A failed load replaces the content with one centred line and a small Try again button ("Failed to load files." in the Library); a partial failure is a one-line banner above the results with its own Try again ("Some search results could not be loaded"). Loading keeps the layout and fills it with placeholders: the Library grid holds shimmer cards of the file card's size (a grey band at 10 to 16% sweeping across `radius.2xl` boxes), and an answer that is being generated shows a pulsing 12px dot in the accent colour while the composer's send button turns into Stop. See [library-dark-loading](screenshots/library-dark-loading.png), [thread-dark-streaming](screenshots/thread-dark-streaming.png),  [home-dark-search-empty](screenshots/home-dark-search-empty.png), [library-dark-error](screenshots/library-dark-error.png) and [library-dark-search-error](screenshots/library-dark-search-error.png).

**Destructive confirmation.** A small dialog (420 wide, `radius.3xl`) on a light scrim: a bold title as a question ("Delete chat?"), one line that names the thing and says it cannot be undone, then Cancel (secondary) and the action named in full ("Delete chat") as a danger pill: red text on a red tint, not a solid red button. Bulk actions in settings use the same danger pill ("Delete all"). See [home-dark-delete-confirm](screenshots/home-dark-delete-confirm.png) and [settings-dark-data-controls](screenshots/settings-dark-data-controls.png).

**Feedback after an action.** A failed action gets a toast at the top centre: a red-tinted surface with a red hairline (`variant.danger.surface.bg`, `variant.danger.outline.border`, `radius.xl`), an icon, a bold line saying what failed and a second line saying what to do, and a close button ("Couldn’t copy to clipboard", then "The content wasn’t copied. Try again while ChatGPT is focused"). After the first answer in a temporary chat a popover under the header offers to save the chat. See [thread-dark-copied](screenshots/thread-dark-copied.png). Feedback while composing: the voice button turns into Send as soon as there is text, and a tooltip names an icon button on focus or hover. See [home-dark-composer-typed](screenshots/home-dark-composer-typed.png) and [home-dark-tooltip](screenshots/home-dark-tooltip.png).

**Search and filtering.** Global search is a 720 wide dialog with the field at the top and Clear and close at the end; empty, it lists recent chats; typed, it adds filter tabs (All, Chats, Images, Documents, Projects) and the results or the empty state. The Library has its own field in the page header with a filter button beside it; Archived chats and Plugins put a field at the top of the list. See [home-dark-search](screenshots/home-dark-search.png), [home-dark-search-empty](screenshots/home-dark-search-empty.png) and [settings-dark-archived](screenshots/settings-dark-archived.png).

**Data display.** Lists are rows on the page ground divided by hairlines (`border.subtle`) or by nothing: sidebar chats, archived chats (title link, date, Unarchive and Delete at the end), plugin cards in two columns (a bordered card with one link laid over all of it). Files are a masonry grid of cards (4 columns at 1440; its classes step from 1 to 6 columns with the container width) with a hover checkbox for selection. Tables appear inside answers only, ruled by horizontal hairlines, with bold first-column cells. See [settings-dark-archived](screenshots/settings-dark-archived.png), [library-dark](screenshots/library-dark.png) and [thread-dark-table](screenshots/thread-dark-table.png).

**Onboarding.** Signed out, ChatGPT is the same shell with fewer rows (New chat, Images, Plugins, Deep research, plans, Help, Settings), Log in (white, inverted) and Sign up for free (grey) at the end of the header, and a card at the foot of the sidebar ("Get responses tailored to you") with a full-width Log in. The composer works without an account; features that need one ask to log in. Sign up opens one dialog for both paths: a heading, one line on what an account adds, three provider buttons as outline pills, an OR rule, an email field and a white Continue. The account's own first run is past; the stylesheets also declare `--text-onboarding-*` styles (title 20, body 14, label 16), which are in `core.typography`. See [onboarding-dark](screenshots/onboarding-dark.png) and [onboarding-dark-signup](screenshots/onboarding-dark-signup.png).
