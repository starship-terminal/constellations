Palette

The Slate ramps: --slate-color-grey-*/--slate-color-neutral-* run white #ffffff to near-black, beside blue/green/orange/red/teal/yellow accents and the seasonal --slate-color-bsr-* ramp. The semantic layer — text/surface/border/icon roles, the action.* state matrix, button/message/tag/toggle/progress/expressive/program roles — resolves to them on the only theme the site paints. The onSurface group is the same roles remapped inside .fs-mode-on-surface for controls over dark media.

Spacing, radius and motion

Everything sits on the 4px --spacing base with the --slate-spacing-element/group/layout/section-* roles and the 4–80px --slate-dimensions-* ramp. Radii are small — 4px on buttons and fields, 999px on filter pills and round controls. Controls transition over 150ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1).

Type

Graphik_fix carries the UI — body, controls, fields at 14/20 with +0.15px tracking; Nantes_fix draws the marketing display (52/64 mastheads); GT Canon draws the category masthead and dialog titles; GT Standard the large CTAs. The declared --slate-typography-* ramp runs 100–800; the older --f_t_* ramp (sizes 12–72) is still resolved on the page.
