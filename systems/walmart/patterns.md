# Patterns

## Home, desktop

A blue bar, `light.background.header`, 132px tall at 1440. Inside it: the spark logo, a navy "Pickup or delivery?" pill, a white search field, EN, Reorder, Sign In, and the cart. Under the bar, a row of white chips (Departments, Services, Pharmacy, Holiday Shop) on the page. Then a hero carousel of photo cards with white type and an underlined Shop now. Then a section heading and a product row.

See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Home, phone

The same blue bar compresses to a menu icon, the spark, a shorter search field, and the cart. "Pickup or delivery?" drops to a second blue row. Departments becomes a round icon. The hero card is one-up.

See [screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png). Viewport was 390 by 844.

## Product row

Tiles are 208px, square image, split price, title. No card chrome. Titles that are links are underlined in the browser default blue.

## Corporate

corporate.walmart.com is a separate site. It shares the blue bar and EverydaySans, then a full-bleed photo hero with a 60px white headline and a white pill button (padding 8px 24px, radius 999px, 16px / 700, label `#2e2f32`). The root font size there is 10px.

See [screenshots/corporate-light-default.png](screenshots/corporate-light-default.png).

## Privacy

The privacy notice is on the corporate site: blue bar, white page, a large black headline, body text, and a blue link list in the rail.

See [screenshots/privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).
