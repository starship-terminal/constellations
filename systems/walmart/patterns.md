# Patterns

## Home, desktop

A blue bar, `light.background.header`. Inside it: the spark logo, a navy "Pickup or delivery?" pill, a white search field, EN, Reorder, Sign In, and the cart at `$0.00`. Under the bar, Departments and Services, then a row of white chips. Then a hero carousel of photo cards with white type and an underlined Shop now. Then a department icon row.

See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

The account menu drops from Sign In as a white panel over a dimmed page. See [screenshots/home-light-account-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-account-menu.png).

## Home, phone

The bar compresses to a menu icon, the spark, a shorter search field ("Search Walmart"), and the cart. "Pickup or delivery?" drops to a second blue row. The hero card is one-up. The department icons stay in a horizontal row.

See [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png). Viewport was 390 by 844.

The menu button opens a white drawer over the page. See [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Category

Toys is a storefront page: the same header and chip row, a red photo hero ("Save big on favorite toys"), then a flat product grid. Each tile has a Rollback or Deal badge, an Add or Options pill, a green "Now" price, and a gray was-price.

See [screenshots/category-light-default.png](screenshots/category-light-default.png). The shot joins the two opaque bands of one frame. The hole between them was not layout.

## Product

The buy box sits on a pale surface: green "Now" price, was-price, a savings line, shipping and returns, a selected New radio, an out-of-stock Open Box radio, a blue Add to cart pill, then optional protection plans with empty checkboxes. Add to cart was not clicked. The gallery and the item-details column did not survive the frame and are not in the shot.

See [screenshots/product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png).

## Walmart+, phone

`/plus` is a marketing page, not a signed-in membership. Blue header with the Walmart+ wordmark, a yellow hero, then a blue headline and a "Join Walmart+" pill. The pill was not clicked.

See [screenshots/mobile-plus-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-plus-light-default.png).

## Corporate and privacy

corporate.walmart.com is a separate site, kept from the 2026-11-19 capture. It shares the blue bar, then a full-bleed photo hero. The privacy notice is on that site.

See [screenshots/corporate-light-default.png](screenshots/corporate-light-default.png) and [screenshots/privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).

## What was not entered

No password, email, phone, code, or address was typed. No identity provider was clicked. No captcha was solved. No item was added to the cart. Checkout was not entered. Search results, help, and the empty login form were not captured. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
