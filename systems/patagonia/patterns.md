# Patterns

## App shell

A black utility bar, 48px, holds Activism, Stories, a rotating promo, and Find a Store. A white header under it holds the wordmark, search, wishlist, log in, and cart, then the department row. See [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Product

A public product opens from a homepage tile. The photo sits left on a warm gray field. Title, price, Shop New, color, and size sit right. Add to Bag is a black pill. It was not clicked. See [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Login wall

Log in is its own page at `/account/login/`. Email and password were empty. Log in was not clicked. See [screenshots/login-light-1440.png](screenshots/login-light-1440.png).

## Signup wall

Create an Account is the same path with `action=register`. Name, email, and password fields were empty. Create Account was not clicked. A reCAPTCHA was visible and was not solved. See [screenshots/signup-light-1440.png](screenshots/signup-light-1440.png).

## Empty, loading, error

Not captured as store states. Direct product navigations in this browser first painted a maintenance page from a service worker. The photographed product is the live page after that cache was cleared.

## Checkout and subscribe

The cart was not opened. The footer email Sign Me Up was not submitted. Search was not submitted.
