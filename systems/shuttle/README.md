# Shuttle

Shuttle (shuttle.zip) is a file sharing product. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out space, login and signup chrome, measured on 2026-11-19 from a live T3 preview tab. https://shuttle.zip/ redirected to https://shuttle.zip/new/s/space/. Signup and login were opened and not submitted. Both include a Cloudflare Turnstile field. No card was entered.

The live CSS viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2. The OS preference was dark. Computed colours matched the declared `.dark` custom properties. A light theme was declared on `:root` and was not the theme on screen.

### Principles

1. Use the black page and the blue tint.
2. Use 8px radius for app buttons and 9999px for marketing pills.
3. Keep fields on the grouped gray.
4. Keep signup as a signed-out form.
5. Do not invent a 1440 screenshot.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the stylesheets on https://shuttle.zip/new/s/space/ and from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`. Component colour, type, radius, padding and size were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements at https://shuttle.zip/about, https://shuttle.zip/pricing, https://shuttle.zip/updates, https://shuttle.zip/signup and https://shuttle.zip/login. Hex values are the browser's computed sRGB. Eight-digit declared colours such as `#0077ffff` are recorded in [raw/foundations.json](raw/foundations.json). The opaque token uses the six-digit form that matched the painted pixel, `#0077ff`.

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on `preview_snapshot`. No screenshots were invented. Headless Playwright was not launched against the login page.

## What to distrust

The CSS viewport was 1169, not 1440 and not 390. Do not treat these readings as a 1440 or 390 capture. `:root` declares a light palette (`--color-background: #f9f9f9`, `--color-label: #000000ff`). Those values were not the computed theme. They are listed in [raw/foundations.json](raw/foundations.json) and are not a token set. `--color-label2` is `#ebebf599`. Painted secondary text is `rgba(235, 235, 245, 0.6)`. The token `dark.text.muted` stores it with the alpha, `#ebebf599`, and the pricing hairline `dark.border.hairline` stores `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` as `#ffffff33`. `--color-link` computed to `#0984ffff` and was not the colour of a painted link. Marketing demos on the about page show names and email addresses. Those strings are not copied into this package. Screenshot folders are empty on purpose. Mona Sans is linked at weight 800 and no measured element used it.

## Fonts

InterVariable is the UI face, loaded from https://rsms.me/inter/inter.css, with Inter and ui-sans-serif in the stack. Marketing display, auth titles, nav, buttons and body all computed to that stack. The font files are not in this package. Tokens keep the measured stack and fall back to ui-sans-serif.

## Do and don't

- **Use the black page and the blue tint.** Marketing, the empty space, login and signup render on `#000000` with `#ffffff` text and `#0077ff` primary actions. [patterns.md](patterns.md) describes that shell.
- **Use 8px radius for app buttons and 9999px for marketing pills.** Share is 32px tall on `#0077ff`. Sign up is a pill. [components.md](components.md) lists the sizes.
- **Keep fields on the grouped gray.** Login and signup fields are 40px, 12px radius, `#2c2c2e`, with transparent inputs. [components.md](components.md) records the wrapper.
- **Keep signup as a signed-out form.** https://shuttle.zip/signup asks for email and name, plus two switches, and stops on a Turnstile. Do not invent a signed-in library behind it. [components.md](components.md) records the gap.
- **Do not invent a 1440 screenshot.** The preview browser produced no frames. [screenshots/](screenshots/) says so.
