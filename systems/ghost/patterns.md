# Patterns

The public site is a light marketing shell: white page, black wordmark, one black pill, a yellow launch chip. Audience pages (Creators, Publishers, Business) reuse that shell with a large headline. Pricing is four cards under a member slider. Explore is a directory of publications with a search field that was left empty.

The account walls leave that shell. Sign in is a centred site-address field. Signup is a modal over a dark mosaic of publication cards, with empty name, email, and password fields.
