# Notion Calendar

Public marketing page at https://www.notion.com/product/calendar. One light theme. Hand-written from measured tokens. Not a generated DESIGN.md.

## Colour

Page is white. Ink is near black. The calendar primary action is a black pill (`#191918`) with white label. Blue `#097fe8` is a declared Tatami swatch and the colour of the auth Continue button.

## Type

NotionInter, falling back to Inter. The homepage heading is 64px, weight 700, line height 64px. Body is 16px.

## Radius

The primary button is 8px. The declared Tatami scale starts at 4px.

## What this file does not cover

Hover, focus, pressed, dark theme, and the signed-in calendar were not measured.
