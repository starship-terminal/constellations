# Patterns

## Marketing shell

The home, pricing, and enterprise pages share a sticky header. At 1440px it is 65px tall, black at 82% opacity, with a 12px backdrop blur. The wordmark sits left. Products and Resources are menu triggers. Customers, Enterprise, and Pricing are links. Talk to sales is the outline pill. Start building is the solid pill. At 390px the link row is `display: none` and a 34px round menu button is shown. Page padding is 48px at 1440 and 20px at 390. The content column maxes at 1320px.

## Pricing

Four plan columns sit in one row at 1440px. Each card is about 330px wide with 28px 20px 32px padding. Pro is the popular column and uses the `#101010` surface. Prices are 48px. The solid action is on Pro. The other plans use the outline pill. Monthly and Yearly are a segmented pill. Yearly is selected by default and carries the sage "Up to 20% off" badge. Checkout was not opened.

## Sign-in

`https://app.spline.design/signin` is a centered card on `#1e1e1e`. The title is "Welcome to Spline". The subtitle is "Log in or register with your email." The email field and Continue button are 40px with an 8px radius. Google is a separate button and was not clicked. reCAPTCHA is disclosed and was not solved. `/signup` and `/register` redirect to this same empty card.

Photographed empty on 2026-11-22. Nothing was typed. Continue was not clicked. The earlier note that an email code was submitted is the 19 November capture. This public pass did not repeat that.
