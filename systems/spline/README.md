### Principles

1. Use the light pill for the primary action.
2. Set display type in Geist.
3. Keep the marketing site on black.
4. Leave payment and company forms out.

## How the values were read

Marketing colours, type, and geometry were read with `getComputedStyle` in the T3 preview tab at 1440x900 and 390x844, device pixel ratio 2. Declared rules were read from the Next.js stylesheets on `https://spline.design/`, including the shared layer that sets `--ink`, `--fg`, `--mute`, `--panel`, `--line`, and `--line-2`. Pricing was read the same way on `https://spline.design/pricing`. App-shell colours were read on `https://app.spline.design/signin` and the two signup steps that followed an email code. Screenshots of the public pages were taken with headless Playwright after those pages loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so it was not used for images.

## What to distrust

The marketing body element computes a line-height of 18.4px. The body token uses the hero paragraph's 24px. The primary button's computed radius is 999px. The stylesheet says 999px. Hero weight is 450, which the cascade also declares as 500 on headings. The token keeps the computed 450. There is no light theme. A dark colour scheme was active and the marketing page did not offer a light switch. Geist and Spline Sans are not in the package. The style guide names them and falls back. The enterprise members table is a public demo. Its row colours were not measured. App ramps (`--color-blue-100` through `--color-yellow-900`) were read and are not tokenised one step at a time. The accent, success, danger, and the elevation opacities that were used are tokenised.

## Fonts

Geist and Geist Mono are loaded by the marketing site as next/font files. Spline Sans Regular and Medium are self-hosted at `/fonts/SplineSans-Regular.woff2` and `/fonts/SplineSans-Medium.woff2`. Brockmann is also declared and was not the computed face of the elements that were measured. The font files are not committed. Tokens name Geist and Spline Sans with system fallbacks.

## Do and don't

- **Use the light pill for the primary action.** Start building and Get Pro are `#eee` with black type and a 999px radius. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Set display type in Geist.** Hero, section, and plan names are Geist at weight 450 with a tight negative tracking. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Keep the marketing site on black.** The page colour is `#000`. The app shell is a separate `#1e1e1e`. [screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png)
- **Leave payment and company forms out.** Checkout was not opened. The company step was not submitted. [patterns.md](patterns.md)
