Palette

Knox paints in named roles: secure-blue (#0364d3) is the action colour, midnight-navy (#1a2860) the brand field, ink is #303136 rather than pure black. Every accent ships default/hover/pressed/weak steps.

Type

One voice: Agile Sans Variable for everything, pulled thin (300) for the 88px display and page titles, and held at the variable-font 350 for body and nav. Agile Mono is the code face.

Radius and geometry

Knox has three shapes: corner-radius.large 90px (the pill profile on buttons, coachmarks and the plan selector), medium 12px (cards, callouts, disclosures) and small 4px (fields, checkboxes).

Motion and layers

Knox motion is measured: a 17ms-500ms duration scale and four named beziers; the CTA colour transition runs at the 300ms slow step, and the nav flyout is a transition-all duration-300 panel under the z-50 header.
