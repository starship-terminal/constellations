# Mailchimp public site tree

Mapped 2026-11-21 from [https://mailchimp.com/](https://mailchimp.com/) while signed out, before the refresh screenshots. Desktop readings are 1440 CSS pixels wide. The page title is "Email & SMS Marketing Platform | Mailchimp". The marketing site paints light: `html` has class `js`, no `data-theme`, and `color-scheme` is `normal`. There is no theme switch on the homepage.

A control that opens a login, signup, or identity-provider wall stops that branch. Nothing was typed. No form was submitted. Google, Apple, and other identity providers were not clicked. Cookie Customize settings and Close were not pressed while mapping this tree. The home survey was not answered.

Query strings on Log In and Start Free Trial carry experiment and signing parameters. Those parameters are not recorded here. Paths below are origin plus pathname.

Shots of opened states live in [screenshots/site-tree/](screenshots/site-tree/).
