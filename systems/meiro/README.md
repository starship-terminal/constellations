# Meiro design reference

A token set and component reference for Meiro's public surface. It was read from **meiro.cc** and the signed-out app at **app.meiro.cc** on **2026-11-19**, in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. Captured: the marketing home, pricing, the marketing use-case page, privacy, terms and refund; the sign-in card, the password-reset form, the reset form with no token, and the app 404. The email field was focused. A one-character probe was typed and cleared, and that crop was not kept. Nothing was submitted, purchased, or signed into.

Meiro is an AI quiz and test builder. The marketing site is a Framer site (generator `Framer ab3275d`) that paints one dark field and a lime pill. The signed-out app repeats the same ink and a slightly deeper lime, with its own radii and SF Pro. **Boundary:** the builder, challenges and billing sit behind the sign-in card and were never reached.

3. Do set marketing display in TT Hoves DemiBold.

## How the values were read

Marketing colours come from the Framer stylesheet's `body` custom properties (`--token-*`, written in [raw/framer-tokens.css](raw/framer-tokens.css)). Roles were assigned by matching those hex values to computed styles on real elements: page `#0e0f0f`, nav and cards `#171818`, field `#202222`, muted text `#ced1d1`, primary fill `#d5fe52`, app email fill `#c0ef2a`. Type, radius, padding and height were read with `getComputedStyle` at 1440×900 and 390×844. Hover, pressed and focus-visible were forced with the Chrome DevTools Protocol on the live elements. Motion was read by `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` on the home, pricing and sign-in pages, including a reduced-motion pass.

The app's own stylesheet (`app.meiro.cc/assets/index-*.css`) declares `:root { color-scheme: light dark; background-color: #0e0f0f }` and `@font-face` for SF Pro and TT Hoves. It does not publish a second semantic palette.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read values from the tokens, not the images.

## What to distrust

- The marketing button's hover and pressed fills did not change under a forced pseudo-class. Framer may swap a variant on a real pointer that this read did not reach. The crops show the forced state, not a proven hover colour.
- Pressed on a marketing link sets the anchor's own `color` to the browser active red. The visible label, a child paragraph, stayed ink. Do not treat that red as a Meiro token.
- Login label line-height computed as 2px on the header control. The nav labels beside it computed as 20px. Use 20px.
- `#ff7c7c` is declared on `body` and was not painted on a captured control. It is recorded as `danger` and marked as declared, not observed.
- The Most powerful badge and the phone menu button use gradient images. They are not flat tokens.
- Affiliate pages (affiliate.meiro.cc) use a different stack and an orange link. Those values are not in this package.
- `core.spacing.1` (4px) is derived from the measured rhythm. The nav pill's padding is 5px, which the scale rounds.
- The open header menu was not measured. A hero image covered it after the click.

## Fonts

TT Hoves (Regular, Medium, DemiBold) is the marketing face, loaded from framerusercontent.com as WOFF. The app loads TT Hoves DemiBold as `TTHoves` and SF Pro Text as `SFPro` (400, 500, 600) from its own `/assets` and `/fonts` paths. Both are commercial. They are not in this package. The tokens name the families the computed style returned, with the placeholder or system fallback the page itself declared. Inter is the Framer fallback for bold and italic runs, and it was not the face of any measured heading or control.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the page near-black.** The field is `#0e0f0f` and the text is white. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do make the primary action a lime pill.** Marketing uses `#d5fe52` at an 18px radius. The app email button uses `#c0ef2a` at 17px. See [button-primary-default](components/crops/button-primary-default.png) and [button-email-default](components/crops/button-email-default.png).
3. **Do set marketing display in TT Hoves DemiBold.** The hero is 50px on 70px at 1440, and 34px on 44.2px at 390. See [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
4. **Do keep the header at 84px on desktop.** It is 72px on the phone, with a 24px bottom radius. See [mobile-home-dark-default](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png).
5. **Do put the sign-in form on a 30px card.** The card is `#171818`, 520px wide, padding 30px 40px 40px. See [card-login-default](components/crops/card-login-default.png).
6. **Don't invent a light theme.** Neither site painted one on a signed-out page. See [login-dark-default](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
7. **Don't submit from this package.** The email form, the reset form and checkout were not completed. See [login-dark-default](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
8. **Don't treat the browser active red as a pressed token.** The marketing link's own color flipped to red under `:active`. The label did not. See [button-primary-pressed](components/crops/button-primary-pressed.png).

## Rebuild

From the package folder:

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

`build_tokens.py` reads [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json) and the constants that cite the measured readings. `build_style_guide.py` renders [style-guide.html](style-guide.html) from [tokens.json](tokens.json). A rebuild that changes nothing means the outputs still match those inputs.

The capture scripts under `tools/capture/` are the kit copy, adapted only where a comment says so. The live readings for this package were taken with a one-off script that is not part of the package. Re-capture by opening the same URLs and writing a new `raw/states.json`.
