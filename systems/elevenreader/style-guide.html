ElevenReader, measured

elevenreader.io is a Next.js site over Tailwind v4 whose public surface paints one light theme: a near-monochrome field (black ink on white, a #F5F3F1 promo band, #FAF8F8 FAQ tints) with pill geometry everywhere — every button, chip, input and badge is a 9999px rounded-full, cards step up to 20-40px radii. The declared token layer carries a full themed palette (the --themed-* triplets bound into --color-* roles, a blue accent #4680fd the marketing surface barely uses), the --f-* type scale (display/heading in Waldenburg, everything else in Inter) and the reader app's own --reader-* palette. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The declared --color-* layer carries the themed ramps (neutral, blue, cyan, green, yellow, magenta, red — each inverted on .dark), the named brand hues, the Tailwind default palette, the alpha overlays and the reader app's own colours; the painted group holds measured fills no variable names (the promo band, the FAQ tint, the plan card, the Save badge). Light and dark roles are the same declared variables, re-bound by the .dark scope — declared, unpainted.

Spacing and radius

The --spacing unit is 4px with 20px inner / 64px outer gutters at desktop; geometry is pill-first — 9999px on every control, 20px FAQ, 40px plan cards, 16px orbit covers, the declared 8px shadcn radius on media.

Type

Two faces: Waldenburg on display and headings (the f-heading scale declares 700 but paints the book weight, ~375-400, with negative tracking), Inter on everything else. The --f-* roles are the type ramp — sizes below resolve at 1440px and step down under 80rem and 64rem.
