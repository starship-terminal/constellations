# Xbox patterns

Public marketing and store, signed out, 2026-11-19. Locale redirected to en-SG.

## App shell

A 54px Microsoft UHF header. Dark (#1b1d1b) on the home, games, consoles, Game Pass, and product pages. Light (white, text #262626) on search. Wordmark, section triggers, Search, Cart, and a Sign in / ME cluster. Footer is #f2f2f2 with 11px links on marketing pages.

Shown in [home-light-desktop.png](screenshots/home-light-desktop.png) and [search-light-desktop.png](screenshots/search-light-desktop.png).

## Hero

Full-bleed photography with a white heading, a subheading, and one lime CTA. Home hero heading measured at 30px/700 when it was in view; games hero measured 46px/700; consoles display measured 49px and 86px in Segoe Pro Black. Square corners.

## Shop strip

Six icon links under the home hero: GAME PASS, GAMES, DEVICES, ACCESSORIES, DEALS, SIGN IN. Labels are Segoe Pro Black 15px/600 in black. SIGN IN was not followed.

## Catalogue

Games page lists titles with `.c-price` (15px, black) such as SG$80.00. Search results are a filter column plus a card grid, 4px radius. Product page is a dark store (`body` #1a1b1e) with Bahnschrift Light 40px title, a green GET GAME PASS, and a grey BUY. BUY and GET GAME PASS were not clicked. No card was entered.

## Game Pass

[game-pass-light-desktop.png](screenshots/game-pass-light-desktop.png) is a marketing page. A caption on the live page read that plans start at $10.99/month. JOIN GAME PASS is an in-page anchor, not a checkout. It was not followed into a subscription form.

## Widths

Desktop screenshots are 1440x900. Phone screenshots are 390x844. The live preview that supplied computed styles was 1169x731 at devicePixelRatio 2, so padding that depends on the viewport (header side padding 58.4453px) is that width, not 1440.
