# The Washington Post design reference

A token set for The Washington Post's public signed-out news surface. It was read on **2026-11-19** from **washingtonpost.com**, signed out, in the T3 preview browser. The live pages were the home page, the Politics section, one article (`/entertainment/video-games/2026/11/18/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-an-astonishing-american-epic/`) and the public sign-in card at `/subscribe/signin/`. The site painted `html` class `wpds-dark`. A 1440 by 900 CSS viewport was measured after resize. A 390 pass was made by forcing the document width to 390px, after which `innerWidth` was 390, `innerHeight` was 844 and `--wpds--screenSize` was `small`.

The product ships a token layer named WPDS. The root computed 577 custom properties, including `--wpds-colors-*`, `--wpds-fonts-*`, `--wpds-fontSizes-*`, `--wpds-space-*`, `--wpds-radii-*`, `--wpds-shadows-*` and `--motion-*`. The public page is dark: page `rgb(20, 20, 20)`, text `rgb(255, 255, 255)`, muted `rgb(161, 161, 161)`, and a blue call to action `rgb(22, 109, 252)`. Franklin is the UI face. Postoni is the headline face. Georgia is the article body face. Section links, Sign in and Save and subscribe are pills with `border-radius: 9999px`.

**Boundary.** Subscribe, checkout, a card, a phone number and account creation were not entered. The email field on the offer card and the sign-in form was focused and not filled. Next, Continue with Amazon, Facebook, Google, Apple, and Continue with SSO were not pressed. View more offers and Save and subscribe were not followed.

## Do and don't

1. **Do use the WPDS names.** The page declares `--wpds-colors-background`, `--wpds-colors-cta`, `--wpds-fonts-headline` and `--wpds-radii-round`. Take those values from [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. **Do keep the page dark.** The public home, Politics and article chrome paint `rgb(20, 20, 20)` with white text. The white surface is the offer card and the sign-in card.
3. **Do spend the blue on the action.** `rgb(22, 109, 252)` fills Save and subscribe and the sign-in Next button. Section pills stay on the page colour.
4. **Do pair the faces.** Franklin for nav, buttons and meta. Postoni for story and article headlines. Georgia for article body.
5. **Do use the pill.** Section links, Sign in, Save and subscribe, Next and the OAuth buttons use `border-radius: 9999px`.
6. **Don't subscribe from this package.** The offer card was measured. No email was submitted and no card was entered.
7. **Don't invent a screenshot.** `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Coverage that needs a PNG is a gap.

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live page in the T3 preview browser. Custom properties were read from `document.documentElement` while `html` had class `wpds-dark`. Component boxes were read from the elements that painted: the home `nav`, the section `ul`, the Politics pill, Save and subscribe, Sign in, the slogan, story `h2`s, the article `h1`, dek, timestamp and body `p`, the offer card, and the sign-in form. Focus on the sign-in email field was applied with `element.focus()` and not by submitting. Hover was not forced. One stylesheet was cross-origin and could not be walked. `prefers-color-scheme` rules in the readable sheets: 0. `prefers-reduced-motion` rules read: 19, including `.wpds-c-kSOqLF { transition: none; }`.

## What to distrust

The 390 pass forced `documentElement` width to 390px because `preview_resize` left the browser viewport at 1280. After that force, `innerWidth` was 390 and `--wpds--screenSize` was `small`. Treat the mobile font sizes as measured under that forced width. The 1440 by 900 resize did report `innerWidth` 1440 and `innerHeight` 900. There are no screenshots. Franklin and Postoni are named in the computed font stack and are not shipped. The sign-in field border `rgb(87, 132, 197)` was read after focus. A resting border on that field was not recorded separately.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/the-washington-post
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 tools/catalog.py design-md the-washington-post
python3 tools/package_tests.py the-washington-post
```

`tools/test_build_tokens.py` holds three tests: the committed tokens.json matches a fresh build, the light accent is the light sign-in reading, and colours are hex while an unknown colour string is refused. `package_tests.py` runs them and exits 0 when they pass.
