# Patterns

Public signed-out pages measured on 2026-11-19. No screenshots were saved.

## Home

The page background is `rgb(20, 20, 20)`. A `nav` sits at 60px tall. Inside it, a 36px round icon button labelled "Search and browse sections", a News Alerts control of the same size, the Save and subscribe pill, and the Sign in pill. Below that, the wordmark SVG filled white, then the slogan "Democracy Dies in Darkness" in Georgia 18px, colour `rgb(161, 161, 161)`. A section bar (`ul`, height 48px, padding 8px 0) holds pills: Election 2026, Politics, Opinions, and the rest. Story headlines are Postoni, 26px, 20px and 18px, colour `rgb(222, 222, 222)`. The first measured image was 628 by 419 CSS pixels. The footer text was Franklin 16px white, and the footer box was 1024px wide at the 1169px viewport.

## Section front

`/politics/` keeps the same dark chrome. The section title is an `h1` in Franklin, 48px on the wide viewport and 28px at the forced 390px width. Topic links such as "Donald Trump" are Franklin 16px weight 400 with padding 8px 0. A 24px Follow circle sits by the title and was not pressed.

## Article

The article column measured 640px. Order: kicker link, Postoni `h1` at 48px, Franklin dek at 20px weight 300, a gray timestamp, then Georgia body at 20px on 32px with 24px bottom padding. A white offer card, 460px wide, overlaps the reading column. It was measured and not submitted.

## Sign-in card

`/subscribe/signin/` is a public page titled "Sign in to The Washington Post". It is a light card, 340px form width, Postoni heading at 24px, an email field, a Next pill, the word "or" in `rgb(89, 89, 89)`, then four OAuth pills and a Continue with SSO text button. Nothing was submitted.
