# Aritzia

A signed-out capture of the public Aritzia shop at [https://www.aritzia.com](https://www.aritzia.com), resolved to [https://www.aritzia.com/intl/en](https://www.aritzia.com/intl/en), region Singapore (S$), on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The homepage is `#000000`, type is Non-Seasonal Geo, and the wordmark on that bar is `#d89f71`. Product and about pages flip the header to white with an ink wordmark. Primary actions are square black fills.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).

| Role | Value | Where |

| Surface | `#ffffff` | product page, login form |

## Type

Non-Seasonal Geo throughout. Body 12px, weight 400, line-height 20px. Nav 16px, weight 400. Product title 16px, weight 500. Hero 48px, line-height 56px, letter-spacing `-1px`. Sign In in the header is 12px and uppercase. Font files are not in this package.

## Controls

Buttons use a `0px` radius. Add to Bag is 40px tall. Login Sign In is 48px tall and 386px wide. The email field is 46px. Header icon targets are 20px. The header is 132px. Promo strips are 28px. Shop Now on the hero is an underline, not a filled button.
