# Unsplash

Unsplash is a public photo and illustration library at [unsplash.com](https://unsplash.com/). This package records the signed-out marketing and search surfaces measured on 2026-11-19: home, explore, a topic, search results, the Plus pricing page, two photo pages, license, about, a missing route, and the login form. Themes in the CSS are light and dark. Every page visited stayed on light. Widths measured were 1440 and 390. Nothing was signed in, downloaded, or paid for.

2. Keep search a gray pill.
3. Keep the shell at 64px.

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://unsplash.com/` in its own tab. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so measurements continued with `preview_evaluate` on the attached page. After `preview_resize` to 1440 by 900, `window.innerWidth` was 1440, `innerHeight` 900, `devicePixelRatio` 2, and `visualViewport` was 1440 by 900 at scale 1. The phone pass measured 390 by 844 at device pixel ratio 2.

Custom properties were read from the five stylesheets and resolved on `:root`. Component styles were `getComputedStyle` on the live controls. Dark tokens were resolved by attaching a detached `.dark` node. A primary, outline, secondary, and disabled button were also measured by inserting the live class names into a hidden node, so those class rules are the page's own CSS.

Pages: `/`, `/explore`, `/plus`, `/s/photos/lighthouse`, `/s/photos/nature`, two `/photos/` pages, `/license`, `/about`, a missing route, `/login`, and `/t/wallpapers`.

## What to distrust

Dark semantic colours were not painted on a signed-out page. They are the `.dark` custom properties, not a screenshot of a dark screen.

The orange focus colour is the computed outline on a focused link and the login field. It is the browser focus ring, `rgb(229, 151, 0)`, not a custom property.

`radius.full` is 999px standing in for a computed `50%` on the avatar and radio. The percentage is the real value.

The secondary blue button was forced with the live class. It was not a button already sitting on the home page.

The apple-touch icon at `/apple-touch-icon.png` is a 180 px 1-bit file of 165 bytes. `icon.png` is the 32 px favicon scaled to 128.

There are no screenshots. Do not treat the style guide drawings as captures.

## Fonts

The computed family on body, headings, buttons, and inputs is `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`. The five stylesheets declared no `@font-face`. The tokens use that stack. Nothing is shipped.

## Do and don't

- **Use the ink button for the main action.** Download and Login are `#111111` with white type and an 8px radius. The outline button is for Submit an image and Share.
- **Keep search a gray pill.** The field is `#eeeeee`, 40px tall, radius 24px, not the 8px field radius.
- **Keep the shell at 64px.** Sidebar and top bar are 64px at desktop. At 390 the bar is 56px and the sidebar is gone.
- **Do not invent a dark screen from the dark tokens.** Those values were resolved on a detached node. The signed-out site stayed light.
- **Do not follow Get Unsplash+ into a payment form.** The price card is public. Checkout was not entered.
