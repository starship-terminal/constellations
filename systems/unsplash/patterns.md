# Unsplash patterns

Public pages only. Signed-in flows stop at the login wall. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## App shell

A left icon rail (photos, illustrations, explore, then account-gated collections, downloads, bookmarks) plus a top bar: search pill, Get Unsplash+, Log in, Submit an image. The topic row sits under the search on the home page. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Menu

Menu opens a three-column dialog over the grid. License, Privacy Policy, Terms, and Security sit on the dialog's bottom edge. See [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png).

## Search

Focusing the search field opens trending searches, topics, and collections. No query was submitted. See [home-light-search-focus](screenshots/home-light-search-focus.png).

## Photo grid

Home, topic, illustrations, and a photo page are masonry photo grids. A photo link opens `/photos/<id>`. See [topic-light-default](screenshots/topic-light-default.png) and [photo-light-default](screenshots/photo-light-default.png).

## Pricing

`/plus` is a public plan card: yearly and monthly tabs, one price, a black Get Unsplash+ button. Checkout was not started. See [plus-light-default](screenshots/plus-light-default.png).

## Login wall

`/login` shows an empty email field, an empty password field, and a black Login button. No Google or Apple button. Nothing was typed. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Marketing

About, license, developers, images, and the awards winners page are public editorial pages. See [about-light-default](screenshots/about-light-default.png) and [developers-light-default](screenshots/developers-light-default.png).
