# Patterns

## Marketing home

[home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) is soundcloud.com at innerWidth 1440, devicePixelRatio 2. The page is `#121212`. A promo banner sits above a 1240px hero with a 16px radius. The hero carries the wordmark, Sign in, Create account, and For artists. The app header computes a 51px bar but `overflow: hidden` keeps it off this page. A cookie banner from OneTrust was hidden for the screenshot and never answered.

## Sign-in

[signin-dark-default.png](screenshots/signin-dark-default.png) is /signin. The same dark page opens a centered card: title, three OAuth buttons, an email field, and a disabled Continue. The form is an iframe on secure.soundcloud.com. Nothing was submitted.

## Discover

[discover-dark-default.png](screenshots/discover-dark-default.png) is /discover. It uses the listener shell: a 28px page title, 22px section titles, and artwork tiles. Some tiles showed a playback error in the capture. That is the page, not a token.

## Contact

[contact-dark-default.png](screenshots/contact-dark-default.png) is /company/contact-us/. It is a separate marketing site: black page, Inter body, 20px cards in pink and lilac, and a black pill button. It is not the listener chrome.

## Phone

[home-light-mobile.png](screenshots/home-light-mobile.png) is the home page at innerWidth 390 with a mobile user agent. The bar is light: Sign in, and Listen in app in a 4px black button. The hero title is 36px/700. A bottom tab row labels Home, Feed, Search, Library, and Download. The track list was still loading when the shot was taken.

## Terms

[terms-dark-default.png](screenshots/terms-dark-default.png) is /terms-of-use in the listener shell. It was not used for type tokens beyond confirming the shell.
