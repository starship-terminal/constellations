# SoundCloud

SoundCloud's public listener home, discover, terms, company contact page, and signed-out sign-in card, captured 2026-11-19 from https://soundcloud.com/ while signed out. Dark on the listener pages. A light form on secure.soundcloud.com/web-auth, and a light phone home bar. Desktop readings at innerWidth 1440. Phone readings at 390. No account was created and no plan was started.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export soundcloud` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use a white pill on the photo and a black pill for the account action.
2. Keep listener controls as pills.
3. Put orange only on playback.
4. Do not paint the hidden header as if it were the hero.
5. Do not treat the contact page as the listener chrome.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read on the live home page in the T3 preview tab at innerWidth 1440, then confirmed in headless Chromium. The same pass read /discover, /signin, /terms-of-use, and /company/contact-us/. The sign-in form is an iframe on secure.soundcloud.com. Its dark styles were read from that frame. A standalone tab of the same host rendered a light form, and those readings are the light set. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so screenshots and crops are from headless Chromium on pages that loaded with HTTP 200 and no Cloudflare challenge. A OneTrust cookie banner was hidden for screenshots and never answered.

Custom properties on `:root` were readable as computed values (`--primary-color` `#121212`, `--special-color` `#f50`, `--link-color` `#044dd2`, `--background-highlight-color` `#f3f3f3`, `--background-surface-color` `#fff`). The stylesheets themselves are cross-origin, so declared rules were not parsed. Tokens are the computed values.

## What to distrust

The app header on the marketing home has `overflow: hidden` and a computed height of 0. Its Sign in and Create account buttons compute a dark tertiary and a white primary, but they are not painted. Trust the hero buttons, which are.

`:root --special-color` is `#f50`. The only painted orange on the home page was the 2px playback progress bar. Do not treat orange as the primary button.

The light auth page and the dark sign-in iframe are the same URL in two presentations. Continue is `#121212` on the light page and white on the dark iframe. Both were disabled at opacity 0.5. Neither was clicked.

Contact cards use Inter and a 20px radius. They are the company site, not the listener shell.

Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json and raw/, not from the PNGs. Photographs in the shots will band.

The wordmark SVG uses `fill="currentColor"`. On the hero it paints white.

## Fonts

Söhne is the listener face. It is proprietary and is not in this package. Tokens fall back to the system sans. The auth email field names SoundCloud Sans, which loaded at weight 500. The auth page body names Inter, which loaded at 400. The company contact page names Inter, Soundcloudsans500, and Sohne Halbfett. None of those files are bundled.

## Do and don't

- **Use a white pill on the photo and a black pill for the account action.** Hero Sign in is white. Hero Create account is black. Get started is `#f3f3f3`. See [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
- **Keep listener controls as pills.** Sign in, Create account, and Get started share a 9999px radius. The phone bar uses 4px. See [hero-create-default.png](components/crops/hero-create-default.png).
- **Put orange only on playback.** `#ff5500` is the progress bar. It is not the sign-up button.
- **Do not paint the hidden header as if it were the hero.** The header computes different button fills and is overflow hidden on this page.
- **Do not treat the contact page as the listener chrome.** Its pink and lilac cards are the company site.
