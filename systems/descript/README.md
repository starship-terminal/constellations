# Descript design reference

A token set and component reference for Descript's public marketing surface and its public sign-in card — for building SaaS marketing pages, workflow-tab heroes, pricing pages and auth cards in the style of the real thing. It was read from **descript.com** on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing, /video-editing, /templates, /customers/saastr, /underlord and the sign-in card at web.descript.com/authenticate, plus a 390px mobile pass on home and pricing. A public refresh on **2026-11-21** added [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and [login-review.json](login-review.json), photographed the header menus, workflow tabs, FAQ, pricing monthly toggle, and the empty authenticate wall, and stopped there. Nothing was typed or submitted.

Descript is worth a capture because a Next.js + Tailwind marketing site paints an unusually warm system: a `#fff7fa` blush field under a 102px floating nav, a deep-maroon hero band carrying an 88px Gamuth display headline and the 610px Record/Edit/Refine/Share/Multiply workflow tab strip (the selected tab fills `#5b142e`), maroon CTAs that ramp `#772a4d` → `#5b142e` on hover and `#390a1a` on the plan cards, `#f73b3b` red accents on eyebrows and secondary CTAs, and a `#110a0d` near-black footer. Three voices carry the type: gamuthDisplay for every display heading (88px hero, 72px page, 56px section), booton at 400-600 on everything else (the auth card reads "Booton VF" at 15.75px), and brett for the 18px uppercase eyebrows. The stylesheet declares the whole palette: `--Red-*`, `--Maroon-*`, `--Lilac-*`, `--Neutral-*`, `--Green-*`, `--Gray-*` step scales, `--Core-*` canvas constants and the `--Tools-Glow-*` pastel accents. **Boundary:** the product lives behind web.descript.com — its public sign-in card was captured and measured but never submitted; Sign in, Sign up, Get started and every plan CTA were measured, never followed; the email field was typed into and cleared, never sent.

### Principles

1. Do put the display headline in Gamuth and keep it huge.
2. Do reserve the red for eyebrows and secondary CTAs.
3. Do let the selected state own the fill.
4. Do keep corners soft but small.
5. Don't darken the page into a dark theme.
6. Don't style the app from this package.
7. Don't invent hover inversions.
8. Don't treat the eyebrow as body text.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every number in this package was read off the live site in headless Chromium, signed out: computed styles from the running DOM on each route (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`), stylesheet custom properties resolved on a probe (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`), and full-page screenshots stitched from inner-scroll tiles (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs` + `tools/capture/quantize.py`). Interactive states were produced locally — hover, Tab focus, DevTools-forced `:active`, and the two toggles that are safe to click: the home workflow tabs and the pricing Monthly/Annual switch. Nothing was submitted, signed into or followed to an authenticated surface.

The declared palette (`--Red-*` … `--Tools-Glow-*`) is exported under `core.color.*`; measured roles under `light.*` alias to declared values where a computed style resolves identically, and carry the raw measured value plus its evidence otherwise. Where a measured value and a declaration match, the role keeps the alias so the token graph stays connected.

**Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Do and don't

1. **Do put the display headline in Gamuth and keep it huge.** 88px on the home hero, 72px on product pages, 56px on sections, 48px on mobile. See [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).
2. **Do reserve the red for eyebrows and secondary CTAs.** `#f73b3b` marks the 18px Brett eyebrow labels and the red CTAs; primary actions stay in the maroon ramp. See [hero-eyebrow](components/crops/hero-eyebrow-default.png) and [vid-cta](components/crops/vid-cta-default.png).
3. **Do let the selected state own the fill.** The workflow tab goes `#5b142e` with near-white text while siblings stay muted `#c4b5bb` on the dark band. See [flow-tab-opened](components/crops/flow-tab-opened.png).
4. **Do keep corners soft but small.** 12px on CTAs and plan cards, 8px on auth controls; the one big pill is the 42px billing-toggle track. See [pr-toggle](components/crops/pr-toggle-default.png).
5. **Don't darken the page into a dark theme.** The hero band and the `#110a0d` footer are surfaces inside the single light theme; the export keeps `dark` empty. See [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).
6. **Don't style the app from this package.** Everything behind web.descript.com/authenticate was never reached; the sign-in card is the boundary. See [auth-light-default](screenshots/auth-light-default.png).
7. **Don't invent hover inversions.** Maroon buttons deepen (`#772a4d` → `#5b142e`), red CTAs deepen (`#f73b3b` → `#8f0c10`), nav links take a `#f1eaed` wash. See [signup-cta-hover](components/crops/signup-cta-hover.png) and [nav-link-hover](components/crops/nav-link-hover.png).
8. **Don't treat the eyebrow as body text.** It's 18px Brett, uppercase, red — a label voice, not a paragraph. See [hero-eyebrow](components/crops/hero-eyebrow-default.png).

## Coverage

- **Captured and measured:** the marketing home, /pricing, /video-editing, /templates, /customers/saastr, /underlord (desktop + full pages), mobile home and pricing, and the public auth card at web.descript.com/authenticate — 45 components, ~130 states.
- **Local interactions measured:** the workflow tab selection and the Monthly/Annual billing toggle (opened states), hover/focus-visible/pressed on every control, focused/filled on the auth email field.
- **Declared but unverified:** the full palette scales are exported from the stylesheet; only the steps the public pages actually paint are aliased by measured roles.
- **Boundaries:** everything behind web.descript.com/authenticate — the buttons and field were measured, never submitted; no authenticated page, editor or account surface was reached. The 2026-11-21 pass photographed the Features, Solutions, and Resources menus and the empty login wall, then stopped. Continue with Google was not clicked. No consent overlay appeared. The walk is in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
- **Known gaps:** pressed was forced via DevTools so a mousedown-only style (if any) is missing; the `-full` mobile pages stitch inner-scroll tiles; gradients and photographs in screenshots are 256-colour quantized, so read colours from tokens.json, never the PNGs.
