Palette

The declared scales: warm pearl-* neutrals and graphite-* inks, the signature forest-* greens, plus olive/royal/iris/coral/rose/scarlet/amber ramps, the *-fade-* alpha partners and the 12 quote-theme-* reader colours. The semantic --color-background-*/--color-text-*/--color-border-*/--color-line-* roles resolve in the light set; the dark set resolves the same names through the declared .dark scope. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius and motion

The declared --radius-0..40 scale plus --radius-full 9999px — catalog pills, chips and buttons run full-round; the emotion skin uses literal 60px pills and a 44px text-input shell. Controls transition over 0.15s ease in the catalog skin and 0.2s on the marketing skin; the marketing club card zooms to scale(1.01) over 0.4s ease-in-out.
