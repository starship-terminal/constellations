## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export perplexity` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use the teal accent for the product mark and for accent text.
2. Use pills for primary actions.
3. Keep the composer as a wide rounded shell, not a single-line input.
4. Leave the signed-out nav sparse.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue` on the live product home, once with `prefers-color-scheme: light` and once with dark. The hub was read the same way in light. 242 of the named properties resolved on the product root. The hub resolved the same semantic colours. Four easing names were absent on the hub, and `--pplx-marketing-header-height` (56px) was present only there.

Component colours, type, radius, and padding were read from the elements on the page: the composer shell, the nav, Sign In, View plans, See a demo, Try Perplexity, Select, Get started, and the Popular badge. Screenshots are viewport PNGs at the measured CSS size, 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at device pixel ratio 2.

The T3 preview tab loaded both the product and the hub and returned computed styles. It then failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". The screenshots in this package are from headless Playwright on public pages that returned HTTP 200 with no Cloudflare challenge.

## What to distrust

The page background on the product home computed to `color(srgb 0.984314 0.984314 0.984314)` (`#fbfbfc` if rounded). `--bg-base` on the same element is `#fbfbfb`. The hub body computed to `rgb(250, 248, 245)` while `--bg-base` stayed `#fbfbfb`. The tokens record the custom property, not the body background.

`--sidebar-width` computed to 220px. The painted nav measured 240px. The component token records 240px.

Font weights on the product are 350 and 450 in dark, and 400 and 500 in light, for the same `--font-weight-normal` and `--font-weight-medium` names. Core records the light numbers and names the dark ones in the source string. `light.fontWeight` and `dark.fontWeight` hold each theme.

See a demo over the hub photograph computed to `#dbdee3`. The same label on the light pricing header computed to `#252525`. Both are recorded. Neither is a hover state.

Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from the tokens.

The product heading on the home is 26px. `core.typography.hero` is the authored `--ui-heading-hero` at 46px, which that screen does not use. The hub h1 computed to about 149px, a layout size, not the token.

## Fonts

pplxSans, pplxSerif, GT Canon VF, and PPLX Mono are proprietary. They are named in the tokens and are not committed. The style guide falls back to the system sans.

## Do and don't

- **Use the teal accent for the product mark and for accent text.** Light accent is `#016a71`. Dark accent is `#4e99a3`. The hub hero and the pricing featured border use the light one. See [hub-light-default.png](screenshots/hub-light-default.png).
- **Use pills for primary actions.** Get started, See a demo, and Continue with Google are 9999px pills. See [button-solid-default.png](components/crops/button-solid-default.png).
- **Keep the composer as a wide rounded shell, not a single-line input.** The signed-out home puts the question in a 20px-radius box. See [text-field-default.png](components/crops/text-field-default.png).
- **Leave the signed-out nav sparse.** It lists the product areas and ends in Sign In. Empty projects and recents stay as empty copy. See [sidebar-default.png](components/crops/sidebar-default.png).
