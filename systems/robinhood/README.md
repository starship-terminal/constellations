# Robinhood design reference

A token set for Robinhood's public signed-out web chrome, for building marketing pages and a signed-out login in the style of the real thing. It was read on **2026-11-19**, signed out. The design surface is the US marketing page at [robinhood.com/us/en/](https://robinhood.com/us/en/) and the signed-out login at [robinhood.com/login](https://robinhood.com/login). `https://robinhood.com/` redirected to the Singapore waitlist (`/sg/en/`), which was not the surface used for tokens.

Marketing paints a black nav, a lime banner (`#CCFF00`), white and black sections, and pills at 36px radius. Login and signup paint one dark theme: `body` class `is-dark-theme theme-closed-up`, page `#000000`, green accent `#00C805` from `--rh__primary-base`. No light theme was painted on the login or signup pages.

**Boundary:** brokerage. No account was created. No email, password, phone, card, bank, SSN, or KYC value was entered. Signup stopped at the country and region search ("Where do you live?"). Google, Apple, and passkey buttons were not clicked. The Usercentrics cookie banner was hidden in screenshot CSS and never accepted or rejected.

### Principles

1. Do paint the marketing nav black and the banner lime.
2. Do use 36px pills on marketing and 44px pills on the app login.
3. Do set login type in Capsule Sans and the hero in Martina Plantijn.
4. Do keep the app page black and the positive green at `#00C805`.
5. Don't style the brokerage from this package.
6. Don't invent an app light theme.
7. Don't enter the signup past the region question.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview tab with `preview_evaluate` on the live page. The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames", so screenshots were taken afterwards in headless Chromium against the same public URLs, which had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. Marketing colours, type, radii, and control sizes come from `getComputedStyle` on the US home page (preview viewport 1169 by 731, devicePixelRatio 2). App colours, the type palette, gutters, and focus colour come from custom properties resolved on `document.body` at `/login`, where `body` has class `is-dark-theme`. Cross-origin stylesheets could not be read with `cssRules`. Their resolved values were read from the element instead.

Screenshot viewports, measured after load: desktop `innerWidth` 1440, `innerHeight` 900, `devicePixelRatio` 1. Phone `innerWidth` 390, `innerHeight` 844, `devicePixelRatio` 2.

## What to distrust

The marketing muted colour `#4D4A46` was in the page's computed colour set and is not pinned to one named role. Disclaimer ink `#35322D` and on-dark grey `#D9D9D9` are pinned to elements. The 3px radius seen in the marketing radius set is not in the tokens, because it was not tied to one control. Marketing pill `line-height` computed as the keyword `normal`. The button type token uses 20px from the login Log In label. App dark has no painted light counterpart. The first read of `--rh__*` on `:root` returned empty strings. The values used are the ones resolved on `body`.

## Fonts

Capsule Sans Text, Capsule Sans Display, and Capsule Sans Book Cover are Robinhood's licensed faces, preloaded as woff2 from `cdn.robinhood.com`. Phonic is the marketing section face. Martina Plantijn is the marketing hero serif. Tokens name the families and a system fallback stack. The font files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the marketing nav black and the banner lime.** The US home nav is `#000000` and the sweepstakes strip is `#CCFF00`. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do use 36px pills on marketing and 44px pills on the app login.** Marketing Sign up is 44px tall with radius 36px. Login Log In is a white pill with radius 44px. See [components/crops/signup-pill-default.png](components/crops/signup-pill-default.png) and [components/crops/login-submit-default.png](components/crops/login-submit-default.png).
- **Do set login type in Capsule Sans and the hero in Martina Plantijn.** Login title is Capsule Sans Display 21px weight 500. The IPO headline is Martina Plantijn 72/78. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do keep the app page black and the positive green at `#00C805`.** That green is `--rh__primary-base` on `body.is-dark-theme`. See [screenshots/login-dark-default.png](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
- **Don't style the brokerage from this package.** Positions, charts, transfers, and settings sit behind login. See [screenshots/signup-dark-default.png](screenshots/signup-dark-default.png).
- **Don't invent an app light theme.** Login and signup only painted `is-dark-theme`. See [screenshots/login-dark-default.png](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
- **Don't enter the signup past the region question.** The next step asks for an email and password, then identity. See [screenshots/signup-dark-default.png](screenshots/signup-dark-default.png).
