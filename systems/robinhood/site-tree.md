# Robinhood public site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-22 from the public US homepage, signed out. Desktop is 1440×900. Headless Chromium opened the menus and the marketing pages. The T3 preview confirmed the same homepage, the same three menus, and the same Log in and Sign up controls. No email, name, password, phone, code, or address was typed. No identity provider was clicked. No form was submitted. No captcha was solved. No application or funding flow was started.

`https://robinhood.com/` returns 200 and redirects to `https://robinhood.com/sg/en/`, a Singapore waitlist ("Robinhood is not yet available to the public in Singapore"). That is a geo redirect, not the US marketing site. The tree below starts at `https://robinhood.com/us/en/`. The site is the live Robinhood marketing site, not a parked page.

A Usercentrics cookie banner sat over the first preview frame. It was hidden in screenshot CSS and never accepted or rejected. Cookie Settings was not clicked.
