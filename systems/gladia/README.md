# Gladia design reference

A token set and component reference for Gladia's public surfaces — for building developer-audio-AI marketing pages, pricing grids, docs-site chrome and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **gladia.io** on **2026-11-15**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing, the /product/speech-to-text-api family (plus /real-time and /async), /product/audio-intelligence, the /use-cases/* family (voice-agents, meeting-assistants, ccaas, media), /about-us, /blog, /changelog, /testimonials, /partners, /library, /demo-request, /security, /compliance-hub, /gladiaflow, /solaria-3, /creator-program, /start-up-studio, /meeting-assistant-market-map, /benchmark-reliability-checker and /competitors/benchmarks; the header's Product mega-menu measured and the other four captured; three docs.gladia.io pages (the Mintlify introduction, a quickstart and an API page — including the docs' own dark theme preference); the app.gladia.io / and /signup auth shells; and 390px passes on home, pricing, demo-request, library and a docs page.

Gladia is worth a capture because its marketing site declares a real token layer to the browser: `--color-neutral-0..1000` and `--color-purple/blue/green/red/yellow/orange/pink` ramps (brand purple `--color-purple` #947afc, `--color-purple-500` #925bff), `--alpha-white-*`/`--alpha-black-*` translucents, a 4px `--space-*` scale, `--radius-*` pill geometry, a `--font-size-10..80` ramp in **Suisse Intl** with **Geist Mono** for code, `--duration-*`/`--ease-*` motion and the semantic `--text-*`/`--bg-*`/`--border-*` roles. The architecture is unusual: `:root` paints **dark** on every marketing page — an emulated `prefers-color-scheme` pass resolves identically and no theme control exists — while `body.light-mode` re-points the roles to the light values /library paints, and `.section--white` re-points a smaller subset for white sections inside dark pages. docs.gladia.io is a separate Mintlify stack with a genuine light/dark preference; app.gladia.io is the product auth shell. **Boundary:** the app console and every post-login surface — API keys, the transcription playground, usage, billing — was never reached; the /demo-request, calculator, login and signup fields were typed into then cleared, never submitted; every CTA, SSO button, nav item and link was measured, never followed; the mega-menu opens by hover intent, the pricing toggle and FAQ accordions are local UI toggles, and the docs dark preference was captured, never held.

### Principles

1. Do let the dark :root carry the page.
2. Do spend the purple on structure, not floods.
3. Do use glass for chrome.
4. Do pair the two voices on purpose.
5. Don't style the signed-in console from this package.
6. Don't treat `light` as a theme toggle.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand or the fonts.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Three passes read the site, all signed out:

- **Stylesheet harvest** (`tools/capture/vars.mjs` → `raw/css-vars.json`): every custom property declared in the live linked `/assets/*.css` and `/css/*.css` bundles plus the inline `<style>` blocks, reparsed with a browser-compatible parser, then resolved through computed styles on the real pages — on `:root` (dark), on a `body.light-mode` probe, on `.section--white`, and under emulated light/dark `prefers-color-scheme` (both resolve identically to the painted `:root`; the site ships no media-query theming).
- **Component measurement** (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` → `raw/components-computed.json`): computed styles, geometry, paint layers and ancestor paint for 58 components across the surfaces, in states — hover by real pointer, focus-visible by real keyboard focus, pressed forced with DevTools `CSS.forcePseudoState` so nothing was ever pressed, fields typed into then cleared, and local UI toggles (mega-menu triggers, the pricing-mode toggle, FAQ accordions, the mobile menu button, the docs theme preference) used the page's own controls.
- **Screenshots and layout** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs`, `layout.mjs`, `motion.mjs`): bounded whole-page captures at 1440 and 390, multi-width layout probes, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` animation audit.

`tools/build_component_tokens.py` turns the harvest and the measurements into the token tree; `tools/build_tokens.py` writes `tokens.json`; `tools/build_style_guide.py` and `tools/build_components_md.py` write the rendered and readable references.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **`lib-card`, `blog-card`, `blog-h1` and `glass-card` read 0x0 or missed**: the selectors matched elements the page kept hidden, zero-sized or renamed — honest misses kept in the data, not gaps filled by hand. `glass-card` and `login-signup-link` mount nothing the selectors could settle on at all.
- **The auth shell's `login-email`/`signup-email` are 0x0** because the app keeps the email step behind the SSO screen; the password field measured 336 x 24.
- **`demo-terms`' `focused` state could not land a keyboard focus** (the real input is visually hidden behind its `label.form-checkbox`), so that reading is the label at rest — the `via` field says so.
- **Quantised screenshots** are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from `tokens.json`, never from a screenshot pixel.
- **The docs `dark` theme and the auth shell are different stacks**: their values live in measurements and screenshots, not in the marketing token sets — do not read `dark.*` tokens as the docs' or app's theme.
- **Calculator prices, plan amounts and marketing copy** are content, not style: only frames and components were measured, and alternate calculator outputs were never captured.

## Fonts

**Suisse Intl** (book/regular/medium/semibold) carries every heading, control and body on the marketing site; **Geist Mono** draws code, labels and the mega-menu eyebrows — both self-hosted woff2 under `/assets` and `/css`, declared by the harvest's 5 @font-face rules. The /blog Webflow stack and the Mintlify docs ship their own faces. The style guide falls back to system fonts; Suisse Intl is a commercial face — substitute a neutral grotesque, never ship the files.

## Coverage

Captured, all signed out: 26 marketing pages plus their header mega-menus, the pricing Async/Real-time toggle and calculator, the /demo-request form, /library on `body.light-mode`, the /blog index on the legacy Webflow stack, three Mintlify docs pages including their dark preference, the app.gladia.io / and /signup auth shells, and 390px mobile passes. Not captured: every post-login surface (the console, API keys, playground, usage, billing — needs an account), form submissions and what they return, blog posts and docs articles beyond the exemplars, the non-Product mega-menu panels' component measurements (screenshots only), and disabled/error/loading states that only appear after submission. Full detail in components.md's Gaps.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the dark :root carry the page.** Every marketing surface paints black `--bg-primary` with white ink ([home](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)); the `dark` token set is the default, not an opt-in.
2. **Do spend the purple on structure, not floods.** #947afc/#925bff mark eyebrow labels, focus rings, gradients and selected states ([mega-menu](components/crops/mega-panel-default.png), [pricing toggle](components/crops/price-mode-async-focus-visible.png)); the header CTA is a plain white pill — restraint is the pattern.
3. **Do use glass for chrome.** The navbar and mega-menu are rgba-black fills over hairline `alpha.white.*` borders at 20–23px radius ([nav pill](components/crops/nav-demo-default.png)); sections stack on the black field.
4. **Do pair the two voices on purpose.** Suisse Intl for everything a reader reads, Geist Mono for code, eyebrows and labels ([hero](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png), [docs code](components/crops/docs-code-default.png)).
5. **Don't style the signed-in console from this package.** The app shell was measured only at its auth edge ([login](screenshots/login-dark-default.png)); every post-login surface is unknown.
6. **Don't treat `light` as a theme toggle.** It is the `body.light-mode` scope /library paints ([library](screenshots/library-light-default.png)); no marketing page offers a switch, and the docs dark preference is a different token layer entirely.
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** Every field was measured and cleared, never submitted ([demo form](components/crops/demo-submit-default.png)) — error, loading and success states are unverified.
8. **Don't copy the brand or the fonts.** The wordmark, icons and Suisse Intl files are Gladia's ([logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png)); the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.
