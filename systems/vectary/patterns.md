# Patterns

## Marketing shell

A yellow announcement bar sits above a light nav. The bar is `#fbd302`, 37px tall, 12px Inter. The nav is 87px tall with 30px side padding. Login is a dark 8px button. Sign up is a white button with a grey stroke. The footer is `#0b0b0b` with `#949494` type and 60px of vertical padding.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

At 390px the nav collapses to the mark, Login, Sign up, and a menu button. The menu was not opened.

[screenshots/pricing-light-phone.png](screenshots/pricing-light-phone.png)

## Hero

The home title is Fira Sans 60px weight 300, line-height 60px, tracking -2px, colour `#252525`. The subtitle is Inter 16px weight 300, colour `#595959`. The two actions are Create account and Let's talk, 15px apart.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

The page background is `#f0f0f0`. The body also has a 16px radius on the top corners.

## Pricing

Four plans in a row: Free, Starter, Team, Business. Each card has a 20px radius, 24px padding, and a `#d0d0d0` stroke. Starter and Team carry a yellow "limited -40% price" pill. Prices read on the page were $0, $15, $150, and Custom. Checkout was not opened.

[screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Signed-out login

The first screen offers Google, Apple, GitHub, Slack, and Use email. Those provider buttons were not clicked. Use email opens a form with email, password, a disabled-looking submit at opacity 0.5, Forgot password, and Log in with SSO. SSO was not opened. Nothing was submitted.

## Signup

The signup form asks for first name, last name, work email, password, an occupation select, a marketing checkbox, and a required terms checkbox. It was not submitted. No screenshot of that form is in the package, because the fields would have held typed values if the capture had continued, and it did not.
