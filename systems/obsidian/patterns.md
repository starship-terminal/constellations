# Patterns

## App shell

The marketing shell is a fixed 90px header and a five-group footer. At 1440 the header shows Download, Pricing, Sync, Publish, Enterprise, Community, and Account. At 390 a menu button opens the same product links in a full-width list.

## Pricing

https://obsidian.md/pricing shows Sync and Publish. The yearly figures read on the page were $4 and $8. The monthly figures were $5 and $10. Sign up on those cards links to `/buy/sync` and `/buy/publish`. Those links were not clicked.

## Download

https://obsidian.md/download lists macOS, iOS, Android, Windows, and Linux. The macOS row links to a dmg. The file was not kept. Store badges were not followed.

## Empty

A missing path shows a 404 page with the heading "You found an unresolved link."

## Auth wall

Account opens https://obsidian.md/auth. Sign in and Create an account share that page. Both were photographed empty. No identity provider was on the page. No field was filled.

## Where it stops

Checkout, downloads, store listings, docs, the forum, Discord, and the signed-in account were not entered.
