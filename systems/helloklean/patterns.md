# Patterns

How Hello Klean's public storefront combines the measured pieces. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Chrome

A 30 px ink announcement bar scrolls "Black Friday Sale | Up to 30% off" and a free-shipping threshold. Under it, a white header: a hamburger, text links (Shop All, Filters, Hair, Body, Refills, Accessories), a centred HKRepro wordmark, a paper Shop Skin Filter pill, search, account, cart, and an EN | market pill.

The hamburger opens a panel over the hero: Meet Skin Filter, Smart Refill Plan, Your Water Report, About Us, FAQs, Expert Insight, and a market pill. On the phone the same control opens a full-screen list with a black Shop Skin Filter pill, then language and market pills.

Evidence: [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png), [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero

A full-bleed product photograph in a rounded frame. The headline sits on the photo in white or near-white, with a short offer line and a paper pill ("Shop the sale", "Shop Shower Head+"). On the phone the headline drops below the product still, inside the same brown frame.

Evidence: [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Shop grid

A centred title ("All Products", "Filters & Refills", "Hair & Scalp Care"), an offer line, then a pill segmented control. Cards are a paper image, a New or Bestseller chip on the image, a title, a grey subtitle, and a black Shop Now pill with the price. Four columns at 1440, two at 390.

Evidence: [shop-light-default](screenshots/shop-light-default.png), [filters-light-default](screenshots/filters-light-default.png), [hair-light-default](screenshots/hair-light-default.png), [mobile-shop-light-default](screenshots/mobile-shop-light-default.png).

## Buy box

The Skin Filter page splits a large product image and a column of numbered steps: device card, colour dots, then supply cards. The selected supply card shows the sale price, a struck compare-at price, a green SAVE chip, and refill copy. A black Start now pill sticks over the column. The shampoo page uses size pills and two purchase radios (one-time, Subscribe & Save) above a full-width Shop Now pill.

Evidence: [product-light-default](screenshots/product-light-default.png), [shampoo-light-default](screenshots/shampoo-light-default.png), [mobile-product-light-default](screenshots/mobile-product-light-default.png).

## Search and empty cart

Search opens a small paper field, "Type here", over the hero, and a results page with the same card grid. The cart drawer is a white sheet: "Your cart is empty", a black Continue shopping pill, and a sign-in prompt. The cart page repeats that empty state above the ink footer.

Evidence: [home-light-search](screenshots/home-light-search.png), [search-light-default](screenshots/search-light-default.png), [home-light-cart](screenshots/home-light-cart.png), [cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png).

## Footer and contact

The footer is an ink band. A newsletter field and a paper Subscribe pill sit on the left. Link columns sit on the right. Language and market pills sit below. Contact is a two-column page: prose on the left, a paper form card on the right (select, fields, textarea, marketing checkbox, Send pill).

Evidence: [cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png), [contact-light-default](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).

## What this package does not show

Orders, Profile, checkout, payment, a filled cart, and the Skio portal. The login screenshot is the Shopify customer-accounts host, not this chrome.
