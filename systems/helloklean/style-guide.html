What was measured

helloklean.com is a Shopify storefront (theme BF26, schema Quickfire 3.0) selling shower filters and hard-water hair and body care. It paints one light theme: warm paper #f5f4f1, near-black ink #1d1d1f, and pill buttons at a 25px radius. Display type is HKRepro. Body type is DM Sans. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.
