# Hello Klean design reference

A token set and component reference for Hello Klean's public storefront. It was read from **helloklean.com** on **2026-11-19**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 (device pixel ratio 1) and 390 x 844 (device pixel ratio 2, so the phone PNGs are 780 px wide). Captured: the international home page, the shop grid, the Filters and Hair collections, the Skin Filter and shampoo product pages, About, FAQ, the subscription marketing page, Contact, the water-report landing, the empty cart, the Shopify customer-account login card, search results, and a 390 px pass of home, shop and product, including the mobile navigation drawer.

Hello Klean sells shower and tap filters, plus hair and body care, on a Shopify storefront (theme BF26, schema Quickfire 3.0.0). The surface is one light theme: warm paper `#f5f4f1` (`--secondary-color`) against near-black ink `#1d1d1f` (`--primary-color`). Type is HKRepro at 400 and 500 for display and chrome, with DM Sans declared as the body fallback. Buttons are 25 px pills (`--button-1-radius`) with 20 px by 12 px padding and 14 px type. The primary button inverts on hover: ink fill and paper text become paper fill and ink text. Product photography sits on paper cards with a soft corner; New and Bestseller chips sit on the image. **Boundary:** checkout, payment, a filled cart and the Skio subscription portal were not entered. A Shopify customer account was created by email code for the capture inbox and the empty Orders and Profile pages were opened in the owner's browser; those screens were not saved, because the preview host stopped producing frames.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do keep the field to paper and ink.
2. Do set buttons as 25 px pills.
3. Do set display in HKRepro at a light weight.
4. Do let the product photograph carry the card.
5. Do keep the buy box as numbered steps.
6. Don't add a dark theme.
7. Don't style the account or checkout from these tokens.
8. Don't treat one currency string as the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every public value came off the signed-out site in headless Chromium. Consent and promo overlays were hidden in the page, never clicked.

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) loads `/en-int`, captures the stylesheet bodies and resolves the `:root` custom properties into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) maps that layer into `core` and `light`, then adds `core.motion` from [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json) through [tools/motion.py](tools/motion.py).
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) reads computed style and box size in default, hover and focus-visible. Results are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Several selectors matched the wrong node; those misses are named in [components.md](components.md) and below.
- **Layout and icons.** [tools/capture/layout.mjs](tools/capture/layout.mjs) records landmarks at 1440, 1024 and 390. [tools/capture/icons.mjs](tools/capture/icons.mjs) lifts inline SVGs into [icons/](icons).
- **Motion.** [tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs](tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs) reads computed transitions and animations on home, filters, the Skin Filter product and FAQ, then again under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. The page verdict is `partial`: almost every moving element keeps its motion, and `dash`, `rotator` and `translateinfinite` still loop.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours. The desktop menu and the opened FAQ row were recaptured in a second pass. Nothing was added to the cart and no checkout, newsletter or contact form was submitted.

A Shopify customer account for the capture inbox was created at `account.helloklean.com` with an email code. Marketing email was left unchecked. The empty Orders page and the Profile page were read in the owner's preview browser. That host then stopped producing screenshot frames, so those screens are not in `screenshots/`. The Skio portal at `/a/account/login` refused the same email until an order exists. No phone number, card or government ID was requested or entered.

## What to distrust

- **This package is the storefront, not the account or checkout.** Orders, Profile, a filled cart, payment and the Skio portal are not in the screenshots. The login shot is the Shopify customer-accounts host: a purple "Continue with shop" button, not the storefront pill matrix.
- **Several component crops are misnamed.** `product-card` is the header Shop Skin Filter pill. `hero-cta` is the cart's Continue Shopping pill. `add-to-cart` is the footer Subscribe pill. `faq-row` is the hamburger glyph. `nav-cta` is the Shop All text link. Use the screenshots for those controls, and the Gaps section of [components.md](components.md).
- **Market and currency drift.** Most shots are the international market with Singapore dollars and "Free Shipping Over $150". The desktop menu shot landed on United Kingdom GBP and "Free Shipping Over £35". Do not treat one announcement string as the brand constant.
- **The opened FAQ answer is below the fold.** [faq-light-open](screenshots/faq-light-open.png) shows a minus on "What is hard water?" and does not show the answer copy.
- **The subscription page is a marketing page.** [subscription-light-default](screenshots/subscription-light-default.png) is the storefront subscription story, with a sticky "Start now" pill over the quote. It is not the Skio account.
- **Some type values were entered by hand.** The heading-2, heading-3, heading-4 and body line heights, the heading-4 weight and `light.text.muted` `#6e6e73` have no raw reading behind them; their sources say Reconstruction. heading-1's line height (1.1) is measured on the hero title, and the button's weight 400 and 14 px line on `hero-cta` (the cart's Continue Shopping pill, a storefront pill at the declared 14 px).
- **Focus rings are not an authored token.** `light.focus.ring` aliases ink because no focus custom property exists. The focus-visible crops record whatever the browser painted.
- **Quantised screenshots.** Read exact colours from [tokens.json](tokens.json) or [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json).
- **Account chrome is Shopify's.** Body type on `account.helloklean.com` computed as Times, main as system UI at 14 px, on a white page. Those readings are not storefront tokens.

## Fonts

- **HKRepro** at 400 and 500 is the display and chrome face. Headings use it at the declared scale (38 / 32 / 28 / 20 px, with mobile steps at 38 / 30 / 24). Buttons and nav use 14 px. Served from the Shopify theme. The licence is not published here, and the files are not vendored.
- **DM Sans** at 400, 500 and 700 is declared as the body fallback and loads on `/en-int`. The tokens name it as `core.fontFamily.body`.
- **Kanit** also loads on the storefront. It is not the heading or button face in the measured chrome, so it is not a token.
- The style guide falls back to sans-serif when HKRepro is absent.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the field to paper and ink.** Page and cards are `#f5f4f1` and white. Text, the announcement bar and the primary fill are `#1d1d1f`. ([home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png))
- **Do set buttons as 25 px pills.** Primary is ink with paper text and inverts on hover. Secondary is paper with ink text. Padding is 20 px by 12 px, type is 14 px. ([hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png))
- **Do set display in HKRepro at a light weight.** Headings run 400 at 38 / 32 / 28 px. The wordmark is the same face. ([logo](components/crops/logo-default.png))
- **Do let the product photograph carry the card.** Shop cards are a paper image, a short title, a grey subtitle, and a black Shop Now pill. New and Bestseller chips sit on the image. ([shop-light-default](screenshots/shop-light-default.png))
- **Do keep the buy box as numbered steps.** Device, colour dots, then a selected supply card with the sale price and a sticky Start now pill. ([product-light-default](screenshots/product-light-default.png))
- **Don't add a dark theme.** None is declared. The announcement bar and the footer are ink bands inside the light theme. ([cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png))
- **Don't style the account or checkout from these tokens.** The login host uses a purple Shopify button and system type. ([login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png))
- **Don't treat one currency string as the brand.** The same chrome shows $150 on the international market and £35 on the UK market. ([home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png))
