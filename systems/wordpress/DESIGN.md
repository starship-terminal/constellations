# WordPress.com

Hand-written from the measured tokens. `make design-md` was not run, so the catalog was not rewritten.

## Colors

Primary action blue is `#3858e9` (`--studio-blue-50`). Ink is `#101517`. Paper is `#f6f7f7`. Muted gray is `#646970`. Green `#008a20` appears on pricing savings. Red and pink are declared and were not used on a photographed control.

## Typography

Display is Recoleta (`recoleta-web`), 80px on the homepage headline, 72px on pricing, 44px on the login heading. UI is Inter (`inter-variable-web`) on the marketing site, 13px in the nav and 16px on the hero button. The login wall falls back to the system sans.

## Geometry

Marketing buttons use a 4px radius. Login controls use 2px. Nav row is 64px. Hero button is 48px. Login field and Continue are 40px.

## Do and don't

Use one blue for WordPress.com's own actions. Keep Log in as text beside the blue button. Set headlines in Recoleta and controls in Inter. Leave signup Continue disabled until an email is present.
