# Attio site tree

Mapped on 2026-11-21 from the T3 in-app preview, default profile. Desktop CSS viewport measured `innerWidth` 1440, `innerHeight` 900, `devicePixelRatio` 2, `visualViewport` 1440 by 900 at scale 1.

The marketing home is public. Sign in, Start for free, and `https://app.attio.com/` all land on the same signed-out page, `https://app.attio.com/onboarding/welcome`. That page offers Google and email sign-in. It is a login wall. No password, code, or onboarding field was filled. Cookie Continue and Reject were not clicked. The Intercom messenger was not opened. External stores, social profiles, docs hosts, and sales forms were recorded from their href and not followed.
