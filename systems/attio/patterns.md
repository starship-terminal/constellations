# Patterns

The [company table](screenshots/companies-light-scoped.png) separates sidebar navigation, view selection, sort/filter controls and the data grid. The [sort dialog](screenshots/companies-sort-light.png) exposes direction and priority; the [column dialog](screenshots/companies-view-settings-light.png) exposes field visibility. These are opened controls, not evidence of saved configuration changes.

The [record overview](screenshots/company-record-overview.png) combines tabs, overview information and an attribute panel. [Notes](screenshots/company-notes-empty.png) provides an empty state. The [create dialog](screenshots/company-create-light.png) is an unsubmitted form. Validation errors, destructive confirmations, global search results, loading and error screens were not captured.

[Onboarding](screenshots/onboarding-proposed-structure.png) offers proposed CRM objects; the capture used basic setup. Those onboarding illustrations are separate from live seeded company records. The [mailbox skip dialog](screenshots/onboarding-skip-email-dialog.png) and [completion](screenshots/onboarding-complete.png) show onboarding feedback. Attio provisioned a trial automatically.

The [public hero](screenshots/landing-first.png) and [mobile menu](screenshots/landing-mobile-menu.png) are marketing evidence. The signed-in [390px table](screenshots/companies-mobile-dark-collapsed.png) retains horizontal overflow and is not a mobile-optimized specimen.

## Public site, 21 November 2026

The marketing home, pricing, and customers pages were open in the T3 preview while the app session was signed out. The home hero is a public demo of the product. Desktop measured 1440 by 900 CSS pixels at device pixel ratio 2. Phone measured 390 by 844 at the same ratio. The Platform menu and the phone menu painted. The desktop Resources menu was in the DOM with `display: none`, so it has no screenshot. The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

The login wall at `https://app.attio.com/onboarding/welcome` is a two-column card on desktop and a single column on phone. Google is a filled blue button. Email is an empty field with a round continue control. Nothing was typed or submitted. See [login-review.json](login-review.json) and [login-wall-desktop-2026-11-21.png](screenshots/login-wall-desktop-2026-11-21.png).
