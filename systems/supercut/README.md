# Supercut

Supercut is a screen recorder for business communication (supercut.ai). This package captures the public signed-out marketing site on 2026-11-19. The site loads with `html.dark`. No account was created. Google, email, and SSO on signup and login were not followed. No card, phone, or company SSO was entered.

Computed styles were read in a T3 preview tab at 1169 by 731 CSS pixels, devicePixelRatio 2. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." Screenshots were then taken with headless Playwright on the same public pages, which loaded without a Cloudflare challenge, at 1169 by 900 and 390 by 844, devicePixelRatio 2. Login and signup were not opened in that headless session.

### Principles

1. Use the cream pill for the main action.
2. Keep page colour `#100e0e` and muted text `#969692`.
3. Do not paint the marketing button with `#ff0055`.
4. Keep headings in Inter Display at weight 500 with the negative tracking from `--type-title-web-*`.
5. Keep the product menu on `#181616` with a 14px radius and the 12px 40px black shadow.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` on https://supercut.ai/ while `html` had class `dark`. The stylesheet is `tailwind_new-BGXbjisN.css` on cdn.supercut.ai. Cross-origin `cssRules` threw, so authored selectors were also fetched with curl and compared with the computed values. Component colours, type, padding, radius, and shadow were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. The cookie note was dismissed with Accept before the button readings. The Product menu was opened with a real click.

## What to distrust

The home h1 computed at 55.2px because of a fluid class. The hero token uses the CSS variable at the desktop breakpoint, 60px. Primary button radius computed as about `1.67772e+07px`. The token records that as a 9999px pill. Muted nav text is `#969692`. `--custom-color-foregroundMuted` is `#858580` and is stored separately. `--custom-color-primary` is `#ff0055` and is not the marketing button fill. The cream button is. Badge text was lab(), stored as the matching Tailwind hex. Header background was an oklab mix at 90 percent. Screenshot viewports are 1169 by 900, not the 731-tall preview tab, and not 1440. Phone is 390 by 844. The wordmark SVG in the page was not saved whole.

## Fonts

Inter Display and InterVariable are loaded from the Supercut CDN for marketing UI. Body and html compute to Geist. Tokens name the families and do not ship the font files. Fallbacks are `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif`.

## Do and don't

- **Use the cream pill for the main action.** "Get started free" and "Sign up" are `#fdfff0` on `#100e0e` text. See [screenshots/home-dark.png](screenshots/home-dark.png).
- **Keep page colour `#100e0e` and muted text `#969692`.** Nav and the home subcopy use that pair.
- **Do not paint the marketing button with `#ff0055`.** That hex is `--custom-color-primary`. The measured buttons do not use it.
- **Keep headings in Inter Display at weight 500 with the negative tracking from `--type-title-web-*`.**
- **Keep the product menu on `#181616` with a 14px radius and the 12px 40px black shadow.** See [screenshots/home-menu-dark.png](screenshots/home-menu-dark.png).
