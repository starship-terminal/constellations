# Patterns

Public signed-out pages only. The signed-in app was not opened.

## Marketing shell

A fixed header, 64px, `bg-background/90` with backdrop blur, over page `#100e0e`. Wordmark left, text nav (Product, Made for, Customers, _Agents, Pricing, Resources), then Log in and a cream Sign up pill. At 390px the text nav collapses to a menu icon. Screenshots: [screenshots/home-dark.png](screenshots/home-dark.png), [screenshots/home-phone.png](screenshots/home-phone.png).

## Hero

Left column: Inter Display headline, muted 18px subcopy, cream primary and frosted secondary. Right column: a public product demo (library and a recording). That demo is not the signed-in app.

## Pricing

Two plans, Pro at $15 per seat/mo billed yearly, Enterprise custom. A yearly switch is on. FAQ rows sit below. Screenshot: [screenshots/pricing-dark.png](screenshots/pricing-dark.png). Checkout was not opened.

## Auth choice

Signup and login are three stacked actions: Google, Email, SSO. Google and SSO were not followed. Email was not opened, because the rule for this capture allows an email-only form and this step is not that form. No card, phone, or company SSO was entered.
