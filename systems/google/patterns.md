# Patterns

## Homepage

A white page. Header links on the left, Gmail, Images, the apps grid and a blue Sign in pill on the right. The wordmark sits above a centered search field. Two quiet buttons sit under the field. A promo line, a language line, then a grey footer with the country and the legal links.

Desktop: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone: [screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Search focus

Focusing the field opens trending searches in the same card. The buttons move to the bottom of that card. No query was typed and no result page was opened.

[screenshots/home-light-search-focused.png](screenshots/home-light-search-focused.png).

## Settings

Footer Settings opens a small menu. Search settings is a public page with a left rail and rows. Advanced search is an empty form. Neither was saved or submitted.

[screenshots/home-light-settings-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-settings-menu.png), [screenshots/preferences-light-default.png](screenshots/preferences-light-default.png), [screenshots/advanced-light-default.png](screenshots/advanced-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

Sign in is a centered card on white: wordmark, "Sign in", an empty email field, Create account, Next. The phone stacks the same card. Create account asks for an empty name. The branch stopped on both.
