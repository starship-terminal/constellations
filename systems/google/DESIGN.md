# Google

This file is a short export of [tokens.json](tokens.json). It was written by hand from measured tokens. It was not generated by `make design-md`, because this capture does not edit [catalog.json](../../catalog.json). Take values from tokens.json. This file has no per-value provenance.

## Overview

Public signed-out Google Search, captured 2026-11-22 from https://www.google.com/. One light theme. The homepage is a white page, a centered search field, quiet grey buttons, and one blue Sign in pill.

## Colors

- page: `#ffffff` (computed body background)
- text: `#16171a` (computed body color)
- sign-in: `#0b57d0` (computed Sign in fill)
- accounts next: `#1a73e8` (computed Next on the empty sign-in wall)
- link: `#1a0dab` (computed promo link)
- button face: `#f8f9fa` (computed Google Search button)
- field border: `#dadce0` (computed search field border)
- footer: `#ececed` (sampled country row, before quantize)
- logo yellow: `#fbbc04` (declared `--ywz01c`, not a control fill)

## Typography

Body: Arial, 14px, weight 400.

Search field: Arial, 16px, weight 400, line height 22px.

Sign in: Google Sans Text, Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, 14px, weight 500, line height 18px.

Sign-in heading: Arial, 24px, weight 400.

Google Sans is named by the page and is not shipped in this package.

## Layout

Search field height 52px, radius 26px, width 584px at 1440. Sign in height 40px, pill radius. Quiet buttons 36px, radius 8px, horizontal padding 16px. Identifier field on the sign-in wall is 56px tall.

Desktop captures are 1440 by 900. Phone captures were requested at 390 by 844 and saved at 780 by 1688 because devicePixelRatio was 2.

## Elevation and depth

Search field shadow: `rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.08) 0px 3px 10px 0px`. Focused field shadow: `rgba(64, 60, 67, 0.24) 0px 2px 8px 1px`. Quiet button shadow while hovered: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1) 0px 1px 1px 0px`.

## Shapes

Field 26px. Quiet button 8px. Sign in pill 100px. Accounts Next 4px.

## Components

Sign in is the only filled action on the homepage. Google Search and I'm Feeling Lucky are quiet. The apps grid and the Settings menu are overlays. The voice control opens a waiting dialog. Full notes are in [components.md](components.md).

## Do's and don'ts

- Do take values from [tokens.json](tokens.json).
- Do not read colours off the screenshots. They are 256-colour PNGs.
- Do not treat Gmail, Maps, YouTube or Drive as this package.
- Do not follow Sign in past the empty wall and call the result this package.
