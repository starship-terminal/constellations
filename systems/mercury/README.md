# Mercury design reference

Tokens and component notes for Mercury, the business bank at [mercury.com](https://mercury.com/). Captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. Public routes only: the marketing site, the sign-in boundary at `app.mercury.com/login`, and the public demo at `demo.mercury.com/dashboard`. Signed out the whole time. No email was typed. No account was opened. No password, passkey, KYC, payment, or dashboard action was submitted.

Mercury marketing is Next.js. Colour is a custom-property system on `:root` (`--color-neutral-base-*`, purple, beige, green, blue, orange, red) with semantic roles (`--background-*`, `--surface-*`, `--text-*`, `--border-*`) that theme classes restyle. The home hero paints `dark-neutral-theme`. Pages below the hero paint the light roles. Display is Arcadia Display. UI is Arcadia. The app login and the public demo paint Arcadia Text.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export mercury` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the hero on the dark neutral and the page below it on the light page.
2. Do set the hero in Arcadia Display at weight 480.
3. Do use the purple fill for Open account.
4. Do keep Log in as a ghost pill on the dark nav.
5. Do keep the login submit as a pill on the light card.
6. Don't invent a second brand colour for the primary button.
7. Don't treat the demo dashboard as a signed-in account.
8. Don't type into the email field or submit Log in.
9. Don't ship the Arcadia files.
10. Don't follow Open account into signup.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-19, desktop 1440×900 at 1x and phone 390×844 at 2x. Custom properties were resolved in the page on a probe div for `:root` and for each theme class (`neutral-theme`, `purple-theme`, `beige-theme`, `green-theme`, `blue-theme`, `orange-theme`, and the six `dark-*-theme` classes). Those readings are [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Component styles are [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). Hover on Open account is a real pointer. Focus on Log in is keyboard. The email field was focused and left empty. The opt-out checkbox was focused and not clicked. Motion is [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json), from the same probe on the home page, pricing, and the login boundary. The Transcend consent manager was present and empty. It was not answered.

## What to distrust

- The signed-in bank. `app.mercury.com/login` was photographed at rest and with the email field focused. Nothing was typed. Log in, Continue with passkey, Forgot password, and Open Account were not followed.
- Signup, KYC, payments, cards, and treasury. Open account on the marketing site points at `app.mercury.com/signup` and was not opened.
- The public demo. `demo.mercury.com/dashboard` is a published demo, labelled on the page as "Explore the Mercury Demo." It is not an account. Open account, Move money, and the task list were not followed. The name "Jane" and the balances are demo content.
- Support. `support.mercury.com` returned a Cloudflare challenge to the headless browser and was not captured.
- Pressed, disabled, error, loading, select, radio, switch, tooltip, dialog, toast, and table states. The public pages did not show them, and none were forced.
- The pricing annual switch. It is visible in [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png). It was not toggled, so the selected state is the one the page painted.
- Font files. Arcadia, Arcadia Display, Tiempos Headline, and IBM Plex Mono are named. The files are not in this package.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from the tokens, not from the PNGs.
- `--spacing` is 1px. The control scale steps by 4. Both are declared.
- [components.md](components.md) is written by hand from the measured crops and [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). `tools/build_components_md.py` only checks that every crop it links exists, so a recapture does not update its values: compare them with the raw readings after one.

## Fonts

Arcadia is the marketing UI, variable weight 360 to 500, from the site's own woff2. Arcadia Display is the hero, variable weight 320 to 480. Tiempos Headline is a 400 serif used on some marketing lines. IBM Plex Mono is the mono. The login and the public demo paint Arcadia Text. None of the files are shipped. The tokens name the families and fall back to Arial, Georgia, and ui-monospace.
