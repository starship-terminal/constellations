# Mercury patterns

Public pages only. The signed-in bank was not entered.

## Marketing shell

The nav is 72px, transparent over the dark hero, with the wordmark at the left and Log in plus Open account at the right. Below the hero the page is `light.page`. A fintech disclaimer sits in a dark pill at the bottom of the first screen.

![Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)

Phone collapses the nav into a menu button. The email field, Open account, and Launch demo stack.

![Phone home](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png)

## Pricing

Three plan cards, Mercury, Plus, and Pro, with an annual switch left as the page painted it. Open account and Start 30-day free trial were not clicked.

![Pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)

## Sign-in boundary

`app.mercury.com/login` is a light card: email, password, Log in, Continue with passkey. The email field was focused. Nothing was typed. Log in, passkey, Forgot password, and Open Account were not followed.

![Login](screenshots/login-light-default.png)

## Public demo

`demo.mercury.com/dashboard` paints a banking shell and labels itself "Explore the Mercury Demo." The balances and the name Jane are demo content. No money movement, settings, or account action was followed.

![Demo](screenshots/demo-light-default.png)

## Where it stops

Signup, KYC, payments, cards, treasury, and the signed-in dashboard sit behind Open account or Log in. Support sits behind a Cloudflare challenge. None of those were captured.
