# Square patterns

Public marketing site, captured 2026-11-21. The signed-in app was not entered.

## App shell

The desktop header is a 72px bar: logo, Business types, Products, Hardware, Pricing, What's new, then Sign up, Log in, Support, and the cart. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

On a 390px phone the same links collapse behind Open menu. See [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Mega-menu

Business types and Products open a white panel. The left column is category buttons. The right column is the links for the selected category. See [home-light-products-menu](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png) and [home-light-products-payments](screenshots/home-light-products-payments.png) and [home-light-business-menu](screenshots/home-light-business-menu.png).

What's new is a short menu: Release notes and Feature log. See [home-light-whats-new](screenshots/home-light-whats-new.png).

## Cart

The cart button opens a popover. Empty state: "No items in your cart", a blue Shop hardware button, View cart, Order history. See [home-light-cart](screenshots/home-light-cart.png). The cart page itself is [cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png). Order history redirects to login. That branch stopped.

## Pricing

Three plan cards on the homepage and a full pricing page. Square Free is $0, Square Plus is $49, Square Premium is $149, each per location. Get started and Try free for 30 days go to signup. Those branches stopped. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [mobile-pricing-light-default](screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png).
