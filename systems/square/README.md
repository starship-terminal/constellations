## How the values were read

A T3 preview tab opened `https://squareup.com/` with `reuseExistingTab` false. The page landed on `https://squareup.com/us/en` and was not a Cloudflare challenge. Computed styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on that tab, then on `/us/en/pricing`, `https://app.squareup.com/login?lang_code=en-us`, and `https://app.squareup.com/signup/en-US`.

Marketing buttons paint their face on a `::before` of `.Action__content-wrapper`, not on the anchor background. Login fields are `market-input-text` custom elements. The rest border and the focused border were read from the host `::after` inset shadow, and the token names were read from computed custom properties on that host. Focus was applied with `element.focus()` and left empty.

`preview_snapshot` was called twice. Both calls returned: "The browser is not producing screenshot frames. For a desktop tab, show or restore the T3 Code window and try again." Measurements already read were kept. No screenshot was invented. Headless Playwright was not launched.

## What to distrust

Headline sizes are what the browser computed at innerWidth 1169. The marketing h1 line-height computed to 0px, so the display token uses 64px for line-height and says so. Feature heading tracking -2.24px is the computed value at this width.

`--accessibility-focus-outline-color` computed to `rgba(0, 106, 255, .8)`. The token stores `#006AFF` and drops the alpha. Login focus that was actually painted is the 2px inset `#101010`, not that blue ring.

Dark custom properties resolved on `:root` (`--color-background-color-mode-dark` `#000000`, muted `#999999`). `color-scheme` was `normal`. No dark surface was painted, so there is no dark set.

Declared error and success colours were not painted on a control. They are tokens because the computed custom property held them, and the README says they were not shown.

No screenshot backs these numbers. A later capture with frames should replace the empty screenshots folder.

## Fonts

Square Sans Text VF, Square Sans Display VF, Cash Sans, and Exact Block are Square's faces. The tokens name them and fall back to Helvetica, Arial, or Georgia. The files are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the measured blue and the login ink.** Marketing actions are `light.color.accent` on `light.background.page`. Login chrome is `light.text.login` on `light.background.canvas`. Readings are in [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Use the blue pill for a marketing primary action.** 44px tall, radius `core.radius.pill`, label weight 500. The login Continue button is black, `light.text.login`, radius `core.radius.login-pill`, 48px tall.
- **Keep the login field empty in any reconstruction of this capture.** The email or phone control was measured blank. No address was entered.
- **Stop at the signup wall.** Create your free account asks for email, password, and an agreement. Do not invent a filled form.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** These pages painted light only. Dark custom properties were declared and not used.
- **Do not treat the 1169px headline sizes as a desktop token for every width.** They are the computed size at this viewport.
