# Patterns

Signed-out pages photographed on 2026-11-22. Viewport 1440 by 900, `devicePixelRatio` 2.

## Header

The header is two rows on white. Search is a grey pill at the left. The wordmark is centered. Sell an item is a black button. Sign in and Sign up are text. The bag is an icon. The second row is a single line of 15px links. Sale is the only red label. A hairline separates the header from the page.

Women opens a full-width white menu. Column headings sit above a vertical list of category links. The menu was opened and left.

## Homepage

A red offer bar sits under the header, with a serif headline and a short line that names a code. Under that, a full-bleed photograph carries a white serif title and an outlined pill, Shop markdowns. Fresh Finds is a row of five square photographs with a label under each. Further down, product rails name a brand, a title, a price, and a country.

The offer amount and the prices change. This pass read them in SGD.

## Category

Women opens as a catalog. A grey breadcrumb sits above an Albra title, "Women", and a count. A row of square chips follows: Sort By, Black Friday, Category, Designers, Condition, Sizes, Colors, Materials, Shipped From, then All filters. The grid is five columns. A card shows the photograph, the brand in small caps, the title, the price, and the shipping country. A sale price is red under a grey price. A black badge marks Black Friday.

## About

About us is a journal page. A photograph of bags sits above a centered serif title, "Vestiaire Collective: Long Live Fashion". The body is 16px, centered, in short paragraphs. Press boilerplate is a dark button under the copy. It was not clicked.

## Where it stops

Sign in, Sign up, Sell an item, the bag, Add to Bag, and Make an Offer were not opened. A product page prints a seller username, so it was measured and not saved as a screenshot.
