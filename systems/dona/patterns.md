# Patterns

## Marketing shell

Every dona.ai page, including 404, uses the same header: About on the left, wordmark in the center, Login on the right. At 390 those three controls stay in one row. There is no drawer.

The footer is a copyright line, Terms of Service, Privacy policy, and a Twitter icon.

See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Hero

A 64px Inter headline, a short lede, then two pills: quiet Watch video and blue Try for free. Below that, a recorded product demo, not a live list.

## Video modal

Watch video opens a centered 16:9 panel over a grey-blue scrim. The page URL does not change. Close is a round button at the top right. Escape closed it in the desktop capture.

See [home-light-video](screenshots/home-light-video.png).

## Newsletter

An email field and an ink submit sit above the footer. Submitting was not done. The success pane (thank-you copy, Twitter, Discord) stays covered until a successful POST.

## Login wall

Login and Try for free open app.dona.ai in a new tab. The root path redirects to /login. The card is a white form on a pale wash: title, short line, fields, black submit, blue text links. Register and forgot-password use the same card.

See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## 404

Large muted "404", ink "Page not found", quiet Back to Home pill, then the shared footer.

See [notfound-light-default](screenshots/notfound-light-default.png).
