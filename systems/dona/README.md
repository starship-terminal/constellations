# Dona design reference

A token set and component reference for Dona's public surface — for building quiet, friendly marketing pages in the style of the real thing. It was read from **dona.ai** on **2026-11-12**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844: the home page (with its Watch video modal and the cookie-consent card), About, the privacy policy and the terms of service, at desktop and phone widths.

Dona ("a more humane to-do list") is worth a capture because its marketing surface is a tight study in friendly restraint: ink `#19191b` on white, one `#008ffd` blue that does all the accent work — the Try for free CTA, the elliptical swooshes behind feature tiles and the newsletter field, the follow button — a ladder of greys (`#eceff2` pills, `#f4f4f5`/`#eff2f5` tiles, `#e0e3e7` chips), 32px pill buttons everywhere, 20px cards, and one typeface — Inter — running from an 84px display headline down to 14px legal. **Boundary:** the product itself, with the light/dark/black UI themes the copy advertises, lives on app.dona.ai behind sign-in; Login and Try for free open that host and were never followed. Everything behind the login is a recorded gap, never inferred.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export dona` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do put ink on white.
2. Do pill every action at 32px.
3. Do let one blue carry the accent.
4. Do use the swoosh motif.
5. Do keep secondary actions quiet.
6. Don't invent borders.
7. Don't ship a dark theme.
8. Don't style the app from this package.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body (the linked `/styles.*.css` bundle from the network response plus the inline `<style data-styled>` blocks re-parsed with `CSSStyleSheet`) and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). dona.ai declares almost no design vars — only component-scoped `--button-color`, `--icon-color`/`--icon-size` and `--blur-opacity` on styled-components classes, plus the Inter `@font-face` faces — so **every design token is a measured computed value**, cross-checked against the declared var values where they exist. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state, plus `focused`/`filled` on the email field and the opened video modal. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars; [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, created, submitted or consented to: the email field was filled with sample text and cleared, never submitted; the Watch video button's own modal was opened, captured and hidden again; Login and Try for free (app.dona.ai) were never followed; the cookie banner was captured as its own state, then hidden — Accept was never clicked.

## What to distrust

- **This is the marketing shell, not the app.** The to-do product — lists, sync, accounts, and the light/dark/black UI themes the copy and video advertise — lives on app.dona.ai behind sign-in and is uncaptured. Treat the app's own design system as unknown.
- **One theme only.** The site paints light everywhere and offers no switcher; the themes in the copy are in-app features. `tokens.json` ships no dark set.
- **The tokens are measured, not declared.** dona.ai compiles styled-components to class rules; only a few component-scoped vars exist. Every token cites the measurement it came from, except three: `core.layout.container` 1140px, `core.layout.gutter` 20px and `core.layout.grid-gap` 32px came from capture probes whose readings were never written to raw/, so their source says Reconstruction (entered by hand).
- **Two radii are percentages,** which DTCG dimensions cannot hold: the 75% swoosh and the 50% round close button are listed under `core.$extensions.notTokenised` with their readings, not as `core.radius` tokens.
- **UA defaults are labelled as such.** The `#0000ee`/`#ff0000` on the unstyled logo/social anchors and the `#005fcc` focus ring are browser defaults, tokenised under `core.measured` with that caveat, not Dona design decisions.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **Three readings are recorded as misses, not values.** `legal-link`'s catch-all selector landed on the footer's "Privacy policy" link, so its saved states were dropped — no in-copy legal link is verified. `modal-close`'s focus-visible reading caught the hover fill (the pointer stayed over the button) and is marked contaminated. The mobile cookie banner's marked node read offscreen (555x64 at `top:1138px` in a 390x844 viewport; it never painted on the phone pass), so its real placement is unverified; `core.shadow.banner-mobile` is read from it and says so (recapture needed). The capture script now records the banner missing when the marked node lies outside the viewport (not re-run).
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Fonts

**Inter** is the single served face for everything — the 84px display headline, the 40px email field, the 14px legal small — at weights 400/500/600/700. dona.ai serves it as inline base64 woff2 plus `/static/inter-*-{weight}-normal-*.woff2` subset files (the `@font-face` harvest is in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json)); none is distributed with the package — the style guide falls back to system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do put ink on white.** `#19191b` on a white field is the whole base; only the section paragraph drops to `#616870`. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do pill every action at 32px.** From the 36px nav buttons to the 52px CTAs and Join — every button is the same radius. See [try-cta](components/crops/try-cta-default.png).
3. **Do let one blue carry the accent.** `#008ffd` is the CTA fill, the swoosh colour and the follow button; hover is one step darker at `#0087fd`. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do use the swoosh motif.** The 75%-radius blue ellipses behind tiles and the email field are the site's signature decoration — sparing and deliberate. See [feature-tile](components/crops/feature-tile-default.png).
5. **Do keep secondary actions quiet.** Grey `#eceff2` pills at one hover step (`#e9ecef`); the ghost About button paints nothing until its `#f2f4f8` hover wash. See [nav-about](components/crops/nav-about-hover.png).
6. **Don't invent borders.** No measured control draws a border; depth comes from the low-alpha `#0c1442` shadows and the inset bevels. See [cookie-accept](components/crops/cookie-accept-default.png).
7. **Don't ship a dark theme.** The surface is light-only; light/dark/black are in-app themes shown in marketing video, not site tokens.
8. **Don't style the app from this package.** Every value is the marketing surface's; app.dona.ai is uncaptured.

## Rebuild

Everything derived regenerates offline from the raw readings (run from `systems/dona/`):

```sh
python3 tools/build_component_tokens.py        # raw/components-computed.json -> raw/component-tokens.json
python3 tools/build_tokens.py                  # -> tokens.json
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py             # -> style-guide.html
python3 tools/build_components_md.py           # -> components.md
node tools/capture/public/build-measurements.mjs  # -> raw/measurements.md
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-dir>    # full-colour PNGs -> screenshots/
```

Then `make brief && make design-md && make grade && make check` from the repo root refreshes the generated sections and validates the catalog.

Live recapture (needs the site; Playwright at `PLAYWRIGHT=~/.design-systems/pw/node_modules/playwright/index.mjs`):

```sh
node tools/capture/vars.mjs                    # stylesheet harvest -> raw/css-vars.json
node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs          # computed styles + crops -> raw/components-computed.json
RAW=<scratch> node tools/capture/public/public.mjs   # screenshots -> <scratch>
```

dona.ai loads fine headless from this client; the product host app.dona.ai stays behind sign-in.
