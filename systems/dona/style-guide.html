Palette

Ink #19191b on white is the base; the blue #008ffd is the only brand colour on the surface (with its #0087fd hover step and the #6fabff glow), and a ladder of greys — #eceff2 pills, #f4f4f5/#eff2f5 tiles, #e0e3e7 chips — carries every secondary control.

Spacing and radius

Buttons are 32px pills, cards 20px, the banner 40px, chips 14px and the close a 50% circle; the swoosh decorations are 75%-radius ellipses. The container is 1140px with 20px gutters and a 32px grid gap.
