# Dona

Public marketing site, signed out, captured 2026-11-21. One painted theme.

## Colors

Page is white. The wash behind the hero is `#f2f4f6`. Text is `#19191b`. The muted half of the display line is `#a8b0b8`. The primary pill is `#008ffd`. Quiet pills are `#eceff2`.

## Typography

Inter throughout. Body is 16px at weight 400. Buttons are 14px in the header and 16px in the hero, weight 500. Display is 64px at weight 700.

## Layout

Content column on the home newsletter field is 1100px. Header buttons are 36px tall. Hero buttons are 52px tall. Pill radius is 32px.

## Components

Primary actions are blue pills. Secondary actions are grey pills. The video control opens a modal. The app itself is behind a login wall.

## Do's and Don'ts

Use one blue pill for the primary action. Keep secondary actions grey. Do not style the to-do app from the marketing demos. Do not invent a dark marketing theme.
