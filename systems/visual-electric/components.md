# Components

Crops are cut from the full-colour captures, then quantized. Colour values are the computed styles in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

## Link

Pricing nav links are 18px Maison Neue Book, `#0F0F0F`, with a 200ms colour transition. The declared hover colour is `#083547` at 50%. It was not painted.

What's New body links sit in the 15px muted paragraph. A separate link colour was not measured.

## Gaps

Text area, select, checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, menu, tooltip, dialog, toast, table, avatar, and badge were not on these signed-out pages. The plan cards have no border. Focus, hover, pressed, disabled, loading, and error were not forced. The sign-up form was not opened.
