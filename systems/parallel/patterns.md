# Patterns

How the public pages combine. Each one links a screenshot.

## Talent home

A photo hero, a white search pill, four filter chips, and a count of live jobs. The header floats over the photo: logo, Explore, Talent and Employers tabs, Sign in, Sign up. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone stacks the same hero and puts navigation in Open menu. See [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu-open](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png).

## Employer marketing

A dark page, a short search for "who you are hiring", feature chips, and a FAQ. See [employers-light-default](screenshots/employers-light-default.png) and [employers-light-faq-open](screenshots/employers-light-faq-open.png). Platform, Recruiting, and Studio are the same shell with different copy. See [platform-light-default](screenshots/platform-light-default.png).

## Job search

A left icon rail, a search field, filter chips, and a grid of job cards. Signed out, the rail links (Matches, Work, Messages, Profile) lead to a wall. See [search-light-default](screenshots/search-light-default.png). A job page is the same rail plus a detail column. See [job-light-default](screenshots/job-light-default.png).

## Blog and legal

Blog is a category row and a list of posts. See [blog-light-default](screenshots/blog-light-default.png). Terms and privacy are long text pages with the marketing footer.

## Walls

Login and signup are centered cards on a pale grid. They are the end of those branches. See [login-wall-light-default](screenshots/login-wall-light-default.png).

## Empty, loading, error, destructive confirm, onboarding

Not present on the public pages that loaded. Onboarding and account creation stop at the LinkedIn or email wall.
