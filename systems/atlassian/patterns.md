# Patterns

The public site is a light marketing shell: white page, blue wordmark, one blue pill, Sign in as text. Product pages reuse that shell with a large headline. The nav opens as a panel of cards, not as a new page. Purchasing and licensing is an FAQ, not a checkout.

The account walls leave that shell. Sign in is a centred card with an empty email field. Signup is a modal over a grey product demo, with an empty work-email field and a placeholder.
