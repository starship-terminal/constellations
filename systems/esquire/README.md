# Esquire design reference

A token set and component reference for Esquire's public, signed-out magazine surface — for building editorial home pages, section fronts, article layouts, plan/offer pages and search results in the style of the real thing. It was read from **esquire.com** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. The public control map was walked again on **2026-11-21** and written to [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). The walls where that pass stopped are [login-review.json](login-review.json). Nothing was submitted. Captured: the home page, the Style / Entertainment / News & Politics / Food & Drinks section fronts, a long cover interview and a shorter essay, the /offers/premium-membership plan page, the /esq-premium-exclusive/ hub, the search results page and the About page, plus 390px passes on home, the article and the Style front.

Esquire is worth a capture because it ships its real design system to the browser: the site is Hearst's **FRE** design-token app, and its `/api/styles.css` bundle declares the token layer outright — ~4,300 custom properties: the `--_palette-*` colour ramps (gray, blue, green, orange, red, yellow), `--_size-*` and `--_font-*` primitives (size, line-height, weight, family stacks), the `--color-*` semantic layer (button/nav/meta/eyebrow/form/offer/list/accordion roles in default/hover/active states), the `--type-*` type styles per breakpoint, the `--dim-*` space/layout scale and a large `--comp-*` component layer. The public surface paints **one light theme**: a white editorial field, `#121212` ink, `#3b3b3b` muted meta, the `#ff3a30` brand red on the 32px utility strip, the Subscribe pill and every Join/Sign-Up action, `#f5f6f8` panels behind newsletter and meta blocks. Five served voices: Lausanne for all UI (nav, headings, cards, buttons), PlayFair for serif display (cover and section headlines to 64px), Lora for article body (18px), Charter and Inter in the stacks; "SF Pro" is the declared mac default and paints the offers headings on this box. **Boundary:** everything behind Sign In (account.esquire.com) and checkout (shop.esquire.com) was never reached — the Sign In/Subscribe/Join actions were measured, never followed; the search and newsletter fields were typed into and cleared, never submitted; the OneTrust consent layer was hidden, never answered; third-party ad and tracker requests were aborted, so ad slots read as blank containers under the site's own "Advertisement - Continue Reading Below" furniture; premium stories render their public lede only.

### Principles

1. Do let FRE carry the design.
2. Do spend the red on actions.
3. Do keep the one theme honest.
4. Do pair the voices.
5. Do use the pill.
6. Do read eyebrow–headline–dek–byline as one unit.
7. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
8. Don't fabricate a paywall state.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let FRE carry the design.** The product ships its token layer: use the `palette.<hue>.<step>` ramps, the `light.button.*` and `light.text.*` roles and the `core.type.*` styles rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [story-thumb](components/crops/story-thumb-default.png).
2. **Do spend the red on actions.** `#ff3a30` fills the utility strip, the Subscribe pill and every Join/Sign-Up button; editorial surfaces stay white with #121212 ink. See [nav-subscribe](components/crops/nav-subscribe-default.png) and [plan-join](components/crops/plan-join-default.png).
3. **Do keep the one theme honest.** Every public surface paints light; there is no dark toggle or dark route. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
4. **Do pair the voices.** Lausanne owns UI — nav, headings, cards, buttons, labels; PlayFair owns serif display headlines; Lora owns article body. See [hero-story](components/crops/hero-story-default.png) and [art-para](components/crops/art-para-default.png).
5. **Do use the pill.** Buttons are full-radius pills (1024px) — Subscribe, Join, Sign Up fill red; See More and the gift CTA paint the same pill as a red outline on white. See [see-more](components/crops/search-more-default.png) and [gift-cta](components/crops/gift-cta-default.png).
6. **Do read eyebrow–headline–dek–byline as one unit.** Cards compose the same four parts at every size: a small uppercase eyebrow, the headline, a muted dek, BY-line + read-time. See [story-card](components/crops/story-card-default.png).
7. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Bookmarks, the archive and account flows live behind account.esquire.com, which was never opened; the plan page and premium hub at rest are the evidence. See [nav-signin](components/crops/nav-signin-default.png).
8. **Don't fabricate a paywall state.** Premium stories gate below the public lede; what the signed-out page paints was captured and the gated remainder is a recorded boundary, not a measured state. See [prem-card](components/crops/prem-card-default.png).

## Known gaps

- `plan-price` (Plan price): the reading is the struck-through list price "$75" (a `line-through` span), not the price the plan charges; the selector took the first short "$" leaf on the card. Needs a recapture; `measure.mjs` now skips struck-through elements (edited, not run). No token cites it.
- Two committed screenshot sidecars record `https://www.esquire.com/search/?q=suits`, the search the capture ran; no credential or personal data. `public.mjs` now records page URLs without their query (edited, not run); the sidecars are left as captured.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.
