Palette

The core.palette groups are FRE's declared --_palette-* ramps (gray, blue, green, orange, red, yellow); the light set carries the --color-* semantic roles — brand red, text tiers, backgrounds, borders, nav, button states, links, badges, chips, meta, offers and list states. The site paints one theme; the dark set is empty.

Spacing, radius and borders

core.space is the 4px-based --dim-space-* scale and core.size the finer --_size-* primitives; core.radius holds the 1024px pill every button paints plus the 4px field/row radius; core.borderWidth the hairline steps. Motion is one 150ms duration over two easing curves.

Elevation

FRE composes each level from declared parts — offsets, blur, spread and colour — exported here as complete box-shadow layers. The stacking ladder sits under core.layout.z.

Type

Three voices: Lausanne for all UI (nav, headings, cards, buttons), PlayFair for serif display (cover and section headlines to 64px), Lora for article body (18px); "SF Pro" is the declared mac default on the offers headings.
