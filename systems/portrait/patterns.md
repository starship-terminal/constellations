# Portrait patterns

Public marketing pages, the empty Sign in dialog, and the empty editor wall. Desktop 1440 by 900. Phone 390 by 844 for home and Wallet. Captured 2026-11-21, signed out.

## Marketing shell

The homepage, `/pages`, `/wallet`, `/pay`, `/blog`, `/points`, and `/hosting` share one shell. Body background `#f7f7f7`, text `#353535`, Switzer 16px / 24px. The header is a sticky white bar with the wordmark, a New / Introducing Portrait Wallet chip, Log in, and Sign up. Sign up wears the rainbow ring.

Headings use Basier Circle at weight 500 in navy `#08304c`. The homepage h1 is 76.29px at 1440. Sections stack a heading, a short lede, and a dark pill.

The footer is a link list: Product, Support, Resources, Developers, Hosting, Socials, then System status, Privacy, and Terms. It is in [screenshots/home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png). A viewport shot of the footer still shows the sticky header over the last FAQ rows.

Plus is a section on the marketing pages, not a `/pricing` URL. The FAQ answer says creating a Portrait is free, and Plus is $10/month. See [screenshots/pages-light-plus.png](screenshots/pages-light-plus.png) and [screenshots/pages-light-faq.png](screenshots/pages-light-faq.png).
