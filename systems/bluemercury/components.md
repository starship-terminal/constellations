# Components

Controls measured on the public shop on 2026-11-22. States that were not on screen are marked not captured.

## Promo bar

35px, full width, fill `#2f394b`. Promo line 12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.7px, white. Sign in, BlueRewards, and Locations share the strip at 14px, white. [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png)

## Wordmark

Inline SVG, viewBox `0 0 177 18`, fill `#2F394B`, rendered 236 by 24. [icons/mark.svg](icons/mark.svg)

## Search

Underline field, 450 by 48, radius 0, bottom border 1px `#6d7275`, type 16px ink, placeholder "What can we help you find?". Not typed into. Hover and focus not captured.

## Department link

16px Neue Haas, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.8px, ink `#1c262e`. Gift Guide is `#0b51c1`. Hover not captured. Mega menus were in the DOM and not opened.

## Shop Now

White rectangle, 300 by 63, padding 20px 40px, radius 0, label 15px uppercase, letter-spacing 0.75px, ink. Not clicked.

## Category chip

36px tall, radius 0, 14px uppercase, letter-spacing 0.7px. Selected: fill `#2f394b`, type white, 1px border of the same colour. Rest: fill `#f5f5f5`, type `#2f394b`. Not clicked. [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png)

## Product card

Product photo, brand name in small caps, then the product name. Brand colour on the product page is `#6d7275`. The grid itself was photographed. Card hover was not captured.

## Price

`$185` on C E Ferulic, 20px Neue Haas, weight 400, letter-spacing 0.2px, ink. Same size as the product title.

## Add to Bag

Rectangle, 471 by 60, fill `#2f394b`, type white, 15px uppercase, letter-spacing 0.75px, radius 0. Not clicked. [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png)

## Quantity

A minus, the number 1, and a plus sit to the left of Add to Bag. Not used.

## Bestseller label

Small uppercase label above the brand name on the product page. Not a separate colour token. The reading for the wrapper was transparent, so the chip fill is not recorded.

## Not on the pages opened

Select, checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, tooltip, toast, table, avatar. The BlueRewards dialog was closed and not measured as a component.
