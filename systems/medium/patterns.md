# Patterns

## Marketing home

A white page in light, `#171717` in dark. The wordmark sits at x=124, y=26 at 1440, and at x=24 at 390. Nav links are 14px sohne. Get started is the right-hand pill. The hero is two lines of gt-super, then a 22px sohne line, then Start reading. The gap from the hero block to the subtitle is 48px, and from the subtitle to the button is 48px. The footer is one row of 16px links at y=856.

At 390 the nav links drop. Get started stays in the header. The hero drops to 80px and the inset is 24px.

See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-dark-phone](screenshots/home-dark-phone.png).

## Membership and about

Membership keeps the white page. The hero is 85px gt-super. The supporting line is 22px sohne in `#6b6b6b`. Two pills sit under it: filled Get started and outlined View plans. Story titles in the band are 32px gt-super.

About paints a dark hero band. The 85px title computed to `#ffffff` while the page background computed to `#ffffff`, so the band behind the title is darker than the page colour. Sign up on that band is a white pill.

## Sign-in card

`/m/signin` is a centered column on a white page. "Welcome back." is 28px gt-super. Six provider rows stack at 300 x 42 with 16px between them (222, 280, 338, 396, 454, 512). "Sign in with email" replaces that list with a label, a 4px grey field, Remember me, and Continue. Nothing in that column was submitted.

## Story

The blog home uses sohne for titles (32px for the publication, 20px weight 500 for cards). A story page sets the title in sohne 42px weight 700 and the body in source-serif-pro inside a 680px column. The publication name sits above the title.

## Plans

A welcome badge in `#ffc017`, an 8px radius, sits above a 48px gt-super title. Billing is a two-part control, 4px radius, 72px tall. Plan names are sohne 20px weight 500. Prices are sohne 16px (`$5/month`, `$15/month`). Select is the primary pill and was not pressed. At 390 dark the title is 32px white and Select is a white pill, 276px wide.

## Help center

help.medium.com is a Zendesk page with its own oklch tokens, not the medium.com `--color-*` set. The h1 is GT Super Text at 60px. Search sits in a 20px radius field. It was not submitted.
