# Shopify patterns

Public site only. The signed-in admin is a wall.

## App shell

The marketing shell is a 72px transparent header over a black hero, then a black footer with four columns. [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png). Phone collapses the nav into a drawer. [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Navigation

Why Shopify and Products are hover menus on desktop and drill-in rows on the phone. [home-light-why-menu](screenshots/home-light-why-menu.png), [home-light-products-menu](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png). The map of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Pricing

Plan cards, a yearly switch, and an FAQ accordion. [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [pricing-light-yearly](screenshots/pricing-light-yearly.png), [pricing-light-faq-open](screenshots/pricing-light-faq-open.png). Phone: [mobile-pricing-light-default](screenshots/mobile-pricing-light-default.png).

## Search

Help Center has a search field and topic lists. [help-light-default](screenshots/help-light-default.png). Theme store and app store are grids with their own search. [themes-light-default](screenshots/themes-light-default.png), [apps-light-default](screenshots/apps-light-default.png). No query was submitted.

## Dialog

Region picker over the footer. [home-light-region-dialog](screenshots/home-light-region-dialog.png). Testimonial video. [home-light-video-dialog](screenshots/home-light-video-dialog.png).

## Content pages

Product stories share the same header and a long black-and-white scroll: [start](screenshots/start-light-default.png), [sell](screenshots/sell-light-default.png), [enterprise](screenshots/enterprise-light-default.png), [checkout](screenshots/checkout-light-default.png), [sidekick](screenshots/sidekick-light-default.png), [editions](screenshots/editions-light-default.png), [blog](screenshots/blog-light-default.png), [legal](screenshots/legal-light-default.png).

## Not captured

Form validation, empty, loading, and error states of the admin, destructive confirmation, toast feedback, and onboarding. Those live behind `https://accounts.shopify.com/lookup` or `https://admin.shopify.com/signup`. No form was submitted.
