# iA Writer patterns

Public marketing pages on ia.net, signed out, 2026-11-21. Light only. Desktop 1440, phone 390.

## App shell

There is no signed-in shell. The page is a header, a long column, and a footer. The header is a wordmark on the left and Support, Buy, Download, Menu on the right. On a phone the right side is Menu only, and the primary action becomes an App Store pill.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png)

## Menu

The Menu summary opens a white card over the page. Writer is expanded. Other products, company pages, and locales sit in the same card. Opening it does not change the URL.

[home-light-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-menu.png), [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)

## Current section

The header link for the current page turns blue. Buy is blue on pricing. Download is blue on the download page. The other links stay black.

[pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [download-light-default.png](screenshots/download-light-default.png)

## One accent

The blue pill is the one loud control. Secondary downloads on the download page are black pills. Platform rows that are off are grey.

[download-light-default.png](screenshots/download-light-default.png)

## Quote expand

A grey "More" control expands a review in place. The URL stays `/writer`.

[home-light-quote.png](screenshots/home-light-quote.png)

## Support index

A left section list, a search field, and a grid of article cards. Search was not typed into. Article pages were not opened one by one.

[support-light-default.png](screenshots/support-light-default.png)
