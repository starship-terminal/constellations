# Pop Site patterns

Public pages only. The signed-in editor was not reached.

## Marketing shell

The header is a row: the black wordmark, then Pricing, Blog, Create Account and Log In. At 390 px the same links stay in the bar. There is no hamburger and no menu. Create Account and Log In leave the marketing host for https://app.pop.site/ and https://app.pop.site/?signin. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Claim band

The repeated call to action is one row: a username field with placeholder `yourname`, a `.pop.site` suffix chip, and a gradient Claim button. The field was not typed into. Pressing Claim with an empty field opens the signup wall, so that press is recorded in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and was not repeated for a screenshot. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## FAQ accordion

Six questions sit closed. Opening one expands its answer on the same page and does not navigate. See [home-light-faq.png](screenshots/home-light-faq.png).

## Article list

The blog is a stack of article cards. Each card is a link to `/articles/…`. Three articles embed a YouTube poster. Play swaps in a youtube.com iframe and was not kept as a Pop state. See [blog-light-default.png](screenshots/blog-light-default.png).

## Footer

Four columns: Use, Support, Legal, More. Support, X and Instagram leave the host. Featured, Press & Media, Terms, Privacy and My Information stay on pop.site. GDPR & CPRA is the same page as Privacy.

## Privacy request

My Information is a form: name, email, four checkboxes, a message, Submit. It was not filled and not submitted. See [myinformation-light-default.png](screenshots/myinformation-light-default.png).
