# New Balance

A signed-out capture of the public New Balance US shop at [https://www.newbalance.com](https://www.newbalance.com) on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The page is white, UI type is Proxima Nova W01 at weight 300, the hero is ITC Garamond W01 at 60px, and the wordmark is `#cf0a2c`. Primary actions are 54px buttons filled `#151415` with a 2px radius.

`catalog.json` was left alone, so this file is handwritten. Values come from [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json). The UK store is a different package.

## Type

Proxima Nova W01. Body and nav 14px at weight 300. Product title 32px at weight 500. Hero 60px ITC Garamond W01 at weight 300. Font files are not in this package.

## Controls

Primary buttons are 54px tall with a 2px radius. Fields are 58px with a 4px radius. Size chips are 43px with a 1px `#bec0c3` border. The utility bar is 57px. The department bar is 81px.
