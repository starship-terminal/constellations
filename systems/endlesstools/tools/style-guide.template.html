Endless Tools, measured

endlesstools.io is a Next.js + Tailwind v4 marketing one-pager for a canvas editor, and app.endlesstools.io is the app host whose /login card and /legal/* documents paint signed out. Both paint a single dark theme over the same grey ramp: #000 marketing field under #fff ink with grey-4 #959595 muted text, and the app shell's #0a0a0a field under #efefef ink. The marketing sheet declares its @theme layer — --color-*, the --text-* ramp from 42/46 title-primary to 12/14 body-secondary, --radius-base/fill — but paints the accents as arbitrary values: the pink CTA #ff13b1 (hover #ff4bc4), the #ff3dae save pill and the blue education #2c5cff (hover #5a65ff), all measured off the live components. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The neutral ramp is the declared --color-* layer plus the painted card greys (#121212, #1d1d1d, #080808, #222, #343434) and the oklab translucencies Tailwind v4 computes from white and black. The accents are arbitrary-value utilities, never declared: pink #ff13b1/#ff4bc4/#ff3dae and blue #2c5cff/#5a65ff.

Spacing and radius

Controls are fully rounded pills at 35-38px tall (10-20px inline padding); surfaces step through 10px app radius, 12px fill, 16px thumbnails, 20px login card and 33px plan cards. Padding runs 15px cards, 20px pills, 50px login card, 150px footer.

Type

One family: Inter variable (100-900) with next/font's Arial-metric fallback, subset woff2 on both hosts.
