# Patterns

Public signed-out pages only. No screenshots: the preview browser did not produce frames.

## Marketing home

A 568px column on a white page. A 56px sticky header holds Scroll, Jobs, Launchpad, Log in, and Create Profile. The headline is Instrument at 48px. A username field sits under it. Sections repeat a 20px/600 Geist title. The footer links Blog, Help, Support, Legal, and ads.

## Auth

Login and signup share a 380px card with 16px radius. Google is the first action. Email and password follow. Signup asks for an email and a password of at least 8 characters, then legal links. No phone field was on the page. The form was not submitted.

## App shell

Jobs, Scroll, Launchpad, and Search share a signed-out shell: the same header, a 16px/600 page title, and a 640px content column at 1440. Jobs is a list of 72px rows. Scroll is a post feed. Launchpad is a ranked project list with week chips. Search has People, Projects, Jobs, and Posts filters and a checkbox.

## Gaps

- Phone width was not captured. The viewport resize stopped at 1280 on the first attempt, then 1440 after navigation. No 390 pass ran after screenshot frames failed.
- Dark theme is declared on `.dark` and was not painted.
- Signed-in feed, profile editor, job apply, and payments were not entered.
