### Principles

1. Use the green only for the primary action.
2. Keep the page white and the type in Geist.
3. Round controls at 12px and cards at 16px.
4. Do not invent a dark screenshot.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the page stylesheets and resolved on the light root. Component values were `getComputedStyle` on the live controls. The green `rgb(0 170 69)`, the gray scale, Geist, and Instrument come from those reads. The dark gray scale was copied from the `.dark` rule and was not painted.

## What to distrust

There are no screenshots. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Do not treat this package as visual evidence of layout. The dark semantic set is declared, not observed. Toastify colours in the stylesheet were left out because no toast was on screen. Body text computed to `rgb(0, 0, 0)` while `text-gray-1k` is `rgb(13, 13, 13)`. Both are recorded.

## Fonts

Geist is the UI face. Instrument is the display face on the home and auth headlines. Geist Mono is declared and was not used on a measured control. Roboto is the Google button face. The tokens fall back to system faces because the webfont files were not saved.

## Do and don't

- **Use the green only for the primary action.** Create Profile is `rgb(0 170 69)` with a `rgb(30 135 75)` border.
- **Keep the page white and the type in Geist.** Display lines use Instrument at 40px or 48px.
- **Round controls at 12px and cards at 16px.** Nav links use 24px. Pills and avatars are full circles.
- **Do not invent a dark screenshot.** The dark tokens are stylesheet declarations.
