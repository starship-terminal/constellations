# Loom design reference

Tokens and component notes for Loom (loom.com), captured **2026-11-18**, public site tree refreshed **2026-11-21**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. The painted theme is light. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing`, `/enterprise`, `/ai`, `/security`, `/screenshot`, `/sdk`, `/download`, `/use-case`, `/desktop`, `/customers`, `/legal`, the 404, `/login` and `/signup` (empty walls), and one public share page. Signed-out only. SSO, signup Continue, Contact Sales, checkout, and recording were not pressed. The OneTrust banner was rejected on the live walk and hidden in the screenshot script.

Loom marketing declares a Lens scale on the html element (`--lns-*`) and paints Atlassian blurple `#1868db` as the one filled action. Charlie Display and Charlie Text are the measured families. The dark Lens tokens are declared and were not painted.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do paint one blurple action.
2. Do use pills in the nav.
3. Do keep the page white.
4. Don't invent a painted dark theme from the declared tokens.
5. Don't style the library from this package.
6. Don't ship Charlie or the Loom mark.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Declared custom properties on `https://www.loom.com/` html style, plus computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-18, light, 1440. `hsla()` and `calc()` were resolved in the builder and labelled Derived. Hover is a real pointer. Focus-visible is keyboard. Pressed is a forced `:active`. The OneTrust banner was hidden with CSS. It was never clicked. Motion was probed on `/`, `/pricing`, and `/login` into [raw/motion.json](raw/motion.json).

## What to distrust

- The library, the recorder, and workspace settings. They sit behind sign-in. `/login` was captured at rest. Continue and every SSO button were not pressed.
- Signup. `/signup` redirects to `id.atlassian.com`. That form was not submitted.
- Checkout and Contact Sales. Pricing CTAs were not followed.
- Dark theme. `--lns-themeDark-*` is declared. Adding `class="dark"` did not rebind `--lns-color-*`. The dark set is declared, not painted.
- The hero size. Declared heading-lg is 48px. The home h1 computed 63.269px.
- Font files. Charlie Display and Charlie Text are named. The files are not in this package.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes. The primary CTA did not change fill under `:active`.
- The public share page is one old public example, titled by the product "Loom Example for Public Sharing". It is not the signed-in library.
- Phone home and menu shots include a Chrome "Sign in to Atlassian with Google" sheet. Continue on that sheet was not pressed.

## Fonts

Charlie Display (headings) and Charlie Text (UI). Both are proprietary. The tokens name the families and fall back to sans-serif. The files are not shipped.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint one blurple action.** Get Loom for free is `#1868db` on white. Secondary actions are quiet pills. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do use pills in the nav.** The filled CTA and Contact Sales are fully rounded. [components/crops/primary-cta-default.png](components/crops/primary-cta-default.png)
- **Do keep the page white.** Body ink is grey8 `#292a2e`. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a painted dark theme from the declared tokens.** The dark set was not on screen. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Don't style the library from this package.** The share page is a public example, and `/login` is a wall. [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png)
- **Don't ship Charlie or the Loom mark.** The families and the icon stay with Atlassian.
