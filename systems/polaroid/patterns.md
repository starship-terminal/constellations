# Patterns

How the public storefront is put together. Signed out. polaroid.com redirects to /en_sg.

## Chrome

A 30px black bar (`light.promo`) carries shipping, membership, and Klarna lines in white. Under it, a white header: section links on the left, the wordmark centered, Journal, Education, Apps, Support, account, search, and the cart count on the right. On the home hero the header text is white and sits on the photograph. On listing pages it is black on white. The current section is red (`light.nav-active`).

See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [cameras-light-default](screenshots/cameras-light-default.png).

## Hero

The home hero is a full-bleed photograph. The headline is Real Head Demibold 40px, white, left aligned. Two square buttons sit under the subcopy: a white Shop Bundles and an outlined Get Inspired.

## Listing

Cameras and film open with a 62px black title, then a camera-type row (icons plus uppercase labels) and Product Finder, Filters, and Compare. Cards are grey (`light.surface`) with a 1px black border and a square tag.

See [cameras-light-default](screenshots/cameras-light-default.png) and [film-light-default](screenshots/film-light-default.png).

## Product

A product page keeps the same title size. Add to Bag is the black square Continue button, Commit Mono 12px uppercase, 45px tall. It was measured and not clicked.

See [product-light-default](screenshots/product-light-default.png). The cameras hub for Now is [pdp-light-default](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png).

## Account edge

Log in or sign up is a public page: an email field, a black Continue button, and a membership note. The form was not submitted. No password, phone, or payment field was shown.

See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Contact

Contact is a public directory of Customer Service, Membership, Press, and Partnerships. No form was submitted.

See [contact-light-default](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).

The home page at 390 CSS pixels, captured at devicePixelRatio 2. See [home-light-mobile](screenshots/home-light-mobile.png).
