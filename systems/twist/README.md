# Twist

Twist is an async messaging app (twist.com, by Doist). This package covers the public marketing site and the signed-out login, read on 19 November 2026. The painted theme is light. Dark tokens are the declared `data-theme="dark"` set on the login stylesheets, not a screen that was captured. Widths are 1440 and 390.

The email field on `/login` was filled and submitted. The next step asked for a full name and a password. Those were not entered. Google, Apple, and the Typeform waitlist were not opened. No payment was submitted.

### Principles

1. Primary actions are the teal button.
2. Nav links have no fill.
3. Headings stay at weight 300.
4. The page is warm off-white.
5. Don't paint the declared blue as the button.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on twist.com, twist.com/pricing, twist.com/async, and twist.com/login, at 1440 by 900 after a freeform resize, device pixel ratio 2. Custom properties were read from the same-origin stylesheets on the home page (`6ac611ef1987375f.css`, `c59ca05c38faa60e.css`) and from the login stylesheets (`vendor.3a1b5af0be9f9616455d.css`, `app.9e5a2da44321aeb84902.css`).

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Screenshots of the three public pages were taken with headless Playwright after those pages had already loaded in the preview browser without a Cloudflare challenge. The login host was not opened in Playwright. Shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at 2x, then quantized to 256 colours.

## What to distrust

Dark tokens are declared on `:root[data-theme="dark"]` in the login CSS. No captured page had `data-theme="dark"`. The marketing site paints light.

`#5C6A6D` and `#20292B` flatten alpha colours over `#FAFAFA` so the token check can store hex. The computed forms are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

The 256-colour screenshots band the illustrations. Read colour from the tokens.

Tiempos Headline, Tiempos Text, and Graphik Web are proprietary and are not in this package. The style guide falls back to Georgia and Inter.

`--color-tw-primary` is `#316FEA`. The painted primary button is `#008AA6`. The blue value is a declared Doist-family token, not the button that was on screen.

## Fonts

Tiempos Headline (weight 300) is the marketing display face. Tiempos Text (weight 300) is the plan-name face. Both are proprietary and are not committed.

Graphik Web is the marketing body face, declared as `--font-family-heading`. It is proprietary and is not committed.

Inter (100 to 900) is loaded and is the computed face on nav, lede, buttons, and pricing rows. Declared `--font-family-body`.

The login page uses `-apple-system` for the heading and the buttons, declared as `--reactist-font-family`. Graphik Web is still loaded for the email label.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are the teal button.** Pricing Log in is `#008AA6` with white Inter 600. See [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Nav links have no fill.** Log in in the bar is ink text, radius 6px. See [components/crops/link-nav-default.png](components/crops/link-nav-default.png).
- **Headings stay at weight 300.** Tiempos Headline on the hero is 86px and weight 300. See [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **The page is warm off-white.** Body background is `#FAFAFA`. The migration banner is `#FFF9F0`. See [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- **Don't paint the declared blue as the button.** `--color-tw-primary` is `#316FEA`. The button on the page is teal.
