# Outchat patterns

## Home

White page. Nav is 1440 x 90, white, padding 24px 0. Links sit on the right: Use cases, Pricing, Help center, then Login (quiet pill) and Sign up (black pill). The hero is centered in a 720px column inside a 1280px frame with 32px side padding. A Product Hunt badge sits above a 60px title, an 18px lede, and a black pill. Section titles are 48px. Step titles are 36px. The footer repeats the links and a legal line at 12px / 50% black.
