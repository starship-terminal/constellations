# Outchat design reference

A token set and component reference for Outchat's public marketing site. It was read on **2026-11-19** from a Wayback Machine copy of www.outchat.ai (snapshot `20260520072344`, page last published 20 Nov 2025), replayed locally and measured in headless Chrome. The live origin at https://outchat.ai/ did not answer from this host (TCP 443 timed out), and the T3 preview browser timed out on `preview_open` four times, so there is no live-page screenshot.

Captured: the marketing home, pricing, use cases, about, blog, examples, and the help introduction, plus the signed-out nav (Login, Sign up). Nothing was submitted. The email field was not filled. Stripe, custom-domain, and the app at app.outchat.ai were not entered. AgentMail was not used.

Outchat's marketing site is a Ycode page. Display and UI type are **Aeonik Regular**. Buttons use **Aeonik Medium**. The page is white, the type is black, and the primary action is a black pill (`#000000`, radius 9999px). Login is the same pill at 5% black. The content column is 1280px. The hero title is 60px at 1440 and 36px at 390, with letter-spacing -0.025em. The app stylesheet (Tailwind v4, Inter, shadcn-style `--background` / `--primary`) was read from a separate Wayback CSS file and resolved in canvas. That form was not on screen.

**Boundary:** app.outchat.ai/login and /register archive as a loading spinner titled "Outchat admin panel". The signed-in builder, chat, and Stripe checkout were not reached.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page white and the type black.
2. Do set the hero in Aeonik Regular at 60px, line-height 1.1, tracking -0.025em.
3. Do make the primary action a black pill.
4. Do keep Login as a 5% black pill.
5. Do fade the pill to 80% opacity on hover.
6. Do keep the column at 1280px and the hero at 720px.
7. Don't treat the PNGs as a live 2026-11-19 capture.
8. Don't style the signed-in builder from this package.
9. Don't invent a dark marketing theme.
10. Don't draw a 1px card stroke from the border colour.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The live site did not load. Wayback HTML for `/`, `/pricing`, `/use-cases`, `/about`, `/blog`, `/examples`, `/help/introduction`, and the two legal pages was saved from snapshot `20260520072344`. CSS came from earlier captures of the same build: `ycode-generated.css` (`20251227183246`) and `published/main.css` (`20251227183157`). Aeonik Regular and Aeonik Medium were the woff files from `20251017184632`. Those files were served on 127.0.0.1 and measured with `getComputedStyle` in headless Chrome at 1440x900 and 390x844, devicePixelRatio 2.

Ycode ships the page with `visibility: hidden` until its animation script runs. That script was not in the replay, so the measurement set `visibility: visible` on `#ycode-main-content`. A white gradient overlay (`pointer-events-none`, absolute, full height of the hero) covers the lower half of the title until scroll animation runs. Screenshots hide that overlay so the title is readable. The overlay is real markup. The hide is a reconstruction step, recorded in each `.png.capture.json`.

App colours come from `app.outchat.ai/_next/static/css/acbf3a87d8e1d0fa.css` (`20251018211621`). Each `--*` value was assigned as a canvas fill and read back as sRGB. `.dark` was applied on the document element for the dark set. No login control was measured from that CSS.

## What to distrust

These screenshots are a local replay of a May 2026 Wayback copy, not a live capture on 2026-11-19. Product images, the wordmark, and the hero video did not load: assets.ycodeapp.com returned 404. Empty image boxes on About are missing webp files, not a layout the live site shows.

The hero title's fade overlay was removed for the screenshot. On the archived page, before the animation script, the lower half of the title is covered by a white gradient.

`dark.app.*` is a stylesheet declaration. The marketing site has no dark theme, and the login page never painted those variables onto a control.

The pricing card sets `border-[#000000]` at 5% opacity but computed `border-top-width: 0px`. The colour is measured. A 1px stroke is not.

Sign up's focus-visible outline computed as `rgb(255, 255, 255) solid 3px` on a white page, so it does not show. The class is `focus:outline-none`.

## Fonts

Aeonik Regular and Aeonik Medium are commercial faces, loaded from Ycode's asset host as woff. They are not committed. The style guide falls back to Inter, which is the family the app stylesheet imports, then the system sans. Inter's own files were not fetched.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the page white and the type black.** The hero and the nav are `#ffffff` and `#000000`. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
2. **Do set the hero in Aeonik Regular at 60px, line-height 1.1, tracking -0.025em.** Phone drops the title to 36px. [screenshots/home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png)
3. **Do make the primary action a black pill.** Sign up, Make your AI chat, and Try for free are `#000000` on `#ffffff`, radius 9999px, Aeonik Medium 16px. [components/crops/button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png)
4. **Do keep Login as a 5% black pill.** Same radius, black type, padding 8px 16px. [components/crops/button-login.png](components/crops/button-login.png)
5. **Do fade the pill to 80% opacity on hover.** The transition is 100ms, `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`. [components/crops/button-signup-hover.png](components/crops/button-signup-hover.png)
6. **Do keep the column at 1280px and the hero at 720px.** Pricing cards sit in a 768px row with 12px gap and 40px padding. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
7. **Don't treat the PNGs as a live 2026-11-19 capture.** They are a Wayback replay with the entrance overlay removed.
8. **Don't style the signed-in builder from this package.** The app token set is a stylesheet reading. The login form was a spinner.
9. **Don't invent a dark marketing theme.** The app `.dark` block is in raw/app-dark.json and was never painted.
10. **Don't draw a 1px card stroke from the border colour.** The colour is 5% black. The computed width is 0.
