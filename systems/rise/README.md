# Rise

Rise was a calendar for projects, tasks and time. The company shut the product down on 31 March 2025. This package is the public marketing site that is still up at [risecalendar.com](https://risecalendar.com/), captured signed out on 2026-11-19. One light theme. Desktop 1440 and phone 390.

The signed-in app is gone. `/signin`, `/login` and the beta login URL return the site's 404. No account was created.

1. Set the essay in one Inter column.
2. Use one purple for the action.
3. Keep the header to the wordmark.

## How the values were read

Custom properties came from `:root` in `/_next/static/css/ec71784565c9a205.css`. Computed styles came from the live pages in the preview browser (`preview_evaluate` on the homepage, pricing, blog, a blog post, the manifesto, the Asana comparison page, and `/signin`). Screenshots came from headless Playwright on those same public URLs after the preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" twice. No Cloudflare challenge appeared. Button hover was read with Playwright's hover on the pricing buttons. The billing switch was clicked once. It only toggles a public price label.

## What to distrust

The page background is white. `--background` is `#fcfcfe` and shows on the date badge and the Solo card, not on the body.

`--sidebar`, `--offset-text`, `--negative`, `--warning`, `--success` and the hue tokens are declared and were not painted on the pages measured. They are in the tokens because the stylesheet declares them. They are not evidence of a component.

The focus utility names `--ring`. The stylesheet does not set it. The focus crop shows no ring.

`bg-offWhite` is a class on the category badge. The stylesheet has no `.bg-offWhite` rule, so that badge's background computed as transparent.

There is no dark theme. Do not invent one from the 404 page. A first computed read of that page reported a black body while it was still settling. The screenshot and a pixel sample are white.

Screenshots are full colour. Read values from the tokens, not from the images.

Seven values have no reading in [raw/](raw): the three motion durations, the 1280px nav width, `background.primary-soft`, `border.quiet` and `border.image`. They were typed in at capture time, and their sources say Reconstruction. The builder holds the `:root` triplets as literals: they agree with [raw/readings.md](raw/readings.md), but a recapture does not update them. [components.md](components.md) is written from text in `tools/build_components_md.py` in the same way.

## Do and don't

- **Set the essay in one Inter column.** Titles are bold and tracking-tight. Body is 16px on 28px at foreground 80%. The column is 768px. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Use one purple for the action.** Primary buttons, inline links and the on-switch share `{color.primary}`. Secondary actions are white with a hairline. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).
- **Keep the header to the wordmark.** It is white, 80px, sticky, and has no other controls on these pages. See [not-found-light-default.png](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png).
- **Mark essays with the small uppercase badge.** 10px, semibold, 0.5px tracking, a 1px border, radius 4px. See [badge-eyebrow.png](components/crops/badge-eyebrow.png).
