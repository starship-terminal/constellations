# Rise patterns

Public pages only. Captured 2026-11-19. The product app is gone.

## Marketing shell

A sticky white header, 80px, padding 28px 0. The only control in it is the wordmark, 76 by 24, linking home. There is no nav, no theme switch and no account menu.

The column is `max-w-screen-md` (768px) with 20px side padding on the essay pages, and `max-w-screen-xl` (1280px) in the header. Comparison heroes use `max-w-screen-lg` (1024px) and centre the type.

## Essay

[Home](screenshots/home-light-default.png) is the sunset letter. An eyebrow badge, a 48px tracking-tight title, a 20px offset-dark dek, a 160px hairline, then 16/28 body at foreground 80%. Section headings are 24px semibold with 32px above. Images sit on the input fill with an 8px radius and a 5% foreground border. The same pattern is the blog post and the manifesto.

## Pricing

[Pricing](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) puts a 36px title and a 16px dek over a full-width rule, then two 480px cards in a row. The quiet card is the free plan. The raised card is the paid plan and holds the billing switch. Phone width stacks them. See [pricing-light-phone.png](screenshots/pricing-light-phone.png).

## Blog index

[Blog](screenshots/blog-light-default.png) is a title, a dek, then a featured essay title at 48px and a list of 30px titles. Each row has the same eyebrow badge as the essay. Titles shift 4px right on hover. There are no cards and no images in the index.

## Comparison landing

[Alternative to Asana](screenshots/compare-light-default.png) is a centred hero: 56px title, 24px dek, a 46px primary button, then a logo strip and a long comparison essay. The same hero is on the other comparison URLs in the sitemap.

## Not found

[Sign-in URL](screenshots/not-found-light-default.png) is the Next.js 404: a 24px medium "404", a hairline, and "This page could not be found." The header wordmark remains. The page background is white.

## Not captured

App shell, a signed-in calendar, a form with a validation error, empty and loading states, a destructive confirmation, search, and onboarding. Those lived in the app. The public site has no form. Login, signup and `/app` do not open a session.
