# Metaview

Metaview is an agentic recruiting platform. This package covers the public marketing site at www.metaview.ai and the signed-out auth screens at app.metaview.ai, captured on 2026-11-19. Marketing ships one warm theme (paper `#FBF8F4`, Euclid Circular A). The app ships light and dark (Inter), and both sheets were read. Desktop captures are 1440 CSS px wide. Phone captures are 390 CSS px wide. Nothing was submitted, and no account was created.

## How the values were read

The marketing site inlines a `:root` block in the HTML of www.metaview.ai. That block is [raw/marketing-root.css](raw/marketing-root.css): 198 `--mv-color-*`, `--mv-radius-*`, `--mv-font-*`, `--mv-typography-*`, `--mv-shadow-*` and `--mv-border-*` declarations. Computed styles on the live page matched those names (page `#FBF8F4`, text `#01051B`, primary pill `rgb(0, 8, 5)`, announcement bar the same ink).

The signed-out app declares 131 `--mv-*` names three times in `app.metaview.ai/static/css/root.*.css`: on `:root` (light), under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, and on `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. [raw/app-vars.json](raw/app-vars.json) is that light block and the dark block, read from the sign-in page. Headless Chromium opened the page in light. The preview browser opened it in dark. Setting `data-theme="dark"` reproduces the dark sheet, and the body then computed to `rgb(5, 6, 6)`, which is `--mv-ui1`.

Screenshots were taken with the kit's Playwright helper at 1440x900 and 1x, and at 390x844 and 2x for the phone. Each PNG has a `.capture.json` sidecar.

## What to distrust

Shadows and border shorthands are in `$extensions.notTokenised`. They are the product's strings, and they are not single DTCG tokens.

The marketing type scale is the declared `--mv-typography-*` ramp. The home h1 computed to 52px at a 1169px viewport, while the token says 48px. The token is the authored size. The computed size is the fluid override at that width.

Euclid Circular A and Inter are not in this package. The style guide falls back to the system face. Do not treat that fallback as the product's font.

The dark sign-in screenshot was taken after setting `data-theme="dark"` in the page. The dark colours are the product's own stylesheet. The screenshot is a reconstruction of the theme the preview browser showed on first load, not a claim that headless Chromium picks dark on its own.

256-colour PNGs keep flat fills. Read colours from the tokens.

## Fonts

Marketing: Euclid Circular A, with a system sans fallback. The sign-in stylesheet also loads Euclid Circular A at 400 and 500. The files are the product's, and they are not committed.

App: Inter-v3_19 at 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700, from `/static/font/Inter-*.woff2`. The computed body stack is Inter-v3_19, then system sans. Roboto Mono and Fira Code are also in that stylesheet. They were not used on the sign-in screen.

## Do and don't

- **Use the paper page and the ink pill.** The marketing page is `#FBF8F4` and the primary action is a near-black pill with a white label. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) shows both.
- **Keep the announcement bar full-bleed and short.** It is 42px of `#000805` with 16px white type. [components/crops/banner-default.png](components/crops/banner-default.png) shows it.
- **Set the app theme with `data-theme`.** Light is the unconditioned `:root`. Dark is `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. [screenshots/auth-signin-dark-default.png](screenshots/auth-signin-dark-default.png) is the dark card.
- **Leave the email field empty in a recreation of the signed-out screen.** The capture did not type an address or submit. [components/crops/form-signin-dark.png](components/crops/form-signin-dark.png) is the empty field.
- **Do not invent a signed-in workspace.** Candidate lists, notes and reports were not opened.

## Surfaces

www.metaview.ai redirects from metaview.app. The product pages captured are home, pricing, demo, fillmore, sourcing, screening, notes, reports, about, integrations, privacy, careers, resources and desktop. Auth captured is `/auth/sign-in` and `/auth/sign-up`. Google and Microsoft continue buttons were not pressed. No payment, phone or government-id step appeared.
