Palette

The declared --blackberry--*/--plum--* layer carries the ink and the accent ramps; the measured color.measured.* and color.auth.* readings carry the one-offs and the Kinde card. Light roles alias the palette, dark roles the two dark surfaces.

Radius, gradient and motion

Pills everywhere: 100px on buttons and selects, 999px on the billing toggle, 50px on the nav Login. Cards run 16-28px; the Kinde controls 8px. Borders are hairlines: #d9d9e0 on fields, 10% ink inside content, 25% plum on the featured card.
