# Patterns

How the public controls sit on the photographed pages. Desktop 1440 by 900, devicePixelRatio 2. One light theme.

## Location picker

https://www.stradivarius.com is a world page. The shop does not paint until a country is chosen. This capture uses United States, English. See [screenshots/gate-light-1440.png](screenshots/gate-light-1440.png).

## Home

A full-bleed campaign sits under a thin header: hamburger, wordmark, search pill, account, wishlist, basket. A delivery marquee runs across the page. Campaign tiles link into New in, coats, skirts, shoes, tops, jeans, and faux fur. See [screenshots/home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Menu

The hamburger opens a dialog. Rows are numbered 01 to 09. Clothing opens a second column of category links. Special prices is the red row. See [screenshots/menu-light-1440.png](screenshots/menu-light-1440.png).

## Category

A large title, a text subnav (View all, Mini, Maxi, Printed, Party, Black), a black Filters button, and a product grid. View1 was pressed. Prices sit under the photograph in 12px semibold. See [screenshots/category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Product

A tall gallery, then the name in 28px semibold uppercase, the price in 22px, a color line, and size chips. Add to bag stays hidden until a size is chosen. No size was chosen. See [screenshots/product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Help

Help & Contact is a public topic list: orders, deliveries, returns, payments, gifts, shop, account. Chat was closed for the day. Login links on that page were not entered. The contact raster did not paint the cards, so the topics were read from the page tree and are listed in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
