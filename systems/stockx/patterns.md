# Patterns

## App shell

A 91px header stays fixed, `#121212` on the shop, white on How It Works. A 48px category bar sits under it. The footer is a `#393939` band of brand columns, about links, sell links, and help links. The footer was read from the DOM and not photographed.

## Homepage

A promo banner, then a product rail titled Recommended For You, then more rails (Trending Sneakers, Collectibles Staff Picks) and a magazine row. Photographed: [home-dark-1440.png](screenshots/home-dark-1440.png).

## Category

Sneakers is a heading, a short paragraph, and a product grid on `#121212`. Photographed: [category-dark-1440.png](screenshots/category-dark-1440.png).

## Product

A white image well on the left, a buy column on the right: title, last sale, Size, Buy Now, Sell Now. The photographed product is Jordan 4 Retro Toro Bravo (2026): [product-dark-1440.png](screenshots/product-dark-1440.png). Buy Now and Size were not clicked.

## Editorial page

How It Works is a light page with a black hero, a 60px serif title, and three green pills: [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png). Company was opened and discarded because it shows leadership portraits.

## Stops

Login, signup, Sell, Buy Now, Size, search submit, help, contact, referral, and the locale picker were not opened. The reasons are in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).
