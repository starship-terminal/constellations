# Components

What was on the public pages. States that were not shown are marked not captured.

## Header

91px, `#121212`. Logo on the left, 125px wide, fill `#95C0A4`. Search in the middle. News, About, Help, Sell, and Sign Up | Login on the right. See [home-dark-1440.png](screenshots/home-dark-1440.png).

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured. Sign Up | Login was not clicked.

## Search

829 by 42 on the 1440 homepage. Background `#242424`, 1px `#5F5F5F` border, radius 8px, padding 0 40px. Placeholder "Search for brand, color, etc." An AI Search switch sits in the field. Nothing was typed. The switch was not toggled.

## Category bar

48px under the header. All is a button. Trending through More are links. Deals is `#EF6958`. See [category-dark-1440.png](screenshots/category-dark-1440.png). All was not opened.

## Sign Up | Login

White pill, 36px, radius 100px, padding 8px 16px, 14px weight 600, text `#0F0F0F`. Not clicked.

On How It Works the same control splits into Login (1px `#0F0F0F` outline, text `#242424`) and Sign Up (fill `#242424`, white text). Neither was clicked. See [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Product card

White image well, a last-sale chip, a two-line title, "New & Verified", and a 20px price. A Follow heart sits on the image. Follow was not clicked. See [home-dark-1440.png](screenshots/home-dark-1440.png).

## Product buy bar

24px title, a 24px last sale, a Size field (40px, radius 8px, not opened), a green Buy Now pill (44px, `#95C0A4`, not clicked), and a green Sell Now text button (not clicked). See [product-dark-1440.png](screenshots/product-dark-1440.png).

## Theme button

56px circle, background `#393939`, label "Switch to Light Mode", `aria-pressed` true. Not clicked. A light shop theme was not measured.

## How It Works hero

Black section. 60px Gestura Reg title, white, letter-spacing -0.64px. A short mission paragraph. Three green pills: Our Process, Buyers, Sellers. Those pills were not clicked. See [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Not captured

Login, signup, Buy Now, Size, Sell Now, Ask, search submit, Follow, theme switch, text field focus, hover, error, loading, toast, and phone width. The footer was read and not photographed.
