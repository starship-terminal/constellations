## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export netflix` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Use the red action for the one next step.
2. Keep the page black and the type white.
3. Put fields on the translucent charcoal chrome.
4. Do not invent a light theme.
5. Do not draw a drop shadow on these controls.
6. Do not treat declared hover colours as a painted capture.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened https://www.netflix.com/ with `reuseExistingTab` false. The site redirected to https://www.netflix.com/sg/. Styles were read with `preview_evaluate` on the live page: computed style on the elements named in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json), and computed custom properties whose names match Hawkins (`--hcw--local-design--*`, `--hcw--focus-ring--*`) in [raw/hawkins-vars.json](raw/hawkins-vars.json). Login was a same-tab navigation to https://www.netflix.com/sg/login. Phone width used `preview_resize` freeform 390 by 844, then `window.innerWidth`.

`preview_snapshot` failed twice with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames." No screenshot or crop was invented. Headless Playwright was not launched.

No stylesheet was blocked. 130 stylesheets were readable. Netflix Sans is named in computed `font-family` and is not shipped in this package.

## What to distrust

- Hover, pressed, disabled, error, success, and warning colours are declared custom properties. They were not painted on the page.
- The FAQ answer set `aria-expanded` to true and kept a painted height of 0. The open panel is a gap.
- The hero scrim gradient was truncated at 160 characters. The two colour stops of the value-card gradient were read in full.
- The inline N logo's third path was truncated, so that SVG was not saved. The wordmark SVG was fetched from the `BrandNetflixLogotype` image URL.
- [screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png](screenshots/home-dark-unavailable.png) is a black label, not a capture of the page. Do not sample colours from it.
- The login URL carried a `serverState` query. That value is not stored here.
- Singapore pricing copy ("Starts at $15.98") is locale content, not a token.

## Do and don't

- **Use the red action for the one next step.** Sign In, Get Started, and Continue share `#e50914`, white type, and a 4px radius.
- **Keep the page black and the type white.** Secondary copy is white at 70%.
- **Put fields on the translucent charcoal chrome.** `rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.7)` with a 1px grey border, not a white input.
- **Do not invent a light theme.** These pages do not switch.
- **Do not draw a drop shadow on these controls.** None was measured.
- **Do not treat declared hover colours as a painted capture.** They are stylesheet values.
