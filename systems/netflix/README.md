# Netflix design reference

A token set and component reference for Netflix's public web surface. Captured from `https://www.netflix.com/` on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Playwright at 1440 x 900 (1x) and 390 x 844 (2x). The homepage redirects to a regional marketing path. The package does not name that region, and it stores no email, phone, or location.

The marketing site is one dark surface: black page, white Netflix Sans, red `#e50914` buttons that darken to `#c11119` on hover, 4px control radii, and grey FAQ and title cards. Help Center, the signup plan step, the investor site, and the speed test are light pages linked from the footer. They are not a theme toggle.

**Boundary.** Sign In, Account, and Get started open a login or signup wall. Those walls were screenshotted and not filled. No email, phone, password, or code was typed. No form was submitted. The signed-in browse, profiles, and player were not reached.

## How the values were read

Playwright opened the public pages and `getComputedStyle` read the nodes named in [raw/computed.json](raw/computed.json). Netflix declared no custom properties on `:root`. Button hover and focus were read by hovering and focusing Get Started. Screenshots were taken with the kit's screenshot helper, then quantised with `tools/capture/quantize.py`. Full-colour PNGs stay in `~/.design-systems/netflix/raw-shots/`.

## What to distrust

- Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Poster art bands. Read colours from tokens.json.
- The help, signup, investor, and speed-test shots are named `*-dark-*` because the capture script stamps the marketing theme. Those pages are light.
- The jobs shot was recaptured after an 8 second wait. The first frame was a loading mark.
- The originals full-page shot is truncated at the kit's tile cap. The page was 8603 px.
- Pressed, disabled, error, and status colours were not on screen. They are Not captured.
- Netflix Sans is named from the computed font stack. The font file is not in this package.
- The cookie dialog is OneTrust, not Netflix's own component system.
- The speed-test readout is one run's number. It is not a token.
- The Media Center homepage names a place in its headline, so that screenshot is not in the package. The footer link is recorded in site-tree.md.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the page black and the type white.** The marketing surface has one background. [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
- **Do use the red fill for the one primary action.** Sign In and Get Started share `#e50914`. Secondary actions are text. [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
- **Do darken the red on hover.** Get Started moves to `#c11119`. Focus is a 2px white outline.
- **Do keep control corners at 4px and cards at 8px.** [home-dark-title-dialog](screenshots/home-dark-title-dialog.png).
- **Don't treat Help Center as a dark theme.** It is a separate light product. [help-dark-default](screenshots/help-dark-default.png).
- **Don't style the signed-in player from this package.** That surface was not reached.

