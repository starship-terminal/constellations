# Douze patterns

How the public storefront combines the components in [components.md](components.md). The control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Home

Announcement, then a floating header (sable bubble, tracked nav, logo, login, cart) over a full-bleed product photograph. Hero heading is Boogy, sable ink, 72px. A square dusk CTA sits on the photograph. Below: product rail, ingredient story, testimonial carousel, recipe cards, footer.

Shot: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Cart drawer over the same page: [screenshots/home-light-cart.png](screenshots/home-light-cart.png).

## Shop

Same chrome. A row of pill tabs (ALL, Cans, Bottles, Gift sets) filters the grid without changing the URL. Cards are 45px radius. Selected tab is dusk fill, white label.

Shot: [screenshots/shop-light-default.png](screenshots/shop-light-default.png), Cans selected: [screenshots/shop-light-cans.png](screenshots/shop-light-cans.png).

## Product

Gallery left, buy box right. Pack size is a radio row. Purchase type is two stacked options (one-time, subscription). ADD TO CART is the square dusk button. Accordions below the buy box. Not submitted.

Shot: [screenshots/pdp-light-default.png](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png).

## Story and why

Editorial pages. Why Douze adds an ingredient filter row (ALL plus flavour names) that stays on the same URL.

Shots: [screenshots/story-light-default.png](screenshots/story-light-default.png), [screenshots/why-light-default.png](screenshots/why-light-default.png).

## Recipes

Index of recipe cards, then an article. One article was shot (Sirocco). The rest share that template.

Shots: [screenshots/recipes-light-default.png](screenshots/recipes-light-default.png), [screenshots/recipe-light-default.png](screenshots/recipe-light-default.png).

## FAQ

A list of `collapsible-toggle` rows. One row opened. The rest stay closed until clicked.

Shot: [screenshots/faqs-light-open.png](screenshots/faqs-light-open.png).

## Login wall

Centered sable card, 16px fields, square SIGN IN. Email and password were not typed. Register is the same family of page. Account URLs redirect here or render a signed-out "Welcome," shell and were not explored.

Shot: [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Empty cart

Cart page and the drawer share the empty state: short copy and START SHOPPING back to the shop. No checkout control while the cart is empty.

Shot: [screenshots/cart-light-default.png](screenshots/cart-light-default.png).

## Phone

Burger replaces the nav list. The drawer lists the same destinations plus ACCOUNT, which is the login wall. Buy box and shop stack in one column.

Shots: [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png), [screenshots/mobile-shop-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-shop-light-default.png), [screenshots/mobile-pdp-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-pdp-light-default.png).
