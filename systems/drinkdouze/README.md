# Douze design reference

A token set and component reference for Douze's public storefront — for building a DTC shop in the style of the real thing. It was read from **drinkdouze.com** on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, the shop grid (`/collections/all`), two product pages (Orange Sanguine & Piment, Trio Douze), the editorial pages (Notre histoire, Engagements, FAQ, Store locator, Abonnement, Parrainage), the recipes blog and one article, the empty cart, the account login card (measured, never submitted), the search results, and a 390px mobile pass including the navigation drawer.

Douze is "Pétillants sans alcool" — mixology-crafted non-alcoholic cocktails sold by the canette, a French Shopify storefront on the Impact-family theme plus a `douze-custom.css` layer. It is worth a capture because the whole surface is one disciplined idea: **boxed rounded sections floating on a sable field**. Every page is white boxes at a 45px radius with 25px of sable `#f6f2e9` air between them; ink is one espresso brown, dusk `#301816`, for text, borders and the primary fill; type is a light display cut — Boogy at weight 300, 52–128px with italic accents inside the line — over Robin Regular 500 body and Robin Semibold 600 labels. The brand's only other hues are the products': each flavour owns an accent (`--color-flavor-orange-piment` `#f26554`, `abricot-zaatar` `#f4a765`, `poire-anis` `#f3eea7`) that re-binds the product card's quick-add fill and the checked pack-size swatch. Buttons and colour swatches are square (`--button-border-radius: 0`), while the pack-size block swatches are 999px pills that fill with the flavour accent when checked; a 50px moving banner scrolls claims between the boxed sections. **Boundary:** checkout, a filled cart, customer account and subscription management were never reached — nothing was added, paid, sent or signed into; the login fields were filled once and emptied.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export drinkdouze` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do float boxed sections on sable.
2. Do keep ink to one brown.
3. Do pair Boogy's light display with Robin's mid body.
4. Do let the flavour own its controls.
5. Do keep buttons square, and let the pack-size chips pill.
6. Don't add a dark theme.
7. Don't reach for borders or shadows for depth.
8. Don't style checkout or account screens from this package.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) loads the home and a product page, captures every stylesheet body (`theme.css`, `douze-custom.css`, the compiled assets and the cross-origin checkout-button sheet from network responses, plus the inline `<style>` blocks — the per-page `:root` token block and each `#shopify-section-*` scope) and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json), resolved on a probe carrying the real sheets at `:root`. Shopify's theme settings arrive as bare `r, g, b` triplets (fed to `rgb()`/`rgba()` by the sheets), which the probe normalises to hex; `douze-custom.css` declares its layer outright. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state — across the chrome, home, shop, product, content, auth and mobile surfaces. The quick-add form is `visibility:hidden` until its card is hovered, so it is measured under its designed reveal. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md).
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route (bounded at 8 chunks) with `.capture.json` sidecars, which publish beside the PNGs, including the local UI toggles: the bag icon opening the empty mini-cart drawer, the hamburger opening the mobile navigation drawer, and the 12-canette pack-size swatch on the product page (`product-light-variant`) — a radio that also reveals the purchase-option radios; the add-to-cart was never pressed. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was bought, sent or signed into: the login card's fields were filled with sample text and emptied, never submitted; every Ajouter-au-panier, quick-add, Acheter-en-1-clic, newsletter and search submit was measured, never pressed; the mini-cart and mobile drawers and the pack-size swatch were toggled as local UI state; Klaviyo popups and any consent layer were hidden, never answered — no consent button was ever clicked and no form was ever posted. No age gate appears on the surface.

## What to distrust

- **This is the storefront shell, not the account or checkout.** A filled cart, `/checkout`, payment, `/account` past the login/register forms and subscription management are all behind actions never taken: the only cart surface captured is the empty one. The mini-cart drawer, free-shipping meter and line items are unseen.
- **The `:root` values are the last-loaded page's.** Shopify emits a per-page inline `:root` block; the probe merged the home and product sheets, so a role that differs per page resolves to whichever sheet loads last — the per-section re-bindings stay as rules in `raw/css-vars.json`, and `light.inverse` holds the measured photo-scoped values instead of a guessed scope.
- **Card titles are images.** The flavour names on `product-item` cards are wordmark `<img>`s, not live text — measure them as pictures, not type. The site likewise leads the shop page with an image-hero band, not a text h1.
- **Vendor widgets paint in the theme's chrome.** Klaviyo reviews (`kl_reviews`), the newsletter embed and the subscription radios are third-party widgets styled to match; their variables are binned `vendor` in css-vars.json, and the subscription widget did not render on load signed out (it appears in `product-light-variant` once a pack-size is settled).
- **Focus rings are the browser's.** Most controls draw a 1px `#005fcc` auto outline — Chromium's default ring — not a declared design token; `newsletter-button` is the exception (2px sable). The text and search fields draw their own ring instead: a 1px inset `#301816` box-shadow, with no outline at all.
- **The announced "free shipping" strip is a boxed section too.** The announcement bar paints sable inside a 45px-radius box of its own — it is not a page-edge strip.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **Rate limiting.** Direct Shopify requests to some product routes and `/products.json` return 429 from a datacentre-looking client; the browser pass succeeded with fresh contexts, but a re-run may need the same care.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Fonts

- **Boogy** and **Boogy Italic** — the display face, weight 300: hero headlines (72px desktop, an 8vw "large" step that resolves to ~115px at 1440), section h2s at 52px, product titles at 60px, card h5s at 12px. Served as WOFF2 from the Shopify CDN.
- **Robin Regular** — the body face, weight 500: nav, buttons, prose, fields. **Robin Semibold** at 600 for prices, labels and button text. Also served WOFF2.
- **Jost** and **Barlow** also ship in the bundles, used by vendor widgets, not the theme's own chrome.
- The style guide and tokens fall back to `sans-serif`/system faces; the served files are the product's artwork and are not vendored.

## Coverage

Captured at both widths (1440 desktop, 390 mobile where noted): home `/` (+ cart drawer, + mobile menu), `/collections/all` (both), `/products/orange-sanguine-piment` (both, + variant-swatched), `/products/trio-douze`, `/pages/notre-histoire`, `/pages/engagements`, `/pages/page-faq`, `/pages/store-locator`, `/pages/page-dabonnement`, `/pages/parrainage`, `/blogs/recipes`, `/blogs/recipes/sirocco`, `/cart`, `/account/login`, `/search?q=douze`. Not captured: anything past a submit or a purchase — checkout, a filled cart, the account area, registration, the recover flow past its toggle; the newsletter drawer (its header toggle is `display:none` at both widths); the Shop nav dropdown (its panel is not in the signed-out DOM); the Klaviyo popup (never mounted); the `/en` locale (a duplicate surface); pagination (the catalogue fits one page); social links (none paint in the footer); sold-out/disabled/error states (none paint on the public surface). ## Do and don't

- **Do float boxed sections on sable.** Sections are white or media boxes at `--block-border-radius` 45px with a 25px `--boxed-sections-margin` of `#f6f2e9` around them — the page field is the gaps. ([home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png))
- **Do keep ink to one brown.** Text, headings, borders, the primary fill and the loading bar are all dusk `#301816` (`--text-color`, `--primary-button-background`); sable `#f6f2e9` is the inverse ink over photos. ([product-light-default](screenshots/product-light-default.png))
- **Do pair Boogy's light display with Robin's mid body.** Display runs weight 300 at 52–128px with an italic accent inside the line; body and buttons run Robin 500/600. ([hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png))
- **Do let the flavour own its controls.** Each product's accent re-binds its quick-add fill and checked swatch — `#f26554` on Orange Sanguine & Piment — while the submit stays dusk. ([product-light-variant](screenshots/product-light-variant.png))
- **Do keep buttons square, and let the pack-size chips pill.** Buttons and colour swatches run 0px (`--button-border-radius`, `--color-swatch-border-radius`); the `block-swatch` pack-size chips are 999px pills whose checked state fills with the flavour accent. ([add-to-cart](components/crops/add-to-cart-default.png), [variant-swatch](components/crops/variant-swatch-default.png))
- **Don't add a dark theme.** None paints — photo sections re-bind text to sable inside the same light surface (`light.inverse.*`), so treat them as scoped inversion, not a theme. ([home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png))
- **Don't reach for borders or shadows for depth.** Sections separate by radius + margin + fill, not hairlines; borders are quiet `#321a18`/`#aca3a2`. The only painted shadows are the buttons' small hover/pressed lift (`rgba(0,0,0,0.15) 0 4px 8px`) and the fields' inset 1px dusk focus ring. ([login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png))
- **Don't style checkout or account screens from this package.** Those surfaces were never reached; only the public storefront is evidence.
