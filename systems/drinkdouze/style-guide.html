Douze, measured

drinkdouze.com is a Shopify storefront in French (the Impact-family theme plus a douze-custom.css layer): "Pétillants sans alcool" — non-alcoholic sparkling cocktails sold by the canette. It paints light only, as boxed rounded sections (45px radius) floating on a sable field #f6f2e9, with dusk-brown ink #301816. Type is Boogy 300 display over Robin Regular 500 body; per-product flavour accents — orange-piment #f26554, abricot-zaatar #f4a765, poire-anis #f3eea7 — re-bind the product card's button fill. Buttons and colour swatches are square; the pack-size swatches are 999px pills. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from drinkdouze.com, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 (mobile at 390 x 844). Checkout, account, payment and every submit control are boundaries; the login fields were filled once and emptied, never sent. Boogy and Robin are served faces and are not loaded here: samples fall back to system serif and sans.

Palette

Two brand hues — dusk #301816 and sable #f6f2e9 — carry the whole surface; the --color-flavor-* accents belong to the products and re-bind per card. Roles arrive as RGB triplets in the theme's inline :root block (resolved to hex here); the douze-custom layer declares them outright.

Spacing and radius

Boxed sections and media round at 45px, reduced blocks at 16px; buttons and colour swatches stay square at 0px, pack-size swatches run a 999px pill radius. The grid is 20 columns on a 1600px container with a 24px gap and 73px gutters; sections breathe 64px, tighten to 48px, and float on a 25px sable margin.
