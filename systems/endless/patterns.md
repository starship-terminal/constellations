# Endless patterns

Public marketing shell, 2026-11-21.

## App shell

A sticky header: logomark, Product, Models, Industries, Pricing, Blog, Sign in, Sign up for free. At 390 the nav collapses into a Menu button that opens a bottom sheet. Screenshots: [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Composer

The home hero is a fake product composer: a textarea, an add button, a model chip, and a filled Create button, plus three prompt chips. The model chip and the add button open a sign-up dialog instead of a picker. [screenshots/home-light-model-gate.png](screenshots/home-light-model-gate.png).

## Pricing

Four plan cards on a page ground. The featured card uses the filled button. Monthly and Annual are a two-part toggle. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Docs

A left sidebar with search, grouped sections, and a theme control. This is a different shell from the marketing header. [screenshots/docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png).

## Sign-in

A split page: form on the left, a testimonial panel on the right. Identity buttons sit above the email field. This is the auth boundary. [screenshots/signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png).
