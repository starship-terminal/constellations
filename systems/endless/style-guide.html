Endless, measured

endless.app (what endless.io redirects to) is one Next.js + Tailwind v4 surface — the marketing pages, ~70 free tool pages, the model catalog, the social radar, /docs and /brand — over a single shadcn-style token layer: semantic roles that re-bind under a .dark class (next-themes, driven by the system scheme or the footer Theme control), the --mode-*/--label-*/--chart-*/--series-* product palettes, and a near-white neutral ramp with one sky accent #01bcff. TikTok Sans is the face everywhere; Geist Mono covers code. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Palette

The declared layer is mostly-neutral: the sky accent #01bcff does all the brand work, supported by the --label-* tag hues, the --mode-* modality colours and the --series-*/--chart-* data ramps. Light and dark roles are the same declared variables, re-bound by the .dark scope.

Spacing, radius and layout

Spacing is the 4px Tailwind unit; containers run 320–1152px. Radii are a small scale — 8px controls, 12px buttons and chips, 16px cards, 20px big tiles and the composer, plus a full pill — and the 22.5% icon mask.

Type
