Palette

The core set carries the measured ink/white neutrals and the brand purples (var-referenced to the declared --colors--* layer where it names them); the legacy --old-swatches--* set is kept as provenance only. Light roles alias the palette; the "dark" set is the small role set the ink banner and the #29007a purple band paint — painted surfaces, not a user toggle.

Spacing and radius

copy.ai mixes radii by control: 4px on the nav CTA and the mobile sales action, 4.5px on the auth buttons, 3.5px on the auth field, 5px on the contact fields, 8px on every big CTA and secondary, a 40px badge pill and a 48px segmented-control pill. Hairlines do the dividing — #e0e0e0 on cards and menu rows, #e4edf1 on fields, #ccd9e0 on the playbook chip.
