# Copy.ai design reference

A token set and component reference for copy.ai's public signed-out surface — for building marketing pages and pricing cards in the style of the real thing. It was read from **copy.ai** on **2026-11-21**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: home (four mega-menus), /prices, the platform page (how-it-works), a use-case page (prospecting-cockpit), /enterprise, the blog index, /contact-us, /changelog, /request-a-demo, /chat, /features/infobase, /tools, /gtm-ai, /webinars, /privacy-notice, the public login.copy.ai auth card (reached through app.copy.ai/login, then stopped), plus a 390px pass on home and pricing. The homepage control map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

copy.ai is worth a capture because a GTM-AI marketing site on Webflow paints an unusually disciplined public system: ABC Normal display (88/88 hero, 600 weight everywhere it's set) over a `#171717` ink and white surfaces, one `#693ee0` action purple, and a signature state move — a black button's fill lifts to purple on hover (`#171717 → #693ee0`). Radii are mixed by control (4px nav CTA, 8px big CTAs and secondaries, 5px contact fields, 40/48px pills), hairlines do the dividing (`#e0e0e0` cards, `#e4edf1` fields), and the dark is painted: a 48px ink banner and `#29007a` deep-purple feature bands inside the light page. The Webflow sheets declare a small named `--colors--*` layer plus a legacy `--old-swatches--*` set, harvested here from the cross-origin bundles. **Boundary:** the workflows product behind the login.copy.ai card — the builder, GTM dashboards, chat, tables — was never reached and is never inferred.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do spend the purple on actions only.
2. Do let black buttons lift to purple on hover.
3. Do ink the display, slate the lede.
4. Do divide with hairlines.
5. Do mix radii by control.
6. Do paint the dark bands, don't toggle a theme.
7. Don't style the product from this package.
8. Don't use the legacy swatches.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out site in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body — copy.ai's Webflow bundles are cross-origin (cdn.prod.website-files.com, invisible to the CSSOM), so bodies are read from network responses plus inline `<style>` blocks re-parsed with `CSSStyleSheet` — and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json), resolving each on a probe document (one light theme; nothing on the surface switches a second). The layer is small but named (`--colors--primary-purple` is #693ee0), so tokens var-reference it where it names a painted value, and everything else — the ink/white/slate neutrals, the auth-host purple ramp, the radii and the type ramp — is measured computed values from [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json). [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route in a fresh context and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state, plus field focused/filled states and the opened mega-menu and mobile menu. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md). The mega-menu auto-closes a few seconds after it opens, so flyout-child states re-open it with the Platform trigger before reading.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the Platform mega-menu on home, an FAQ accordion row on /prices, the contact form's first field focused, and the mobile menu overlay. Full-page shots are capped at eight chunks; the changelog and both mobile full pages overflowed the GPU texture limit and stitched from two chunks. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, created, submitted or paid for: the login.copy.ai auth card was measured as a surface — its email field was measured, never sent; the contact form's fields were focused and filled then cleared, never submitted; every Get a Demo, Try for free, Get started, Sign up, Contact Sales and OAuth action was measured or screenshotted, never followed. The HubSpot cookie banner and chat widget were hidden per capture, never answered — no consent was ever posted.

## What to distrust

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- **This is the public surface, not the product.** The workflows builder, the GTM dashboards, chat, tables, teams and account sit behind the auth card and are uncaptured. Marketing pages describe the product; nothing here verifies how it behaves. Treat the product's own design system as unknown.
- **"Dark" is a painted surface, not a toggle.** The top banner fills #171717 and the platform feature card bands are #29007a deep purple — painted treatments inside a light page. The `dark` token set is the small role set those bands paint, not a user-selectable mode.
- **The login card is a second system.** login.copy.ai paints its own tokens — #5753c6 links, #e5e7eb borders, 4.5px radii, an `interFont` stack — that differ from the marketing site's. Both are tokenised and labelled; don't mix them.
- **The mega-menu auto-closes.** It dismisses a few seconds after opening; flyout-state readings re-opened it first, so hover/focus values are real but the panel is ephemeral by design.
- **Legacy swatches are provenance, not design.** The `--old-swatches--*` set still declares on `:root` (the previous design's #160647 navy, #47beb9 teal, #6122e8 purple) but nothing measured paints it; `core.color.legacy` records it so the declared layer is complete, not because the site uses it.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.

## Fonts

Two families style the marketing surface: **ABC Normal** at 600 on every display and card heading (the 88/88 hero, 72/82 contact display, 64/74 page titles, 56/66 sections, 32/42 and 28px card titles, 24/33.6 H4s) and **Inter** at 400-700 for controls and body. The auth host (login.copy.ai) paints its own `interFont` stack. The `@font-face` blocks live in the cross-origin cdn.prod.website-files.com bundles — the harvest lists 74 faces with woff2 srcs in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). ABC Normal is licensed and not distributed with the package; the tokens carry the stacks and the style guide falls back to Inter/system sans.

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the purple on actions only.** `#693ee0` is the Get a Demo nav pill, the plan price, the SAVING badge and the auth Continue — never a background or a text colour. See [btn-nav](components/crops/btn-nav-default.png).
2. **Do let black buttons lift to purple on hover.** Every dark button — hero submit, plan CTA, Send message, the inverted band's controls — moves `#171717 → #693ee0` on hover in 0.3s. It's the signature state move. See [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-hover.png) and [hero-submit](components/crops/hero-submit-hover.png).
3. **Do ink the display, slate the lede.** ABC Normal headings sit in `#171717`; the supporting lede takes `#566b76`, fine print `#3a4554`. See [hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png) and [hero-sub](components/crops/hero-sub-default.png).
4. **Do divide with hairlines.** `#e0e0e0` on cards and menu rows, `#e4edf1` on fields, `#ccd9e0` on the playbook chip — not shadows. See [plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png).
5. **Do mix radii by control.** 4px nav CTA, 8px big CTAs and secondaries, 5px contact fields, 40px badge and 48px toggle pills — not one radius everywhere. See [plan-toggle](components/crops/plan-toggle-default.png) and [saving-badge](components/crops/saving-badge-default.png).
6. **Do paint the dark bands, don't toggle a theme.** The top banner is ink and the feature cards #29007a purple inside a light page; white fills + ink text for the inverted CTA. See [top-banner](components/crops/top-banner-default.png) and [invert-cta](components/crops/invert-cta-default.png).
7. **Don't style the product from this package.** Every value is the public surface's; the workflows app behind login.copy.ai is uncaptured — and the login card's own tokens are a separate system, labelled as such.
8. **Don't use the legacy swatches.** `core.color.legacy` is the old design's declared palette kept for provenance; nothing paints it.
