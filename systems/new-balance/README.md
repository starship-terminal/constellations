# New Balance design reference

A token set for the New Balance UK storefront (newbalance.co.uk), read signed out on 2026-11-19. Surfaces: the home page, the men's listing, one product page (530 LACE), customer care, and the login form. The account was signed out. Nothing was added to bag, checked out, or submitted.

The live browser was the T3 preview tab. Its pane measured innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731, devicePixelRatio 2. That is the width these numbers belong to. It is not a 1440 or 390 capture. The preview browser could not produce screenshot frames. A headless Chromium load of the homepage returned HTTP 403, so that session was not used.

The storefront is a Salesforce Commerce Cloud site (`Sites-NBUK-Site`) on a white page, a near-black promo bar, and a red NB wordmark. Body type is Proxima Nova at 14px, weight 300. Category and help titles use ITC Garamond. Buttons on `.page.design-refresh` are near-black or white, with a 2px radius. The red `#cf0a2c` is the logo and a declared primary, not the button that was on screen.

### Principles

1. Do paint primary buttons near-black on the design-refresh pages.
2. Do keep button corners at 2px and fields at 4px.
3. Do set body copy in Proxima Nova at 14px, weight 300.
4. Don't treat `#cf0a2c` as the button fill on these pages.
5. Don't add a dark theme from the `.dark-mode` button rules.
6. Don't submit the login form or Add to Bag to finish a capture.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the CSSOM on the home page, from stylesheets the page allowed. `fast.fonts.net` was cross-origin and blocked. Computed styles were read with `getComputedStyle` on the home page, `/men/`, the 530 LACE product page, `/customer-care/`, and the login page. Button hover, disabled and keyboard-focus colours were read from `global.css` rules, not from a live pointer state. The wordmark path was copied from `symbol#logo` in the DOM.

Cookies were accepted with the OneTrust Accept All Cookies control so the pages underneath could be measured. No other form was submitted.

## What to distrust

- Screenshot-dependent claims. There are none. Do not invent a layout from these notes.
- Footer link colour computed to `#000000` on the login page. That is likely the body colour, not a designed footer link. It is not a token.
- Help side links on customer care computed to `rgb(230, 230, 230)`. That is what the page returned. It may be a colour meant for a dark panel that was not the visible ground. It is not a token.
- `.nb-button.button-primary` in the stylesheet is red `#cf0a2c`. Every captured page also has `.design-refresh`, which paints that button `#151415`. The red is the brand token. The near-black is the button that was on screen.
- `--nb-color-black` is `#151415`. The banner and buttons computed to `rgb(21, 20, 21)`, which is `#151415` as well (21, 20, 21). Those match.
- ITC Garamond and Proxima Nova are commercial faces. The style guide does not load them.
- Spacing steps `--nb-spacing-01` through `--nb-spacing-11` are declared. Control padding was also measured (16px by 12px on buttons, 28px top on fields) and does not only use that scale.
- The viewport was 1169 wide. A 1440 layout may reflow the same components.

## Fonts

Proxima Nova W01 is the body, nav, button, field and product-title face. Weights 300, 400, 500 and 700 were loaded. Weights 100, 600 and 800 were in the font list and not loaded. ITC Garamond W01 is the category title and the Contact Us heading, weight 500, loaded. Both are licensed. Tokens name them and fall back only where the computed style already named a fallback (the body stack). Do not ship the font files.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint primary buttons near-black on the design-refresh pages.** Add to Bag and Log in were `#151415` with white text. The red is the wordmark.
- **Do keep button corners at 2px and fields at 4px.** That is the computed radius, not a pill.
- **Do set body copy in Proxima Nova at 14px, weight 300.** Category titles switch to ITC Garamond.
- **Don't treat `#cf0a2c` as the button fill on these pages.** It is `--nb-color-primary` and the logo. `.design-refresh` overrides the red button rule.
- **Don't add a dark theme from the `.dark-mode` button rules.** Those rules exist. No captured page used them as the site theme.
- **Don't submit the login form or Add to Bag to finish a capture.** Both were left unpressed.
