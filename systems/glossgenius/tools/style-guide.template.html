Palette

Three vocabularies stay separate: the marketing Webflow layer under core (color.primary.*, color.super.*, the flat bg.* names and the stock apps.* collections — the [data-theme="dark"] declarations are evidence, never painted), the app's glossDS layer under app and the booking site's family under booking. Measured roles alias the declared values they match, inside their own set.

Radius and motion

Radii run from small control radii through card radii to the fully-round 1000px/999px pill the surfaces put on every button (the app declares it: --button-default-radius: 1000px). The control transition is the measured value on nav links and buttons.

Type

Basel Grotesk carries UI text (--font-family-ui), Basel Classic the display face (--font-family-display), Inter the base stack (--font-family-base) and Fraunces/Beau Rivage the editorial faces (--font-fraunces, --font-beau-rivage); the marketing sizes are measured on real components.
