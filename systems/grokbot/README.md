# Grok Bot design reference

A token set and component reference for Grok Bot, X Corp's iOS app for messaging AI agents ("Bots"; built by Anysphere, https://x.ai/bot). Use it to build a native iOS chat or agent app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-black or near-white page, grouped grey cards, iOS 26 glass controls floating over the content, one colour per bot that tints its avatar, its switches and the owner's bubbles, and a primary button drawn in the theme's ink rather than an accent. It was captured from the shipped app, version 1.16.0 on iOS 26, on 2026-10-23, through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account on a paid plan. Dark is the phone's own appearance; light was set with the app's Settings > Appearance > Light and put back afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 58 captures: 37 in dark (home, threads, menus, sheets, settings, search, bot pages, loading and empty states) and 21 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) nothing in them is masked: they show the owner's bots, chats and account, and no credentials or personally identifiable data.

This package contains no Grok Bot code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are X Corp's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the blob characters, icons or brand colours.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export grokbot` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do give every bot one colour and let it carry the bot's identity.
2. Do draw the primary action in the theme's ink, not an accent.
3. Do float controls as glass over the content instead of drawing bars.
4. Don't fill destructive actions red.
5. Do group a sender's messages into one run.
6. Do show focus with the caret alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Grok Bot is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 139 named readings, most in both themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a gradient, a thin mark) | Sampled | The dominant pixel, not an exact fill; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height |
| By eye | Reconstruction | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font family |

Scale steps (spacing, radii, the 52 pt field) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in dark only (the owner's bubble, the code block), the light token is Derived and says what it was filled from.

## What to distrust

- **The font.** Not determinable from pixels. The shapes are a neo-grotesque close to the iOS system face, and the measured sizes land on 22, 17, 16, 15, 14, 13 and 12 pt, which fits it, but `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured.
- **Glass.** The nav buttons, title pill, composer, search bar and menus are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it (the menu reads warm over the orange and pink avatars). Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: cards read 12 to 15 pt (14 used), bubbles 20.5, joined bubble corners 8.5, menus 30.7, sheets 36 to 38.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Light covers 21 of 58 screens.** The owner's bubble, code block, filter menu, Usage and the empty and loading states were captured in dark only; their light tokens are Derived.
- **The bot colour rule was read on two bots.** The purple bot's switch (#9158FE to #6F44C3) and the amber bot's own-message bubble (#FF9700 to #C37300) both darken the avatar colour to about 77%. Other bots are assumed to follow it.
- **The one-to-one bubble is a warm grey in dark** (#232020) where the group bubble is neutral (#202020). Only one one-to-one thread was read, so this may depend on the bot.
- **The neutral ramp is a list.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the two themes use, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published ramp.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; Delete was not tapped, so its confirmation (if any) is unseen.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass and avatars band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do give every bot one colour and let it carry the bot's identity.** The avatar, the owner's bubbles in that bot's thread and the switches on its pages all use it, darkened for fills. The system blue appears only on unread dots, the unread pill and the update card. See [01-home](screenshots/dark/01-home.png), [37-own-bubble](screenshots/dark/37-own-bubble.png) and [12-bot-profile](screenshots/dark/12-bot-profile.png).
2. **Do draw the primary action in the theme's ink, not an accent.** Create is a white capsule in dark and a black one in light, and the send pill inverts the same way; no button is ever filled blue. See [05-new-bot-typed](screenshots/dark/05-new-bot-typed.png), [05-new-bot-typed light](screenshots/light/05-new-bot-typed.png) and [10-composer-typed](screenshots/dark/10-composer-typed.png).
3. **Do float controls as glass over the content instead of drawing bars.** Back and more are glass circles, the title is a glass pill, and the composer and search bar are glass capsules; the thread scrolls under all of them. See [06-chat](screenshots/dark/06-chat.png) and [01-home](screenshots/dark/01-home.png).
4. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Delete, Hide and Sign Out are red text with a red outline icon in an ordinary menu or card. See [11-chat-more-menu](screenshots/dark/11-chat-more-menu.png), [33-bot-context-menu](screenshots/dark/33-bot-context-menu.png) and [22-settings-bottom](screenshots/dark/22-settings-bottom.png).
5. **Do group a sender's messages into one run.** Consecutive bubbles stack 8 pt apart with tighter joined corners, and a group chat labels the run once with the sender's name and a small avatar. See [07-chat-bubbles](screenshots/dark/07-chat-bubbles.png) and [30-group-chat](screenshots/dark/30-group-chat.png).
6. **Do show focus with the caret alone.** A focused field keeps its fill and rim and gains only a blue caret. See [04-new-bot-focus](screenshots/dark/04-new-bot-focus.png), [09-composer-focus](screenshots/dark/09-composer-focus.png) and [26-search-focus](screenshots/dark/26-search-focus.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/grokbot/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/grokbot/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass nav buttons, menus, sheets, switches and pop-up selects rather than redrawing them, and give each bot its own `color.avatar.*` with its fills darkened to about 77%. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
