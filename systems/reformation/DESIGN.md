# Reformation

Public clothing shop at https://www.thereformation.com, measured signed out on 2026-11-22. This file is handwritten because a generated DESIGN.md needs a catalog entry, and catalog.json was left alone.

Prices on the product page read Singapore dollars. The CSS viewport was 1440 by 900, `devicePixelRatio` 2.

## Color

| Role | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| page | #ffffff | body background |
| ink | #000000 | body, wordmark, add to bag |
| on-ink | #ffffff | add to bag type, promo type |
| announcement | #000000 | promo bar |
| footer | #f7f7f7 | footer background |
| field | #ffffff | login email field |

## Typography

Newtime R at 16px, weight 400, for body and nav. Newtime R Book at 14px on the promo bar and the price, and at 18px on the product title. Hero line is TimesNow at 48px, white. Font files are not in this package.

## Shape

Promo bar 32px. Header 88px, nav row 56px. Wordmark 595 by 79 on the homepage. Add to bag 44px, square corners. Login field 44px, 1px ink underline. Colour swatch 32px, round.

## Do and don't

Keep the page white and the type black. Use the black bar only for the promo line. Stop before sign in, check status, and sign up.
