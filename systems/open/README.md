## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export open` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint marketing type in cream `#eeece7` on black `#000000`.
2. Do paint the light pages black on cream `#eeece7`.
3. Do draw the primary control as a 60px pill with radius 999px.
4. Do set labels in 12px Semi-Mono, uppercase.
5. Don't use white for marketing text.
6. Don't invent a focus ring.
7. Don't enter Apple, phone, card, or booking from this package.
8. Don't ship Monument Grotesk.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The entry stylesheet on o-p-e-n.com declares colour utilities (`.color-black`, `.color-cream`, `.color-copper`, and the rest of that set) and type utilities (`.typo-display-mono`, `.typo-title-l`, `.typo-bodycopy`, `.typo-tag-s-mono`). Those rules were read from the live page's CSSStyleSheet. Component colours were read with `getComputedStyle` on `/`, `/about`, `/faq`, `/signin`, `/signin?auth=check-email`, `/in-person`, `/all`, `/global`, and `/privacy`.

Screenshots were taken in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844, device pixel ratio 2, after the same URLs loaded without a challenge. The T3 preview browser read the DOM and did not produce screenshot frames (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`).

Two families are loaded from the site: Monument Grotesk Regular and Monument Grotesk Semi-Mono, both woff2 from the origin. Computed weight on every measured control was 400.

## What to distrust

The preview tab was 1169px wide, not 1440. Pixel sizes in the CSS readings are from that width. The screenshots are a separate 1440 and 390 pass. A control whose size changes with the viewport can differ between the two.

The header clock renders the machine timezone. The first screenshot pass showed `+08`. That is the capture host, not a product token, and it is not copied into the tokens.

`.typo-display-l` in the stylesheet is 80px / 73.6px / -2.4px. The About title computed 80px / 96px. The token uses the computed value.

Sign-in primary buttons computed 12px Semi-Mono even though `.o-btn--primary` declares 18px Regular. The trial pill at `/global` computed 18px. Both are recorded. Neither was averaged.

Named inks that were not painted on these pages (steel blue, sage, piper, sepia, citrus, sapphire, red, and the cream percentage steps other than 60 and 40) are in `core.color` from the stylesheet. They are not semantic roles.

Legal pages use white `#ffffff` on black. Marketing pages use cream `#eeece7` on black. They are not the same text colour.

Focus on the email field and Next computed `outline: none`. A 2px cream `focus-visible` outline is declared on event-booking controls that were not opened.

Motion durations are from stylesheet rules. `motion-probe.mjs` was not run, so there is no reduce verdict.

## Fonts

Monument Grotesk Regular and Monument Grotesk Semi-Mono are the product faces. The site serves them from `/MonumentGrotesk-Regular.woff2` and `/MonumentGrotesk-Semi-Mono.woff2`. They are not in this package. The token fallback is Helvetica Neue, then Helvetica, which is the stack the stylesheet declares after the product family.

Computed weight was 400 only. No medium or bold face was on the captured pages.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint marketing type in cream `#eeece7` on black `#000000`.** The home header, hero, and footer all computed that pair. [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png)
- **Do paint the light pages black on cream `#eeece7`.** FAQ and sign-in set `html--light` and that page colour. [screenshots/faq-light-default.png](screenshots/faq-light-default.png)
- **Do draw the primary control as a 60px pill with radius 999px.** Try for free, Continue with Apple, and Try Open all computed that height and radius. [components/crops/button-primary-glow.png](components/crops/button-primary-glow.png)
- **Do set labels in 12px Semi-Mono, uppercase.** Sign in, DIGITAL, and the email label all computed that. [screenshots/signin-email-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-email-light-default.png)
- **Don't use white for marketing text.** White `#ffffff` is the legal page. Marketing text is cream. [screenshots/privacy-dark-default.png](screenshots/privacy-dark-default.png)
- **Don't invent a focus ring.** The email field and Next computed outline none. [components/crops/text-field-focus.png](components/crops/text-field-focus.png)
- **Don't enter Apple, phone, card, or booking from this package.** Those steps were left alone. [screenshots/signin-light-default.png](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)
- **Don't ship Monument Grotesk.** Name it, and fall back to Helvetica Neue, Helvetica.
