# Patterns

The public site is a white marketing page with a sticky wordmark nav, a large left-aligned heading, and a black pill next to a ghost pill. The homepage hero sits beside a product illustration. That illustration is demo art (a fictional session named "Add enterprise SSO"), not a signed-in account.

Pricing is two cards under a segmented control. Teams is the recommended card at $80 per month. Enterprise asks you to talk. The Individual tab was left unselected.

Login and signup are a centered card on `#fcfcfc`: wordmark, three identity-provider buttons, an OR rule, an empty email field, and a dark submit button. The branch stopped on that empty card.

The phone homepage keeps the same heading and pills, and hides the nav links behind a full-screen drawer.

Empty sales forms on `/demo` and `/contact` use stacked labelled fields and a small black Submit. They were not submitted. The 404 is a centered blue numeral with two pills.
