## Type

Skandia for marketing UI and headings. The home h1 paints 36px, weight 400, line-height 43.2px, tracking -1.08px. The pricing h1 paints 64px, which matches `--text-dt-display` at 4rem. Body is 16px. The auth wall uses Inter. Geist Mono and STK Bureau Serif are declared and were not the painted marketing body. The font files are licensed and are not in this package.

## Radius

Nav buttons paint a pill. The declared scale is 0.25rem, 0.375rem, 0.5rem, 0.75rem, 1rem, and 1.5rem.

## What this file does not claim

Hover, focus, pressed, disabled, and dark theme were not measured. The auth wall is a separate app. Do not style it from the marketing buttons. The Individual pricing tab was not switched.
