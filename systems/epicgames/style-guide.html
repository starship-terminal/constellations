Palette

core.color.palette carries the eleven declared hue ramps (amber, blue, brown, cyan, green, indigo, lime, orange, pink, purple, red, yellow); the semantic --color-* layer resolves in the dark set: backgrounds (default #101014, the elevated ramp, the fill ladder, backdrops), foregrounds, borders, neutrals and the transparent scales. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, size and radius

--size-dimension-* is the spacing/size scale, --size-borderRadius-* runs 2-32px plus the 9999px pill, --size-borderWidth-* the stroke widths, --size-font/lineHeight/letterSpacing the text scales. Measured radii: 6px nav buttons, 8px cards, 10px CTAs, 9999px fields and pagers.
