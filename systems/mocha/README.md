# Mocha design reference

Tokens and component notes for Mocha, captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. Two themes on the docs site, light and dark. Signed out the whole time.

Mocha (getmocha.com) was an AI app builder. On this date the apex has no DNS A record, and mocha.app redirects to it and fails. The product hosts (app, login, auth, dashboard, studio, build on mocha.app) return HTTP 410 with one static page: "This app is no longer available." The live designed surface is the public docs at docs.getmocha.com, a Mintlify site. Nothing was signed into. No form was submitted. No payment, card, phone, or government ID was requested or given, because those flows are gone.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export mocha` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint docs on `#f9f7ef` in light and `#1a1a1a` in dark.
2. Do use `#513a06` as the light accent and `#b4ff6a` as the dark accent.
3. Do draw the primary control as a 12px radius, 36px tall face.
4. Do keep the shutdown page on `#f7f5f1` with a 999px pill.
5. Don't invent a marketing homepage.
6. Don't treat the 410 page as a theme of the docs.
7. Don't follow Go to Mocha, Support, or the shutdown announcement into a signup or a payment.
8. Don't ship Paper Mono.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

docs.getmocha.com declares RGB triplets on `:root`: `--primary` (81 58 6), `--primary-light` (180 255 106), `--primary-dark` (0 0 0), `--background-light` (249 247 239), `--background-dark` (26 26 26), and `--gray-50` through `--gray-950`. `html` carries `light` or `dark` from `prefers-color-scheme`. Those names were read as computed custom properties on `/welcome` in both themes.

Component colours were read with `getComputedStyle` on `/getting-started/faq` (the Go to Mocha face, search, current sidebar link, FAQ summary, shutdown callout, theme menu, search dialog) and on the 410 page at app.mocha.app. Screenshots were taken in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844, device pixel ratio 2, then quantised to 256 colours. Motion was read with `tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs` on `/welcome`, the FAQ, and the 410 page.

A second pass on 2026-11-21 opened `https://getmocha.com/` first. Chromium reported `net::ERR_NAME_NOT_RESOLVED`. `https://getmocha.com/login` failed the same way. `dig` returned no A or AAAA record. The product hosts that do resolve (`app`, `login`, `auth`, `dashboard`, `studio`, `build` on mocha.app, plus vidsync.mocha.app) still return the 410 page. docs.getmocha.com still serves the same 35 pages. Custom properties on `/welcome` matched the 2026-11-19 reading (`--primary` 81 58 6, `--background-light` 249 247 239, `--background-dark` 26 26 26, the gray ramp). Those values were not rewritten. The new shots are in [screenshots/](screenshots/). The map is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md). The walk is [login-review.json](login-review.json).

## What to distrust

The marketing site is not in this package. getmocha.com does not resolve, so home, pricing, and the shutdown blog were not measured. Wayback has copies. Those are not this capture.

The 410 page is one light theme. It does not use the docs tokens. Its colours live under `core.color.shutdown`.

The Go to Mocha anchor is transparent. The black or white fill is a child span. A reading of the anchor's background is not the button.

Search-dialog and theme-menu screenshots were taken after a click. They are the product's own UI, empty. No query was typed.

oklab() values in the nav-active and dark callout tokens are the computed colours the browser returned. They are not converted.

## Fonts

Docs body is Inter, then system-ui. `--font-paper-mono` names Paper Mono, then ui-monospace. Inter is loaded in the style guide from Google Fonts. Paper Mono is not shipped here. The 410 page also asks for Inter, then system-ui.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint docs on `#f9f7ef` in light and `#1a1a1a` in dark.** That is `--background-light` and `--background-dark`. [screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png](screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png)
- **Do use `#513a06` as the light accent and `#b4ff6a` as the dark accent.** Those are `--primary` and `--primary-light`. [components/crops/nav-active-light.png](components/crops/nav-active-light.png)
- **Do draw the primary control as a 12px radius, 36px tall face.** Light face is black. Dark face is white. [components/crops/button-primary-light.png](components/crops/button-primary-light.png)
- **Do keep the shutdown page on `#f7f5f1` with a 999px pill.** It is a different palette from the docs. [screenshots/shutdown-light.png](screenshots/shutdown-light.png)
- **Don't invent a marketing homepage.** The apex does not resolve. [screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png](screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png)
- **Don't treat the 410 page as a theme of the docs.** It does not read those custom properties. [screenshots/shutdown-light.png](screenshots/shutdown-light.png)
- **Don't follow Go to Mocha, Support, or the shutdown announcement into a signup or a payment.** Those hosts are down or off the captured surface. [screenshots/shutdown-light.png](screenshots/shutdown-light.png)
- **Don't ship Paper Mono.** It is named, not included. [screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png](screenshots/docs-welcome-light.png)
