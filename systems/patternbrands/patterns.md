# Pattern Brands patterns

## Home

One column of content, max 736px, inside 40px page padding at 1440 and 20px at 390. Logo left of the heading on desktop, stacked on phone. Three brand cards in a row at 1440, stacked at 390. The green footer closes the page. Screenshots: [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-footer.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-footer.png).

## Legal

Terms and privacy are long single-column pages in the same type and the same footer. No sidebar, no table of contents. Screenshots: [terms-light-default.png](screenshots/terms-light-default.png), [privacy-light-default.png](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png), [mobile-terms-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-terms-light-default.png).

## Error

Unknown paths, including `/login`, render Webflow's Page Not Found: a document icon, a title, one sentence, and a green Go Home pill. Screenshot: [notfound-light-default.png](screenshots/notfound-light-default.png).

## Not present

No app shell, no search, no filter, no list or table, no onboarding, no toast, no destructive confirm. Feedback after an action is limited to the button hover and the card hover.
