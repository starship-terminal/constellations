# Wrike patterns

Public marketing patterns from www.wrike.com, 2026-11-21. The app behind login is not in these shots.

## Shell

A light top banner, then a sticky header: wordmark, three menu buttons, Pricing, Enterprise, Contact Sales, a language button, Log in, and a green Try Wrike for free button. See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

Opening Products, Solutions or Resources replaces the hero with a full-width panel and a sticky Try / Contact Sales bar. See [home-light-products-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-products-menu.png), [home-light-solutions-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-solutions-menu.png), [home-light-resources-menu.png](screenshots/home-light-resources-menu.png).

The phone shell collapses that nav into one icon button. The open menu is a stack of rows plus an email field that was not filled. See [mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero

A 64px heading, ink `#162136`, with the phrase "powered by" in accent green. A green primary button sits beside a blue text link. A product illustration occupies the right side. A chat card (Kate) sits over the illustration and was not used.

## Pricing and enterprise

Pricing and Enterprise are public pages with the same header. See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [enterprise-light-default.png](screenshots/enterprise-light-default.png). Plan calls to action use the same green button. No plan was started.

## Contact

Contact Sales is a public form. One field was focused and not submitted. See [contact-sales-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-sales-light-default.png).

## Login wall

Log in leaves the marketing site for https://login.wrike.com/login/. The first screen asks for an email or corporate ID. The branch stops there. See [login-wall-light-default.png](screenshots/login-wall-light-default.png) and [login-review.json](login-review.json).

## Language

The EN button opens a two-column list of locales. See [home-light-language.png](screenshots/home-light-language.png).
