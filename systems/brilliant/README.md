# Brilliant design reference

A token set, component reference and pattern guide for Brilliant's web app, for building mockups in Figma or Paper that match the product. It was extracted from brilliant.org on 2026-09-25, signed in to a new free account on the Math path, in the app's light and dark themes, at desktop width and (for public pages) at a 390px mobile width. It was refreshed on 2026-10-17 on a second free account: Home, Topics, You, settings (with the deactivate-account page), Koji, the header menu and the lesson quit dialog were copied from the signed-in app and measured headless in both themes at 1440 and 390, every component state was forced and read, and the stylesheet was read for font stacks, focus rings and reduced motion.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export brilliant` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do give each screen one filled main button and keep every other action grey, outlined or text.
2. Do let the main button's colour say what the moment is.
3. Do keep the blue, pink and yellow iridescent gradient for Premium.
4. Do show the main button disabled until the step can go ahead.
5. Do mark the learner's place by lighting one step and dimming the rest.
6. Do keep uppercase for small tags.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Brilliant styles its app with Panda CSS, which writes every token onto `:root` as a custom property (`--panda-colors-bg-primary`, `--panda-radii-2xl`, `--panda-shadows-elevation-base`) and overrides the colour ones under `[data-theme="dark"]`. The token file is built from a dump of those variables, so every palette step, semantic colour role (text, background, border, icon), spacing step, z-index and gradient, the ten Panda radii and five of the eight shadows trace to a named variable. The other radii and shadows, the accent roles and the component colours were read from computed styles or stylesheet rules, and say so in their source. Type styles come from Panda's `textStyle` rules in the production stylesheet.

Component measurements are computed styles read live in Chrome for each state: hover and focus were held with the pointer or keyboard, selected and disabled states were reached in the product, and pressed states were caught mid-transition. The theme was switched by setting `data-theme` on the page, never in account settings. Values that could only be sampled from a screenshot are marked "estimated from screenshot"; in the token file that is one token, `size.icon-md`, and in the specs it is the canvas-drawn Koji suggestion cards and the iridescent frames on the pricing page.

The 2026-10 refresh read the app a second way. The signed-in pages were opened in the built-in browser pane, signed in by the owner, and each state was saved as a static copy (`tools/capture/signed-in/snapshot.js`, which inlines every stylesheet and replaces the learner's name, email and account id before the copy leaves the page). The copies were then rendered and measured headless: `measure.mjs` finds 49 component instances by `data-slot`, role or label and reads their computed styles at rest and with hover, focus-visible and pressed forced through the DevTools protocol, in dark and again with `<html data-theme="light">`. `css-rules.mjs` reads the production stylesheet for the font stacks, the reduced-motion rules, every focus rule and the button3d hover rules, and `layout.mjs` reads the page grids. Two groups are Derived rather than measured: accent hover and pressed (the product changes the button's opacity, not its colour, so those two tokens are the face composited at .85 over the page), and the solid fill and border of `feedback.info` (the app's info alert uses only a tinted fill and text, which are measured).

Panda defines no duration tokens that survive to production (`trs-dur_fast` compiles to invalid CSS), so `core.duration` lists the durations the app's transitions actually use, each with the rule it was read from. Springs are real variables: `--spring-smooth` is `cubic-bezier(.2,1.1,.36,1)` over .833s, with a `linear()` version in `easing-linear`.

The screenshots were converted from the display profile (Display P3 or the external monitor's) to sRGB and stored as 256-colour PNGs to keep the repo small. Flat UI colours are exact, but gradients can band slightly, so read exact values from the tokens, not from the images. The signed-in account's name and email were replaced with "Learner", "Example" and learner@example.com before every capture, and in 2026-10 the initials avatar shows "L".

## What to distrust

- Lesson copies from 2026-10 show an empty lesson frame, because the interactive renders in a shadow root that a DOM copy does not carry (the 2026-09 lesson screenshots stand in).
- The 390 copies were taken from the pane at its phone emulation, whose layout viewport came out 406 wide, and re-rendered at 390, so the header's right edge clips at 390 as it would on a narrow phone. The two phone full-page renders (`mobile-settings-dark-account-full`, `mobile-you-dark-empty-full`) came out 798 pixels wide, not 780.
- `feedback.info.fill` and `feedback.info.border` are Derived: the only info surface, the Settings info alert, has no solid fill and no border. `accent.hover` and `accent.pressed` are Derived too (the face composited at .85 opacity over the page).
- `size.icon-md`, the Koji suggestion cards and the pricing page's iridescent frames are estimated from screenshots, not read from CSS.
- 18 of the 2026-09 signed-in screenshots are stored at 1x (1384 pixels wide), not 2x: the dark course, courses, home, settings and You captures, the quit dialog pair, `lesson-dark-tutor-highlight` and `legacy-light-account-settings`. They and the 26 crops cut from them have half the resolution of the rest.
- The real radio was only read unchecked. The "selected" state in the Radio row of Coverage is the segmented control's; no checked radio was captured.

## Fonts

| Family | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| CoFo Brilliant | 400, 500, 700, with italics | All UI text |
| CoFo Robert | 500, with italic | Display headlines (`display.*` styles) |
| CoFo Semi Mono | 400, 500, 700 | Lesson interactive tiles |
| Source Code Pro | 400, 500, 700, with italics | Code |
| KaTeX_Main | 400, 700, with italics | Maths |
| Soleil | 400, 600, 700, 400 italic | Legacy Django pages only |

Each family's stack is the one the production stylesheet declares (`--panda-fonts-*`, read 2026-10): `'CoFo Brilliant', sans-serif`; `'CoFo Robert', sans-serif`; `'CoFo Semi Mono', monospace`; `'Source Code Pro', monospace`; `'KaTeX_Main', serif`. So if CoFo Robert fails to load, the product's display headlines fall back to a sans, not a serif. No `font-feature-settings` are set anywhere. The CoFo families are proprietary and are not committed. The style guide looks for them in a local `fonts/` folder next to it (gitignored) and falls back to Figtree and Georgia (its own choice, to keep the display serif), showing a notice when it does.

## Motion and reduced motion

Durations and easings are in `core.duration` and `core.easing`: 100ms for presses and the nav underline, 150ms for menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs and switches, 200ms for progress and bubbles, and an overshooting spring (`easing.spring-smooth` over `duration.spring`, 833ms) for the tutor panel and lesson transitions.

Preferences has a Reduce motion setting (On, Off, Auto). It sets `<html data-reduced-motion>`, and Brilliant writes every entrance, shake and height animation under Panda's `allowMotion` condition, so those 46 rules run only when the attribute is `false`: the wrong-answer shake, sheet slide-in and out, fade and reveal, slide-up, the You page blur-in and the spring height transitions. With it on, the end state appears at once. The operating system's reduce-motion setting additionally applies six `motionReduce` rules that set transitions to none or 0s. Both are recorded in `core.motion` and counted in [raw/css-rules.json](raw/css-rules.json).

## Layout

Breakpoints are Panda's (`core.breakpoint`, `lg` at 769px is the desktop switch). Content sits in one centred `layout.container` (1216px, 112px margins at 1440; 24px gutters at 390). There is no global column grid: Home is two columns (`layout.grid-home`, 384 and 592 with a 24px gap), Settings three (`layout.grid-settings`: side menu, 592px form, balancing column), and every page is one column on a phone. Layers are in `core.zIndex` (`app-bar`, `overlay`, `modal` and the lesson layers).

## Do and don't

1. **Do give each screen one filled main button and keep every other action grey, outlined or text.** Home and the course page have one blue "Start"; the lesson pairs its coloured button with a grey "Why?" or "Get help"; the quit dialog sets "Keep learning" over a red text-only "Quit"; Settings has one black "Add another email" under an outlined "Send verification". See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [lesson-dark-correct](screenshots/lesson-dark-correct.png), [modal-quit-dark](components/crops/modal-quit-dark.png) and [settings-light-account](screenshots/settings-light-account.png).
2. **Do let the main button's colour say what the moment is.** Blue starts a course or lesson, and onboarding and dialogs use the neutral primary (near-white in dark, black in light). After Check, a green "Continue" or a yellow "Try again" arrives with the panel border (green.700 or yellow.700) and Koji's bubble in the same hue. See [lesson-dark-correct](screenshots/lesson-dark-correct.png), [lesson-dark-retryable](screenshots/lesson-dark-retryable.png) and [course-light-default](screenshots/course-light-default.png).
3. **Do keep the blue, pink and yellow iridescent gradient for Premium.** It appears only on the "Go Premium" border, the promo banner, the "Explore" pill, the keys popover's "Unlock all lessons now" and the pricing page; course and lesson buttons are one flat colour. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [popover-keys-light](components/crops/popover-keys-light.png) and [pricing-light-fullpage](screenshots/pricing-light-fullpage.png).
4. **Do show the main button disabled until the step can go ahead.** Sign in, onboarding Continue, lesson Check and "Update personal info" sit full width in `bg.disabled` with `text.disabled` and no lift, then fill in place. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png), [onboarding-dark-subject-choice](screenshots/onboarding-dark-subject-choice.png) and [lesson-light-default](screenshots/lesson-light-default.png).
5. **Do mark the learner's place by lighting one step and dimming the rest.** The current path node or tile is in full colour with Koji or a "START HERE" tag on it; later steps get grey discs, dimmed art and `text.tertiary` or `text.disabled` labels. See [course-light-default](screenshots/course-light-default.png), [plan-dark-learning-path](screenshots/plan-dark-learning-path.png) and [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
6. **Do keep uppercase for small tags.** "LEVEL 1", "RECOMMENDED", "GR 4–5", "NEW", "START HERE" and "OR" are 12/13.2 700 uppercase with .48px tracking. Headings, buttons, nav and field labels are never uppercase ("Your learning paths", "Personal info", "Keep learning"). See [courses-light-default](screenshots/courses-light-default.png), [settings-light-account](screenshots/settings-light-account.png) and [plan-dark-learning-path](screenshots/plan-dark-learning-path.png).

```bash
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
```

```bash

```

```bash

```

`build_tokens.py` reads `raw/css-vars-public.json`, `raw/text-styles.tsv` and the 2026-10 readings (`raw/css-rules.json`, `raw/components-computed.json`, `raw/layout.json`) and writes `tokens.json`; component colours and their sources are listed in its `comp()` block, accent and feedback roles in `accent_feedback()`. `build_style_guide.py` renders `style-guide.html` from `tokens.json` and `tools/style-guide.template.html`, turning every token into a CSS variable so the page cannot drift from the file. `build_components_md.py` writes `components.md` from `raw/components-computed.json`, naming each value by its token, followed by `tools/components-2026-09.md` and `tools/components-gaps.md`. All three are deterministic: re-running them on this commit reproduces their files byte for byte. Edit the raw inputs or the builders, never the generated files. [patterns.md](patterns.md) is written by hand from the screenshots it links.

To capture again (2026-10 route): the owner signs in to brilliant.org in a browser pane; paste `tools/capture/signed-in/snapshot.js` into the page and call `__snap('<page>-dark-<state>', REDACT)` with the account's redaction rules from `~/.design-systems/brilliant-redact.json`; write each copy with `unsnap.py` (it refuses a copy that still holds a redacted string, and needs `SNAP_REDACT` naming the same rules file); list it in `shots.tsv`.

```bash
SNAP_REDACT=~/.design-systems/brilliant-redact.json python3 tools/capture/signed-in/unsnap.py <spill file> ~/.design-systems/snaps/brilliant
```

Then, with `PLAYWRIGHT` and `SNAPS` set:

```bash
tools/capture/signed-in/render-all.sh
```

```bash
node tools/capture/signed-in/measure.mjs && node tools/capture/signed-in/layout.mjs && node tools/capture/css-rules.mjs
```

```bash
python3 tools/capture/quantize.py "$SNAPS/renders" app-2026-10 && python3 tools/capture/quantize.py "$SNAPS/crops" components/crops/2026-10
```

`OUT=<dir> node tools/capture/public-help.mjs` captures the Help Center search states. `render.mjs` switches a dark copy to light by setting `data-theme="light"` on `<html>` only, so the lesson's Koji areas stay dark as they do in the product.

`tools/capture/` also holds the 2026-09 kit: the in-page helpers (`helpers.js`), the window screenshot cropper (`shot.sh`, with `wins.swift` to find the Chrome window), the sRGB converter (`srgb.py RAW_DIR SRGB_DIR`, which also writes the 256-colour screenshots), the crop list (`crops.py [SRGB_DIR]`, which writes `components/crops/`), and headless Playwright scripts for the variable dump (`vars.mjs`), public pages (`public.mjs`), pricing (`pricing.mjs`), icons (`icons.mjs`) and the style guide check (`sg.mjs`). They were written for one session: the Playwright scripts write next to this folder and take `PLAYWRIGHT` as the path to a Playwright install when `playwright` is not resolvable, and `shot.sh` needs `SP` pointing at the compiled window lister.

## Use in Figma

Import `tokens.json` with Tokens Studio and choose the Light or Dark theme. Set fonts to CoFo Brilliant (or Figtree as a stand-in) and CoFo Robert (or Georgia, if a serif stand-in matters more than matching the product's own sans-serif fallback) for display headlines.

The component to get right is the 3D button: a pill face lifted 4px over a same-hue darker lip, which drops to 0 on press over 100ms. Most cards are flat with a 2px border and large radii (20 to 40); the course card on Topics is 3D like the button, with a 16px radius and a 4px lip. Elevation is a soft, unoffset black glow: `shadow.base` is `0 0 15px` in 10% black on light and 70% black on dark. The lesson's Koji areas stay dark in the light theme. Anything the capture could not reach is listed under Gaps in [components.md](components.md#gaps).
