# Patterns

## Marketing shell

Two bars. A CoreWeave bar (Cloud, Forge, Contact sales) sits on black above the Weights & Biases header. The header is ink, with a wordmark, four hover menus, Docs, Pricing, and uppercase CONTACT, LOG IN, SIGN UP. See [screenshots/home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).

## Hero

Display type is Source Serif 4 at 72px, white, on `{color.ink}`. One teal gradient call to action. A quiet REQUEST DEMO sits under it. A code sample sits between the subhead and the button. The sample is a public demo, not a signed-in app.

## Pricing

Two cards on the privately hosted pricing page. Personal is $0/mo. Advanced Enterprise is a custom plan. A gold pill says cloud-hosted pricing moved to CoreWeave Forge. See [screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png).

## Contact form

A two-column page. Copy on the left, an empty form on the right, a gold gradient SUBMIT. The form was not submitted. See [screenshots/contact-dark-default.png](screenshots/contact-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

Forge splits the viewport. A light marketing panel on the left. A white form on the right: identity providers, then an email field, then a cobalt button. See [screenshots/login-light-default.png](screenshots/login-light-default.png). Signup is the same shell with fewer providers. See [screenshots/signup-light-default.png](screenshots/signup-light-default.png).

## Empty, loading, error

The app host redirected to a Forge shell that had not painted a form. That is the only loading state captured, and it was not kept as a screenshot because the frame was blank. No empty app list, no validation error, and no destructive confirmation were reached. Those sit behind sign-in.
