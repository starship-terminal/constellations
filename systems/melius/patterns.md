# Patterns

How the public Melius pages are built. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots).

## Marketing frame

[home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) is the signed-out home at 1440. A `#202020` bar holds the wordmark, Product, Solutions, Resources, Company, Creator Program, Enterprise, Pricing, a yellow Sign In and an orange Start for Free. Under it, a yellow announcement bar: "New: Ditto swaps in any avatar or style in any video", with Learn more and Try Ditto. The page ground is `#fafafa`. The hero is Reckless at 56px, "Create anything you imagine", over a fan of generated images. Get started for free is orange, 48px tall, 6px radius. Connect your agent is white with a hairline.

The nav is 56px and sits in a sticky slot. Lenis owns the scroll. A hover that opens Product also scrolls the page, so [home-light-product-menu](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png) shows the panel with the hero partly out of frame.

## Product menu

[home-light-product-menu](screenshots/home-light-product-menu.png). Hovering Product opens a dark panel: Features (Canvas, Explore, Playground, Cast, Ditto, Assets), Core platform (Web app, Desktop app, Mobile app, MCP, API, and CLI, Models), Integrations (ElevenLabs, Figma, Slack, Vibe), and a preview card. No menu link was followed.

## Pricing

[pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png). A dark island. "Flexible pricing for every stage of creative work." Individual and Team pills. Monthly and Annual, with Annual on and an orange "Save up to 20%" chip. Three white cards: Creator $17, Growth $43, Professional $248, the last ringed in orange. Start with Creator goes to `app.melius.com/billing`. That link was not clicked. The switch was not flipped.

## Auth cards

[login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png) and [signup-light-default](screenshots/signup-light-default.png). The product shell is visible behind a modal: dark sidebar, promo chip, a large generated still. The card is dark, radius about 16px, with the orange mark, "Log in to Melius" or "Create an account", a white Log in with Google or Sign up with Google, an email field whose placeholder is `you@company.com`, and a legal line. Nothing was submitted. Google was not clicked. The password field was never filled.

## Docs

[docs-light-default](screenshots/docs-light-default.png). A separate light surface. Orange mark, a search field, Ask Assistant, a black Go to Melius pill, and a sidebar. Body type is Inter. The page was left at the docs home.
