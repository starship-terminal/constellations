# Melius design reference

Tokens and component notes for Melius (melius.com), captured **2026-11-19**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. The marketing site paints one light theme, with a dark pricing island. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing`, `/enterprise`, `/about`, `/manifesto`, `/models`, `/brand`, `/contact`, `/creator-program`, `/product/ditto`, `/product/ai-image-generator`, `app.melius.com/login`, `app.melius.com/signup`, and `docs.melius.com`. Signed out the whole time. No form was submitted. No purchase. No OAuth.

Melius marketing is Next.js and Tailwind v4. Colour is a named hex palette on `:root` (`--color-orange` `#f04e23`, `--color-yellow` `#fff78b`, `--color-gray-dark-1` `#202020`, `--color-black` `#0e0e0e`, `--color-white` `#fafafa`). Display is Reckless Standard. UI is Ease Standard. Mono is Geist Mono. The signed-out app paints a dark card over the product canvas. Docs paint Inter on white.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

### Principles

1. Do paint the page on offwhite and the nav on gray dark 1.
2. Do set the hero in Reckless at 56px.
3. Do use orange for Start for Free and yellow for Sign In, both at 6px.
4. Do keep the announcement bar on the same yellow.
5. Do paint pricing as a dark island with white plan cards.
6. Don't invent a dark theme for the marketing site.
7. Don't submit the login form or follow Google.
8. Don't treat the phone headline as settled copy.
9. Don't ship the Reckless files.
10. Don't follow billing from a plan card.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-19, in the T3 preview browser at 1440×900 and in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844. Headless Chromium loaded the marketing site, the login card, the signup card and the docs with no Cloudflare challenge. Hover is a real pointer. Focus-visible is keyboard. Pressed is a forced `:active` pseudo-class, so no control fired. Stylesheets were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The marketing sheet declares the palette as hex. A later sheet redefines `--color-black` as `#000` and `--color-white` as `#fff`. The hero ink measured `rgb(14, 14, 14)`, so `core.color.black` keeps the declared `#0e0e0e`. A c15t consent stylesheet is in the harvest. No banner was clicked.

## What to distrust

- The signed-in canvas, billing, and Google. `app.melius.com/login` and `/signup` render a card over the product, and the sidebar is visible behind it. Nothing was submitted. Google was not clicked. `/billing` was not opened.
- The phone hero. At 390 the headline is still cycling words in both shots. The settled desktop line is "Create anything you imagine".
- The phone menu. The hamburger click did not open a drawer in the capture. [mobile-home-light-menu-open](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu-open.png) matches the closed shot.
- The home product menu shot is scrolled. Hover on Product opened the panel and Lenis moved the hero. The panel contents are the live DOM.
- Reckless, Ease and Geist Mono files. The family names are measured. The files are not in this package.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes.
- `--color-red-500` in the harvest stayed `lab()`. The token uses the declared hex `#fb2c36`.
- Pricing is a dark island on the same light theme. It is not a second theme.

## Fonts

Reckless Standard is the marketing display, weight 400, from the Next font stylesheet `398ln9hj2tk0d.css`. Ease Standard is the UI, weight 400, from the same sheet. Geist Mono is the mono. Inter is the docs UI (`--font-inter`). None of the files are shipped. The tokens name the families and fall back to Georgia, system sans, and system mono.
