Fingerprint, measured

The .dark scope is code, not a theme

The stylesheet's only dark redeclaration is the 11 --fpds-color-syntax-* names: inside a .dark scope (code blocks) they re-point at lighter FPDS ramp steps — keyword/number/null to purple-5, string to orange-6, comment to gray-6. Palette

Two honest layers: fpds.* keeps the declared H S% L% channel triplets; color.* is the resolved hex ramp the Tailwind utilities actually paint — orange 700 is the #f35820 brand primary. The semantic roles under light.theme.* alias the ramp; measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing, radius, layout and motion

The space base is 4px; radii run 2-24px. Content width caps at 1248px (--container-content-lg) inside the 448-1280px --container-* steps. Easing tokens cover the cubic and quart families; the default transition is 150ms ease-in-out.

Shadows

Type

Four served faces: Inter carries every heading, control and body; Petrona is the serif display face on editorial sections; JetBrains Mono draws code, prompts and signal values; Geist Pixel is the pixel display face. The --text-* ramp runs 2xs 11px to 6xl 48px with per-step line-heights and em-tracking. Sizes are the declared steps; samples fall back to system sans.
