# Patterns

## Marketing shell

A 72px announcement bar sits above an 88px header. On the home hero both are painted on `#2A222B`. On pricing the header sits on `#FAF9FB` and the announcement bar stays dark. Logo is 48px from the left edge. Nav items are 16px with 6px horizontal padding. The header does not stick in the readings. Page gutter on the hero container is 48px.

At 390 the nav collapses to the mark, a Sign up pill and a menu icon. The menu was not opened.

## Hero

Dark section, Tobias display at 80px / 80px / -1px, a 14px lavender eyebrow, a 20px lede, then a 48px light pill. Three cards sit under the fold of the first viewport.

## Pricing

Light page, same Tobias display in `#2A222B`. A billing pill group defaults to Yearly (Save 30%). Four plan cards in a row at 1440. Growth Flow carries a Free trial pill and the words "Credit card required". That card was not clicked.

## Sign-in

`admin.typeform.com/login` is a 1400px panel on `#F7F7F8`. The form column is 400px. Heading is 24px system-ui, not Tobias. Google and Microsoft are outlined 44px buttons. Email is a field. SSO is a text button. None of those were continued.

Signup starts at Google, Microsoft or email. The email path asks for a password and a terms checkbox, then "Create my free account". That form was not submitted.
