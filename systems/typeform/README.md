# Typeform

Typeform is a form builder (typeform.com). This package covers the public marketing site and the signed-out login, read on 19 November 2026. The painted theme is light, with a dark hero and footer. There is no separate dark theme on these pages. Widths are 1440 and 390. Nothing was submitted.

### Principles

1. Primary actions are a 12px pill in the page ink.
2. Secondary actions on the dark hero are an outline.
3. Display type is Tobias at weight 400.
4. The page is warm off-white with a dark hero.
5. Don't invent a hover colour.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles and custom properties were read in the T3 preview browser on www.typeform.com and www.typeform.com/pricing, at 1440 by 900, device pixel ratio 2, visual viewport scale 1. The same browser then opened admin.typeform.com/login and admin.typeform.com/signup. The email field on login was focused so the outline could be read. It was not submitted.

The stylesheet is `typeform-website.webflow.shared.8fcc5a9b2.min.css`. `:root` declares 245 custom properties. The resolved `:root` values match the live computed properties that were spot-checked (primary button `#2A222B`, page `#FAF9FB`, border `#E5E1E5`, radius sm `.75rem`, display size `5rem`).

Screenshots were taken with headless Playwright after the preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". That session loaded only www.typeform.com and www.typeform.com/pricing, which had already loaded in the preview browser without a Cloudflare challenge. The login host was not opened in Playwright. Shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at 2x, then quantized to 256 colours.

## What to distrust

The `dark` token set is the dark hero and footer, not a product dark theme. The site did not offer a theme switch on these pages.

Declared heading-display size is `5rem` (80px). The painted hero was 80px at 1440. At 390 the heading wraps and was not re-measured.

Twklausanne family names include a weight (`"Twklausanne 500"`) while computed `font-weight` stayed 400. The token records the computed weight.

Auth type is the system-ui stack, not Tobias or Twklausanne. `font.typeform.com/dist/font.css` is cross-origin and was not read.

The 256-colour screenshots band the hero gradient. Read colour from the tokens.

Growth Flow's struck price in the screenshot is a mid-animation frame. The card text in the DOM read "266 USD/mo after 14 days" and "Credit card required". That card was not clicked.

## Fonts

Tobias (400 loaded) is the display face, computed as `Tobias, "Times New Roman", sans-serif`. Proprietary. Not committed.

Twklausanne (350, 400, 500, 600 loaded) is the text face, with Arial in the stack. Proprietary. Not committed.

Inter (300 to 700) is loaded from Google Fonts on the marketing page. The measured marketing roles did not use it.

The login and signup pages compute the system-ui stack. The style guide falls back to Arial.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are a 12px pill in the page ink.** On a light header, Sign up and Get Basic are `#2A222B` with `#FAF9FB` type. On the dark hero the same control flips to a light fill. [components/crops/button-primary.png](components/crops/button-primary.png)
- **Secondary actions on the dark hero are an outline.** Contact sales is a 1px `#FAF9FB` border, 40px, radius 12px. On the light header that outline becomes `#2A222B`. [components/crops/button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png)
- **Display type is Tobias at weight 400.** The hero and the pricing title are 80px / 80px with -1px tracking. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **The page is warm off-white with a dark hero.** Paint `#FAF9FB` and `#2A222B`. The lavender eyebrow is `#ECCFFA`. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a hover colour.** Declared hover is `#564B58`. It was not painted. Focus on the marketing Sign up link is `#E59700`.
