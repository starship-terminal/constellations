# Patterns

## Marketing shell

A fixed 110px bar: Bottle, Travel Mug, Tap, Mission, Get Involved on the left, the wordmark in the centre, Impact report, Find a Store, For Businesses, EN and a cart icon on the right. The bar is transparent over the hero. Each left item opens a two-card panel. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-nav](screenshots/home-light-nav.png).

## Hero

Full-bleed blue field, cream display type in the Dopper face, a black pill and a cream outline pill. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Shop shell

The same marketing bar, then a second row: All products, Mugs, Bottles, Accessories, Spare parts, E-Gift Card, Last chance. The current item is blue. See [shop-light-default](screenshots/shop-light-default.png).

## Collection

A centred title, a green Filters pill, then a product grid. Filters open a blue sheet with Collection, Size and Colour. Sort By is a select (Position, Product Name, Price). See [shop-bottles-light-default](screenshots/shop-bottles-light-default.png) and [shop-bottles-light-filters](screenshots/shop-bottles-light-filters.png).

## Product

Breadcrumb, title, price, Add to cart, then a short feature list. See [pdp-light-default](screenshots/pdp-light-default.png).

## Empty cart

Blue title, an illustration, Back to shop. Continue shopping and Customer Service sit in a blue strip. The cart icon in the bar links straight to this page. There is no cart drawer. See [cart-light-default](screenshots/cart-light-default.png).

## Login wall

Reached only by the Magento URL, not from the public nav. Two cards: Registered Customers (email, password, Sign in) and New Customers (Create an Account). The branch stopped there. See [login-wall-light-default](screenshots/login-wall-light-default.png).

## Phone

The marketing menu becomes a blue full-screen list with a close mark. The shop category row wraps under the bar. See [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png) and [mobile-shop-light-default](screenshots/mobile-shop-light-default.png).
