# Dopper design reference

A token set and component reference for Dopper's public signed-out surface — the reusable water-bottle brand behind "Just Refill" — for building marketing pages and storefronts in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.dopper.com** (Next.js/Storyblok marketing) and **shop.dopper.com** (Magento 2, theme `Dopper/dopper_b2c`) on **2026-11-13**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: home, the mission story, the insulated product page, the blog, the shop home, the Bottles product list, the Pacific Blue product detail, the empty cart, the customer login, plus a 390px pass on home, shop and the product list. Prices render `EUR`, English locale only.

Dopper is worth a capture because a mission-driven e-commerce brand paints a single consistent system across two different stacks: Dopper (the custom display face) over Gilroy 400/600, cream `#fcfaf2` fields, deep-sea `#000f2e` ink, darkBlue `#0067e5` actions and bands, a `#fed200` yellow mega-menu, sand `#f6ecc8` footers, carribean `#0abeaa` filter accents and glowing-coral `#d0254a` badges — all declared as real `:root` custom-property ramps with three tints each, plus a declared 4/8/16/20px radius scale and expo/quint easings. The shop compiles the same palette to literals, measured here off real elements. **Boundary:** the customer account, checkout and the filled cart behind the login and cart cards were never reached and are never inferred; the marketing pages, storefront and the uncaptured account/checkout areas are three different surfaces.

## How the values were read

Every value came off the running signed-out sites in headless Chromium:

- **Tokens.** [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) captures every stylesheet body and inline `<style>` block and writes every custom property to [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json): the marketing app's own `:root` layer (`--darkBlue`, `--deep-sea`, `--sand`, `--yellow`, `--darkTeal`, `--carribean`, `--glowing-coral` and their `-1`/`-2`/`-3` tints, `--border-radius-xs/s/m/l`, `--touch-target-min`, `--z-*`, the `--ease-*/--bounce-*` easings — 44 product vars), resolved on a probe document. Vendor layers (HubSpot/Trellis, Cookiebot, widgets) are binned separately and excluded. The shop's compiled stylesheets declare no product custom properties, so shop colours are measured computed values. [tools/build_component_tokens.py](tools/build_component_tokens.py) writes the spec, [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) the sets.
- **Components.** [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opens each route on both hosts and reads `getComputedStyle`, painted layers and boxes per element in default, hover, focus-visible and a CDP-forced pressed state. The marketing nav lives inside `dopper-navigation`'s shadow root, so the measurer searches shadow DOM and re-opens the flyout for each child state; lazy home content is scrolled into view before reading. Results in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json) and [raw/measurements.md](raw/measurements.md); missing elements keep earlier evidence and are recorded as gaps.
- **Screenshots.** [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs) takes viewport and full-page shots per route with `.capture.json` sidecars, including the local UI toggles: the Bottle mega-menu on home, the mobile menu, and the mobile PLP filter sheet. Full-page shots are capped at eight chunks — the shop (~13k px), category (~11.9k px) and mobile pages exceeded it and were truncated or stitched, flagged in their sidecars. [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) posterises them to 256 colours.

Nothing was signed into, created, submitted or paid for: the login fields were focused and typed into, then cleared, and Sign in was never pressed; Add to cart, the PDP configurator links and every checkout step were measured or screenshotted, never followed. The Cookiebot dialog (Allow all / Allow selection / Deny) was hidden per capture, never answered — no consent was ever posted.

## What to distrust

- **This is the public surface, not the product's internals.** The customer account, order flow, checkout, the filled cart and the "For Businesses" B2B portal are uncaptured. Marketing pages describe the mission; nothing here verifies account behaviour.
- **Two hosts, one brand.** www.dopper.com (Next.js + Storyblok, `dopper-navigation` shadow-DOM header) and shop.dopper.com (Magento 2) share the header, palette and type, but the shop's values are compiled literals — marketing tokens are declared vars, shop tokens are measurements. A few same-named surfaces genuinely differ (header heights, menu treatment).
- **No dark theme.** Neither host switches themes; the darkBlue mobile menu and deep-sea/darkBlue bands are brand-painted surfaces of the light page.
- **The hero is video.** "Whatever the season" sits on a full-bleed video; screenshots freeze a frame and no token claims the footage's colours.
- **A few elements were not found.** product-badge, pdp-swatch, m-filter-sheet (latest pass), the flyout's inner CTAs (closed shadow root) and cart-empty in the latest pass are recorded as gaps — earlier evidence kept where it existed.
- **Shadow-DOM pressed states.** `CSS.forcePseudoState` cannot reach nodes inside `dopper-navigation`'s shadow root, so nav triggers and mobile menu items have default/hover/focus but no pressed reading — recorded, not absent.
- **Four entries were flagged by hand, not by a run.** `flyout`, `flyout-tab`, `flyout-card` and `m-filter-radio` carry `missing: true` and a note in raw/components-computed.json while keeping the states the selector misread (the static "Explore our solutions" section, a product link). raw/ is left as committed; components.md and raw/measurements.md now treat the four as missing and none of their states reaches a tally, a motion row or a token. The capture script now drops a flagged entry's states before re-measuring it. Recapture needed.
- **Three focus rings were never confirmed.** `nav-link`, `m-burger` and `m-menu-pill` focus-visible were recorded `via: focus failed`; their via was later rewritten by hand, not re-measured. They are treated as failed focus, and the focus tokens cite `btn-outline` and `collection-tab`, whose keyboard focus was confirmed. Recapture needed.
- **`cart-empty`** is the "Your cart" h1, kept from an earlier run when the latest pass did not find the empty-basket block (recaptureMissed); components.md flags it.
- **Role colours with no reading.** `light.text.muted` (#515a8a), `light.surface.card` (#f9f3df: the only readings are 0 x 0 footer chips), `light.surface.menu` and `light.action.warm` (#fed200) carry their declared variables only: no captured component paints them (the opened mega-menu was screenshotted, not measured).
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw files.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Fonts

Two served families do everything the surface paints: **Dopper** (regular — the display faces and the dopper. wordmark) and **Gilroy** (Medium/400 and Bold/600 on marketing, 400/600/800 on the shop, plus a `Dopper-Icons` icon font). The `@font-face` blocks and woff2 srcs are harvested in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). The fonts are licensed and not distributed with the package — tokens carry the stacks and the style guide falls back to system sans.
