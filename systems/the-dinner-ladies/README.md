# The Dinner Ladies

Public marketing site for The Dinner Ladies, an Australian ready-to-heat meal delivery shop at [dinnerladies.com.au](https://www.dinnerladies.com.au/). Captured signed out on 19 November 2026 from the live Shopify theme `dinnerladies-v12/main` (schema "The Dinner Ladies" 3.0.0). One light page. The black announcement bar and a few colour islands are the only dark paint. Measured at innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731, devicePixelRatio 2.

The preview browser attached to the page and returned computed styles. It did not produce screenshot frames, so this package has measurements and no page screenshots.

### Principles

1. Keep the page cream and the action red.
2. Set buttons square.
3. Set headings in Denim SemiBold, uppercase, with 0.6px tracking.
4. Mark a selected filter with the grey fill.
5. Leave checkout alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

The viewport is the preview pane at 1169 by 731, not a 1440 or 390 capture. Phone layout is unmeasured.

Screenshot frames failed with "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". Colours and type are from CSS, which is the source this repo wants, but there is no image to check the layout against.

`--brand-extended-yellow` is `#ffb402`. The sibling `--brand-extended-yellow-rgb` is `25, 180, 2`, which does not match. The hex is the token.

The nav computes `font-family: "Demin SemiBold"`. The `@font-face` family is `Denim SemiBold`. The misspelling is in the stylesheet.

`--media-radius` and `--text-boxes-radius` are 20px, and `--variant-pills-radius` is 40px. The painted cards, buttons and variant labels compute to 0px. The tokens keep both, and the component tokens follow the paint.

`--font-body-family` is declared twice (`'Denim-Regular'` and `'Denim Regular'`). The computed value is `"Denim Regular"`.

A few brand colours (`#790000`, `#932c00`, `#337bff`, `#7ebd4c`, `#efbfb9`) are declared on `:root` and were not seen as a fill on the pages read.

Reduced motion was not emulated. The stylesheets contain `prefers-reduced-motion` rules. Whether the page honours them is unmeasured.

## Fonts

Denim Regular (body, 400), Denim Medium (500) and Denim SemiBold (heading, 600) are loaded from the shop's own `@font-face` rules as woff and woff2. They are commercial faces and are not in this package. The tokens name them and fall back to `sans-serif`. Assistant and Poppins are also declared and were unloaded on the pages read. The newsletter field computes to Arial. Reviews.io icon fonts load for the review carousel.

## Do and don't

- **Keep the page cream and the action red.** The page is `#fff1e6` and the button is `#dd1f26`. A second red, `#dd1d1d`, paints the octagon menu button.
- **Set buttons square.** Primary buttons, cards and the search field compute to 0 radius. The 5px radius belongs to the cart pill and the newsletter controls.
- **Set headings in Denim SemiBold, uppercase, with 0.6px tracking.** Display size on the home and product pages is 48px.
- **Mark a selected filter with the grey fill.** Active chips and the selected size use `#dbe7ef` inside a black or ink border.
- **Leave checkout alone.** This capture did not order, did not open the cart, and did not enter an email or a phone number.
