# Spotify design reference

A token set and component reference for Spotify, the music and podcast streaming app, as it runs on iOS 26. Use it to build a native iOS media app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-black page where artwork carries the colour, dark grey tiles and cards, a floating mini player tinted by the playing track, Spotify green spent only on what is selected or playable, white and outlined pill buttons, and iOS 26 glass for chips, sheets and nav buttons. It was captured from the shipped app on 2026-10-23 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account on a Premium Family plan. Spotify for iOS has one appearance, dark, so this package has no light theme. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 22 dark captures: Home, Search (resting, browse grid, focused, typed, results), Your Library with its chips and sort sheet, the share sheet, context sheets, the profile drawer, Settings and Playback, and an artist page. Two more captures were taken and left out because they repeat another (see [Coverage](#coverage)). They show the owner's profile, library and messages, and the artists, playlists and podcasts Spotify offers. Other people are masked with [tools/redact-rules/spotify.json](../../tools/redact-rules/spotify.json): the friends offered as share targets (14, 15, 18), and a friend's listening activity and a message sender's avatar and name in the profile drawer (19). The crops cut from those screens (`share-targets`, `sheet-share-playlist`, `drawer-friends`) are rebuilt from the masked copies, so they show the row's layout without the people.

This package contains no Spotify code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Spotify's; the album art, podcast covers and artist photos in them belong to their owners and are kept for layout only ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, icons, artwork or Spotify green.

## Brief

### Principles

1. Do keep the page near-black and let artwork carry the colour.
2. Do spend Spotify green only on what is selected or playable.
3. Don't make buttons green pills.
4. Do tint now-playing surfaces from the artwork.
5. Do put an item's actions in a bottom sheet of icon rows.
6. Do change the search field's whole shape on focus.
7. Don't restyle system controls.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Spotify is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/spotify/raw-shots/dark/`, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 172 named readings. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a gradient, a thin mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag` and `diagr`, the inset along a top-left or top-right corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font family |

`diagr` is the one op this package adds to the kit: Spotify's shortcut tiles and promo card hold artwork at their top-left corner, so their radius is read at the top-right. Scale steps (spacing, radii, rounded sizes) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. The low-resolution layout references of Now Playing and a playlist page (outside the repo, see [Coverage](#coverage)) were used for layout notes in [patterns.md](patterns.md) only, never for a value.

## What to distrust

- **The font.** Not determinable from pixels. The letterforms are Spotify's own geometric sans, not the iOS system face; Spotify's typeface is not public. `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured.
- **Artwork-tinted surfaces are one reading of a moving value.** The mini player (`surface.miniPlayer`, #69501E) takes its colour from the playing track's artwork, and the share sheet and its preview card do the same (reddish over a lofi playlist in [14-share-sheet](screenshots/dark/14-share-sheet.png), green-black in [18-share-song-segmented](screenshots/dark/18-share-song-segmented.png)). Derive these from artwork; do not hard-code them.
- **Glass.** Unselected chips, the focused search field, Cancel, the round back button and every sheet are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it (the sort sheet reads blue and gold where artwork shows through). Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **Green text reads two ways.** On a solid panel the active label reads #20D361 (`text.accent`); in the glass sort sheet the selected option reads #1BB551 (`text.accentOnSheet`). Whether the glass dims it or Spotify uses a second green is not determinable from pixels.
- **Switches are not Spotify green.** They read #2ED158, the iOS system green, because the app keeps the stock switch.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: tiles 3.4 (4 used), cards and the mini player 8.5 (8 used), the search field 6.8 (6 used), sheets 34.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em; the real face's cap height is not known, so the whole scale may sit a point off. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **`text.tertiary` was read once**, on the DJ row's 'Tap to start'.
- **The neutral ramp is a list.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the dark set uses, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published ramp.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass, artwork and the tinted surfaces band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page near-black and let artwork carry the colour.** The page, tiles and cards are greys from #121212 to #333333; colour comes from album art, the browse categories and surfaces tinted by the artwork. See [02-home](screenshots/dark/02-home.png) and [06-search-grid](screenshots/dark/06-search-grid.png).
2. **Do spend Spotify green only on what is selected or playable.** The selected chip, the play button, the saved tick, pins, the tab underline, the slider's fill and the caret are green; no panel or bar is. See [11-library-chip-selected](screenshots/dark/11-library-chip-selected.png) and [23-artist-tabs-following](screenshots/dark/23-artist-tabs-following.png).
3. **Don't make buttons green pills.** Apart from the round play button, actions are white pills (Pre-save, Turn on), outlined pills (Following) or bare icons (shuffle, more, add). See [02-home](screenshots/dark/02-home.png), [23-artist-tabs-following](screenshots/dark/23-artist-tabs-following.png) and [19-profile-drawer](screenshots/dark/19-profile-drawer.png).
4. **Do tint now-playing surfaces from the artwork.** The mini player and the share sheet take the current artwork's colour instead of a fixed fill. See [02-home](screenshots/dark/02-home.png), [14-share-sheet](screenshots/dark/14-share-sheet.png) and [18-share-song-segmented](screenshots/dark/18-share-song-segmented.png).
5. **Do put an item's actions in a bottom sheet of icon rows.** A context sheet opens with the item's artwork and name, a divider, then a line icon and label per action; a sort sheet marks its choice in green with a tick. See [24-artist-context-sheet](screenshots/dark/24-artist-context-sheet.png), [25-track-context-sheet](screenshots/dark/25-track-context-sheet.png) and [12-sort-sheet](screenshots/dark/12-sort-sheet.png).
6. **Do change the search field's whole shape on focus.** At rest it is a white rectangle with a dark placeholder; focused it becomes a glass capsule with a green caret and a Cancel button. See [05-search](screenshots/dark/05-search.png) and [07-search-focused-recents](screenshots/dark/07-search-focused-recents.png).
7. **Don't restyle system controls.** Switches keep the iOS system green and shape, and back buttons are stock glass circles. See [21-settings-playback-switches-slider](screenshots/dark/21-settings-playback-switches-slider.png) and [20-settings](screenshots/dark/20-settings.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/spotify/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/spotify/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. Re-quantizing from the raw shots brings back the people masked in `screenshots/`: after `quantize.py`, run `python3 tools/redact_images.py apply systems/spotify --rules tools/redact-rules/spotify.json` from the repository root, confirm every edited file is still a 256-colour PNG with its alpha and that only pixels inside the rules' rectangles changed (the committed masks were painted that way, on each file's original palette and transparency), and only then run `tools/crops.py`. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.tile`) are the contract. Spotify has one appearance, so give each asset the same value for any appearance, or pin the app to dark. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for switches, glass buttons, sheets and the focused search field rather than redrawing them, and compute the mini player's fill from the current artwork. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Dark theme.
