# Exactly.ai design reference

A token set and component reference for Exactly.ai's public marketing site, plus the signed-in onboarding shell. It was read from **exactly.ai** on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing, /showcase and /showcase/revolut, /case-studies and one case-study article, /industries and /industries/saas-n-mobile-apps, the /help-center and its FAQ article, /book-a-demo at rest, plus 390px passes on home and pricing. On **2026-11-21** a signed-in session was already on `https://threads.exactly.ai/onboarding`. That pass captured the audience step at the same two widths and left the choices blank.

Exactly.ai is a Framer-published site, and it shows: the stylesheet declares a compact `--token-*` palette (the `#fb411f` accent, `#1e1e1e` ink, `#f7f7f7` canvas, `#404040` and `#7c7c7c` alpha washes, translucent whites for the photo surfaces) that the components' computed values resolve back to. Two served faces — **Inter** at wght 300 for the 47px/60px display and longform, **Inter Variable** for nav, CTAs, card titles and the footer. The whole public site paints one light theme; a single `prefers-color-scheme: dark` media rule repaints nothing. **Boundary:** the studio, model training, the image generator, credits and billing stay behind the unanswered onboarding step. The 2026-11-14 public capture measured Log in, Start for free and Book a demo and did not follow them. The 2026-11-21 pass found the session already signed in and stopped on the audience question. The help search was typed into and cleared, never submitted. The scheduler was measured at rest, never touched.

### Principles

1. Do spend the orange once.
2. Do keep the on-image recipe.
3. Do use the wght-300 display.
4. Do pill the actions.
5. Do pair the pill paints.
6. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
7. Don't invent a dark theme.
8. Don't treat wrapper colours as the product's.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## Do and don't

1. **Do spend the orange once.** `#fb411f` (`light.accent`) fills the Start-for-free pill on interior pages and the For-free chip — on the photo hero the same action paints white instead (`light.pill.light`). Everything else is black, white or grey. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and the orange variant inside [compare-bar](components/crops/compare-bar-default.png).
2. **Do keep the on-image recipe.** Over photography, text is white at 100/60/40% (`light.on-image.*`), pills are solid white or 10% glass with a 20% white edge — never tinted. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
3. **Do use the wght-300 display.** Headings are Inter at weight 300, 47px/60px with −1.88px tracking on desktop, 27px/36px on mobile — a light voice, not a bold one. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
4. **Do pill the actions.** CTAs, nav chips and the compare bar are 1000px pills; cards take 40px, the drawer shell 100px. See [plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png).
5. **Do pair the pill paints.** Light surfaces use the dark `#1e1e1e` pill with white ink (`light.pill.dark`); over imagery it's the white pill with black ink (`light.pill.light`). The orange accent is reserved for the top-level conversion action. See [plan-card](components/crops/plan-card-default.png).
6. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** The 2026-11-21 pass reached only the unanswered onboarding shell on threads.exactly.ai. The studio, generator and account surfaces stay behind that step.
7. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site paints light only; the near-black footer is a section, not a theme. See [footer](components/crops/footer-default.png).
8. **Don't treat wrapper colours as the product's.** Several Framer anchor wrappers report the browser's default link blue/pressed red while the painted text inside carries the real value — read the text node's colour, as each entry's Text row does.

## Known gaps

- `showcase-chip`, `read-more` and `help-search`: keyboard focus never reached them, so their `focus-visible` readings are the element at rest: components.md leaves them out and `raw/measurements.md` shows them as Skipped. Needs a recapture; `measure.mjs` now records such a state as skipped (edited, not run).
- `article-quote` is a stale entry: a 2026-11-13 run marked it missing and the script no longer measures it, but the prune runs only on a full run, so it stays in `raw/components-computed.json` and components.md until the next one.
- `light.focus.ring` (#101010) and `light.focus.ring-mobile` (#e59700) are `outline: auto` rings in the colours Chrome uses for its own focus ring on the desktop pass and under the phone emulation. The site sets no focus colour; the tokens say so in their source.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.
- **Signed-in product, 2026-11-21.** A signed-in session opened on `https://threads.exactly.ai/onboarding` and stopped on the audience step. The four choices, Log out, the help link and the chat bubble were visible and not used. Studio, settings, billing and later onboarding steps are Not captured. App colour roles were read from computed custom properties into [raw/signed-in-vars.json](raw/signed-in-vars.json). They are not in [tokens.json](tokens.json), which still rebuilds from the public-site readings. Dark theme: Not captured. The page painted light while the OS preference was dark.
