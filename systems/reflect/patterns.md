# Reflect patterns

Public site, 2026-11-21. The signed-in notes app was not entered.

## Marketing shell

A dark page (`#030014`) with a left wordmark, a centre pill of anchors, and a right pair: a text link (Log in to Classic) and a glass primary button (Download). At 390px the pill becomes a full-screen drawer. See [home](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Hero

One display line in AeonikPro at 72px / 80px, a 16px muted subcopy, then two 36px buttons. A product demo sits under a purple glow. The demo is marketing artwork, not a signed-in dashboard.

## Pricing

An on-page section, not a `/pricing` route (`/pricing` 404s). Two columns: Mac Free, iPhone and iPad paid with the price left to the App Store. See [pricing](screenshots/home-light-pricing.png).

## Blog

A second header: Login plus a solid purple Start Free Trial. Both go to the auth wall. Tag chips filter the list on the same page. See [blog](screenshots/blog-light-default.png).

## Auth wall

Centred mark, "Sign in with" Google and Apple, a divider, an empty email field, Continue. Discord, Twitter, Terms, and Privacy sit in the footer. Nothing was typed. See [login](screenshots/login-light-default.png).

## Empty and error

A made-up path renders a branded 404 with Back to home. See [404](screenshots/notfound-light-default.png). Legal pages drop the dark marketing chrome and use a light document. See [privacy](screenshots/privacy-light-default.png).
