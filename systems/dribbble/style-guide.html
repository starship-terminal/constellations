Dribbble, measured

dribbble.com is a Rails app in three CSS eras painting one light surface. Its declared token layer is Shoelace's — the --sl-* set with the --sl-color-primary-* ramp re-bound to the Dribbble pink (#ea4c89), plus neutral and gray ramps, radii, spacing, shadows, transitions and the focus ring. Most of the signed-out surface is styled by the older mini-master bundle instead: Mona Sans at 52px/600 on the hero, the #0d0c22 ink, #6e6d7a muted text, the #f8f8fc promo and chip tint, 8px cards, the 12px search field, 20px pills and the 40px pink Go circle — measured off the live components. The dark set names the ink surfaces the page paints (the dark pills, the certified chip), not a user theme. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from dribbble.com, signed out, in headless Chromium (the capture date is in README.md and raw/css-vars.json). The signed-out feed ends at a Sign-up-to-continue wall — a documented boundary; nothing was signed into, filled, followed, liked or submitted. Mona Sans, Source Serif 4 and IBM Plex Mono are served faces and are not loaded here: samples fall back to system sans.

Palette

core.color.pink is the declared Shoelace primary ramp re-bound to Dribbble's pinks; neutral and gray are the declared ramps; hue holds the full Shoelace hue set; measured holds the mini-master values the page actually paints. Light roles name the semantic reads; the dark set names the ink surfaces inside the light page.

Spacing and radius

Spacing is the declared --sl-spacing scale (4px-64px); radii run 2-16px on the declared scale, with the measured 12px search field, 20px pills, 8px cards and the circle on top.
