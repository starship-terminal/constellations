# Cohere design reference

A token set and component reference for Cohere's public surface — for building clean light marketing pages with the white ground, volcanic ink, named Tailwind palette and gradient-swept pill controls of the real thing. It was read from **cohere.com** on **2026-11-17**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844: the marketing routes (home, pricing, north, command, models-overview, developers, customer-stories, about, privacy and terms-of-use), the mobile home/pricing/north passes with the menu open, plus the public sign-in card on **dashboard.cohere.com/welcome/login** as a boundary.

Cohere is worth a capture because the site is a **Next.js App Router / Tailwind v3 / Sanity build whose declared design layer ships entirely as utility rules** — there is no `:root` token block. The harvest preserves 1,086 literal utility declarations instead: the named palette (`volcanic-900` `#212121` through `volcanic-50`, `coral` `#ff7759`, `mushroom`, `marble`, `quartz` `#d18ee2`, `onyx`, `neutral-*`, `blue`, `green`, `danger`, `success`, `pureBlack`/`pureWhite`), the `web3-*` type scale (`text-web3-7` 0.5rem/140%/0.02em up), the `z-background`/`z-content`/`z-nav` layer names, the `max-w-web3-*` wrappers (2280/1920/1390px), `.ease-*`/`.duration-*` motion primitives and a custom radius scale (`rounded-xl` 22px, `rounded-2xl` 30px — not the Tailwind defaults). It paints a **single light theme** on pureWhite: the pureBlack promo banner, the `neutral-15` `#17171c` footer band and the `#355146` green "Ready to put AI to work" band are dark islands inside it, not a second theme. The signature control is the **ease-button-gradient pill**: a 9999px-radius, 53.2px-tall CTA whose `::before` holds a 270deg `quartz-500 → coral-500` gradient sweep revealed through a sliding mask on hover; the rest of the swap is `ease` over `background-color,color` in 0.3s. **Boundary:** Cohere's product lives behind `dashboard.cohere.com`; every nav/hero CTA points at sign-up or sales forms and was never followed, and the login card (566 x 235: Google/GitHub OAuth rows, an email field and a green `#39594d` Log-in button) was measured at rest — no credentials typed, nothing submitted, no OAuth flow started.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export cohere` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do take colours from `core.color`'s named families.
2. Do reproduce the CTA pill the way the site does.
3. Do treat the promo banner, footer and green band as islands inside the single light theme.
4. Don't reach for a dark theme.
5. Don't infer the Cohere product from the marketing pages or the login card.
6. Don't copy the wordmark, the marquee customer logos, or the hero artwork into anything that ships.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What was captured

Routes: `/` (with the promo banner, glass header, hero, customer marquee, callout cards and footer), `/pricing` (the per-model rate table plus the FAQ accordion), `/north` and `/command` (product pages), `/models-overview`, `/developers`, `/customer-stories`, `/about`, `/privacy`, `/terms-of-use`, the mobile passes for home/pricing/north at 390 x 844 with the hamburger menu opened by its own press, and `dashboard.cohere.com/welcome/login` at rest. 28 screenshots, 39 measured components in 97 states, 93 crops.

## Known gaps

- **Captured with the automation flag masked.** The public capture (`tools/capture/public/`) hid `navigator.webdriver` from every page, so the committed readings and screenshots it took were taken past that mask. The scripts no longer hide it (edited, not run); a recapture that meets a bot check records it as a boundary.
- `cta-dark`'s hover state could not be captured: under a real hover the pill's `ease-button-gradient` animation re-renders the node, so the locator goes stale mid-evaluate (the failure is recorded in the state's `error` field). The identical hover swap is measured on `nav-cta` (background → pureBlack), which backs `light.accentHover`.
- The pricing tabs were measured in their two states (on/off) without switching the tab — the rate table content under the inactive tab was never painted for capture.
- The visited-link colour is declared (`.text-coral-700` #ca492d via the `:visited` variant) but no visited link could be rendered signed-out; the token carries the declared provenance.
- Shadows: nothing on the public surface paints a real drop shadow — the pill CTAs use border/outline rings and the `focus-visible` shadow stacks are transparent. No shadow ladder exists to tokenise.
- The models-overview, developers, customer-stories, about, privacy and terms pages are captured as full screenshots but their internal components were not individually measured (the spec covers shell + home + pricing + product titles + footer + mobile + sign-in).
- `dashboard.cohere.com` beyond the login card, the North/Command product trials, billing and every post-auth surface are out of scope by design — the login was a boundary only.

## How the values were read

- **Declared** values come from the stylesheets themselves. Cohere declares no `:root` variables; instead `tools/capture/vars.mjs` walks every rule in the 8 fetched sheets and preserves literal declarations of the design-bearing utilities (palette, `text-web3-*`, `rounded-*` including the corner-specific `border-*-radius` properties, `z-*`, `max-w-*`, `ease-*`, `duration-*`, `gap-*`) into `raw/css-vars.json`'s `utilities` bin — 1,086 records.
- **Computed** values come from the running page: `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` drives headless Chromium, settles each component into default/hover/focus-visible/pressed (or `opened`/`focused` where a real interaction exists) and records computed styles plus the painted child layers (`paint[]`) so fills that live on `::before` or nested divs are not lost. Nothing that would sign in, pay, submit or navigate was performed.
- **Tokens** are built by `tools/build_component_tokens.py` into `raw/component-tokens.json` (the spec), then `tools/build_tokens.py` emits `tokens.json`. Roles are read from measured components; when a measured value equals a declared one the description names the declared utility (`volcanic-900`, `neutral-15`, …). The CTA gradient was restated as a DTCG gradient token from the measured `backgroundImage` of the `::before` layer.
- **Docs**: `components.md`, `style-guide.html`, `DESIGN.md` and the Brief are generated; `make check` fails if they drift from the inputs.

## Do and don't

- **Do take colours from `core.color`'s named families.** The site uses them as first-class names: `volcanic-900` ink on the white ground, `neutral-15` for the dark islands, `quartz`/`coral` the gradient pair ([footer](screenshots/home-light-full.png)).
- **Do reproduce the CTA pill the way the site does.** A 9999px pill with a 0.3s `ease` colour swap and the 270deg `quartz-500 → coral-500` `::before` sweep under a sliding mask ([cta-dark](components/crops/cta-dark-default.png)); the light variant is the same pill inverted ([hero-cta](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png)).
- **Do treat the promo banner, footer and green band as islands inside the single light theme.** White on `#17171c` and `#355146` is the only inversion ([footer-cta](components/crops/footer-cta-default.png)).
- **Don't reach for a dark theme.** Every route paints the same light surface; the dark hero bands on /north and /command are bands, not a theme ([north hero](screenshots/north-light-default.png)).
- **Don't infer the Cohere product from the marketing pages or the login card.** Everything behind `dashboard.cohere.com` was measured at rest only ([sign-in](screenshots/signin-light-default.png)).
- **Don't copy the wordmark, the marquee customer logos, or the hero artwork into anything that ships.** They are Cohere's and its customers' marks ([marquee](screenshots/home-light-full.png)).
