Flask, measured

Every value on this page is a CSS custom property generated from tokens.json: the site's declared --ds-* layer — the surface-0…4 near-black ramp, the fg-1…4 foreground steps and fg-lead/-subhead/-placeholder roles, the line-* hairlines, the neutral brand grey, the danger/warning/success/info/live palettes, the flair-1…3 gradient accents, the glass/glow/metal/showcase ingredient colours, the interaction-opacity roles (fill, hover, pressed, selected, row-hover, control-*), the scrim/media/scrollbar roles and the shadow-* ingredients — plus --ds-font-sans, the 12px/9999px radii and the --ease-* curves. The real pixels are in screenshots/ and components/crops/.

Read from flask.do, signed out, in headless Chromium. The workspace and every post-login surface is a documented boundary (/home redirects to /login); the login form was measured, never submitted. Every public page paints the dark default theme — the only theme the bundle declares; no light variant or toggle exists. Most colours are declared as rgb channel lists; the guide shows the rgba() they paint.

Palette

The declared --ds-* roles are the palette: the near-black surface-0…4 ramp under fg-1…4 foreground steps, the line-* hairlines, the neutral brand grey with its tint/highlight/shade, the danger/warning/success/info and red live palettes, the flair/glow gradient ingredients and the translucent glass/interaction washes — every one a channel list the utilities composite. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Radius and motion

Two radii: radius 12px on cards and panels, radius-control 9999px on buttons, chips and the switch — and two declared easing curves, --ease-out and --ease-in-out, under the measured control transitions.

Type

One face: --ds-font-sans is the ui-sans-serif/system-ui stack — the site ships no self-hosted display face. Sizes and weights are the measured steps from the live pages.
