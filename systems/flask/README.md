# Flask design reference

A token set and component reference for Flask's public surfaces — for building video-review product marketing pages, pricing grids, documentation pages and the sign-in edge in the style of the real thing. It was read from **flask.do** on **2026-11-15**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page (with the embedded product demo), /pricing (plus its FAQ-open and billed-quarterly states), /mcp, /about, /security, /video-collaboration, /frame-io-alternative, /privacy-policy, /terms-of-service and /login, plus 390px passes on home, pricing and login.

Flask is worth a capture because it ships a complete dark token layer to the browser: every public page paints `color-scheme: dark` on `:root` and the inline `<style id="ds-theme">` block plus the linked root-*.css bundle declare 182 custom properties — the `--ds-surface-0…4` near-black ramp (12/18/24/31/42 greys), a `--ds-fg-*` foreground ramp plus lead/subhead/placeholder roles, `--ds-line-*` hairlines, a neutral `--ds-brand` grey (173 173 173) with tint/highlight/fg, four full status palettes (danger, warning, success, info — base, edge, fg, highlight, shade, text, tint, bevel each), a red `--ds-live-*` recording palette, `--ds-flair-*` gradient accents, translucent `--ds-glass-*`/`--ds-glow-*`/`--ds-metal-*`/`--ds-showcase-*` ingredient colours, interaction-opacity roles (`--ds-fill`, `--ds-hover`, `--ds-pressed`, `--ds-row-hover`, `--ds-control-*`, `--ds-chip-*`, `--ds-marker-*`, `--ds-scrim-*`), `--ds-shadow-*` colours plus four real shadow declarations, `--ds-radius` 12px / `--ds-radius-control` 9999px, `--ds-font-sans` and the `--ease-*` curves. Every colour is declared as a Tailwind-style channel list ("250 250 250" or "250 250 250 / 0.18"); the tokens record the hex/rgba() each paints. **Boundary:** the product workspace behind /home and every post-login surface was never reached — /home redirects to /login, which was measured as a form, never submitted; Sign Up and Open Flask link to /home and were measured, never followed.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export flask` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let the declared layer carry the design.
2. Do paint on the near-black ramp.
3. Do keep controls as pills.
4. Do spend ink as alpha.
5. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
6. Don't expect a light theme.
7. Don't fabricate form outcomes.
8. Don't copy the brand.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **Gradient-faced buttons.** The hero CTA, Continue with Google and the plan Get Started buttons paint `linear-gradient(to top, #adadad 20%, #cacaca 80%)` over a transparent `backgroundColor`; their `dark.action.*Fill` tokens are that gradient. The demo share popover paints no fill of its own, so `dark.surface.demoCardBg` is not a token. The focus ring is the 2px `box-shadow` ring (`rgba(173, 173, 173, 0.5)`, the declared `--focus-ring`); the outline is `none`.
- **The signed-in product.** The workspace behind /home — uploads, the canvas, feedback threads, folders, versions, sharing, settings — needs an account this capture does not have. /home redirects to /login; the Sign Up and Open Flask CTAs were measured, never followed. The product UI on the home page is a pre-rendered marketing demo, not the product.
- **The demo mock's deeper states.** The embedded demo's controls were measured as painted; pointer attempts on its overlay-guarded controls were intercepted, so opened/menu states inside the mock are uncaptured — only the Version chip was toggled (`home-dark-demo-asset-2`).
- **The login form.** The e-mail field was typed into then cleared and Continue with Google/Email were never pressed — validation, error, loading and success states are unverified.
- **Mobile chrome.** At 390 wide the home page mounted only the Flask logo link during measurement — no `<header>` element and no visible menu button in that pass; a separate probe found an `Open menu` button with zero-size bounds and another capture rendered a bottom-sheet menu layer (`mobile-home-dark-menu`). Treat mobile chrome as route/pass-specific, not a universal drawer.
- **The billing switch.** /pricing's switch was toggled to "Billed quarterly" for one capture and measurement, then toggled back; the default yearly grid is the resting state.
- **Pressed states.** DevTools-forced (`CSS.forcePseudoState` :active:hover); any script-applied mousedown state is missing.
- **Channel-list colours.** The `--ds-*` tokens hold the hex/rgba() each declared channel list resolves to; rgba() values with fractional alphas (e.g. `--ds-row-hover` 0.034) are rounded to 4 places.
- **Quantised screenshots.** `screenshots/` are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours from tokens or the raw values, never the pixels.

## Fonts

- **`--ds-font-sans`** — the single voice: `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif, "Apple Color Emoji", "Segoe UI Emoji", "Segoe UI Symbol", "Noto Color Emoji"`. The site loads no self-hosted display face; the style guide and tokens carry the same system stack the browser paints.

## Coverage

- 10 desktop routes and 3 mobile routes captured signed out; every public page paints the dark `default` theme — the only `--ds-*` layer the bundle declares. No light variant or theme toggle exists.
- Component measurements across the chrome, hero, embedded demo, pricing, docs, login and footer groups, including the toggled Version chip, the opened FAQ accordion, the toggled billing switch, forced-pressed controls and the typed-then-cleared login field.
- Missing surfaces (declared in the catalog's `capture.gaps`): the workspace and every post-login surface, the remaining sitemap SEO/comparison pages (same landing template), submitted form states, and the demo mock's overlay-guarded opened states.
- Icons: layout gap — no standalone SVG set lifted; the Flask mark and demo glyphs are visible in the crops and screenshots.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the declared layer carry the design.** Flask ships its tokens: use the `dark.surface.*`/`dark.fg.*`/`dark.line.*`/`dark.brand.*` roles rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json) and [home-dark-default](screenshots/home-dark-default.png).
2. **Do paint on the near-black ramp.** The page field is `dark.surface.0` `#0c0c0c`; cards and raised surfaces step up `surface.1…4` (18/24/31/42 greys), never lighter. See [mkt-header](components/crops/mkt-header-default.png).
3. **Do keep controls as pills.** Buttons and chips use `core.radius.control` 9999px; cards and fields use `core.radius.default` 12px. See [nav-cta](components/crops/nav-cta-default.png) and [login-email](components/crops/login-email-default.png).
4. **Do spend ink as alpha.** Hierarchy is translucent white: `--ds-row-hover`, `--ds-fill`, `--ds-control-*` and the glass washes are channel lists with small alphas over the dark field, not greys. See [demo-share-scope](components/crops/demo-share-scope-default.png).
5. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** The workspace was never reached; the home page's product UI is a marketing demo, and /login is the only app surface a signed-out visitor sees. See [login-dark-default](screenshots/login-dark-default.png).
6. **Don't expect a light theme.** Every public surface paints dark only; there is no toggle and no light `--ds-*` layer to alias. See [mobile-home-dark-default](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-default.png).
7. **Don't fabricate form outcomes.** The login field was typed into then cleared — validation, error and success states are unverified. See [login-email](components/crops/login-email-filled.png).
8. **Don't copy the brand.** The Flask mark, the demo mock and the marketing imagery in captures are Flask's published artwork; borrow structure and proportion, not identity. See [mkt-logo](components/crops/mkt-logo-default.png).
