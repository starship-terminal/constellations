## Typography

Plain is the UI face: nav 13.3333px, utility and footer 14px, buttons 16px. Arizona is the display face, 24px on the product title and the Welcome back heading. Files are not in this package.

## Geometry

Header 86px. Wordmark 275 by 23. Quiz button 32px, radius 4px. Search 38px. MEMBER ADD TO BAG 48px, radius 4px. Sign-in submit 42 by 350px, radius 4px.

## Do and don't

Keep the page white and the type black. Use charcoal for the quiz and the sign-in submit. Use lilac for the member action. Stop at the empty email wall. Get Started was not clicked. MEMBER ADD TO BAG was not clicked.
