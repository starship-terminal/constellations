# Claude for iOS design reference

A token set and component reference for Claude, Anthropic's native iOS app. Use it to build a native iOS chat app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a soft near-black or warm off-white page, Claude's replies set in a serif straight on the page with no bubble, the owner's messages in a grey bubble, a sidebar instead of a tab bar, iOS 26 glass controls floating over the content, grouped cards in sheets, one inverse pill (white in dark, black in light) for the main action, blue for every interactive state, and Claude's orange kept for the spark and the send button. It was captured from the shipped app on iOS 26 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account on the Max plan: dark on 2026-10-23, light on 2026-10-24. Dark is the phone's own appearance; light was set with the app's Settings > App > Appearance > Light and put back to System afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 36 captures: 24 in dark (new chat, composer, model, effort and attach sheets, the sidebar, a thread with its menus and tool-step timeline, Chats and search, Settings and four of its pages, Projects, Archived, Artifacts, Code, the gift sheet) and 12 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) they show the owner's account line, chat titles, chat text and photo thumbnails, and no credentials or personally identifiable data; one other person's first name in a chat title in the Chats list (`dark/11`) is painted out ([tools/redact-rules/claude-ios.json](../../tools/redact-rules/claude-ios.json)).

This is the native app. The web app has its own package, [systems/claude](../claude/README.md), read from claude.ai's CSS; no value here was taken from it. [Web and iOS compared](#web-and-ios-compared) says how the two differ.

This package contains no Claude code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Anthropic's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the spark, the icons, the illustrations or the brand colours.

### Principles

1. Do set Claude's words in the serif, on the page, with no bubble.
2. Do give each screen one inverse pill for its main action.
3. Do keep the orange for the brand and for sending.
4. Do use blue for every interactive state.
5. Do navigate with a sidebar, not a tab bar.
6. Do float controls as glass and put choices in bottom sheets.
7. Don't fill destructive actions red.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Claude for iOS is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 149 named readings, most in both themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a thin mark) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height, or the pitch of rows without dividers |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | Both font families |

Scale steps (spacing, radii) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in dark only (the selected sidebar row, the search field, the empty-state circle, the artifact card rim, the amber usage badge, the archive filter), the light token is Derived and says what it was filled from. The core `neutral`, `blue` and `clay` groups list every value the app shows in those hues, sorted light to dark and named by order.

## What to distrust

- **The fonts.** Not determinable from pixels. The interface sans is not the iOS system face (its g is single-storey), and the response serif is a book serif with bracketed serifs; both are Anthropic's own choice. `fontFamily.sans` is `system` and `fontFamily.serif` is `serif`, both marked Not captured.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em, which may not fit these faces. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Glass.** The nav buttons, composer, search bar, menus and the Add device pill are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it (the composer reads lighter at its foot than at its top). Use the system material, not a flat fill. Light glass controls also carry a faint shadow that was not measured.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: cards 25.6 pt, sheet cards and the bubble 23.9, the composer 22.2, the sheet 35.8, the selected row 13.7. The menu's 17.1 pt was read on a soft glass edge, so plus or minus 4 pt.
- **Two dark page greys.** A chat reads #151515 and list pages read #0B0B0B in dark; in light both are #F9F9F7. `color.bg.page` and `color.bg.list` keep them apart.
- **Light covers 12 of 36 screens.** Effort, the long-press menu, the tool-step sheet, lists, search, empty states, Artifacts, Code and the gift sheet are dark only.
- **Composer widths differ by screen.** The new-chat composer reads 374 pt wide in dark and the thread composer 358 pt; the light new chat used the narrower one. Read sizes from the token, which is the thread's.
- **The greeting changes with the time of day** ("It's a late-night jam session." in dark, "Hello, night owl" in light); only its colour and size are tokens.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no streaming.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; no message was sent, so streaming and loading are unseen; Delete was not tapped.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass, photos and the artifact thumbnails band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do set Claude's words in the serif, on the page, with no bubble.** Responses and the greeting are serif; the owner's message is a grey sans bubble on the right; everything else is the sans. See [07-chat-thread](screenshots/dark/07-chat-thread-user-bubble-response-actions.png), [07 light](screenshots/light/07-chat-thread-user-bubble-response-actions.png) and [01-new-chat](screenshots/dark/01-new-chat-greeting-composer.png).
2. **Do give each screen one inverse pill for its main action.** Talk, New chat, New project, New session and Share are white in dark and black in light; no button is filled with the brand orange or the blue. See [07-chat-thread](screenshots/dark/07-chat-thread-user-bubble-response-actions.png), [06-sidebar light](screenshots/light/06-sidebar-nav-pinned-recents.png) and [22-gift-sheet](screenshots/dark/22-gift-sheet-illustration-primary.png).
3. **Do keep the orange for the brand and for sending.** The spark and the send button are the only orange on most screens, the same value in both themes; an active filter is the one exception. See [02-composer-typed](screenshots/dark/02-composer-typed-send-enabled.png) and [19-archived](screenshots/dark/19-archived-empty-state-accent-icon.png).
4. **Do use blue for every interactive state.** Switches, the chosen-model tick, the chosen Appearance tile, links and usage meters are one blue family. See [15-capabilities](screenshots/dark/15-capabilities-switches-on-disabled.png), [03-model-picker](screenshots/dark/03-model-picker-sheet.png) and [24-appearance light](screenshots/light/24-settings-app-appearance-cards.png).
5. **Do navigate with a sidebar, not a tab bar.** Every destination, pinned chat and recent chat lives in the sidebar that slides over the page; the selected row is a grey rounded fill. See [06-sidebar](screenshots/dark/06-sidebar-nav-pinned-recents.png) and [23-sidebar-selected-row](screenshots/dark/23-sidebar-selected-row.png).
6. **Do float controls as glass and put choices in bottom sheets.** Nav buttons, the composer and search are glass over the content; the model, effort and attachments open as sheets of grouped cards. See [03-model-picker](screenshots/dark/03-model-picker-sheet.png), [05-add-to-chat](screenshots/dark/05-add-to-chat-sheet-switch.png) and [11-chats-list](screenshots/dark/11-chats-list-search-new-chat.png).
7. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Delete is red text with a red icon at the bottom of a menu, below a divider. See [08-chat-menu](screenshots/dark/08-chat-menu-destructive.png) and [08 light](screenshots/light/08-chat-menu-destructive.png).

## Web and iOS compared

[systems/claude](../claude/README.md) is claude.ai in a browser, measured from its CSS. This package is the native app, sampled from pixels. They share a voice (a serif for Claude's words, a sans for the interface, an ink-coloured main action) and differ in most of the frame:

| | Web ([systems/claude](../claude/README.md)) | iOS (this package) |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Measured CSS custom properties and computed styles | Sampled pixels; fonts not determinable |
| Navigation | A persistent sidebar beside the chat | A sidebar that slides over the page; no tab bar |
| Controls | Flat buttons and menus with hover states | iOS 26 glass buttons, composer and menus; no hover |
| Choices | Dropdown menus and a Settings dialog | Bottom sheets of grouped cards; Settings is a full-height sheet |
| Composer | One card with the model menu inside | Two rows in a glass block, with a Talk pill that becomes the orange send button |
| Interactive colour | The web package's own accent roles | One blue family for switches, ticks, selection and meters |
| Pages | One page colour per theme | Two in dark (chat and list pages), one in light |
| Values | Its own tokens, at web sizes | Read independently here, in points; never copy one package's value into the other |

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for glass buttons, menus, sheets, switches and pop-up selects rather than redrawing them, and pick your own serif and sans for the two families. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
