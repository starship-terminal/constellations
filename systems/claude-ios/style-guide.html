Claude for iOS style guide

Claude for iOS on iOS 26, read from stills captured through iPhone Mirroring on 2026-10-23 (dark) and 2026-10-24 (light). Every value below comes from tokens.json; points are 1:1 with CSS pixels. The fonts are not known, so the page uses the platform sans and serif. Glass is drawn as its centre colour: the real material blurs what is behind it.

Light and dark side by side. The badge says how each value was read: Sampled (a flat fill, exact), Sampled (text) (the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill), Derived (worked out, or a glass or gradient reading). Hover a swatch for its capture.
