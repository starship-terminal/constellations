# Patterns

Signed-out public pages, 2026-11-22, desktop 1440.

## Shop shell

Every photographed page shares the same shell. A burgundy promo bar, 40px, sits above the header. On the homepage the header is transparent over the hero, so the wordmark and department labels are white. On a white page the header fills white and the labels turn ink. Find My Fit is a white pill on the left of the nav. Search, account, and bag sit on the right.
