Palette

Space names, one accent family: nebula/lightfall/navy-deep dark grounds, offwhite/satellite/regolith light surfaces, then the oranges — brand #fc5200 for held states and focus, brand-accent #e75e15 for every filled CTA, horizon-dark #c74100 for text links on light.

Type

Two voices: Aeonik for display — 60/63 hero at −1.5px tracking, 50/55 page titles at 500 weight, 38px FAQ heads — and Inter for everything else: 15px nav links, 14px segments and buttons, 16px body, 18px form text. Fira Code covers code.

Radius and geometry

Pills all the way down for actions (radius.pill 999px), 20px segments, 16px fields and the consent card, 24–32px cards. The wide card rail measures 1344px inside a 1440 page; the visible nav bar is 65px.

Motion and layout

Colour and border swaps run at duration.fast 150ms, the toggle segments and submit at 200ms, nav transforms and field borders at 300ms, card lifts at 500ms — mostly on the galactic easings.
