# Apollo GraphQL design reference

A token set and component reference for Apollo GraphQL's public marketing site (https://www.apollographql.com), the website of the GraphQL platform company — GraphOS, the Router and Apollo Client. Use it to build a marketing page or account-entry screen that borrows its look: near-black "lightfall" hero fields and deep `nebula` navy bands, the signature conic-ring primary CTA, burnt-orange `#e75e15` pill buttons, offwhite segment toggles, 32px cards and Aeonik display type over Inter. It was captured signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 (mobile pages at 390 x 844) on 2026-11-10.

It holds 31 screenshots across eight marketing routes — home, /graphos, /graphos-agent-services, /pricing, /customers, /blog, /why-apollo, /contact-sales — plus the Studio login and signup pages at rest and three mobile routes, and 55 measured components with 143 states and one crop each. The product itself lives on studio.apollographql.com behind sign-in: its login and signup pages rendered signed out and were captured, but nothing was submitted or consented to anywhere — the only typing was a sample `name@example.com` into the contact form, deleted again before the capture closed — so every authenticated surface is a recorded boundary (see [components.md](components.md) > Gaps).

This package contains no Apollo code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots, icon SVGs, photography and visual system belong to Apollo GraphQL ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the logo, imagery or brand orange.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export apollographql` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the hero on near-black, not pure navy.
2. Do give the primary CTA its conic ring.
3. Do keep every action a pill.
4. Do use the two oranges for two jobs.
5. Do alternate dark and light sections.
6. Do pair Aeonik display with Inter UI.
7. Don't ship the marketing style as Studio.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

- Every component was located on the live page by [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) in headless Chromium: the site is Next.js with Tailwind utilities and CSS modules, so selectors key off roles, text and the stable part of module class names (`M(name)` matches `[class*="name"]`), never a hash. Computed styles were read per state — default, hover, focus-visible, pressed (forced via `CSS.forcePseudoState`) — and one 2x crop saved per state.
- CSS custom properties were read from the live CSSOM by [tools/capture/vars.mjs](tools/capture/vars.mjs) into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). Unlike most marketing sites, apollographql.com ships a real token layer — the space-named `--color-*` palette, `--font-*` stacks, `--radius-*`/`--duration-*` scales — so palette, radius and duration leaves in tokens.json name the variable; the rest cite the measurement.
- The site paints two surfaces: dark sections re-bind the semantic `--color-*` names under `[data-theme="dark"]`; vars.mjs resolved both declarations. Both sets live in tokens.json.
- Screenshots were taken on the same pages by [tools/capture/public/public.mjs](tools/capture/public/public.mjs), quantised to 256 colours by [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py). The OneTrust card was allowed to mount for its capture, then hidden by CSS — never answered. The only presses were the Products nav disclosure and the pricing billing segment, both client-side.
- Icons were lifted by [tools/capture/icons.mjs](tools/capture/icons.mjs), which collects every inline SVG 10–64px, dedupes by geometry and writes them with `currentColor` where the icon inherits its colour.

## What to distrust

- **Readings past a masked bot signal.** The committed screenshots and readings were taken with `navigator.webdriver` masked, the flag bot checks read to spot automation. The capture scripts no longer mask it (not run since), so a recapture may record a walled route as a boundary instead of a reading.
- **The conic ring is a ramp, not a circle.** `component.cta-ring` stores the hero CTA's `conic-gradient(from ~412deg, teal, orange, teal)` as a gradient token; in the browser it spins slowly around the pill. The exact angle and animation vary.
- **oklab whites are recorded as rgba.** Nav text and hover fills compute as `oklab(…/0.72)` etc.; tokens record the equivalent `rgba(255,255,255,…)` with the oklab form noted in source.
- **`field` colours look inverted.** The contact form sits on a navy band inside a light page: field fill is `nebula` navy with `offwhite` borders — correct, not a bug.
- **The eyebrow strip may be gone.** The "Apollo Summit 2026" promo band is event-tied; a re-capture may not find it.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs** — read exact colours from tokens.json or the raw JSON, never from a screenshot pixel.
- **Studio auth pages are at rest only.** Login/signup were captured signed out; their form states, errors and everything past submit are uncaptured.
- **`nav-menu-item` was read from the wrong element.** It is a page-body "Explore GraphOS" link (the open state at y=3177), not a row inside the Products dropdown. Needs a recapture. The horizon-dark-3 tokens now cite only `text-link`, which holds the same colour.

## Fonts

- **Aeonik** — every display heading (400/500), hero CTAs and page titles; proprietary to Apollo, self-hosted through next/font as `__aeonikFont_*` woff2.
- **Aeonik Air** — a lighter cut declared on the same pages.
- **Inter** — nav, buttons, forms, footer and body text at 400–700; next/font `__interFont_*`. SIL OFL.
- **Fira Code** — code accents and command rows; next/font `__firaCodeFont_*`. SIL OFL.
- The tokens name the readable stacks (`Aeonik, -apple-system, …` / `Inter, -apple-system, …`); the style guide renders the system fallback.

## Do and don't

- **Do paint the hero on near-black, not pure navy.** The home hero field is `lightfall` #02060a; `nebula` #15252d is for cards, form bands and later sections, and `navy-deep` #091b23 for the product-page bands. Don't flatten them into one dark. ([hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [stats-card](components/crops/stats-card-default.png))
- **Do give the primary CTA its conic ring.** The hero's "Talk to Apollo" pill spins a teal→orange conic gradient around a space-dark fill — it is the site's signature move. Secondary CTAs stay a quiet 1px white-35 outline; don't fill them or reuse the ring. ([cta-primary](components/crops/cta-primary-default.png), [cta-secondary](components/crops/cta-secondary-default.png))
- **Do keep every action a pill.** Buttons, segments and link chips are fully round (`radius.pill` 999px; segments 20px, volume pills 8px); cards stop at 32px and fields at 16px. Don't introduce square buttons or tiny radii under 8px. ([plan-cta](components/crops/plan-cta-default.png), [tier-segment](components/crops/tier-segment-default.png))
- **Do use the two oranges for two jobs.** `brand-accent` #e75e15 is the filled-CTA colour that darkens to #d84f08; `brand` #fc5200 marks held states, focus borders and accent dots; `horizon-dark` #c74100 is for text links on light. Don't swap them. ([nav-cta hover](components/crops/nav-cta-hover.png), [volume-pill-on](components/crops/volume-pill-on-default.png), [text-link](components/crops/text-link-default.png))
- **Do alternate dark and light sections.** Pages move freely between space-dark bands and `offwhite`/`satellite`/`faq-gray` light islands — the same token names re-bound by `[data-theme="dark"]`. Don't force a whole page into one theme. ([pricing full](screenshots/pricing-dark-full.png), [why-apollo full](screenshots/why-apollo-dark-full.png))
- **Do pair Aeonik display with Inter UI.** The hero and blog titles (60/63) and section heads (42) take Aeonik at 400–500, while the 50/55 page titles and everything else — nav, buttons, forms, body, the 24px FAQ questions — take Inter 14–50. Don't set buttons in the display face or page titles in Aeonik. ([hero-title](components/crops/hero-title-default.png), [nav-trigger](components/crops/nav-trigger-default.png))
- **Don't ship the marketing style as Studio.** Nothing here was measured inside the console; a screen claiming Apollo Studio's app UI from these tokens would be invented. The honest gap is recorded in components.md.
