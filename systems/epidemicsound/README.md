# Epidemic Sound design reference

A token set and component reference for Epidemic Sound's public signed-out website — for building music-licensing marketing pages, dark catalog shells and auth forms in the style of the real thing. It was read from **epidemicsound.com** on **2026-11-14**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844. Captured: the home page, /pricing/, /music/, /sound-effects/, /how-it-works/, the public catalog pages /music/genres/pop/, /music/themes/vlogs/lifestyle-vlog/, /artists/ooyy/ and /sound-effects/categories/ambience/, the login.epidemicsound.com login and registration forms, plus 390px passes on the home and genre pages.

Epidemic Sound is worth a capture because it ships its real token layer to the browser: the stylesheets declare a full `--es-*` design system outright — 151 semantic `--es-color-*` roles (action/background/border/icon/input/misc/platform/text/track plus brand and data-viz palettes), `--es-heading-*`/`--es-subheading-*`/`--es-text-*` type scales, `--es-radius-*`, `--es-elevation-*`, `--es-grid-*`, `--es-trackrow-*` and component internals — resolved per body theme class. Two surfaces paint signed out: the ecru **alternative-light** marketing site (`#f1f0eb` field, black ink and black pill CTAs, `#ff82c2` accent, Sebenta serif display) and the **dark** catalog app (`#121418` canvas, white pill CTA, the pink `#ff82c2` genre banner, 56px track rows, translucent-white ghost controls, `#20afff` azur focus rings). The Keycloak login and registration cards paint dark too. **Boundary:** everything behind login.epidemicsound.com was never reached — the login and signup forms were measured at rest, never filled with real credentials or submitted; Play, Download, Subscribe, Create-free-account and Log-in actions were measured, never pressed or followed; the `#es-cookie-banner` consent card was hidden, never answered.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export epidemicsound` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let the `--es-*` layer carry the design.
2. Do match the pill header.
3. Do invert the primary button per theme.
4. Do spend the pink on moments, not chrome.
5. Do set display type in Sebenta at weight 500.
6. Do use ghost washes on the dark surface.
7. Do draw focus as a real ring.
8. Don't style the signed-in product from this package.
9. Don't fabricate playback or downloads.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## What to distrust

- **The base radius scale is zeroed by design.** `:root` declares `--es-radius-small/medium/large` (2/4/8px) but `body` zeroes them; the shipped pages carry `radii-override`, under which only `button` (10000px), `surface` (16px), `element` (8px), `detail` (4px) and `tiny` (2px) paint. The resolved values in tokens.json are the `radii-override` ones — read `core.radius.small`/`medium`/`large` as 0, not as the declared 2/4/8.
- **The cover play button's hover is not a reading.** Row hover swaps the album-cover button out of the layout, so `track-play` and `sfx-play` hover hold a 0x0 box: components.md leaves those states out and says so, and the hover look is on the row (`track-row`, `sfx-row`).
- **Two entries read the wrong element.** `plan-price` is an empty 688 x 384 div at y 6525, not a price line, and `su-submit` is the "Continue with Google" link, not the registration submit. No token cites them; recapture needed.
- **Some roles have no reading.** `dark.border.divider` and `dark.action.liked` were entered by hand (no measured border or liked icon paints them) and say so in their source; `light.text.muted`, `dark.text.muted` and `core.motion.control` have no reading at all and are listed under `_gaps` in raw/component-tokens.json.
- **Quantised screenshots band gradients.** Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs; read exact colours, gradients included, from tokens.json or the raw JSON. The full-colour page captures are kept outside the repo.
- **Hidden twins exist.** The header ships a hidden duplicate "Log in" link and pricing a hidden "Compare plans" heading before the painted ones; the package measures the painted copy (the last match) only.
- **SFX pages have no large category card.** The nearest real subcategory surface is a small periwinkle in-row text link (`sfx-category-card`, 39 x 20); do not look for tile-style category cards that were never there.
- **Automation was masked during capture.** Until 2026-11-20, `tools/capture/public/lib.mjs` hid `navigator.webdriver` in every page it opened, so every committed reading was taken with that mask. The mask is removed (a bot check is now recorded as a boundary, not bypassed); whether any page needed it to load is not known, and a recapture without it is the check.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let the `--es-*` layer carry the design.** The product ships its token system: use the semantic `color.*` roles and the declared type/radius/elevation scales rather than restating hex. See [tokens.json](tokens.json).
2. **Do match the pill header.** Every surface runs the same inset pill bar: logo, mega-menu triggers, Pricing, a ghost Log in and a full-radius primary CTA. See [c-header](components/crops/c-header-default.png).
3. **Do invert the primary button per theme.** Black fill on ecru, white fill on the dark catalog and Keycloak surfaces — never a coloured primary. See [btn-signup](components/crops/btn-signup-default.png) and [c-signup](components/crops/c-signup-default.png).
4. **Do spend the pink on moments, not chrome.** `#ff82c2` paints the genre banner and `action.accent`; it is a backdrop for black Sebenta headings, not a button colour. See [genre-banner](components/crops/genre-banner-default.png).
5. **Do set display type in Sebenta at weight 500.** The 80px hero, 40px banner headings and 32px sections share the serif voice with tight tracking; controls and body stay in Inter. See [hero-h1](components/crops/hero-h1-default.png).
6. **Do use ghost washes on the dark surface.** Hover lays `rgba(207,214,229,.08)`, pressed `.16`, secondary fills `.12–.16`; track rows and icon buttons read through translucency, not solid greys. See [track-row hover](components/crops/track-row-hover.png).
7. **Do draw focus as a real ring.** 2px solid black on ecru, 2px `#20afff` azur on dark; fields instead lift their `form-group` well from `.16` to `.24` ghost fill. See [track-like focus-visible](components/crops/track-like-focus-visible.png).
8. **Don't style the signed-in product from this package.** Everything behind login.epidemicsound.com was never reached; the at-rest forms and the public catalog are the only evidence.
9. **Don't fabricate playback or downloads.** Play, Like, Add-to-playlist, Download and Copy-link were measured at rest; what they do signed out is unverified.
