Palette

The declared --es-color-* layer resolves per theme in the light (alternative-light) and dark sets: action/background/border/icon/input/misc/platform/text/track roles plus the brand palette (azur, ecru, pink, red, yellow, off-black, off-white) and the extended/data-viz ramps. Measured roles alias the declared values they match.

Spacing and radius

Under radii-override the shipped pages draw full-pill buttons (radius.button 10000px), 16px surfaces, 8px elements, 4px details and 2px tiny corners — while small/medium/large stay 0. Borders are hairline tints: rgba(0,0,0,.08)/rgba(0,0,0,.64) on light, rgba(207,214,229,.08)/(.56) on dark, with a #20afff azur (dark) or #000 (light) focus ring.
