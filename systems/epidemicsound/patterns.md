# Patterns

## App shell

The public site is a marketing shell, not the signed-in player. A sticky header sits on an off-white page. Desktop nav is a row of pill buttons that open white dropdowns. At 390 the same items sit in a drawer opened by Show navigation menu. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Pricing

`/pricing/` is a plan comparison. Individuals and Businesses switch the cards. Monthly and Yearly switch the price. "Get 30 days free" leaves for checkout and was not followed. See [pricing-light-default](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Catalog

`/music/` and `/sound-effects/` are public catalogs with a search field and category links. Track and artist pages are public. Download and save actions that need an account were not used.

## Empty, loading, error, onboarding

Not captured. The public pages loaded with content. No form was submitted, so there is no validation error. Onboarding sits behind signup and was not opened.
