# Hims UK design reference

A token set and component reference for Hims' UK marketing site, www.forhims.co.uk. Read on 2026-11-19 in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, signed out, plus the signed-in care portal (Treatments, Messaging, Profile) from an account created with the capture inbox. forhims.co.uk does not redirect to forhims.com. It is its own UK surface: `html.brand-hims.variant-care-sans`, Care Sans, a warm canvas, and a clay accent. The US host www.hims.com is a different domain and is not this package.

The site paints one light theme. A migration strip sits above the nav ("We're moving to a new platform"). Primary actions are black pills. Italic words in headlines take the clay accent `#cc835c`. Ketch consent ("About cookies on our site") was hidden and never clicked.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep the page on the warm canvas and the word in clay.** The hero sits on `#fbf8f5` (`light.color.page`). Category cards sit on `#eee2d7` (`light.color.surface`). Italic emphasis is `#cc835c` (`light.color.accent`). See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
2. **Do use a black pill for the primary action.** "Explore treatments" is black, white type, 48px tall, 48px radius. Hover keeps the white type. See [hero-cta-default](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png) and [hero-cta-hover](components/crops/hero-cta-hover.png).
3. **Do round fields at 12px and cards at about 20px.** Login and register inputs are white, 60px tall, 12px radius. Category cards are warm and softly rounded. See [input-default](components/crops/input-default.png) and [category-card-default](components/crops/category-card-default.png).
4. **Do set type in Care Sans, with Ivar Soft only where the product uses a serif.** The declared stack is `CareSans, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` (`core.font.sans`). See [hero-title-default](components/crops/hero-title-default.png).
5. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site and the care portal paint one light theme. No theme toggle was found. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [portal-treatments-light-default](screenshots/portal-treatments-light-default.png).
6. **Don't click the cookie banner.** Ketch's Customise and Accept all post consent. Hide the dialog. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png).
7. **Don't start a consultation, add a card, or enter a phone number.** Account creation stopped at email and password. Profile Edit, payment Edit, and every treatment quiz were left closed. See [portal-profile-light-default](screenshots/portal-profile-light-default.png).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were resolved on `documentElement` at www.forhims.co.uk in headless Chromium (Playwright) on 2026-11-19. Nine stylesheets were readable; none were blocked. 900 names resolved. Semantic colour comes from `--colors-*` (canvas, text, button, brand, ui, form). Type sizes come from `--fontSizes-*`. The fluid `--b-font-size-*` calcs stay in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) and are not flattened to one px. Component colours, radii and type were read from the live DOM with [tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs). Screenshots were quantised to 256-colour sRGB PNGs. The care portal screenshots were taken in a second headless context that held only the session cookie. The profile email was painted out before the PNG was quantised. Ketch consent was hidden with CSS, never clicked.

## What to distrust

- **Typography line heights were entered by hand.** Each `core.typography.*` size and family is declared (`--fontSizes-*`, `--b-font-family-sans`), and heading-large and heading-small take the declared `--fontWeights-*`; the line heights (1.05 to 1.45), the letter spacing and the other weights have no reading behind them and their sources say Reconstruction. The site's own `--b-line-height-*` roles are `calc()` expressions on the newer `--b-*` scale (about 1.01 at 32px for headings, 1.24 at 18px for body), and the measured titles paint 1.12.
The hero CTA's computed `backgroundColor` is transparent because the black fill is painted by a child or a pseudo layer. The screenshot shows the black pill. Use `light.color.button.primary` (`#000000`) for that control, and the crop for the shape. Care Sans and Ivar Soft are the product's faces and are not committed. The style guide falls back to Helvetica. `--colors-button-primaryHover` resolves to `transparent` with black text, which is the declared hover token, not a guess. The account menu drawer in the signed-in capture did not paint in the 1440 screenshot (the click registered, the drawer stayed off-canvas). The signed-out menu drawer in [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png) is the one to trust. The profile email region is a flat fill, not a measured text style.

## Fonts

Care Sans (variable, roman and italic) is loaded from www.hims.com and is proprietary. Ivar Soft is the declared serif. Sofia Pro is also declared in the vendor sheet and was not the painted marketing face (`html` carries `variant-care-sans`). Tokens fall back to Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif, and to Georgia for the serif. None of the product fonts are in this package.
