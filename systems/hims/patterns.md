# Patterns

## Marketing home

A migration strip, then a centred nav: wordmark, Weight / Hair / Sex / Learn, Login, menu. The hero is a warm page (`#fbf8f5`) with a large Care Sans headline, one italic clay word, a grey subhead, and a black pill. Four category cards sit under it. See [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [home-light-full](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

Sex and Learn open menus without leaving the page: [home-light-sex-menu](screenshots/home-light-sex-menu.png), [home-light-learn-menu](screenshots/home-light-learn-menu.png). The menu button opens a drawer: [home-light-menu](screenshots/home-light-menu.png). Phone width stacks the hero and hides the centre links: [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png), [mobile-home-light-menu](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Category pages

Weight, hair, ED and PE each have a long marketing page on the same chrome. [weight-loss-light-default](screenshots/weight-loss-light-default.png), [hair-loss-light-default](screenshots/hair-loss-light-default.png), [ed-light-default](screenshots/ed-light-default.png), [pe-light-default](screenshots/pe-light-default.png). About and how-it-works use the same shell: [about-light-default](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [how-it-works-light-default](screenshots/how-it-works-light-default.png).

`/c` is a short chooser, not the care portal: [treatments-light-default](screenshots/treatments-light-default.png). The three options were not followed. Following one starts a consultation.

## Login and register

`/customer/login` is a split card: product photo, Welcome back, email, password, Forgot your password, a disabled Login, and Create an account. See [login-light-default](screenshots/login-light-default.png). Empty email shows "Your email is required": [login-light-email-focus](screenshots/login-light-email-focus.png). Create an account opens a right drawer with email, password, a terms checkbox, a marketing opt-out, and a disabled Create account: [login-light-register](screenshots/login-light-register.png). Forgot password opens its own drawer: [login-light-forgot](screenshots/login-light-forgot.png). Nothing in the measurement run was submitted.

## Care portal

After an account exists, the nav swaps Login for Treatments, Messaging, Profile, and an Account pill. A new account's Treatments page is a welcome card and a black "View all other treatments" pill, not a list of plans: [portal-treatments-light-default](screenshots/portal-treatments-light-default.png). Messaging is `/customer/support`, a set of pill questions for patient support and a clinician. Those pills were not opened: [portal-support-light-default](screenshots/portal-support-light-default.png). Profile lists name, email, phone and birthday as empty or masked, then payment and password, each with an Edit control that was not pressed: [portal-profile-light-default](screenshots/portal-profile-light-default.png).

## Boundaries

Phone number, birthday, payment method, and any consultation or clinician thread were not entered. The UK site did not ask for a government ID on the account step. The next medical step was left closed on purpose.
