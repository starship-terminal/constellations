# Nudea patterns

Public marketing pages on nudea.com, signed out, 2026-11-19. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read values from [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Storefront shell

A 30px raven announcement bar sits above an 88px header (57px at 390). The header element is transparent over the page. On hover the stylesheet paints cotton at 80% opacity. The wordmark is an SVG, 141 by 20, filled raven. Desktop nav is eight words: All, Gifting, Sleep, Bras, Briefs, Black Friday, Fitting, World. Black Friday is the only red item.

[home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) at 1440. [home-light-phone.png](screenshots/home-light-phone.png) at 390, with the menu button in place of the word nav.

## Hero

The homepage opens on a full-bleed photograph. "Black Friday" is set in cotton on the photo, with an uppercase line under it and a cotton outline pill, "Shop now". The dark field is the photograph, not a theme token.

## Product

[product-light-default.png](screenshots/product-light-default.png). Title, struck compare-at price, sale price, a short Abril paragraph, circular colour swatches, the size row, a quantity stepper, and a raven pill. A red "Save" tag sits on the photograph. Accordions sit below the fold and were not opened.

## Collection

[collection-light-default.png](screenshots/collection-light-default.png). An uppercase eyebrow is the page title ("Collection: Nudea bras"). The grid is four 360px columns at 1440 and two 195px columns at 390. [collection-light-phone.png](screenshots/collection-light-phone.png).

## Editorial

[about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png) and [sustainability-light-default.png](screenshots/sustainability-light-default.png). A 36px Abril headline, 14px body, and a 20px pull quote. The founder credit is an uppercase 12px Neue Haas line.

## Search

[search-light-default.png](screenshots/search-light-default.png). The query "bra" returned a public results page. The search field style was read from `.search__input` on the product page. No account was used.
