# Nudea

Nudea is a Shopify store for underwear and sleepwear (nudea.com). This package records the public signed-out marketing surfaces: home, a product, a collection, about, sustainability, and search. Captured 2026-11-19, signed out, light only, at 1440 and 390. No account, no checkout, no payment.

### Principles

1. Set the page on cotton and the ink in raven.
2. Make the one filled action a raven pill.
3. Keep sale and the promo nav in the red.
4. Set headlines in Abril at weight 300.
5. Leave product photography square-cornered on the page and round the card image by 10px.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live page in the T3 preview browser, plus the `:root` custom properties in nudea.css and the Shopify Dawn base.css (`--cotton`, `--raven`, `--grid`, `--red`, the font families, the duration scale). Button hover and focus come from the stylesheet rules, because a programmatic hover could not be held. Screenshots were saved afterwards in headless Chromium on the same public URLs, after a probe returned the real storefront and not a challenge. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Do not sample colours from them.

## What to distrust

The header looks like a solid bar in the screenshots. The element is transparent. The only painted header fill that was read is `header-component:hover::after`, cotton at 80% opacity.

`--dark-cotton`, `--brick`, `--nude`, `--olive`, `--lilac` and `--peach-fuzz` are declared on `:root` and were not seen as fills on the pages opened. They are in the colour scale so the declared palette is not dropped. They are not semantic roles.

Dawn's `--buttons-radius` is 0. The button that is drawn uses `border-radius: 10rem`, which computes to 100px. Trust the computed pill, not the Dawn variable.

The hero dark field is a photograph. It is not a token.

Abril Text and Neue Haas Grotesk Display come from Adobe Fonts (kit `wxz8tft`). The style guide uses system stand-ins.

## Do and don't

- **Set the page on cotton and the ink in raven.** Body background is `#fbfaf2` and text is `#333333`. [screenshots/about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png).
- **Make the one filled action a raven pill.** Pre-order is 50px, radius 100px, cotton type in Neue Haas, uppercase, tracked out. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Keep sale and the promo nav in the red.** The sale tag and "Black Friday" are `#ad2c2c`. Nothing else in the header is. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Set headlines in Abril at weight 300.** The about headline is 36px on 46px. Do not bold it. [screenshots/about-light-default.png](screenshots/about-light-default.png).
- **Leave product photography square-cornered on the page and round the card image by 10px.** The product photo on the product page has no radius. The collection card image does. [components/crops/card-product.png](components/crops/card-product.png).

```bash
cd systems/nudea && python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/catalog.py design-md nudea
```
