# Nomad Goods design reference

Public marketing capture of [nomadgoods.com](https://nomadgoods.com/), measured signed out on 2026-11-19. The storefront redirected to `https://nomadgoods.com/sg/`. Pages read: home, the iPhone cases collection, one product page (Leather Mag Wallet with Find My), the about page, and a 404. Desktop viewport was 1440 by 900. Phone viewport was 390 by 844, on the about page and the open menu. No account, no email, no cart, no checkout.

The site is a light storefront. Page ground is `#f3f3f3`, cards and fields are white, type is black, and the primary action is a near-black pill. Gotham Bold carries headings and buttons. Gotham Book carries fields. Aleo carries body copy. A blue pill marks NEW. A clay pill marks sale labels such as Black Friday and HOT.

Screenshot frames failed in the preview browser (`The browser is not producing screenshot frames`). Values below come from `getComputedStyle` on the live page and from the Shopify stylesheets that page linked. Cross-origin sheets were not readable from the CSSOM, so those files were fetched from the same URLs.

### Principles

1. Black pill, white card.
2. One warm colour, one blue.
3. Gotham for chrome, Aleo for reading.
4. Do not invent a dark theme.
5. Do not submit the signup.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

The T3 preview browser opened `https://nomadgoods.com/` with a new tab. It landed on `https://nomadgoods.com/sg/` with no Cloudflare challenge. Cookie consent was accepted so the banner would not cover the header. A Klaviyo signup dialog was measured, then closed with its Close dialog control. No email was typed.

Custom properties on the root were layout names only (`--grid-edge`, `--grid-gutter`, `--frame-width`, `--universalMaxWidth`). Colour and type live in class rules: `type-*.css`, `buttons-*.css`, `fields-*.css`, `surfaces-*.css`, `links-*.css`, `app-*.css`, and `css-bundle-*.css` on `cdn.shopify.com`. Those files were fetched, then the classes that appear on the page were confirmed with `getComputedStyle`.

A rem in the stylesheets is stored as the pixel size the browser computed at a 16px root. Desktop type sizes are the 768px-and-up rules. The 390px capture confirms the smaller display size (47px, from `2.9375rem`).

## What to distrust

`raw/readings.json` holds the computed styles and the stylesheet names, not the stylesheets. Every value that came only from `type.css`, `buttons.css`, `fields.css`, `surfaces.css`, `labels.css`, `links.css` or `css-bundle.css` (the error red, the disabled grey, the placeholder grey, `#007aff`, the checkbox, tab-bar, status and textarea radii, the card shadow, every motion value, and four type styles) is labelled Reconstruction in tokens.json: typed in by hand at capture time, with no reading in the repo. No success colour was read, so there is no `light.status.success`; `light.status.warning` is the sale pill colour standing in.

The cookie consent banner was accepted in the preview tab (see How the values were read); every reading was taken after that click.

There are no screenshots. The preview browser returned `The browser is not producing screenshot frames` on both snapshot attempts. Do not treat a missing image as a layout you have seen.

Dark theme was not on the public pages. `color-scheme` was `normal`. There is no dark token set.

`button-dark` and `button-light` have an `:active` rule and no `:hover` colour change. Hover was not forced. Pressed was not held. The pressed token records the inset colour from the stylesheet, labelled as not held.

Error, checkbox, radio, textarea, tab bar, and disabled button colours come from `fields.css` and `buttons.css`. They were not painted on the pages that were open. The error red `#e3163b` and the disabled grey `#adadad` are authored, not seen in a validation state.

`#007aff` appears five times in the CSS bundle and was not the colour of any measured control. It is recorded as a bundle colour, not as the link colour. Links inherit black.

The Klaviyo dialog uses `NomadGothamBold` and a `#050505` signup button. That widget is not the storefront button system. The storefront primary button is `button-dark` (`#2f2f2f` face, white label, 48px, 100px radius).

Sale and NEW pill colours are inline on `.type-pill`, not custom properties. A later campaign can change them without changing the class names.

## Fonts

Gotham Bold and Gotham Book are loaded from Nomad's Shopify files as woff2, with `local("Gotham Bold")` and `local("Gotham Book")`. Aleo Regular is loaded from the same shop as a ttf. The tokens fall back to `sans-serif` and to Helvetica Neue, which is what `app.css` sets on `body` and `input`. The font files are proprietary and are not in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Black pill, white card.** Primary actions are the near-black `button-dark` pill. Secondary actions are the white `button-light` pill. See the Shop Now control on the home hero and the waitlist control on the product page, both 48px tall with a 100px radius.
- **One warm colour, one blue.** NEW is `#005bd3`. Sale labels such as Black Friday and HOT are `#cb794c`. Body text stays black on `#f3f3f3` or white.
- **Gotham for chrome, Aleo for reading.** Headings, prices, buttons, and pills use Gotham Bold with `-0.04em` tracking. Paragraphs use Aleo at 1.5 line height.
- **Do not invent a dark theme.** The public site did not ship one in this capture.
- **Do not submit the signup.** The footer email field and the Klaviyo dialog were measured and left empty.

## Storefront notes

The Singapore path (`/sg/`) is what the root URL served. Prices on the product page were in dollars (`$99`, `$118`) with a `$ SGD` currency control in the chrome. Announcement copy was a Black Friday countdown. That copy will change. The type and colour classes will not, unless Nomad ships a new CSS build.
