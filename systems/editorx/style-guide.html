Palette

The core set carries the measured neutrals and the Wix Studio brand actions (measured off the components, var-referenced where a --color_*/--wst-* variable resolves the same value); the declared blue/lime/clay/orange ramps and status hues come from the thunderbolt site theme. Light roles alias the palette; the "dark" set is the #0e0e0e chrome the page paints — a painted surface, not a user toggle.

Spacing and radius

Corners are sharp by default: the header CTA, plan cards and bands take 0px. The pills are stadiums — the hero CTA's computed cap, the 50px link pill (section links, the enterprise CTA), 21px on the template card's action, 50% circles for the footer socials, 2px on the template card itself.

Shadows

Almost no elevation: the plan card's soft ink-plans wash is the only measured shadow; focus rings draw as double box-shadows, not outlines.

Type

One family under five served names: Madefor — wix-madefor-display-v2 on the thunderbolt displays, wix-madefor-text-v2 on body, Madefor-Display/-Text on the marketing chrome, plain Madefor on plans. Display sizes are spx-scaled (they track the viewport): the measured 157.5/80.25/75/72.6/61/60/54/48 displays are the 1440px readings; the ramp runs down to 14/20 footer meta and the 10/24 badge.
