# Patterns

How the signed-out marketing pages combine the controls. Captured 2026-11-18 at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844.

## Marketing shell

A white sticky header, 85px, with a hairline `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)` along the bottom. The wordmark sits left. Products, Solutions, Developers and Resources sit in the middle. Log in (white, 8px radius) and Get Started (black, 8px radius) sit right. On a phone the middle links collapse and the two buttons move into the hero.

See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png) and [mobile-home-light-default.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Hero

One headline, Euclid Circular A at 96px, weight 500, tracking -3.84px, ink `#1e232e`. One word, Stablecoin, is indigo `#4c63ed`. A grey sentence sits under it. Two 36px buttons sit under that: Contact Sales (white) and Get Started (black).

See [home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png).

## Product cards

Two indigo panels sit under the hero. Each names a product and a one-line description in white on indigo. They are marketing cards, not the signed-in product.

See [home-light-full.png](screenshots/home-light-full.png).

## Account choice

Log in and Get Started open a dialog titled Choose your account. Two rows: Stablecoin Payment Gateway, which points at `dashboard.copperx.io`, and Stablecoin Financial Account, which points at `payout.copperx.io`. The dialog was photographed and closed. Neither host was opened.

See [home-light-account-dialog.png](screenshots/home-light-account-dialog.png).

## Pricing

A separate marketing page, still light, still Euclid Circular A. No plan was selected and no checkout was started.

See [pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png) and [pricing-light-full.png](screenshots/pricing-light-full.png).

## What is not a pattern here

Empty, loading, error, destructive confirmation, and feedback after an action do not appear on the public pages. The contact form is a Google Form iframe and was not filled. Search is not on the marketing header.
