# Components

Public controls measured on 2026-11-22, signed out. The earlier signed-in inventory stays in [components.md](components.md).

## Button, Get started

Transparent anchor, 50px tall, 12px radius, 15px duolingo-sans 700, uppercase, 0.8px letter-spacing, white label. The painted face is a `::before` at 46px, `rgb(88, 204, 2)`. Homepage width 330px. See [home-light-default](screenshots/public-2026-11/home-light-default.png).

## Button, I already have an account

Same size and type. Label `rgb(28, 176, 246)`. A 2px transparent border. The `::before` is white at 48px, so the lip is quieter than Get started. See [home-light-default](screenshots/public-2026-11/home-light-default.png).

## Button, Log in

On the empty overlay. 50px tall, 16px radius, white uppercase label. The `::before` is `rgb(28, 176, 246)` at 46px. Not clicked. See [login-light-empty](screenshots/public-2026-11/login-light-empty.png).

## Button, Sign in with Google, Facebook, Apple

White faces, 16px radius, 50px tall. Labels stay in the provider colours: Google `rgb(66, 133, 244)`, Facebook `rgb(59, 89, 152)`, Apple eel. Not clicked.

## Button, Next

Disabled on the empty age step. Label hare `rgb(175, 175, 175)`. The `::before` is swan `rgb(229, 229, 229)`. Not clicked. See [signup-light-empty](screenshots/public-2026-11/signup-light-empty.png).

## Text field

The login shell is polar `rgb(247, 247, 247)`, 2px swan border, 12px radius, 48px tall, 375px wide. The input itself is transparent, 44px tall, 20px duolingo-sans, eel text. Placeholders were "Email or username" and "Password". Both values were empty.

The age step shows one empty field, placeholder "Age". It was not filled.

## Link

Help Center breadcrumbs and efficacy body links use macaw. Course-strip labels are wolf `rgb(119, 119, 119)`. Footer links sit on owl green.

## Card

Course cards on `/courses` and `/register` are white tiles with a flag or subject icon, a bold name, and a learner count in gray. Hindi on the register page was in a gray selected state when the page loaded. It was not clicked. See [courses-light-default](screenshots/public-2026-11/courses-light-default.png).

## Menu

At 390, the courses hamburger opens a small white menu: Sign in, Get started, Download the Duolingo app, Site language: English. See [courses-light-phone-menu](screenshots/public-2026-11/courses-light-phone-menu.png).

## Not measured this pass

Hover, focus-visible, pressed, loading, and error. Checkbox, radio, switch, tabs beyond the efficacy nav, tooltip, toast, table, avatar, and badge. Those states live in the earlier signed-in specs and were not retaken.
