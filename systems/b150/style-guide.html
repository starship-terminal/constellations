Type

Two declared families: Fragment Mono for chrome (nav, buttons, footer at 10px uppercase) and Geist Variable for body and display (12px fields up to the 120px hero). The --text-* scale names sizes h1–h8, label, body and small with line-height/letter-spacing pairs; the measured components wear label 10px, body 12px, h2 96px and h1 120px.

Radius, layout and controls

Radius is a role, not a scale: 8px on fields and cards, 16px declared on cards, and literally infinite on the pill CTAs (Tailwind rounded-full computes to calc(infinity * 1px)). Controls run 40–56px: 40px pills, the 41px CTA, 56px fields, the 65px header.
