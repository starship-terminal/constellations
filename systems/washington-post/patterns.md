# Patterns

How the public Washington Post site combines the components above. Signed out, 2026-11-21.

## App shell

A white page, a thin header, the Postoni wordmark centered, section names in Franklin underneath. Left of the header: the sections button and the alerts button. Right: Save and subscribe, then Sign in.

Desktop home: [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png). Phone home: [mobile-home-light-default](screenshots/mobile-home-light-default.png).

## Navigation

The sections button opens a left drawer of every public section, a search field, and a subscribe link. Chevron rows open a second list. Section fronts then add their own link row (Politics, Opinions, Games, Election 2026). See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Story grid

The homepage and section fronts are a newspaper grid: a large headline, a dek, a byline link, and smaller cards. Politics: [politics-light-default](screenshots/politics-light-default.png). Sports: [sports-light-default](screenshots/sports-light-default.png). World: [world-light-default](screenshots/world-light-default.png). Story destinations were not opened for screenshots.

## Subscribe offer

A blue pill in the header, and a full offer page: [subscribe-light-default](screenshots/subscribe-light-default.png). The offer page was not submitted. The Black Friday softwall repeats the same offer as a dialog.

## Sign-in wall

[signin-light-default](screenshots/signin-light-default.png) and [mobile-signin-light-default](screenshots/mobile-signin-light-default.png). Email, then Amazon, Facebook, Google, Apple, and SSO. The branch stops there.

## Search

The drawer field is the search control. The empty results URL is [search-light-default](screenshots/search-light-default.png). The form was not submitted.

## Alerts

News alerts is a dialog of published headlines plus a create-account link that goes to the sign-in wall. The dialog screenshot was left out because a headline named a street sale.

## Games, weather, live, about

Index pages, not players: [games-light-default](screenshots/games-light-default.png), [weather-light-default](screenshots/weather-light-default.png), [live-light-default](screenshots/live-light-default.png), [about-light-default](screenshots/about-light-default.png), [accessibility-light-default](screenshots/accessibility-light-default.png).

## Empty, loading, error, destructive, onboarding

Not present on the public pages opened. Validation errors were not produced, because no form was submitted. Onboarding and account settings sit behind the sign-in wall.
