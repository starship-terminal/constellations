# Washington Post design reference

A token set and component reference for the public Washington Post site (https://www.washingtonpost.com/). Captured signed out on 2026-11-21 in headed Chrome through Playwright, at 1440×900 and 390×844. One light theme. The site's own token layer is WPDS (`--wpds-*`), read as computed custom properties on `documentElement`.

Headless Chromium and HTTP/2 both failed to load the site (`ERR_HTTP2_PROTOCOL_ERROR`, then a timeout). Headed Chrome returned 200. No password, code, Google, Apple, or form was submitted. Article and author pages were mapped and left out of screenshots.

## How the values were read

[tools/capture/public/measure.mjs](tools/capture/public/measure.mjs) opened the homepage and seven other public URLs in headed Chrome, walked every stylesheet the CSSOM would expose, and read the computed value of each `--wpds-*` and related custom property on `documentElement`. That dump is [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) (496 properties on the homepage). [tools/build_tokens.py](tools/build_tokens.py) copies the ones the package tokens use and labels each `$extensions.source` Measured, or Derived when a role is an alias or a second is converted to milliseconds.

Component boxes, fonts, and colours are in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json), from `getComputedStyle` on the live controls. Screenshots came from [tools/capture/public/shoot.mjs](tools/capture/public/shoot.mjs). [tools/capture/quantize.py](tools/capture/quantize.py) reduced them to 256 colours.

## What to distrust

- One theme. `color-scheme` was `normal` on every page. There is no dark set.
- Screenshots are 256-colour. Read colours from tokens.json.
- Some `*-full` phone shots are only the first four viewport tiles (about 3376 CSS px). The homepage grows past 14000 px. That boundary is recorded, not a full scroll of an infinite page.
- The subscribe hover crop may match the default if the site did not repaint on hover in the 200 ms wait. The hover token `light.accentHover` is the declared `--wpds-colors-cta-hover`, not a pixel sample.
- Postoni and Franklin are licensed. The style guide falls back to Georgia and system-ui. They are not in the repo.
- The sign-in screenshot shows the login wall, including the Google and Apple buttons. Those buttons were not clicked.
- Article text, bylines, and author pages are absent on purpose.

## Fonts

`--wpds-fonts-headline` is Postoni, then Postoni-fallback, then serif. `--wpds-fonts-meta` and `--wpds-fonts-subhead` are Franklin, then Franklin-fallback, then sans-serif. `--wpds-fonts-body` is Georgia, then Times New Roman, then serif. Postoni and Franklin are licensed to The Washington Post and are not distributed. The style guide uses Georgia and system-ui.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep the page white and the text near-black.** The homepage is `--wpds-colors-background` on `--wpds-colors-onBackground`. [home-light-default](screenshots/home-light-default.png).
- **Do use one blue for the subscribe action.** `--wpds-colors-cta` is the only filled button in the header. [button-subscribe-default](components/crops/button-subscribe-default.png).
- **Do set headlines in the serif and labels in Franklin.** The lead is Postoni. Section names are Franklin. [headline-default](components/crops/headline-default.png).
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site paints light. No toggle was on the pages opened.
- **Do pill the subscribe button and leave search as an underline.** The header CTA is a fully rounded blue pill. The drawer field is a box with a bottom rule, not a second pill. [button-subscribe-default](components/crops/button-subscribe-default.png). [text-field-default](components/crops/text-field-default.png).
- **Don't follow a story card into an account or a byline email.** Article and people pages were mapped and left out.

## Rebuild

```bash
cd systems/washington-post
node tools/capture/public/measure.mjs
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
```

Screenshots need headed Chrome. `PLAYWRIGHT` is the design-systems Playwright install. Full-colour originals stay in `~/.design-systems/washington-post/raw-shots`.
