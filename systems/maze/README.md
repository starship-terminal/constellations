# Maze design reference

Tokens and component notes for Maze (maze.co), captured **2026-11-18**. Desktop is 1440×900. Phone is 390×844. One light theme. Public routes: `/`, `/pricing/`, `/platform/`, `/platform/ux-reporting/`, `/customers/`, `/demo/`, `/resources/user-research-report/`, `auth.maze.co/login`, and the published sample report at `app.maze.co/report/[share-link]`. Signed out the whole time. No study was started. No form was submitted. No purchase.

Maze marketing is Next.js and Tailwind v4. Colour is an oklch custom-property palette on `:root` (`--color-neutral-*`, `--color-blue-*`, lime, cyan, purple, red, yellow). Display is Phonic, self-hosted. Auth and the report paint Inter and a blue action, `rgb(5, 104, 253)`.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export maze` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do paint the page on neutral 100 and the cards on white.
2. Do set the hero in Phonic light.
3. Do use a pill for Try Maze for free and a near-black fill for Get a demo.
4. Do use the blue action on Log in and on the report Sign up.
5. Do keep the login card at 8px radius on a lime collage.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.
7. Don't start a study from the promo bar.
8. Don't submit the demo form or the login form.
9. Don't treat the phone report as the product.
10. Don't ship the Phonic files.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles on the live DOM, 2026-11-18, light, 1440. Hover is a real pointer. Focus-visible is keyboard. Pressed is a forced `:active` pseudo-class, so no control fired. Stylesheets were harvested into [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json). oklch custom properties were converted to sRGB there. CookieYes on maze.co and OneTrust on auth.maze.co were hidden with CSS after the page painted. An earlier hide blanks the React tree. Neither banner was clicked. The survey promo was hidden the same way. Take the survey was never opened.

## What to distrust

- Studies, the app past login, and signup. `app.maze.co` redirects to login. Starter's "Sign up now" was not clicked. The survey at `t.maze.co` was not opened.
- The demo form and the footer newsletter. Both were left empty.
- The phone report. At 390 it paints "Window size too small" and asks for 800px.
- Phonic and Inter files. The family names are measured. The files are not in this package.
- Pressed states. They are forced pseudo-classes.
- The desktop platform menu shot was forced visible after hover missed in the capture session. The panel contents are the live DOM. The open animation is not.
- The home viewport shot is the top 900px of the full-page capture. Lenis can leave a viewport screenshot mid-scroll while reporting `scrollY` 0.
- `--color-neutral-800` (`#454545`) is lighter than `--color-neutral-700` (`#1c1c1c`). That is the declared scale, not a sorted ramp.

## Fonts

Phonic is the marketing display, weights 300, 400, 500 and 700, from `https://maze.co/fonts/phonic/phonic.css`. Inter is the auth and report UI. Neither file is shipped. The tokens name the families and fall back to the system sans.
