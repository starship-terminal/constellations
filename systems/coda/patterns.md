# Patterns

## Marketing shell

An orange rebrand banner sits above a cream header. The wordmark is Superhuman Docs. Product, Solutions, and Resources are menus. Gallery, Blog, and Pricing are links. Request a demo is a white pill. Get started is a black pill. The page body is white. The footer is peach (#fff6ec).

## Homepage

The first screen announces the Superhuman Docs rename. The h1 is "Your all-in-one collaborative workspace." Get started for free and Contact sales sit under it. Get started for free goes to the signup wall. Contact sales goes to a sales form that was not submitted.

## Pricing

https://coda.io/pricing redirects to https://superhuman.com/plans/docs. Plans are listed as Free, Pro, and sales-led tiers. Get Superhuman Free and Sign up it's free go to an account wall. Those links were not followed past the empty signup wall. Contact sales was not submitted.

## Login and signup

Both walls are a centered card: identity buttons, an empty work-email field, and a purple Continue. Login says "Welcome back". Signup says "Welcome to Superhuman Docs". Nothing was typed. No identity provider was clicked. No doc was created.
