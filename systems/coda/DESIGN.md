## Type

Body is Super Sans VF at 14px / 20px. Display headings are Calibre-R. The homepage h1 is 72px bold. Button labels are Inter, 16px semibold in the header and 20px semibold in the hero.

## Controls

Header Get started is a 40px black pill with 8px radius. Hero Get started for free is 62px with 12px radius. The login email field is 36px, 4px radius, fill #fcfaf7. Login Continue is 40px, fill #714cb6.
