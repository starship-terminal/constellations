# SKIMS

A signed-out capture of the public SKIMS shop at [https://skims.com](https://skims.com), redirected to `/en-sg`, prices in SGD, on 2026-11-22. Desktop 1440 by 900. The page is `#ffffff`, body type is Inter, and the hero is T-Star at 48px, weight 900, `#f6f6f6`. The buy button is 52px with a `2px` radius.
