# Telescope design reference

A token set for Telescope's public marketing page and its signed-out login. Read from **telescope.fyi** on **2026-11-19**, signed out. Marketing computed styles were read in the T3 preview tab at 1169 x 731, device pixel ratio 2. Login computed styles were read in the same tab at the same size. Desktop screenshots of the public home were taken in a separate headless load at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, both at 2x, because the preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". No account was created. No email was submitted. Google was not used.

The marketing page is beige `#f4f3f0`, ink `#1a1915`, Beausite for text, CM Geom for display ornaments, and DM Mono uppercase for labels. Sign up is a yellow pill `#e3f794`. Login is a separate dark app: page `#2b2b2e`, 8px controls, Beausite at 16px.

### Principles

1. Paint the marketing page beige and the action yellow.
2. Set labels in DM Mono, uppercase.
3. Keep the login form dark.
4. Leave Continue disabled until an email is present.
5. Do not invent a marketing dark theme.
6. Do not submit the login form from this reference.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Marketing custom properties were read from the live stylesheet `https://telescope.fyi/_nuxt/entry.tFlogPss.css` (550 rules) through the page's CSSOM, then checked against `getComputedStyle` on `html`, `h1.title.t-h1`, `.t-mono`, and `.ui-button.-yellow .btn-inner`. Login custom properties were read from `https://telescope.fyi/_next/static/chunks/3d7b25ofmhl7u.css` through the same page, on `body.dark` at `/login`. RGB channel triples were converted to hex (`227, 229, 231` is `#e3e5e7`). Component sizes came from computed styles, not from the screenshot.

The marketing root font-size is fluid. At 1169px it computed to 19px. A 1440px capture will compute a different root size. Token type sizes use the declared rem where the rule is a rem, and the computed px where the reading was the fluid result.

## What to distrust

The login light set (`authLight`) is a declaration. The signed-out page mounts `.dark`, so those colours were not computed.

`light.color.border` is derived from `color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-black), transparent 80%)`, written as `#1a191533`. It is not a declared hex.

`core.radius.full` is `9999px`. The stylesheet says `1e+08px` for `.rounded-full`. The marketing pill is not that token: its ends are `border-radius: 100%` caps plus an SVG.

Hover colours on Sign up are declared (`--button-hover-bg`, `--button-hover-color`). They were not computed from a real hover.

Screenshots are a second load of the public home page. They are not frames from the tab that was measured. Login has no screenshot. The preview browser error was "The browser is not producing screenshot frames".

Sohne, Romie and Founders Grotesk are named in the login font variables and were unloaded. Do not design the signed-out form in those faces.

## Fonts

Beausite is the marketing and login text face. The marketing site ships Beausite Classic Clear (400) and Beausite Classic Bold (700) as woff2 from its own host. Licence was not published on the page. Tokens fall back to `sans-serif`. The style guide uses Source Serif 4 only as a stand-in when Beausite is not installed.

CM Geom (400) is the marketing display face on `.t-h2-geom`, `.t-h3` and `.t-huge`. It is self-hosted. It was not painted on the login form.

DM Mono (400) is the marketing label face, self-hosted, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.03em`. Google Fonts ships DM Mono under the SIL Open Font License. The style guide loads that cut for the labels.

The login page also declares Sohne, Sohne Mono, Romie and Founders Grotesk. `document.fonts` reported them unloaded. They are not in the tokens as the form face.

## Do and don't

- **Paint the marketing page beige and the action yellow.** Page `#f4f3f0`, ink `#1a1915`, Sign up `#e3f794`. See [patterns.md](patterns.md).
- **Set labels in DM Mono, uppercase.** "Currently in beta", "Login" and "Sign up" use `.t-mono` at `0.65rem` on desktop, letter-spacing `0.03em`.
- **Keep the login form dark.** The signed-out page mounts `.dark` (`#2b2b2e`), with 8px fields and 40px buttons. Do not restyle it with the marketing yellow pill.
- **Leave Continue disabled until an email is present.** The empty state uses `#4e4f50` on `#3b3d3f`.
- **Do not invent a marketing dark theme.** The marketing CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` block.
- **Do not submit the login form from this reference.** Email and Google were not used.
