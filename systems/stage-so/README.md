# Stage.so

Stage.so was a browser wireframing product. On 19 November 2026, https://stage.so/ answered with a Spaceship domain-for-sale listing, title "stage.so for sale | Spaceship.com". This package records that public page. No Stage product surface, login, or account was available. Nothing was typed. Next was not followed, because it leaves for a spaceship.com checkout.

The listing was read in a T3 preview tab at innerWidth 1169, and again in headless Chromium at 1440 and 390 after that same public URL loaded with no Cloudflare challenge. The preview tab could not produce screenshot frames. The screenshots are the headless captures.

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview tab on https://stage.so/ (innerWidth 1169, innerHeight 731, devicePixelRatio 2). The same URL was then opened in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844, both at devicePixelRatio 2, after the preview tab had already loaded it with no Cloudflare challenge. Stylesheets exposed no custom properties. Fonts in use were GB Proxima Nova at 400, 500 and 700. Museo Sans was declared and not loaded.

The h1 computed color is `#2b2b2d`. At 1169 that word sits on the white side of the split, so the computed color matches the paint. The screenshots at 1440 and 390 paint the same word white on the navy field. The token keeps the computed value and says so.

Focus and hover were applied to Next in headless Chromium. The background stayed `#394eff`, the shadow stayed none, and the outline stayed none. Hover on the spaceship.com link added an underline.

## What to distrust

The page is a domain marketplace template. Its colours, type and geometry are Spaceship's, painted on the stage.so host. They are not evidence of the discontinued Stage product.

The domain name's computed color (`#2b2b2d`) and the white paint in the 1440 and 390 screenshots disagree. Use the screenshot for that word's paint, and the token for the computed style.

Headless screenshots are not preview-tab frames. The preview tab returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". The headless page title, body text and computed colours matched the preview readings.

The offer modal fields were in the DOM and not visible. Their 8px radius is a computed style of a hidden input, not a shown control.

## Fonts

GB Proxima Nova is the computed family, with a system sans stack after it. The family is loaded by the Spaceship listing. This package does not ship the font files. The tokens name the family and fall back to the system stack that the page itself declares.

## Do and don't

- **Use the navy field and the white card.** The listing paints `#171f66` behind a white 16px card. [screenshots/listing-desktop-default.png](screenshots/listing-desktop-default.png)
- **Use the brand pill for the primary action.** Next is `#394eff`, 40px tall, radius 24px. [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png)
- **Do not treat this as the Stage design product.** The host is a for-sale page. There is no Stage UI to copy.
- **Do not open checkout or the offer form from a capture.** Next goes to spaceship.com checkout. The offer modal asks for a phone number. Neither was entered.
