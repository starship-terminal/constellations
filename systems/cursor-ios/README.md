# Cursor for iOS design reference

A token set and component reference for Cursor's iOS app (Anysphere), the phone client for Cursor's coding agents: an Inbox of agents grouped by state, agent threads with their changes and pull requests, and a composer to start or follow up an agent. Use it to build a native iOS agent, developer-tool or dashboard app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: a near-black or off-white page, plain-text replies with no bubbles, two-line rows that carry state in small coloured marks (dots, branch icons, a red-green diffstat), rounded tiles and cards one step lighter than the page, iOS 26 glass circles and a glass composer floating over the content, and a single blue accent. It was captured from the shipped app, version 1.14.0 on iOS 26, through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account: dark on 2026-10-23 and light on 2026-10-24. Cursor follows the phone's appearance and has no setting of its own, so dark is the phone in dark mode and light is the phone switched to light mode for the second pass and back afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 38 captures: 18 in dark (Inbox, agent lists, a thread, the Changes, Customize, Model, Search, Profile, Add Workspace and Context sheets, menus and empty states) and the same 18 in light, plus 2 in light only (the Pull Requests sheet and the bottom of the profile). Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) nothing in them is masked: they show the owner's profile, repositories and agent titles, and no credentials or personally identifiable data. There is no Cursor web or desktop package in [catalog.json](../../catalog.json) to compare with.

This package contains no Cursor code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Anysphere's ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the icons, the tile glyphs or the brand.

## Brief

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export cursor-ios` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do let agent replies sit on the page as plain text.
2. Do carry state in small coloured marks, not in fills.
3. Do float the composer and nav controls as glass over the content.
4. Do draw the send button in the theme's inverse, not the accent.
5. Don't colour destructive actions.
6. Do lift surfaces by one small step of grey.
7. Do show focus with the caret alone.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Cursor for iOS is a native app, so there is no CSS. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 237 readings of 141 named values, most in both themes. Where a screen is laid out differently in light (the Changes sheet gains a header), the reading names one box per theme. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps the readings to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a thin mark, a small dot) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph or outline-icon colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `bbox`, `extent`, `find` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font families |

Scale steps (spacing, radii, the 42 pt icon button) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Two roles were read in light only, because their screens exist only in the light pass: the selected segment and the closed pull-request icon. Their dark tokens point at the closest dark role and carry the label Not captured. The activity heatmap's orange steps sit in `core.color.orange`, read in light.

## What to distrust

- **The fonts.** Not determinable from pixels. The interface face is a neo-grotesque close to the iOS system face, and the measured sizes land on 22, 21, 19, 17, 16, 15 and 10 pt. Inline code, chart axes and the version footer use a monospace face. Both families are `Not captured`.
- **Glass.** The nav circles, composer, search fields, '7 PRs' and Continue capsules and the pull-down menu are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a material that takes colour from what is behind it. Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: tiles 15.4 to 17.1 (16 used), the Changes card 15.4 (16), the banner 11.9, the expanded composer 25.6, menus 30.7 to 32.4, sheets 35.8 to 37.6.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Light-only screens.** The Pull Requests sheet and the profile bottom (streak, heatmap, Support and More, version) were captured in light only, so their components have no dark crop and `surface.segment` and `status.closed` have no dark reading.
- **The light caret straddles two pixel columns**, so `light.color.focus.caret` (#6F8DF2) reads paler than the dark caret (#436BF3); the light caret may be the same blue as the dark one.
- **Light and dark list content differs.** The light pass was a day later, so some agents, counts and orders changed (Needs Attention shows 3 in dark and 2 in light). Layout and roles are the same.
- **The neutral list is not a ramp.** `core.color.neutral` holds every grey the two themes use, sorted and numbered by order. It is not a published scale.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; Archive and Sign out were not tapped.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass and the profile charts band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do let agent replies sit on the page as plain text.** Replies have no bubble or card: numbered lists, semibold runs and inline code on a grey chip (`color.surface.code`), set straight on `color.bg.page`. Only a reply's changed files get a card. See [06-agent-thread](screenshots/dark/06-agent-thread-changes-card-diffstat.png) and [reply-text](components/crops/reply-text.png).
2. **Do carry state in small coloured marks, not in fills.** An agent row is grey text with a blue or grey dot, a violet merged or grey draft icon and a green and pink-red diffstat; nothing else on it is coloured. See [03-all-agents](screenshots/dark/03-all-agents-grouped-status-dots.png) and [list-row-agent-unread](components/crops/list-row-agent-unread.png).
3. **Do float the composer and nav controls as glass over the content.** The nav buttons are glass circles, the composer a glass capsule pinned to the bottom of every list and thread, and content scrolls under both; there are no bars. See [02-inbox](screenshots/dark/02-inbox-tiles-projects-workspaces.png) and [01-repo-agents-list](screenshots/dark/01-repo-agents-list-composer.png).
4. **Do draw the send button in the theme's inverse, not the accent.** Send is a near-white circle in dark and a near-black one in light; the blue accent is kept for unread dots, the caret and switches. See [composer-typed](components/crops/composer-typed.png) and its [light twin](components/crops/composer-typed-light.png).
5. **Don't colour destructive actions.** Archive sits in the more menu like Rename or Share, and Sign out is an ordinary row. See [11-agent-menu](screenshots/dark/11-agent-menu-pulldown.png) and [list-row-support-light](components/crops/list-row-support-light.png).
6. **Do lift surfaces by one small step of grey.** Tiles, cards and sheets are #181818 on a #141414 page in dark and #FCFCFC on #F7F7F7 in light,, with a hairline rim around the tiles. See [tile-inbox](components/crops/tile-inbox.png) and [tile-inbox-light](components/crops/tile-inbox-light.png).
7. **Do show focus with the caret alone.** A focused search field or composer keeps its glass fill and gains only a blue caret. See [13-search](screenshots/dark/13-search-sheet-focused-chips-rows.png) and [search-field-focused](components/crops/search-field-focused.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/cursor-ios/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/cursor-ios/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.surface.card`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass nav circles, menus, sheets, switches and pop-up selects rather than redrawing them, and SF Symbols for row and menu icons in place of Cursor's own. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
