# Rox design reference

A token set and component reference for Rox's public site. Captured from www.rox.com on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440x900 and 390x844. The marketing site is Framer. Docs are a separate public shell. The product itself is behind `run.rox.com` and Auth0. That wall was recorded and not entered. No email was typed. No form was submitted.

Rox paints a warm paper page (`#f7f6f4`) with square ink buttons (`#1c1917`), Geist at 14 for chrome, and Season Mix for display. Radius is 0 on buttons and cards, 4px on fields. There is no dark theme.

### Principles

1. Do keep buttons square.
2. Do spend ink on the one primary action.
3. Do set display in Season Mix and chrome in Geist.
4. Do open menus on hover on desktop and as a black drawer on the phone.
5. Don't style the agent from this package.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the pricing page CSSOM. Framer names them `--token-<hash>`. 106 of 194 names resolved on `body`. Semantic roles were then read from computed styles on the painted controls: body background, header buttons, plan CTA, contact field wrapper, display type. Hover and pressed were read with Playwright and CDP on the live page. Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from tokens.json.

## What to distrust

- The first crop pass measured Framer link wrappers (64px nav hits, default blue link colour). Those crops are not the painted label. States in `raw/states.json` are the ones to trust.
- Declared blue, amber, orange, red, and violet exist as Framer colours. They were not seen as status chips on the pages captured. They are labelled as declared, not as status UI.
- Body page colour `#f7f6f4` is computed on `body` and is not one of the hashed `--token-*` values.
- Season Mix and Geist are proprietary. The guide falls back to Georgia and system-ui.
- Article and docs leaves share templates. One of each was screenshotted. The sitemap lists 526 articles and 162 docs pages.
- The Gartner widget and customer logos are marketing. They are not product components.

## Fonts

Geist (400, 500, 600, 700) paints nav, buttons, and fields. Season Mix-TRIAL Regular (weight 420) paints display. Geist Mono and Fragment Mono are declared. Inter is declared as a fallback face. All are loaded by the site. They are not committed.

## Do and don't

- **Do keep buttons square.** Header and plan buttons use 0 radius. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Do spend ink on the one primary action.** Start now is `#1c1917` on paper. Contact Sales stays quiet. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **Do set display in Season Mix and chrome in Geist.** [screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png)
- **Do open menus on hover on desktop and as a black drawer on the phone.** [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png)
- **Don't style the agent from this package.** The workspace is behind Auth0. [site-tree.md](site-tree.md)
- **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public site has one.
