# Rox patterns

Public marketing site and docs. The signed-in agent is behind the login wall and is not in these shots.

## Shell

A 40px announcement bar, then a 64px nav: logo, six links, Contact Sales, Start now. On a light page the nav is paper. On the home hero it sits over the video. Phone replaces the links with a 33px menu button and a black drawer.

[screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png), [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png), [screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png](screenshots/mobile-home-light-menu.png).

## Pricing

Three square cards in a row. Individual and Teams show a price and "Start for free". Enterprise says "Contact us". Feature lists sit under the cards, outside the card border.

[screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png).

## Form

Contact is a centered heading, a logo strip, then one column of fields. Required marks are asterisks on the labels. The submit button is "Get in touch". The form was not filled and not submitted.

[screenshots/contact-light-default.png](screenshots/contact-light-default.png).

## Docs

A separate shell: logo plus "Docs", a Login button, a horizontal section nav, a left page list, and a search field that opens a dialog.

[screenshots/docs-light-default.png](screenshots/docs-light-default.png), [screenshots/docs-light-search.png](screenshots/docs-light-search.png).

Customer pages are a long editorial template. One story, MongoDB, stands in for the template. The other 25 stories share it and were not each screenshotted.
