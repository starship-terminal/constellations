### Principles

1. Use the cyan brand for the primary action.
2. Keep the app page on the cool gray.
3. Set body type in ABC Repro, with a system fallback.
4. Use 12px and 16px radii on buttons.
5. Leave prices out of the system.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)` in the T3 preview browser on 2026-11-19.

The watch page (`https://vimeo.com/watch`, html class `light`) exposed 409 `--vimeo-*` properties. Those are in [raw/css-vars.json](raw/css-vars.json) and become `core`. The marketing page (`https://vimeo.com/solutions/marketing`) uses a second set, `--colors-chroma-*` stored as RGB triplets, plus button and form colours. That subset is in [raw/marketing-vars.json](raw/marketing-vars.json) under `core.color.marketing`. Control metrics are computed styles in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

One Next.js chunk and `player.css` were cross-origin, so `cssRules` could not be read. The custom properties were still resolved on the element. Nothing was sampled from a screenshot.

## What to distrust

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on every snapshot. Watch, pricing, and login have computed-style evidence and no preview screenshot. The marketing screenshots were taken later in headless Playwright on the same public URL, which had already loaded without a Cloudflare challenge. They are 256-colour PNGs. Read colours from the tokens.

Pricing figures on `/upgrade-plan` were shown in Singapore dollars. Those amounts are geo-priced and are not tokens. The plan card radius computed as `36.8px`, while `--vimeo-radii-3xl` is `36px`. The card token keeps the computed value.

`--vimeo-fontWeights-regular` computed as `350`. Several buttons computed `font-weight: 400` on `ABCRepro-Medium`. Both readings are kept. Dark mode was not present on these signed-out pages.

## Fonts

ABC Repro, ABC Repro Medium, and ABC Repro Mono are proprietary. The tokens name them and fall back to system-ui, Helvetica, and Arial. They are not shipped in this package.

## Do and don't

- **Use the cyan brand for the primary action.** The marketing Join and Start for free controls, and the auth Continue with email button, are `#17d5ff`. See [components/crops/button-primary-default.png](components/crops/button-primary-default.png).
- **Keep the app page on the cool gray.** Watch and pricing use `#f6f8fa`, not pure white. Surfaces and the auth dialog are white.
- **Set body type in ABC Repro, with a system fallback.** Marketing display is 72px at weight 500 and -3.6px tracking. The app uses the same family at smaller sizes.
- **Use 12px and 16px radii on buttons.** Header controls are 12px. The cyan email submit and pricing trial buttons are 16px. Do not invent a single radius.
- **Leave prices out of the system.** The amounts on the pricing page depend on the visitor's region.
