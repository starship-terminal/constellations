# Intercom design reference

A token set and component reference for Intercom's public, signed-out marketing surface — for building marketing pages, pricing pages, forms and help surfaces in the style of the real thing. It was read from **www.intercom.com** on **2026-11-18**, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844 (plus 1920 and 1024 layout passes). Captured: every public route the marketing site serves — /, /pricing, /helpdesk, /helpdesk/inbox, /helpdesk/copilot, /customers, /integrations, /security, /view-demos, /contact-sales (first step only), /why-choose-intercom, /enterprise, /blog — plus the help center (/help/en/) and the changelog (/changes/en) — in the painted light theme; the nav mega-menu, the pricing FAQ, the contact-sales select listbox and the mobile menu sheet as transient states; plus 390px passes on home, pricing, helpdesk, help and contact-sales.

intercom.com is worth a capture because it declares a complete design layer to the browser: a Tailwind v4 `@layer theme` on `:root` — the warm neutral ramp (`--color-white/-off-white/-cream/-chalk/-canvas/-linen/-clay`, the gray steps, `--color-off-black`, `--color-navy`), the blueprint blues (`--color-blueprint-*`, `--color-blue` **#000ce1**, `--color-helpdesk` **#0007cb**), the product accents (`--color-fin` **#ff5600**, `--color-sales`, `--color-ecom`, `--color-operator`, `--color-voice-*`), a responsive `--text-{style}-{breakpoint}` scale (heading-1..6, callout, body-xl/lg/base, label-lg, button-lg/sm across mobile/tablet/small-desktop/desktop), the `--font-sans/-serif/-mono` stacks (Saans, Serrif, SaansMono plus the campaign faces Beretta/Honey/Maxi/Ozik/Planar/Raster), a `--radius-*/--blur-*/--container-*/--z-index-*` geometry and the full `--ease-*` Penner library — plus the unlayered `--button-*` state matrix, `--body-bg` and `--color-focus-outline`. Scoped sets resolve on their own probes: `.theme-light` (the `--color-nav-*` roles), `.brand-i2y4` (the campaign palette), `.intercom-legacy` (the older MediumLL-era palette the help center and changelog still run) and `[data-media-player]`. The architecture: every marketing page boots **body.theme-light** on a cream ground (`--body-bg` = `--color-cream`); there is no dark theme to capture — no dark class, attribute or repaint anywhere (`themeChecks` in raw/css-vars.json). **Boundaries:** app.intercom.com (the real product behind Log in), /signup trial onboarding and every post-auth or purchase path were never entered; the contact-sales form was never advanced past its first step — Next was measured with forced pseudo states, never pressed, and no option was chosen in its select. Nothing was signed into, typed (beyond the one throwaway filled-state reading, deleted on the spot), searched, chosen, bought or submitted; the only presses were local UI toggles the pages' own chrome provides (the nav mega-menu, the billing switch, a FAQ row, the select's own listbox, the mobile menu button), and pressed states are DevTools-forced pseudo-classes, never real presses.

## How the values were read

- **Variables** come from the stylesheets themselves (`tools/capture/vars.mjs`): every same-origin CSS response plus the inline CSSOM is reparsed, rules are binned theme/nav/brand/legacy/media/component/utility/tailwind, and every custom property is resolved in probe documents carrying the real sheets and the live pages' own classes — the root probe in `body.theme-light`, then the `.brand-i2y4`, `.intercom-legacy` and `[data-media-player]` probes for the scoped sets. `tokens.json` never publishes a raw `oklab()`/`lch()`/`color(srgb)` declaration — every colour is the painted sRGB value.
- **Geometry** is the live DOM (`tools/capture/public/measure.mjs`): bounding boxes and computed styles per component and state — hover by real mouse, focus-visible by keyboard, pressed by CDP `CSS.forcePseudoState` (no real press, so no link can fire and no form can submit), opened/focused states via the component's own toggle. Hidden responsive duplicates are skipped by picking the first visible match; components a recapture misses keep their earlier states under `recaptureMissed` provenance.
- **Screenshots** (`tools/capture/public/public.mjs` + `tools/capture/screenshot.mjs`) verify the painted theme before writing a sidecar: the capture record carries the viewport, device scale factor, theme class and document size next to every PNG. Pages taller than one GPU texture are shot in `-full.partN.png` chunks and stitched.
- **Layout** (`tools/capture/layout.mjs`) reads shell geometry, grids, fixed elements, max-widths and the h1 at each width — one fresh context per page because the CDN resets a session after a run of rapid loads.
- **Motion** (`tools/capture/motion-probe.mjs`) reads computed transitions and animations at rest, then again under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, on six pages — 780 moving elements total.
- **The icon collection** (`tools/capture/icons.mjs`) hashes each inline svg's geometry, names it from its control's label, and records where each was seen in `icons/INDEX.tsv`.

## What to distrust

- **app.intercom.com and /signup**: the product and trial flow were never entered — the Log in, Start free trial, Get started and contact links were measured, never followed.
- **The contact-sales form past step one**: Next was measured with CDP-forced pseudo states only, so step two onward is a boundary, not a capture. No option was chosen in the company-size select and the marketing checkbox was never ticked.
- **The five recapture-sensitive header components** (logo, nav-login, nav-cta): measured on one pass, preserved on a later pass that missed them — recorded as `recaptureMissed` provenance in the JSON, not measured as absent.
- **Dark theme**: none exists on the public surface — no dark class or attribute, no `prefers-color-scheme` repaint (`themeChecks` in raw/css-vars.json). `tokens.json` ships no dark set; that is evidence of absence, not a gap.
- **Serrif**: declared (`--font-serif` under `.brand-i2y4`) but no captured page painted it, so its tokens describe the declared stack, not a rendered sample.
- **Pressed states** are DevTools-forced; a script-applied mousedown style would not show.
- **Reduced motion**: the probe reports a partial story — most pages keep their transitions under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the per-page results are in `raw/motion.json`.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs**; read exact colours from the tokens or the raw files. Animated sections (hero canvases, marquee rows) keep moving, so two captures of the same view can differ.
- **Sidecar theme classes.** The 44 committed `screenshots/*.capture.json` have the kit's flat `actual.theme` with an empty `classes` list: they do not record `body.theme-light`. This package's `tools/capture/screenshot.mjs` later merged `<body>`'s classes into that list; it now writes the kit's shape, `<html>` only in `actual.theme.classes`, with `<body>`'s markers (`theme-light` / `theme-dark` included) in `actual.theme.body`. The committed sidecars stay as they are until a recapture rewrites them (the script was edited, not run).
- **The footer was read on /blog.** `footer`, `footer-link` and `locale-select` were measured on whatever page the run opened last, which was /blog. The footer is the same on every page, so the values hold; `tools/capture/public/measure.mjs` now opens the home page for the footer group (edited in the 2026-11 audit, not run).
- **2026-11-21 added pages, not new token readings.** The site-tree pass shot the public routes the first capture skipped. Those screenshots are evidence of layout. Token values were not re-read from them. Unread values stay Not captured.
- **The tickets demo is a published marketing mock.** The email and the street in that mock were painted out of `tickets-light-default.png`, `tickets-light-full.png`, `mobile-tickets-light-default.png` and `mobile-tickets-light-full.png`. Names in the same mock were left, because the product publishes them.
- **The Fin 4 dialog** opens and shows a Close control and a Play control. The player stayed on its loading frame in headless Chromium, so the played state is Not captured.

## Do and don't

1. **Do paint the one theme honestly.** Every page is `body.theme-light`: cream `--body-bg` ground, off-black ink, white chrome ([home](screenshots/home-light-default.png)). There is no dark set to invent.
2. **Do spend the helpdesk blue on interaction, not surfaces.** #0007cb is the hover/pressed repaint of every primary button and the tint of the savings badge; resting chrome stays off-black on white or ghost on cream ([CTA](components/crops/hero-cta-default.png), [badge](components/crops/promo-badge-default.png)).
3. **Do build controls on the declared `--button-*` matrix.** 40px height, 14px padding-x, 4px radius — the primary's off-black → helpdesk → 85%-helpdesk path is tokenised as `component.button.*` and aliased into `light.action.*` ([states](components/crops/nav-cta-hover.png)).
4. **Do pair the font chains the way the site does.** Saans carries the marketing site, SaansMono the eyebrow/label micro type, IvoryLLSub-Light the help center headings, the MediumLL stack the changelog ([eyebrow](components/crops/eyebrow-default.png), [help hero](components/crops/help-hero-default.png)).
5. **Do keep fields quiet and square-ish.** Off-white fill, black/20 ring, 2px radius, 40px tall — the input and select share the chrome, the listbox hangs off the select's own press ([field](components/crops/input-default.png), [listbox](screenshots/contact-sales-light-select-open.png)).
6. **Do respect the boundary list.** Everything behind Log in, /signup, purchase and the contact form's later steps is unmeasured — describe it as a boundary, never as captured.
7. **Don't style the product app from this package.** The inbox/tickets/reports product at app.intercom.com is a different surface that was never entered ([Log in](components/crops/nav-login-default.png)).
8. **Don't invent a dark theme or a `dark` set.** The stylesheets declare no dark surface and no control repaints one — that absence is the finding.
9. **Don't fake the form.** The form was never advanced or submitted; the one filled-state reading used a throwaway address, deleted immediately ([form](components/crops/form-default.png)).
10. **Don't copy the brand assets.** The wordmark, icons, illustrations, product screenshots and the Saans/Serrif/MediumLL faces are Intercom's and its licensors'; the tokens describe the system, not its artwork.
11. **Don't treat the older surfaces as the current system.** `/help/en/` and `/changes/en` run the `.intercom-legacy` palette and MediumLL-era faces — real evidence, but the i2y4 marketing system is what the core set describes.
