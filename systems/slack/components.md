## Gaps

- Checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, tooltip, toast, table, avatar and badge were not present as interactive public controls on the captured pages.
- Disabled, loading and error states were not shown without submitting a form. No form was submitted.
- Dialog: the region dialog and the product mega menu were screenshotted (home-light-region.png, home-light-product-menu.png). Their open state is Observed, not a separate measured component.
- Search field on the marketing header was not found by role after the search button opened; the search screenshot is home-light-search.png. The contact-sales text field was measured instead.
- Billing toggle was not found as a button named monthly or annual.
- Dark theme: not painted on the public pages. Not captured.
- Signed-in workspace components: behind https://slack.com/signin and https://app.slack.com/get-started. Stopped. Not captured.
