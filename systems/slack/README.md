# Slack

Slack (slack.com) is a work platform. This package records the public marketing site and the signed-out sign-in chrome, measured on 2026-11-19 from a live T3 preview tab. No workspace was created. No email was submitted. Google, Microsoft, Apple, and company SSO were not followed. No card was entered. The signup wall at https://slack.com/get-started#/createnew was not entered.

The live CSS viewport was 1169 by 731 at devicePixelRatio 2, then 390 by 844 on the home tab. Marketing and sign-in both paint a light page. A signed-in dark theme exists in the stylesheet and was not applied.

## How the values were read

Custom properties were read from the stylesheets on https://slack.com/intl/en-sg/ and https://slack.com/signin#/signin, then resolved with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement)`. Component colour, type, radius, padding and size were read with `getComputedStyle` on the live elements. Marketing colour names came from Spacesuit classes (`.c-button.v--primary`, `.c-dark-ai-container`). Sign-in colour names came from Slack Kit (`--dt_color-*`, `--dt_static_*`).

The preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames" on `preview_snapshot`, twice. No screenshots were invented. Headless Playwright was not launched against the sign-in page.

## What to distrust

The CSS viewport was 1169, then 390 on the home tab. `preview_status` still reported 1280 by 800 after `preview_resize`. The 390 readings use `innerWidth` from the page. Do not treat the 1169 readings as a 1440 capture. The sign-in email field was already focused, so its box-shadow is the focus ring, not the resting border. Marketing primary `#730394` and Slack Kit aubergine `#4a154b` are both measured and are not the same colour. The aubergine section `#481a54` is a third value. The nav background is transparent. Screenshot folders hold a note, not images.

## Fonts

Salesforce-Avant-Garde is the marketing and sign-in heading face (`--font-family-heading`). Salesforce-Sans is the marketing body (`--font-family-body`). Slack-Lato is the sign-in body, with Slack-Fractions and appleLogo in the stack. Slack-Roboto-Mono is the monospace face on the sign-in page. These families are named as computed. The font files are not in this package. Tokens keep the measured stacks and fall back to system-ui and monospace.

## Do and don't

- **Use the white marketing page.** Home and pricing render on `#ffffff` with black headings and `#1d1c1d` body text on the sign-in chrome. [patterns.md](patterns.md) describes that shell.
- **Use the purple 4px button for the marketing primary action.** "GET STARTED" is white text on `#730394`, 14px, weight 700, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.798px. [components.md](components.md) lists the sizes.
- **Use aubergine 12px controls on the sign-in form.** The submit is `#4a154b`, 44px tall, radius 12px. The email field matches that height and radius. [components.md](components.md) records the focused ring.
- **Keep signup as a wall.** https://slack.com/get-started#/createnew was not entered. Do not invent a workspace behind it. [components.md](components.md) records the gap.
- **Do not invent a screenshot.** The preview browser produced no frames. [screenshots/](screenshots/) says so.
