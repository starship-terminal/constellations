# Mapbox public site tree

Mapped from the live homepage at https://www.mapbox.com/ on 2026-11-21, signed out, in headless Chromium at 1440×900, before screenshots. The page loaded (HTTP 200, title "Mapbox | Maps, Navigation, Search, and Data"). No password, code, or form was submitted.

The marketing site is one Webflow site (`data-wf-site=6050a76fa6a633d5d54ae714`). It paints light. No theme toggle was found. A OneTrust banner ("We care about your privacy") sits at the bottom with Customize, Strictly necessary only, and Accept all. Those buttons were not clicked. The banner was hidden in CSS for later shots so it does not cover controls.
