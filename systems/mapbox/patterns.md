# Mapbox patterns

Public marketing site, 2026-11-21. The signed-in console was not captured.

## Header

A 63px bar: wordmark, EN language button, Log in, Sign up pill, then Products, Solutions, Developers, Company, Resources, Pricing, Contact us. Products through Resources open mega menus. Pricing and Contact us are pages. Log in and Sign up leave for account.mapbox.com. See [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Hero

White page, large Cera Pro headline, one blue pill ("Get started for free") and a second action ("Contact us"). A carousel of product stories sits under the headline. Slide buttons change the card. They do not leave the page.

## Pricing

Plan blocks on a white page. Subscribe and Contact us are the same blue pill. A stroke "Get started" sits on the free tier. The signup links are walls and were not followed.

## Contact

A HubSpot form: a combobox labelled "How can we help you?" and a text field. Focus turns the border `#007afc`. The form was not filled and not submitted.

## Menus

Opening Products shows a multi-column list of product pages, each a public URL on www.mapbox.com. The language button opens English and 日本語.

## Phone

The same pages at 390×844. The header collapses to a menu control. Product pages keep the white page and the blue pill.

## What is not a pattern here

Docs search, the console, Studio, and onboarding live on other hosts or behind the sign-in wall. They are recorded in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md) and not drawn from this package.
