## Gaps

- Signup form. The URL returned a captcha. Fields were not reached.
- Identity buttons on the login wall. Not clicked, so their hover and next screens are unknown.
- Hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled, loading, and error.
- Checkbox, radio, switch, textarea, toast, tooltip, table, avatar.
- Signed-in repository chrome, issues, pull requests, settings, and the dashboard.
- Marketing dark theme. Not painted. Dark tokens are from the login wall.
