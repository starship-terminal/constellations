# Rive patterns

How the public pages combine. Screenshots are in [screenshots/](screenshots). The site tree of which control opens which page is [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## App shell

A fixed black header, 63px, with uppercase Inter labels and one white GET STARTED chip on the right. PRODUCTS, USE CASES, and RESOURCES open mega menus in place. At 390px those labels collapse into `Open menu`. See [home-dark-default.png](screenshots/home-dark-default.png) and [mobile-home-dark-menu-open.png](screenshots/mobile-home-dark-menu-open.png).

## Marketing hero

Black page, Orbitron display, a white mono COPY chip, and a segmented demo switcher (`Show Rive City` through `Show Duolingo`). PLAY IT LIVE leaves the host. The capture stays on rive.app.

## Pricing

Four plan columns under a YEARLY / MONTHLY text toggle. GET STARTED leaves toward the editor or signup. GET IN TOUCH goes to `/contact`. See [pricing-dark-default.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-default.png) and [pricing-dark-monthly.png](screenshots/pricing-dark-monthly.png).

## Newsletter

The same email field and SIGN UP button repeat in the footer. The field was not filled and the form was not submitted. See [newsletter-dark-default.png](screenshots/newsletter-dark-default.png).

## Login wall

`/login/` shows email or username, password, CONTINUE WITH GOOGLE, and Use single sign-on. The branch stops there. Screenshot [login-dark-wall.png](screenshots/login-dark-wall.png) records the wall. Password was not submitted.

## Empty, loading, error, destructive, onboarding

Not present on the public marketing pages that loaded. The editor, account, and file library sit behind the login wall.
