# Patterns

No screenshots. The preview browser did not produce frames. These notes are from the DOM and computed boxes at 1169 x 731 CSS px.

## Marketing home

https://rive.app/ is a long black page (scroll height about 20015px). A 64px bar holds uppercase Inter links (Products, Use cases, Resources, Pricing, Downloads, Docs) and a white GET STARTED pill that points at https://editor.rive.app/. The hero headline is Orbitron, 48px, weight 900, uppercase, white. Body copy is Inter 16px at 75% white. A prompt block and a COPY button sit under the hero. The newsletter field at the foot asks for an email and was not submitted.

## Pricing

https://rive.app/pricing uses the same black page and the same nav. Four plans sit in a row: Free $0, Cadet $9, Voyager $32, Enterprise $120. Names and prices are Orbitron. A yearly/monthly pair sets the active label to white and the other to rgb(170, 170, 170). Plan buttons were not clicked. No checkout was opened.

## Login and signup

https://rive.app/login/ and https://rive.app/signup/ share a split. The left half is a purple canvas, rgb(60, 43, 75), with a white caption card (radius 2px, padding 16px 20px). The right half is a white panel, 585px in this viewport. The form column is 360px, padded 48px on the block axis. Google sits above the fields. A text divider reads OR. Login asks for email or username and password, then LOG IN, a single-sign-on link, and a create-account link. Signup asks for email and password, shows a checked marketing checkbox, and a SIGN UP button. Neither form was submitted. SSO was not opened. Google was not clicked.

The auth footer is black, with muted links, and sits under the split.

## Docs

https://rive.app/docs/getting-started/introduction is a Mintlify page. The page background is rgb(14, 12, 11). The sidebar measured 288px. The heading is Tomorrow at 36px, color rgb(230, 227, 223). Nav links are Inter 14px, rgb(166, 163, 159), and the current item is the heading color. Search is a 36px pill with 12px radius. The dialog it opens was not opened.
