# West Elm design reference

A token set for West Elm's public web storefront (westelm.com), for building in the style of a real furniture retailer. It was read from www.westelm.com on 2026-11-19, signed out, in the T3 preview browser at a measured 1169×731 CSS viewport (devicePixelRatio 2, visualViewport scale 1): the homepage, The Winter Edit collection, the Hyde Dining Chair product page, and the public account email form. Nothing was added to cart, checked out, or submitted. The email overlay also asks for a mobile number, and that field was not filled.

West Elm paints one light theme. Ink is #212121, not pure black. Headlines are Sanomat. UI text is GT Eesti Text at a light weight. Corners are square. Red (#af1a31) is reserved for the sale price and Add to cart. A burgundy strip (#5b0819) carries the sale banner.

### Principles

1. Do keep ink at #212121.
2. Do set headlines in Sanomat and UI in GT Eesti Text.
3. Do keep corners square.
4. Do reserve red for conversion.
5. Do track the small caps.
6. Don't invent a dark theme.
7. Don't round the swatches.

## How the values were read

Everything was read from the live page in the T3 preview browser, signed out.

- Custom properties on the page are Tailwind internals (`--tw-*`) plus a few animation variables. West Elm does not expose a `:root` design-token layer. Colours and type were read with `getComputedStyle` on the elements named in [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).
- Same-origin stylesheets were fetched and searched for button, focus and banner rules: `global-we-486d7fda434f11c2ad3eb0e98c1067c705836808.css` and `content-we-534fcc0714be69e51163ae92750dfff97dbcb28c.css`.
- The email overlay was measured, then dismissed with NO THANKS. It was not submitted.
- `tools/build_tokens.py` writes tokens.json from those readings. `tools/build_style_guide.py` and `tools/build_components_md.py` write the guide and the inventory. The inventory's text, values included, was typed into `build_components_md.py` at capture time; it does not read raw/, so a recapture must update that text by hand.

## What to distrust

- The viewport is 1169×731, the size the preview pane actually had. It is not a 1440 or 390 capture.
- [screenshots/specimen-light-default.png](screenshots/specimen-light-default.png) is a local render of the style guide, not a capture of westelm.com. `preview_snapshot` returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". A headless Playwright load of the public URLs returned "403 - Restricted Access", and those images were deleted.
- Add to cart hover (#7a0000) and disabled (#d48c8c), and the primary button hover (#858585), come from stylesheet rules. They were not hovered live.
- Focus outlines in the stylesheet do not all agree (`solid 2px #2563eb`, `dotted 1px currentColor`, `.125rem solid #1a1a1a`). The token records the blue one. A live focus ring was not forced.
- Karla and freight-display-pro load on the homepage. No measured heading, link, button, price or field used them.
- The filter button's computed family is `GT-Eesti`, an older face name, not `GT Eesti Text`.

## Fonts

**GT Eesti Text** is the UI face (weights 300, 375, 400, 500), served from `westelm.com/netstorage/fonts/design-system/`. **Sanomat** is the display face (weights 300, 400, 500), from the same host. Both are proprietary. The tokens name them and fall back to Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif and Georgia, Times, serif. They are not committed.

## Do and don't

1. **Do keep ink at #212121.** Body text, nav and filled buttons use that ink. Pure black shows up on the overlay body and the filter label, and it is not the house text colour. See [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json).
2. **Do set headlines in Sanomat and UI in GT Eesti Text.** Hero titles are 48px weight 300, tracking 0.96px. Nav and buttons stay in GT Eesti Text. See [style-guide.html](style-guide.html).
3. **Do keep corners square.** Buttons, fields, cards, swatches and the dialog are `border-radius: 0`. The quantity stepper's 2px left corners are the exception. See [components.md](components.md).
4. **Do reserve red for conversion.** #af1a31 is the sale price and Add to cart. Other CTAs are ink on white or white on ink. The sale banner is burgundy #5b0819, not the same red. See [patterns.md](patterns.md).
5. **Do track the small caps.** Category nav, utility links, buttons and footer headings are uppercase with 0.4px to 1.4px of tracking. A button with no tracking looks wrong. See [components.md](components.md).
6. **Don't invent a dark theme.** The public pages did not show one.
7. **Don't round the swatches.** Fabric choices are square thumbnails with a 1px #d9d9d9 border.
