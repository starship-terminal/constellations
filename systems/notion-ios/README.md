# Notion for iOS design reference

A token set and component reference for Notion's iPhone app: the sidebar tree, Notion AI chat, search, pages, databases, sheets and Settings. Use it to build a native iOS notes, docs or workspace app (SwiftUI) that borrows its proportions and structure: two warm greys (a navigation grey and a content canvas), top tabs instead of a tab bar, floating iOS 26 glass at the bottom, text-first rows with no cards in the main lists, grouped cards only inside sheets, and one blue accent. It was captured from the shipped app on iOS 26 through iPhone Mirroring, signed in to the owner's account: dark on 2026-10-23 with the app on Use system setting and the phone in dark mode, light on 2026-10-24 with the app's Settings > Preferences > Theme set to Light and put back to Use system setting afterwards. The device screen is 390 x 844 pt; the captures are 812 x 1780 px at 2 px per pt, with the screen at (16, 76) inside each PNG.

It holds 46 captures: 29 in dark (home, the four tabs, search, Notion AI, a page, a database, sheets, menus and Settings) and 17 of the same screens in light. Under the repository's rule (AGENTS.md) other people's names and avatars are painted out (the Inbox access requests and two Meetings note titles, by [tools/redact-rules/notion-ios.json](../../tools/redact-rules/notion-ios.json)); the owner's own pages, chats and profile stay, and no credentials or personally identifiable data appear.

This package contains no Notion code, and none of its artwork has been redrawn. The screenshots and the visual system they show are Notion Labs' ([docs/RIGHTS.md](../../docs/RIGHTS.md)). Borrow structure and proportions, not the AI face, icons or brand colours.

## Brief

<!-- brief:start. Generated by tools/brief.py from catalog.json, the tokens file and this README; `make brief` rewrites it. Do not edit. -->

Read this before designing in this style. Take every value from [tokens.json](tokens.json), never from the screenshots; `python3 tools/catalog.py export notion-ios` prints them all with aliases resolved.

### Principles

1. Do put navigation and content on two different greys.
2. Do keep main lists as plain text rows.
3. Do put the tabs at the top and float glass at the bottom.
4. Do keep blue for the one action or state that matters.
5. Don't fill destructive actions red.
6. Do let the AI answer in plain text, and bubble only the user.
7. Don't give Settings the grouped-card look.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Notion for iOS is a native app, so there is no CSS to read. Each screen was captured as the iPhone Mirroring window alone (`tools/capture/native/shot.sh`: `screencapture -l` on the window, then converted from Display P3 to sRGB). `tools/measure.py` reads the full-colour captures, which stay outside the repo, and writes [raw/readings.json](raw/readings.json): 151 named readings, 279 values across the two themes. `tools/build_tokens.py` maps them to tokens. The committed screenshots are the same captures quantised to 256 colours, so a reading's box can be checked against them, but no value was read from the quantised copies.

| Method in readings.json | Label in tokens.json | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| `fill`, the box's dominant colour covering 90% or more of it | Sampled | An exact flat surface |
| `fill` under 90% (glass, a caret, a small dot) | Derived | The dominant pixel; the description gives the share |
| `text`, the anti-aliased pixel furthest from the fill | Sampled (text) | A glyph colour; the true value is the same or slightly further from the fill |
| `text` on a hairline | Derived | A divider or rim colour: the hairline's most extreme pixel |
| `bbox`, `extent` | Sampled | Sizes and positions exact to the pixel (half a point) |
| `diag`, the inset along a corner's diagonal; `rimdiag`, the same for an outlined shape | Derived | A corner radius, plus or minus 2 pt |
| `cap`, a capital's ink height less a pixel of anti-aliasing, at a 0.72 em cap height | Derived | A font size, plus or minus 1 pt |
| `lines`, the pitch of text bands | Derived | A line height, or a row height when the bands are row titles |
| By eye | Derived | Font weights |
| Not determinable | Not captured | The font family |

`rimdiag` was added to this package's `measure.py` for Notion's outlined controls (Settings buttons and selects, the result card, the database frame), whose fill is the page colour, so the kit's `diag` has no fill edge to walk to. Scale steps (spacing, radii) are Derived from the readings named in each token's description. Where a role was read in dark only (the Sort list, radio rings), the light token is Derived and says what it was filled from.

## What to distrust

- **The font.** Not determinable from pixels. The shapes are a neo-grotesque close to the iOS system face, and the measured sizes land on 33, 29, 23, 20, 17, 16, 15, 13 and 12 pt, which fits it, but `fontFamily.base` is `system` and marked Not captured.
- **Two inks in dark.** The sidebar, pages and Settings use `text.primary` (#F2F0EE), but the AI chat, Inbox and small sheet titles read #D4D4D4 (`text.prose`). Light shows the same split, much smaller (#2B2A2A against #302F2D). It may come from two rendering layers; it is not a published role.
- **Settings is warmer than the native sheets.** Its off switch reads #65635F against the sheets' #58585A in dark (#E1DFDD against #C5C5C7 in light), so `switch.offSettings` is a separate token.
- **Glass.** The tabs, bottom bar, nav buttons, composer and menus are iOS 26 glass. Their tokens are centre readings of a translucent material that takes colour from what is behind it; in light, several sit within a step of the page. Use the system material, not a flat fill.
- **Radii are estimates**, plus or minus 2 pt: outlined controls read 3.4 pt, the database frame 6.8, cards 8.5 to 13.7 (10 used), the composer 19 to 21, the bubble 22, the system Sort list 25.6, menus 32 and sheets 36.
- **Type sizes are estimates**, plus or minus 1 pt, from cap heights at an assumed 0.72 em. Weights are judged by eye. Only multi-line blocks have line heights.
- **Only four group colours.** The database showed blue, green, pink and orange groups. Notion's other select and group colours were not on screen.
- **Light covers 17 of 29 screens.** The Sort radio list, the typed composer, the Inbox filter menu, Views, the row Actions sheet, the workspace menu and the open Settings select were captured in dark only. Light 26 and 28 were scrolled to a different place than dark, so their crops have their own boxes.
- **Every switch was off.** No on state was captured, so there is no on colour.
- **No pressed states, no motion, no keyboard, no dialogs.** The captures are stills made with clicks; iPhone Mirroring hides the software keyboard; Move to Bin was not tapped.
- **Screenshots are 256-colour PNGs.** Flat UI colours survive exactly; glass and the cover image band slightly. Read values from tokens.json.

## Do and don't

1. **Do put navigation and content on two different greys.** Home, Chats, Inbox and Settings sit on `bg.sidebar` (#202020 dark, #FAF8F8 light); a page, search and the AI chat sit on `bg.page` (#191919, #FFFFFF). See [01-home-sidebar-tree](screenshots/dark/01-home-sidebar-tree.png), [17-page-headings-table](screenshots/dark/17-page-headings-table.png) and [17 light](screenshots/light/17-page-headings-table.png).
2. **Do keep main lists as plain text rows.** The sidebar tree, Chats and search are rows of an icon and a title on the canvas, with no cards and at most a hairline; grouped cards appear only inside sheets. See [02-chats-tab-list](screenshots/dark/02-chats-tab-list.png), [06-search-focused-recents](screenshots/dark/06-search-focused-recents.png) and [19-page-actions-sheet](screenshots/dark/19-page-actions-sheet.png).
3. **Do put the tabs at the top and float glass at the bottom.** The four tabs sit beside the account button, and the bottom holds only search, Ask AI and compose as glass over the content. See [01-home-sidebar-tree](screenshots/dark/01-home-sidebar-tree.png) and [04-inbox-tab](screenshots/dark/04-inbox-tab.png).
4. **Do keep blue for the one action or state that matters.** The + Chat button, the send button, the database + button, a chosen radio, Done and an active filter are blue; everything else is warm grey. See [15-chats-sheet-primary-button](screenshots/dark/15-chats-sheet-primary-button.png), [12-ai-composer-typed-send-enabled](screenshots/dark/12-ai-composer-typed-send-enabled.png) and [22-filter-sheet-properties](screenshots/dark/22-filter-sheet-properties.png).
5. **Don't fill destructive actions red.** Move to Bin is red text with a red icon at the end of an ordinary card. See [19-page-actions-sheet](screenshots/dark/19-page-actions-sheet.png) and [23-row-longpress-actions](screenshots/dark/23-row-longpress-actions.png).
6. **Do let the AI answer in plain text, and bubble only the user.** The owner's message is a grey bubble on the right; the answer is prose on the canvas with an outlined card for the pages it changed. See [16-ai-chat-thread-bubbles](screenshots/dark/16-ai-chat-thread-bubbles.png).
7. **Don't give Settings the grouped-card look.** Settings is one scrolling list of title, subtitle and an outlined control, with inset hairlines between rows. See [25-settings-notifications-switches-off](screenshots/dark/25-settings-notifications-switches-off.png) and [26-settings-select-and-secondary-button](screenshots/dark/26-settings-select-and-secondary-button.png).

## Rebuild

From this folder. `measure.py` needs the full-colour captures, which live outside the repo in `~/.design-systems/notion-ios/raw-shots/`; the other builders run from the committed files.

```bash
python3 tools/measure.py ~/.design-systems/notion-ios/raw-shots
python3 tools/build_tokens.py
python3 tools/build_style_guide.py
python3 tools/build_components_md.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools
git status --short .
```

`tools/capture/quantize.py <raw-shots>` and `tools/crops.py` (both need Pillow) rewrite `screenshots/` and `components/crops/`. The raw shots are unmasked, so after `quantize.py` and before `crops.py`, mask the screenshots again from the repository root: `python3 tools/redact_images.py apply systems/notion-ios --rules tools/redact-rules/notion-ios.json --only 'screenshots/*'`. `apply` keeps each image's palette, its transparency and every pixel outside the boxes. `crops.py` cuts the crops from the masked screenshots. A rebuild that changes nothing proves the outputs still match their inputs. From the repository root, `make design-md` regenerates DESIGN.md and the Brief.

## Use in SwiftUI and Figma

A colour asset per semantic key, with a light and a dark variant, maps one to one: the key names (`color.bg.sidebar`) are the contract, and both sets have the same keys. Sizes and radii in `core` are points. Use the system components for the glass tabs, bottom bar, menus, sheets, switches and the Sort list rather than redrawing them; draw the outlined Settings controls and the database groups yourself. In Figma, import tokens.json with Tokens Studio and pick the Light or Dark theme.
