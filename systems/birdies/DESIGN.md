## Geometry

Buttons, Shop all, and size chips use 0 radius. The announcement bar is 44px. The header is 88px. The hero button is 50px. Add to bag is 58px. A material tab on the product is a 48px pill with 50px radius. That pill was not switched.

## Components

The primary button is ink with white type, 50px, square. Add to bag is the same ink, 58px, and was not clicked.
