# Components

Public United States shop, signed out, 22 November 2026. Crops were not taken. The screenshots show the components in place.

## Header

White bar on interior pages, 70px, wordmark at 120 by 50, uppercase Cabin nav, then country, search, account, and cart. On the homepage hero the bar is transparent and the same marks paint white. See [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png) and [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Promo bar

30px, `#4d4d4d`, white Cabin 12px, letter-spacing 1.2px. Our store locations sits at the right in 8px uppercase. See the homepage shot.

## Menu

Hovering Luggage opens a white panel: three link columns (Shop luggage, Collections, More) and a Spotlight row of two photos. Column labels are small uppercase. Links are Cabin 12.32px, `#4d4d4d`. The panel was not photographed as its own file. The links are in [site-tree.md](site-tree.md).

## Button

Add to cart is `#4d4d4d`, white uppercase Cabin 14px, weight 700, letter-spacing 2.8px, 48px tall, square corners, 560px wide on the product column. It was not clicked. The hero Use code button and Learn more are white 2px outlines, transparent fill, 20px 60px padding, square corners. Sign up is a white fill, black label, 30px tall, and was not clicked.

## Swatch

24px circle, 1px `#b3b3b3` border. Desert Taupe paints `#c7b9ad`. See [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Link

Department links are uppercase and not underlined at rest. Footer links are Cabin 12px `#b3b3b3`.

## Card

Luggage landing uses four photo tiles with Cabin 24px names (Classic, Hybrid, Expandable, Aluminum). Homepage product cards use an 18px title and a `#666666` price. See the luggage and homepage shots.

## Text field

The footer email field and the header search field exist. Neither was typed in or submitted.

## Gaps

Hover, focus, and pressed were not measured. No text area, select, checkbox, radio, switch, tabs, tooltip, dialog, toast, table, avatar, or badge was opened as its own state. The account dialog, cart, and store locator were not opened. No element crops.
