# Patterns

## Storefront chrome

A 30px `#4d4d4d` promo line sits above a 70px header. The wordmark is 120 by 50. Departments are uppercase Cabin. Search, account, and cart are icons. On the homepage hero the header paints white over the photo. On interior pages it paints `#4d4d4d` on white. See [home-light-1440.png](screenshots/home-light-1440.png).

## Homepage

A full-bleed hero with a stacked white title, a kicker, an outline code button, and an italic sale line. Further down, a collection row and a product strip. The footer was read from the DOM and not photographed, because it holds the email field.

## Collection

`/collections/luggage` is a centered 36px title, a gray Vollkorn intro, then a four-tile row. See [category-light-1440.png](screenshots/category-light-1440.png).

## Product

Photo on the left, buy box on the right: title, price, colour circles, then Add to cart. Add to cart was not clicked. See [product-light-1440.png](screenshots/product-light-1440.png).

## Story

`/pages/our-story` opens on a full-bleed photo, a white 48px title, a white dek, and an outline Learn more button. See [about-light-1440.png](screenshots/about-light-1440.png).

## Not reached

Account, cart, checkout, gift-card purchase, newsletter submit, search submit, contact, returns, rewards, and the store locator.
