## Typography

Cabin is the UI face. Vollkorn is the body and intro face. Vollkorn SC is the eyebrow. The homepage hero is 63px Cabin, weight 500, letter-spacing 15px. The product title is 36px Cabin, weight 700. Nav links are 12.32px Cabin, weight 700, uppercase.

## Geometry

Add to cart, outline buttons, and the newsletter field use a 0px radius. The code chip is 2px. Colour swatches are circles. The promo bar is 30px. The header is 70px. The logo box is 120 by 50. Add to cart is 48px tall.

## Components

The primary button is `#4d4d4d` on white, 48px tall, square corners, uppercase 14px Cabin. Outline buttons are a 2px white stroke on a transparent fill.
