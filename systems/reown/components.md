## Gaps

- Disabled, loading and error states were not shown on any public control.
- The contact sales dialog and the status subscribe dialog were opened and not submitted. Their fields were not measured as filled states.
- Dashboard components behind https://dashboard.reown.com/sign-in are not captured.
- Phone nav at 390 clips the Log in label and the bottom of the overlay. Recorded in screenshots/mobile-home-light-nav.png.
- Hover and focus-visible were taken. Several controls do not change computed color on hover. Pressed was forced with the site's `is-active` class, not a real mousedown.
