# Tella

Tella is a screen recorder and video editor (tella.com, formerly tella.tv). This package covers the public marketing site and the signed-out sign-in, read on 19 November 2026. Themes in the token file are the light marketing surface that was on screen, and the `.dark` custom properties declared in the same stylesheet. Widths are 1440 and 390. Nothing was submitted.

### Principles

1. Primary actions are the indigo pill.
2. Secondary actions are a white pill with an 8% border.
3. Headings stay at weight 400.
4. The page is warm off-white, not the declared dark tokens.
5. Don't invent a hover colour for the marketing buttons.

Each rule links the screenshot or crop that shows it, under [Do and don't](#do-and-dont).

## How the values were read

Computed styles were read in the T3 preview browser on www.tella.com (the tella.tv URL redirects there) and on www.tella.com/pricing, at 1440 by 900 after a freeform resize, device pixel ratio 2. Custom properties were read from the same-origin stylesheet `2i_uebu3oc35x.css` (deploy `production-44367545421-1`): the `:root, :host` rule for light, the `.dark` rule for dark. Component boxes were `getComputedStyle` on the live elements.

Sign-in was opened through the Log in link, which lands on authkit.tella.tv (WorkOS AuthKit). The email field was focused so its outline could be read. It was not submitted. Google and Apple were not clicked.

Screenshots were taken with headless Playwright after the preview browser returned "The browser is not producing screenshot frames". That session loaded only www.tella.com and www.tella.com/pricing, which had already loaded in the preview browser without a Cloudflare challenge. The login host was not opened in Playwright. Shots are 1440 by 900 and 390 by 844, at 2x, then quantized to 256 colours.

## What to distrust

The marketing page carries `class="dark"` on `html`, and the computed custom properties on the root are the dark set. The painted page is light. Tokens in `light` follow the paint. Tokens in `dark` follow the `.dark` declaration. They are not a screen that was captured.

`bg-indigo-800` paints `#5E51F8`. Declared `--primary` is `#4F46E5` in both themes. Buttons use the first.

Black at 50% and black at 8% are flattened over `#FDFDFD` in the tokens (`#848483`, `#EAEAE9`) because the token check stores hex. The computed forms are in the token descriptions and in [raw/components-computed.json](raw/components-computed.json).

The 256-colour screenshots band photographs and gradients. Read colour from the tokens.

AuthKit colours that were `color(display-p3 ...)` are stored as the sRGB alpha the browser reported. The focus outline `#939BFF` is the computed `rgb(147, 155, 255)`.

ABC Oracle, Inter, Untitled Sans and the AuthKit interFont files are not in this package. The style guide falls back to Arial.

## Fonts

ABC Oracle (Regular 400, Medium 500, and the italic cuts) is the marketing face, loaded as woff2 from the site, with a local Arial fallback. It is proprietary and is not committed.

Inter (100 to 900) is loaded and is the computed face on `html`. The marketing chrome does not use it.

AuthKit uses interFont on the card and Untitled Sans on the body, then system-ui. Neither file is here.

Source Serif 4 and EB Garamond are loaded (`--font-quote`, `--font-eb-garamond`). No measured heading used them.

Inconsolata and Roboto Mono are loaded. `--font-mono` is the system mono stack. A code specimen was not measured.

## Do and don't

- **Primary actions are the indigo pill.** Sign up, Get started and the highlighted Buy now use `#5E51F8`, white type, full pill, 44px on the body size. [components/crops/button-primary-large.png](components/crops/button-primary-large.png)
- **Secondary actions are a white pill with an 8% border.** Log in and the other plan buttons do not use the indigo fill. [components/crops/button-secondary.png](components/crops/button-secondary.png)
- **Headings stay at weight 400.** The marketing display sizes (52, 32, 24) are regular Oracle, not bold. [screenshots/home-light-default.png](screenshots/home-light-default.png)
- **The page is warm off-white, not the declared dark tokens.** Paint `#FDFDFD` and `#0C0A09` for the marketing shell. The `.dark` set belongs to the app theme that was not on screen. [screenshots/pricing-light-default.png](screenshots/pricing-light-default.png)
- **Don't invent a hover colour for the marketing buttons.** AuthKit publishes hover `#5345E1` and active `#4833CF`. The marketing buttons only declare a 150ms transition. Those states were not painted.
